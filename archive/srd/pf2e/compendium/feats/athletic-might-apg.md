# Athletic Might  *Feat 5*  
[orc](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/orc.md "Orc Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Surviving in [hostile](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hostile) terrain has given you a great talent for mobility. Whenever you roll a success on an [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check to [Climb](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/climb.md) or [Swim](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/swim.md), you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 18*
