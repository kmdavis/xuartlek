# Holistic Care  *Feat 6*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: trained in [Diplomacy](skills.md#Diplomacy), [Treat Condition](treat-condition-apg.md)

You provide emotional and spiritual care.

Add [frightened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Frightened), [stupefied](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Stupefied), and [stunned](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) to the list of conditions you can reduce with Treat Condition. If the [stunned](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) condition has a duration instead of a value, you can't use Treat Condition to reduce it.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 184*
