# Lifeblood's Call  *Feat 13*  
[orc](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/orc.md "Orc Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


The impulse to survive and continue fighting resonates deep within you. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage rolls on your melee [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) equal to twice the sum of your [wounded](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Wounded) and [doomed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Doomed) conditions (to a maximum of a +8 circumstance bonus to damage) whenever you have one or both conditions.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 52*
