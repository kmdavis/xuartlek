# Expanded Splash  *Feat 10*  
[alchemist](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/alchemist.md "Alchemist Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Calculated Splash](calculated-splash.md)

The particularly volatile compounds that you brew into your bombs result in them creating especially large and powerful explosions. When you throw an [alchemical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/alchemical.md "Alchemical Item Trait") [bomb](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/bomb.md "Bomb Item Trait") and that bomb has the [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") trait, you can add your Intelligence modifier to the bomb's usual [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage, and it deals [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage to every creature within 10 feet of the target.

## Expanded Splash leads to...

[Mega Bomb](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/mega-bomb.md), [Controlled Blast](controlled-blast-g-g.md)
