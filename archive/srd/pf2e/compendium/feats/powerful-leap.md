# Powerful Leap  *Feat 2*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: expert in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics)

When you [Leap](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/leap.md), you can jump 5 feet up with a vertical [Leap](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/leap.md), and you increase the distance you can jump horizontally by 5 feet.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 264*
