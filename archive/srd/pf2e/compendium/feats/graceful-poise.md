# Graceful Poise  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 16*  
[fighter](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fighter.md "Fighter Class Trait")  [stance](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/stance.md "Stance Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Double Slice](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/double-slice.md)
- **Activity** Single Action

With the right positioning, your off-hand weapon can strike like a scorpion's stinger. While you are in this stance, if you make your second [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) from Double Slice with an [agile](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/agile.md "Agile Weapon Trait") weapon, Double Slice counts as one attack when calculating your multiple attack penalty.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 153*
