# Transcribe Moment  *Feat 8*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Scrollmaster Dedication](scrollmaster-dedication-locg.md)

You gain the [transcribe moment](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/transcribe-moment-lopsg.md) focus spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can [Refocus](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/refocus.md) by spending 10 minutes to study a text or record notes on your recent experiences.

*Source: Lost Omens: Pathfinder Society Guide p. 45*
