# Tumble Behind (Rogue)  *Feat 1*  
[rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  


You tumble under and behind your foe to catch them off guard. When you successfully [Tumble Through](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/tumble-through.md), the foe whose space you passed through is [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 134*
