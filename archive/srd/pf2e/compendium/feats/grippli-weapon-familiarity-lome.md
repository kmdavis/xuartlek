# Grippli Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*  
[grippli](grippli-b2.md "Grippli Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You've trained with weapons ideally suited to marshes and dense forests. You are trained with [blowguns](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/blowgun.md), [hatchets](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/hatchet.md), [scythes](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/scythe.md), [shortbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/shortbow.md), and [composite shortbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/composite-shortbow.md).

You also gain access to all uncommon grippli weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial grippli weapons are simple weapons, and advanced grippli weapons are martial weapons.

## Grippli Weapon Familiarity leads to...

[Grippli Weapon Expertise](grippli-weapon-expertise-lome.md), [Grippli Weapon Innovator](grippli-weapon-innovator-lome.md)
