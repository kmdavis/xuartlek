# Jalmeri Rakshasa Magic  *Feat 9*  
[tiefling](tiefling-b1.md "Tiefling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Beastbrood](beastbrood-loag.md)

You can cast [dispel magic](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/dispel-magic.md) and [reaper's lantern](reapers-lantern-apg.md) once per day each as 2nd-level [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait") innate spells. [Reaper's lantern](reapers-lantern-apg.md) manifests as a lantern shaped like the head of your rakshasa incarnation.

*Source: Lost Omens: Impossible Lands p. 33*
