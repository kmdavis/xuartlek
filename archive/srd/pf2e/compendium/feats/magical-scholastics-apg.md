# Magical Scholastics  *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Archaeologist Dedication](archaeologist-dedication-apg.md)

While study is key to your success, a bit of magic is a helpful tool in discovering the secrets of the past. You gain [detect magic](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/detect-magic.md), [guidance](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/guidance.md), and [read aura](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/read-aura.md) as occult innate cantrips.
