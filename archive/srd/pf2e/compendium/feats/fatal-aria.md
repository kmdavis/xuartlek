# Fatal Aria  *Feat 20*  
[bard](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/bard.md "Bard Class Trait")  


Your songs overwhelm the target with unbearable emotion, potentially striking them dead on the spot. You learn the [fatal aria](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/fatal-aria.md) composition spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 103*
