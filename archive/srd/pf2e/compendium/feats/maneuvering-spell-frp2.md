# Maneuvering Spell  [F](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Free Action") *Feat 12*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Sixth Pillar Dedication](sixth-pillar-dedication-frp2.md)
- **Trigger** You begin to [Cast a Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md) that requires at least 2 actions to cast.
- **Activity** Free Action

You instantly tap into the magical power of a spell you're casting to quickly pounce around the battlefield. You [Step](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/step.md), [Leap](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/leap.md), or [Stand](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stand.md). This action can be before or after you [Cast the Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md).

*Source: Fists of the Ruby Phoenix #2: Ready? Fight! p. 77*
