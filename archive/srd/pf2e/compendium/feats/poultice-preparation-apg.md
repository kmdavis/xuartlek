# Poultice Preparation  *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Herbalist Dedication](herbalist-dedication-apg.md)

When you create an herbal elixir, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the [oil](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/oil.md "Oil Item Trait") trait (but remains alchemical, not magical) and loses the [elixir](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/elixir.md "Elixir Item Trait") and [ingested](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/ingested.md "Ingested Item Trait") traits. You can apply a poultice by Interacting with one hand.

When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item's normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [persistent acid, bleed, or fire damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 176*
