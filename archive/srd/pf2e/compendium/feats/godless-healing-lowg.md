# Godless Healing  *Feat 2*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Battle Medicine](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battle-medicine.md), can't have a patron deity

You recover an additional 5 Hit Points from a successful attempt to [Treat your Wounds](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/treat-wounds.md) or use [Battle Medicine](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battle-medicine.md) on you. After you or an ally use [Battle Medicine](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battle-medicine.md) on you, you become temporarily immune to that [Battle Medicine](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battle-medicine.md) for only 1 hour, instead of 1 day.
