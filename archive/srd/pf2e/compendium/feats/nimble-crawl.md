# Nimble Crawl  *Feat 2*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: expert in [Acrobatics](skills.md#Acrobatics)

You can [Crawl](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/crawl.md) incredibly swiftly—up to half your Speed, rather than 5 feet. If you're a master in [Acrobatics](skills.md#Acrobatics), you can [Crawl](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/crawl.md) at full Speed, and if you're legendary, you aren't [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) while [prone](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Prone).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 264*
