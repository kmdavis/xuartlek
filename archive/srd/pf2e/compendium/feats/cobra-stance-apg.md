# Cobra Stance  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 4*  
[monk](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/monk.md "Monk Class Trait")  [stance](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/stance.md "Stance Combat Trait")  

- **Activity** Single Action

You enter a tight stance, coiled up like a lashing cobra with your hands poised as venomous fangs. While in this stance, the only [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) you can make are cobra fang unarmed attacks.

These deal `1d4` poison damage; are in the brawling group; and have the [agile](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/agile.md "Agile Weapon Trait"), [deadly <d10>](rules/traits/deadly-d10.md "Deadly Weapon Trait"), [finesse](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/finesse.md "Finesse Weapon Trait"), [nonlethal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/nonlethal.md "Nonlethal Weapon Trait"), [poison](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/poison.md "Poison Effect Trait"), and [unarmed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/unarmed.md "Unarmed Weapon Trait") traits.

While in Cobra Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves and your Fortitude DC, and you gain poison resistance equal to half your level.
