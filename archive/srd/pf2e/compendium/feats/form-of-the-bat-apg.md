# Form Of The Bat  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 13*

- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Activity** Single Action

You can transform into a harmless bat. You gain the effects of a 4th-level [pest form](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/pest-form.md), except you always transform into a bat.
