# Skim Scroll  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 8*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Scroll Trickster Dedication](scroll-trickster-dedication-apg.md)
- **Activity** Single Action

You can activate the magic of a scroll with a cursory read as you draw it from your belt. You [Interact](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/interact.md) to draw forth a scroll, then use Trick Magic Item on the scroll.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 189*
