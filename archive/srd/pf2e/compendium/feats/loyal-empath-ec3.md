# Loyal Empath  *Feat 5*  
[shoony](shoony-ec3.md "Shoony Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You have incredible empathy for your friends and family, and you have an innate timing for helping others in distress.

You can use the [Aid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/aid.md) reaction to grant a bonus to another creature's Will saving throw. As usual for [Aid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/aid.md), you need to prepare by using an action on your turn to encourage the creature to bravely withstand the effect.

*Source: Extinction Curse #3: Life's Long Shadows p. 73*
