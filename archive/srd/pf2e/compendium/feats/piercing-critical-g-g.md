# Piercing Critical  *Feat 18*  
[gunslinger](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/gunslinger-g-g.md "Gunslinger Class Trait")  


Your attacks with your preferred weapons deal especially devastating wounds. When you [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) with a firearm or crossbow for which you have legendary proficiency, you critically succeed if you roll a 19 on the die, as long as that result is a success.

*Source: Guns & Gears p. 119*
