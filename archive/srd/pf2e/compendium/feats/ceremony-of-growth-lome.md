# Ceremony Of Growth  *Feat 13*  
[conrasu](conrasu-loag.md "Conrasu Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You can alter your exoskeleton's size. You constantly gain the effects of enlarge. You can dismiss or resume these effects as an action, which has the [concentrate](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/concentrate.md "Concentrate Action & Ability Trait") trait.

*Source: Lost Omens: The Mwangi Expanse p. 110*
