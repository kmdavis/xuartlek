# Quick Recognition  *Feat 7*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: master in [Arcana](skills.md#Arcana), [Nature](skills.md#Nature), [Occultism](skills.md#Occultism), or [Religion](skills.md#Religion); [Recognize Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/recognize-spell.md)

You Recognize Spells swiftly. Once per round, you can Recognize a Spell using a skill in which you're a master as a free action.
