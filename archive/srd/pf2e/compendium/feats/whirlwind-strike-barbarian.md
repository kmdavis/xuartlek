# Whirlwind Strike (Barbarian)  [>>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Three-Action") *Feat 14*  
[barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  [flourish](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/flourish.md "Flourish Combat Trait")  [open](open.md "Open Combat Trait")  

- **Activity** Three-Action

You attack all nearby adversaries. Make a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) against each enemy within your melee reach. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 93*
