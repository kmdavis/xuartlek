# Rescuer's Press  *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [rare](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rare.md "Rare Rarity Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Lastwall Sentry Dedication](lastwall-sentry-dedication-lowg.md)

Any shield you wield gains the [shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/shove.md "Shove Weapon Trait") trait. When you [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) using a shield and [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) as part of that action, you can move an additional 5 feet to either side of the creature you Shoved. In addition, if you are in the [Everstand Stance](everstand-stance-locg.md) and successfully [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) a creature using a shield that you wield two-handed, you can increase the distance of your [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) to 10 feet.

*Source: Lost Omens: Legends p. 68*
