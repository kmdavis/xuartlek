# Runtsage  *Feat 5*  
[hobgoblin](hobgoblin-locg.md "Hobgoblin Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Unlike most of your kind, who dismiss goblins as embarrassments or expendable annoyances, you have studied the methodology behind their irresponsible and incomprehensible actions. You gain the [Adopted Ancestry](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/adopted-ancestry.md) general feat and must select goblin as the feat's chosen ancestry. You also gain one 1st-level goblin ancestry feat.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 35*
