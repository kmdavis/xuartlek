- **Activity** Two-Action

You make a vicious attack that maims your enemy. Make a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md). If the [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) hits and deals damage, the target is [drained](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Drained), or [drained](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Drained) on a critical success.
