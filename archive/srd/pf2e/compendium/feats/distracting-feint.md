# Distracting Feint  *Feat 2*  
[rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: Scoundrel racket

Your [Feints](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/feint.md) are far more distracting than normal, drawing your foes' attention and allowing you and your allies to take greater advantage. While a creature is [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) by your [Feint](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/feint.md), it also takes a –2 circumstance penalty to [Perception](skills.md#Perception) checks and Reflex saves.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 183*
