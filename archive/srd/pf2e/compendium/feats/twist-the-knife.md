# Twist The Knife  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 6*  
[rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  

- **Requirements**: Your last action was a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) that dealt sneak attack damage to a [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) target.
- **Activity** Single Action

After stabbing your opponent in a weak spot, you tear the wound open. You deal [persistent bleed damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) to the target equal to your number of sneak attack damage dice.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 186*
