# Shattering Blows  *Feat 16*  
[barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  [rage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rage.md "Rage Combat Trait")  


Your forceful blows shatter objects with ease. While you are [raging](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/rage.md), your melee [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) ignore 5 points of an object's Hardness. If you have the devastator class feature, you instead ignore 10 points of an object's Hardness.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 111*
