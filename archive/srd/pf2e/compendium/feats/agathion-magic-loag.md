# Agathion Magic  *Feat 9*  
[aasimar](aasimar-apg.md "Aasimar Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Idyllkin](idyllkin-loag.md)

The harmonious magic of Nirvana flows through you. You can cast [animal form](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/animal-form.md) and [calm emotions](calm-emotions.md) each once per day as 2nd-level divine innate spells.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 11*
