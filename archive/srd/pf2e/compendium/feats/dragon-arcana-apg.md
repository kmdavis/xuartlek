# Dragon Arcana  *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: ability to cast spells from spell slots, [Dragon Disciple Dedication](dragon-disciple-dedication-apg.md)

You've discovered how to add the magic of dragons to your tradition. Add the draconic bloodline's granted spells to your spell list; you must still learn them or add them to your repertoire as normal. These spells are [shield](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/shield.md), [true strike](true-strike.md), [resist energy](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/resist-energy.md), [haste](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/haste.md), [spell immunity](spell-immunity.md), [chromatic wall](chromatic-wall.md), [dragon form](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/dragon-form.md), [mask of terror](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/mask-of-terror.md), [prismatic wall](prismatic-wall.md), and [overwhelming presence](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/overwhelming-presence.md).

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 168*
