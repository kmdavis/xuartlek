# Adroit Manipulation  *Feat 1*  
[halfling](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/halfling.md "Halfling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  

- **Access**: Mihrini ethnicity

You've fiddled with knots, locks, and blacksmith's puzzles since you were a child to give your hands something to do. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [Thievery](skills.md#Thievery) (or another skill of your choice, if you're already trained in [Thievery](skills.md#Thievery)). If you roll a success on a [Thievery](skills.md#Thievery) check to [Pick a Lock](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/pick-a-lock.md), you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Lost Omens: Character Guide p. 44*
