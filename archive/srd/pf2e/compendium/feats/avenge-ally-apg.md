# Avenge Ally  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 13*  
[elf](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/elf.md "Elf Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  [fortune](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fortune.md "Fortune Effect Trait")  

- **Frequency**: once every 10 minutes
- **Requirements**: You are adjacent to an ally with the [dying](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Dying) condition.
- **Activity** Single Action

Though you know that you will eventually outlive your companions, seeing them at death's door brings clarity to your attacks. Make a [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md). Roll twice on the attack roll and use the higher result.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 43*
