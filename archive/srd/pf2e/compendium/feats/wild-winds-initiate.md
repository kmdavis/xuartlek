# Wild Winds Initiate  *Feat 8*  
[monk](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/monk.md "Monk Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: ki spells

You learn a mystical stance that lets you attack from a distance. You gain the [wild winds stance](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/wild-winds-stance.md) ki spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. While entering the stance is a ki spell, the wind crash [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) the stance grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.

## Wild Winds Initiate leads to...

[Wild Winds Gust](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/wild-winds-gust.md), [Electric Counter](electric-counter-frp2.md)
