# Discordant Voice  *Feat 18*  
[bard](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/bard.md "Bard Class Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  [sonic](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/sonic.md "Sonic Energy & Element Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [inspire courage](inspire-courage.md)

Your inspire courage lets loose a discordant shriek that imbues your allies' attacks with powerful sonic reverberations. While your allies are affected by your inspire courage, their weapon [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) and unarmed attacks deal an additional `1d6` sonic damage.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 115*
