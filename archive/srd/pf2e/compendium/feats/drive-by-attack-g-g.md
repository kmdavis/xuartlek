# Drive-by Attack  [V](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Varies") Varies ([>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") TO [>>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Three-Action")) *Feat 12*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [flourish](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/flourish.md "Flourish Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Trick Driver Dedication](trick-driver-dedication-g-g.md)
- **Requirements**: You are piloting a vehicle.
- **Activity** Varies ([>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") TO [>>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Three-Action"))

You can smoothly line up an attack without disrupting your control. You [Drive](drive-gmg.md), gaining the effects of the [Drive](drive-gmg.md) action for the same number of actions you spent to [Drive](drive-gmg.md)- By Attack. At any point during your vehicle's movement, you [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md).
