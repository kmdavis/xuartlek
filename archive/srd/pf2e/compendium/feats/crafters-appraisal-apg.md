# Crafter's Appraisal  *Feat 1*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: trained in [Crafting](skills.md#Crafting)

Your knowledge of items' construction allows you to discern their [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait") effects as well. You can use [Crafting](skills.md#Crafting) instead of a skill associated with a magic tradition to [Identify Magic](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/identify-magic.md) on magic items, though not on any other sorts of magic.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 204*
