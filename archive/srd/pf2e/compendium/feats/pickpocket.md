# Pickpocket  *Feat 1*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: trained in [Thievery](skills.md#Thievery)

You can [Steal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/steal.md) or [Palm an Object](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/palm-an-object.md) that's closely guarded, such as in a pocket, without taking the –5 penalty. You can't steal objects that would be extremely noticeable or time consuming to remove (like worn shoes or armor or actively wielded objects). If you're a master in [Thievery](skills.md#Thievery), you can attempt to [Steal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/steal.md) from a creature in combat or otherwise on guard. When doing so, Stealing requires 2 manipulate actions instead of 1, and you take a –5 penalty.
