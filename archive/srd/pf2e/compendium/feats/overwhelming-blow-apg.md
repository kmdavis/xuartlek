# Overwhelming Blow  [>>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Three-Action") *Feat 16*  
[fighter](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fighter.md "Fighter Class Trait")  

- **Activity** Three-Action

You throw your weight into a powerful attack that leaves you vulnerable. Make a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md). This counts as three attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If this [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) hits, you get a critical hit. If you roll a critical hit, your attack also gains the [deadly <d12>](rules/traits/deadly-d12.md "Deadly Weapon Trait") trait. Whether or not you hit, you become [stunned](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) and are [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 127*
