# Basic Bloodline Spell  *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Sorcerer Dedication](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/sorcerer-dedication.md)

You gain your bloodline|Sorcerer||1's initial bloodline spell. If you don't already have one, you also gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can [Refocus](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/refocus.md) without any special effort.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 230*
