# Quicken Heartbeat  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 10*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Golem Grafter Dedication](golem-grafter-dedication-ec3.md)
- **Frequency**: once per turn
- **Activity** Single Action

You've replaced your heart with one made of animated quicksilver and living adamantine. You reduce your [slowed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Slowed) condition by 1. Alternatively, if you are not [slowed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Slowed), you are [quickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Quickened) during your next turn, and can use your extra action to [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) or [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md).

*Source: Extinction Curse #3: Life's Long Shadows p. 74*
