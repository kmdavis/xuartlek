# Educated Assessment  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 12*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [secret](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/secret.md "Secret General Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Pathfinder Agent Dedication](pathfinder-agent-dedication-lowg.md)
- **Activity** Single Action

You attempt to [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md) about a creature, and the GM uses the same secret roll result against the creature's [Deception](skills.md#Deception) or [Stealth](skills.md#Stealth) DC, giving you the information from a [Battle Assessment](battle-assessment.md). You might get a different degree of success on the [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md) check than what you get for the Battle Assessment. If you have the Battle Assessment feat, you gain the effects of Educated Assessment whenever you [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md) (such as with the [Automatic Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/automatic-knowledge.md) feat)

*Source: Lost Omens: Character Guide p. 112*
