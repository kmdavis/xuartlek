# Reflective Ripple Stance  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 1*  
[evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  [monk](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/monk.md "Monk Class Trait")  [stance](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/stance.md "Stance Combat Trait")  [water](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/water.md "Water Energy & Element Trait")  

- **Requirements**: You're unarmored.
- **Activity** Single Action

You enter a stance of fluid grace as small amounts of water flow with your movements and attacks. You can make flowing wave attacks that deal `1d6` bludgeoning damage.

They are in the brawling group and have the [agile](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/agile.md "Agile Weapon Trait"), [disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/disarm.md "Disarm Weapon Trait"), [finesse](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/finesse.md "Finesse Weapon Trait"), [nonlethal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/nonlethal.md "Nonlethal Weapon Trait"), [trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/trip.md "Trip Weapon Trait"), [unarmed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/unarmed.md "Unarmed Weapon Trait"), and [water](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/water.md "Water Energy & Element Trait") traits.

While in Reflective Ripple Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) checks to [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md), [Swim](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/swim.md), or [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md), and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC to avoid being Disarmed and Tripped.

**Special.** This feat gains your choice of either the [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait") or [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait") trait, matching your ki spell tradition if possible.

[Rippling Spin](rippling-spin-som.md), [Wave Spiral](wave-spiral-som.md)
