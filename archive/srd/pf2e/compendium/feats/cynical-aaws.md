# Cynical  *Feat 1*  
[azarketi](azarketi-loag.md "Azarketi Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You've been approached with suspicion and distrust throughout your life and return these sentiments back to the strangers you encounter.

As a result, you are difficult to deceive. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your [Perception](skills.md#Perception) DC against [Lies](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/lie.md) and [Impersonations](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/impersonate.md).

*Source: Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement p. 4*
