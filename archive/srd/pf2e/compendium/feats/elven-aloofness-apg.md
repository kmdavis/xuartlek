# Elven Aloofness  *Feat 1*  
[elf](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/elf.md "Elf Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


As much as you might care for them, you've come to terms with the ephemeral nature of non-elves, and it makes their threats feel less troublesome. If a non-elf rolls a failure on a check to [Coerce](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/coerce.md) you using [Intimidation](skills.md#Intimidation), it gets a critical failure instead (and thus can't try to [Coerce](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/coerce.md) you again for 1 week). When a non-elf attempts to [Demoralize](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/demoralize.md) you, you become temporarily immune for 1 day, instead of 10 minutes.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 43*
