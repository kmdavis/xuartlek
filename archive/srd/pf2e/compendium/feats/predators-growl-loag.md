# Predator's Growl  [R](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Reaction") *Feat 9*  
[catfolk](catfolk-b1.md "Catfolk Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: expert in [Intimidation](skills.md#Intimidation)
- **Trigger** You successfully [Seek](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/seek.md) a [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) or [undetected](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Undetected) creature.
- **Activity** Reaction

You give a throaty growl to attempt to [Demoralize](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/demoralize.md) a creature you just found. You don't take the –4 penalty for not sharing a language with the creature.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 20*
