# Flinging Shove  *Feat 12*  
[fighter](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fighter.md "Fighter Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Aggressive Block](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/aggressive-block.md) or [Brutish Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/brutish-shove.md)

Increase the distance you [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) your opponent with Aggressive Block or [Brutish Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/brutish-shove.md) to 10 feet on a success or 20 feet on a critical success. When you use Aggressive Block, you can choose whether the target is [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) or [Shoved](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md). When you make a [Brutish Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/brutish-shove.md), you also [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) the target 5 feet on a failure.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 151*
