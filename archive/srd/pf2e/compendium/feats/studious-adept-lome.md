# Studious Adept  *Feat 9*  
[anadi](anadi-lome.md "Anadi Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Studious Magic](studious-magic-lome.md)

You achieve a breakthrough in your exploration of magic. You gain [humanoid form](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/humanoid-form.md) and [mirror image](mirror-image.md) as 2nd-level arcane innate spells.

You can cast each of these arcane innate spells once per day.

*Source: Lost Omens: The Mwangi Expanse p. 106*
