# Nocturnal Sense  *Feat 6*  
[barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  [rage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rage.md "Rage Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Acute Scent](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/acute-scent.md), or precise or imprecise [scent](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/scent.md) and [low-light vision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/low-light-vision.md)

Your senses gain even greater clarity. While [raging](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/rage.md), you gain [darkvision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/darkvision.md) if you don't already have it, and the range of your imprecise [scent](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/scent.md) increases to 60 feet.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 110*
