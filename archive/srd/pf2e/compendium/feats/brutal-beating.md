# Brutal Beating  *Feat 2*  
[rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: Ruffian racket

The brutality of your critical hits shakes your foes' confidence. Whenever your [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) is a critical hit and deals damage, the target is [frightened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Frightened).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 183*
