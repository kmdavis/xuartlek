# Elven Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*  
[elf](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/elf.md "Elf Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You favor bows and other elegant weapons. You are trained with [longbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/longbow.md), [composite longbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/composite-longbow.md), [longswords](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/longsword.md), [rapiers](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/rapier.md), [shortbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/shortbow.md), and [composite shortbows](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/composite-shortbow.md).

In addition, you gain access to all uncommon elf weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial elf weapons are simple weapons and advanced elf weapons are martial weapons.

[Elven Weapon Elegance](elven-weapon-elegance.md), [Elven Weapon Expertise](elven-weapon-expertise.md)
