# Targeting Shot  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 14*  
[concentrate](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/concentrate.md "Concentrate Action & Ability Trait")  [press](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/press.md "Press Combat Trait")  [ranger](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/ranger.md "Ranger Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Hunter's Aim](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/hunters-aim.md)
- **Activity** Single Action

You carefully track your prey's position and defenses, allowing you to follow up around obstacles that block your shot. Make a ranged weapon [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) against your hunted prey. You ignore the target's [concealed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Concealed) condition and all cover.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 176*
