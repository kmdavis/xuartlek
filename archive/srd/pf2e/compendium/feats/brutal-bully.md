# Brutal Bully  *Feat 6*  
[barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: expert in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics)

You push your foes around and leave bruises. While raging, when you successfully [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md), [Grapple](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/grapple.md), [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md), or [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md) a foe, you deal that foe bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier; add this to the damage from a critical success to [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 90*
