# Kitsune Spell Mysteries  *Feat 5*  
[kitsune](kitsune-loag.md "Kitsune Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: at least one innate spell from a kitsune heritage or ancestry feat

You know more kitsune magic. During your daily preparations, choose [bane](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/bane.md), [illusory object](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/illusory-object.md), or [sanctuary](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/sanctuary.md). You can cast this as a 1st-level divine innate spell once that day.
