# Thorned Seedpod  *Feat 9*  
[leshy](leshy-b1.md "Leshy Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Seedpod](seedpod-locg.md) (Character Guide 54)

When your seedpods strike a felling blow, they burst into viciously thorny vines. When you critically succeed at a seedpod [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md), vines twist and stab your foe, dealing `1d4` [persistent piercing damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage).

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 44*
