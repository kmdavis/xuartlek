# Heroic Presence  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 17*  
[emotion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/emotion.md "Emotion Effect Trait")  [human](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/human.md "Human Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  [mental](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/mental.md "Mental Effect Trait")  

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Activity** Single Action

The blood of heroes courses through your veins, and you inspire your allies to dig deep and find a new level of resolve. You grant up to 10 willing creatures within 30 feet the effects of a 6th-level [zealous conviction](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/zealous-conviction.md), though the effect automatically ends on a target if you give that target a command they would normally find repugnant. This action has the [auditory](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/auditory.md "Auditory Effect Trait") trait or [visual](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/visual.md "Visual Effect Trait") trait, depending on how you inspire your allies.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 47*
