# Parting Shot  [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") *Feat 4*  
[fighter](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fighter.md "Fighter Class Trait")  

- **Requirements**: You are wielding a loaded ranged weapon or a ranged weapon with reload 0.
- **Activity** Two-Action

You jump back and fire a quick shot that catches your opponent off guard. You [Step](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/step.md) and then make a ranged [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) with the required weapon. Your target is [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) against the attack.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 126*
