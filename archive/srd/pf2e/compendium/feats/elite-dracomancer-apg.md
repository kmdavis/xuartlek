# Elite Dracomancer  *Feat 13*  
[kobold](kobold-b1.md "Kobold Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Dracomancer](dracomancer-apg.md)

Your magic rivals that of lesser dragons. Choose one 3rd-level spell and one 4th-level spell from those listed for a dragon spellcaster of your draconic exemplar's type, as on pages 104–126 of the Bestiary (such as [paralyze](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/paralyze.md) and [stinking cloud](stinking-cloud.md) for a black dragon exemplar). You can cast each of these spells once per day as arcane innate spells.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 15*
