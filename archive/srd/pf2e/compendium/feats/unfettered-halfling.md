# Unfettered Halfling  *Feat 1*  
[halfling](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/halfling.md "Halfling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You were forced into service as a laborer, either pressed into indentured servitude or shackled by the evils of slavery, but you've since escaped and have trained to ensure you'll never be caught again. Whenever you roll a success on a check to [Escape](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/escape.md) or a saving throw against an effect that would impose the [grabbed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Grabbed) or [restrained](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition on you, you get a critical success instead. Whenever a creature rolls a failure on a check to [Grapple](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/grapple.md) you, they get a critical failure instead. If a creature uses the [Grab](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/grab.md) ability on you, it must succeed at an [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check to grab you instead of automatically grabbing you.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 52*
