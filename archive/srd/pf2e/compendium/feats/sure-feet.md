# Sure Feet  *Feat 1*  
[halfling](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/halfling.md "Halfling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Whether keeping your balance or scrambling up a tricky climb, your hairy, calloused feet easily find purchase. If you roll a success on an [Acrobatics](skills.md#Acrobatics) check to [Balance](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/balance.md) or an [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check to [Climb](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/climb.md), you get a critical success instead.

You're not [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) when you attempt to [Balance](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/balance.md) or [Climb](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/climb.md).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 52*
