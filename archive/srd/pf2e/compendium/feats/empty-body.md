# Empty Body  *Feat 18*  
[monk](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/monk.md "Monk Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: ki spells

You transmute your body into an ethereal form. You gain the [empty body](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/empty-body.md) ki spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 165*
