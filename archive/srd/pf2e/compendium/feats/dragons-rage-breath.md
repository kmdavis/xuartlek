# Dragon's Rage Breath  [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") *Feat 6*  
[arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait")  [barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  [concentrate](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/concentrate.md "Concentrate Action & Ability Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  [instinct](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/instinct.md "Instinct Action & Ability Trait")  [rage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rage.md "Rage Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: dragon instinct
- **Requirements**: You haven't used this ability since you last Raged.
- **Activity** Two-Action

You breathe deeply and exhale powerful energy in a 30-foot cone or 60-foot line, dealing 1d6 damage per level. The area and damage type match those of your dragon (see [Table 3–4](dragon-instincts.md)). If you used this ability in the last hour, the area and the damage are halved (15-foot cone or 30-foot line; 1d6 damage for every 2 levels). Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Reflex save.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 90*
