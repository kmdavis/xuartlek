# Kobold Weapon Innovator  *Feat 5*  
[kobold](kobold-b1.md "Kobold Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Kobold Weapon Familiarity](kobold-weapon-familiarity-loag.md)

You've learned devious tactics with your kobold weapons. Whenever you critically hit with a [crossbow](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/crossbow.md), [greatpick](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/greatpick.md), [light pick](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/light-pick.md), [pick](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/pick.md), [spear](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/spear.md), or kobold weapon, you apply the weapon's critical specialization effect.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 39*
