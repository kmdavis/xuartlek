# Primal Focus  *Feat 12*  
[druid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/druid.md "Druid Class Trait")  


Your connection to nature is particularly strong, and the spirits of nature flock around you, helping you replenish your focus. If you have spent at least 2 Focus Points since the last time you [Refocused](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/refocus.md), you recover 2 Focus Points when you [Refocus](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/refocus.md) instead of 1.
