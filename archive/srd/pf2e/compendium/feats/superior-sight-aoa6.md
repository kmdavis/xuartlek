# Superior Sight  *Feat 20*  
[ranger](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/ranger.md "Ranger Class Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  


Your senses are peerless. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [Perception](skills.md#Perception) checks, and you gain [low-light vision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/low-light-vision.md). If you already have low-light vision, gain [darkvision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/darkvision.md) instead. Furthermore, when you target an enemy, you automatically succeed at the flat check if that enemy is [concealed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Concealed), [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden), or [undetected](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Undetected).

*Source: Age of Ashes #6: Broken Promises p. 77*
