# Disrupt Prey  [R](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Reaction") *Feat 4*  
[ranger](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/ranger.md "Ranger Class Trait")  

- **Trigger** Your hunted prey is within your reach, and it uses a [manipulate](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/manipulate.md "Manipulate General Trait") action, uses a [move](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/move.md "Move Combat Trait") action, or leaves a square during a move action it's using.
- **Activity** Reaction

Make a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) against your prey. If the attack is a critical hit, you disrupt the triggering action.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 172*
