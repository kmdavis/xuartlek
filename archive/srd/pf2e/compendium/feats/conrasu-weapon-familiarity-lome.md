# Conrasu Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*  
[conrasu](conrasu-loag.md "Conrasu Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


You have taken it upon yourself to learn the ways of combat to aid with your tasks, perhaps in a role as a corrector. You are trained with the [composite shortbow](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/composite-shortbow.md), [glaive](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/glaive.md), [longspear](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/longspear.md), [longsword](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/longsword.md), [shortbow](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/shortbow.md), and [spear](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/spear.md). You also gain access to all uncommon conrasu weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial conrasu weapons are simple weapons and advanced conrasu weapons are martial weapons.

*Source: Lost Omens: The Mwangi Expanse p. 109*
