# Thrash  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 8*  
[barbarian](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/barbarian.md "Barbarian Class Trait")  [rage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rage.md "Rage Combat Trait")  

- **Requirements**: You have a foe [grabbed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Grabbed).
- **Activity** Single Action

You thrash the [grabbed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Grabbed) foe around. It takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier plus your weapon specialization damage plus your [Rage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/rage.md) damage. The foe must attempt a basic Fortitude save against your class DC.
