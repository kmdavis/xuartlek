# Smoldering Explosion  *Feat 8*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Spell Trickster Dedication](spell-trickster-dedication-lotgb.md), ability to cast [fireball](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/fireball.md)

When you cast [fireball](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/fireball.md) heightened to at least 4th level, you can modify the spell to add the following to its standard effects: Your [fireball](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/fireball.md) leaves behind a brief cloud of smoke in its area. While it's smoke instead of mist, this cloud otherwise has the effects of [obscuring mist](obscuring-mist.md), except that it lasts only 1 round.

## Smoldering Explosion leads to...

[Choking Smoke](choking-smoke-lotgb.md)
