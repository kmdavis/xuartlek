# Cadence Call  [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") *Feat 6*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [auditory](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/auditory.md "Auditory Effect Trait")  [flourish](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/flourish.md "Flourish Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Marshal Dedication](marshal-dedication-apg.md)
- **Frequency**: once per minute
- **Activity** Single Action

You call out a quick cadence, guiding your allies into a more efficient rhythm. Each willing ally within your marshal's aura is [quickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Quickened) until the end of their next turn, and they can use the extra action only to [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md). At the end of each ally's turn, if they used the extra action, they then become [slowed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Slowed) until the end of their following turn.
