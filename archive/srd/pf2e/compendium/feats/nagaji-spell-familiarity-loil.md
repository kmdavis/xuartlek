# Nagaji Spell Familiarity  *Feat 1*  
[nagaji](nagaji-loil.md "Nagaji Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Either through study, exposure, or familial devotion, you have the magic of nagas bubbling in your blood. During your daily preparations, choose [daze](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/daze.md), [detect magic](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/detect-magic.md), or [mage hand](mage-hand.md). Until your next daily preparations, you can cast the chosen spell as an occult innate cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Lost Omens: Impossible Lands p. 49*
