# Mixed Maneuver  [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") *Feat 8*  
[monk](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/monk.md "Monk Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: master in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics)
- **Activity** Two-Action

You combine two different maneuvers together into a single flowing whole. Choose any two of [Grapple](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/grapple.md), [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md), and [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md). Attempt both of the attacks you chose against the same or different creatures, but don't apply the multiple attack penalty until after resolving both attacks.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 163*
