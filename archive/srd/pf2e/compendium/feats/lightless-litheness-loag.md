# Lightless Litheness  *Feat 5*  
[fetchling](fetchling-b2.md "Fetchling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Your body is almost as flexible as your shadow. When you critically fail at [Squeezing](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/squeeze.md), you get a failure instead. Additionally, when you roll a success at an [Escape](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/escape.md) check, you get a critical success instead; if you roll a critical success, you can [Step](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/step.md) instead of [Striding](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) up to 5 feet.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 87*
