# Relentless Disarm  *Feat 6*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Zephyr Guard Dedication](zephyr-guard-dedication-aoa5.md), trained in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics)

When you succeed, but don't critically succeed, at a check to [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md) a creature, your circumstance bonus to further checks to [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md) that creature is +4 instead of +2. Other creatures gain only the normal bonus.

When you critically succeed at a [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md) check, you can immediately [Demoralize](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/demoralize.md) your target as a reaction.

*Source: Age of Ashes #5: Against the Scarlet Triad p. 81*
