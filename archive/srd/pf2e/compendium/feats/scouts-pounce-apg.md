# Scout's Pounce  [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") *Feat 10*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  [flourish](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/flourish.md "Flourish Combat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Scout Dedication](scout-dedication-apg.md)
- **Requirements**: You are [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) from or [undetected](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Undetected) by all of your opponents, and you aren't within 10 feet of any enemy.
- **Activity** Two-Action

You leap from the shadows to strike your foes. [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) up to your Speed, then [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) twice. If you were [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) or [unnoticed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Unnoticed) by the target of these [Strikes](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md), your foe is [flat-footed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Flat-footed) against both attacks. Your multiple attack penalty applies normally for both attacks.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 188*
