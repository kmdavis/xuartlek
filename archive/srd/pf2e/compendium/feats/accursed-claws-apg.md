# Accursed Claws  *Feat 9*  
[changeling](changeling-b1.md "Changeling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: claw unarmed attack

Your claws carry your mother's hag magic. When you critically hit with a claw [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md), the target takes an additional `1d4` [persistent mental damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage).

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 31*
