# Gnome Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*  
[gnome](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/gnome.md "Gnome Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Gnome Weapon Familiarity](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/gnome-weapon-familiarity.md)

Your gnome affinity blends with your class training, granting you great skill with gnome weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [glaive](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/glaive.md), [kukri](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/kukri.md), and all gnome weapons in which you are trained.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 45*
