# Rapid Response  [R](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Reaction") *Feat 2*  
[cleric](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/cleric.md "Cleric Class Trait")  

- **Trigger** An ally is reduced to 0 Hit Points.
- **Activity** Reaction

You work quickly and efficiently in times of danger to deliver needed assistance. You [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) towards the triggering ally. You gain a +10-foot circumstance bonus to your Speed during this movement.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 122*
