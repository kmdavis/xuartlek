# Improved Knockdown  *Feat 10*  
[fighter](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/fighter.md "Fighter Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Knockdown](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/knockdown.md)

You can dash your foe to the ground with a single blow. When you use Knockdown, instead of making a [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) followed by a [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md), you can attempt a single [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md). If you do and your [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) hits, instead of rolling a check for your [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md) attempt, you also apply the critical success effect of a [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md). If you used a two-handed melee weapon for the [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md), you can use the weapon's damage die size instead of the regular die size for the damage from a critical [Trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/trip.md).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 151*
