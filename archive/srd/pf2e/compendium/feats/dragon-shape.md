# Dragon Shape  *Feat 12*  
[druid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/druid.md "Druid Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Soaring Shape](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/soaring-shape.md)

You can take on the form of some of the world's most fearsome creatures. Add the forms listed in [dragon form](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/dragon-form.md) to your [wild shape](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/wild-shape.md) list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another form using wild shape, you gain resistance 5 to your choice of acid, cold, electricity, fire, or poison.

## Dragon Shape leads to...

[True Shapeshifter](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/true-shapeshifter.md), [Reactive Transformation](reactive-transformation-apg.md)
