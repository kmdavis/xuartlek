# Devil Magic  *Feat 9*  
[tiefling](tiefling-b1.md "Tiefling Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Hellspawn](hellspawn-apg.md)

Drawing on the infernal power of your sinister forbears, you mislead your foes with magical deception. You can cast [invisibility](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/invisibility.md) and [misdirection](misdirection.md) each once per day as 2nd-level divine innate spells.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 41*
