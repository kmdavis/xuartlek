# Scout's Warning (Rogue)  [F](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Free Action") *Feat 4*  
[rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  

- **Trigger** You are about to roll a [Perception](skills.md#Perception) or [Survival](skills.md#Survival) check for initiative.
- **Activity** Free Action

You visually or audibly warn your allies of danger, granting them each a +1 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls. Depending on whether you use gestures or call out, this action gains either the [visual](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/visual.md "Visual Effect Trait") or [auditory](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/auditory.md "Auditory Effect Trait") trait, respectively.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 186*
