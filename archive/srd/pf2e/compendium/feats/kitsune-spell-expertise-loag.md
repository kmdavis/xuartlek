# Kitsune Spell Expertise  *Feat 13*  
[kitsune](kitsune-loag.md "Kitsune Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Kitsune Spell Mysteries](kitsune-spell-mysteries-loag.md)

Your tails show your magical mastery. During your daily preparations, choose [confusion](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/confusion.md), [death ward](death-ward.md), or [illusory scene](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/illusory-scene.md). You can [Cast this Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md) as a 5th-level divine innate spell once that day. You become an expert in divine spell DCs and divine spell attack rolls.

*Source: Lost Omens: Ancestry Guide p. 126*
