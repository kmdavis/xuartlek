# Eerie Environs  *Feat 8*  
[druid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/druid.md "Druid Class Trait")  [ranger](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/ranger.md "Ranger Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: trained in [Intimidation](skills.md#Intimidation), trained in [Stealth](skills.md#Stealth)

The natural world can be scary to those not used to it—and you make it scarier still. If you're [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) from a creature, you can attempt to [Demoralize](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/demoralize.md) it without losing your [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) condition—imitating the sounds of strange beasts or causing the foliage to rustle menacingly. When you do so, you don't take a penalty to your check if the target doesn't understand your language.

*Source: Dark Archive p. 65*
