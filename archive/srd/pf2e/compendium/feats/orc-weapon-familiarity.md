# Orc Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*  
[orc](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/orc.md "Orc Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


In combat, you favor the brutal weapons that are traditional for your orc ancestors. You are trained with the [falchion](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/falchion.md) and [greataxe](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/items/greataxe.md). In addition, you gain access to all uncommon orc weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial orc weapons are simple weapons and advanced orc weapons are martial weapons.

## Orc Weapon Familiarity leads to...

[Orc Weapon Carnage](orc-weapon-carnage.md), [Orc Weapon Expertise](orc-weapon-expertise.md)
