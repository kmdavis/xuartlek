# Anticipate Ambush  *Feat 6*  
[exploration](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/exploration.md "Exploration Action & Ability Trait")  [rogue](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rogue.md "Rogue Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: expert in [Stealth](skills.md#Stealth)

Using your extensive knowledge of ambush tactics, you keep watch for any [hidden](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Hidden) creatures or dangerous sites, moving at half your travel speed. If you're Anticipating an Ambush at the start of an encounter, you roll a [Perception](skills.md#Perception) check for initiative, and all enemies rolling [Stealth](skills.md#Stealth) for initiative take a –2 circumstance penalty to their [Stealth](skills.md#Stealth) checks.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 135*
