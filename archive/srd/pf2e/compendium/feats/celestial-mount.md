# Celestial Mount  *Feat 20*  
[champion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/champion.md "Champion Class Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: divine ally (steed), tenets of good

Your steed gains incredible celestial powers granted by your deity. It gains [darkvision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/darkvision.md), its maximum Hit Points increase by 40, and it gains weakness 10 to evil damage.

Additionally, it grows wings appropriate to a servitor of your deity (such as metallic wings for an archon), granting it a fly Speed equal to its Speed. It gains the [celestial](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/celestial.md "Celestial Creature Type Trait") trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor it has become ([archon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archon.md "Archon Creature Trait"), [angel](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/angel.md "Angel Creature Trait"), or [azata](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/azata.md "Azata Creature Trait"), for example).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 115*
