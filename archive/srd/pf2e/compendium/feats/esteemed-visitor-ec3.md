# Esteemed Visitor  *Feat 1*  
[shoony](shoony-ec3.md "Shoony Ancestry & Heritage Trait")  


Your polite manners and natural ability to comfort others make it easy for you to break down cultural barriers and earn strangers' trust. While you are in a settlement, when you roll a critical failure on a [Diplomacy](skills.md#Diplomacy) check to [Gather Information](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/gather-information.md) or [Make an Impression](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/make-an-impression.md), you get a failure instead.

*Source: Extinction Curse #3: Life's Long Shadows p. 72*
