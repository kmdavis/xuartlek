# Student Of The Canon  *Feat 1*  
[general](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/general.md "General Feat Trait")  [skill](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/skill.md "Skill Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: trained in [Religion](skills.md#Religion)

You've researched many faiths enough to recognize notions about them that are unlikely to be true. If you roll a critical failure at a [Religion](skills.md#Religion) check to [Decipher Writing](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/decipher-writing.md) of a religious nature or to [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md) about the tenets of faiths, you get a failure instead. When attempting to [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md) about the tenets of your own faith, if you roll a failure, you get a success instead, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 267*
