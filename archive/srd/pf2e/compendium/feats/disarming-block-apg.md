# Disarming Block  [F](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Free Action") *Feat 4*  
[archetype](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/archetype.md "Archetype Feat Trait")  

- **Prerequisites**: [Bastion Dedication](bastion-dedication-apg.md), trained in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics)
- **Trigger** You [Shield Block](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/shield-block.md) a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) made with a held weapon.
- **Activity** Free Action

You attempt to [Disarm](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/disarm.md) the creature whose attack you blocked of the weapon they attacked you with. You can do so even if you don't have a hand free.

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 159*
