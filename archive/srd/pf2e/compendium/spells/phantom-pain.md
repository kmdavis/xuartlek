# Phantom Pain *Spell 1*   
[illusion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/illusion.md "Illusion School Trait")  [mental](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/mental.md "Mental Effect Trait")  [nonlethal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/nonlethal.md "Nonlethal Weapon Trait")  

- **Traditions** [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: 30 foot
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Illusory pain wracks the target, dealing `2d4` mental damage and `1d4` [persistent mental damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage). The target must attempt a Will save.

> [!success-degree] 
> - **Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
> - **Success** The target takes full initial damage but no [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and the spell ends immediately.
> - **Failure** The target takes full initial and [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and the target is [sickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Sickened). If the target recovers from being [sickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Sickened), the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) ends and the spell ends.
> - **Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is [sickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Sickened).

**Heightened (+ 1)** The damage increases by `2d4` and the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) by `1d4`.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 357*
