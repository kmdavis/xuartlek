# Familiar's Face *Spell 3*   
[divination](divination.md "Divination School Trait")  [scrying](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/scrying.md "Scrying Effect Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait"), [primal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/primal.md "Primal Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: mile
- **Targets**: 1 willing creature that is your animal companion or familiar
- **Duration**: sustained

The target becomes a scrying sensor, allowing you to see through its eyes, smell what it smells, and similarly use its other senses.

If you can cast a spell with the [revelation](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/revelation.md "Revelation Effect Trait") trait that affects your senses, such as [see invisibility](see-invisibility.md), while this spell is active, you gain the benefit of the spell through the target's senses instead of your own. You can also speak through the target, with a voice much like yours, though it takes on some of the timbre and character of the target's growls or squawks. You can use [Command an Animal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/command-an-animal.md) on the target as part of [Sustaining this Spell](sustain-a-spell.md). You don't need line of sight or line of effect to your target when you [Cast this Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md).

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 218*
