# Loose Time's Arrow *Spell 2*   
[transmutation](transmutation.md "Transmutation School Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait"), [primal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/primal.md "Primal Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: 30 foot
- **Targets**: up to 6 creatures
- **Duration**: until the start of your next turn

You pluck the time stream like a bow—pull one string back, release, and watch a creature fly. All affected targets are [quickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Quickened). They can use the extra action only to [Step](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/step.md) or [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md).
