# Darkness *Spell 2*   
[darkness](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/darkness.md "Darkness Effect Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait"), [primal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/primal.md "Primal Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Three-Action") 
- **Range**: 120 foot
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You create a shroud of darkness that prevents light from penetrating or emanating within the area. Light does not enter the area and any non-magical light sources, such as a torch or lantern, do not emanate any light while inside the area, even if their light radius would extend beyond the darkness.

This also suppresses magical light of your [darkness](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/darkness.md) spell's level or lower. Light can't pass through, so creatures in the area can't see outside. From outside, it appears as a globe of pure darkness.

**Heightened (4th)** Even creatures with [darkvision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/darkvision.md) (but not [greater darkvision](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/darkvision.md)) can barely see through the darkness. They treat targets seen through the darkness as [concealed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Concealed).

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 327*
