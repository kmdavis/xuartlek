# Spellwrack *Spell 6*   
[abjuration](abjuration.md "Abjuration School Trait")  [curse](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/curse.md "Curse Effect Trait")  [force](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/force.md "Force Energy & Element Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: 30 foot
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will

You cause any spells cast on the target to spill out their energy in harmful surges. The target must attempt a Will save.

> [!success-degree] 
> - **Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
> - **Success** Whenever the target becomes affected by a spell with a duration, the target takes `2d12` [persistent force damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage). Each time it takes [persistent force damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) from spellwrack, it reduces the remaining duration of spells affecting it by 1 round. Only a successful [Arcana](skills.md#Arcana) check against your spell DC can help the target recover from the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage); the curse and the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) end after 1 minute.
> - **Failure** As success, but the curse and [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) do not end on their own.
> - **Critical Failure** As failure, but the [persistent force damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) is `4d12`.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 371*
