# Vampiric Touch *Spell 3*   
[death](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/death.md "Death Effect Trait")  [necromancy](necromancy.md "Necromancy School Trait")  [negative](negative.md "Negative Energy & Element Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**:  basic Fortitude

Your touch leeches the lifeblood out of a target to empower yourself. You deal `6d6` negative damage to the target. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to half the negative damage the target takes (after applying resistances and the like). You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.

**Heightened (+ 1)** The damage increases by `2d6`.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 380*
