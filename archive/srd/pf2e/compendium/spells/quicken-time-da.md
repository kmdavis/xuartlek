# Quicken Time *Spell 5*   
[transmutation](transmutation.md "Transmutation School Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: 120 foot
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You speed up the time stream in an area, stretching and pulling it until time flows swift and wild. Any creature that begins its turn in the area is [quickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Quickened) and can use the extra action each round to [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md) or [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md). Unfortunately, speeding up time is much more difficult than slowing it down, and the effect is uneven and jittery, making accuracy between times painfully difficult; creatures inside the area are [concealed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Concealed) to those outside it, and vice versa.

*Source: Dark Archive p. 182*
