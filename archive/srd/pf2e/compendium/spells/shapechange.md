# Shapechange *Spell 9*   
[polymorph](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/polymorph.md "Polymorph Effect Trait")  [transmutation](transmutation.md "Transmutation School Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [primal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/primal.md "Primal Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- 
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Harnessing your mastery of transformative magic, you take on a mutable form. You transform yourself into any form you could choose with a polymorph spell in your spell repertoire or that you could prepare of 8th level or lower (including any 8th-level or lower heightened versions of spells you know). You choose the type of creature as you [Cast the Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md) rather than when you prepare it. You can change your form to any other form you could choose with this spell by using a single action, which has the [concentrate](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/concentrate.md "Concentrate Action & Ability Trait") trait. You can [Dismiss](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/dismiss.md) this spell.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 368*
