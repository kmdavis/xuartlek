# Unimpeded Stride *Focus 1*   
[cleric](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/cleric.md "Cleric Class Trait")  [transmutation](transmutation.md "Transmutation School Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  

- **Domains** [freedom](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/setting/domains.md#Freedom)
- **Cast** [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action") 
- 

Nothing can hold you in place. You immediately escape from every [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait") effect that has you [immobilized](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Immobilized) or [grabbed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Grabbed) unless the effect is of a higher level than your [unimpeded stride](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/unimpeded-stride.md) spell.

You then [Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md). During this movement, you ignore difficult terrain and any circumstance or status penalties to your Speed.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 398*
