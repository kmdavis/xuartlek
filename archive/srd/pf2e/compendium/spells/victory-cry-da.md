# Victory Cry *Focus 1*   
[cleric](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/cleric.md "Cleric Class Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  [rare](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rare.md "Rare Rarity Trait")  [sonic](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/sonic.md "Sonic Energy & Element Trait")  

- **Domains** [might (apocryphal)](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/setting/domains.md#Might)
- **Cast** [R](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Reaction") 
- **Trigger**: An ally within range critically succeeds at a melee [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md).
- **Range**: 30 foot
- **Targets**: the triggering ally

Your true might comes from your bonds with your allies, and you glory in their achievements. The weapon used to make the [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) deals an additional `1d6` sonic damage (not doubled due to the critical hit), and the ally can immediately attempt to [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md) the creature struck, even if the ally's hands are full.

Your ally can use its item bonus to the triggering attack roll on the [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check, if any, and it gains a +1 status bonus to the [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check if the triggering [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md) was with a weapon that has the [trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/trip.md "Trip Weapon Trait") trait or an unarmed attack that has the [trip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/trip.md "Trip Weapon Trait") trait.

**Heightened (+ 2)** The sonic damage increases by `1d6`.

*Source: Dark Archive p. 143*
