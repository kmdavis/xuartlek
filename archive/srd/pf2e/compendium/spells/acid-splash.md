# Acid Splash *Cantrip 1*   
[acid](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/acid.md "Acid Energy & Element Trait")  [attack](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/attack.md "Attack Combat Trait")  [cantrip](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/cantrip.md "Cantrip Spell Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [primal](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/primal.md "Primal Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: 30 foot
- **Targets**: 1 creature

You splash a glob of acid that splatters your target and nearby creatures. Make a spell attack. If you hit, you deal `1d6` acid damage plus 1 [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") acid damage. On a critical success, the target also takes 1 [persistent acid damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage).

**Heightened (3rd)** The initial damage increases to `1d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier, and the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) increases to 2.

**Heightened (5th)** The initial damage increases to `2d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier, the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) increases to 3, and the [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage increases to 2.

**Heightened (7th)** The initial damage increases to `3d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier, the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) increases to 4, and the [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage increases to 3.

**Heightened (9th)** The initial damage increases to `4d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier, the [persistent damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) increases to 5, and the [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage increases to 4.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 316*
