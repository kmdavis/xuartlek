# Tongues *Spell 5*   
[divination](divination.md "Divination School Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  

- **Traditions** [arcane](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/arcane.md "Arcane Tradition Trait"), [divine](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/divine.md "Divine Tradition Trait"), [occult](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/occult.md "Occult Tradition Trait")
- **Cast** [>>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action") 
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 hour

The target can understand all words regardless of language and also speak the languages of other creatures. When in a mixed group of creatures, each time the target speaks, it can choose a creature and speak in a language that creature understands, even if the target doesn't know what language that is.

**Heightened (7th)** The duration is 8 hours.

*Source: Core Rulebook p. 378*
