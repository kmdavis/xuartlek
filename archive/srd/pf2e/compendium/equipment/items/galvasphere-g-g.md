# Galvasphere *Item 4*  
[consumable](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/consumable.md "Consumable Item Trait")  [gadget](gadget-g-g.md "Gadget  Trait")  [rare](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/rare.md "Rare Rarity Trait")  

- **Price** 20 gp
- **Bulk** L; **Usage** held in 1 hand
- **Activate** [Interact](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/interact.md)

A galvasphere animates a corpse to motion via electricity, rather than necromancy. When you Activate the galvasphere by inserting it into an adjacent intact Medium or Small humanoid corpse, the corpse animates as a galvaheart zombie for 1 minute. The galvaheart zombie has the statistics of a zombie shambler except that it is a [construct](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/construct.md "Construct Creature Type Trait") instead of an [undead](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/undead.md "Undead Creature Type Trait"), isn't [evil](evil.md "Evil Alignment Trait"), can't be harmed by positive energy, and is the same size as the corpse (Medium or Small). The zombie is your [minion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/minion.md "Minion Creature Trait") and performs the actions you choose when you [Command](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/command-an-animal.md) it. If you don't [Command](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/command-an-animal.md) it, it takes no action, twitching in place as the electricity that animates it slowly expends itself.
