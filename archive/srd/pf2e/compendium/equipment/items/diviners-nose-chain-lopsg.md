# Diviner's Nose Chain *Item 9*  
[divination](divination.md "Divination School Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  

- **Price** 650 gp
- **Bulk** –; **Usage** worn

The diviner's nose chain is worn by attaching piercings to the ear and nose, creating a connection between the senses of hearing and smell. The diviner's nose chain grants you [scent](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/abilities/scent.md) out to 30 feet as an imprecise sense, and a +1 item bonus to checks to [Seek](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/seek.md) or [Sense Motive](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/sense-motive.md).
