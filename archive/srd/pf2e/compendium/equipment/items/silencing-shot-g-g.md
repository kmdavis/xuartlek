# Silencing Shot *Item 11*  
[consumable](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/consumable.md "Consumable Item Trait")  [illusion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/illusion.md "Illusion School Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  

- **Price** 300 gp
- **Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of silence at 4th level.
- **Ammunition** any
- **Activate** envision

This shimmering, golden-hued ammunition never makes any sound. A creature hit by a silencing shot is subject to the effects of a 4th-level [silence](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/silence.md) spell (DC 25).
