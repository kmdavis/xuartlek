# Potion of Undetectability *Item 18*  
[consumable](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/consumable.md "Consumable Item Trait")  [illusion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/illusion.md "Illusion School Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  [potion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/potion.md "Potion Item Trait")  

- **Price** 4400 gp
- **Bulk** L; **Usage** held in 1 hand
- **Activate** [Interact](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/interact.md)

Drinking this dull-black liquid makes you undetectable to divinations. This grants the same effects as mind blank, but without the bonus against [mental](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/mental.md "Mental Effect Trait") effects. You also gain the effects of a 4th-level [invisibility](archive/srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/invisibility.md) spell, which protects against [see invisibility](see-invisibility.md) spells of 8th level and lower and has a DC of 36 against true seeing. The potion's effects last for 10 minutes.
