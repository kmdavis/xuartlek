# Ring Of Discretion *Item 1*  
[illusion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/illusion.md "Illusion School Trait")  [invested](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/invested.md "Invested Item Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  [uncommon](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait")  [visual](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/visual.md "Visual Effect Trait")  

- **Price** 15 gp
- **Bulk** –; **Usage** worn ring

This ring magically conceals any armor and sheathed weapons you're wearing by either turning them [invisible](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) or creating the illusion of ordinary clothes. The ring doesn't change your appearance beyond concealing weapons and armor. As soon as you wield a weapon, the weapon becomes obvious to onlookers and is no longer affected by the ring until you sheathe the weapon for at least 1 minute. A creature can 0 by Seeking or any time it hits you with an attack.
