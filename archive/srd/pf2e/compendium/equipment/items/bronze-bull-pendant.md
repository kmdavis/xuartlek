# Bronze Bull Pendant *Item 2*  
[consumable](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/consumable.md "Consumable Item Trait")  [evocation](evocation.md "Evocation School Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  [talisman](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/talisman.md "Talisman Item Trait")  

- **Price** 7 gp
- **Bulk** —; **Usage** affixed to armor
- **Activate** envision; **Trigger** You attempt an [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check to [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md), but you haven't rolled yet; **Requirements** You are trained in [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics).

This pendant is forged from grainy steel and depicts a snorting bull's face. The pendant must be attached to the chest area or on a shoulder guard. When you activate the pendant, you gain a +2 status bonus to the [Athletics](skills.md#Athletics) check to [Shove](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/shove.md), and if you roll a critical failure on the check, you get a failure instead.
