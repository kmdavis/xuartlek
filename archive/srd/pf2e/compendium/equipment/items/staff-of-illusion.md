# Staff of Illusion *Item 6+*  
[illusion](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/illusion.md "Illusion School Trait")  [magical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/magical.md "Magical Item Trait")  [staff](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/staff.md "Staff Item Trait")  

- **Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
- **Bulk** 1; **Usage** held in 1 hand

This ornately designed metal staff shines with precious inlays of gold. When you [Cast a Spell](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md) from the staff, the illusory image of something you desire flashes across its surface.

While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to identify illusion magic.

```ad-embed-ability
title: **Activate**  [Cast a Spell](rules/actions/cast-a-spell.md)


You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.
```
