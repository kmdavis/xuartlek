# Blight Bomb *Item 1+*  
[alchemical](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/alchemical.md "Alchemical Item Trait")  [bomb](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/bomb.md "Bomb Item Trait")  [consumable](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/consumable.md "Consumable Item Trait")  [poison](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/poison.md "Poison Effect Trait")  [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait")  

- **Bulk** L; **Usage** held in 1 hand
- **Activate** [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md)

Blight bombs contain volatile toxic chemicals that rot flesh. A blight bomb deals the listed poison damage, [persistent poison damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

---

### Variants

#### lesser *Item 1*

- **Price**: 3 gp

The bomb deals `1d6` poison damage, `1d4` [persistent poison damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and 1 poison [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage.

#### moderate *Item 3*

- **Price**: 10 gp

You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals `2d6` poison damage, `2d4` [persistent poison damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and 2 poison [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage.

#### greater *Item 11*

- **Price**: 250 gp

You gain a +2 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals `3d6` poison damage, `3d4` [persistent poison damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and 3 poison [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage.

#### major *Item 17*

- **Price**: 2500 gp

You gain a +3 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals `4d6` poison damage, `4d4` [persistent poison damage](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage), and 4 poison [splash](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/splash.md "Splash Weapon Trait") damage.
