# Expeditious Inspection [F](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Free Action")
[investigator](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/traits/investigator-apg.md "Investigator Class Trait")  
*Source: Advanced Player's Guide p. 57*  

**Class** investigator; **Subclass** Empiricism
- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Activity**: Free Action

You observe and assess your surroundings with great speed. You [Recall Knowledge](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/recall-knowledge.md), [Seek](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/seek.md), or [Sense Motive](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/sense-motive.md).
