# Throw Rock [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action")

- **Activate**: Single Action

The monster picks up a rock within reach or retrieves a stowed rock and throws it, making a ranged [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md).
