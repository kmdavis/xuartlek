# Warpwave

Many proteans can inflict disorienting alterations in time and space called warpwaves. When a creature fails its saving throw and is affected by a warpwave, roll `1d8` and consult the table below for the specific effect on that creature. Unless indicated otherwise, a warpwave effect lasts for `1d4` rounds, and a new warpwave effect negates any previous warpwave effect already affecting a creature.

| `D8` | Warpwave Effect |
|------|-----------------|
| 1 | [Clumsy](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Clumsy) ([clumsy](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Clumsy) on a critical failure) |
| 2 | [Confused](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Confused) and gains `4d6` temporary Hit Points |
| 3 | [Dazzled](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Dazzled) (permanent on a critical failure) |
| 4 | [Enfeebled](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Enfeebled) (3 on a critical failure) |
| 5 | [Immobilized](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Immobilized) by filaments of energy |
| 6 | [Quickened](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Quickened) ([Stride](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/stride.md), [Strike](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/strike.md), or [Step](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/actions/step.md) only) |
| 7 | [Slowed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Slowed) |
| 8 | [Stupefied](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Stupefied) (3 on a critical failure) |
