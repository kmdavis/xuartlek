# Constrict [>](chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action")

- **Activate**: Single Action

The monster deals the listed amount of damage to any number of creatures [grabbed](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Grabbed) or [restrained](archive/srd/pf2e/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by it. Each of those creatures can attempt a basic Fortitude save with the listed DC.
