## The setting

Xuartlek is a city built on the shell of a living demigod who walks from world to world, staying thirty years at each. The empire is twenty-three districts scattered across twelve worlds, joined by portals that only exist because he walked there first.

## Timekeeping

The in-world date is pinned and advances when play advances, not with the calendar on your wall. Each world keeps its own reckoning, and the [[setting/timeline/Xuartlek Imperial Calendar|Xuartlek Imperial Calendar]] is the one the empire runs on: thirteen months of twenty-eight days, no leap days, because the capital does not orbit anything.
