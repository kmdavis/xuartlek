One paragraph per character, for a GM who has not run this table before. These
were written against the party as it stood at the time and describe playstyle
and history rather than current statistics; the sheets in
[[Party Composition]] are authoritative for numbers.


## [[Belegost]]

Belegost is the party's front-line brawler--an orc farmhand turned monk who found his calling on the seas. His Mountain Stance makes him incredibly hard to hit, and Stunning Blows can lock down dangerous enemies. He delivered the critical hit that gave Harlan "Scar" a collapsed lung.

## [[Flick]]

Flick is the party's face and flashiest combatant--a goblin swashbuckler who lives for the dramatic moment. His Braggart style means he gains panache by intimidating foes, and his Explosive Dogslicer is as unpredictable as he is. He's the one who provoked the fight with Korren Red Sash by stepping on the playing card.


## [[Gripp]]

Gripp is the party's support specialist--a gnome barkeep turned chirurgeon alchemist. His bombs control the battlefield while his elixirs keep the party fighting. The Smoke Bomb additive is particularly useful for tactical retreats or ambushes. His animal accomplice adds extra utility.


## [[Gteek]]

Gteek is a tripkee (grippli variant) cleric of Shumunue, a wood-domain deity. He's the party's primary healer with Communal Healing spreading the love to nearby allies. His Sentinel Dedication will let him wear heavier armor as he levels. The only party member without Pirate Dedication--he's the moral center, perhaps.

## [[John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt]]

John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt is a rock dwarf gunslinger on the Way of the Spellshot, and a detective by background. Spellshot Dedication loads a spell into his clan pistol, so Electric Arc arrives with a bullet behind it. Fake Out and Thoughtful Reload make him a setup artist rather than a finisher, and at Cha -1 he is not the one to send to negotiate.

## [[Lorde Morthonk]]

Lorde Morthonk is an awakened domestic goose and a necromancer, seven months awake and not once sorry about anything. It fights as a puppeteer, from behind a small flock of translucent, honking, extremely angry ghost geese: its thralls, which it raises and spends to fuel its spells. It is the party's occult caster and its best Arcana and Occultism, and it keeps the crew on their feet with Battle Medicine and Soothe out of spite rather than kindness, because they are its property and it maintains its property. Everything within reach is also its property, and giving anything back is against its principles, so do not hand shit anything you want to see again. It joins at level 2.


## Retired characters

Kept for continuity. These two appear in earlier play but are not on the current
roster, so their sheets are out of date and their statistics should not be used.

```spoiler Espera
[[Espera]] was a catfolk bounty hunter turned ranger, which was ironic given the
bounties on the party's own heads. Her Outwit edge made her the group's tracker,
and she was carrying Doomed 1 when she left the roster.
```

```spoiler Vaelendil
[[Vaelendil]] was a seer elf wizard with a criminal past, and the party's arcane
artillery. Battle Magic and a Spell Substitution thesis made him flexible in a
fight, while Mind Smith and Underworld Lore pointed at psionics and older
contacts. He was the group's only full caster.
```
