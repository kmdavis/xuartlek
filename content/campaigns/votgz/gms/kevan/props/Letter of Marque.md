*Issued by the Principality of Zolnuth*

---

By the authority vested in me, **Governor Alandrin Thesalor**, Warden of the Eastern Tides, Keeper of the Seventeen Sacred Maritime Scrolls, and Designated Signatory of Naval Writs (Third Class), acting on behalf of His Serene Highness **Prince Calanelmë of House Tsolunethiel**, Sovereign of the Principality of Zolnuth and Protector of the Eastern Seas:

This **Letter of Marque and Reprisal** is hereby granted to the vessel known as **The Grim Zephyr** and her crew, authorizing them to act as lawful privateers in service to the Principality.

## Approved Targets

The bearers of this letter are authorized to engage, board, and seize vessels belonging to:

- **The Kingdom of Arinia** - Enemy of Zolnuth in the Thousand-Year War
- **The Grand Duchy of Getzciria**
- **The Grand Duchy of Kaiseond**
- **Pirates** who have attacked Zolnuthian shipping or exceeded the bounds of their own commissions

## Prohibited Actions

The following are **strictly forbidden** and will result in immediate revocation of this letter and prosecution under maritime law:

- Attacks on vessels of **The Republic of Shuamë**
- Attacks on vessels of **Viseleyn**
- Attacks on Zolnuthian vessels or allies

Violation of these prohibitions transforms this letter of marque into a death warrant.

## Treasury Obligations

One-third (1/3) of all prizes, cargo, and valuables seized under this commission shall be remitted to the Principality's Treasury within thirty days of making port.

## Duration

This letter remains valid for one year from the date of issue, renewable at the Governor's discretion.

---

*Signed and sealed this 14th day of Leptisiel, 1751*

*In the month of Melqartmar*

**Governor Alandrin Thesalor**
*Sielmoro, Principality of Zolnuth*
