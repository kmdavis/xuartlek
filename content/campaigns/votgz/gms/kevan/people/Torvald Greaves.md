**Full Name**: Torvald Greaves
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Proprietor of The Mariner's Rest (neutral)

## Description

Human tavernkeeper. Former sailor, barrel-chested, booming laugh. Runs the main tavern with competence and good humor. Hears everything, shares selectively.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: The Mariner's Rest (always)
- **Concern**: The shootout happened right outside his establishment. Bad for business--or good, depending on how you look at it. Everyone wants to see where it happened.
- **Position**: Neutral. He serves drinks to whoever pays.

## What Torvald Knows

- Everything that passes through his common room
- Local gossip, ship news, who's in town
- The party (they've been drinking here, presumably)
- That Donal Vess (the surviving ruffian) has been drinking heavily since the incident

## What Torvald Could Offer

- **Orientation**: Local knowledge for newcomers
- **Job Board**: Posts work opportunities near the entrance
- **Rumors**: Hears things, might share for the right price or relationship
- **Neutrality**: His tavern is safe ground for deals and meetings

## Relationships

- **Rask Embertooth**: Regular customer. Friendly.
- **Donal Vess**: Currently drinking his trauma away at the bar.
- **The Party**: New faces, but they've proven themselves capable.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Jovial but observant
- Tells stories about his sailing days
- Protective of his establishment's reputation
- Won't take sides in disputes--bad for business
