**Full Name**: Caelvorn Ashward
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Principality of Zolnuth (official law enforcement)

## Description

Elven constable captain who represents Zolnuthian authority in Thelonésë. Professional, competent, and experienced. Has served in the outer islands for decades.

## Current Situation

- **Immediate Concern**: The ambush at the Mariner's Rest resulted in two deaths and multiple injuries
- **Prisoners**: Harlan "Scar" Tomlin (bounty hunter) in custody, awaiting the magistrate
- **Restricted**: Flick (party swashbuckler) forbidden from leaving town pending review
- **Assessment**: The party acted in self-defense, but the deaths require judicial review

## What Ashward Knows

- The ambush was bounty hunter-related (Scar admitted as much)
- The bounty came from "the Commodore" (Ashward knows of Thorne by reputation)
- The party appears to be privateers, not pirates (subtle distinction, but legally important)
- One of the dead was killed by Scar's own arrow (complicates the case)
- Donal Vess (surviving ruffian) is cooperating with the investigation

## Ashward's Position

He's not hostile to the party--they defended themselves, and the bounty was from an outlaw (Thorne). But he's bound by procedure. Deaths require the magistrate's review.

**His Goals**:
1. Maintain order until Magistrate Thornwood arrives
2. Keep Scar secure (unaware of the silencing threat)
3. Resolve the case efficiently
4. Avoid political complications with the Commodore

## Relationships

- **Magistrate Thornwood**: Professional respect. Follows her rulings without question.
- **The Party**: Neutral-to-cautiously-positive. They handled themselves well but are still under review.
- **Harlan Tomlin**: Just another bounty hunter who got caught. No special treatment.
- **Yannick Corwen**: Knows The Spring Tide is a gambling house. Looks the other way as long as things stay civil.
