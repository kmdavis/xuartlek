**Full Name**: Orlan Thatch
**Status**: Active, cover intact
**Affiliation**: Commodore Marcellus "Three Sheets" Thorne (informant)

## Description

Human general merchant at the edge of the bazaar. Friendly, helpful, seemingly harmless. Been in Thelonésë for years, knows everyone, always has time for a chat. The perfect shopkeeper.

## The Truth

Orlan is Commodore Thorne's informant in Thelonésë. He reports on:
- Ship movements and valuable cargoes
- Notable arrivals and departures
- Persons of interest to the Commodore
- Local gossip that might be useful

He's paid well for this service and has been doing it for years without being caught. He genuinely enjoys his cover identity--being the friendly merchant is more pleasant than being a pirate.

## Current Situation

**What Orlan Knows About the Ambush**: Like everyone in town, Orlan heard about the shootout at the Mariner's Rest. He's pieced together that:
- The targets match descriptions from Thorne's bounty
- A bounty hunter named Scar tried to collect
- Scar failed and is now in custody
- The party is stuck in town waiting for the judge

**What Orlan Has Done**:
- Sent a coded message to Thorne's network (via outbound ship) reporting the party's presence
- Included details about the failed bounty attempt and Scar's arrest

**What Orlan Will Do Next**:
- Await instructions from Thorne
- Continue gathering information about the party
- If ordered, arrange to silence Scar

## The Silencing Plot

Harlan "Scar" Tomlin has done jobs for Thorne in the past. Independent work, not as an employee--but during one of those jobs, Harlan saw or heard something he shouldn't have. That photographic memory of his recorded it, even if Harlan doesn't realize the significance.

With Harlan now in custody, talking to constables, and potentially testifying before a magistrate... Thorne can't take the chance that Harlan will mention the wrong detail to the wrong person.

**The Order**: Orlan will receive instructions to silence Harlan--not because he failed a bounty, but because of what he might remember from *previous* jobs.

**Methods Orlan Might Use**:
- Bribe a guard to allow access, then poison Harlan's food
- Hire someone expendable to do the job
- Provide a "concerned citizen" cover to visit Harlan in jail

**Timeline**: Instructions will arrive 4-6 days after news of Harlan's capture reaches Thorne. Attempt on Harlan's life 1-2 days after that.

**Complication**: Orlan doesn't know *what* secret Harlan possesses. He just knows Thorne wants the man dead. This could make Orlan nervous--if Thorne is willing to kill over secrets, what does that mean for Orlan's own position?

## What Orlan Knows

- He works for Commodore Thorne as an informant
- Thorne has bounties out on the party (he's seen the posters)
- How to contact Thorne's network (dead drops, coded messages, trusted ship captains)
- The identities of a few other Thorne contacts in nearby ports

## What Orlan Doesn't Know

- Why exactly Thorne wants the party
- The details of Thorne's larger operations
- Anything about the extortion ring or the love letter (completely separate)

## Behavior Around the Party

Orlan will be:
- Friendly and helpful (as always)
- Interested in their plans and whereabouts
- Subtly probing for information
- Never suspicious unless someone's specifically watching for it

**Red Flags** (if players are perceptive):
- He asks a lot of questions for a shopkeeper
- He remembers details they mentioned casually
- He seems very interested in where they're staying, how long they'll be in town
- He's *too* friendly, even for a merchant
