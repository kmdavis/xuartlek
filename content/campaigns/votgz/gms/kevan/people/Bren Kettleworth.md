## Description

Halfling ship chandler. Practical, business-minded, unflappable. Sells rope, canvas, provisions, tar--everything a ship needs.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Kettleworth Chandlery near the harbor
- **Business**: Normal operations
- **Knowledge**: Knows the party's ship needs supplies (all ships do)

## What Bren Knows

- Ship supply needs and quality
- Which captains pay their debts
- Trade routes and their profitability
- Maritime commerce patterns

## What Bren Could Offer

- **Supplies**: Everything a ship needs
- **Credit**: For crews she trusts (not newcomers)
- **Advice**: On routes, supplies, maritime matters

## Relationships

- **Mira Saltwell**: Regular business dealings
- **Ship crews**: Knows most of the regulars

## Roleplaying Notes

- Businesslike, efficient
- Family has run chandleries for generations
- Proud of her expertise
- Cash upfront for strangers, credit for trusted customers
