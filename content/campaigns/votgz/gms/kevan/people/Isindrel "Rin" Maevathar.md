**Full Name**: Isindrel Maevathar
**Nickname**: "Rin" (to friends and card partners)
**Status**: Active, in town, unaware of the extortion plot
**Affiliation**: Daughter of Lord Aldren Maevathar

## Description

Young elven noblewoman who finds her family's timber estate tedious. Escapes to Thelonésë frequently, where she spends her time at The Mariner's Rest and The Spring Tide. Charming, reckless, burning through her allowance at the card tables. Goes by "Rin" in casual settings--it sounds less stuffy than her full name.

## The Connection

Her father, Lord Aldren Maevathar, wrote the love letter to Lady Jyl--the letter now in the party's possession. Rin has no idea:
- That her father wrote such a letter
- That the letter was stolen
- That an extortion ring planned to use it against her family
- That the courier carrying it was killed in the ambush

She thinks her parents' marriage is merely *cold*, not compromised. She'd be devastated, furious, or pragmatic if she learned the truth--hard to predict which.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: The Maevathar townhouse in Thelonésë (permanent accommodations maintained by the family for visits to port)
- **Activities**: Card games at The Spring Tide, drinking at The Mariner's Rest
- **Awareness**: Heard about the shootout--it's all anyone's talking about--but doesn't connect it to anything relevant to her

## Noble Townhouses

Each of the three noble families maintains a townhouse in Thelonésë:
- **Maevathar House**: The largest, befitting the wealthiest family. Near the government complex.
- **Olythos House**: Modest but well-maintained. Lady Seraphine visits occasionally.
- **Thassaril House**: Rarely occupied--Lord Caelen prefers the capital.

These aren't mere lodgings--they're status symbols, permanent presences that remind the port who owns the land that feeds its trade.

## What Rin Knows

- Local gossip about the nobility, merchants, and workers
- The political tensions between the lords and the guild
- Her father's personality (strict, focused on business, boring)
- That her mother is unhappy but proper about it
- The regulars at The Spring Tide (including Yannick)

## What Rin Doesn't Know

- Her father's infidelity
- The extortion plot
- That the party might be holding blackmail material against her family

## If She Learns the Truth

**Option A: Devastation**
She genuinely believed her father was boring but faithful. Learning otherwise shatters her worldview.

**Option B: Fury**
Angry at her father for the betrayal, angry at whoever stole the letter, angry at everyone.

**Option C: Pragmatism**
Uses the information as leverage--against her father, against the extortionists, or as a bargaining chip for something she wants.

**Option D: Denial**
Refuses to believe it, attacks the messenger.

## Relationships

- **Lord Maevathar (father)**: Thinks he's boring and strict. Resents his control over her allowance.
- **Lady Maevathar (mother)**: Distant relationship. Rin blames her for the cold household.
- **Yannick Corwen**: Friendly acquaintance at The Spring Tide. Doesn't know he's planning to extort her father.
- **Rask Embertooth**: Knows of him (the workers' guild leader). Probably condescending about "labor issues."

## Roleplaying Notes

- Bored, privileged, seeking excitement
- Uses "Rin" to seem more approachable--"Isindrel" is for formal occasions
- Friendly to interesting newcomers
- Gossipy--will share information freely if she likes someone
- Competitive at cards (and a sore loser)
- Drinks more than she should
