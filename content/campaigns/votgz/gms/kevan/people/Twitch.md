## Description

Small, wiry, and brimming with violent energy, Twitch is a goblin deckhand who lives for explosions, fire, and mayhem. His yellow eyes are perpetually wide with manic glee, and his grin reveals rows of sharp teeth. He wears a battered tricorn hat over his pointed ears and ragged pirate clothing that's clearly seen better days--and probably several fires.

At any given moment, Twitch is either holding a lit bomb, about to hold a lit bomb, or recovering from having just thrown a lit bomb. His belt bristles with explosive devices. Whether hurling incendiaries from a sling or scampering across the ship's rigging, he's tireless, manic, and perpetually dangerous.

**Don't touch anything he's touched** if you value your fingers. Always have an escape route in case something goes boom.

**Ancestry**: Goblin (Male)
**Role**: Deckhand, Demolitions Expert, Chaos Agent

## Background

How Twitch ended up on Stormeye's crew is anyone's guess--probably involving fire, a daring escape, and at least one explosion. He's a goblin, so his life story likely involves chaos at every turn. What matters is that he's surprisingly loyal to those he considers "his crew," even if his methods of showing loyalty often involve property damage.

When Stormeye transformed and the crew scattered, Twitch's instinct for survival kicked in. He made it to safety with the others, probably cackling the whole way.

## Current Status

Now serves aboard the party's ship as a deckhand and unofficial demolitions expert. Small but dangerous, Twitch gravitates to wherever chaos is happening. The party has another goblin (Flick), which either makes things better or much, much worse.

## GM Notes

Twitch is comic relief with teeth. He's genuinely dangerous and his Dread Striker ability makes him tactically useful when combined with the party's Demoralize-heavy composition (Flick is also built around intimidation).

Consider:
- Twitch and Flick are both goblins--do they get along, or is there rivalry?
- His love of explosions could be a problem in certain situations
- He's low Wisdom and Will--vulnerable to mental effects
- Could his "collections" include something dangerous or plot-relevant?
