**Full Name**: Grumm "Blackpot"
**Status**: Active (party crew)
**Affiliation**: Party's ship (formerly Captain Stormeye's crew)

## Description

Stubborn, loud, and enduringly gruff, Grumm Blackpot is a stout dwarf who prefers tending to cauldrons and mess pots over conversation. He has a magnificent braided beard adorned with metal rings, a perpetually stained apron, and a headscarf tied over his head. His most distinctive feature--and his weapon of choice--is an iron cooking pan that he wields with surprising lethality.

The "Blackpot" moniker comes from his signature cooking vessel, which has seen as much combat as it has stew. Grumm snarls and grumbles, appearing heartless, but actually values his crewmates and cares about feeding them more than he'd ever admit. His affection comes through in generous portions and aggressive insistence that everyone eat properly.

Loud, undiplomatic, and boisterous, he tends to punctuate even heated battles with cooking puns and rough laughter.

## Background

Grumm has been a ship's cook for decades, feeding crews across dozens of vessels. He signed on with Stormeye because he paid well and didn't complain about Grumm's cooking. When the expedition went wrong, he fought his way out alongside the others--his cooking pan proving just as effective against spectres as it was against stubborn vegetables.

He doesn't talk much about those days trapped in the ruins, but he made sure everyone ate. Even when supplies ran low. Even when it seemed hopeless. That's what cooks do.

## Current Status

Now serves aboard the party's ship as cook. The party already has Gripp (an alchemist with Alcohol Lore), which creates an interesting dynamic in the galley. Grumm is territorial about his kitchen but respects competence.

## GM Notes

Grumm is the most mechanically capable combatant among the survivors--Level 2, solid stats, and some nasty abilities. His Hot Skillet Riposte makes him dangerous to ignore, and Tenderizer means his crits are particularly memorable.

Consider:
- Grumm and Gripp both work with food/drink--rivalry or collaboration?
- His gruff exterior hides genuine care--could be exploited by enemies or recognized by perceptive PCs
- He's a dwarf cook with a combat-capable pan--endless opportunity for cooking puns
- What does he think of Belegost (the party's orc monk)? Dwarves and orcs have history.
