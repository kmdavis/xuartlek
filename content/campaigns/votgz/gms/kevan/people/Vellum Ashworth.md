**Affiliation**: Independent harbor surgeon (neutral)

Athamaru (triton) harbor doctor. Thin, precise, perpetually tired. Patches up sailors, treats fevers, doesn't ask questions.

- **Location**: Harbor Surgery near the docks
- **Recent Work**: Treated Harlan Tomlin's injuries (collapsed lung, serious condition)
- **Professional Opinion**: Harlan will survive but needs weeks to fully recover

## What Vellum Knows

- Medical details about his patients (discreet unless motivated)
- That Harlan Tomlin is in bad shape
- General health of the port community
- Who gets injured and how (useful patterns)

## What Vellum Could Offer

- **Healing**: Medical services, no paperwork required
- **Information**: What he's seen, who he's treated (carefully, discreetly)
- **Assessment**: Medical evaluation of injuries or bodies

## Relationships
