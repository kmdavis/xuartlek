**Full Name**: Korva Steelhand
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Independent weaponsmith (neutral)

## Description

Orc weaponsmith. Quiet, competent, proudly professional. One of the few orcs in town, earned her place through skill rather than words.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Steelhand Forge in the bazaar
- **Awareness**: Heard about the ambush. Respects anyone who can handle themselves in a fight.
- **Business**: Normal operations

## What Korva Knows

- Quality of weapons and armor
- Who buys what (useful for gauging threat levels)
- That Harlan Scar has purchased from her before (recognizes him)
- Practical combat assessment of equipment

## What Korva Could Offer

- **Weapons/Armor**: Quality goods, fair prices
- **Repairs**: Fix damaged equipment
- **Assessment**: Judge the quality of found items
- **Respect**: Discounts for proven fighters

## Relationships

- **The Party**: Potential customers. If they handled themselves well in the fight, she'd respect that.
- **Harlan Tomlin**: Sold him arrows once. Remembers his face.

- Lets her work speak for her
- Nods more than she talks
