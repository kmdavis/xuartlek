**Full Name**: Marsh (everyone calls him Old Marsh)
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Camp Six worker (guild member), senior logger

## Description

Human male, late fifties. Skin like tanned leather. Missing two fingers on his left hand (sawmill accident, years ago). Chews tobacco constantly. The oldest worker at Camp Six and the most outspoken. Fenwick can't shut him up and has stopped trying.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Camp Six
- **Mood**: Bitter and angry. He knows something is wrong and he knows the overseer is lying.

## What Old Marsh Knows

- Harget's tools are still in his bunk. Oiled, cared for, worth more than three weeks' wages. "What man deserts and leaves his tools?"
- Myla had family in Millbrook. She talked about her kids every day. She wouldn't walk away from wages owed to her children.
- Doss was scared but not a quitter. The kid wanted to prove himself.
- The cutting schedule makes no sense -- they're pushing into old-growth that's harder to cut and more dangerous to fell. "There's a reason nobody touched those trees before us."

## What Old Marsh Will Say

Doesn't need much coaxing. If the party shows any sympathy:
- *"Harget's tools are still in his bunk. His good axe. His father's hatchet. You tell me a man walks away from his father's hatchet."*
- *"Fenwick says deserted. I say horseshit. Pardon my language. No I don't."*
- *"The lord wants his ironwood. Fenwick wants to keep his position. And three people are gone. You do the arithmetic."*
- On the jungle: *"We pushed too deep. The old-growth -- that forest isn't ours. Never was. Something in there disagrees with us being here."*

## Personality

- Blunt to the point of rudeness
- Protective of younger workers
- Deeply angry at Fenwick but channels it into sarcasm rather than confrontation
- Hoards candles -- his bunk has four burning at all times

## Roleplaying Notes

- Spits tobacco before making important points
- Calls everyone "kid" regardless of age
- Voice: gravelly, unhurried, like he's got all day to tell you how wrong everyone is
- The four candles in his bunk: if asked why, he just shrugs: "Helps me sleep."

## Dramatic Purpose

Old Marsh is the **physical evidence** clue. He points the party to Harget's tools (proving the desertion story is false) and voices the connection between the old-growth logging and the disappearances. Also a secondary light clue (the candles).
