**Full Name**: Marissa Ironhand
**Status**: Active (party crew)
**Affiliation**: Party's ship (formerly Captain Stormeye's crew)

## Description

A stern and no-nonsense human woman, Marissa is tough, disciplined, and fiercely loyal. Her most distinctive feature is her iron prosthetic hand--a heavy gauntlet that earned her the "Ironhand" moniker and which she uses to keep order both on and off the ship. She wears practical pirate garb: a brown coat, tricorn hat, and a red sash at her waist. Her expression rarely softens; she's seen too much to waste time on pleasantries.

Marissa never backs down from a fight and always backs her crew. She's the type to never use one word when silence will do--but when she does speak, people listen.

**Ancestry**: Human (Female)

## Background

Marissa served as first mate under Captain Stormeye for years. When the captain led his ill-fated expedition into the jungle ruins, she was among those who followed. She survived his transformation and the massacre that followed by leading the retreat--but couldn't save most of the crew.

Trapped between a sealed door and their undead captain, Marissa kept the four other survivors alive through sheer force of will until rescue arrived.

## Current Status

Now serves aboard the party's ship, having pledged her loyalty to those who saved her life. She and the other survivors owe the party a significant favor--a debt they take seriously. During the confrontation with Korren Red Sash, she and the other survivors waited outside the tavern, ready to intervene if needed.

## GM Notes

Marissa is the natural leader among the survivors. If the party needs someone to manage crew affairs or take charge in their absence, she's the obvious choice. Her discipline and experience make her invaluable.

Consider:
- How does she feel about Stormeye's fate? Relief? Grief? Both?
- Will she eventually want her own command again?
- Her loyalty is to the party now, but she has her own sense of honor
