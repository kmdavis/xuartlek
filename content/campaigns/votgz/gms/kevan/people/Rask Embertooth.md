**Full Name**: Rask Embertooth
**Status**: Active, in town for negotiations
**Affiliation**: Head of the Timber Workers' Guild

## Description

Catfolk guild representative who speaks for the workers in the logging camps and agricultural estates. Broad-shouldered, scarred from years of logging work, patient in the way of someone who's learned that losing his temper loses negotiations.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Staying at The Mariner's Rest while conducting business
- **Purpose**: Negotiating this season's labor terms with the lords and port merchants
- **Mood**: Cautiously optimistic--terms are improving, slowly

## What Rask Represents

The Timber Workers' Guild isn't a union in the modern sense--it's a collective bargaining organization for the inland workers. They negotiate:
- Wages for logging and agricultural work
- Working conditions in the camps
- Prices for goods sold through the port
- Safety standards (such as they are)

The lords tolerate the guild because the alternative is worse--workers who won't work, timber that doesn't get cut, goods that don't reach port.

## What Rask Knows

- The inland communities (villages, camps, work sites)
- The three lords and their management styles
- Historical tensions between workers and nobility
- Which merchants offer fair prices and which don't
- Rumors from the interior (disappearances, strange sightings, old ruins)

## What Rask Could Offer the Party

- **Information**: About the island's interior, the lords, the workers
- **Contacts**: Among the inland population
- **Work**: Problems that need solving (escort missions, investigations, protection)
- **Goodwill**: Help the workers, earn the trust of hundreds of people

## Relationships

- **Lord Maevathar**: Adversary at the bargaining table. Respects his business acumen, resents his indifference to worker safety.
- **Lady Seraphine Olythos**: The most reasonable of the lords. Treats workers fairly.
- **Lord Caelen Thassaril**: Rarely deals with him directly--his stewards handle negotiations.
- **Torvald Greaves**: Friendly acquaintance. Shares drinks at the Mariner's Rest.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Speaks carefully, measures his words
- Protective of "his people"--the workers
- Slow to trust outsiders, but loyal once trust is earned
- Has a dry sense of humor
- Scratches behind his ear when thinking (catfolk habit)

## Potential Plot Hooks

- Workers have gone missing in a remote logging camp
- Something dangerous has been spotted in the deep forest
- A merchant is shorting workers on promised pay
- Tensions with one of the lords are reaching a breaking point
