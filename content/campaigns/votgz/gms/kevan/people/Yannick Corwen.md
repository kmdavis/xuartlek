**Full Name**: Yannick Corwen
**Alias**: "Y" (signature on handler communications)
**Status**: Active, increasingly concerned about a missing delivery
**Affiliation**: Local extortion ring (NOT connected to Commodore Thorne)

## Description

Half-elf proprietor of The Spring Tide, Thelonésë's "private social club." Charming, well-dressed, unfailingly polite. Runs a gambling house under the veneer of respectability--and uses it as a front for less savory business.

## The Truth

Yannick is "Y"--the handler for a local extortion ring. Their operation:

1. **Acquire compromising information** on merchants passing through (letters, documents, witnessed indiscretions)
2. **Use couriers** to transport sensitive materials
3. **Apply pressure** through intermediaries
4. **Collect payment** through The Spring Tide (gambling debts provide cover)

**Important**: The ring normally targets **transient merchants**, not local nobles. There aren't many nobles to extort, and nobles have the means to exact retribution. When the ring acquired Lord Maevathar's love letter, it was an opportunistic grab--too tempting to pass up, even though it breaks their usual rules.

This is the first time they've targeted someone this powerful. They're in over their heads and don't fully realize it yet.

## Current Situation

**The Problem**: Courier "D" was supposed to deliver a package (Lord Maevathar's love letter) to The Spring Tide.

**The Reality**: Courier "D" is dead--killed in the ambush crossfire. The party has the letter.

**The Complication**: Yannick doesn't yet know the courier is dead or that the letter has been intercepted.

**What Yannick Knows About the Ambush**: Everyone in town heard about the shootout at the Mariner's Rest--it's the talk of Thelonésë. Yannick knows there was a fight, people died, constables got involved. What he doesn't know yet is that his courier was one of the dead.

## The Drop Location

At The Spring Tide, there's a ladder leading to a storage loft above the main room. At the base of the ladder, tucked behind some crates, is a hidden compartment--the drop box. Yannick checks it regularly.

The note found on the courier reads:
> "D: 1 parcel, Fragile. Spring tide, no lader. --Y"

- "D" = the courier (dead)
- "1 parcel, Fragile" = the love letter
- "Spring tide" = The Spring Tide social club
- "no lader" = the drop box is by the ladder (not "no later")
- "Y" = Yannick

## What Yannick Will Do

**Day 1**: Expects delivery. Doesn't worry when it doesn't arrive--couriers are sometimes late.

**Day 2**: Starts to wonder. Checks the drop box multiple times. Hears more details about the ambush, but doesn't connect it to his courier yet.

**Day 3**: Realizes something is wrong. Starts asking careful questions. "Has anyone seen [D's description]?" Eventually connects the dots--his courier was one of the thugs in the ambush.

**Day 4+**: Realizes the letter might have been looted. Tries to figure out who took it. If he learns the party has it, he'll consider his options carefully.

**If Yannick identifies the party as having the letter**:
- He'll approach subtly, not aggressively
- Might claim it's "personal correspondence for a friend"
- Might try to buy it back
- Might try to steal it if he can do so without exposure
- He's *not* going to threaten experienced fighters--he's smarter than that

## What Yannick Knows

- The letter was written by Lord Aldren Maevathar to "Lady Jyl"
- It's explicit enough to ruin Maevathar's reputation and marriage
- Maevathar doesn't know it was stolen yet
- The planned extortion amount was 2,000 gold
- This is the ring's first noble target--they're nervous about it

## Relationships

- **Isindrel Maevathar**: A regular at The Spring Tide. Yannick is polite to her, knows she's Maevathar's daughter. He's *very* careful around her now--if she found out, things would get complicated fast.
- **Constable Captain Ashward**: Aware The Spring Tide is a gambling house; looks the other way as long as things stay civil.
- **Lord Maevathar**: Target of extortion. Has never met Yannick personally.
