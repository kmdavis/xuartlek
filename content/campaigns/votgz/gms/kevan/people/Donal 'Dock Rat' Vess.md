**Full Name**: Donal Vess
**Nickname**: "Dock Rat"
**Status**: Free (questioned and released)
**Affiliation**: None (former hired muscle for Scar)

## Description

A dock worker who took Scar's coin for what was supposed to be an easy job. It was not an easy job. Young, nervous, and currently reconsidering every life choice that led him to this moment.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Getting drunk at The Mariner's Rest
- **Condition**: Physically unharmed but deeply shaken
- **Legal Status**: Questioned by constables, released with a warning
- **Mental State**: Terrified. Watched an orc punch a man so hard he flew into another man. Saw an ally killed by friendly fire. Done with this line of work.

## What Donal Knows

- Scar hired him and several other dock workers for "easy money"
- The job was to capture some sailors with a bounty
- The bounty came from "the Commodore" (doesn't know details)
- The pay was 50 gold total split between all the ruffians (not per head)
- Scar carried a wanted poster with pictures of six people

## What Donal Doesn't Know

- Anything about Commodore Thorne's larger operations
- Why the party has a bounty on them
- Anything about the love letter (that was on courier "D," a different thug who died)
- Who Scar's local contact is

## Potential Recruit

Donal is traumatized, broke, and needs work. He's seen the party fight and knows they're winners. If approached with respect (not pity), he might be willing to sign on as crew.

**Selling Points for Recruitment**:
- He already saw them win
- Working for the winning side seems smart
- Regular pay beats bounty hunting
- He needs to get out of town anyway (too many people know he was involved)

**Concerns**:
- He's not a fighter (just a dock worker with a club)
- He might bolt under pressure
- Trust takes time

## Roleplaying Notes

- Jumpy, especially around large humanoids
- Drinks to calm his nerves (making him more talkative)
- Keeps glancing toward the door
- Will cooperate if he thinks it keeps him safe
