**Full Name**: Fenwick Dray
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Lord Maevathar's estate -- Camp Six overseer

## Description

Human male, late forties. Wiry, weathered, permanently sunburned. Sharp eyes under heavy brows. Moves with the efficient economy of someone who's managed laborers for decades. Clean-shaven, hair cropped short -- military background, long ago. Wears a leather vest over work clothes, a ring of keys at his belt, and an expression that says you're wasting his time.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Camp Six, eastern old-growth forest
- **Purpose**: Managing the accelerated timber harvest for Lord Maevathar
- **Mood**: Tense. Trapped between a dangerous truth and a lord's orders.

## The Cover-Up

Fenwick knows about the predator. He's seen the drag marks, the claw gouges, the evidence. He reported it to Lord Maevathar two weeks ago. The lord's response: "Mark them as deserters. Keep cutting. I'll send hunters after the harvest is in."

Fenwick is following orders, but he's not comfortable. He's:
- **Telling workers to stay in groups**, but framing it as efficiency ("pair up, you cut faster")
- **Ordering early curfews**, claiming it's to prevent "theft of company time"
- **Keeping the workers ignorant** of the real danger -- they're scared, but they don't know why
- **Falsifying the ledgers** -- the three missing are listed as "voluntarily departed"

He's not evil. He's a company man who's been told the problem will be handled soon, and he believes it -- or needs to believe it. Every day he waits for the hunters that Lord Maevathar promised and haven't come.

## What Fenwick Will Do

**When the party arrives:**
- Polite but unwelcoming. "The guild sent auditors? Fine. The books are in order."
- Sticks to the script: "They deserted. Happens every season."
- Won't let them speak to workers unsupervised if he can help it (but he can't watch everyone)
- Offers food, lodging, and a strong suggestion they leave in the morning

**When Torvin asks for the originals:**
- Reluctantly complies (guild audit rights are contractual)
- The originals will show the "voluntarily departed" entries -- in notably fresher ink than surrounding entries
- If confronted about discrepancies, he deflects: "Clerical error. I'll have it corrected."

**When pressed hard:**
- He's not a good liar under pressure. Sweat on his brow, won't meet eyes.
- He won't voluntarily reveal Maevathar's role -- that's career-ending
- But if the party finds physical evidence AND the ledger discrepancies AND a worker's testimony, he cracks: "What was I supposed to do? He's the lord. He said he'd send hunters. He said it would be handled."

**After Torvin vanishes:**
- Genuinely shaken. This is the first time someone has disappeared while *witnesses were present*
- His composure breaks: "Gods. Right in the camp. It's never--it's never been this close before."
- May become an ally at this point -- the cover-up has failed and he knows it

## What Fenwick Knows

- **The predator**: Something hunts at twilight. Fast. Silent. Takes people without a sound. He's never seen it clearly.
- **The pattern**: Only at dawn and dusk. Only in dim light. The old-growth canopy makes Camp Six dim even at midday.
- **Maevathar's orders**: Keep cutting, keep quiet, hunters are coming. (They're not.)
- **The old-growth**: The cutting pushed into ancient forest three months ago. The disappearances started two months ago. He's made the connection but won't say it aloud.

## Personality

- **Efficient**: Doesn't waste words or motion
- **Loyal to the estate**: Twenty years serving Maevathar. This is his life.
- **Guilty**: Knows he's complicit. Justifies it to himself daily.
- **Not cruel**: He genuinely cares about the workers' safety -- he just cares about his position more
- **Breaking**: The stress is showing. Drinking more. Sleeping less. Jumping at shadows.

## Relationships

- **Lord Maevathar (employer)**: Loyal servant. Increasingly questioning that loyalty.
- **Rask Embertooth**: Professional antagonist. Fenwick knows Rask suspects something.
- **Torvin Greaves**: Dislikes -- auditors are trouble. But recognizes Torvin's right to be there.
- **The workers**: Paternalistic. Thinks he's protecting them by keeping them ignorant. He's wrong.
- **Harget (missing)**: Respected him. Harget was the best logger in Camp Six. His loss hurt.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Clipped sentences. Military bearing slipping under stress.
- Fidgets with his key ring when lying
- Won't look at the tree line after dark
- Voice: flat, controlled, occasional crack when emotions surface
- If he breaks: not dramatic sobbing -- quiet admission, defeated, staring at his hands
