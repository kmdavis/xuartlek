**Full Name**: Harlan "Scar" Tomlin
**Status**: In custody (Thelonésë jail)
**Affiliation**: Independent bounty hunter (no faction loyalty)

## Description

An opportunistic bounty hunter who makes his living tracking down people with prices on their heads. Recognizable by the prominent scar across his face. Armed with a shortbow and shortsword. Notable for his photographic memory--he memorizes wanted posters and bounty notices, recognizing targets on sight.

## The Photographic Memory

Harlan's greatest asset isn't his combat skill--it's his memory. He can recall:
- Every wanted poster he's ever seen in detail
- Faces, names, bounty amounts, alleged crimes
- Where he saw each poster (which port, which board, which tavern)
- Other details from his travels (ship names, cargo manifests, overheard conversations)

This makes him a potential goldmine of information if properly motivated--or a significant liability to anyone whose secrets he's inadvertently memorized.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Thelonésë jail, under Constable Captain Ashward's custody
- **Condition**: Badly injured (collapsed lung from Belegost's critical hit), enfeebled, fatigued
- **Legal Status**: Arrested for assault with a deadly weapon; killed one of his own men (manslaughter via friendly fire)
- **Medical Care**: Treated by Vellum Ashworth; recovering but slowly

## What Harlan Knows

- The party's bounty: 500 gold per head, "crimes against the Commodore"
- He recognized them from a wanted poster (can describe where he saw it)
- The bounty was posted by Commodore Marcellus "Three Sheets" Thorne
- He hired local dock workers (the ruffians) as muscle--cheaper than professionals
- He's seen *many* bounty posters in his career (and remembers them all)
- Random details from years of traveling the islands that might prove useful

**The Secret**: Harlan has done jobs for Commodore Thorne in the past. During one of those jobs, he saw or heard something he shouldn't have. Something Thorne would prefer stayed buried. Harlan might not even realize the significance of what he knows--but that photographic memory of his recorded it anyway.

## What Harlan Doesn't Know

- That Thorne considers him a liability
- That someone might be coming to silence him
- The full significance of whatever secret he inadvertently memorized

## The Silencing Threat

Thorne doesn't trust Harlan's memory. Even though Harlan isn't a Thorne employee, he's done jobs for the Commodore before--and that photographic memory has captured things Thorne would rather stay forgotten.

With Harlan now in custody, talking to constables, and potentially testifying before a magistrate... Thorne can't take the chance that Harlan will mention the wrong detail to the wrong person.

**The Order**: Orlan Thatch will receive instructions to silence Harlan. Not because Harlan failed a job--he was never working for Thorne on this one--but because Harlan knows too much from *previous* jobs.

**Timeline**: Instructions arrive 4-6 days after news of Harlan's capture reaches Thorne. Attempt on Harlan's life 1-2 days after that.

**The Party's Choice**: If they learn about the threat, they must decide whether to protect a man who tried to kill them. Harlan's information could be valuable. His death would be convenient. The moral calculus is... complicated.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Pragmatic, not cruel--bounty hunting is just a job
- Respects capable opponents (the party impressed him, even if they beat him)
- Will negotiate if he thinks it benefits him
- Doesn't realize he's in danger from Thorne
- His memory makes him valuable alive--he knows this and might try to leverage it
