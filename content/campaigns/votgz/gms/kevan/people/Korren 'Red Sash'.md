**Full Name**: Korren "Red Sash"
**Status**: Active (at large)
**Affiliation**: Commodore Thorne's primary enforcer

## Description

Thorne's enforcer--a pit fighter who favors a whip over a sword. Lean and confident, he wears an open-collared shirt under a dark coat, with his namesake red sash tied at the waist. A peacock feather adorns his tricorn. His expression carries the easy swagger of a man who knows he's dangerous--and enjoys making sure you know it too. Theatrical in his intimidation style; slams fists on tables for emphasis.

**Role**: Enforcer, Pit Fighter

## Combat Profile

**Pit Fighter Challenge**: Ability to demoralize opponents through intimidation
**Primary Weapon**: Whip (preferred)--deals critical hits with 2d6 bleed damage
**Secondary**: Sword, firearms
**Style**: Opens with intimidation, follows with whip attacks; capable of delivering devastating critical hits

## Personality

- **Aggressive and confrontational** - leads with presence and threat
- **Dramatic** - slams fists on tables, makes declarations
- **Loyal** to Commodore Thorne
- **Respects strength** - willing to "settle this right proper" rather than simply attack

## The Tavern Confrontation (Session 07)

Korren entered the tavern with three armed associates, weapons sheathed--suggesting a brawl rather than lethal intent. He confronted the party about Demitri's death and their ship:

1. Slammed fist on table: "Don't you be playing games with me."
2. Presented a singed playing card as proof of claim
3. "See this? This means you're marked, donut. That ship belonged to young Demitri. The Commodore's own blood."
4. "Here's how it goes. You surrender both ships peaceable like, or will we settle this right proper?"

When Flick stepped on the card and drew a sword, combat commenced. Korren used Pit Fighter Challenge to demoralize Flick, then struck with his whip for critical damage with bleed. Other patrons in the tavern took bets--one wagered "20 gold says red sash runs before it's over."

## What Korren Knows

- The party killed Demitri
- They had possession of Demitri's ship (now sold)
- He has orders from Thorne to recover the ships and/or punish the party
- Thorne's informant network extends to Thelonésë

## Current Status

After the tavern brawl, Korren escaped. His current whereabouts are unknown, but he:
- May return for a rematch
- Could appear with reinforcements
- Might show up with different colored sashes (players joked about "blue sash, green sash, crimson sash" variants)

## GM Notes

Korren represents the physical threat of Thorne's organization. While Thorne operates through networks and bounties, Korren is the fist. His escape from the tavern brawl sets up a potential rematch.

Consider:
