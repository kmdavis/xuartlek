**Full Name**: Tanis (no surname given)
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Camp Six worker (guild member), apprentice logger

## Description

Human female, late teens. Small for a logger, quick on her feet. Hired on the same day as Doss -- they were friends. Dark hair tied back, perpetually sawdust-covered. Quiet around authority, sharp-eyed when she thinks no one's watching.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Camp Six
- **Mood**: Scared and grieving. She lost her friend and nobody will acknowledge it.

## What Tanis Knows

She heard Myla scream. It was dusk, three weeks ago. A short, cut-off sound from the direction of the latrine trench at the tree line. Tanis reported it. Fenwick said it was a bird.

*"That wasn't a bird."*

She also knows Doss was asking questions about Harget's disappearance in the days before he vanished. He was talking to other workers, poking around the tree line, trying to find tracks. Then Doss was gone too.

## What Tanis Will Say

- Only speaks if the party approaches her away from Fenwick and the older workers
- If she trusts them: *"I heard Myla scream. They said it was a bird. That wasn't a bird. I know what a bird sounds like."*
- About Doss: *"He was asking questions. Same questions you're asking. A week later he was gone. You should be careful."*
- If asked about the sound: *"Short. Like she started to scream and then... stopped. Not like she chose to stop. Like something made her stop."*

## Personality

- Observant and cautious
- Grief makes her brave -- she wants someone to *listen*
- Doesn't trust adults in authority (with good reason)
- Fiercely loyal to Doss's memory

## Roleplaying Notes

- Speaks barely above a whisper
- Watches the party before approaching -- sizing them up
- If Fenwick appears, she goes silent immediately and finds somewhere else to be
- Picks at the sawdust on her sleeves when talking about Doss

## Dramatic Purpose

Tanis provides the **testimony** clue -- an earwitness to Myla's disappearance. She also establishes the pattern: Doss investigated and vanished, implying the party might be next. Her youth and grief should make the players want to protect her.
