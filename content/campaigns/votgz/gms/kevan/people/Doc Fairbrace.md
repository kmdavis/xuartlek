**Full Name**: Eldon "Doc" Fairbrace
**Status**: Active (party crew)
**Affiliation**: Party's ship (formerly Captain Stormeye's crew)

## Description

A compassionate and cautious halfling known simply as "Doc," Eldon Fairbrace is always ready with a healing salve or encouraging word. He has a round, kindly face behind round spectacles, with tousled brown hair that suggests he's been too busy tending patients to worry about grooming. He wears a practical vest over a simple shirt, with a bandolier of potion bottles and vials crossing his chest. A scalpel is never far from his hand.

Although not especially brave or tough in combat, Doc knows his place is vital: patching up injuries and looking after his crewmates. He worries constantly about everyone's wellbeing, and his friendly, fretful demeanor brings comfort whenever stormy seas call--or when things go terribly wrong.

**Ancestry**: Halfling (Male)

## Background

Doc Fairbrace has been patching up sailors for longer than most of them have been alive. His medical knowledge comes from a combination of formal training and hard-won experience--you learn a lot about anatomy when your patients come in with cutlass wounds and cannonball injuries.

He signed on with Stormeye's crew as ship's doctor and found himself responsible for keeping everyone alive through the expedition into the ruins. When everything went wrong, he couldn't save most of them--but he kept the five survivors patched up and functioning until rescue arrived. It haunts him.

## Current Status

Now serves aboard the party's ship as the primary medical officer. His two daily Heal spells and Medical Lore make him invaluable. He worries about the party constantly, which means he's always prepared.

The party already has Gteek (cleric) and Gripp (chirurgeon alchemist) for healing--Doc provides additional support and redundancy.

## GM Notes

Doc is the heart of the survivor group in a different way than Rannos. Where Rannos lifts spirits, Doc tends wounds--physical and emotional. His constant worrying is endearing rather than annoying, and his Steady Nerves ability suggests he's braver than he thinks when it matters.

Consider:
- He's haunted by those he couldn't save--how does that manifest?
- His Medical Lore might identify unusual ailments or poisons
- He's a halfling in a party with a goblin swashbuckler--potential for comedic height disparity
- Could he mentor Gripp or learn from Gteek?
