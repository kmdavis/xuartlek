**Full Name**: Lady Aejylinn Olythos ("Jyl" to close friends)
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: House Olythos (noble)

## Description

Elf noblewoman, daughter of the respected widow Lady Seraphine Olythos. Poised, socially adept, perhaps a year or two older than Rin. Where Rin is reckless and charming, Aejylinn is careful and composed--at least in public. Close friends call her "Jyl," but she introduces herself formally to strangers.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Olythos townhouse in Thelonésë (or her mother's estate)
- **Social Circle**: Best friends with Isindrel Maevathar since childhood
- **Secret**: Having an affair with Rin's father, Lord Aldren Maevathar
- **Awareness**: Knows about the love letters; doesn't know one was intercepted

## The Affair

Aejylinn and Lord Maevathar began their relationship sometime in the past year. She's complicit, not a victim--though the power dynamics are complicated. He's older, married, her best friend's father, and a peer of her mother's. She knows it's wrong. She also hasn't stopped.

**How it started**: Left deliberately vague. Could be:
- Gradual--friendly visits that became something else
- Opportunistic--a moment of weakness at a social gathering
- Deliberate pursuit by either party

**Her feelings**: Genuinely conflicted. There's attraction, but also guilt about betraying Rin's trust. She compartmentalizes ruthlessly.

## What Aejylinn Knows

- The affair (obviously)
- That Maevathar has written her several letters (she has his; he has hers)
- That their relationship could destroy both families if exposed
- Island politics and noble gossip
- Her mother's business affairs (agriculture, trade)

## What Aejylinn Doesn't Know

- That one of Maevathar's letters was intercepted
- That Yannick Corwen is blackmailing him
- That the party has the letter

## Relationships

- **Isindrel "Rin" Maevathar**: Best friend since childhood. They met through social occasions--seasonal gatherings, festivals, the endless circuit of noble obligations. Now they're both young women navigating the same party scene. The guilt is constant.
- **Lord Aldren Maevathar**: Her lover. Rin's father. The wrongness is part of it.
- **Lady Seraphine Olythos**: Her mother. Respected, competent, would be devastated and furious if she learned the truth.
- **Lady Verenis Maevathar**: Rin's mother, Aldren's wife. Aejylinn avoids her when possible.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Introduces herself as "Aejylinn Olythos" to strangers
- Only close friends (Rin, a few others) call her "Jyl"
- Composed, proper, harder to read than Rin
- Watches conversations more than she participates
- Deflects personal questions with practiced grace
- If the party befriends Rin, they'll likely meet Aejylinn at The Spring Tide

## Dramatic Irony

The party carries a letter addressed to "my dearest Jyl" and signed "Al"--intimate nicknames that provide a fig leaf of deniability. When they meet "Aejylinn Olythos" at Rin's card table, there's no obvious connection. To piece it together, they'd need to:

- Hear someone call her "Jyl" (only close friends do)
- Learn that Rin's father's first name is "Aldren" (he's "Lord Maevathar" to most)
- Connect the dots
