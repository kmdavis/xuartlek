**Full Name**: Torvin Greaves
**Status**: Active (endangered)
**Affiliation**: Timber Workers' Guild -- bookkeeper

## Description

Human male, early thirties. Lean where his cousin Torvald is barrel-chested, but they share the same broad smile and easy warmth. Wire-rimmed spectacles, ink-stained fingers, always patting his vest pockets to make sure his notebook is still there. Dresses practically but neatly -- he's an office man who takes pride in his work.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Traveling with the party to Camp Six
- **Purpose**: Audit the Camp Six wage ledgers on-site, brief the party on workers and operations
- **Mood**: Nervous but determined. He knew Harget personally.

## Why He's Here

Rask's instructions were clear: outsiders ask the questions, guild folk stay out of sight. Torvin's role isn't to investigate -- it's to **audit the books**. Fenwick Dray sends copies of the Camp Six ledgers to Thelonésë, but Torvin suspects the copies have been altered. He needs to see the originals.

His cover: he's there to conduct a routine seasonal audit. Fenwick won't like it, but he can't refuse -- the guild has contractual audit rights. While Fenwick is annoyed about the bookkeeper, the party (as unrelated outsiders) can talk to workers freely.

Torvin insisted on coming all the way to Camp Six despite Rask's preference that he stay in Thornhaven. *"I knew Harget. I can't just sit in a tavern while you look at the place where he disappeared. And I need to see the originals -- Fenwick could have altered the copies he sends us."*

## What Torvin Knows

- **Wage records**: Every worker's pay history, hire date, next-of-kin
- **The missing three**: Harget (veteran, 15 years), Myla (saw operator, family in Millbrook), Doss (apprentice, first season)
- **None collected final wages**: Confirmed from guild records in Thelonésë
- **Camp Six operations**: Cutting schedule, crew size, supply requests
- **Fenwick Dray**: Lord Maevathar's man, not guild. Been overseer for years. "Competent but loyal to the estate, not the workers."
- **The accelerated schedule**: Camp Six has been pushing harder than any other camp this season. More timber, longer hours.

## What Torvin Shares on the Road

During travel, he briefs the party naturally:
- Names and personalities of the missing workers
- Who at Camp Six will talk honestly vs. who's loyal to Fenwick
- The wage discrepancy -- proof the "desertion" story doesn't hold
- Background on Fenwick and the estate's relationship with the guild
- His worry that the cutting has gone into old-growth territory: "Those trees have been there longer than the Principality. There's a reason nobody cut them before."

## Personality

- **Talkative**: Fills silence with useful information. Not annoying -- genuinely informative and warm.
- **Nervous in the jungle**: An office man out of his element. Jokes to cover anxiety. Flinches at animal sounds.
- **Stubborn about his people**: The guild isn't abstract to him. These are names he writes in ledgers, families he processes wages for.
- **The Greaves warmth**: Like Torvald, he makes people feel at ease. Where Torvald does it with drinks and stories, Torvin does it with earnest attention.

## Relationships

- **Torvald Greaves (uncle)**: Close. Torvin drinks at the Mariner's Rest most evenings. Torvald helped raise him after his father (Torvald's brother) died at sea. Worries about him constantly.
- **Rask Embertooth (boss)**: Respects deeply. Rask trusts Torvin's eye for numbers.
- **Harget (missing worker)**: Knew him personally. Harget would stop by the guild office to drop off reports. They'd share tea.
- **Fenwick Dray (overseer)**: Professional dislike. Fenwick treats audits as insults.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Pats his vest pocket (notebook) when nervous
- Pushes spectacles up his nose constantly
- Uses precise numbers: "Fifteen years, seven months. That's how long Harget worked the camps."
- When scared, gets quieter rather than louder
- Voice: earnest, slightly reedy, occasional dry humor

## Dramatic Purpose

Torvin exists to:
1. **Brief the party** during travel (exposition that feels natural)
2. **Give them someone to care about** before the predator strikes
3. **Vanish at twilight** -- the session cliffhanger
4. **Connect the investigation to Thelonésë** -- Torvald's nephew is now missing, making this deeply personal for an NPC the party already knows
