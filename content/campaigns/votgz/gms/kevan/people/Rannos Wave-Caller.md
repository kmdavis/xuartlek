**Full Name**: Rannos Wave-Caller
**Status**: Active (party crew)
**Affiliation**: Party's ship (formerly Captain Stormeye's crew)

## Description

A charismatic and confident elf with long, flowing orange-red hair that streams behind him like a banner. Rannos is rarely seen without a smile and even more rarely without an instrument--usually a lute, though he's proficient with several. He carries a sextant and keeps scrolls and maps tucked in his belt, marking him as the ship's navigator as well as its entertainment.

The "Wave-Caller" epithet hints at something more than musical talent--he has a way of reading the sea that borders on the supernatural, and his shanties have been known to lift spirits even in the darkest moments.

**Ancestry**: Elf (Male)
**Role**: Navigator, Bard, Morale Officer

## Background

Rannos served as navigator and ship's bard under Captain Stormeye. His knowledge of currents, stars, and weather made him invaluable; his music made him beloved. When the expedition went wrong, his quick thinking helped guide the survivors to temporary safety.

He's a storyteller and a sage who knows how to keep sailors' spirits high even in desperate circumstances. With a smirk and a quick adjustment of his instrument, he's always ready to share a tale, lift spirits, and remind everyone what they're fighting for.

## Current Status

Now serves aboard the party's ship as navigator and morale officer. His skills with the sextant and his supernatural sense for the sea make him genuinely valuable beyond entertainment. Owes the party his life.
