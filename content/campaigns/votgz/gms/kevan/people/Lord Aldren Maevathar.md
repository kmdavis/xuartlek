**Full Name**: Lord Aldren Maevathar
**Status**: Active, unaware of the extortion plot
**Affiliation**: Zolnuthian nobility (rural lord, timber operations)

## Description

The eldest and wealthiest of the island's three lords. Controls most of the timber operations from an estate in the eastern hills. Outwardly respectable, strict about accounts and schedules. Father of Isindrel. Husband to Lady Maevathar.

Privately... less respectable than he appears.

## The Love Letter

Lord Maevathar wrote an explicit love letter to "Lady Jyl"--a woman who is decidedly *not* his wife. The letter is:
- Romantic and explicit enough to ruin his reputation
- Proof of infidelity that would destroy his marriage
- Leverage worth thousands of gold to anyone willing to use it

**How it was stolen**: [TBD--possibly a servant, a visit to Lady Jyl's residence, a message intercepted]

**Current status**: The letter was being transported by courier "D" when he was killed in the ambush. The party now has it without knowing what it is.

## What Maevathar Knows

- He wrote the letter
- He sent it to Lady Jyl
- He hasn't heard back (worrying, but not yet alarming)
- He has no idea it was intercepted

## What Maevathar Doesn't Know

- That the extortion ring has (had) his letter
- That anyone plans to use it against him
- That his daughter Isindrel is in town while this unfolds

## If He Learns About the Extortion

Lord Maevathar has resources:
- Money (2,000 gold is the planned demand--he could pay it)
- Connections (he knows people in the Principality)
- Ruthlessness (he didn't build a timber empire by being soft)

He might:
- Pay the extortion quietly
- Hire someone to deal with the extortionists permanently
- Try to recover the letter before it can be used
- Deny everything and dare them to publish (risky)
