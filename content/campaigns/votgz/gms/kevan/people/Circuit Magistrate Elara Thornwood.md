**Full Name**: Elara Thornwood
**Status**: En route to Thelonésë
**Affiliation**: Principality of Zolnuth (judiciary)

## Description

Elven circuit magistrate who travels between the outer island settlements dispensing justice. Meticulous, thorough, scrupulously fair. Known for wanting every relevant fact before rendering judgment--which means her cases take time.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Currently completing her circuit; expected in Thelonésë in 4-6 days
- **Pending Cases**: Will review the Mariner's Rest incident (Flick, Harlan, deaths)
- **Reputation**: Fair but thorough. Defendants who tell the truth fare better than those who evade.

## How She'll Handle the Case

Magistrate Thornwood will want:
1. **Witness statements** from everyone present
2. **Physical evidence** (the bounty poster, weapons, the scene)
3. **Testimony** from the party, Harlan, Donal, and any bystanders
4. **Context** about the bounty and who posted it

**Likely Outcomes**:
- **Flick**: Probable acquittal on self-defense grounds, unless something complicates the case
- **Harlan**: Assault charges stand; manslaughter charge for the friendly-fire death is more complex
- **The Party**: May be asked to testify about the incident, the bounty, and their history with Thorne

## What Thornwood Cares About

- **The Law**: She applies it fairly, without favor
- **The Truth**: Lies and evasion annoy her; honesty is rewarded
- **Justice**: Not vengeance, not convenience--actual justice
- **Efficiency**: She has other settlements to visit; drawn-out cases frustrate her

## What Thornwood Doesn't Know

- The details of Commodore Thorne's operations
- That someone might try to silence Harlan before she arrives
- Anything about the extortion ring or the love letter

## Potential Complications

- **If Harlan is killed before trial**: She'll investigate thoroughly. A murder in custody is a serious breach.
- **If new evidence emerges**: She'll consider it, even if it delays her schedule.
- **If the party lies**: She's perceptive. Lying to a magistrate is a crime.
