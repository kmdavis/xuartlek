**Full Name**: Brennan (no surname given)
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Camp Six worker (guild member)

## Description

Human male, mid-twenties. Broad shoulders, hollow eyes. Was muscular and confident when the season started; now he's jumpy, thin, not sleeping. Flinches at sudden movements. Keeps a lantern lit even in daylight.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Camp Six
- **Mood**: Terrified. Barely holding it together.

## What Brennan Knows

Three nights ago, he was on watch. His lantern guttered in a breeze. In the half-second of dimness before he got it relit, he saw *something*. Right at the edge of the tree line. Close enough to touch. A shape -- low, fast, wrong -- and then the light came back and it was gone.

He reported it. Fenwick told him it was a shadow. Brennan knows what he saw.

## What Brennan Will Say

- Hesitant at first. Won't talk near Fenwick or the overseer's quarters.
- If approached privately: *"Something's out there. In the trees. I've seen... I don't know what I've seen."*
- If pressed: Describes the lantern guttering. The shape. *"It was right there. When the light came back, nothing. Just trees. But I swear on my mother--it was right there."*
- If asked about light: *"The lanterns help. I don't know why. I just know when my light went out, that's when I saw it. And when the light came back, it was gone."*

## Roleplaying Notes

- Eyes dart constantly toward the tree line
- Keeps one hand on his lantern at all times
- Speaks in short, halting sentences
- Not a coward -- he's still here, still working. But he's running on fumes.

## Dramatic Purpose

Brennan is the **light clue**. His testimony should lead the party (especially G'Teek) to connect the predator's vulnerability to bright light.
