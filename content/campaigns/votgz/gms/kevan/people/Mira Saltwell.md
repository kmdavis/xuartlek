**Full Name**: Mira Saltwell
**Status**: Active
**Affiliation**: Port Master's Office (official)

## Description

Human port master. Sharp-eyed, efficient, runs the harbor like clockwork. Knows every ship, every cargo, every captain's reputation.

## Current Situation

- **Location**: Port Master's Office at the harbor
- **Concern**: Normal port operations--the ambush didn't affect shipping
- **Awareness**: Knows the party's ship is docked; has their manifest on file

## What Mira Knows

- All ship traffic in and out of Thelonésë
- Cargo manifests (official and sometimes unofficial)
- Captain reputations across the islands
- When the magistrate's ship is expected
- Work opportunities for crews

## What Mira Could Offer

- **Work**: Cargo runs, message delivery, escort jobs
- **Information**: Ship schedules, who's asking about whom
- **Records**: Official documentation if needed

## Relationships

- **Constable Captain Ashward**: Professional cooperation
- **Bren Kettleworth**: Regular dealings over ship supplies
- **The Party**: Their ship is in her harbor; she has their paperwork

## Roleplaying Notes

- All business, little small talk
- Remembers everything about ships and schedules
- Impatient with people who waste her time
- Respects competent sailors
