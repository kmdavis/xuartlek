**Full Name**: Commodore Marcellus "Three Sheets" Thorne
**Status**: Active (at large)
**Affiliation**: Commands a pirate fleet in the Shubae region

## Description

A successful privateer turned pirate who commands several ships across the Shubae waters. Stocky and bearded, he cuts a confident figure in his naval coat and tricorn hat with its red plume. Often seen with a glass of rum in hand and a self-satisfied smile--a man who's clearly enjoying the spoils of his trade. Carries multiple pistols on his person (historically accurate for the era of single-shot flintlocks). The epithet "Three Sheets" has a story behind it, though it's not commonly discussed.

**Ancestry**: Half-Elf (Male)
**Role**: Pirate Commodore

## Position & Resources

Thorne isn't just a pirate captain--he's a commodore with:
- **Multiple ships** under his command
- **A secret base** port where captured vessels are brought
- **An informant network** across Shubae ports (Orlan Thatch in Thelonésë)
- **Bounty hunters** and enforcers on retainer
- **Influence** extending to ports like Sielmoro

## Personality

- **Practical**--focused on results over tradition
- **Ruthless** when crossed--the bounty on the party demonstrates this
- **Family-oriented** in his own way--Demitri's death is personal
- **Connected** enough to place bounties and maintain informants across multiple ports

## The Blood Feud

When Demitri--the Commodore's own blood--attacked the party and was killed, it became personal. The party then added insult to injury by repairing and selling Demitri's ship. Thorne's response:

1. Sent Korren Red Sash to confront them
2. Posted a 500 gold bounty per head for "crimes against the Commodore"
3. Activated informants to track their movements
4. Hired bounty hunters to bring them in

## What Thorne Knows

- The party killed Demitri and took his ship
- They fought Korren and escaped
- They're currently in Thelonésë (via Orlan Thatch's reports)
- Harlan "Scar" Tomlin has been captured

## What Thorne Wants

- **Vengeance** for Demitri's death
- **The party** captured or killed
- **Harlan silenced** before he reveals secrets from past jobs
- **His reputation** maintained--no one crosses the Commodore without consequence

## Key Subordinates

- **Korren "Red Sash"** - Primary enforcer, pit fighter
- **Demitri Thorne** (deceased) - Blood relation, killed by the party
- **Orlan Thatch** - Informant in Thelonésë (operates as a general merchant)

## GM Notes

Thorne is a looming threat--present through his network rather than in person. He represents:
- **Consequence** for the party's early actions
- **A web of influence** they must navigate
- **An eventual confrontation** when the party is ready (or desperate)

The secret base location is unknown to the party. Finding it could shift the arc from reactive to proactive.
