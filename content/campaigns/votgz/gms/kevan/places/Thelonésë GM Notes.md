## Current Situation

The party arrived in Thelonésë and was immediately ambushed by bounty hunter Harlan "Scar" Tomlin and his thugs. The fight spilled onto the main street; constables intervened. Scar is now in jail with a collapsed lung, his surviving thug Donal Vess beside him. Flick faces charges and cannot leave until Magistrate Thornwood arrives in 4-6 days.

After the dust settled, they visited The Mariner's Rest where Torvald Greaves recognized them for "putting down Scar." They met Rask Embertooth at the bar and accepted two synergistic jobs: courier work for the timber workers' guild (10gp) and a cargo escort to Moss Hollow Village (50gp). Both jobs head inland, allowing them to combine the trips. Departing tomorrow at dawn.

**What the party doesn't know:**
- Commodore Thorne has an informant here (Orlan Thatch)
- Orlan has orders to silence Scar before he can talk
- The love letter they looted belongs to a local extortion ring
