## Current Situation

Camp Six is Lord Maevathar's most remote logging operation, situated on the eastern edge of the Isle of Thelonath where the cleared land gives way to ancient old-growth jungle. The camp has been cutting into this old-growth for approximately three months -- premium ironwood that's worth a fortune.

Two months ago, the disappearances started. Three workers have vanished: Harget (veteran, 15 years), Myla (saw operator, family in Millbrook), and Doss (apprentice, first season). All were taken at twilight. All were reported as "deserters" by the overseer, Fenwick Dray, on Lord Maevathar's orders.

The cause: a predator -- extremely fast, nearly invisible in dim light -- that hunts during the twilight hours. The logging disturbed its territory. It has been picking off workers who venture beyond the camp's lantern light at dawn and dusk.

Lord Maevathar knows. He ordered the cover-up to keep the timber flowing until the most valuable stands are harvested. He promised to send professional hunters "after the season." The hunters have not come.

## The Camp

- **Size**: ~20 workers (down from 30), plus Fenwick Dray (overseer)
- **Layout**: Central clearing with barracks, mess hall, supply tent, overseer's office. Surrounded by partially felled old-growth on three sides. The canopy creates permanent dim light even at midday.
- **Atmosphere**: Oppressive. Workers are scared but can't articulate why. Early curfews. Lanterns everywhere. The sound of axes has replaced conversation.
- **Key locations**:
  - Overseer's office (Fenwick's quarters, contains the original wage ledgers)
  - Barracks (workers' bunks -- Harget's tools still in his bunk)
  - Eastern tree line (drag marks, claw gouges, the hunting ground)
  - Supply tent (where Torvin sets up to audit the books)

## Clue Trail

### Physical Evidence
- Harget's tools in his bunk (oiled, cared for -- no one deserts and leaves their tools)
- Drag marks at the eastern tree line, ending abruptly
- Claw gouges on tree trunks, fifteen feet up
- A torn piece of clothing snagged on branches, high in the canopy
- A tool belt, abandoned mid-work near the tree line

### Testimony
- **Brennan**: Saw a shape when his lantern guttered -- gone when the light returned
- **Old Marsh**: Harget's tools, Myla's family, Doss's determination -- none of them deserted
- **Tanis**: Heard Myla scream at dusk. Short, cut off. "That wasn't a bird."
- **Workers generally**: Early curfews, hoarded lanterns, fear they can't name

### Paper Trail
- Fenwick's ledger lists all three as "voluntarily departed"
- Guild records (Torvin's knowledge) confirm none collected final wages
- The cutting schedule accelerated shortly after the first disappearance
- Transfer records: Lira (Doss's friend) was moved to Camp Two after asking questions

### The Light Pattern
- Disappearances only at dawn and dusk
- Workers instinctively hoard lanterns and candles
- Brennan's sighting: creature visible in dim light, gone when lantern relit
- Old Marsh sleeps with four candles burning
- The old-growth canopy keeps the camp in permanent dim light
