Campaign-specific rules, snap decisions, and clarifications made at the table.

## Ship & Crew

### Travel Pace
- Ship speed: ~4 mph
- Each hex on world map: 24 miles
- Travel rate: ~4 hexes per day

### Crew Recruitment
- Base DC for recruiting: standard Diplomacy check
- Spending gold on drinks grants circumstance bonus:
  - 1 silver to 1 gold: +1 bonus
  - 1 gold: +10 bonus (Session 07)
- Critical success: recruit 2 crew members
- Critical failure: potential negative consequences (waived in Session 07)
- Can recruit from crowds/bystanders with Diplomacy check (Session 13)

### Crew Wages
- Paid at port (can't spend money at sea)
- Track days owed, settle when docking

### Docking Fees
- ~1 gold per day per ship at Sielmoro
- Premium berths cost more
- Fees may increase geometrically for extended stays (Session 05)

### Ship Weapons
- Ballistas/arbalests available on Grim Zephyr
- Rules partially manual within Foundry (Session 04)

## Combat & Mechanics

### Attacks of Opportunity
- Rare in PF2e--confirmed safe to move away from enemies without provoking (Session 07)

### Bleed Damage
- Persistent damage at end of turn
- Flat check to end, OR any healing stops bleed immediately (Session 07)

### Non-Lethal Damage
- Full damage, knocks out instead of kills
- Useful for capturing enemies for questioning or recruitment (Session 12, Session 13)

### Critical Hits
- Attack exceeds target's AC by 10+ (Session 11)
- Using critical hit deck for special effects

### Critical Fumbles
- Using critical fumble deck for special effects (Session 12)
- Effects include: weapon breaks, weapon tangles, self-damage

### Gnome Yeeting
- Athletics check to throw Gripp onto roof
- +2 circumstance bonus for "shot put" style intent
- DC 15 to land on roof (Session 12)
- Target can make Acrobatics check to recover from failed throw

### Recall Knowledge
- Action to learn enemy capabilities from GM
- Alternative: "Marshall roll" against fixed DC reveals mechanics (Session 12)

## Conditions (PF2e Clarifications)

### Doomed
- Reduces the dying value at which you die
- Makes death faster if knocked to 0 HP (Session 11)

### Stupefied
- -1 status penalty to mental-based rolls and DCs
- Affects: Will saves, spell attacks, mental skill checks
- Lasts until full night's rest (Session 11)

### Enfeebled
- Status penalty to Strength-based rolls and DCs (Session 12)

### Fatigued
- -1 penalty to AC and saving throws (Session 12)

## Healing

### Treat Wounds
- Takes 10 minutes, usable once per hour per patient
- Successful check restores HP
- Continuing treatment for full hour doubles healing (Session 04)

## Spellcasting

### Sustaining Spells
- Sustaining a spell does NOT count as multi-action attack
- Does not trigger Multiple Attack Penalty (MAP) (Session 11)

### Bless
- 15ft radius base
- Can sustain to increase radius by 10ft per turn (Session 11)

## Items & Equipment

### Item Investment
- Multiple investments per character per day allowed
- Repeated invest/uninvest cycles (6x) consume daily investment capacity (Session 04)

### Shortbow
- Reload 0: allows multiple shots per turn (Session 11)

## Downtime

### Earn Income
- One roll covers multiple days of work
- Task level affects DC and potential earnings
- Skills used flexibly (Alcohol Lore for bartending, etc.)
- Higher task level = higher DC but higher potential reward (Session 07)

## XP & Advancement

- Started at 200 XP out of 800 for level 2 (Session 04)
- XP calculated asynchronously after combat
