Trade goods are bulk commodities that can be bought and sold at various ports. Prices fluctuate based on the port's traits and settlement size.

## Quick Reference: Pricing Formula

**Buy Price = Quality Price × (1 + Port%) × Bulk × Negotiation**
**Sell Price = Quality Price × (1 − Port%) × Bulk × Negotiation × 0.80**

| Factor | Modifier |
|--------|----------|
| **Quality:** Low / Standard / High / Exceptional | ×0.70 / ×1.00 / ×1.40 / ×2.00 |
| **Port Modifier:** See tables below | Varies by trait + settlement size |
| **Bulk (Buy):** 1 / 2-4 / 5-9 / 10-19 / 20+ | ×1.00 / ×0.95 / ×0.90 / ×0.85 / ×0.80 |
| **Bulk (Sell):** 1 / 2-4 / 5-9 / 10-19 / 20+ | ×0.80 / ×0.85 / ×0.90 / ×0.92 / ×0.95 |
| **Negotiation:** Crit Fail / Fail / Success / Crit Success | ×1.10 / ×1.00 / ×0.95 / ×0.85 (buy) |
| | ×0.90 / ×1.00 / ×1.05 / ×1.15 (sell) |

*Negotiation DC = 15 + settlement modifier (Village +0, Town +2, City +5)*
*Skills: Diplomacy, Deception, Intimidation, Mercantile Lore, Society*

## Detailed Trading Rules

### Understanding Port Modifiers

The port modifier tables show price adjustments based on **supply and demand**:
- **Negative modifiers** = goods are plentiful here (cheap to buy, poor to sell)
- **Positive modifiers** = goods are scarce here (expensive to buy, good to sell)

**When Buying:** Apply the modifier as shown (positive = more expensive)
**When Selling:** Invert the modifier (where goods are scarce, merchants pay more)

**Multiple Traits:** If a port has multiple traits, merchants use the modifier most favorable to themselves--least favorable for buying, most favorable for selling.

---

### Identifying Quality

Before purchasing, a character may attempt to assess the true quality of goods.

**Check:** Mercantile Lore, Crafting, or relevant Lore (DC 15)

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Critical Success | Learn true quality + any defects or special properties |
| Success | Learn true quality |
| Failure | Seller claims one tier higher than actual |
| Critical Failure | Seller claims Exceptional regardless of actual quality |

*Attempting to identify quality takes 10 minutes per crate inspected.*

---

### Negotiation

#### Making the Check

Choose **one** skill. DC = 15 + Settlement Modifier

| Settlement | DC Modifier |
|------------|-------------|
| Village | +0 (DC 15) |
| Town | +2 (DC 17) |
| City | +5 (DC 20) |

#### Available Skills

| Skill | Approach | Bonus Effect |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Diplomacy** | Honest negotiation | Works anywhere, no risk |
| **Deception** | Misrepresent goods/urgency | Better ceiling, but risky |
| **Intimidation** | Pressure tactics | Fast, but burns bridges |
| **Mercantile Lore** | Know the market | Also reveals port modifiers |
| **Society** | Read social cues | Also reveals merchant disposition |

#### Results

| Result | Buying | Selling |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Critical Success | ×0.85 | ×1.15 |
| Success | ×0.95 | ×1.05 |
| Failure | ×1.00 | ×1.00 |
| Critical Failure | ×1.10 | ×0.90 |

#### Critical Failure Consequences

| Skill | Critical Failure |
|-------|------------------|
| Diplomacy | Merchant offended; won't negotiate further today |
| Deception | Caught lying; −2 to all future checks with this merchant |
| Intimidation | Merchant calls guards or refuses future business |
| Mercantile Lore | Badly misread market; GM secretly applies wrong port modifier |
| Society | Misread situation; merchant adds 10% "difficult customer" fee |

### Bulk Pricing

Buying and selling in quantity affects prices differently.

| Crates | Buying Modifier | Selling Modifier |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|
| 1 | ×1.00 | ×0.80 |
| 2-4 | ×0.95 | ×0.85 |
| 5-9 | ×0.90 | ×0.90 |
| 10-19 | ×0.85 | ×0.92 |
| 20+ | ×0.80 | ×0.95 |

*Buying in bulk earns discounts; selling in bulk approaches wholesale value.*

---

### The Merchant's Margin

The ×0.80 factor when selling represents the merchant's profit margin on resale. They need to make money too--nobody pays full retail when buying to resell.

---

### Market Volatility (Optional)

For added uncertainty, roll 1d20 when arriving at a new port:

| Roll | Market Condition | Selling Price Effect |
|------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Glut | −20% |
| 2-5 | Oversupplied | −10% |
| 6-15 | Normal | No change |
| 16-19 | High Demand | +10% |
| 20 | Shortage | +25% |

*This creates "right place, right time" moments that make trading memorable.*

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Buying Naval Supplies

**Scenario:** Buying 5 crates of High-quality Naval Supplies at Thelonésë (Naval Town)

1. **Quality Price:** High = 126 gp
2. **Port Modifier:** Naval Town = −50% → ×0.50
3. **Adjusted Price:** 126 × 0.50 = 63 gp
4. **Bulk Discount:** 5 crates = ×0.90 → 56.7 gp per crate
5. **Negotiation:** Diplomacy Success (×0.95) → **53.9 gp per crate**

**Total: 269.5 gp for 5 crates**

---

### Example 2: Selling Those Supplies

**Scenario:** Selling the same 5 crates at a Pirate Town

1. **Quality Price:** High = 126 gp
2. **Port Modifier (Inverted):** Pirate Town = +70% for Naval Supplies → ×1.70
3. **Adjusted Price:** 126 × 1.70 = 214.2 gp
4. **Bulk Modifier:** 5 crates = ×0.90 → 192.8 gp
5. **Merchant Margin:** ×0.80 → 154.2 gp
6. **Negotiation:** Deception Success (×1.05) → **161.9 gp per crate**

**Total: 809.5 gp for 5 crates**
**Profit: 540 gp (~108 gp per crate)**

---

### Example 3: Getting Swindled

**Scenario:** A PC buys 2 crates of "Exceptional" Spices at a Frontier Village without checking quality

1. **Claimed Quality:** Exceptional = 240 gp each
2. **Port Modifier:** Frontier Village = −10% → ×0.90
3. **Adjusted:** 240 × 0.90 = 216 gp
4. **Bulk:** 2 crates = ×0.95 → 205.2 gp per crate
5. **Negotiation:** Failed → ×1.00 → **205.2 gp per crate**

**Total Paid: 410.4 gp**

*Later, the PC discovers the spices are actually Low quality (worth 84 gp base). They paid nearly 2.5× the true value. Always inspect your goods!*
