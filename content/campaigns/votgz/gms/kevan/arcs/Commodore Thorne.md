**Status**: Active

## Overview

The party has earned the enmity of Commodore Marcellus "Three Sheets" Thorne, a pirate captain who commands a small fleet in the Shubae region. They're now targets--wanted for bounty, hunted by his network. The blood feud began when "young Demitri," the Commodore's own blood, attacked the party and was killed in the resulting battle.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Commodore Marcellus "Three Sheets" Thorne]]** - Pirate commodore with a fleet and an ego
- **[[Korren "Red Sash"]]** - Thorne's primary enforcer, pit fighter with a whip
- **Demitri Thorne** (deceased) - "Young Demitri," the Commodore's blood relation, killed when he attacked the party
- **[[Harlan "Scar" Tomlin (Bounty Hunter)|Harlan "Scar" Tomlin]]** - Bounty hunter who recognized the party (now in custody)
- **[[Orlan Thatch]]** - Thorne's informant in Thelonésë

## The Conflict

When Demitri attacked the party, they defended themselves and killed him. They then repaired and sold his ship. To Thorne, this isn't just a business loss--it's the murder of family. His response has been escalating:

1. **The Claim**: Korren Red Sash confronted the party, presenting a singed playing card as proof the ship belonged to Demitri
2. **Bounty Posted**: 500 gold per head for "crimes against the Commodore"
3. **Active Hunters**: Bounty hunters like Scar actively pursuing
4. **Informant Network**: Eyes in various ports reporting movement (Orlan Thatch in Thelonésë)
5. **Silencing Operations**: Eliminating anyone who knows too much about his operations

## Current State

- The party is in Thelonésë awaiting the magistrate
- Harlan "Scar" Tomlin is in jail, badly wounded
- Thorne believes Harlan knows secrets from past jobs
- Orlan Thatch has been ordered to silence Harlan before he can talk
- The party doesn't know about the informant
- Korren Red Sash escaped the tavern fight--he may return
- The party doesn't yet know where Thorne's base is located

## Stakes Questions

- Will the party discover Thorne's informant network?
- Can they turn Harlan to their side with information about the silencing attempt?
- Will they confront Thorne directly, or work to undermine him?
- Can they clear the bounty, or will they always be looking over their shoulders?
- Will Korren seek a rematch?
- Can they find Thorne's secret base?
