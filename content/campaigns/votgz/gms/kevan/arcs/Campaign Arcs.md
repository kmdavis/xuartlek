## Arc Structure

Each arc file contains:
- **Overview**: What this arc is about
- **Key NPCs**: Major characters involved (links to location NPC files)
- **Current State**: Where things stand now
- **Connections**: Links to active threads in various locations
- **Stakes Questions**: What the arc is asking (answered by play)
- **Session History**: When this arc has been in focus
