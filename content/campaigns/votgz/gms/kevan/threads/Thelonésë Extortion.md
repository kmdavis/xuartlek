**Status**: Active
**Parent Arc**: None (standalone thread)

## Situation

When Flick pickpocketed the dead thugs after the ambush, they found more than expected: a five-page love letter from Lord Aldren Maevathar to a "Lady Jyl"--not his wife. The letter was being used as blackmail material by a local extortion ring.

The ring is run by Yannick Corwen, proprietor of The Spring Tide (a "social club" that's really a gambling house). They normally target merchants passing through, but the Maevathar letter was too tempting to pass up.

The coded note--"D: 1 parcel, Fragile. Spring tide, no lader. --Y"--was instructions to a courier who died in the ambush. The "parcel" was the letter, "Spring Tide" is the drop location, and "no lader" means use the ladder entrance.

The party has the letter. They don't know what it means yet. Yannick doesn't know his courier is dead.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Yannick Corwen]]** - Ring handler, Spring Tide proprietor
- **[[Lord Aldren Maevathar]]** - The compromised noble
- **[[Isindrel "Rin" Maevathar]]** - Lord's daughter, unaware, frequents Spring Tide
- **[[Aejylinn Olythos|Aejylinn "Jyl" Olythos]]** - The other woman; Rin's best friend, daughter of Lady Seraphine
- **Dead Courier** - One of the thugs killed in the ambush

## Grim Portents

*What happens if the party doesn't intervene:*

1. [ ] **Day 1-2**: Yannick notices courier is overdue; investigates
2. [ ] **Day 2-3**: Yannick learns of the ambush; realizes his people were the thugs
3. [ ] **Day 3-4**: Yannick discovers the party looted the bodies; begins watching them
4. [ ] **Day 4-5**: Yannick sends someone to search the party's quarters/ship for the letter
5. [ ] **Day 5-6**: If letter not found, Yannick escalates--direct approach or intimidation

## Impending Doom

Yannick recovers the letter by force or deception, and the party makes a dangerous enemy who now knows they're a threat. Alternatively: the party unknowingly reveals the letter's existence, and Yannick frames them for the extortion.

## Stakes Questions

- Will the party realize what they have?
- Will they use the letter themselves (blackmail), return it (to whom?), or destroy it?
- Will they encounter Isindrel and make the connection?
- Can they expose the ring, or will they become targets?

## Player Hooks

- **Investigating the coded note**: "Spring tide" and "no lader" are clues
- **The Spring Tide itself**: Gambling, social climbing, Isindrel is a regular
- **Lord Maevathar connection**: If they meet Rin and learn her surname
- **Direct approach from Yannick**: He may try to buy the letter back, claiming it's "sensitive correspondence"

## Resolution Triggers

- Letter destroyed (ring loses leverage, may not know who did it)
- Letter returned to Maevathar (grateful noble, potential ally)
- Letter used by party (they become blackmailers)
- Letter falls to Yannick (ring continues, party endangered)
- Ring exposed (constables involved, Yannick arrested or fled)

## Dramatic Irony

The party carries a letter addressed to "my dearest Jyl" and signed "Al"--intimate nicknames that provide a fig leaf of deniability. At The Spring Tide, they may meet:

- **Isindrel Maevathar** -- friendly, reckless, invites them to her card game
- **Aejylinn Olythos** -- her composed best friend, introduces herself formally

The party has no reason to connect "Aejylinn" with "Jyl" unless:
- Someone uses the nickname in conversation
- They notice Aejylinn's reaction to mentions of Rin's father

And no reason to connect "Al" with Lord Aldren Maevathar unless:
- They learn his first name (not commonly used--he's "Lord Maevathar" to most)
- They hear family or close associates use it

If they befriend both women before realizing the truth, the eventual revelation carries maximum moral weight. Who do they tell? Do they protect Rin from the truth? Confront Aejylinn? Use the information?
