**Location**: Thelonésë
**Status**: Active
**Parent Arc**: [[Commodore Thorne]]

## Situation

Harlan "Scar" Tomlin, an independent bounty hunter, recognized the party from wanted posters and ambushed them on Thelonésë's main street. The fight went badly for Harlan--he's now in jail with a collapsed lung, his surviving thug Donal Vess beside him. Flick is also facing charges and can't leave town until Magistrate Thornwood arrives in 4-6 days.

What the party doesn't know: Commodore Thorne has ordered his local informant, Orlan Thatch, to silence Harlan before he can be questioned. Not because Harlan works for Thorne--he doesn't--but because Harlan's photographic memory captured something from a past job that Thorne needs buried.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Harlan "Scar" Tomlin (Bounty Hunter)|Harlan "Scar" Tomlin]]** - Bounty hunter in custody, unknowingly marked for death
- **[[Donal "Dock Rat" Vess|Donal Vess]]** - Surviving thug, also in jail
- **[[Orlan Thatch]]** - Thorne's informant, tasked with silencing
- **[[Constable Captain Caelvorn Ashward]]** - Guards the prisoners
- **[[Circuit Magistrate Elara Thornwood|Magistrate Elara Thornwood]]** - Arrives in 4-6 days to rule on cases

## Grim Portents

*What happens if the party doesn't intervene:*

1. [ ] **Day 1-2**: Orlan Thatch learns Harlan survived and reports to his handler
2. [ ] **Day 2-3**: Orlan receives instructions to silence Harlan; begins planning
3. [ ] **Day 3-4**: Orlan bribes a guard or arranges "accident" (poisoned food, hired knife)
4. [ ] **Day 4-5**: Harlan dies in custody; ruled natural causes or suicide
5. [ ] **Day 5-6**: Magistrate arrives, rules on Flick's case without Harlan's testimony

## Impending Doom

Harlan dies. Whatever secret he carries about Thorne's operations dies with him. The party never learns that Thorne has informants in every port, and remains blind to the true scope of the threat against them.

## Stakes Questions

- Will the party discover the silencing plot in time?
- Can they turn Harlan to their side by warning him?
- What secret does Harlan's memory hold about Thorne?
- Will they expose Orlan, and if so, what are the consequences?

## Player Hooks

- **Visiting Harlan in jail**: Might notice nervousness in guards, or Harlan's paranoia
- **Investigating the ambush**: Following money/connections leads to Orlan
- **Befriending Donal Vess**: The traumatized survivor knows Harlan's habits
- **Watching the merchant**: Orlan's behavior will become suspicious as the deadline approaches

## Resolution Triggers

- Harlan dies (doom)
- Harlan survives to testify (partial resolution--what does he say?)
- Silencing plot exposed (Orlan arrested/fled, Thorne's network partially revealed)
- Party leaves Thelonésë before resolution (thread pauses, consequences follow later)
