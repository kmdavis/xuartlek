*Uncommon, Large, Animal*

A pack-hunting apex predator of old-growth jungle, adapted to the brief window of twilight when light fails but prey is still moving. Superficially feline, with mottled grey-green hide that makes it nearly invisible in dim light, forward-set eyes with vertical pupils, and six limbs -- four for locomotion, two vestigial forelimbs with prehensile claws used for gripping prey. Hunts to eat. Kills quickly.

The pack in the Thelonésë old-growth is a breeding pair with three juveniles, recently established after the logging pushed deep enough to disturb their previous territory.

**The Light Mechanic:** Both adults gain Photosensitive penalties in bright light (see below). Juveniles scatter entirely. If the party doesn't discover this through G'Teek's *light* cantrip, the Tax Collector's arrival (see end of file) provides an in-fiction save.

**Father's arrival:** Roll a d4 privately at session start. He arrives on that round (minimum round 1 for 6 players, minimum round 2 for 5, minimum round 3 for 4). If you want him absent entirely for 3 players, he was driven further from the den while hunting.

## Dusk Stalker (Mother) -- Creature 4

*Uncommon, Large, Animal*

The larger of the pair. Grabbed Torvin. Defensive of the den and the juveniles above all else.

**Perception** +11; darkvision, scent (imprecise) 40 ft

**Skills** Acrobatics +11, Athletics +13, Stealth +13 (+15 in dim light), Survival +9

**Str** +5, **Dex** +3, **Con** +4, **Int** −4, **Wis** +2, **Cha** −2

---

**AC** 21; **Fort** +13, **Ref** +11, **Will** +9

**HP** 65

---

### Traits

**Photosensitive** While in bright light, the Mother is dazzled and flat-footed. She loses Twilight Camouflage and her Stealth bonus from dim light. She will actively move away from a bright light source on her turn if able.

**Twilight Camouflage** While in dim light, the Mother is concealed. Creatures without darkvision or low-light vision must succeed at a DC 25 Perception check to detect her while motionless, DC 22 while moving.

**Den Mother** ⬲ **Trigger:** A creature within 30 feet deals damage to a Dusk Stalker Juvenile or grabs one; **Effect:** The Mother Strides up to her Speed toward the triggering creature and makes a Jaws Strike against it with a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll.

**Pinned Prey** *(Setup)* At the start of combat, Torvin Greaves is already Grabbed (see Grabbed condition). The Mother does not need to spend an action to maintain this Grab on her first turn. Torvin may attempt to Escape each round (Athletics or Acrobatics vs. DC 23 -- he needs an 18+ on the die; party should be creating openings for him).

---

### Speed 45 ft, climb 25 ft

**Jaws** ⬛ +13 (reach 10 ft, deadly 1d10); **2d8+8 piercing**; triggers Grab on a hit

**Claws** ⬛ +13 (agile); **1d10+8 slashing**

**Pounce** ⬛ The Mother Strides then Strikes with her Jaws. If she began this action undetected, the target is flat-footed against the Strike regardless of when it detects her.

**Grab** ⬲ **Trigger:** The Mother hits with her Jaws; **Effect:** She attempts to Grapple the target (Athletics +13 vs. target's Fortitude DC) as a free action. This doesn't count toward her MAP.

**Drag and Retreat** ⬛ *Requirements:* The Mother has a creature Grabbed; **Effect:** She Strides up to half her Speed (22 ft). The grabbed creature moves with her. She may move through difficult terrain without penalty during this movement.

---

### Tactics

**Round 1:** Already has Torvin grabbed near the back of the den. If the party enters with light, she recoils and uses Drag and Retreat to pull Torvin deeper. If they enter in darkness, she charges the nearest figure with Pounce.

**On damage:** Her first priority is always the juveniles. If one is threatened, Den Mother fires regardless of her other plans.

**Killing blow threshold:** If reduced below 20 HP, she releases Torvin and focuses entirely on escaping with the juveniles. She will not fight to the death if her pups can flee.

## Dusk Stalker (Father) -- Creature 3

*Uncommon, Large, Animal*

Rangier than the Mother, built for speed and distance hunting. He doesn't guard the den -- he feeds it. Returns when he hears combat sounds or the Mother's distress call (a subsonic rumble the party may feel in their chests before they hear it).

**Perception** +10; darkvision, scent (imprecise) 40 ft

**Skills** Acrobatics +10, Athletics +11, Stealth +12 (+14 in dim light), Survival +10

**Str** +4, **Dex** +3, **Con** +3, **Int** −4, **Wis** +3, **Cha** −2

---

**AC** 20; **Fort** +12, **Ref** +10, **Will** +9

**HP** 50

---

### Traits

**Photosensitive** As Mother.

**Twilight Camouflage** As Mother (DC 22 motionless, DC 19 moving).

**Pack Hunter** When the Father flanks a creature with the Mother or another ally, he deals an additional 1d6 precision damage on Strikes.

---

### Speed 50 ft, climb 25 ft

**Jaws** ⬛ +12 (reach 10 ft, deadly 1d8); **2d6+7 piercing**; triggers Grab

**Claws** ⬛ +12 (agile); **1d8+7 slashing**

**Pounce** ⬛ As Mother.

**Grab** ⬲ As Mother (Athletics +11 vs. target's Fortitude DC).

**Haul Off** ⬛ As Mother's Drag and Retreat. He attempts to drag a second victim away from the group.

---

### Tactics

**Arrival:** He enters from the direction opposite the party -- he came in from outside. His first action is always Pounce on the most isolated party member.

**Priority:** He targets whoever has light. A creature holding a torch or casting *light* is a threat he doesn't understand but instinctively wants to eliminate.

**Retreat trigger:** If the Mother flees, he follows immediately.

## Dusk Stalker Juvenile -- Creature 0

*Small, Animal*

Three in the den. Not yet old enough to hunt. They scatter from bright light, bite at ankles, and complicate the moral calculus of the fight.

**Perception** +5; darkvision, scent (imprecise) 20 ft

**Skills** Athletics +4, Stealth +6

**Str** +1, **Dex** +3, **Con** +1, **Int** −4, **Wis** +1, **Cha** −2

---

**AC** 15; **Fort** +5, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4

**HP** 12

---

### Traits

**Skittish** If a Juvenile takes damage while in bright light, it immediately Flees -- it must use all its actions to Stride away from the light source until it reaches dim light or darkness.

**Chaotic Presence** The Juvenile's space is difficult terrain for Medium or larger creatures.

---

### Speed 30 ft, climb 15 ft

**Nip** ⬛ +5; **1d4+1 piercing** (nuisance, not lethal)

**Snatch** ⬛ The Juvenile attempts to steal a held item from an adjacent creature (Thievery +6 vs. Perception DC). On a success, the item drops in the Juvenile's space. It will not carry it away -- it just grabs and releases.

---

### GM Notes on Juveniles

Don't use them as damage dealers. Use them as complications:
- One drags at Belegost's leg (difficult terrain, flavour)
- One gets between the party and the Mother (blocking charge lanes)
- One nearly gets stepped on -- does a player pull their strike?

Killing a Juvenile triggers Den Mother. Killing all three is the moral weight that lands after the fight -- the party walks out having orphaned something, or exterminated a family. Let that sit.

## Pender Holst, Revenue Clerk -- NPC Cameo

*Office of the Collector-General, Thelonath Principality*

A slight man in a road-stained coat, carrying a large leather satchel, a bullseye lantern, and an expression of profound administrative grievance. He has been trailing the party since Camp Six, where he learned their ship is registered at Thornhaven but has not filed a commercial transit declaration for this season's voyages.

He has no idea what a Dusk Stalker is. He is about to find out.

**HP** 18, **AC** 12, **Fort** +3, **Ref** +2, **Will** +5

**Speed** 25 ft

---

**Bullseye Lantern** Creates a 60-foot cone of bright light. When dropped or set down, creates a 20-foot radius of bright light. Both adults immediately suffer Photosensitive penalties within this area. *This is the "oh shit" lever.*

**Frantic Distraction** ⬛ *(once per encounter)* Pender waves his ledger and reads a tax citation at a creature within 30 feet. That creature is flat-footed until the end of its next turn. He does not understand why this works. Neither does the creature.

**Instinctive Recoil** ⬲ **Trigger:** Pender is attacked or takes damage; **Effect:** He drops whatever he's holding and Strides away from the attacker. *If he drops the lantern, it stays lit where it falls.*

**Bureaucratic Tenacity** When reduced to 0 HP, Pender drops prone and plays dead instead of falling unconscious. He must succeed at a DC 12 flat check each round to maintain this. The Dusk Stalkers do not eat things that aren't moving.

---

### Arrival Modes

**Party is handling it:** Pender crashes in noisily, immediately has a Juvenile latch onto his satchel. He drops his lantern trying to shake it off. Lantern hits the floor -- bright light floods the den. Adults recoil. It's in play.

**Party is struggling:** Pender trips at the den entrance. Lantern swings in a wide arc, casting bright light across half the den. Adults slam into the far wall. *"I-- I have papers here, for a Mister-- what in the--"*

**Party is about to wipe:** He's been hollering from outside for three rounds. They couldn't hear him over the fighting. He finally finds the entrance, holds the lantern up, strides in: *"I am looking for the crew of a vessel registered as the-- good lord what is that."* Both adults hit the den wall. Full freeze. His lantern is now the most important object in the room.

---

### After the Fight

Pender will absolutely still try to serve his transit paperwork. He will do this while Torvin is being carried out. He will apologize for the "inconvenient timing" while stepping over bones. He has a form for this.

His fee assessment: the party owes 3 GP 4 SP in unpaid commercial transit duties, plus a 2 GP late penalty. He is willing to waive the late penalty given the "exceptional circumstances" and will note in his report that they were "cooperative upon contact, if difficult to locate."
