## Contribution and absence

### Flick

**Brings.** Melee striker and sole party face. Only character trained in Thievery, and the only one in Performance.

**If absent.** **No Thievery at all** -- locks, traps and pockets go uncovered. Performance vanishes. Intimidation falls to +4. Stealth drops from +8 to +5.

### Belegost

**Brings.** Front-line striker and the party's Athletics muscle: Shove, Trip and Grapple.

**If absent.** Loses the best Athletics by +2 and a durable body in the front rank. Nobody else reliably controls position.

### Gripp

**Brings.** Chirurgeon alchemist -- healing, Crafting, and the only Society training. Backs up Deception and Diplomacy at +7.

**If absent.** Crafting and Society drop to nothing. If Gteek is also away there is **no healing whatsoever**.

### Gteek

**Brings.** Cloistered cleric: the only spellcaster, the only Religion training, best Perception (+8) and best Will (+10). Sentinel Dedication keeps him in armour.

**If absent.** **No spellcasting and no divine healing.** Religion goes uncovered, the party loses its best scout, and Will saves fall off sharply.

## Standing gaps

Missing whoever is at the table:

- **Ranged damage.** Espera and Vaelendil were both ranged; neither is playing. Gripp throws the occasional bomb but is built as a healer. Everyone else is melee.
- **Arcana.** Nobody trained. No identifying arcane items or wards.
- **Occultism.** Nobody trained. No reading curses or occult remains.

Arcana and Occultism are ordinary skills -- any class can train
them with a skill increase. A caster is only needed to *counteract*
curses and wards, not to identify them.
