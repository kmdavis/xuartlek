# Spirit Snare  *Item 8*

`Consumable`  `Electricity`  `Mechanical`  `Rare`  `Snare`  `Trap`

- **Price**: 90 gp

This complicated snare is affixed with various crystals and Stasian coils attached to strange electrical relays, working on the same principles as etheric [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/spirit-singer-incredible-handheld|spirit singers]]. When an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature enters its square, the device lets loose an ectoplasmic web that lashes around the creature's spectral form. The creature must succeed a DC 26 Reflex saving throw or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round. On a critical failure, the creature becomes immobilized for 1 minute. In either case, the incorporeal creature can attempt to Escape (DC 26).

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 80*
