# Octopus Potion (Moderate)  *Item 6+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Morph`  `Potion`  `Water`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Eight flailing octopus arms covered in suckers pop out from the sides of your body when you imbibe this potion. The arms share your multiple attack penalty and attempt to Grapple a random enemy within 15 feet of you. On a success, roll 1d4 to determine an additional effect of the arms, which lasts as long as the target remains grabbed or restrained by the arms. On subsequent turns, you can use a single action, which has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/attack|attack]] trait, to have the arms either Grapple a creature currently grappled or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Restrained|restrained]] (with no added effect) or release any creature they currently hold and repeat their initial effect. After 1 minute, the arms disappear and the potion's effects end.

**1d4****Added Effect** 1The arms cover the eyes; the target is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Blinded|blinded]]. 2The arms cover the mouth; the target can't speak. 3The arms wrap around the limbs; the flat check for the target to use a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] action while grabbed is DC 7 instead of DC 5. 4 The arms crush your enemy; the target takes bludgeoning damage equal to the potion's level immediately and at the end of each of its turns.
