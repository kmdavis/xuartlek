# Sure-Step Potion  *Item 4*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Price**: 17 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

The light-brown liquid of a _sure-step potion_ helps you find your footing. After drinking it, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Balance|Balance]] for 1 hour. In addition, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]] into [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], and you aren’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] on uneven ground.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 91*
