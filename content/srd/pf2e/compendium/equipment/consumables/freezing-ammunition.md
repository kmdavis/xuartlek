# Freezing Ammunition  *Item 5*

`Cold`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 25 gp
- **Ammunition**: any
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

This chilly ammunition is dark blue and cold to the touch. When activated _freezing ammunition_ hits a target, the target must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 for 1 round by the intense cold (slowed 1 for 1 minute on a critical failure).
