# Frenzied Quintessence  *Item 14*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Whetstone`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Contained within this small iron-bound glass sphere is a raging whirlwind of colors, each fighting for prominence. After a successful Strike with a weapon enhanced by _frenzied quintessence_, you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Quickened|quickened]] for 1 round. You can use the additional action only to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] toward a foe or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]].
