# Ghost Fowl Porridge  *Item 9*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 140 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Access**: Tian Xia Origin
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

This robust porridge—made from a cockatrice distilled into a broth, hot peppers from a demonic source, and various toppings—causes diners to utter a ghostly wail from the intense heat. Consuming the porridge grants you a +2 item bonus to saving throws against being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] for 1 hour. You also gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 3 against physical damage for the duration.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 105*
