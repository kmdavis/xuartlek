# Hunter's Bane  *Item 2*

`Consumable`  `Detection`  `Magical`  `Talisman`

- **Price**: 6 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]])
- **Trigger**: A [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]] enemy hits you with an attack
- **Requirement**: You're trained in Survivial.

This talisman is a ring of dried, interwoven pieces of straw. When you activate the _hunter's bane_, you sense the exact location of the attacker. It becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]] by you if it was [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] from you or becomes hidden from you if it was [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]]. If the attacker is behind lead, the _hunter's bane_ has no effect.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 305*
