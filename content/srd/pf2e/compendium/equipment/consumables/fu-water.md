# Fu Water  *Item 5*

`Consumable`  `Divine`  `Holy`  `Potion`

- **Price**: 21 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand

Burnt fulu ashes float in this liquid, lending a distinctive red color and imparting it with a variety of purifying effects. Unlike many fulu items, _fu water_ must be created using divine magic. 

**Activate—Bitter Medicine** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You drink the _fu water_ to counteract ailments within yourself. Attempt a counteract check with a +9 counteract modifier and a counteract rank of 3 against one effect that imposes the confused, fascinated, frightened, or stupefied condition. You’re then sickened 1. 

**Activate—Divine Bomb** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (splash) **Effect** This potion functions as a bottle of _holy water_. Like _holy water_, you throw it as a Strike as part of the activation. It’s a simple thrown weapon with a range increment of 20 feet. Unlike an alchemical bomb, it doesn’t add the manipulate trait to the attack made with it.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 221*
