# Glimmering Missive  *Item 4*

`Consumable`  `Light`  `Magical`  `Missive`

- **Price**: 20 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Activate**: [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

A _glimmering missive_ sparkles as you compose it. When activated, it explodes, disintegrating into a shower of multicolored motes in a 10-foot burst from a corner of the missive's space. Creatures in the area are covered in sparkling dust that remains luminous for 1 hour. Visible creatures can't be concealed while covered by the luminous dust; any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] while covered in the luminous dust, rather than being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]].

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 85*
