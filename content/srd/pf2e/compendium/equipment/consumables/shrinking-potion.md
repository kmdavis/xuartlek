# Shrinking Potion  *Item 4+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Onset**: 1 minute
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

This fungus-flavored potion conveys the effects of the _shrink_ spell to make you and all your gear smaller. After the onset, you remain small for 10 minutes.

### Shrinking Potion

**Price** 15 gp

### Shrinking Potion (Greater)

**Price** 90 gp

---

This potion has no onset, lasts for 1 hour, and grants the effects of a 4th-rank _shrink_ spell. In addition, you gain a +2 item bonus to Stealth checks while shrunken.

*Source: GM Core p. 261*
