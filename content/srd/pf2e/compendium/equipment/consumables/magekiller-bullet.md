# Magekiller Bullet  *Item 16*

`Consumable`  `Curse`  `Force`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 1,500 gp
- **Ammunition**: round or sling bullet
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

_Magekiller bullets_ are forged by carving a spellcaster’s bones with sigils of unbinding, grinding them to powder, and sealing the powder within the core of specially made rounds or sling bullets. When an activated _magekiller bullet_ hits a target, the ammunition burrows deep into its body and interferes with its connection to magic. Each time within the next week an affected target Casts a Spell, it must attempt a DC 11 flat check. On a failure, it takes 2d12 force damage. This curse can be counteracted with _cleanse affliction_ as normal, but if the ammunition is first removed from the target with a successful DC 35 Medicine check and an operation that takes 1 hour, the counteract check gains a +4 circumstance bonus.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 213*
