# Dischoran Rubble  *Item 4*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 18 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

Rock fragments exposed to any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/dischoran|dischoran's]] sonic blasts can sometimes retain a portion of the attack's energy, trembling slightly whenever they are touched. When you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/noise-blast|_noise blast_]] using a piece of _dischoran rubble_, the cacophony reverberates through the targets' forms for 1 round. If a creature that failed or critically failed its initial saving throw moves 10 feet or more on their next turn, it takes 2d10 additional sonic damage.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 118*
