# Stormfeather  *Item 8*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Talisman`

- **Price**: 100 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])
- **Requirements**: You’re an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]].

Even while affixed, this electric blue feather sways lightly in the air, as if always accompanied by a breeze. When activated, the talisman casts a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/fly|_fly_]]_ spell on you, though the duration is 1 minute. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] this activation. If you do, you’re affected by _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/gentle-landing|_gentle landing_]]_.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 225*
