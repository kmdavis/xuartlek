# Waterproofing Wax  *Item 3*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 10 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell

Many books and spellbooks are treated in this wax formula to protect them from the elements, but _waterproofing wax_’s liquid-repellent properties can be further applied to split up a __grease__ spell into useful, smaller sections. When the spell is cast in an area while using this catalyst, the conjured grease fills three 5-foot squares within 30 feet instead of its normal area; these squares don’t need to be contiguous.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 217*
