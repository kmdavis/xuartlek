# Bottled Melody  *Item 7*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Price**: 70 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

This potion is a melody in clear liquid form, with little musical notations swimming and darting through it like fish. You gain the Songburst action for 1 hour after you imbibe this concoction.

**Songburst** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (auditory, sonic) You vocalize a high-pitched melody that deals 4d6 sonic damage to all creatures in a 10-foot emanation (DC 23 basic Fortitude save). A creature that fails the saving throw is also deafened for 1 round (or 1 minute on a critical failure). After you use Songburst, you can’t do so again for 1d4 rounds.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 223*
