# Iron Cudgel  *Item 14*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Talisman`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to a melee weapon
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

This miniature club is typically affixed to a weapon by an iron chain. When you activate the cudgel, you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/archetype/brutal-finish|Brutal Finish]], as the fighter feat. You must meet the normal requirements, including those of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/press|press]] trait. 
If you have the Brutal Finish feat, add an additional weapon damage die on a success or a failure to the normal results.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 305*
