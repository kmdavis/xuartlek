# Chilled Fire Noodles  *Item 1+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Access**: Tian Xia Origin
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

These cool noodles are served with dark fermented sauces and vinegars before finally being tossed with spicy chili oil. When you consume the noodles, you temporarily ignore the -1 status penalty to AC and saving throws caused by the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Fatigued|fatigued]] condition for 1 hour.

### Chilled Fire Noodles

**Price** 3 gp

### Chilled Fire Noodles (Greater)
