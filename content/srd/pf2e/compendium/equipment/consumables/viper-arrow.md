# Viper Arrow  *Item 4*

`Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 17 gp
- **Ammunition**: arrow
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

The shaft of this arrow is covered in fine green scales, and its iron head comes to a pair of points almost like fangs. After an activated viper arrow hits a target, the arrow transforms into a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/viper|viper]]. The target is affected by the viper's venom, as if it had been bitten. The viper then lands in an open space adjacent to the target. 
The viper has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/summoned|summoned]] trait and acts at the end of your turn, even though you didn't use the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] action. It's under the GM's control, but it generally attacks the creature the arrow struck. The viper vanishes after 1 minute or when slain.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/summon-animal|_summon animal_]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 302*
