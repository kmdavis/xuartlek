# Potion of Resistance (Moderate)  *Item 6+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Drinking this thick, fortifying potion grants resistance against a single damage type for 1 hour. Each potion of resistance is created to defend against acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage (and is called a _lesser potion of fire resistance_ or the like).

### Potion of Resistance (Lesser)

**Price** 45 gp

---

You gain resistance 5 to the appropriate energy type

### Potion of Resistance (Moderate)

**Price** 180 gp

---

You gain resistance 10 to the appropriate energy type.

### Potion of Resistance (Greater)

**Price** 850 gp

---

You gain resistance 15 to the appropriate energy type.
