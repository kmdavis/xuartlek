# Greater Spirit-sealing Fulu  *Item 3+*

`Consumable`  `Fulu`  `Incapacitation`  `Magical`

- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Usage**: affixed to one undead creature

This classic fulu seals the void energy within an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]], freezing it in its tracks. Upon affixing, the target undead must attempt a DC 17 Will save or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round.

### Spirit-sealing Fulu

**Price** 10 gp

### Greater Spirit-sealing Fulu

**Price** 155 gp

---

The DC is 27. On a critical failure, the undead is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 4 rounds. At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt a new Will save to reduce the remaining duration by 1 round or end it entirely on a critical success.
