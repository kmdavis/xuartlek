# Oil of Revelation  *Item 5*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Oil`

- **Price**: 25 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

This bright oil, first created by humans as a tool to help them fight in darkness, holds flecks of tiny gemstones in suspension and smells like a struck matchstick. 
The first time a weapon coated with this oil damages a creature, the wound glows with light for 1 minute. If the creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]], the light's position means it's merely [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] to creatures that would otherwise be unable to see it, rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]]. The light also negates the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] condition due to lighting conditions. If the coated weapon doesn't damage a creature within 1 hour, the oil sloughs off and loses its power.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 303*
