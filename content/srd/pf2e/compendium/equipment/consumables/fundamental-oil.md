# Fundamental Oil  *Item 11*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Oil`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 220 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Made of elemental salts and essences from the Inner Sphere or where such planes leak onto [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#The Universe|the Universe]], _fundamental oil_ is anathema to elementals and other creatures with a weakness to elemental damage. A weapon anointed with this oil acts as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/consumables/bane-oil|_bane oil_]] to elementals, but the damage type is the same as the target’s greatest weakness if the target has [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|weakness]] to acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. If it has none of these, the additional damage is the same type as the weapon’s damage type. These effects last 1 minute.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 86*
