# Wolliped Fleece  *Item 1*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 4 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

A small bundle of _wolliped fleece_ contains the memory of winter snows. Adding this catalyst to a casting of _chilling spray_ coats all squares within the area of effect with a thin layer of ice. These squares become difficult terrain until the beginning of your next turn.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 119*
