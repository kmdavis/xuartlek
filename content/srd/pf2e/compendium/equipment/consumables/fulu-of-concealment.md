# Fulu Of Concealment  *Item 6*

`Consumable`  `Fulu`  `Illusion`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 40 gp
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Usage**: affixed to the ground in four spaces along a 10-foot radius circle

This fulu comes in four pieces, one placed in each cardinal direction. Choose one of the following traits when Activating the fulus: animal, beast, celestial, fey, fiend, humanoid, monitor, plant, or undead. Those within the fulus’ circle upon activation (including the fulus themselves) gain the effects of __shared invisibility__, except that the area is the fulus’ circle instead of an emanation and the invisibility is only effective against creatures with the chosen trait. If any of the fulus are moved or destroyed after activation, the effect ends.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 218*
