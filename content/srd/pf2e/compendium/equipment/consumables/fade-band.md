# Fade Band  *Item 12*

`Consumable`  `Illusion`  `Magical`  `Talisman`

- **Price**: 320 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate)
- **Trigger**: An attack misses you
- **Requirement**: You are trained in Stealth

This thin, silvery wire wraps around your armor. When you activate the band, it casts a 2nd-rank _invisibility_ spell on you.
