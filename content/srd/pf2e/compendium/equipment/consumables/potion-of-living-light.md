# Potion Of Living Light  *Item 10*

`Consumable`  `Light`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Price**: 200 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

This glass vial has a dusky purple liquid with a small orb of light suspended in it. It sheds bright light in a 5-foot radius. Upon drinking the potion, your skin sheds an aura of bright light in a 20-foot emanation (and dim light for another 20 feet), even through your clothing and armor, for 1 hour. While glowing, you gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against darkness and shadow effects. You can Dismiss this effect, causing a flash of light that causes every creature in a 20-foot emanation to be blinded for 1 round unless they succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 223*
