# Steel Cloud Road  *Item 7*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 60 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

This carved iron cloud appears unfinished and full of spurs, blemishes, and other imperfections. When used as a catalyst to cast a __weapon storm__ spell, the caster’s weapon propels them forward. Before choosing the spell’s area, Stride up to twice your Speed. This movement must be in a straight line and doesn’t trigger reactions. The spell is then cast from your new location.
