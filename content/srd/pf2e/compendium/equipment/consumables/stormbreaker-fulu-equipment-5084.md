# Stormbreaker Fulu  *Item 16*

`Consumable`  `Fulu`  `Magical`  `Talisman`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 1,900 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]])
- **Trigger**: You would be forced to move, you would be teleported, or you would take cold, electricity, or sonic damage.

This unassuming paper tag is inscribed with magical symbols. When you activate the fulu, it vanishes in a wisp of cloud, and you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 15 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]] damage until the end of your next turn. For that duration, you [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore difficult terrain]] from wind and weather, and you can’t be forcibly moved or teleported unless the effect [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteracts]] the fulu (DC 40). These protections apply against the triggering effect.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 221*
