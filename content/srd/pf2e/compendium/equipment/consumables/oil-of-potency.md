# Oil of Potency  *Item 2+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Oil`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

When you apply this thick, viscous oil to a weapon or suit of armor, that item immediately becomes magically potent. If the item is a weapon, it temporarily becomes a _+1 striking_ weapon, or, if it's armor, it temporarily becomes +1 resilient armor. This lasts for 1 minute.

### Oil of Potency

**Price** 7 gp

### Oil of Potency (Greater)

**Price** 400 gp

---

The weapon becomes a +2 greater striking weapon or armor becomes +2 greater resilient armor.

### Oil of Potency (Major)

**Price** 8,000 gp

---

The weapon becomes a +3 major striking weapon or armor becomes +3 major resilient armor.
