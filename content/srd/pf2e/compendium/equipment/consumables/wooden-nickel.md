# Wooden Nickel  *Item 9*

`Consumable`  `Illusion`  `Magical`  `Talisman`  `Visual`

- **Price**: 130 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]])
- **Requirement**: You are a master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]].

This rough wooden coin hangs from a string drilled through a hole at its center. When you Activate the _wooden nickel_, for 10 minutes, you can cause any object you touch of 2 Bulk or less to look, feel, and smell like valuables of a similar size crafted from a precious metal of your choice.

 A creature that touches or Interacts with an affected object can attempt a Perception check against your Deception DC to disbelieve the illusion, and a successful check against your Deception DC with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] or an appropriate [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Lore]] skill will also reveal the item's true nature. After 10 minutes have elapsed since you Activated the _wooden nickel_, all affected objects revert to their true form.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 95*
