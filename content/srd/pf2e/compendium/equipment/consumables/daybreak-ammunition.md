# Daybreak Ammunition  *Item 3*

`Consumable`  `Light`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 10 gp
- **Ammunition**: any
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

The bronze inlays of _daybreak ammunition_ glow noticeably, even under direct sunlight. When activated _daybreak ammunition_ hits a target, the missile flares with an aura of bright light in a 10-foot emanation from the target for 1 round. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 18 Fortitude save. Creatures with darkvision take a –2 circumstance penalty to this save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is dazzled for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The target is blinded for 1 round.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 212*
