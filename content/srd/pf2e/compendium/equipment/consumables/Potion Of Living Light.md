# Potion of Living Light  *Item 10*

`Consumable`  `Light`  `Magical`  `Potion`

- **Price**: 200 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

This glass vial has a dusky purple liquid with a small orb of light suspended in it. It sheds [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 5-foot radius. Upon drinking the potion, your skin sheds an aura of bright light in a 20-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Emanation|emanation]] (and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Dim Light|dim light]] for another 20 feet), even through your clothing and armor, for 1 hour. While glowing, you gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Darkness|darkness]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Shadow|shadow]] effects. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] this effect, causing a flash of light that causes every creature in a 20-foot emanation to be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round unless they succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 223*
