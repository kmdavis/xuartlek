# Gravebane Ammunition  *Item 4+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Ammunition**: any
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

First made to deal with a wicked necromancer’s encroaching horde, _gravebane ammunition_ is crafted with materials soaked in blessed waters. On a successful Strike, the attack deals an additional 1d10 vitality damage to creatures with void healing. If the target takes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] damage, it must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]] 1 (or enfeebled 2 on a critical failure) until the start of your next turn.

**Price** 16 gp
