# Wormhole Shot  *Item 2*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 7 gp
- **Ammunition**: arrow, bolt
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

The shaft of a _wormhole shot_ gleams like polished ebony and seems to be not quite straight; nevertheless, this ammunition flies true. When an activated _wormhole shot_ hits a target, which can be an unoccupied square, it embeds itself and remains active for up to 1 minute. A creature can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] to dislodge the ammunition, but it remains active unless the creature spends another Interact action to destroy it. Once during the _wormhole shot_’s duration, if you’re within 60 feet of it, you can use it as the point of origin for a spell or ranged attack, calculating range and cover from whatever space the _wormhole shot_ is in. After being used this way, it quickly dissolves into black dust.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 213*
