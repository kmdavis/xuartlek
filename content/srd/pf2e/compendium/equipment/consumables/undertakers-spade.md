# Undertaker's Spade  *Item 9*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 125 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell

Rites of rest are etched deep into the broken-off blade of a deceased gravekeeper’s shovel. When used as a catalyst to cast an _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/impaling-spike|_impaling spike_]]_ spell, the blade disintegrates into dust, mixing with the earth and allowing the spell to damage [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures as if it were a weapon imbued with a _ghost touch_ property rune. An incorporeal creature can be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] by a spike if it’s adjacent to a solid surface when struck; it can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]] as normal but can’t phase through the spike or escape to the Ethereal Plane.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 217*
