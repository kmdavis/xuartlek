# Hippogriff Feather  *Item 3*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 12 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell

Tawny-colored hippogriff feathers can be up to 2 feet long. Used as a catalyst with a _ghostly carrier_ spell, a single _hippogriff feather_ grants the hand semicorporeal wings that increase the hand's maneuverability. The hand has a range of only 60 feet, but its increased agility grants it a +1 status bonus to its AC and Reflex saves.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 118*
