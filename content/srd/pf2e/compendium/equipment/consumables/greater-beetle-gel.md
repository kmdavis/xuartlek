# Greater Beetle Gel  *Item 2+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Oil`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

This midnight-blue gel shimmers with rainbow hues when exposed to light. When you apply this gel on an unattended and non-magical object of 1 Bulk, the object grows iridescent insect wings and transforms into a flash beetle, keeping some of the same colors and patterns of the original item. This false beetle has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/minion|minion]] trait. It remains in this beetle form for 10 minutes before the wings fall off and it returns to normal. If the beetle is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the item returns to its original form, undamaged.

### Beetle Gel

**Price** 10 gp

### Greater Beetle Gel

**Price** 130 gp
