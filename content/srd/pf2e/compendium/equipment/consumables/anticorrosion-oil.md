# Anticorrosion Oil  *Item 2*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Oil`

- **Price**: 6 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

You can coat objects of 6 Bulk or less with anticorrosion oil. For 24 hours, the object takes half damage from acid and from all effects that specifically cause it to rust or corrode, such as contact with an [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/beast/ore-louse|ore louse’s]] saliva.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 86*
