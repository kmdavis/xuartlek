# Skyfisher Vapors  *Item 5*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 25 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]]

_Skyfisher vapors_ are only visible via their effect on a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/toxic-cloud|_toxic cloud_]] spell, or apparent lack thereof. These gases cause the conjured cloud to be as transparent as a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/howl-of-the-wild/animal/sky-fisher|sky fisher]]. The cloud is invisible, meaning it does not provide [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealment]], but it requires a successful [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Perception|Perception]] check against your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Spell DC|spell DC]] to notice the cloud by faint distortions in the air. Your magical connection to the cloud means you always know where it is. This has no effect on its damage.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 119*
