# Resonating Ammunition  *Item 11*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Sonic`

- **Price**: 300 gp
- **Ammunition**: arrow, bolt
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

The end of this ammunition is a metallic tuning fork with magical etchings. When an activated piece of _resonating ammunition_ hits its target, the tuning fork resonates with residual energy from the shot, transforming it into dangerous sound waves. This deals 5d10 sonic damage to the target and each creature within a 10-foot emanation (DC 28 basic Fortitude save).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 213*
