# Imp Shot  *Item 5*

`Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 24 gp
- **Ammunition**: any
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]])

This black-and-red ammunition contains an egg-shaped capsule. When an activated _imp shot_ hits, the capsule cracks open and releases a manifestation that resembles a Tiny imp that can't act in any way or provide benefits outside those described here. If the Strike misses the target, the imp appears, makes a rude gesture at you, and vanishes in a puff of sulfuric smoke. On a hit, though, the imp harries the target for up to 1 minute, remaining in the target's space, slapping, nipping, hurling insults, and moving with the target as it moves. A creature harried by the imp is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls and skill checks. At the start of your turn on each round while the imp is active, you must attempt a DC 11 flat check. On a failure, the imp makes a final vulgar gesture at the target and vanishes in a cloud of brimstone.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 82*
