# Deep Pockets  *Item 6*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 220 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Armor|Leather Armor]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Armor|leather armor]]_ has two medium-sized pockets just above the waist where you might normally place your hands if they were cold. Each pocket is covered with a leather flap that surprisingly remains closed even with dynamic movement and heavy winds. Each individual pocket functions as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/held-items/spacious-pouch-type-iv|_type I spacious pouch_]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include two _type I spacious pouches_.
