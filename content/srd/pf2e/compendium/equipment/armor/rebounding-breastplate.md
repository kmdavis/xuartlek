# Rebounding Breastplate  *Item 20*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 70,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Breastplate|Breastplate]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+3 greater resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/fortification-greater|greater fortification]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Breastplate|breastplate]]_ absorbs impacts for later release. While wearing the armor, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 5 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage and resistance 10 to force damage. Keep track of how much damage the armor prevents from enemy attacks, as the armor absorbs that damage. After 1 minute, the absorbed damage disperses harmlessly and resets to 0. Only damage caused by foes or hazards powers the armor, not damage you take from yourself, allies, or the environment. 

**Activate—Kinetic Pulse** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** Your armor has absorbed at least 30 damage; **Effect** The absorbed energy explodes from your armor in a pulse of force. Creatures in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] take 12d6 force damage with a DC 43 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failure, they’re also pushed 10 feet away from you; on a critical failure, they’re instead pushed 20 feet away from you and fall [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]]. The armor’s absorbed damage resets to 0.
