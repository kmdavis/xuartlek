# Thunder Mail  *Item 15*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 6,500 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Armor**: Chain Mail

This _+2 greater resilient greater electricity-resistant chain mail_ is adorned with two golden thunderbolts, one along each forearm. Even on a seemingly cloudless day, you can raise your arms to the heavens and draw down a bolt of lightning upon yourself, empowering your armor and blasting your enemies.

**Activate—Call Down Lightning** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, electricity, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You conjure a bolt of electricity down onto yourself, dealing 6d6 electricity damage to all creatures in a 10-foot emanation. Additionally, for the next minute, whenever a creature touches you or hits you with a melee unarmed attack or a non-reach melee weapon attack, it takes 1d8 electricity damage.
