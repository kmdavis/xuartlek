# Deep Sea Plate  *Item 8*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 500 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Armor**: Full Plate

The interior of this heavy, brass _+1 full plate_ is lined with waterproof fabric, especially covering the seams between plates. When worn, it provides a sealed environment that protects you from drowning as well as allowing you to move more freely while underwater.

**Activate—Deep Dive** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _deep sea plate_ enables you to breathe underwater and gives you a swim Speed equal to half your land Speed while in depths of 500 feet or less for 1 hour.
