# Energy Shield Tunic  *Item 15+*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: worn garment
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: Explorer's Clothing

This sleek, color-changing tunic has a higher thread count than most garments. It’s a _+2 greater resilient explorer’s clothing_ that helps the wearer survive powerful energy magic. During your daily preparations, you choose one of the following energy types: acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic. You gain resistance 10 to the chosen energy type until your next daily preparations. The tunic changes color and gains thematic embroidery to reflect the energy type you’ve chosen. • **Acid** The tunic turns brown and has acid splashes embroidered in bright green thread. • **Cold** The tunic turns sky blue and has snowflakes embroidered in silver thread. • **Electricity** The tunic turns dark gray and has lightning bolts embroidered in light blue thread. • **Fire** The tunic turns orange and has balls of fire embroidered in red and yellow threads. • **Sonic** The tunic turns white and has musical notes embroidered in golden thread. 

**Activate—Shielding Command** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (fortune, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against a spell that deals acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage, but you haven’t rolled yet; **Effect** Roll the triggering saving throw twice and take the better result.
