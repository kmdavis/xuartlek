# Chained Mist  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 3, 000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Chain Shirt|Chain Shirt]]

Made of standard-grade dawnsilver, mist spirals around this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/Magic Armor|+1 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Shadow|greater shadow]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_. When worn, silver vapor trails interwoven with wispy threads of shadow bedeck and move in harmony with the wearer’s limbs and torso. If the wearer is in [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Dim Light|dim light]], the mist obscures the wearer’s form, granting the wearer the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Concealed|concealed]] condition. 

**Activate—Misty Mesh** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The mist immediately solidifies into a protective mesh of silvery chains that gives you [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Spirit|spirit]] damage for 1 minute.
