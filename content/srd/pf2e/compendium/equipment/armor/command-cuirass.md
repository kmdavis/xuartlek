# Command Cuirass  *Item 8*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 500 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 3
- **Base Armor**: Half Plate

This _+1 half plate_ is imbued with a series of runes designed to amplify your voice on the battlefield. You are more readily able to command and motivate the troops, even over the din of battle.

**Activate—Motivate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** For 1 minute, the volume of your voice is temporarily enhanced, and you gain a +1 status bonus to Diplomacy and Intimidation checks.
