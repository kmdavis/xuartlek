# Eagle Wing  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 950 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Armor|Leather Armor]]

Long black feathers cover the leather pieces of this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+1 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Armor|leather armor]]_. The armor gives you the ability to glide safely to earth from high above the battlefield. It also grants you a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks you attempt while in the air.

**Activate—Soar** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Effect** You glide slowly toward the ground, 5 feet down and 30 feet forward through the air. Provided you spend at least 1 action gliding on your turn and haven’t yet reached the ground, you remain in the air at the end of your turn. Otherwise, you fall.
