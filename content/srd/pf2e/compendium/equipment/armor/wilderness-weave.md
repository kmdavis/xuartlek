# Wilderness Weave  *Item 5*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 160 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Padded Armor|Padded Armor]]
