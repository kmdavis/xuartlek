# Chained Mist  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 3,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Shirt|Chain Shirt]]

Made of standard-grade dawnsilver, mist spirals around this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+1 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/shadow-major|greater shadow]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/dawnsilver-object-high-grade|dawnsilver]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_. When worn, silver vapor trails interwoven with wispy threads of shadow bedeck and move in harmony with the wearer’s limbs and torso. If the wearer is in [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Dim Light|dim light]], the mist obscures the wearer’s form, granting the wearer the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] condition.
