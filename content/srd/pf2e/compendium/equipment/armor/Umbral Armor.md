# Umbral Armor  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 1, 000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Studded Leather Armor|Studded Leather Armor]]

This dark _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/Magic Armor|+1 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Shadow|shadow]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Studded Leather Armor|studded leather armor]]_ is frequently used to bypass enemy positions or quickly travel around battlefield obstacles as it allows you to partially transit through [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/Planes#The Netherworld|the Netherworld]].

**Activate—Shroud Stride** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Shadow|shadow]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You slip into the Netherworld for a moment, allowing you to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] twice your speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions and ignores [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult]] and [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Running Exploration#Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]. Shadows lingers on the armor, causing you to become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Concealed|concealed]] for 1 round afterward.
