# Reactive Mail  *Item 5+*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Armor**: Chain Mail

Often heavily battle scared from use, this chain mail is commonly issued to frontline soldiers who are tasked with battling through enemies to reach high-value targets across the battlefield. 

**Activate—Quick Spike** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate, metal) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature within 15 feet uses a manipulate action; **Effect** An iron spike launches from your armor at the target, dealing 2d8 piercing damage to the target (DC 20 basic Reflex save). If the target critically fails, the triggering action is disrupted.

### Reactive Mail

**Price** 160 gp

### Greater Reactive Mail

**Price** 1,400 gp

---

The armor is _+1 resilient chain mail_. The damage increases to 5d8 and the DC increases to 28.

### Major Reactive Mail

**Price** 14,000 gp
