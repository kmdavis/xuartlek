# Reactive Mail  *Item 5+*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Mail|Chain Mail]]

Often heavily battle scared from use, this [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Mail|chain mail]] is commonly issued to frontline soldiers who are tasked with battling through enemies to reach high-value targets across the battlefield. 

**Activate—Quick Spike** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/metal|metal]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature within 15 feet uses a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] action; **Effect** An iron spike launches from your armor at the target, dealing 2d8 piercing damage to the target (DC 20 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save). If the target critically fails, the triggering action is disrupted.
