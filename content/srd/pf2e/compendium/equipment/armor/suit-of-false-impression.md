# Suit Of False Impression  *Item 6*

`Illusion`  `Invested`  `Magical`  `Visual`

- **Price**: 200 gp
- **Usage**: worn garment
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Explorer's Clothing|Explorer's Clothing]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/armor-potency-3|+1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Explorer's Clothing|explorer’s clothing]]_ is a simple, practical, and unassuming two-piece outfit. The comfortable fabric is enchanted with illusory magic to make it look as if the wearer is wearing heavy armor (as _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/illusory-disguise|_illusory disguise_]]_). While wearing the suit, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Impersonate|Impersonate]] someone who would normally wear heavy armor. 

**Activate—Faux Bulwark** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/emotion|emotion]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You strike an intimidating pose that causes enemies to fear your supposedly heavily armored form. Each foe in a 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] who can see you must attempt a DC 22 Will saving throw. On a failure, they’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).
