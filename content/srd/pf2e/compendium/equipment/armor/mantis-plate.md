# Mantis Plate  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 2,800 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Armor**: Full Plate

The beautiful plates of this _+2 resilient greater ready full plate_ armor are shaped from the carapace of a deadly mantis, providing excellent protection with added flexibility. When wearing a suit of _mantis plate_, you gain a +2 item bonus to your Athletics DC.

**Activate—Mandible Vambraces** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Effect** Make a melee Strike. If you succeed, the target is caught by the spikes on the armor’s forearms, taking an additional 6d6 piercing damage (DC 30 basic Reflex save).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the arms and carapace of a deadly mantis.
