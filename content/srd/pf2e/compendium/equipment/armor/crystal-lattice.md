# Crystal Lattice  *Item 12*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,900 gp
- **Usage**: worn garment
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Explorer's Clothing|Explorer's Clothing]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+2 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/raiment|raiment]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Explorer's Clothing|explorer’s clothing]]_ is a longline vest made of a flexible, durable lattice of many fine crystals. A wide variety of these vests exist, and their colors depend on the type of crystal that forms the lattice. The crystals glow briefly when an enemy hits the wearer. 

**Activate—Crystal Exoskeleton** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The crystals on your vest grow and extend, becoming an architecturally sculpted exoskeleton that covers your form. You gain 20 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 hour. The exoskeleton crumbles into dust when the temporary Hit Points are depleted or when the duration ends, and the crystals return to their original state.
