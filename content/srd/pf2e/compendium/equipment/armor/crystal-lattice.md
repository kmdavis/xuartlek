# Crystal Lattice  *Item 12*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,900 gp
- **Usage**: worn garment
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: Explorer's Clothing

This _+2 resilient raiment explorer’s clothing_ is a longline vest made of a flexible, durable lattice of many fine crystals. A wide variety of these vests exist, and their colors depend on the type of crystal that forms the lattice. The crystals glow briefly when an enemy hits the wearer. 

**Activate—Crystal Exoskeleton** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The crystals on your vest grow and extend, becoming an architecturally sculpted exoskeleton that covers your form. You gain 20 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 hour. The exoskeleton crumbles into dust when the temporary Hit Points are depleted or when the duration ends, and the crystals return to their original state.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 202*
