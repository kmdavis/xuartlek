# Bismuth Armor  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 2,800 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Armor**: Full Plate

This silvery, pink-tinged _+2 resilient full plate_ is polished to the point that its surface is completely reflective. This armor can reflect some of the light generated by magical attacks.

**Activate—Reflect Shine** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (arcane, light, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature targets you with a magical effect that has the attack trait; **Effect** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the spell reflects toward the caster, who becomes dazzled for 1 round.
