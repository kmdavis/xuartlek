# Bismuth Armor  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 2,800 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Full Plate|Full Plate]]

This silvery, pink-tinged _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+2 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Full Plate|full plate]]_ is polished to the point that its surface is completely reflective. If the attack misses, the spell reflects toward the caster, who becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.
