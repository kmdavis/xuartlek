# Invisible Chain Shirt  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 2,750 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Shirt|Chain Shirt]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor/magic-armor-3-major-resilient|+2 resilient]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/invisibility-greater|invisibility]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ is itself [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]]. Other creatures can’t see it at all, allowing you to wear it surreptitiously. Additionally, the armor’s invisible composition is quieter and more comfortable than a normal chain shirt. It loses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/noisy|noisy]] trait and gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/comfort|comfort]] trait. 

**Activate—Obscured Approach** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] and turn partially [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]], becoming [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] until the end of your next turn or until you use a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/hostile-actions|hostile action]], whichever comes first. As normal, you can use the concealment from this activation to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]].
