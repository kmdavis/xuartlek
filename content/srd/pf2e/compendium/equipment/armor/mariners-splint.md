# Mariner's Splint  *Item 5*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 180 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 3
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Splint Mail|Splint Mail]]

This suit of _+1 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Splint Mail|splint mail]]_ is worn by veteran sailors aboard warships who want as much protection as possible but still need to remain mobile enough to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|climb]] up rigging or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|swim]] should the need arise. The plates are arranged for maximum flexibility and the undercoat of padded armor is often no more than a cotton shrift. The armor grants you a +1 item bonus to Athletics checks to Climb or Swim and increases the distance you move when you successfully [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] by 5 feet.
