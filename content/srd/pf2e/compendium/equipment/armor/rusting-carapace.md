# Rusting Carapace  *Item 8*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 476 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Lamellar|Leather Lamellar]]

This [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/armor-potency-3|_+1_]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Leather Lamellar|_leather lamellar_]] _armor_ incorporates the plates of an [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/beast/ore-louse|ore louse’s]] hide, woven together with the creature’s own sinew into a functional set of armor. While wearing the _rusting carapace_, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against any effect that would rust you or your items, such as the ore louse’s Strikes that have the rust ability or the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/rust-cloud|_rust cloud_]] spell.

**Activate** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Oxidizing liquid seeps from between the segments of the rusting hide onto a nearby metal creature, non-magical metal hazard, or non-magical metal item of up to 1 Bulk. A targeted creature or hazard gets a DC 24 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/defenses#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save; a targeted item automatically gets a critical failure if unattended, but a creature holding or wearing it can attempt a DC 24 basic Reflex save. The rust deals 8d6 damage to a creature or hazard, plus 2d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent damage]] on a failed save; it deals 2d6 damage to an item, plus 1d6 persistent damage on a failed save.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide of a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/beast/ore-louse|ore louse]].

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 19*
