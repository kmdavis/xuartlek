# Lamentation of the Faithless  *Item 25*

`Artifact`  `Divine`  `Magical`  `Unholy`  `Unique`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/greatsword|Greatsword]]

The weapon wielded in battle by Szuriel, Rider of War, is a perpetually blood-covered blade that has taken more lives than even the gods can count. _Lamentation of the Faithless_ is a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/mythic-weapon-potency|+4]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|major striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/quickstrike|speed]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/unholy|unholy]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/greatsword|greatsword]]_ with a blade of unyielding jet-black steel that absorbs all incident light, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteracting]] all light effects in a 15-foot radius with a +37 modifier as long as the weapon is unsheathed. Any creature within range attempting to use a spell or ability with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/light|light]] trait must first succeed at a DC 15 flat check, or the attempt fails. Whenever the blade strikes and deals damage to a living creature, its wielder regains 1d12 Hit Points.

**Activate—Lo and Behold** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You raise _Lamentation of the Faithless_ high above the battlefield, presenting its terrible glory for all to witness. All creatures within 60 feet that are able to see the sword must attempt a DC 50 Will save. Creatures with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] trait are immune to this effect, but creatures with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] trait take a –2 status penalty on this save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is stricken by crushing despair. It becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 as it sobs uncontrollably, and it can’t use reactions while it’s slowed.

**Failure** As success, but the creature is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the duration of the blinded condition is 1 minute.

**Destruction** _Lamentation of the Faithless_ can only be destroyed if it’s driven into the ground within the walls of the secluded garden at the summit of Heaven’s mountain by a risen, repentant [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiend]], whereupon it shatters and crumbles to dust.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 128*
