# Flawless Celestial Shawl  *Item 9*

`Artifact`  `Divine`  `Invested`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: worn garment

Each _flawless celestial shawl_ functions only for a specific tennin and is the culmination of their immortal cultivation. The sash is made of celestial silken threads stronger than adamantine yet lighter than gossamer. The tennin can draw thread from the _flawless celestial shawl_ to serve as a sterling artisan’s toolkit and workshop to Craft. The sash can be used to work with precious materials of any grade. On any Crafting check the tennin attempts using the shawl, they use the outcome one degree of success better than the result of their roll.

**Activate—Reweave** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ; **Effect** The tennin uses the sash to Repair an item. This Repair activity loses the exploration trait.
