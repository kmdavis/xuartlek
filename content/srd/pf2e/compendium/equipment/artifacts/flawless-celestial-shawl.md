# Flawless Celestial Shawl  *Item 9*

`Artifact`  `Divine`  `Invested`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: worn garment

Each _flawless celestial shawl_ functions only for a specific tennin and is the culmination of their immortal cultivation. The sash is made of celestial silken threads stronger than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/adamantine-object-high-grade|adamantine]] yet lighter than gossamer. The tennin can draw thread from the _flawless celestial shawl_ to serve as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/artisans-toolkit-sterling|sterling artisan’s toolkit]] and workshop to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]]. The sash can be used to work with [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/materials#Precious Materials|precious materials]] of any grade. On any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] check the tennin attempts using the shawl, they use the outcome one degree of success better than the result of their roll.

**Activate—Reweave** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ; **Effect** The tennin uses the sash to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] an item. This Repair activity loses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/exploration|exploration]] trait.
