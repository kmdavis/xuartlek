# Titan's Standard  *Item 18*

`Aura`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 24,000 gp
- **Usage**: affixed or held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This magical banner stands largest on any battlefield. While holding a _titan’s standard_, you can use the following ability.

**Activate—Titan’s Stature** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** The magical banner causes a rapid surge of growth. A Medium or smaller ally within the banner’s aura becomes Large for 1 round. Its equipment grows with it but returns to its natural size afterwards. While Large, the ally is clumsy 1, and its reach increases by 5 feet (or by 10 feet if it started out Tiny).
