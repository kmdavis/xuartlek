# Stalwart's Banner  *Item 7+*

`Aura`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: affixed or held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This magical banner mimics the rich green of summer grass. While holding a _stalwart’s banner_, you can use the following ability.

**Activate—Stand Firm** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You and allies within your banner’s aura gain 5 temporary Hit Points and a +1 status bonus to your Fortitude DC and Reflex DC against any effect that would move you or knock you prone. These effects last for 1 round.

### Stalwart’s Banner

**Price** 360 gp

### Greater Stalwart’s Banner

**Price** 2,000 gp

---

The temporary Hit Points gained increase to 10, and the status bonus increases to +2.

### Major Stalwart’s Banner

**Price** 15,000 gp

---

The temporary Hit Points gained increase to 15, and the status bonus increases to +3.
