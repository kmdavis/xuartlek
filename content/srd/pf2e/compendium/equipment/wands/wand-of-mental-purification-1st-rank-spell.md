# Wand of Mental Purification (1st-Rank Spell)  *Item 4+*

`Emotion`  `Healing`  `Magical`  `Mental`  `Wand`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

Red feathers hang from the handle of this ivory wand. Holding it brings a sense of gentle calm.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _soothe_ of the indicated rank, and can attempt to counteract one mental effect on the same target. Treat the _soothe_ spell's level as 1 higher for this counteract check.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _soothe_ of the appropriate rank.
