# Wand of Noisome Acid (2nd-Rank Spell)  *Item 6+*

`Acid`  `Magical`  `Wand`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This greasy stick emits a stomach-churning scent when held in hand.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/acid-grip|_acid grip_]] of the indicated rank. A creature that takes initial acid damage from this spell become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]] 1. Use your spell DC if the creature attempts to recover from this sickness. This is an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]] effect.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _acid grip_ of the appropriate rank.
