# Fauna Guardian  *Item 9*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Tattoo`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 675 gp
- **Usage**: tattooed on the body

A _fauna guardian_ is a tattoo of an animal, which you can temporarily animate to protect you. The animal must be chosen when you get the tattoo, and can be any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|animal]] of 4th level or lower. (Your GM might allow other options.)

**Activate** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You animate your tattoo using the duration and other parameters of a 5th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/summon-animal|_summon animal_]] spell. It appears in a space adjacent to you. If your tattoo animal drops to 0 HP, the activation ends, and the inanimate tattoo returns to your skin.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 120*
