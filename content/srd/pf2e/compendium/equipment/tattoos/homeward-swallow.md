# Homeward Swallow  *Item 5*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Tattoo`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 150 gp
- **Usage**: tattooed on the body

This small tattoo of a swallow always points toward your home. The tattooing must take place at a location you consider to be your home, or the magic fails to bind with the ink. When you travel to your home using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/teleportation|teleportation]] that can be off target, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/teleport|_teleport_]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/interplanar-teleport|_interplanar teleport_]], you arrive exactly at your home. If your home is destroyed or you come to believe a new place is your home, this tattoo fades from your skin.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Effect** You sense the direction toward your home.
