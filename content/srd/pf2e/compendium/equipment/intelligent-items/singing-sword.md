# Singing Sword  *Item 5*

`Intelligent`  `Occult`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/longsword|Longsword]]

**Perception** +11; precise vision 30 feet, imprecise hearing 30 feet

**Communication** speech (Common and four other common languages)

**Skills** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]] +15

**Int** +2, **Wis** +2, **Cha** +4

**Will** +13 +17 against attempts to quiet its singing

--- 

A _singing sword_ is a _+1 striking longsword_ imbued with the consciousness of a boisterous bard, and therefore constantly sings at all times. A singing sword can't stop singing and in fact communicates in no way other than by singing. A successful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]] check against its Will DC can convince it to quiet its singing to a whisper for 10 minutes, or 20 minutes on a critical success, though it quickly grows displeased at anyone who attempts this repeatedly. The _singing sword_ can spend its actions to attack on its own, with the effects of a _dancing_ weapon's activation, except that its attack modifier is +12. Additionally, it can perform the following activations; each casts a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/composition|composition]] spell and follows all the usual rules for compositions.

**Activate—Courageous Anthem** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/courageous-anthem|_courageous anthem_]].

**Activate—Rallying Anthem** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/rallying-anthem|rallying anthem]].

**Activate—Counter Performance** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You or an ally within 60 feet rolls a saving throw against an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] effect; **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts _counter performance_.
