# Shield Of Mental Fortitude  *Item 10*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 1,000 gp
- **Usage**: strapped to 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Shield**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Buckler|Buckler]]

This small steel [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Buckler|buckler]] ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/shields#Hardness|Hardness]] 6, HP 24, BT 12) is engraved with an unbroken golden circle. While you have this shield raised, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 5 to mental damage. 

**Activate—Mind Backlash** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You succeed at a Will save against a mental effect while the _shield of mental fortitude_ is raised; **Effect** You return a wave of psychic energy to the creature. The creature that caused the triggering mental effect must succeed at a DC 27 Will save or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 206*
