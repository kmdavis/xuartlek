# Coward's Shield  *Item 6*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 225 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: Steel Shield

This eerie, blackened steel shield (Hardness 6, HP 36, BT 18) seems to actively reject light that touches it. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Stealth checks to Hide while this shield is raised. 

**Activate—Shadow Slip** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would take damage while this shield is raised; **Effect** Shadows swallow you. You become concealed until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 206*
