# Dawnsilver Shield  *Item 8+*

`Uncommon`

- **Usage**: varies by shield
- **Base Material**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/dawnsilver-object-high-grade|Dawnsilver]]

Dawnsilver shields are 1 Bulk lighter than normal (or light Bulk if their normal Bulk is 1, with no effect on a shield that normally has light Bulk). When used for a shield bash, they're treated as silver weapons.

### Dawnsilver Buckler (Standard-Grade)

**Price** 400 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The shield has Hardness 3, HP 12, and BT 6.

**Craft Requirements** dawn silver worth at least 50 gp

### Dawnsilver Shield (Standard-Grade)

**Price** 440 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The shield has Hardness 5, HP 20, and BT 10.

**Craft Requirements** dawnsilver worth at least 55 gp

### Dawnsilver Buckler (High-Grade)

**Price** 8,000 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The shield has Hardness 6, HP 24, and BT 12.

**Craft Requirements** dawnsilver worth at least 4,000 gp

### Dawnsilver Shield (High-Grade)

**Price** 8,800 gp

**Bulk** L

---
