# Shield Of Endurance  *Item 9*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 600 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: Steel Shield

This steel shield (Hardness 8, HP 32, BT 16) is engraved with the image of an unbroken fortress wall. 

**Activate—Temporary Endurance** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** Your shield isn’t broken; **Effect** You Raise the Shield, and it gains 10 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 minute.
