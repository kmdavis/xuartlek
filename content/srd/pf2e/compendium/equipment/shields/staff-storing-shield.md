# Staff-storing Shield  *Item 7*

`Extradimensional`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: Wooden Shield

This magically reinforced wooden shield (Hardness 6, HP 36, BT 18) normally has a blank face. It can absorb a staff and transform between a shield and staff. When you prepare a staff, you can hold it up to the shield, at which point the items merge until your next daily preparations, and the shield’s face becomes an image corresponding to the type of magic, such as a skull for a _staff of the dead_. 

**Activate—Astonishing Transfiguration** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You change the _staff-storing shield_ from its shield form into the stored staff, which has an image of the shield on it, or change it from the stored staff back into a shield. _Reinforcing_ runes etched on the shield have no effect in the staff form, and fundamental weapon runes etched on the stored staff have no effect in the shield form.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 207*
