# Staff-storing Shield  *Item 7*

`Extradimensional`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Wooden Shield|Wooden Shield]]

This magically reinforced [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Wooden Shield|wooden shield]] ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/shields#Hardness|Hardness]] 6, HP 36, BT 18) normally has a blank face. It can absorb a staff and transform between a shield and staff. When you prepare a staff, you can hold it up to the shield, at which point the items merge until your next [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/running-exploration#Daily Preparations|daily preparations]], and the shield’s face becomes an image corresponding to the type of magic, such as a skull for a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/staves/staff-of-the-dead-major|staff of the dead]]_. 

**Activate—Astonishing Transfiguration** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Effect** You change the _staff-storing shield_ from its shield form into the stored staff, which has an image of the shield on it, or change it from the stored staff back into a shield. _Reinforcing_ runes etched on the shield have no effect in the staff form, and fundamental weapon runes etched on the stored staff have no effect in the shield form.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 207*
