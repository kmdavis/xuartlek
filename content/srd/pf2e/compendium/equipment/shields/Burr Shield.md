# Burr Shield  *Item 5*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 160 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Shields#Wooden Shield|Wooden Shield]]

This well-crafted [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Shields#Wooden Shield|wooden shield]] ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/Shields#Hardness|Hardness]] 5, HP 30, BT 15) is covered in numerous seed pods with long spurs. You can Strike with these burrs as though they were _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/shield/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]]_. 

**Activate—Prickly Sticker** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** Your previous action was a successful Strike with the burrs; **Effect** One of the burrs catches on the Strike’s target, inflicting 1d10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent bleed damage]].
