# Medic's Shield  *Item 14*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields/dawnsilver-shield-high-grade|standard-grade dawnsilver shield]] (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10) is inscribed with the symbol of a deity dedicated to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Healing|healing]] and medicine. The shield is designed for combat medics to aid combatants or adventurers in the midst of battle. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/healers-toolkit-expanded|Healer’s tools]] can be stored on the backside of this shield. This shield grants a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/medicine|Medicine]] checks.

**Activate—Adrenaline Boost** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You grant a medicinal boost to bring someone back into the fray. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Administer First Aid|Administer First Aid]] to stabilize an adjacent ally with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dying|dying]] condition. On a success, they gain 6d6+20 Hit Points, can immediately [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stand|Stand]] as a free action, and become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Quickened|quickened]] for 1 round. They can use this extra action only to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]].
