# Shield Of Silent Words  *Item 5*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 150 gp
- **Usage**: strapped to 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Shield**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Buckler|Buckler]]

The surface of this steel [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Buckler|buckler]] ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/shields#Hardness|Hardness]] 6, HP 24, BT 12) has been brushed into soft swirling patterns that evoke snowstorms. While you have this shield raised, if you attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] or perform an action that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] trait while you’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Deafened|deafened]], reduce the DC of the flat check by 2.
