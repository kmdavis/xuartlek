# Navaratna of the Solar Ruby  *Item 13*

`Artifact`  `Divine`  `Unique`

- **Usage**: worn

These nine flawless gems are set within a golden necklace, their centerpiece a holy ruby pulled from the center of a sun. So long as you are wearing the navaratna, you ignore all [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Temperature|environmental effects due to temperature]], do not take penalties due to [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Wind|wind]], and ignore all damage and effects from droughts, [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Floods|floods]], and [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Earthquakes|earthquakes]]. You are also immune to damage from [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Running Exploration#Starvation and Thirst|starvation]]. The navaratna does not otherwise grant you resistance against damage (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]] damage).

**Activate—Sutra of the Flawless Servant** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fortune|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per hour;; **Trigger** An ally within 30 feet fails a saving throw against an [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Temperature|temperature]] environmental effect; **Effect** You offer a prayer for your divine protection to extend to your companion. The creature rerolls the triggering saving throw with a +2 item bonus. They must take the new result, even if it is worse.

**Destruction** The navaratna must be swallowed by a fiendish [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Divine|divine]] [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/Lion|lion]]; the lion must then by killed by drowning in a lake of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/Naga|naga]] venom and left to rot for a year. At the stroke of midnight on the final day, the artifact is destroyed.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 131*
