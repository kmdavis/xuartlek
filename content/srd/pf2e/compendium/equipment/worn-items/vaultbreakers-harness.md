# Vaultbreaker's Harness  *Item 6*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Transmutation`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 230 gp
- **Usage**: worn backpack
- **Bulk**: 1

This bulky leather harness has four pockets across the chest. The pockets contain a set of infiltrator's thieves' tools, infiltrator picks, a levered crowbar, and a glass cutter. These items are magically bound to the harness; if they are more than 1 foot away from you, they disappear, then reappear in the harness at the next sunset. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Broken|Broken]] or destroyed items similarly reappear, restored, in their proper pockets at sunset.

**Activate** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") Interact; **Effect** You cinch the harness to prepare for mischief. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks and a +10-foot item bonus to your Speed for 1 minute.
