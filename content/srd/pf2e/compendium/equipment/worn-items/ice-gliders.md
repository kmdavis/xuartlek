# Ice Gliders  *Item 8+*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: worn shoes
- **Bulk**: L

These ornately embroidered boots are crafted by Varki artisans to help their hunters and warriors as they cross back and forth over the snow and ice of their homelands. Empowered by the magic woven into them, these boots enable their wearer to ignore any uneven ground or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by snow and ice. Additionally, while [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Striding]] on ice, you can move 10 feet for every 5 feet of movement you spend, as icy blades grow from the boots. 

**Activate—Rough Ice** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You stomp your foot on the ground, creating a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] of jagged ice on the ground that lasts for 1 minute before melting. This ice is [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]].
