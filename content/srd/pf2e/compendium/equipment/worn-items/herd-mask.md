# Herd Mask  *Item 6*

`Conjuration`  `Invested`  `Magical`  `Teleportation`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 210 gp
- **Usage**: worn mask

All _herd masks_ are linked to at least one other _herd mask_ and are usually sold in sets of multiple masks. Linked _herd masks_ look similar to one another, with only the slightest of differences to tell them apart.

**Activate** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") envision; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You swap locations with another creature wearing a linked herd mask within 100 feet. If you and the creature you swapped with are disguised as each other, other creatures gain an immediate Perception check against the lower of your or the other wearer's [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] DCs to Impersonate each other. On a failure, they don't realize the swap occurred.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _dimension door_. You can link a _herd mask_ you create to another _herd mask_ in your possession when you finish crafting it, which causes it to be linked to the chosen mask as well as all other _herd masks_ that are also linked to the chosen mask.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 154*
