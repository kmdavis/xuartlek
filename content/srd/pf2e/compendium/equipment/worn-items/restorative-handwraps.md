# Restorative Handwraps  *Item 5*

- **Price**: 150 gp
- **Usage**: worn gloves

Glowing runes cover these _+1 striking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/handwraps-of-mighty-blows-3-major-striking|handwraps of mighty blows]]_. When you critically hit an enemy with a Strike as part of a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Flurry of Blows|Flurry of Blows]], you can regain 4 Hit Points. This is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] effect. The handwraps can heal you only once every 10 minutes.
