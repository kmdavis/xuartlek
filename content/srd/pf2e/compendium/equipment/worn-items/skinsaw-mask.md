# Skinsaw Mask  *Item 3*

`Divination`  `Divine`  `Evil`  `Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 30 gp
- **Usage**: worn mask
- **Bulk**: L

A patchwork of humanoid flesh makes up this grotesque mask stitched together with black silk or wire. It is distinctive for its bulbous orange eye— crafted from a magical glass bauble—and wide row of teeth. When worn, the mask amplifies your ability to sense fear in other creatures. You automatically know the value of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition of any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]] creature, and you gain a +1 item bonus to Perception checks to Seek frightened creatures. Whenever you deal precision damage to a frightened creature, you deal 1 additional precision damage. If you are not evil, you are drained 2 while wearing the _Skinsaw mask_.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 155*
