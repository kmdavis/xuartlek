# Collar of Inconspicuousness  *Item 8*

`Companion`  `Invested`  `Primal`

- **Price**: 475 gp
- **Usage**: worn collar
- **Bulk**: 1

This leather collar's almost threadbare look belies its magical nature. When your companion wears and invests the collar, it gains the ability to change its appearance from that of a ferocious animal into a more inconspicuous form. 

**Activate—Adorable Guise** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Effect** You touch your animal companion to transform it into a nonthreatening Tiny creature of the same family or a similar creature (for instance, a house cat instead of a tiger, or a puppy instead of a wolf). This has the effects of _pest form_ (2nd rank, or 4th rank if your companion can fly). The effect lasts until you Dismiss it.
