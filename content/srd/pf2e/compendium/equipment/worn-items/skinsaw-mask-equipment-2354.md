# Skinsaw Mask  *Item 3*

`Divine`  `Invested`  `Uncommon`  `Unholy`

- **Price**: 50 gp
- **Usage**: worn mask
- **Bulk**: L

A patchwork of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/humanoid|humanoid]] flesh makes up a _skinsaw mask_, which is stitched together with black silk or wire. It is distinctive for its bulbous orange eye—crafted from a magical glass bauble—and wide row of teeth. When worn, the mask amplifies your ability to sense fear in other creatures. You know the value of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition of any observed creature, and you gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Perception|Perception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Seek|Seek]] frightened creatures. Whenever you deal precision damage to a frightened creature, you deal 1 additional precision damage. If you aren’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]], you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Drained|drained]] 2 while wearing a _skinsaw mask_.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 155*
