# Sturdy Neck Stock  *Item 11*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,300 gp
- **Usage**: worn collar
- **Bulk**: L

This thick piece of leather decorated with purple threads makes an attractive necktie but also serves a functional purpose: protecting the neck from hits that you don’t see coming. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against attacks while off-guard from flanking.

**Activate—Stretch out Stock** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _sturdy neck_ stock expands to cover not only your neck but also your shoulders and the back of your head. For 1 minute, you aren’t off-guard to hidden, undetected, or flanking creatures, or creatures using surprise attack of your level or lower.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 153*
