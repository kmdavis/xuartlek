# Dragon Rune Bracelet  *Item 11*

`Focused`  `Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,400 gp
- **Usage**: worn

A _dragon rune bracelet_ is a gold bangle formed around the scale of a famous [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/dragon|dragon]]. The bracelet is etched with esoteric symbols or words in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Draconic|Draconic]] that indicate kinship with dragons. As many types of _dragon rune bracelet_ exist as there are types of dragons, though bracelets associated with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/uncommon|uncommon]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/rare|rare]] dragons have the same rarity as the dragon.

 While wearing a _dragon rune bracelet_, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]] checks while interacting with dragons of the same type as the bracelet's scale (such as red dragons or cloud dragons). You also gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]] effects from any kind of dragon.

**Activate** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/sorcerer|sorcerer]] draconic bloodline spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a sorcerer of a draconic bloodline.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 148*
