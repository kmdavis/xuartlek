# Medic’s Armband  *Item 4+*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: L

You wear this white armband with a bright blue symbol identifying you as a combat medic. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]] checks to change the attitudes of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|diseased]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poisoned]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Wounded|wounded]] creatures.

**Activate—Do No Harm** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy within 30 feet targets you with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] or a spell that deals damage; **Effect** Your armband glows, showing that you’re here as a medic and not as a combatant. Both you and the triggering enemy take a –4 status penalty to damage rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Medic’s Armband

**Price** 80 gp

### Greater Medic’s Armband

**Price** 900 gp

You gain a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/medicine|Medicine]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Administer First Aid|Administer First Aid]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Wounds|Treat Wounds]], and the status penalty to damage rolls from Do No Harm increases to –8.
