# Mantle of the Tikbalang  *Item 8*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 425 gp
- **Usage**: worn cloak
- **Bulk**: 1

This long hide cloak is stitched with patches of the ebony hair of a tikbalang. While wearing this cloak, you take a –2 item penalty to saves against illusions but gain a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks.

**Activate—Illusory Thrash** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You wrap the mantle around your body, causing you to briefly appear much larger than you are. Make a melee Strike. This Strike deals an additional 4d6 mental damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide of a tikbalang.
