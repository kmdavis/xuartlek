# Amulet of the Hellcat  *Item 7*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: worn

This glass amulet is set on a steel backing shaped like a cage and flickers with an eerie crimson glow. Magically sealed within is a portion of a hellcat's ever-burning heart. While wearing the amulet, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks made while in bright light, and you can cast the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/light|_light_]] cantrip emanating from the amulet as an [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/innate-spells|innate]] divine spell.

**Activate—Invisible Pounce** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You are in bright light; **Effect** You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to your speed, and then make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] at the end of that movement. After making the Strike, you are no longer invisible. If at any point during your Stride, you pass out of [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Bright Light|bright light]], you are no longer invisible and must stop moving. You may make a melee Strike if you have a target within reach.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the heart of a hellcat.
