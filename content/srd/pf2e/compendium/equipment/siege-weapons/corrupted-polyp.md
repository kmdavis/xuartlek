When demonic forces invaded through the Worldwound, some brought terrible siege weapons with them. One particularly vile device is known as a _corrupted polyp_, a cultivated fungal colony from the Outer Rifts that can quickly mulch a steady stream of corpses and other decaying organic matter into toxic growths. With the right coaxing, the foul weapon can then explosively eject a cluster of these growths to devastating effect. Thankfully, few _corrupted polyps_ were brought over from the Outer Rifts, and even fewer survive now that the Worldwound has been closed.
