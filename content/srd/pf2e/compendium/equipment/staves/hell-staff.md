# Hell Staff  *Item 17*

`Magical`  `Staff`  `Uncommon`  `Unholy`

- **Price**: 13,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

A _hell staff_ is a tall, pointed staff forged of red-tinted steel with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Diabolic|Diabolic]] inscriptions that march neatly down its sides. At its top sits an inverted ruby pyramid divided into nine sections. Found mostly in Cheliax or other lands where diabolic influences hold sway, when used as a weapon the staff is a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+3 greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/flaming-greater|flaming]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/unholy|unholy]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/staff|staff]]_. When you prepare this staff, if you’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Drained|drained]] 1 until your next daily preparations. The staff’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/summon-lesser-servitor|_summon lesser servitor_]] spell can be used only to summon animals with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiend]] trait, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/devil|devils]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/hell-hound|hell hounds]] (at 4th rank). Its [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/summon-fiend|_summon fiend_]] spell can summon only devils or hell hounds.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list. 
**Cantrip** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/divine-lance|_divine lance_]]**1st** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/protection|_protection_]], _summon lesser servitor_**2nd** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/familiar-abilities#Darkvision|darkvision]], _summon lesser servitor_**3rd** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/chilling-darkness|_chilling darkness_]], _darkvision_, _summon lesser servitor_**4th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/divine-wrath|_divine wrath_]], _summon lesser servitor_**5th** _darkvision_, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/divine-immolation|_divine immolation_]], _summon fiend_**6th** _devil form_, _summon fiend_**7th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/divine-decree|_divine decree_]], _summon fiend_

**Craft Requirements** You’re unholy. Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
