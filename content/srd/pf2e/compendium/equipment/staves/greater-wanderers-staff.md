# Greater Wanderer's Staff  *Item 6+*

`Magical`  `Staff`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

The outlines of topographical maps are burned into this wooden staff’s surface, though they constantly change to reflect the surrounding terrain. While wielding this staff, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/survival|Survival]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sense Direction|Sense Direction]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Subsist|Subsist]] in the wild. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
