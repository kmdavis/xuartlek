# Fiddle of the Maestro  *Item 16*

`Coda`  `Occult`  `Rare`  `Staff`

- **Price**: 10,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L

This exquisite fiddle is perfectly carved and balanced to produce the purest sound while granting its player perfect balance and poise. It grants a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]] checks. If you’re a master in Performance, while playing it you also gain a +1 status bonus to Reflex saves and a +1 item bonus to Dexterity-based skill checks. If you’re legendary in Performance, the bonuses are +2. 

When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Perform|Perform]] with the fiddle, you can choose to create a harsh and discordant note. You critically fail the Performance check, shred the strings of the fiddle, and create an 8th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/noise-blast|_noise blast_]], with a 40-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] around you instead of a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]. The fiddle can’t be played again until the strings are replaced at a cost of 200 gp.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from this instrument to cast a spell from its list.
