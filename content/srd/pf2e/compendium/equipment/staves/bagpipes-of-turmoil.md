# Bagpipes of Turmoil  *Item 4+*

`Coda`  `Occult`  `Staff`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 1

While those who appreciate bagpipes may like the sound of this gray reed and black leather instrument, its real purpose is to sow turmoil against a performer's enemies, spreading discord with each note. While playing the bagpipes, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]] checks and to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made to Demoralize.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from this instrument to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
