# Fanged Staff  *Item 10*

`Magical`  `Staff`

- **Price**: 925 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

Carved of the finest bloodwood, this staff is an elegant walking stick with a sharp metal point at its base. _Fanged staves_ are popular among favored mortal retainers of vampires. While wielding a _fanged staff_, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against void effects. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.
**• Cantrip** __daze__
**• 1st** __command__, __grim tendrils__
**• 2nd** __blood vendetta_, _draw blood__
**• 3rd** __enthrall__, __vampiric feast__
**• 4th** __sanguine mist__, __vampiric maiden__

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
