# Rainbow Vinegar  *Item 4+*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Elixir`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Access**: Tian Xia Origin
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

Black swill with multicolored ribbons makes this vinegar look like an oil spill. Ingesting a dose of rainbow vinegar makes your sweat acidic and nonconductive for 10 minutes. During this time, your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks deal an additional 1d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]] damage, and you have [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]]. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/Vampire|Vampires]] find this vinegar particularly harmful and take an additional 2d4 acid damage instead. Taking more than one dose of rainbow vinegar in a single day gives you [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weakness]]5 to acid until your next daily preparations.

### Rainbow Vinegar

**Price** 15 gp

### Rainbow Vinegar (Greater)

**Price** 110 gp

The resistance increases to 15. You deal an additional 1d8 acid damage with your unarmed attacks, or 2d8 acid damage against vampires.
