# Revealing Mist (Lesser)  *Item 3+*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Kept in an airtight spray bottle, revealing mist is an alchemical concoction that creates a sticky and clinging mist of chemicals in a 15-foot cone when sprayed. It doesn't affect visibility but causes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures in the area to be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]]. Revealing mist is ineffective in water or in areas with other factors affecting the spread of the mist, as determined by the GM. It remains in the area for 1 minute or until any significant wind disperses it, whichever comes first.
