# Iron Wine  *Item 1*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Elixir`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 3 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Access**: Tian Xia Origin
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

This strong, clear liquor is made from fermented rice. When you drink a cup of iron wine, your sweat becomes highly combustive for the next 10 minutes, igniting with the slightest bit of friction. This causes your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks to deal an additional 1d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]] damage for the duration of the effect. Drinking more than one cup of iron wine in a single day gives you [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Diagonal Movement|weakness]] 5 to fire until your next daily preparations.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 104*
