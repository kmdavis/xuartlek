# Aroma Concealer  *Item 1*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Oil`

- **Price**: 3 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: 1 minute ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]])

This oily mix of herbs and natural detergents can be applied to a creature to reduce and cover any ordinary odors they produce. The creature receives a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]] against creatures using primarily smell. This bonus also applies to the DC to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Track|Track]] the creature by scent. The listed amount is enough to cover one Medium or smaller creature and takes 1 minute to apply. The effect lasts for 1 hour after applying.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 109*
