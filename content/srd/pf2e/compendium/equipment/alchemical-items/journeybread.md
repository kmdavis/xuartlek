# Journeybread  *Item 1+*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Journeybread contains a mix of fruits, nuts, and grains with an alchemical boost. Eating one journeybread provides all the food and water you need for a day. If you subsist on nothing else for a week, you become temporarily immune to journeybread until you eat real food and drink water normally for 24 hours.

### Journeybread

**Price** 3 gp

### Journeybread (Power)

**Price** 15 gp

---

In addition to the benefits of journeybread, power journeybread grants you a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] checks to Climb, Force Open, and Swim, and to Fortitude saving throws against being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]]. The number of minutes you can Hustle changes to your Constitution modifier × 20. These benefits last for 4 hours.
