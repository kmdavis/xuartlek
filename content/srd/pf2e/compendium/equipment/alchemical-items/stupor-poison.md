# Stupor Poison  *Item 7*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Incapacitation`  `Injury`  `Poison`  `Sleep`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 16 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])

Stupor poison is a more potent distillation of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/lethargy-poison-equipment-3340|lethargy poison]]. Further exposure to stupor poison doesn’t require the target to attempt additional saving throws; only failing a saving throw against an ongoing exposure can progress its stage.

**Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 hours; **Stage 1** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] (1 round); **Stage 2** slowed 2 and off-guard (1 round); **Stage 3** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] with no Perception check to wake up (1 round); **Stage 4** unconscious with no Perception check to wake up (1d6 hours)
