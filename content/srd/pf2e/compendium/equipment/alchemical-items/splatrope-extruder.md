# Splatrope Extruder  *Item 3*

`Alchemical`

- **Price**: 50 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This portable nozzle-and-trigger assembly based on spider spinnerets can extrude and weave alchemical adhesives into temporary constructions. As an Interact action, you can attach a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/glue-bomb-major|glue bomb]] to the extruder.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** A glue bomb is installed in the extruder; **Effect** The extruder converts the glue bomb into a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/rope|rope]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/whip|whip]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/net|net]], depending on the nozzle die you choose when activating the device. The created object lasts for 1 hour. The DC to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]] a created rope (if used to bind a creature) or net is equal to the consumed glue bomb’s DC and Escaping destroys the created object.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 65*
