# Preserved Moonflower  *Item 7*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 60 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Access**: Tian Xia Origin
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

Though these preserved vegetables aren't actual moonflowers, they're made using a moonflower-distilled vinegar. When consumed, tendrils sprout from your veins and curl around you, granting you a +2 status bonus to saves against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]] effects for 10 minutes and reducing your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Wounded|wounded]] condition by up to 2. Eating more than one preserved moonflower dish in a day doesn't grant further benefits and makes you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Drained|drained]] 1.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 105*
