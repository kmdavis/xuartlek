# Pallesthetic Mutagen  *Item 9*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Elixir`  `Mutagen`  `Polymorph`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 150 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]])

You gain unparalleled sensitivity to the tiniest of vibrations through solid surfaces and even the very air around you, but your eyes become useless.

**Benefit** For 1 minute, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Precise Senses|precise]] echolocation out to 40 feet, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] out to 60 feet, and a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Perception|Perception]] checks using either of these senses.

**Drawback** You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 110*
