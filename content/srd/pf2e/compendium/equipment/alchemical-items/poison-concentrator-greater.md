# Poison Concentrator (Greater)  *Item 5+*

`Alchemical`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L

This compression apparatus can reduce two poisons into a more concentrated dose. As a 10-minute activity that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] trait, you can use a poison concentrator to combine two doses of the same [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/infused|infused]] alchemical poison, creating a single concentrated poison. The concentrated potion has a +1 item bonus to its DC, and its level is increased by 1.
