# Unfathomable Stargazer  *Item 15*

`Cursed`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

An _unfathomable stargazer_ is a handheld brass telescope etched with constellations, the patterns of which form bizarre, shifting runes. You can observe the night sky with an _unfathomable stargazer_, however, the constellations you glimpse through the telescope distort with expanding and contracting fields of darkness among them. You use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/occultism|Occultism]] instead of Perception or Astronomy Lore when observing the skies with this item, and gain a +2 item bonus on all checks to do so. Once you use it, it fuses to you, and you must succeed at a DC 35 Will save to use another device to observe the stars, including your naked eyes. The telescope imposes a –4 circumstance penalty to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/survival|Survival]] checks to Sense Direction or navigate. If you critically fail this Survival check, you're subjected to _warp mind_ (DC 34) as you glimpse something horrifying and alien amid the darkness.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 193*
