# Shrieking Key  *Item 5*

`Cursed`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand

A skull-topped metal shrieking key appears to be a _skeleton key_, useful in place of thieves' tools when attempting to Pick a Lock. A _shrieking key_ has no activation, however. When you use it to Pick a Lock, the key emits a loud shriek audible for 500 feet despite ambient noise. Physical barriers still block or muffle the shriek as normal. You also take a –2 circumstance penalty to the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/thievery|Thievery]] check rather than enjoying a bonus. After you attempt such a check with the key the first time, it fuses to you, returning to your possession if discarded. To use another device to Pick a Lock, you must first succeed at a DC 20 Will save.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 192*
