# Spurned Lute  *Item 5*

`Cursed`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 1

Made of a deep-brown rosewood, a _spurned lute_ is adorned with carved flowers. The lute appears to be and functions as a virtuoso instrument. (Other _spurned instruments_ exist, but the lute is the least rare.) This lute has a jealous streak, demanding total loyalty from its “partner” musician. After you play the lute for the first time, it fuses to you. If you go a day without using it to Perform, you become stupefied 1 until you next do so. After that, when you attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]] check using an instrument other than the lute, you take a –4 circumstance penalty to do so, and you must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become stupefied 1 for 1 minute.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 193*
