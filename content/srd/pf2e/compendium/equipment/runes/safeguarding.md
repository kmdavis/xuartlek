# Safeguarding  *Item 13*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 2,600 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

Your armor is overlayed with a shimmering mosaic of pink, turquoise, and blue energy that protects the most vital aspects of your person. When activated, the armor’s hue shifts according to the type of resistance you gain: turquoise (mental), blue (poison), or pink (spirit). 

**Activate—Adaptive Aegis** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would take mental, poison, or spirit damage; **Effect** You gain resistance 5 to the triggering damage type that lasts for 10 minutes.
