# Mythic Armor Potency  *Item 20*

`Magical`  `Mythic`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 70,000 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

This armor is etched with a mythical ward providing unparalleled defense. Increase the armor’s item bonus to AC by 4, and the armor can be etched with four property runes.

**Activate—Survive Devastation** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** An enemy critically succeeds against you with a weapon or unarmed Strike; **Effect** Spend a Mythic Point; if the triggering Strike was made by a mythic creature, it’s a normal success instead. If it was made by a non-mythic creature, it’s a failure.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic armor potency runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character capable of making a Crafting check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).
