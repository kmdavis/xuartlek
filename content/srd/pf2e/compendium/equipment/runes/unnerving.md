# Unnerving  *Item 7*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

The sound of weapons clanging against a suit of _unnerving_ armor transforms into an inhuman screech that unsettles enemies and makes them hesitate to attack again. Any creature that hits you with a melee Strike takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its next melee Strike that targets you before the end of your next turn.
