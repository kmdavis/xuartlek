# Astral (Greater)  *Item 8+*

`Magical`  `Spirit`

- **Usage**: etched onto a weapon

_Astral_ weapons command powerful spiritual energy from the Astral Plane. This rune has the same effects as a ghost touch rune, plus Strikes with it deal an extra 1d6 spirit damage. If used to attack a creature that's possessing another creature, this weapon deals no damage to the possessed creature. On a critical hit against a creature possessing another creature, the possessing creature must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or be expelled and unable to possess a creature for 1d4 rounds.

### Astral

**Price** 450 gp

### Astral (Greater)

**Price** 6,000 gp

---

The save DC is 36. Spirit damage dealt by this weapon ignores the target's spirit resistance.
