# Striking  *Item 4+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: etched onto a weapon

A _striking rune_ stores destructive magic in the weapon, increasing the weapon damage dice it deals to two instead of one. For instance, a _+1 striking dagger_ would deal 2d4 damage instead of 1d4 damage.
 You can upgrade the _striking rune_ already etched on a weapon to a stronger version, increasing the values of the existing rune to those of the new rune. The Price of the upgrade is the difference between the two runes' Prices.

### Striking

**Price** 65 gp

### Striking (Greater)

**Price** 1,065 gp

---

The weapon deals three weapon damage dice.

### Striking (Major)

**Price** 31,065 gp

---

The weapon deals four weapon damage dice.
