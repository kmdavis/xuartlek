# Necro Roamer  *Vehicle 15*

`Rare`

- **Price**: 19,900 gp

Favored by necromancers, this vehicle is a heavily fortified, armored wooden shed mounted on the legs of a dozen [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creatures.

---

**Space** 15 feet long, 15 feet wide, 15 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 2 crew

**Passengers** 3

**Piloting Check** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]] (DC 36) or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Driving Lore]] (DC 34)

---

**AC** 34

**Fort** +26

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 220 (BT 110)

**Immunities** [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/item-damage#Object Immunities|object immunities]], precision damage

---

**Speed** 30 feet ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]])

**Collision** 9d12 (DC 32)

**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has two rooftop weapon mounts. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 36 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. These weapon mounts can be equipped with Large or smaller ranged siege weapons.
