# Cavern Crawler  *Vehicle 20*

`Rare`

- **Price**: 210,000 gp

This massive undead vehicle is created by crafting a piloting and cargo compartment within the husk of a massive cave worm and then infusing the remains with void energy. This compartment has breathable air as well as sealable weapon ports. Designed specifically to burrow under and then behind enemy positions, cavern crawlers can attack enemies on their flank or bring supplies and reinforcements to troops deep behind enemy lines.

---

**Space** 100 feet long, 30 feet wide, 30 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 3 crew

**Passengers** 30

**Piloting Check** Arcana (DC 42) or Driving Lore (DC 40)

---

**AC** 43

**Fort** +34

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 300 (BT 150)

**Immunities** object immunities, precision damage

---

**Speed** 30 feet, burrow 30 feet (clockwork, magical)

**Collision** 14d10 (DC 40)

A creature can choose to fail. On a failure, the creature is swallowed by the cavern crawler, moved into the passenger bay, and knocked prone. This action fails if the passenger bay is full.
**Tunnel** The cavern crawler can burrow through solid stone at a Speed of 15 feet. The pilot can choose to leave a tunnel.
