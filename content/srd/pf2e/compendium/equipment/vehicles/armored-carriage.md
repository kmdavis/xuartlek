# Armored Carriage  *Vehicle 5*

`Uncommon`

**Crew** 1 pilot

**Passengers** 3

**Piloting Check** Driving Lore (DC 20) or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] (DC 22)

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +12

**Hardness** 5

**HP** 70 (BT 35)

**Immunities** object immunities

---

**Speed** 25 feet (clockwork)

**Collision** 3d10 (DC 20)
