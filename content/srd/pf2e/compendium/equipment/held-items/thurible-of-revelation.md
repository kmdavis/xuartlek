# Thurible of Revelation  *Item 3+*

`Divine`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

This brass censer dangles on a length of chain. Most _thuribles of revelation_ are adorned with swirling [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Empyrean|Empyrean]] text, though some are iron and feature [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Diabolic|Diabolic]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Chthonian|Chthonian]] text. 

**Activate—Burn Incense** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]])**Cost** incense worth
at least 5 gp; **Effect** You light the incense inside the censer, and it burns for 1 hour. During that time, as long you are holding the thurible, you gain a +1 item bonus to Religion checks, and any critical failure you roll when you Decipher Writing of a religious nature is a failure instead.

### Thurible of Revelation (Lesser)

**Price** 55 gp

### Thurible of Revelation (Moderate)

**Price** 900 gp

---

The thurible grants a +2 bonus. Once per day, when you activate the thurible, you can increase its revelations. During that activation, you can hold the thurible up to your eyes with an Interact action to gain the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/see-the-unseen|_see the unseen_]] for 1 round by peering through the smoke. 

### Thurible of Revelation (Greater)

**Price** 19,000 gp

---

The thurible grants a +3 bonus. The greater version shares the once-per-day enhanced revelations of the moderate version, except peering through the smoke also grants you the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/truesight|_truesight_]].
