# Doom Switch  *Item 3*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 50 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This short length of wood is decorated with fine carvings of symbols representing fate.

**Activate—Bragging Rights** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/attack|attack]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] a significant enemy with the _doom switch_, marking them for defeat. The switch is treated as a simple melee weapon for the purpose of proficiency. This attack deals no damage.

**Critical Success** You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls against the target for 1 minute. If you reduce the target to 0 Hit Points during this time, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice the target’s level for 1 round.

**Success** As critical success, except you gain temporary Hit Points equal to the target’s level.

**Failure** You and your allies take a –1 status penalty to attack rolls against the target for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** You and your allies take a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against the target for 1 round.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 143*
