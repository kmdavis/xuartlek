# Bottle of Infinite Dust  *Item 7*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This plain, faintly green bottle full of sand looks deceptively mundane, though it contains a near endless supply of sand. A very slow but steady stream of sand can be poured from the bottle. Up to 1 pound of sand can be produced a day this way.

**Activate—Grit Geyser** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/earth|earth]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/primal|primal]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You aim the bottle and unleash a blast of sand. Scouring grit is released in a 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]], dealing 4d4 slashing damage to all creatures in the area (DC 23 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save).

**Activate—Sandstorm** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/earth|earth]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/primal|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You dump out the bottle, creating a swirling sandstorm around you. A 20-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] is filled with blowing sand that obscures vision. This has the effects of mist. The air within the sandstorm is unbreathable; creatures in the area must [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/encounter-mode#Drowning and Suffocating|hold their breath]]. Creatures entering or starting their turn in the sandstorm take 2d4 slashing damage (DC 23 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]] trait or that are primarily made of liquid take double damage. This sandstorm lasts 10 minutes or until the bottle is corked with an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] action, whichever comes first.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 143*
