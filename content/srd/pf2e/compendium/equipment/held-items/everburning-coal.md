# Everburning Coal  *Item 12*

`Fire`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 1,750 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand

This lump of coal is always warm to the touch and glows faintly red from within, as if holding an ember of flame waiting to be stoked. No amount of water or coldness can extinguish the coal's warmth. When you hold an _everburning coal_ in your hand, you gain resistance 10 to cold and are protected from mild, severe, and extreme cold.

 In addition, an _everburning coal_ is a planar key for _interplanar teleport_ and similar magic to travel to the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Plane of Fire|Plane of Fire]]. When using it this way, you can attune it to the fires of your destination to make it more likely to arrive where you intend to be, appearing 1d6 × 25 miles from your intended destination instead of 1d10 × 25 miles away.

This has the effect of _wall of ice_, except for the following adjustments. 

The wall has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]] trait instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]].The wall deals fire damage instead of cold damage.The weakness to fire is instead weakness to cold and to water.Destroying a section of the wall with cold or water (rather than with fire) causes a section to evaporate.
