# Anointed Waterskin  *Item 9*

`Divine`  `Holy`  `Water`

- **Price**: 600 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

This waterskin coruscates with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] energy. After using any of the activations, the waterskin is empty, but slowly refills itself. It becomes full enough to use again at the next dawn. 

**Activate—Blessed Bombard** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You throw the _anointed waterskin_ up to 60 feet. The water explodes out of it, with the effects of a DC 25 4th-rank _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/holy-cascade|_holy cascade_]]_. 

**Activate—Divine Decanting** 1 minute ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You decant the water, creating up to 10 vials of _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/consumables/holy-water|holy water]]_. You must provide the vials. Holy water created this clearly temporary upon inspection, and fades away after 8 hours. 

**Activate—Sacred Sip** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You drink the water within the waterskin, granting you the effects of _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/bless|_bless_]]_. As normal, you can increase the spell’s radius by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustaining]] it.
