# Sun Herald's Stylus  *Item 14*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 4, 000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

This writing instrument was made from the spur of one of the giant divine roosters that heralds the presence of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/tian-gods/Shizuru|Shizuru]]. While you hold it, you gain a +2 item bonus to Calligraphy Lore and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks.

**Activate—Rejuvenating Ink** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Drawing a circle on the ground with your stylus, you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/Field of Life|_field of life_]] centered on yourself.
