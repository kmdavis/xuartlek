# Horn of Exorcism  *Item 11*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 1,250 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

A _horn of exorcism_ is an instrument made from an animal horn, or an object of the same shape from carved wood or ivory. 

**Activate—Rattle the Dead** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (auditory, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** Blowing into the horn frightens ghosts and evil spirits who can hear its call. Make an Intimidation check to Demoralize against all creatures with the undead or unholy trait in a 30-foot emanation. This can affect even a mindless creature with that trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language. 

**Activate—Sacred Seeds** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You fill the horn with sacred seeds and then scatter them around you with a twist of your wrist. The horn grants you and your allies in a 30-foot emanation the ghost touch property rune on all of your weapon and unarmed Strikes for 1 minute.
