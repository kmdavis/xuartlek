# Celestial Peachwood Sword  *Item 17*

`Holy`  `Magical`  `Rare`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Price**: 15, 000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/Shortsword|Shortsword]]

From blade to pommel, this sword is carved from a branch of the now-extinct celestial peach tree. The blade of this +[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|3]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Striking|greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Holy|holy]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Vitalizing|vitalizing]] peachwood [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/Shortsword|shortsword]] has ancient runes that can permanently destroy the most powerful undead—if you are willing to pay the price. 
An [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Undead|undead]] creature of 12th level or lower that takes damage from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with the sword takes 10d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Vitality|vitality]] damage with a DC 35 basic Fortitude save. This damage is separate from the Strike itself and isn't included in any effect based on the Strike's damage.

**Activate—Violent Exorcism** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; Trigger You critically hit an undead creature with the sword; **Effect** The creature must succeed at a DC 35 Fortitude save or be destroyed. If the undead fails its saving throw and is destroyed, you suffer a backlash, taking 1d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]] damage per level of the destroyed undead.
