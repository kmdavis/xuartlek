# Phase Spike  *Item 10*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/spear|Spear]]

The head of this _+[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/astral-greater|astral]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/spear|spear]]_ is partially translucent. Thieves and battlefield spellcasters favor it for its ability to pierce magical barriers and temporarily disable enchanted objects. A creature that has [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Raise a Shield|Raised its Shield]] (or similar ability) takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its AC against attacks from a _phase spike_. 

**Activate—Incorporeal Stride** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You focus on the head of the spear and channel its properties into your form. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to your Speed. You can move easily through solid objects and creatures during this movement, but you can’t end this movement within a solid object or in a creature’s space. You also gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 10 to all damage during this Stride (except [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage and damage caused by a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|ghost touch]]_ rune); double this resistance against non-magical damage.
