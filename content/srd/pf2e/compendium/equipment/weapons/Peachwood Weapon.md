# Peachwood Weapon  *Item 12+*

`Uncommon`

- **Usage**: varies by weapon
- **Bulk**: varies by weapon
- **Base Material**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Peachwood|Peachwood]]

Peachwood weapons have an auburn tinge in direct sunlight. Peachwood is treated as duskwood for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Undead|undead]] creatures' [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistances]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weaknesses]] related to duskwood (bypassing a jiang-shi's resistance, for example). In addition, peachwood weapons bypass a small portion of the resistances of any undead creature. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] with a peachwood weapon treat an undead's resistances against physical damage as 2 lower for standard-grade peachwood, and 4 lower for high-grade.

### Peachwood Weapon (Standard-Grade)

**Price** 2,000 gp (+ 200 gp per bulk)

**Craft Requirements** At least 250 gp of peachwood + 25 gp per Bulk

### Peachwood Weapon (High-Grade)

**Price** 19,000 gp (+ 1,900 gp per Bulk)

**Craft Requirements** At least 9,500 gp of peachwood + 950 gp per Bulk
