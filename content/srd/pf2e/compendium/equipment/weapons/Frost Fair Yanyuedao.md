# Frost Fair Yanyuedao  *Item 14*

`Artifact`  `Cold`  `Magical`  `Unique`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/polearm/Glaive|Glaive]]

The Frost Fair Yanyuedao is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|+2]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|greater striking]] yanyuedao (use statistics for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/polearm/Glaive|glaive]]) once wielded by a legendary military general from Goka. This weapon was constructed from pieces harvested from a dragon’s body. When in an area of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields/Force Shield|severe cold or colder]], the Frost Fair Yanyuedao becomes a +3 major striking yanyuedao with a glowing blue cutting edge and the following ability.

**Activate—Dragon Chill** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Effect** Until the end of your turn, the Frost Fair Yanyuedao gains the effects of a greater [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Frost|frost]] rune. While under this effect, if you critically succeed at a Strike using this weapon against a creature who has [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Immunity|immunity]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], that creature must attempt a DC 31 Fortitude save or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 for 1 minute.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 131*
