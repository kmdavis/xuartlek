# North Wind's Night Verse  *Item 10*

`Cold`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/katana|Katana]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/frost-greater|frost]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/katana|katana]]_ is always cool to the touch, nearly uncomfortably so. Unadorned and with no tsuba, its honed blade is carved from a single tusk of dragon-turtle ivory and wrapped in strips of [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/beast/witchwarg|witchwarg]] hide. Strikes with the katana gain a +2 status bonus to damage rolls against creatures that have a status penalty to their Speed or that are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]]. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking_ rune and +4 for a _major striking_ rune. 

**Activate—Frigid Breeze** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (air, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You deal cold damage to a creature with a spell or ability; **Requirements** ; **Effect** You point the _north wind’s night verse_ at the triggering creature. A gust of wind from nowhere freezes its bones, imparting a –5-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.
