# Conflagration Club  *Item 6*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 250 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/greatclub|Greatclub]]

A ring of magical crystals encircles the base of this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/greatclub|greatclub]]_, allowing it to absorb and store magical energy. These crystals irregularly flash a deep crimson light. 

**Activate—Absorb Arcana** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** The club has at least 1 charge; **Effect** You flip the club around to blast a cone of energy from its base. Each creature in a 15-foot cone takes 1d6 damage per charge stored in the club, with a DC 20 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save. The damage type is the same type the charges were attuned to. The club loses all its charges.
