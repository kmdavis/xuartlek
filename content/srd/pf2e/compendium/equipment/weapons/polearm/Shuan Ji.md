# Shuan Ji  *Weapon 0*

`Backswing`  `Forceful`  `Reach`  `Uncommon`  `Versatile`

- **PFS Note**: Characters from Tian Xia have access to this option
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin
- **Price**: 3 gp
- **Damage**: 1d6 S
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Hands**: 2
- **Type**: Melee
- **Category**: Martial
- **Group**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Polearm|Polearm]]

The shuan ji is a polearm featuring a long spear point on one end and two crescent-shaped blades that allow the wielder to strike with either side of the weapon.

### Critical Specialization Effects

Certain feats, class features, weapon runes, and other effects can grant you additional benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success. This is called a critical specialization effect. The exact effect depends on which weapon group your weapon belongs to, as listed below. You can always decide not to add the critical specialization effect of your weapon.
