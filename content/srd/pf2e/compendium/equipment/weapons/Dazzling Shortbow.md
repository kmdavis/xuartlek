# Dazzling Shortbow  *Item 5*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 160 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/bow/Shortbow|Shortbow]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/bow/Shortbow|shortbow]]_ is a favorite of mage hunters and those who frequently fight enemies who can turn themselves [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Invisible|invisible]]. A creature who is critically hit with a ranged Strike from a dazzling shortbow must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute.

**Activate—Show Yourself!** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You fire an arrow that glows with purple light at a spot. Each creature in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet is affected by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Revealing Light|_revealing light_]] (DC 19).

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _revealing light_.
