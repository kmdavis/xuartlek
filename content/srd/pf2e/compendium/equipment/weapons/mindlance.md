# Mindlance  *Item 8*

`Arcane`  `Mental`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: Arquebus

This _+1 striking arquebus_ is used by caravan guards to nonlethally—though powerfully—deter large game and bandits. When fired, the spark gun deals mental damage and adds the nonlethal trait to the attack. Each _mindlance_ also includes a reinforced stock that benefits from any fundamental runes on the firearm. When you critically succeed at an attack roll with a _mindlance_, the target becomes frightened 2 unless it succeeds at a DC 24 Will save.
