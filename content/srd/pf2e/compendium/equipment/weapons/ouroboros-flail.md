# Ouroboros Flail  *Item 14+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: War Flail

You can easily magically expand and contract the chain of this _+2 greater striking extending war flail_. 

**Activate—Chained Grasp** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You extend the flail’s chain, then wrap it across itself and strike two links together. This severs up to a 60-foot loop of usable chain from the flail, which instantly returns to its normal length. If you use Infinite Links again, any previous chain you created with it disappears after 1 round. As with most obviously magical and temporary constructs, the chain can’t be sold.

### Ouroboros Flail

**Price** 4,400 gp

### Greater Ouroboros Flail

**Price** 15,000 gp
