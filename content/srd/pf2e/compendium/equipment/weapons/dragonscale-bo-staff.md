# Dragonscale Bo Staff  *Item 6*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 240 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: Bo Staff

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/bo-staff|bo staff]]_ is covered in scales shed by or harvested from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/dragon|dragon]]. When benefiting from the circumstance bonus to AC granted by the bo staff’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/parry|parry]] trait, you also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]] effects of the tradition matching the dragon who provided the scales, plus [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|resistance]] 5 to a damage type determined by that tradition: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]] for arcane, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] for divine, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] for occult, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]] for primal. For instance, a dragonscale bo staff made with scales taken from an [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/dragon/young-omen-dragon|omen dragon]] would provide a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against occult effects and resistance 5 to mental damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include scales from one type of dragon.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 36*
