# Calvary Commander's Lance  *Item 6*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 225 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/lance|Lance]]
