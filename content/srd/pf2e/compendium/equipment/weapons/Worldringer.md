# Worldringer  *Item 5+*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 1 or 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Khakkhara|Khakkhara]]

This [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|+1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Striking|striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Khakkhara|khakkhara]] is topped by an ornate finial depiction of a small-statured traveler with animal companions. While the rings of a khakkhara are normally meant to alert others of one’s presence, the magic of the worldringer enhances the chimes to entreat upon those who hear it.

**Activate—Chimes of Command** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You call upon beings of this plane or another to hear your call. Choose [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Summon Lesser Servitor|_summon lesser servitor_]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Command|_command_]]. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast the chosen Spell]] at 1st rank (DC 19). When casting summon lesser servitor in this way, you must choose to summon a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Animal|animal]].
