# Ghoul Stiletto  *Item 3*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 60 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: Dagger

A _ghoul stiletto_ is a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/weapon-potency-3|+1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/dagger|dagger]]_ wrapped in the still-undead skin of a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/ghoul|ghoul]]. While you wield the dagger, you gain a +1 status bonus to all saves against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/curse|curses]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]] effects.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The _ghoul stiletto_ afflicts the next living target struck by the dagger with a terrible wound that emits the foul stench of the grave. The creature emits a stench aura in a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] for 1 minute. During that time, the affected creature, as well as any creature that starts or ends its turn in the aura, must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude save or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]] 1 (plus [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune to all stench auras for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include skin from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/ghoul|ghoul]].

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 36*
