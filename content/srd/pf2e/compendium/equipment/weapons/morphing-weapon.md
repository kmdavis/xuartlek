# Morphing Weapon  *Item 7*

`Magical`  `Metal`

- **Price**: 360 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 or 2 hands
- **Bulk**: varies

In its base form, this armament looks like a smooth bar of soft, gleaming metal shaped like a horseshoe. Its ability to adjust to any battle situation makes it a popular weapon for elite warriors of the Plane of Metal. It can be shaped into a melee weapon using its _shifting_ rune, but it can shift only into weapons primarily made of metal or back to its base shape. In weapon form, it's a +_1 striking shifting weapon_.

**Activate—Reshape** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** Strike with the _morphing weapon_, choosing one of the following benefits to apply to the Strike. 

**Reach** Increase the reach by 5 feet.**Shift** The weapon's damage becomes your choice of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing instead of its normal type.**Take** If the Strike hits, you can attempt to Disarm or Steal from your target immediately after as a reaction.**Weigh** Gain a status bonus to the damage roll equal to the weapon's number of damage dice.
