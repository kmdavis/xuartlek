# Leydroth Spellbreaker  *Item 17*

`Magical`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 15,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/firearm/blunderbuss|Blunderbuss]]

The larynx of a leydroth, a dangerous creature whose roar can unravel magic, is integrated into the firing mechanism of this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/weapon-potency-3|+3]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/striking-major|greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/firearm/blunderbuss|blunderbuss]]_.

**Activate—Dispelling Blast** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _leydroth spellbreaker_ emits a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]] of dispelling energy. Attempt a single [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] check at +25 against each spell or magical effect in the area, plus one item or effect on each creature in the area, with the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/dispel-magic|_dispel magic_]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the larynx of a leydroth.
