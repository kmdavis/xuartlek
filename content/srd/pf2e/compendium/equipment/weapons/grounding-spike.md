# Grounding Spike  *Item 10*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 950 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/dancers-spear|Dancer's Spear]]

Metal caps the bottom of this _+1 striking thundering [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/dancers-spear|dancer's spear]]_ and its point gives off the faint smell of ozone. If you hit a target that has been struck by a _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/polarizing-mace|polarizing mace]]_ within the last round, you deal additional electricity damage to the target equal to the number of _grounding spike's_ damage dice. If you critically hit such a target, the creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.

 **Special** The _grounding spike_ pairs with the _polarizing mace_.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 34*
