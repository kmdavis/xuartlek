# Blessed Reformer  *Item 14*

`Divine`  `Holy`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4,500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Warhammer

The Empyreal word for “repent” is etched in golden lettering on the shaft of this _+2 greater striking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/merciful|merciful]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/dawnsilver-object-high-grade|dawnsilver]] warhammer_. If you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]], you are enfeebled 2 while carrying or wielding this weapon.

You cast _protection_ targeting yourself.

 **Redeemer Wielder** If you're a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/champion|champion]] of the redemption cause, you also gain the following two benefits.
