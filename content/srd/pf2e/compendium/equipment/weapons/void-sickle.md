# Void Sickle  *Item 6*

`Magical`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Price**: 235 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/sickle|Sickle]]

This _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|ghost touch]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/sickle|sickle]]_ is made of an impossibly dark steel that absorbs all surrounding light, causing shadows to cling to its blade and those around it to feel an unnatural chill. 

**Activate—Light Harvest** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (darkness, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You swing the _void sickle_ in a wide arc, drawing light into the blade. All non-magical light sources within 30 feet are immediately extinguished, and the next melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] you make with the _void sickle_ before the end of your turn deals an additional 1d6 void damage.
