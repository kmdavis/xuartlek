# Kalis  *Weapon 0*

`Deadly`  `Uncommon`  `Versatile`

- **PFS Note**: Characters from Tian Xia have access to this option
- **Access**: Characters from Jalmeray or Vudra have access to this weapon
- **Price**: 3 gp
- **Damage**: 1d8 S
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Hands**: 1
- **Type**: Melee
- **Category**: Advanced
- **Group**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Sword|Sword]]

A larger version of the asymmetrical, wavy-bladed kris, this double-edged sword is effective at creating grievous injuries.

Certain feats, class features, weapon runes, and other effects can grant you additional benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success.
