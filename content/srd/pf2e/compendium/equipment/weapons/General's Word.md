# General's Word  *Item 14*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4, 500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Mace|Mace]]

The mere act of wielding this heavy _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|+2 greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Thundering|thundering]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Mace|mace]]_ grants you a distinct air of authority and gravitas. While wielding the weapon, you can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/Bullhorn|_bullhorn_]] as a 1st-rank cantrip at will.

**Activate—Battlefield Broadcast** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Linguistic|linguistic]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The weapon amplifies your force of personality across the battlefield with the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/Telepathy|_telepathy_]] for 10 minutes, except the range is 500 feet. The effect ends immediately if you drop your weapon.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _bullhorn_ and _telepathy_.
