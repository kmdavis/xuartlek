# Last Hope  *Item 16*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 10,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/longsword|Longsword]]
- **Access**: A character who is a member of the Knights of Lastwall has access to this weapon.

These _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+2 greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/vitalizing-greater|vitalizing]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/longsword|longswords]]_ are granted as special commendations to Knights of Lastwall who perform acts of exceptional heroism or strike decisive blows against the forces of the Whispering Tyrant.

**Activate—Comes the Dawn** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You regain Hit Points from a magical effect; **Effect** You release a wave of vital energy in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]]. For every 10 Hit Points you are healed, you restore 1d10 Hit Points to all other living creatures and deal that amount of vitality damage to all [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creatures in the area, with a DC 37 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save.
