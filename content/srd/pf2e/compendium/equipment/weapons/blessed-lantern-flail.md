# Blessed Lantern Flail  *Item 13*

`Holy`  `Light`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 3,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Flail

This _+2 striking holy flail_ doubles as both a hooded lantern and a bludgeoning weapon, giving off bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for the next 30 feet when wielded. The lantern’s light can’t be extinguished by non-magical means. It’s said that an enlightened gravekeeper crafted the first _blessed lantern flail_. 

**Activate—Purge Unholy Magic** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** You use the flail to temporarily disrupt an unholy spellcaster’s connection to their magic. Make a melee Strike with the flail against an unholy creature with innate or prepared spells. If you hit and deal damage, the target must attempt a DC 31 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is otherwise unaffected.

**Success** Until the beginning of your next turn, the creature must succeed at a DC 5 flat check to Cast a Spell.

**Failure** As success, but the effect lasts for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the DC of the flat check is 11.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 208*
