# Constricting Meteor  *Item 8*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 450 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/meteor-hammer|Meteor Hammer]]

The weights of this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/weapon-potency-3|+1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/striking-major|striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/meteor-hammer|meteor hammer]]_ are shaped like snake heads, and anaconda scales adorn the chain.

**Activate—Coiling Chain** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Requirements** Your last action was a successful melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]] with the _constricting meteor_; **Effect** The chain of the meteor hammer wraps around the target and squeezes. Attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grapple]] the creature with a +1 item bonus. In addition to being grabbed on a success, the target takes 1d10+7 bludgeoning damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the scales of a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/giant-anaconda|giant anaconda]].
