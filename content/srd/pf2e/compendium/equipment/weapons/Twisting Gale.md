# Twisting Gale  *Item 14*

`Force`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4, 200 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/Sansetsukon|Sansetsukon]]

This +[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|2]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|greater striking]] impactful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/Sansetsukon|sansetsukon]] is made of metal colored pale blue that echoes howling winds when swung and emits the force of a storm when it connects.

**Activate—Sonic Gale** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Force|force]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You critically hit with the twisting gale; **Effect** The energy of your follow-through erupts from your opponent in a wave of concussive energy. You deal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]] damage equal to your normal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] damage with the twisting gale in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Cone|cone]] behind your target. Creatures in the area, not including your target, must attempt a basic Fortitude save against your class DC.
