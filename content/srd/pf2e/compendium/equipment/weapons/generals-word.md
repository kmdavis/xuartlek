# General's Word  *Item 14*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4,500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/mace|Mace]]

The mere act of wielding this heavy _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+2 greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/thundering-greater|thundering]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/mace|mace]]_ grants you a distinct air of authority and gravitas. While wielding the weapon, you can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/bullhorn|_bullhorn_]] as a 1st-rank cantrip at will.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _bullhorn_ and _telepathy_.
