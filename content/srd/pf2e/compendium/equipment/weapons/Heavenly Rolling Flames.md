# Heavenly Rolling Flames  *Item 14*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 4, 500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand each
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/Feng Huo Lun|Feng Huo Lun]]

This set of +[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|2]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Striking|greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Flaming|flaming]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/Feng Huo Lun|feng huo lun]] is warm to the touch and wreathed in the bright red and orange glow of a constantly flickering flame. While wielded, you gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]] [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 2, and you treat the effects of [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Environment#Climate|environmental cold]] as one degree lower. Heavenly rolling flames can be Activated only if you wield two of them, and Activating them counts against the frequency for both weapons.

**Activate—Rolling Flight** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You attach the wheels to your feet, and their constant movement and energy allow you to move through the air. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] of 20 feet for 10 minutes. While in use in this way, you can’t wield the wheels as weapons. You can spend an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] action at any time to end this effect and re-grip the heavenly rolling flames. If you lack enough free hands to wield them, you drop them in your space.

**Activate—Speed of Heaven** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/Haste|_haste_]] as a 3rd-rank [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3) spell, but you can target only yourself.
