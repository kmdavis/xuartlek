# Mageslayer  *Item 8*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 500 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/scimitar|Scimitar]]

This roughly forged _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/scimitar|scimitar]]_ is a favorite instrument of battlefield operatives seeking to weaken an enemy’s forces by strategically eliminating their magical support. A mageslayer deals an additional 1d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage to any creature it Strikes that’s capable of casting spells from the arcane tradition. When wearing or wielding a mageslayer, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|resistance]] 5 to damage from spells from the arcane tradition.
