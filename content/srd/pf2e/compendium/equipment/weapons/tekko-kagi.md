# Tekko-Kagi  *Weapon 0*

`Agile`  `Disarm`  `Finesse`  `Free-Hand`  `Monk`  `Parry`  `Uncommon`

- **PFS Note**: Characters from Tian Xia have access to this option
- **Favored Weapon**: Bastet, Essence Dancers, Suyuddha, Uirch
- **Price**: 2 sp
- **Damage**: 1d4 S
- **Bulk**: L
- **Hands**: 1
- **Type**: Melee
- **Category**: Martial
- **Group**: Brawling

Four curved blades attached to a sturdy handlebar give the wielder of this close-combat weapon the illusion of having claws that extend from their fist. Adherents of Bastet favor the tekko-kagi for catching their foes off guard.

### Critical Specialization Effects

Certain feats, class features, weapon runes, and other effects can grant you additional benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success.
