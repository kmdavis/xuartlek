# Dragontooth Leiomano  *Item 13*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 3,000 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/leiomano-weapon-292|Leiomano]]

Dragon teeth line the edges of this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+2 greater striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/leiomano-weapon-292|leiomano]]_ . The leiomano deals an additional 1d6 damage of a type determined by the tradition of the dragon from which the teeth were taken: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]] for arcane, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] for divine, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] for occult, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]] for primal. The weapon also gains the relevant trait (for instance, fire for a club made with teeth taken from an [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/dragon/young-adamantine-dragon|adamantine dragon]]).

**Activate** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You swing the leiomano, sending several of the dragon teeth shooting through the air on jets of energy. The dragon teeth deal 3d6 piercing damage and 3d6 damage of the damage type corresponding to the dragon’s tradition in a 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]] (DC 29 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/defenses#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save). The teeth hunt down their targets, correcting their flight in midair, which reduces any circumstance bonus from [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Cover|cover]] by 2.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include teeth from a dragon.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 36*
