# Thundercrasher  *Item 5*

`Arcane`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 155 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/firearm/blunderbuss|Blunderbuss]]

While looking straight down the barrel of this _+1 striking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/firearm/blunderbuss|blunderbuss]]_, the spark gun's magical core is visible amid several reflectors. All damage dealt by a _thundercrasher_ is sonic damage. On a critical hit, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You overload the _thundercrasher_ to emit chaotic sonic frequencies that soften earth and stone. When you next fire the _thundercrasher_ it also partially liquefies any natural earth or stone surfaces within range of its [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/guns-gears-remastered/scatter|scatter]] trait, making the area difficult terrain.
