# Bow of Sun Slaying  *Item 20*

`Artifact`  `Cold`  `Divine`  `Unique`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/bow/Composite Shortbow|Composite Shortbow]]

The Bow of Sun Slaying is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|+3]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Magic Weapon|major striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Frost|greater frost]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/bow/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]] constructed out of wood, horn, and sinew. It bears carvings of a long-forgotten demigod who legends say possessed the ability to destroy the sun with a single arrow.

**Activate—Darkness for My Foes** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** Your last action was a successful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against your foe with this weapon; **Effect** You whisper, “Darkness for my foes,” and the target of your last attack takes 10d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]] damage and 10d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Spirit|spirit]] damage (DC 45 basic Will save).
