# Dragon-Mouth Pistol  *Weapon 0*

`Concussive`  `Scatter`  `Uncommon`

- **PFS Note**: All Pathfinder Society agents have access to all uncommon weapons, ammunition, and related items from Chapter 4
- **Price**: 7 gp
- **Damage**: 1d6 P
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Hands**: 1
- **Range**: 20 ft.
- **Reload**: 1
- **Type**: Ranged
- **Category**: Martial
- **Group**: Firearm
- **Ammunition**: Firearm Ammunition (10 rounds)

Similar to the blunderbuss, a dragon-mouth pistol fires pellets from a flared barrel. Though less powerful than a blunderbuss, the dragonmouth pistol is appreciated for its portability and onehanded design. The name was coined because of the destructive belch of this handheld scatter weapon, but many gunsmiths craft dragon-mouth pistols with elaborate embellishments that resemble a stylized dragon's maw framing the barrel.
