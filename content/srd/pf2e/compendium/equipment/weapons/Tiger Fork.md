# Tiger Fork  *Item 5*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 275 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/Trident|Trident]]

The tiger fork is a +[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|1]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/spear/Trident|trident]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Disarm|disarm]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Grapple|grapple]] traits. It has wide, flaring prongs that can be used to fend off deadly beasts and entrap opponents during combat. While you have another creature [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Grabbed|Grabbed]] with the tiger fork, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Forced Movement|forced movement]] effects.

**Activate—Fork Grip** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike against a creature; **Effect** You can activate the tiger fork to attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grapple]] the creature. This attempt uses the same multiple attack penalty as the required Strike.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 130*
