# South Wind's Scorch Song  *Item 10*

`Fire`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hands
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Scimitar

This _+1 striking flaming scimitar_ is always warm to the touch, nearly unbearably so. Lines of crackling flame radiate from carnelians affixed to its blackened blade. While you have a status bonus to at least one of your Speeds, your Strikes with this scimitar that deal fire damage gain a +2 status bonus to their fire damage. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking_ rune and +4 for a _major striking_ rune. 

**Activate—Warming Zephyr** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (air, fire, magical, manipulate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You deal fire damage to a creature with a spell or ability; **Effect** You point the _south wind’s scorch song_ upward, summoning a wind from nowhere that grants you a +10-foot status bonus to your Speeds until the end of your next turn.
