# Debilitating Dart  *Item 9*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 650 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/dart/dart|Dart]]

Forged from black steel, this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/magic-weapon-3-major-striking|+1 striking]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/returning|returning]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/dart/dart|dart]]_ is etched with runes that shimmer with an iridescent glow. A creature struck and damaged by this dart takes a –2 status penalty to its next saving throw against a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]] effect within 1 round. The target then becomes immune to this effect for 24 hours.
