# Debilitating Dart  *Item 9*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 650 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: Dart

Forged from black steel, this _+1 striking returning dart_ is etched with runes that shimmer with an iridescent glow. A creature struck and damaged by this dart takes a –2 status penalty to its next saving throw against a magical effect within 1 round. The target then becomes immune to this effect for 24 hours.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 209*
