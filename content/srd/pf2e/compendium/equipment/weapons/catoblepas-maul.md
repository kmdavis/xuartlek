# Catoblepas Maul  *Item 13*

`Magical`  `Poison`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 2,800 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/hammer/maul|Maul]]

The putrid stench emanating from the catoblepas antlers adorning this _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/weapon-potency-3|+2]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/striking-major|greater striking]] greater crushing [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/hammer/maul|maul]]_ interferes with your sense of smell. While wielding this weapon, you gain a +2 item bonus to saves against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]] effects.

**Activate—Vile Stench** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]]) **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with the _catoblepas maul_; **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]] 1 (plus [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 for as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save becomes temporarily immune to Vile Stench for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include antlers from a catoblepas.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 101*
