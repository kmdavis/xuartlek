# Fighter's Fork  *Item 3*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 50 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 or 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Trident

This _+1 trident_, usually engraved with a decorative pattern resembling fish scales, is a common weapon among warriors of aquatic ancestries. 

**Activate—Fluid Length** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You extend or shorten the trident's haft. When extended, the trident requires two hands to wield and gains the reach trait, but loses the trident's normal thrown trait.
