**Activate—Bolt of War** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ; **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** Make a Strike with the _Jistkan war crossbow_, ignoring the penalty for making a Strike within your second or third range increment.
