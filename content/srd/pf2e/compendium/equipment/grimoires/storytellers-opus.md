# Storyteller's Opus  *Item 6*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 230 gp
- **Bulk**: L

This green spellbook with gold trim contains a cautionary story about a boy who was eaten by a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/wolf|wolf]] after he previously lied about wolf attacks. When opened, the engravings on the front seem to move in a loop, enacting this story. 

**Activate—Hoodwink** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature succeeds, but doesn’t critically succeed, at a Perception check to disbelieve an illusion spell you’ve cast today; **Effect** You quickly tell a fib to try to smooth over the inconsistencies in your illusion. Attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] check against the triggering creature’s [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Perception|Perception]] DC. If you succeed, the creature doesn’t disbelieve the illusion.
