# Bloodstained Treatise  *Item 7*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 325 gp
- **Bulk**: L

The white leather cover of this book is splotched with rusty red bloodstains, and its pages seep red when turned, although this never stains the bearer’s hands or clothing. Anatomical drawings illustrate its pages. 

**Activate—Sanguine Expulsion** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared Spell that manipulates blood, such as __blood vendetta__ or __vampiric exsanguination__, or a spell dealing piercing or slashing damage, all targets of the triggering spell take 1d6 persistent bleed damage; if the spell already causes bleed damage, the bleed damage increases by 1d6. The wounds of one affected target (chosen by you) gush uncontrollably, covering the ground with blood in a 5-foot emanation that lasts for 1 round. Creatures that begin their turn in the blood must succeed at a Reflex save against your spell DC or fall prone.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 228*
