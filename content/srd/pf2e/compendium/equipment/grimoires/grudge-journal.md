# Grudge Journal  *Item 11*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,350 gp
- **Bulk**: L

The margins of this clothbound journal’s pages are filled with complaints on all manner of topics, shifting to match whatever angers the bearer most. 

**Activate—Air Grievance** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/curse|curse]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You inscribe the name or description of a creature you can see that has wronged you in some way. If your next action is to target that creature with a prepared spell with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/curse|curse]] trait that requires a saving throw, the creature also becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Drained|drained]] 2 unless it critically succeeds its saving throw.
