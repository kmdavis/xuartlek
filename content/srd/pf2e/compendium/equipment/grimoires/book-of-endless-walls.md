# Book Of Endless Walls  *Item 14*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 3,900 gp
- **Bulk**: L

A circular labyrinth is etched onto this tome’s cover, its lines shimmering silver. Touching the book causes a sense of faint discombobulation. 

**Activate—Bewildering Walls** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a prepared Wall Spell]], such as _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/wall-of-fire|_wall of fire_]]_ or _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/wall-of-thorns|_wall of thorns_]]_, you imbue the wall with an uncanny sense of disorientation, causing creatures to perceive it as an endless labyrinth. Any creature that begins its turn within the wall’s spaces or passes through the wall’s spaces must attempt a Will save against your spell DC, in addition to the wall’s normal effect. Regardless of the results, the target then becomes immune to Bewildering Walls for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The wall has no additional effect for that creature.

**Success** The creature is bewildered by its perceptions of labyrinthine walls and treats all other creatures as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature treats all other creatures as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature treats all other creatures as hidden for 1 round and concealed for 1 minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 228*
