# Calligraphic Illumination  *Item 5*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 135 gp
- **Bulk**: L

An ever-shifting series of calligraphic runes scrawls across the pages of this leather-bound book, as if written by an unseen hand. _Calligraphic illuminations_ are sacred to the faith of Irez, Lady of Inscribed Wonder. 

**Activate—Inscribed Insight** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your last action was to Cast a prepared Spell that creates or manipulates written text or runes, such as _runic body_, the writing briefly forms into words conveying valuable information. Attempt to Recall Knowledge about any one creature or object that you can see with a +2 item bonus to the check.
