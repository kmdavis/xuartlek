# Book of Warding Prayers  *Item 8*

`Divine`  `Grimoire`

- **Price**: 425 gp
- **Bulk**: L

Script in a language of the Outer Planes adorns this book's spine, and a deity's symbol is etched on its cover. _Books of warding prayers_ are each devoted to a particular deity and are also a religious symbol of that deity.

**Activate** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your last action was to cast a prepared divine spell granted by your deity; **Effect** You and all your allies in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage for 1 minute. You can instead choose to grant resistance to holy effects instead of spirit damage if the spell you cast had the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] trait, or resistance to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] effects instead of spirit if your spell had the unholy trait.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 111*
