# Empathy Charm  *Item 2*

`Companion`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 35 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to harness

This charm, usually placed on an animal companion's collar, contains a single strand of your hair, as well as one of your animal companion's, creating a link that better transmits emotional cues to a trained psychological assistance animal.

**Activate** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/emotion|emotion]] effect; **Requirements** Your animal companion wearing the _empathy charm_ is within 10 feet; **Effect** Your animal companion senses the effect and attempts to calm you. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus against the triggering save.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 105*
