# Olfactory Stimulators  *Item 1+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: worn

This cluster of sensitive wire whiskers fits over the nose or snout to provide sensory information as it reacts to nearby odors and other scents. A creature wearing _olfactory stimulators_ gains a sense of smell, which is as precise as an average member of its species, as well as the scent special ability if members of its species typically have that ability. _Olfactory stimulators_ can be fitted to animal companions as well as sapient creatures; stimulators produced for companion use have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/companion|companion]] trait.
