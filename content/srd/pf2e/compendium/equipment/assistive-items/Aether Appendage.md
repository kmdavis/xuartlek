# Aether Appendage  *Item 5*

`Incorporeal`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 160 gp
- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: L

This otherworldly prosthetic arm is the product of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] magic. An _aether appendage_ is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] so long as no item is being held in the hand or worn on the arm.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Effect** You cause the arm to become corporeal until the end of your turn, allowing you to use it to make Strikes or grasp objects. It remains corporeal if you’re holding an item or it’s wearing an item at the end of your turn. Your unarmed attack Strikes made with the aether appendage are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]]. If they’re already magical, they instead gain the effect of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You're holding a non-magical item of light or negligible Bulk; **Effect** The item becomes incorporeal for 1 minute. Your _aether appendage_ can use the incorporeal item normally.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 108*
