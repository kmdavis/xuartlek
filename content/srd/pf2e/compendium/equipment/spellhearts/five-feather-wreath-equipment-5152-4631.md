# Five-feather Wreath  *Item 4+*

`Air`  `Magical`  `Spellheart`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

Identical feathers radiate from the center of this spellheart, held in place by woven straw. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 18.
**• Armor** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 2 to falling damage.
**• Weapon** After you cast an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/air|air]] spell by Activating the _wreath_, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|Fly]] 5 feet as a single action, or you can Fly 10 feet as a free action if the spell you cast wasn’t a cantrip. This must be your next action, and if you don’t have a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]], you must end the flight on solid ground or fall. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/gale-blast|_gale blast_]]_.
