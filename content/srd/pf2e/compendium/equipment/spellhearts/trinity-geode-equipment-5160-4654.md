# Trinity Geode  *Item 3+*

`Earth`  `Magical`  `Spellheart`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

The crystal chamber within this split geode is divided into three lobes of equal size. The crystals within are usually a deep purple, though other colors are prevalent in more remote climates. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 17.
**• Armor** After you cast an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/earth|earth]] spell by Activating the _geode_, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 1 to physical damage (except [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/adamantine-object-high-grade|adamantine]]) until the end of your next turn, or double the resistance for a non-cantrip spell.
**• Weapon** After you cast an earth spell by Activating the _geode_, your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 bludgeoning damage until the end of your next turn. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/scatter-scree|_scatter scree_]]._
