# Greater Silver Tongue  *Item 8+*

`Magical`  `Spellheart`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

This tongue-shaped silver trinket typically dangles from a silver chain. It seems to catch the light wherever it is affixed. When affixed to armor or a weapon with any metal components, it makes those pieces gleam brightly. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 24.
**• Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/linguistic|linguistic]] effects.
**• Weapon** After you cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] spell by Activating the _silver tongue_, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] until the end of your next turn. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/daze|_daze_]]_.
