# Perfect Droplet  *Item 3+*

`Magical`  `Spellheart`  `Water`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

Intense blue water magically holds its shape—a perfect sphere. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 17.
**• Armor** You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 2 to water effects and attacks by water creatures.
**• Weapon** After you cast a water spell by Activating the _droplet_, your body becomes mist-like. Until the end of the turn, you can move through enemies’ spaces, treating each square in their spaces as [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. You can’t move through creatures that have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]] trait in this way. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/spout|_spout_]]._
