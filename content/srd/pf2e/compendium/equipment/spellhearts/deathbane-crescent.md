# Deathbane Crescent  *Item 3+*

`Magical`  `Spellheart`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

This crescent moon carved out of bone holds the power to turn back the undead. This spellheart covers any item it’s affixed to with spiderwebs that reappear even if damaged or removed. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 18.
**• Armor** After you cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] spell by Activating the crescent, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 2 to attacks and effects from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creatures until the end of your next turn.
**• Weapon** After you cast a vitality spell by Activating the crescent, your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 vitality damage until the end of your next turn. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/vitality-lash|_vitality lash_]]._
