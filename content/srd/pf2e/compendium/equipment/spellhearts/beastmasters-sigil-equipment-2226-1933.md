# Beastmaster's Sigil  *Item 5+*

`Magical`  `Spellheart`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

This silver disc displays an ever-changing etching of an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|animal]]. When you affix the _beastmaster's sigil_, the animal settles into a single form based on where you affix it, showing the animal the item can summon when affixed that way. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 19.

 A weapon can benefit from only the melee weapon benefit or ranged weapon benefit. A [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/thrown|thrown]] melee weapon gets the melee weapon benefit, and a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/treasure-vault-remastered/combination|combination]] weapon gets only one benefit, which you choose when you affix the spellheart. If it's unclear which one should apply, the GM decides. 
**Armor** The sigil grants you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]].**Melee Weapon** The sigil grants you a +1 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] checks to Trip.**Ranged Weapon** If you critically hit with the affixed weapon, and the target is adjacent to a creature summoned with the sigil, the target takes 1d6 persistent bleed damage.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You cast _tame_.

### Beastmaster's Sigil

**Price** 160 gp

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cast 2nd-rank _summon animal_ to summon a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/hunting-spider|hunting spider]] (armor), wolf (melee weapon), or bat swarm (ranged weapon).

### Beastmaster's Sigil (Greater)

**Price** 360 gp

---

The spell DC is 23 and the bleed damage is 2d6.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cast 3rd-rank _summon animal_ to summon a giant monitor lizard (armor), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/boar|boar]] (melee), or giant bat (ranged).

### Beastmaster's Sigil (Major)

**Price** 700 gp

---

The spell DC is 25, the item bonuses are +2, and the bleed damage is 3d6.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cast 4th-rank _summon animal_ to summon a giant scorpion (armor), dire wolf (melee), or fen mosquito swarm (ranged).
