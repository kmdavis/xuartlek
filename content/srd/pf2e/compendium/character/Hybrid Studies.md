# Hybrid Studies

## Aloof Firmament

You're a philosopher of the sword who codifies your study of qinggong along principles arcane and martial. Your blade teaches morality and immortality, and you illustrate its lessons with an academician's rigor. Through your training, you've learned to move about with grace and create opportunity to strike foes.

 You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Cat Fall|Cat Fall]] general feat, though you benefit from it only as long as you aren't using a shield and are wearing light or no armor.

 While in Arcane Cascade stance, your steps become even lighter, giving you the benefits of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Quick Jump|Quick Jump]] general feat. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|Fly]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]] while in that stance, your movement doesn't trigger reactions. If you take one of these actions, the next Strike you make before the end of your turn with a one-handed weapon in the sword group has greater damage from Arcane Cascade. Increase the extra damage to 4; increase it to 6 if you have weapon specialization, or to 8 if you have greater weapon specialization. You must have your other hand completely free; the extra damage doesn't apply if you have a free-hand weapon or other item in that hand, even if you would normally be able to use the hand for other things.

## Inexorable Iron

Once you begin along a path, nothing can stop you from reaching its end. You transform the mass of an enormous weapon into an unstoppable force to augment your own striking power or keep you standing on the battlefield.

 When you enter [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance and at the start of each of your turns while you're in that stance, if you're wielding a melee weapon in two hands, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level (minimum 1 temporary HP).

## Laughing Shadow

Magic is freeing, a means to your ends, and you can use it to go where you want, do as you please, and avoid the consequences. You are a laughing shadow of spell and blade, always one step ahead of your foes, always with a trick up your sleeve.

 While in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance, you gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speeds, or a +10-foot bonus if you're unarmored. If you Strike an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature while you're in the stance and have a free hand, that Strike's extra damage from Arcane Cascade is increased to 3. The increased extra damage is 5 if you have weapon specialization, or 7 if you have greater weapon specialization. You must have your other hand completely free; the extra damage doesn't apply if you have a free-hand weapon or other item in that hand, even if you would normally be able to use the hand for other things.

## Sparkling Targe

You've studied the applications of magic, training yourself to perform not just offensive maneuvers but defensive tactics as well. When magic flows through you, your shield can block impossible things, even a dragon's breath or a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Force Barrage|_force barrage_]].

 You gain the general [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Shield Block|Shield Block]] feat.

 When you're in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance with your shield raised, your circumstance bonus to AC from your shield also applies to your saves against spells and other magical effects. In addition, damage you take from a spell or other magical effect while you're in Arcane Cascade can trigger your Shield Block reaction, even if the damage isn't physical. When blocking damage from a magical effect in this way, increase your shield's Hardness by an amount equal to the resistance you have from Arcane Cascade. These benefits apply whether you're using an actual shield, the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/Shield|_shield_]] spell, or something else that works like a shield (such as a raised tome if you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/Raise a Tome|Raise a Tome]] feat).

## Starlit Span

With magic, the sky's the limit, and you can't be bound by the confines of physical proximity. Your power reaches as far as your senses can perceive, transcending the space between you and your target even with spells that normally require direct physical contact.

 When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Spellstrike|Spellstrike]], you can make a ranged weapon or ranged unarmed Strike, as long as the target is within the first range increment of your ranged weapon or ranged unarmed attack. This overrides the actual range of the spell.

 While you're in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance, your ranged attacks ignore any [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Cover|lesser cover]] your allies would grant to your target.

## Twisting Tree

The staff is perhaps the simplest of weapons, but this simplicity belies its elegance and versatility. To you, a staff is casting implement and martial weapon alike—the foundation of a fighting style.

 While you wield a staff in one hand, the staff adjusts in shape and weight, gaining the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Agile|agile]] trait and increasing its damage die size to 1d6. While you wield a staff in both hands, it lengthens, twists, and reshapes, gaining the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Parry|parry]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Reach|reach]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Trip|trip]] traits.

 While you're in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance, once per round you can Interact to change your grip on a staff as a free action.

## Twofold Tine

Magic dances between the two weapons you wield with equally deadly grace. Fighting groups of enemies takes full advantage of your capabilities as you move from one foe to another.

 While in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]] stance, if you make a Strike with a melee weapon you're holding, you can intensify the stance's power for another melee weapon of your choice you're holding in a different hand. Until the end of your turn, any extra damage the chosen weapon gains from Arcane Cascade is increased to 2. The extra damage increases to 4 if you have weapon specialization or 6 if you have greater weapon specialization.

## Unfurling Brocade

You disdain others’ reliance on crude contrivances of iron, instead favoring the elegance of fabric, with thread and brocade your panoply. Your mastery of qinggong allows qi-infused fabric to tear flesh, bind blades, and pierce steel.

 You can create a special weapon, qi-infused fabric, by Interacting to grip a length of cloth of 1 Bulk or less and filling it with your qi. If you Interact to draw the object, you can do this as part of that action. For most unfurling brocade magi, this item is a scarf, sash, ribbon, or a similarly voluminous article of worn clothing, such as long sleeves. You can use the fabric as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/flail/Bladed Scarf|bladed scarf]]. Unlike a normal bladed scarf, you have the option to wield the fabric in one hand instead of two. Its damage die is d4 if held in one hand. If you’re wearing handwraps of mighty blows, you can apply any of its runes to the fabric, provided those runes could be added to a bladed scarf. This begins when you wield the fabric and ends when you cease doing so.

 While in Arcane Cascade stance, the magical energy flowing through your fabric partially animates it to accomplish an even greater range of tricks.

If you critically succeed at an Athletics check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Disarm|Disarm]], you can spend a single action immediately afterward to flick your fabric, causing the disarmed object to fall to the ground in your space rather than in your opponent’s.Your fabric gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Grapple|grapple]] trait. If you succeed at an Athletics check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grapple]] using your fabric, you can spend a single action immediately afterward to pull the creature 5 feet toward you.When you attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]] a foe using your fabric, the target can be two sizes larger than you instead of one.

## Volatile Spark

Your crashing spells meet their match with booming gunshots in a magical fusillade. By modifying your shots with your magic, you create explosions and cut through defenses.

 When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Spellstrike|Spellstrike]], you can make a ranged firearm Strike, as long as the target is within the first range increment of your firearm. This overrides the actual range of the spell. If your firearm has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/guns-gears-remastered/Concussive|concussive]] trait, any damage from the spell you use in the Spellstrike uses the target's weakest resistance to any of the following: the spell's damage type, piercing, or bludgeoning. This benefit applies only to creatures you target with your firearm Strike during the Spellstrike, so it wouldn't apply to other creatures damaged with Expansive Spellstrike or similar abilities.

 In addition, you gain the Reloading Cascade action.
 **Reloading Cascade** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") You use Arcane Cascade and Interact to reload a firearm.
