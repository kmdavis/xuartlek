# Research Fields

## Bomber

You specialize in explosions and other violent reactions.

### Formulas

Two common 1st-level [alchemical bombs](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=6&Subcategory=7).

### Field Benefit

When throwing an alchemical bomb with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Splash|splash]] trait, you can choose to deal splash damage to only your primary target instead of the usual splash area.

### Field Vials

When you Strike with a versatile vial, you can choose to have it deal cold, electricity, or fire damage instead of acid damage.

### Field Discovery

You have calculated all the angles to maximize a bomb’s splash. When you throw an alchemical bomb with the splash trait, you can cause the bomb to deal splash damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) instead of the normal amount.

### Advanced Vials

You can mix special ingredients into your vials, such as magical dust or flakes of precious minerals. When you create a versatile vial, you can choose to have it treated as one special material of your choice, chosen from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Adamantine|adamantine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Cold Iron|cold iron]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]]. If you’re wearing or wielding an item of a different precious material when you make the vial, you can choose that material instead.

### Greater Field Discovery

You can increase the splash on your bombs to damage creatures within 10 feet, or 15 feet if you have [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Expanded Splash|Expanded Splash]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 61*

## Chirurgeon

You concentrate on healing others with alchemy.

### Formulas

Two common 1st-level [alchemical elixirs](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=6&Subcategory=8) with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] trait (like lesser antidote, lesser antiplague, or minor elixir of life).

### Field Benefit

You can use your proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] for anything that requires a proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] (such as prerequisites) and use your Crafting modifier in place of your Medicine modifier for all Medicine checks._ Paizo ClarificationWith the chirurgeon's field benefit, it might not always be clear how to apply bonuses and penalties when you're attempting a Medicine check that uses your Crafting modifier. You can apply any bonus that would affect either your Crafting modifier or Medicine modifier to the check as long as it's relevant to the action. For example:

The +1 item bonus to Crafting checks from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/Crafter's Eyepiece|crafter's eyepiece]] applies to all your Medicine checks, as does the +1 bonus to Medicine checks from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/Healer's Gloves|healer's gloves]]. If you were wearing both items, you'd still apply only one item bonus, as normal.

If a bonus or ability applies only to specific actions or categories of actions, that restriction still applies as normal. So healer's gloves wouldn't apply to an attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]], since you're not substituting your Crafting modifier for a Medicine check—you're just using it for a normal Crafting activity. An expanded alchemist's lab wouldn't grant you a bonus to when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Wounds|Treat Wounds]] using your Crafting modifier, since it applies only when crafting alchemical items, not to all Crafting checks.

The same broad application applies to penalties. For example, if a curse gives you a status penalty to all Crafting checks, it would also apply to your Crafting check to Treat Wounds. If stage 2 of the curse gives you a status penalty to all Medicine checks, the penalty would apply if you were to Treat Wounds, even if you were using Crafting instead of Medicine. The stage 2 penalty wouldn't apply if you tried to Repair something.

 The field benefit is meant to let you use Crafting in place of Medicine broadly, and not face a lot of restrictions because of the replacement. As always, the GM might make exceptions in certain cases. 

### Field Vials

Your versatile vials can be used to heal a living creature a number of Hit Points equal to the vial’s initial damage. A creature can drink the vial for this benefit, or you can throw the vial at a willing creature within 20 feet as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] action to heal that creature. In either case, a vial used this way loses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Splash|splash]] traits and gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Coagulant|coagulant]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] traits, plus the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Elixir|elixir]] trait if a creature drinks it.

### Field Discovery

Your medicinal elixirs are quite fortifying. When a creature drinks an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Infused|infused]] elixir with the healing trait that you have created, that creature gains a number of [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0); these temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.

### Advanced Vials

When you use a field vial to heal a creature that has half its maximum Hit Points or fewer, the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Coagulant|coagulant]] trait doesn’t apply to that healing.

### Greater Field Discovery

When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Quick Alchemy|Quick Alchemy]] to create any type of elixir of life, the creature healed by the item regains the maximum Hit Points possible, instead of rolling to determine the number of Hit Points regained.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 61*

## Mutagenist

You focus on bizarre mutagenic transformations that sacrifice one aspect of a creature’s physical or psychological being in order to strengthen another.

### Formulas

Two common 1st-level [alchemical mutagens](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=6&Subcategory=8).

### Field Benefit

When you use a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Mutagen|mutagen]], you gain a number of [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0) plus half your level. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute or until the mutagen’s duration expires, whichever comes first. You can’t gain temporary HP from drinking a mutagen again for 1 minute.

### Field Vials

You can drink the contents of one your versatile vials to suppress the drawback you take from one mutagen currently affecting you for 1 minute. A vial used this way loses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Bomb|bomb]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Splash|splash]] traits and gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Elixir|elixir]] trait. If you have more than one drawback due to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/Combine Elixirs|Combine Elixirs]] or a similar ability, drinking the vial suppresses one drawback of your choice.

### Field Discovery

The mutagens flowing through your blood have altered your body chemistry. When you roll a Fortitude save while affected by a mutagen and dislike the result, you can end the effect of the mutagen to reroll the save. This is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fortune|fortune]] effect.

### Advanced Vials

When you drink one of your field vials, you also gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to physical damage for the same duration. The resistance is equal to half your level.

### Greater Field Discovery

If you imbibe another mutagen while you are under the effects of a mutagen, you can gain the benefits and the drawbacks of both mutagens at once, despite the fact that they both have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Polymorph|polymorph]] trait and would not normally function together. If you come under the effects of any further mutagens while benefiting from two mutagens, you lose the benefit of one of the former mutagens of your choice, while retaining the drawbacks of all the mutagens. If you are under the effects of two mutagens and you come under the effect of a non-mutagen polymorph effect, you lose the benefits of the mutagens while retaining the drawbacks of both. Your field vial benefit applies to one mutagen of your choice.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 61*

## Toxicologist

You specialize in toxins and venoms of all types. 

### Formulas

Two common 1st-level [alchemical poisons](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=6&Subcategory=9).

### Field Benefit

You can Activate an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Injury|injury]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Poison|poison]] as a single action, provided you apply it to a weapon you're holding or a piece of ammunition on your person or loaded in a weapon you're holding. This can be an item you're holding or you can Interact to draw an eligible item or make one using Quick Alchemy as part of the single action; if you can make more than one item with Quick Alchemy (such as with the double brew class feature), you can still Activate only one.

 In addition, you flexibly mix acidic and poisonous alchemical compounds. Your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Infused|infused]] items with the poison trait can affect creatures [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Immunity|immune]] to poison. A creature takes acid damage instead of poison damage from such items if either the creature is immune to poison or that would be more detrimental to the creature (as determined by the GM).

### Field Vials

Your versatile vials have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Poison|poison]] trait and deal poison damage instead of having the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]] trait and dealing acid damage (though your field benefit still applies). You can apply the contents of a versatile vial to a weapon or piece of ammunition as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Injury|injury]] poison. Add the versatile vial’s initial damage to the first successful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with that weapon or ammunition. The substance becomes inert at the end of your current turn.

### Field Discovery

You have handled enough poisons to become inured to their effects. You gain poison [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] equal to half your level.

### Advanced Vials

When you damage a creature with a versatile vial you’ve used as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Injury|injury]] poison, that creature takes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent poison damage]] equal to the vial’s splash damage in addition to the initial damage.

### Greater Field Discovery

When a creature fails its initial saving throw against an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Infused|infused]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Injury|injury]] poison you created, the wound sprays poison onto another creature adjacent to it. The attacker who caused the injury chooses which creature, if there’s more than one, and can choose to forgo this effect. That creature is exposed to the poison. The second creature doesn’t spread the poison further.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 62*
