## Ahriman  *Deity*

- **PFS Note**: This deity has the must choose unholy sanctification and is therefore restricted for society play._ The dread shadow known as Ahriman counts servants mainly among div as well as a scattering of followers throughout the mortal realm.
- **Category**: Other Gods
- **Edicts**: Foil rulers, the proud, and the powerful; ruin anything created by mortals
- **Anathema**: Create arts or crafts, serve a mortal, assist in mortal aims except to subvert and corrupt them
- **Area of Concern**: Destruction, divs, nihilism
