# Styles

## Battledancer  *Swashbuckler Style*

To you, a fight is a kind of performance art, and you command your foes’ attention with mesmerizing motions. You are trained in Performance and gain the Fascinating Performance skill feat. When you Perform, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

You can Step as a free action immediately after the finisher.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 160*

## Braggart  *Swashbuckler Style*

You boast, taunt, and psychologically needle your foes. You are trained in Intimidation. When you Demoralize, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

If the foe was temporarily immune to your
Demoralize, their temporary immunity ends.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 160*

## Fencer  *Swashbuckler Style*

You move carefully, feinting and creating false openings to lead your foes into inopportune attacks. You are trained in Deception. When you Create a Diversion or Feint, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

The foe is off-guard until your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*

## Gymnast  *Swashbuckler Style*

You reposition, maneuver, and bewilder your foes with daring feats of physical prowess. You are trained in Athletics. When you Grapple, Reposition, Shove, or Trip, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

If the foe is grabbed, restrained, or prone, you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to double the number of weapon damage dice.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*

## Rascal  *Swashbuckler Style*

You aren’t afraid to use underhanded tactics to get the edge over your opponents. You are trained in Thievery and gain the Dirty Trick general feat. When you use Dirty Trick, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

The foe takes a –10 circumstance penalty to its speed until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*

## Wit  *Swashbuckler Style*

You are friendly, clever, and full of humor, always knowing just what to say. Your witticisms leave foes unprepared for the skill and speed of your attacks. You are trained in Diplomacy and gain the Bon Mot skill feat. When you use Bon Mot, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

The foe takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*
