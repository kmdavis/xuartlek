# Warrior Of Legend  *Archetype 2*

`Uncommon`

#### Warrior of Legend 1st

You have received a blessing that will ensure your ascension to legend, and a curse that foretells your inevitable fall. You must select [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/Warrior Of Legend Dedication|Warrior of Legend Dedication]] as your 2nd-level class feat. 

**Prerequisites:** You must be a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. 

**Warrior of Legend Adjustments:** You aren’t trained in heavy armor. The armor expertise and armor mastery class features only improve your proficiency rank for light armor, medium armor, and unarmored defense. 

You don’t gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Shield Block|Shield Block]] as a class feature. 

You are trained in both [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], rather than one or the other.

Choose one physical damage type, which becomes your cursed weakness: bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weakness]] to this damage type equal to half your level (minimum 1).

You gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Diehard|Diehard]] as a bonus feat, but you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Doomed|doomed]] 2 whenever you take damage from your cursed weakness (unless your doomed value is already higher).

Your fighter weapon mastery and weapon legend class features grant you increased proficiency in both [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Spear|spears]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Polearm|polearms]], but you can’t choose any other weapon group with these class features.
