# Ranger  *Archetype 2*

#### Multiclass Ranger characters

The ranger archetype has access to excellent options to improve a character’s monster knowledge and survival skills, but ranger is of particular interest to any character wanting to become a dedicated archer. 
[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/bard|Bard]] rangers support their allies and animal companions with expanded combat options. The bard’s knowledge can also complement the ranger’s investigation of their foes.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/cleric|Cleric]] rangers are particularly fitting for deities who connect thematically to nature or have a bow as their favored weapon. (Or, in the case of Erastil, both!)[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/druid|Druids]] have the most thematic overlap with rangers among the spellcasters, allowing you to create a true master of the wilderness.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/fighter|Fighter]] rangers are among the most fearsome archers, combining the feats from both classes to create a fighting style unique to each fighter ranger. Some warden spells can also be particularly helpful to a fighter.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/rogue|Rogue]] rangers make excellent snipers or ranged skirmishers, with their rogue skills naturally playing into the ranger’s strengths.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/witch|Witch]] rangers add a new prowess scouting to the information they can gain through their familiar and patron.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/wizard|Wizard]] rangers benefit greatly from ranger’s feat support, backing up their spells with ranged weapon attacks.
