# Sorcerer  *Archetype 2*

#### Multiclass Sorcerer Characters

The sorcerer archetype is a method for anyone to pick up commonly useful spells, such as _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/invisibility|_invisibility_]]_ and _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/haste|_haste_]]_. You might choose to become a sorcerer with a bloodline that matches other aspects of your character, such as the dragon bloodline if you have the dragonblood versatile heritage or the undead bloodline if you're a dhampir.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 181*
