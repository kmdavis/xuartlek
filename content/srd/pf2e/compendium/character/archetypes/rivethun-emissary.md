# Rivethun Emissary  *Archetype 2*

`Uncommon`

- **PFS Note**: Rivethun is a philosophy which characters may choose as their religion. Doing so fulfills the “follower of Rivethun” access condition for relevant options._ Rivethun emissaries form bonds with external spirits, using their own spiritual power as an anchor. This is a diplomatic endeavor, not a sycophantic one; they form mutual alliances and bonds of friendship and respect, they don't swear their service. In this way, Rivethun emissaries serve as intermediaries between the Universe and the spirit world.
