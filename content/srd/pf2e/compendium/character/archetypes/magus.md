# Magus  *Archetype 2*

#### Multiclass Magus Characters

The magus archetype grants you the ability to cast a limited amount spells, though you can learn more. It’s a good choice for martial characters who want to add a little magic to their repertoire. After some study, a magus multiclass character can learn the ability to combine their magic and their weapon strikes into a single devastating blow.
