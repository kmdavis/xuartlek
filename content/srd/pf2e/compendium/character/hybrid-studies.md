# Hybrid Studies

## Inexorable Iron  *Magus Hybrid Study*

Once you begin along a path, nothing can stop you from reaching its end. You transform the mass of an enormous weapon into an unstoppable force to augment your own striking power or keep you standing on the battlefield.

 When you enter Arcane Cascade stance and at the start of each of your turns while you're in that stance, if you're wielding a melee weapon in two hands, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level (minimum 1 temporary HP).

**Conflux Spell** Thunderous Strike

**Studious Spell (7th)** Enlarge

**Studious Spell (11th)** Earthbind

**Studious Spell (13th)** Planar Tether

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 13*

## Laughing Shadow  *Magus Hybrid Study*

Magic is freeing, a means to your ends, and you can use it to go where you want, do as you please, and avoid the consequences. You are a laughing shadow of spell and blade, always one step ahead of your foes, always with a trick up your sleeve.

 While in Arcane Cascade stance, you gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speeds, or a +10-foot bonus if you're unarmored. If you Strike an off-guard creature while you're in the stance and have a free hand, that Strike's extra damage from Arcane Cascade is increased to 3. The increased extra damage is 5 if you have weapon specialization, or 7 if you have greater weapon specialization. You must have your other hand completely free; the extra damage doesn't apply if you have a free-hand weapon or other item in that hand, even if you would normally be able to use the hand for other things.

**Conflux Spell** Dimensional Assault

**Studious Spell (7th)** Blur

**Studious Spell (11th)** Ghostly Weapon

**Studious Spell (13th)** Translocate

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 13*

## Sparkling Targe  *Magus Hybrid Study*

You've studied the applications of magic, training yourself to perform not just offensive maneuvers but defensive tactics as well. When magic flows through you, your shield can block impossible things, even a dragon's breath or a _force barrage_.

 You gain the general Shield Block feat.

 When you're in Arcane Cascade stance with your shield raised, your circumstance bonus to AC from your shield also applies to your saves against spells and other magical effects. In addition, damage you take from a spell or other magical effect while you're in Arcane Cascade can trigger your Shield Block reaction, even if the damage isn't physical. When blocking damage from a magical effect in this way, increase your shield's Hardness by an amount equal to the resistance you have from Arcane Cascade. These benefits apply whether you're using an actual shield, the _shield_ spell, or something else that works like a shield (such as a raised tome if you have the Raise a Tome feat).

**Conflux Spell** Shielding Strike

**Studious Spell (7th)** Resist Energy

**Studious Spell (11th)** Warding Aggression

**Studious Spell (13th)** Mountain Resilience

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 13*

## Starlit Span  *Magus Hybrid Study*

With magic, the sky's the limit, and you can't be bound by the confines of physical proximity. Your power reaches as far as your senses can perceive, transcending the space between you and your target even with spells that normally require direct physical contact.

 When you use Spellstrike, you can make a ranged weapon or ranged unarmed Strike, as long as the target is within the first range increment of your ranged weapon or ranged unarmed attack. This overrides the actual range of the spell.

 While you're in Arcane Cascade stance, your ranged attacks ignore any lesser cover your allies would grant to your target.

**Conflux Spell** Shooting Star

**Studious Spell (7th)** Telekinetic Maneuver

**Studious Spell (11th)** Wall of Wind

**Studious Spell (13th)** Unfettered Movement

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 13*

## Twisting Tree  *Magus Hybrid Study*

The staff is perhaps the simplest of weapons, but this simplicity belies its elegance and versatility. To you, a staff is casting implement and martial weapon alike—the foundation of a fighting style.

 While you wield a staff in one hand, the staff adjusts in shape and weight, gaining the agile trait and increasing its damage die size to 1d6. While you wield a staff in both hands, it lengthens, twists, and reshapes, gaining the parry, reach, and trip traits.

 While you're inArcane Cascade stance, once per round you can Interact to change your grip on a staff as a free action.

**Conflux Spell** Spinning Staff

**Studious Spell (7th)** Oaken Resilience

**Studious Spell (11th)** Slow

**Studious Spell (13th)** Flicker

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 14*

## Twofold Tine  *Magus Hybrid Study*

Magic dances between the two weapons you wield with equally deadly grace. Fighting groups of enemies takes full advantage of your capabilities as you move from one foe to another.

 While in Arcane Cascade stance, if you make a Strike with a melee weapon you're holding, you can intensify the stance's power for another melee weapon of your choice you're holding in a different hand. Until the end of your turn, any extra damage the chosen weapon gains from Arcane Cascade is increased to 2. The extra damage increases to 4 if you have weapon specialization or 6 if you have greater weapon specialization.

**Conflux Spell** Force Fling

**Studious Spell (7th)** Blur

**Studious Spell (11th)** Gravity Well

**Studious Spell (13th)** Unfettered Movement

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 14*

## Volatile Spark  *Magus Hybrid Study*

Your crashing spells meet their match with booming gunshots in a magical fusillade. By modifying your shots with your magic, you create explosions and cut through defenses.

 When you use Spellstrike, you can make a ranged firearm Strike, as long as the target is within the first range increment of your firearm. This overrides the actual range of the spell. If your firearm has the concussive trait, any damage from the spell you use in the Spellstrike uses the target's weakest resistance to any of the following: the spell's damage type, piercing, or bludgeoning. This benefit applies only to creatures you target with your firearm Strike during the Spellstrike, so it wouldn't apply to other creatures damaged with Expansive Spellstrike or similar abilities.

 In addition, you gain the Reloading Cascade action.
 **Reloading Cascade** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") You use Arcane Cascade and Interact to reload a firearm.

**Conflux Spell** Booming Blast

**Studious Spell (7th)** Entangling Flora

**Studious Spell (11th)** Earthbind

**Studious Spell (13th)** Seal Fate

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 14*
