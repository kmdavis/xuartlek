# Heritages

## Shimmertongue Nagaji

You were born with a keen sense for magic, able to taste its unique effects in the air. You gain magicsense as a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Vague Senses|vague sense]] that has a range of 30 feet—like all vague senses, it's only about as precise as an average human's sense of smell, meaning you generally can predict only if magic is present; however, each tradition of magic has a unique taste to you, allowing you to identify the tradition of magic present.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 51*
