# Familiars Specific

## Aeon Wyrd

`Construct`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 3
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Construct|Construct]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Flier|Flier]]

An aeon wyrd is a floating swarm of gemstones surrounding a larger stone. It grants the power of its internal aeon stone to its master.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 170*

## Cullitox Shardling

`Earth`  `Elemental`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 3
- **Granted Abilities**: Burrower, elemental (earth only), Speech

A cullitox shardling is a younger, smaller version of [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/elemental/Cullitox|cullitox]]. When acting as a familiar, a shardling's hunger for crystals is sated by magic, turning an urgent need into a mere interest.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 42*

## Elemental Scamp

`Elemental`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 5
- **Granted Abilities**: Elemental, Flier, Speech

The elementals called scamps look similar to bats, but their bodies are made primarily of elemental matter. They have large, staring eyes and small, curved fangs. . An elemental scamp familiar is a bit smaller than other scamps but makes up for it by being more mischievous. Driven by ego, scamps often take on long names with many titles and insist on being addressed in full.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 43*

## Elemental Wisp

`Elemental`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 3
- **Granted Abilities**: Accompanist, Elemental, Speech

Wisps are tiny elementals that often act as familiars. They each usually have different personalities: air wisps are playful and capricious, earth wisps are timid but loyal, fire wisps are carefree and boisterous, metal wisps are serious and morose, water wisps are gentle and nurturing, and wood wisps are fierce and controlling.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 43*

## Fey Dragonet

`Dragon`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 5
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Darkvision|Darkvision]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Flier|Flier]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Manual Dexterity|Manual Dexterity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Speech|Speech]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Touch Telepathy|Touch Telepathy]]

These tiny, mischievous dragons make natural allies for benevolent or capricious characters.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 170*

## Gennayn

`Elemental`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 5
- **Granted Abilities**: Elemental, Speech

Minor genies, [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/elemental/Gennayn|gennayn]] are elementally adaptable and personable. They look like miniature humanoids with elemental energy surrounding them, and frequently carry messages or announce the arrival of their allies.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 43*

## Homunculus

`Construct`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 6
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Construct|Construct]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Darkvision|Darkvision]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Manual Dexterity|Manual Dexterity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Poison Reservoir|Poison Reservoir]]

Homunculi are tiny servitor constructs created by a crafter to serve as a spy, scout, messenger, or assistant. A homunculus is crafted using a drop of its creator’s blood in addition to any other components used in crafting its body.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 171*

## Imp

`Fiend`  `Unholy`

- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Darkvision|Darkvision]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Flier|Flier]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Manual Dexterity|Manual Dexterity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Resistance|resistance (poison)]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Skilled|skilled (Deception)]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Speech|Speech]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Touch Telepathy|Touch Telepathy]]

Imp familiars pretend to be subservient in order to trick their master into losing their soul or finding an early grave.

## Kinnars

`Celestial`  `Uncommon`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 6
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Darkvision|Darkvision]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Independent|Independent]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Lifelink|Lifelink]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Manual Dexterity|Manual Dexterity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Speech|Speech]]

The kinnari seats herself, pressing an instrument of gourds and strings to her shoulder. The kinnara stands ready, with his tail upright, preparing for his beloved’s rhythm. Kinnars are half-human, half-songbird lovers who sing, dance, and perform for and with each other, forever accompanying each other in their arts. Severance creates suffering, and neither can stay apart from the other else they both become heartbroken and die. While most kinnar pairings serving as familiars include a female kinnari and male kinnara, kinnar pairings of different genders aren’t uncommon. Kinnar familiars have access to all of their familiar abilities regardless of the genders of the kinnars.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 124*

## Lantern Wisp

`Construct`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 6
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Construct|Construct]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Flier|Flier]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Kindling|Kindling]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Resistance|Resistance (Fire and Cold)]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Tough|Tough]]

Lantern wisps appear as colorful, hand-made lanterns aimlessly floating around in the dark of the night, seemingly always with its flame lit in full. They house harmless, little wisps that have settled into forgotten children’s playthings.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 125*

## Makhluk Wayang

`Construct`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 8
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Construct|Construct]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Manual Dexterity|Manual Dexterity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Play Dead|Play Dead]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Speech|Speech]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Tough|Tough]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Versatile Form|Versatile Form]]

Makhluk wayangs are shadow puppets, pieces of show and tell made animate, carrying no more than the personalities they were crafted to inhabit. Unlike the wayang people, your familiar is a shadowy incorporeal being without a body of its own. A makhluk wayang possesses a simple puppet made of leather or, sometimes, from thick parchment.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 125*

## Mood Cloud

`Air`  `Elemental`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 3
- **Granted Abilities**: elemental (air only), Flier

This air elemental is little more than a tiny cloud with just enough internality to have emotions. Elemental scholars believe mood clouds to be related to [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/rage-of-elements/elemental/Despairing Pall|despairing palls]], though far less evolved.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 42*

## Shikigami

`Construct`

- **Required Number of Abilities**: 6
- **Granted Abilities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Construct|Construct]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Flier|Flier]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Kindling|Kindling]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Play Dead|Play Dead]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Tough|Tough]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Versatile Form|Versatile Form]]

Shikigami are small spirits typically summoned and bound to a paper figure, so confined to carry out the deeds and embody the will of their masters. More often than not, the naivete of these amateur practitioners of esoteric magics means they’ll find themselves at the mercy of their own shikigami.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 125*
