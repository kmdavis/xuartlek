# Evolution Feat  *Class Feature 1*

- **Class**: Summoner

Evolution feats are a special type of summoner class feat that allow you to customize your eidolon through your shared link. You select one [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/impossible-magic/evolution|evolution]] feat for your eidolon at 1st level, representing the differences that make them unique from other eidolons of their type. If you retrain this feat, the feat you replace it with must also be an evolution feat.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 63*
