# Weapon Specialization  *Class Feature 13*

- **Class**: Psychic

As you touch a weapon, you glimpse the hands that made and wielded it, teaching you how to inflict greater injuries. You deal 2 additional damage with weapons and unarmed attacks in which you're an expert. This damage increases to 3 if you're a master and 4 if you're legendary.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 9*
