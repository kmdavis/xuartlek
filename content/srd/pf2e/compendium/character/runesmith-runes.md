# Runesmith Runes

## Aiuen, The Elf-Gate Key  *Runesmith Rune 17*

`Arcane`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Teleportation`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

This masterful circular rune is a key to the aiudara network of elf gates that stretch across the stars. Once per turn, when the rune-bearer [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Strides]], it can instead teleport a distance equal to its Speed.

**Invocation**
You teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your other runes, as long as it is on the same plane. An unwilling target can be teleported a maximum distance of 60 feet, and it avoids being teleported at all if it succeeds at a Will save against your class DC.

---
If you have the ability to cast the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/sigil|_sigil_]] cantrip, then you can use _aiuen, the elf-gate key's_ invocation to teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your sigils on the same plane, in addition to any of your runes.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 61*

## Astillu, Rune of Submersion  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Water`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

Originating from Thalassic script, this wavy rune is popular with iruxi practitioners of rune magic who want to easily traverse their watery environs. The rune-bearer can breathe underwater and gains a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Swim Speed|swim Speed]] equal to its land Speed or 30 feet, whichever is greater. Its melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] don't take the usual circumstance penalty for passing through water.

**Invocation**
The rune's power pushes the bearer forward in a surprising surge of speed. The rune-bearer can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] up to 60 feet as a free action, and this movement doesn't trigger reactions.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 59*

`Fire`  `Primal`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 55*
