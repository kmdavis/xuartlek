# Animal Companion Specializations

## Ambusher

In your companion’s natural environment, it can use a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]] action even if it’s currently [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Observed|observed]]. Its proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank for unarmored defense increases to expert.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*

## Bully

Your companion terrorizes foes with dominance displays and pushes them around the battlefield. Its proficiency ranks for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] increase to expert (or master if it was already expert from its type), its Strength modifier increases by 1, and its Charisma modifier increases by 3.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*

## Daredevil

Your companion joins the fray with graceful leaps and dives. It gains the deny advantage ability, so it isn’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Hidden|hidden]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Undetected|undetected]], or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Flanking|flanking]] creatures unless such a creature’s level is greater than yours. Its proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] increases to master, and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank in unarmored defense increases to expert.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*

## Racer

Your companion races. It gains a +10-foot status bonus to its Speed, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Swim Speed|swim Speed]], or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] (your choice). Its proficiency in Fortitude saves increases to legendary, and its Constitution modifier increases by 1.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*

## Tracker

Your companion is an incredible tracker. It can move at full Speed while [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Track|following tracks]]. Its proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Survival|Survival]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Wisdom modifier increases by 1.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*

## Wrecker

Your companion smashes things. Its unarmed attacks ignore half an object’s [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/Item Damage|Hardness]]. Its [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] proficiency increases to master, and its Strength modifier increases by 1.

*Source: Player Core p. 211*
