**Eidolon Abilities**

- **Initial** Hallowed Strikes
- **Symbiosis** Traveler's Aura
- **Transcendence** Angelic Mercy

### Hallowed Strikes

Your eidolon's unarmed Strikes gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Holy|holy]] trait and deal 1 extra spirit damage to unholy creatures and creatures with weakness to holy. Additionally, your eidolon can make [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks with their unarmed attacks without taking the usual –2 circumstance penalty.

### Traveler's Aura

Your eidolon emanates a powerful aura that protects creatures as they travel. Your eidolon and their allies (including you) within a 20-foot emanation are protected from severe heat and cold, and your eidolon is never [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] to creatures inside the aura that are of a lower level than you. When you gain the transcendence class feature, your eidolon and allies in the aura are protected from environmental damage from any plane, including severe and extreme heat and cold as well as more unusual dangers.

### Angelic Mercy

Your eidolon can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Clear Mind|_clear mind_]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Heal|_heal_]] once per day each as 9th-rank divine innate spells.
