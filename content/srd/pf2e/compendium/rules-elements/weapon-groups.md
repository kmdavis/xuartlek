# Weapon Groups

## Axe  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

Choose one creature adjacent to the initial target and within reach. If its AC is lower than your attack roll result for the critical hit, you deal damage to that creature equal to the result of the weapon damage die you rolled (including extra dice for its _striking_ rune, if any). This amount isn't doubled, and no bonuses or other additional dice apply to this damage.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Bomb  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

Increase the radius of the bomb's [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/splash|splash]] damage (if any) to 10 feet.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Bow  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

If the target of the critical hit is adjacent to a surface, it gets stuck to that surface by the missile. The target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] and must spend an Interact action to attempt a DC 10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check to pull the missile free; it can't move from its space until it succeeds. The creature doesn't become stuck if it is incorporeal, is liquid (like a water elemental or some oozes), or could otherwise escape without effort.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Brawling  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be slowed 1 until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Club  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

You knock the target away from you up to 10 feet (you choose the distance). This is forced movement.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Crossbow  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target takes 1d8 persistent bleed damage. You gain an item bonus to this bleed damage equal to the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Dart  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target takes 1d6 persistent bleed damage. You gain an item bonus to this bleed damage equal to the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Firearm  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be stunned 1.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 152*

## Flail  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target is knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]] unless they succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Hammer  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target is knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]] unless they succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Knife  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target takes 1d6 persistent bleed damage. You gain an item bonus to this bleed damage equal to the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Pick  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The weapon viciously pierces the target, who takes 2 additional damage per weapon damage die.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Polearm  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target is moved 5 feet in a direction of your choice. This is forced movement.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Shield  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

You knock the target back from you 5 feet. This is forced movement.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Sling  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be stunned 1.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Spear  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The weapon pierces the target, weakening its attacks. The target is clumsy 1 until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*

## Sword  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

The target is made off-balance by your attack, becoming [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core p. 283*
