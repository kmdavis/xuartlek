# Actions: Monster Core

## (concentrate, death, healing, positive)

**Activate—Rejuvenating Deforestation** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/death|death]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|positive]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** Make a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against a living tree with _Blackaxe_. If it hits, the tree withers to ash and you heal 250 Hit Points and gain the benefit of a 6th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/sound-body|_sound body_]] spell.

*Source: Monster Core p. 329*

## (concentrate, scrying)

**Activate—Owner’s Authority** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/scrying|scrying]]) **Requirements** You aren’t wielding _Blackaxe_ and you are its true owner; **Effect** You sense the world around _Blackaxe_ as though you were in its location and can use any of your innate spells through the link as if it were the source of the spell. If another creature is wielding _Blackaxe_, it must succeed at a DC 50 Will save or be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed 2]] until it relinquishes the weapon.

*Source: Monster Core p. 329*

## (concentrate, teleportation)

**Activate—Owner’s Reclamation** [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/teleportation|teleportation]]) **Requirements** You aren’t wielding _Blackaxe_ and you are its true owner; **Effect** _Blackaxe_ appears in your hands, teleporting instantly from its prior location.

*Source: Monster Core p. 329*
