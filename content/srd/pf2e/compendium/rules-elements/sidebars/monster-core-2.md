# Sidebars: Monster Core 2

## Custom Contracts

- **Parent page**: Hellbound Creatures

You can modify the abilities in the Hellbound creature template to suit the devil granting them or the specific agreement. For example, a mortal seeking allure might gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]] rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Charm|_charm_]] rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]].

*Source: Monster Core 2 p. 98*
