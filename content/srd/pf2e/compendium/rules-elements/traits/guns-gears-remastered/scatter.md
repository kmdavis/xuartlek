# Scatter  *Trait*

`Scatter`

This weapon fires a cluster of pellets in a wide spray. Scatter always has an area listed with it, indicating the radius of the spray. On a hit, the primary target of attacks with a scatter weapon take the listed damage, and the target and all other creatures within the listed radius around it take 1 point of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/splash|splash]] damage per weapon damage die.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 235*
