# Thrall  *Trait*

`Thrall`

The mindless undead you can create as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Necromancer|necromancer]] are called thralls. Spells or abilities that create thralls have the thrall trait.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 31*
