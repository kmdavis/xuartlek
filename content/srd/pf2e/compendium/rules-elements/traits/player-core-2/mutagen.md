# Mutagen  *Trait*

`Mutagen`

Mutagens are a special type of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/elixir|elixir]] that temporarily transmogrify the subject's body and mind. Typically, only [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/alchemist|alchemists]] have the expertise to craft mutagens. 

A mutagen always conveys one or more beneficial effects (listed in the Benefit entry) and one or more detrimental effects (shown in the Drawback entry). Mutagens are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/polymorph|polymorph]] effects, and a subsequent polymorph effect attempts to counteract an existing effect; the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] check for a mutagen uses the item's level and a modifier equal to that [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-items#Fill in the Numbers|level's DC]] – 10.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 289*
