# Venomous  *Trait*

`Venomous`

These weapons inject poison into every hit. When you hit a creature with this weapon, it deals an additional 1 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent poison damage]]. This increases to 2 persistent poison damage if the weapon has a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/striking-major|greater striking rune]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 119*
