# Thrown  *Trait*

`Thrown`

You can throw this weapon as a ranged attack; it is a ranged weapon when thrown. You add your Strength modifier to damage as you would for a melee weapon. When this trait appears on a melee weapon, it also includes the range increment. Ranged weapons with this trait use the range increment in the weapon's Range entry._ Paizo ClarificationWhether you're wielding something can get complicated when you're using a thrown weapon with the returning rune or another ability that returns a thrown weapon to your hand. Typically, you're no longer counted as wielding a weapon when you throw it.

The GM might allow you to still count as wielding the item for some purposes, like staying in a stance that requires you to be wielding a weapon, but this is very much based on the GM's judgment.

When it comes to items built to share runes, the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/Thrower's Bandolier|thrower's bandolier]] is expressly built to duplicate runes on thrown weapons. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/Doubling Rings|Doubling rings]] are meant for melee weapons and blazons of shared power are for a melee weapon and a one-handed firearm, crossbow, or the like. Again, your GM might make an exception based on the treasure available in your game.
