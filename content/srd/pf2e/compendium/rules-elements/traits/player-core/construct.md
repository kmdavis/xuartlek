# Construct  *Trait*

`Construct`

A construct is an artificial creature empowered by a force other than vitality or void. Constructs are often mindless; they’re immune to bleed damage, death effects, disease, healing, nonlethal attacks, poison, vitality, void, and the doomed, drained, fatigued, paralyzed, sickened, and unconscious conditions; and they might have Hardness based on the materials used to construct their bodies. Constructs are not living creatures, nor are they undead. When reduced to 0 Hit Points, a construct creature is destroyed.

*Source: Player Core p. 454*
