# Sahkil  *Trait*

`Sahkil`

Sahkils are fiends that delight in spreading fear and unease among mortal creatures. They typically have darkvision, immunity to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]], and weakness to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]].
