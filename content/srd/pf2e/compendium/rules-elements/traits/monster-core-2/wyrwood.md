# Wyrwood  *Trait*

`Wyrwood`

A wyrwood is a small, sapient living construct crafted of wood and powered with an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/aeon-stone-sprouting|aeon stone]] or a similar magical stone.
