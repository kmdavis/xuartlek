# Elemental Transformation  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Kineticist`  `Polymorph`  `Primal`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: exactly one kinetic element
- **Requirements**: Your kinetic gate is deactivated.

You open your kinetic gate, but rather than activating your kinetic aura, you instead become overwhelmed with transformative elemental energy. You're affected by an _elemental form_ spell with the same trait as your kinetic element. You can heighten it to any spell rank up to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 23*
