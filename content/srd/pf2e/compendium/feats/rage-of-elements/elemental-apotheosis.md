# Elemental Apotheosis  *Feat 18*

`Kineticist`

- **Prerequisites**: Nourishing Gate; exactly one kinetic element

You take on an elemental countenance, appearing like a type of elemental of your element. You gain any Speeds listed in the _elemental form_ spell for your element and gain darkvision if you don't already have it. You also gain the elemental trait and the trait of your chosen element.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 23*
