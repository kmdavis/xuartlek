# Fearsome Familiar  *Feat 6*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Impulse`  `Kineticist`  `Primal`

- **Prerequisites**: a familiar

Your familiar trades places with an elemental from its elemental plane. Summon a common creature that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/elemental|elemental]] trait and a level no greater than your level – 4. This creature gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/summoned|summoned]] trait. This creature appears in the same space as your familiar, which disappears for the duration of this impulse. You can Sustain this impulse up to 1 minute. When the impulse ends, the familiar appears in the space the summoned elemental occupied. A familiar can endure this process only so often—if you use this impulse on your familiar more than once per day, it dies as soon as it returns the second time.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 22*
