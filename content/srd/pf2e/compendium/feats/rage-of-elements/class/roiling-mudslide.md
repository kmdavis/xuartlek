# Roiling Mudslide  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Composite`  `Earth`  `Impulse`  `Kineticist`  `Primal`  `Water`

You form water and earth into a mudslide that smashes your opponents and coats them in mud. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC. A creature that fails is also pushed 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure) and coated in mud until the end of its next turn. While coated in mud, the creature falls [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]] at the end of its movement any time it ends a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/move|move]] action other than a Crawl or Step. The creature can attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save against your class DC, avoiding the fall if it succeeds.

---
**Level (+4)** The damage increases by 1d8.
