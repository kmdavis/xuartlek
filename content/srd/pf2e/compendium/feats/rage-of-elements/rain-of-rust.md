# Rain of Rust  *Feat 4*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Composite`  `Impulse`  `Kineticist`  `Metal`  `Primal`  `Water`

You conjure a red raincloud in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet, which pours rust-colored rain directly below it. Any creature in the rain with the metal trait, that is made of metal, or is wearing metal armor is covered in corrosive build-up. Any such creature is clumsy 1 as long as it remains in the area. If such a creature is in the rain at the start of its turn, it takes 3d6 damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC, plus 1d6 persistent damage if it fails. Damage from this impulse ignores Hardness. The cloud lasts 1 minute but ends if you use the impulse again.

---
**Level (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d6.
