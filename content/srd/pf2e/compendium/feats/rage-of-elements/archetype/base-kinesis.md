# Base Kinesis  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/kineticist|Kineticist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/rage-of-elements/archetype/kineticist-dedication|Kineticist Dedication]]

You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/rage-of-elements#Base Kinesis|Base Kinesis]] impulse. For the purposes of determining when the range and Bulk improvements of the action occur, your kineticist level is equal to half your level (for example, Base Kinesis for a 10th-level character with this feat would have a range of 45 feet and could affect 1 Bulk of their chosen element).

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 57*
