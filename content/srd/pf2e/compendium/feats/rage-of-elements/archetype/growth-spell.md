# Growth Spell  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/elementalist|Elementalist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/rage-of-elements/archetype/elementalist-dedication|Elementalist Dedication]]; wood is in your elemental philosophy

You enhance your spell with elemental wood, causing your magic to expand and grow beyond its original limitations. If the next action you use this turn is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that affects an area, expand the area of the spell. For bursts, expand the radius by 5 feet; for cones, expand the cone by 10 feet; and for lines, expand the length by 15 feet. The spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/wood|wood]] trait.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 59*
