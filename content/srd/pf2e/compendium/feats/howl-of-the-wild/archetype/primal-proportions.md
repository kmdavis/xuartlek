# Primal Proportions  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/wild-mimic|Wild Mimic]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/archetype/wild-mimic-dedication|Wild Mimic Dedication]]

You can mimic the sheer mass of the largest beasts. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/enlarge|_enlarge_]], targeting yourself once per 10 minutes as an innate 2nd-rank primal spell. At 12th level, the spell is heightened to 4th rank.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 80*
