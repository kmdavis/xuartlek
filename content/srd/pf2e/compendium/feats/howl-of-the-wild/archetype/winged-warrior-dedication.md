# Winged Warrior Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/winged-warrior|Winged Warrior]]
- **Prerequisites**: you have permanent wings (such as from an ancestry or graft)

Through rigorous training, you have strengthened your wings, granting you enough thrust to gain additional altitude. When you make a horizontal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]], increase the distance by 10 feet up to a maximum of your Speed. Any fly Speed granted by ancestry feats and other permanent wings increases by 5 feet.
