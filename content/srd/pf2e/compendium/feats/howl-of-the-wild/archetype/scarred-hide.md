# Scarred Hide  *Feat 14*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/werecreature|Werecreature]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/archetype/werecreature-dedication|Werecreature Dedication]]

Your hide is an impenetrable mass of scars, accrued over countless bloody conflicts. When in your hybrid or animal shape, you gain resistance to non-[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/silver-object-high-grade|silver]] slashing damage equal to half your level.
