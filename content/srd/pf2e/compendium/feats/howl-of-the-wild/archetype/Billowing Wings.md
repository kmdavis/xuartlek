# Billowing Wings  *Feat 12*

`Air`  `Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Beastmaster|Beastmaster]]
- **Prerequisites**: Beastmaster Dedication; animal companion with wings

You've trained your winged companions to gather air in their wings, whipping up a concentrated gust of wind at opponents, potentially staggering them. Your companions with wings gain a gust ranged [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, which deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage with a range of 30 feet and has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Air|air]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Propulsive|propulsive]] traits. On a critical hit, the target is pushed back 5 feet. This is forced movement.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 67*
