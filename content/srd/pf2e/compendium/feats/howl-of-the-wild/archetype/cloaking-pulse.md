# Cloaking Pulse  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/ostilli-host|Ostilli Host]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/archetype/ostilli-host-dedication|Ostilli Host Dedication]]

Your ostilli can emit illusion magic to mask your position, granting you the Drape Ambient Magic action.

 **Drape Ambient Magic** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Your ostilli turns clear as it converts its stored magic into a bubble of refracting light around you. You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] to all creatures until the end of your turn. If you Strike a creature, that creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against that attack, and you then become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]].

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 70*
