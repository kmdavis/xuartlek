# Echolocation  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/werecreature|Werecreature]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/archetype/werecreature-dedication|Werecreature Dedication]]; werebat

When you take the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Seek|Seek]] action, you can use hearing as a precise sense with a range of 40 feet until the start of your next turn. You can also use this sense while you Search.
