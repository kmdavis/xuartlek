# Lassoing Lash  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Attack`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/thlipit-contestant|Thlipit Contestant]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/archetype/thlipit-contestant-dedication|Thlipit Contestant Dedication]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]
- **Requirements**: The target can’t be more than one size larger than you.

You quickly wrap your lash around an opponent and pull them toward you. Attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check against the Fortitude DC of a target within your lash's reach.

**Special** If you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/titan-wrestler|Titan Wrestler]] feat, you can target a creature up to two sizes larger than you, or up to three sizes larger than you if you’re legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]].

**Critical Success** You pull the target to any open space adjacent to you.

**Success** You pull the target to the nearest open space adjacent to you.

**Critical Failure** You misjudge your leverage and fall [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]].

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 74*
