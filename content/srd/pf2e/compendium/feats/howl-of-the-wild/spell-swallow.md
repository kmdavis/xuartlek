# Spell Swallow  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Ostilli Host
- **Prerequisites**: Ostilli Host Dedication

Your ostilli has grown strong enough to completely consume a spell cast at you, granting you the Devour Ambient Magic action.

 **Devour Ambient Magic** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature Casts a Spell with you as the only target; **Effect** Your ostilli radiates cyan light as its tentacle-like filters attempt to consume the magical effect. You can attempt to counteract the triggering spell with an Arcana or Nature check and a counteract rank equal to half your level.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 71*
