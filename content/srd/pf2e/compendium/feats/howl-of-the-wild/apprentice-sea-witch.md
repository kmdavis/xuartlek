# Apprentice Sea Witch  *Feat 5*

`Merfolk`

You're familiar with the basic spells that most educated merfolk know. Once per day, you can cast either _create water_ or _hydraulic push_ as a 1st-rank primal innate spell. Each time you cast _create water_, you choose if you create fresh water or salt water.
