# Animal Strength  *Feat 6*

`Ranger`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/animal-feature|_animal feature_]] warden spell

You tap into the primal strength of your _animal feature_. When you gain a claw or jaws attack from _animal feature_, you can choose a weapon you're carrying and apply all runes from that weapon (if they're applicable) to that [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unarmed|unarmed]] attack. This replaces any runes the unarmed attacks would normally have from other sources, like [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/handwraps-of-mighty-blows-3-major-striking|_handwraps of mighty blows_]]. In addition, when you critically hit with a claw or jaws attack from animal feature, you deal 1d6 persistent bleed damage.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 59*
