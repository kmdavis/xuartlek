# Community-Minded  *Feat 1*

`Athamaru`  `Locathah`

You are familiar with all the members of your community and their typical movements, and you have learned how to apply these patterns outside those circles. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC against someone attempting to Lie to you and to Perception checks to Sense Motive or to detect a creature trying to Impersonate someone they are not.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 19*
