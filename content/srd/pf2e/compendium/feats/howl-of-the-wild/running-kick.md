# Running Kick  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Beastmaster
- **Prerequisites**: Beastmaster Dedication; animal companion with a foot, hoof, or talon unarmed attack

Your companion weaves in and out of combat, swiftly attacking before getting to a better position. Your companions with a qualifying unarmed attack learn the Running Kick activity.

 **Running Kick** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") This companion Strides up to twice its Speed and makes a hoof, foot, or talon Strike at any point during the movement. This movement doesn't provoke reactions from a creature damaged by the Strike.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 67*
