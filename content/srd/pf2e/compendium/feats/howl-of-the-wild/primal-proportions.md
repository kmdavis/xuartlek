# Primal Proportions  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: Wild Mimic
- **Prerequisites**: Wild Mimic Dedication

You can mimic the sheer mass of the largest beasts. You can cast _enlarge_, targeting yourself once per 10 minutes as an innate 2nd-rank primal spell. At 12th level, the spell is heightened to 4th rank.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 80*
