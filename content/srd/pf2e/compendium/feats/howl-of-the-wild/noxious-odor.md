# Noxious Odor  *Feat 5*

`Athamaru`  `Poison`  `Locathah`

- **Prerequisites**: Emit Defensive Odor

You have developed your skill with your defensive pheromones and have concentrated them into a noxious form. A creature that fails its save against your Emit Defensive Odor action also becomes sickened 1 (or sickened 2 on a critical failure).
