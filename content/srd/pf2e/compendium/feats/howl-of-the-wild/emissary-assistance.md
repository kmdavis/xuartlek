# Emissary Assistance  *Feat 9*

`Athamaru`  `Locathah`

As an emissary, you have assisted land-bound creatures in journeying underwater to visit your settlements. You gain _feet to fins_ and _water breathing_ as 3rd-rank primal innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 20*
