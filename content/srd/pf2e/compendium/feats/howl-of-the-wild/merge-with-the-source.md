# Merge with the Source  *Feat 17*

`Centaur`

- **Prerequisites**: Speaker in Training or Budding Speaker Centaur heritage

You're among the most powerful of Speakers and can adopt the form of the spirits you entreat with. Select one of the following spells, which you can cast once per day as a 7th-rank innate spell. If you're a Faithspeaker, select from _angel form_, _daemon form_, _demon form_, or _devil form_, which you cast as a divine spell; if you're a Greenspeaker, select from _elemental form_ or _plant form_, which you cast as a primal spell.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 33*
