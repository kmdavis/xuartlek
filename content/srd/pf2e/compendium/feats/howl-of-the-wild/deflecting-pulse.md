# Deflecting Pulse  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Ostilli Host
- **Prerequisites**: Ostilli Host Dedication

Your ostilli can use its stored magic to protect you from other magic, granting you the Turn Aside Ambient Magic action.

 **Turn Aside Ambient Magic** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) Your ostilli glows a faint yellow as it establishes a barrier. Choose acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain resistance against the chosen damage type equal to half your level.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 70*
