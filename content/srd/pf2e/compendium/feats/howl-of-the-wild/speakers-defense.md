# Speaker's Defense  *Feat 9*

`Centaur`

- **Prerequisites**: Budding Speaker Centaur heritage or Speaker in Training

As a Speaker, you've forged a deep bond with powerful spirits who you can urge to rise up to defend you and your people. If you're a Faithspeaker, you can call on gods or powerful planar beings to cast _share life_ and _status_, each once per day as innate divine spells; if you're a Greenspeaker, you can call on the forces of nature to cast _entangling flora_ and _environmental endurance_, each once per day as innate primal spells.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 33*
