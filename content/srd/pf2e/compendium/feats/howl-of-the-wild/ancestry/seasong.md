# Seasong  *Feat 1*

`Merfolk`

Sound carries far beneath the waves, and your people have strong, enchanting voices. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]]. If you would automatically become trained in Performance (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. In addition, you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/virtuosic-performer|Virtuosic Performer]] skill feat for singing, and your voice carries equally well above and below water.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 36*
