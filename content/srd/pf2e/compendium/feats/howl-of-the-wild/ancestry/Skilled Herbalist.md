# Skilled Herbalist  *Feat 1*

`Centaur`

You've been taught how to craft healing remedies with a variety of herbs and plants. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Alchemical Crafting|Alchemical Crafting]] feat, except you must select the following items to add to your formula book: lesser antidote, lesser antiplague, and minor elixir of life, as well as a fourth 1st-level common alchemical formula of your choice. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Alchemical|alchemical]] item that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] trait, rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]].
