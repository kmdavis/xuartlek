# Skilled Herbalist  *Feat 1*

`Centaur`

You've been taught how to craft healing remedies with a variety of herbs and plants. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/alchemical-crafting|Alchemical Crafting]] feat, except you must select the following items to add to your formula book: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/antidote|lesser antidote]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/antiplague|lesser antiplague]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/elixir-of-life|minor elixir of life]], as well as a fourth 1st-level common alchemical formula of your choice. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/nature|Nature]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/alchemical|alchemical]] item that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]] trait, rather than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]].
