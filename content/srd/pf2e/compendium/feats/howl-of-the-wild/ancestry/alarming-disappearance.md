# Alarming Disappearance  *Feat 5*

`Minotaur`

- **Prerequisites**: Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]]

Your ability to move unseen is startling for one your size, causing panic among your enemies. When you successfully [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]] when previously [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]], creatures you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] from become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened 1]]. They are then temporarily immune to Alarming Disappearance for 1 hour. Typically, creatures that have traveled with you for a significant time, such as your fellow party members, are immune to your Alarming Disappearance.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 43*
