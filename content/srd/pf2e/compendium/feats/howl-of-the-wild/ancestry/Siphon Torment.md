# Siphon Torment  *Feat 9*

`Minotaur`

Your progenitors suffered greatly at the hands of a curse, a fate you willingly take upon yourself rather than let fall upon others. Once per day, you can cast _claim curse_ as an innate divine spell. When you do, the target creature gains temporary Hit Points equal to your level.
