# Moray Eel Mount  *Feat 9*

`Athamaru`  `Locathah`

You have either trained a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/giant-moray-eel|giant moray eel]] since its hatching or have tamed one in the wild. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/bonded-animal|Bonded Animal]] skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites, to bond with a giant moray eel. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Command an Animal|Command this Animal]] while you are mounted on it to take a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/move|move]] action, you automatically succeed instead of needing to attempt a check.

**Special** If you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/ancestry/elver-pet|Elver Pet]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/ancestry/growing-eel-friend|Growing Eel Friend]] ancestry feats, you can choose to have that pet become your bonded animal. You don’t need to spend 7 days of downtime to bond with the eel, and you automatically succeed at the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/nature|Nature]] check. You can then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Retraining|retrain]] those feats.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 20*
