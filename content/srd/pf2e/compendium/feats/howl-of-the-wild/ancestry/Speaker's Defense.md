# Speaker's Defense  *Feat 9*

`Centaur`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Heritages#Budding Speaker Centaur|Budding Speaker Centaur]] heritage or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/ancestry/Speaker in Training|Speaker in Training]]

As a Speaker, you've forged a deep bond with powerful spirits who you can urge to rise up to defend you and your people. If you're a Faithspeaker, you can call on gods or powerful planar beings to cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Share Life|_share life_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Status|_status_]], each once per day as innate divine spells; if you're a Greenspeaker, you can call on the forces of nature to cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Entangling Flora|_entangling flora_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Environmental Endurance|_environmental endurance_]], each once per day as innate primal spells.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 33*
