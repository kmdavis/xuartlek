# Into the Labyrinth  *Feat 17*

`Minotaur`

Calling on your connection to minotaurs of myth, you shunt an enemy into a complex maze full of puzzles of your own devising. Once per day, you can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/quandary|_quandary_]] as an 8th-rank occult innate spell, which takes the form of a labyrinth instead of a puzzle room. Targets cannot attempt to use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/occultism|Occultism]] to escape the spell (as normal for _quandary_), but they can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/survival|Survival]] to find their way through the labyrinth's corridors.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 45*
