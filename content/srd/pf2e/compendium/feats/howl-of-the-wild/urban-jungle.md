# Urban Jungle  *Feat 5*

`Awakened Animal`

- **Prerequisites**: Fascinated by Society; Expert in Society or Expert in Survival

Cities are just a new form of wilderness. You've adapted to this wilderness and now can navigate it as easily as where you once came from. When in an urban area, you can move at your full Speed while you Track and you can use your choice of Survival or Society to Track.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 26*
