# Trapsmith Dedication  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Trapsmith|Trapsmith]]
- **Prerequisites**: Snarecrafter Dedication or Snare Specialist

You use glimmering gears and gnashing mechanisms to manufacture [snares](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=31) using specialized parts. Your snares include clockwork and steam-powered cogwheels and gears, and their gush of steam can hinder those you ensnare. If you choose to construct a snare using gears and a creature fails their saving throw against the snare, all creatures are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Concealed|concealed]] to that creature for 1 round, as a burst of steam obscures its vision.
