# Phase Bullet  *Feat 14*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/spellshot|Spellshot]]
- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/guns-gears-remastered/archetype/spellshot-dedication|Spellshot Dedication]]

You can aim your bullets not just in three normal dimensions, but in additional dimensions as well, removing all obstacles between you and your target. Attempt a crossbow or firearm [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against a foe who’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] to you (but not [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]]). The ammunition travels to your target in a straight line, passing through any non‑magical barriers or walls in its way, though magical barriers and force effects stop the bullet. The shot ignores all [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Cover|cover]], the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] condition, the hidden condition, and circumstance bonuses to AC from shields. It has a +4 status bonus to hit creatures wearing any type of armor. The Strike’s damage can’t be reduced with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/shield-block|Shield Block]] reaction using a non‑magical shield.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 141*
