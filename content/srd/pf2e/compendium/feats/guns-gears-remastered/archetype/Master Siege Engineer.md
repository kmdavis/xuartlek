# Master Siege Engineer  *Feat 16*

`Archetype`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Artillerist|Artillerist]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Munitions Master|Munitions Master]] (Level 16)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/guns-gears-remastered/archetype/Artillerist Dedication|Artillerist Dedication]]

What you don't know about artillery isn't worth knowing and your mastery allows you to perform an impossible number of actions as part of a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/siege-weapons/siege-weapons|siege weapon]]'s crew. You're permanently [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Quickened|quickened]]. You can only use the extra action to [[srd/pf2e/books/guns-gears-remastered/gears-equipment/Siege Weapons#Loading|Aim]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/guns-gears-remastered/gears-equipment/Siege Weapons#Launching|Launch]] a siege weapon.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 129*
