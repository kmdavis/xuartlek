# Pistol Twirl  *Feat 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Gunslinger`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/bullet-dancer|Bullet Dancer]] (Level 4)
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]]
- **Requirements**: You're wielding a loaded one-handed ranged weapon.

Your quick gestures and flair for performance distract your opponent, leaving it vulnerable to your follow-up attacks. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]] against an opponent within the required weapon’s first range increment, rather than an opponent within melee reach. If you succeed, the foe is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against your melee and ranged attacks, rather than only your melee attacks. On a critical failure, you’re off-guard against the target’s melee and ranged attacks, rather than only its melee attacks.
