# Munitions Machinist  *Feat 6*

`Gunslinger`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/guns-gears-remastered/class/munitions-crafter|Munitions Crafter]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

You're adept at crafting ammunition. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/archetypes#Alchemical Archetypes|Quick Alchemy benefits]], creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. You can use Quick Alchemy only to create bombs or alchemical ammunition. Using Quick Alchemy to create alchemical ammunition also counts as 1 action toward activating that ammunition.
