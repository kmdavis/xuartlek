# Rolling White Bottle Form  *Feat 9*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Tanuki`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Teakettle Form|Teakettle Form]]
- **Trigger**: You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Drop Prone|Drop Prone]] or othwerwise become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Prone|prone]]

As you fall to the ground, you transform into a white bottle, gourd, or sake jar, then roll away before anyone can pick you up. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Crawl|Crawl]] up to two times, and your movement doesn’t provoke reactions—you somehow always roll just out of reach. When you come to a stop, you return to your tanuki form, face down on the ground.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 68*
