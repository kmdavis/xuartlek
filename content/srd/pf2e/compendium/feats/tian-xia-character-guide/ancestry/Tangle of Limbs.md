# Tangle of Limbs  *Feat 1*

`Goblin`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: dokkaebi goblin heritage or Tian Xia origin

Dokkaebi have a strong tradition of wrestling. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], and you gain the Titan Wrestler feat. If you would automatically become trained in Athletics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
