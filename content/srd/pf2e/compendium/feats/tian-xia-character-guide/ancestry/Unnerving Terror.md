# Unnerving Terror  *Feat 13*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Auditory`  `Emotion`  `Fear`  `Mental`  `Yaoguai`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Requirements**: You’re in yaoguai form.

You let out a mighty howl, chant of death, or speak with eerie repetition that plants fear in others. All enemies within 30 feet must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] 1.

**Failure** The target is frightened 1 and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]].

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 2 and off-guard.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 87*
