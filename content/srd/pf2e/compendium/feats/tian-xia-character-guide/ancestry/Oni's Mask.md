# Oni's Mask  *Feat 5*

`Hungerseed`

You aren’t as malleable as your oni ancestors but still can disguise yourself. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] as a 1st-rank primal innate spell once per day. This spell can’t disguise your horns, though you can still hide them normally with a hairstyle or a hood.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 37*
