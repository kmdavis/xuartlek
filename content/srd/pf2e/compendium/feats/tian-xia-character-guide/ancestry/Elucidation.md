# Elucidation  *Feat 13*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Yaoguai`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Brilliant Vision|Brilliant Vision]]
- **Trigger**: You succeed at a saving throw to disbelieve an illusion.

You can cut through [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Illusion|illusions]] with such precision that all those around you find their senses sharpened as well. Whenever you disbelieve an illusion, all allies within 15 feet can use a free action to attempt a saving throw to disbelieve the illusion as well.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 87*
