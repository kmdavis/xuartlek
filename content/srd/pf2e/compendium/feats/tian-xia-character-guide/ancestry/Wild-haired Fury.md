# Wild-haired Fury  *Feat 13*

`Yaksha`

Your broken bones jut at odd angles, yet this pain only adds to your anger and prowess. You add the combined value of your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Doomed|doomed]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Wounded|wounded]] conditions as a circumstance bonus to damage rolls with your melee attacks (for example, if you were doomed 1 and wounded 2, you would gain a +3 circumstance bonus to your melee damage rolls).

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 81*
