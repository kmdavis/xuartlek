# Improved Signature Weapon  *Feat 13*

`Yaoguai`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Signature Weapon|Signature Weapon]]

Your signature weapon takes on the properties of mystical materials renowned for defeating undead, spirits, and other such creatures. Your signature weapon is treated as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Duskwood|duskwood]], and peachwood for the purposes of overcoming resistance.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 87*
