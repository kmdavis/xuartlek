# Colugo's Traversal  *Feat 5*

`Primal`  `Yaksha`

Forbidding valleys and sheer cliffs can’t curb your vows to reunite the lost. You can cast your choice of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Jump|_jump_]] as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] innate spell once per day. At 9th level, these spells are heightened to 3rd rank.
