# Vulpine Scamper  *Feat 5*

`Kitsune`

- **Prerequisites**: fox alternative form

You’re especially nimble while in your full fox shape. While you’ve Changed Shape into a fox, you gain the following benefits.
You can use your own Speed if it’s higher than 20 feet.You can use your own [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] modifier for checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]].You can scale short distances with a bit of a running start. When you attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]], and your previous action was to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] at least 20 feet along solid ground, you gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Climb Speed|climb Speed]] of 20 feet for the duration of that Climb action.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 50*
