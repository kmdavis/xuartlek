# Ponpoko  *Feat 5*

`Tanuki`

No true tanuki would ever be caught without a musical instrument to play under the full moon. You can attempt [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks and cast spells by drumming on your belly; this requires that you have at least one hand free, and like playing an instrument, it grants the Perform action the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Auditory|auditory]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]] traits. When [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Casting a Spell]], your gestures can include drumming your belly. If you do, any attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Recognize Spell|recognize a spell]] cast this way gains a -2 circumstance penalty.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 68*
