# Form of the Beloved Mother  *Feat 17*

`Nagaji`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: Tian Xia origin or follower of Nalinivati

You channel the blessings of Nalinivati to transform into a manifestation of her will. You can cast 5th-rank animal form as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] innate spell once per day, but you can choose only snake form with the spell. You can spend an additional action casting [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Animal Form|_animal form_]] to also cast 5th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/Slither|_slither_]] centered on your location; Nalinivati’s will ensures that the shadow snakes created by the spell never harm you, so you’re unaffected by the spell. If you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] animal form, the effects of slither end as well.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 51*
