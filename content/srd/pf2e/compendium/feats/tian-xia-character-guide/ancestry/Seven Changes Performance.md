# Seven Changes Performance  *Feat 1*

`Kitsune`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]]

While kitsune are known for their magical [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Illusion|illusion]] powers, you’re skilled at stage magic, especially costuming and quick changes. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Quick Disguise|Quick Disguise]] feat. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]] in place of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]] for the purposes of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Impersonate|Impersonating]] someone.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 50*
