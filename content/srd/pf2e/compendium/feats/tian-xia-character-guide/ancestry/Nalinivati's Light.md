# Nalinivati's Light  *Feat 1*

`Nagaji`

Your slit eyes expand to absorb light and can see in the darkest of places. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level, and you can’t retrain into or out of this feat.
