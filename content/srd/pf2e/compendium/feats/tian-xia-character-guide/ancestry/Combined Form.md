# Combined Form  *Feat 13*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Polymorph`  `Tanuki`

- **PFS Note**: The Combined Form tanuki feat overrides the normal restrictions that a target can only be under the effect of one polymorph effect at a time.
- **Frequency**: Once per hour
- **Trigger**: An ally within 30 feet uses a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect.

Cooperation and coordination are the secrets to getting by in tanuki society. When the triggering ally transforms, you Stride to their square and join shape with them, melding into their transformation. While you’re melded in this way, you grant them the effects of either a 2nd- or 4th‑rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Enlarge|_enlarge_]] spell as well as temporary Hit Points equal to your level; if their [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect would normally grant them temporary Hit Points, you increase the amount by your level instead. While you’re melded, you can’t be separately targeted and you can’t act except to speak or to use Change Shape to exit the Combined Form, which ends the effects of enlarge on your ally and removes any remaining temporary Hit Points they received from this ability at the time. If the triggering ally becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] or the triggering polymorph effect ends, you automatically exit the Combined Form.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 68*
