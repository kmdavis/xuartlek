# Unwavering Guide  *Feat 1*

`Yaksha`

Sharp-eyed and sure-footed, your presence is a boon to those you guide. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Scout|Scout]], you grant your party a +2 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls instead of the normal +1. When your group uses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hustle|Hustle]] activity, they can use your Constitution modifier to determine how fast the group can Hustle together, even if it isn’t the lowest in the group.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 79*
