# Crawling Form  *Feat 1*

`Yaoguai`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Heritages#Born of Animal|Born of Animal]] heritage or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Heritages#Born of Celestial|Born of Celestial]] heritage

You can transform into a small [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Animal|animal]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Celestial|celestial]] creature to scurry and spy. You can Change Shape to assume a crawling form. While in crawling form, you gain the effect of a 1st-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Pest Form|_pest form_]], except you can transform only into a shape matching your heritage; for example, if you were once were a wolf, you turn into a wolf puppy.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 85*
