# Tangle-tongue's Wit  *Feat 1*

`Yaksha`

Your talent for language is a blessing from the yaksha makers of old. You learn two new languages, chosen from languages you have access to. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Linguistic|linguistic]] effects.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 79*
