# Oni Rampage  *Feat 13*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Hungerseed`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Trigger**: You're reduced to less than half your maximum Hit Points by a creature within your reach.

Nothing invigorates you more than your own freshly spilled blood. You gain the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/Haste|_haste_]] for 1 minute.
