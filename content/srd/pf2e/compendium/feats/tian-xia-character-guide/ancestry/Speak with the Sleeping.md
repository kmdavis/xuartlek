# Speak with the Sleeping  *Feat 5*

`Sprite`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: Tian Xia origin

You can speak to all inanimate objects and the slumbering spirits within them. Once per day, you can speak to an object and ask it a question to gain the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Object Reading|_object reading_]], though the object gives you its response 1 minute later (as it takes the spirit within the object some time to rouse). An object’s ability to answer is limited by its particular nature; a pair of shoes might know where their owner has walked, while a house knows the history of the denizens within, but not what they do elsewhere.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 47*
