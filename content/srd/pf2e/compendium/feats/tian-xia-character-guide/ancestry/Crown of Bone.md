# Crown of Bone  *Feat 1*

`Sarangay`

When you came of age, you were blessed by the Mother Earth with a thickened skull, which was celebrated as an auspicious sign among your community. Your horns come together to form a bony plate that protects your head from heavy impacts. The damage die for your horns unarmed attack increases to 1d8, and it gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/guns-gears-remastered/Concussive|concussive]] trait.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 61*
