# Synchronous Slither  *Feat 13*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Nagaji`

- **Trigger**: An ally within your reach leaves a square during a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Move|move]] action they're using and ends their movement adjacent to an enemy.

You’ve learned to travel at your ally’s rhythm and use their movement to adjust your own position. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to half your speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions from the enemy your ally moved adjacent to.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 51*
