# Twilight Dweller  *Feat 1*

`Yaoguai`

You’re accustomed to shaded forests and caves. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If you already have low-light vision, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

**Special** You can select this feat twice.
