# Smoke Through Bamboo  *Feat 5*

`Sarangay`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Heritages#New Moon Sarangay|New Moon Sarangay]] heritage

You’ve spent significant time slipping silently between bamboo thickets, and your light-footedness allows you to navigate even the thickest forests and brush with ease. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]], you can move 10 feet instead of 5. This enables you to Step into and within [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] as long as you can meet the extra movement cost.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 62*
