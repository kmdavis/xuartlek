# Green Dash  *Feat 9*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Leshy`

- **Frequency**: Once per 10 Minutes
- **Requirements**: You're adjacent to a tree, shrub, or plant that occupies at least a 5-foot square.

The spirits of vegetation send you on your way, letting you move in a burst of speed. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] with a +15-foot status bonus to your Speed. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Burrow|Burrow]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|Fly]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] instead if you have the corresponding movement type.
