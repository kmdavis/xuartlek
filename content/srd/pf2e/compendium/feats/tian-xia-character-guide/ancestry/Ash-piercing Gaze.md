# Ash-piercing Gaze  *Feat 1*

`Yaksha`

Neither cinders nor winds shall prevent you from seeing those who need help. You automatically succeed at the flat check to target a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Concealed|concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by smoke or mist.
