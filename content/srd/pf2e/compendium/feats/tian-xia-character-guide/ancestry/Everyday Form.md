# Everyday Form  *Feat 1*

`Tanuki`

There’s nothing better than being a tanuki, but sometimes you need a less conspicuous form when going into the big city or other crowded places. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Tanuki|Change Shape]], you can assume the form of a common Small or Medium [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Humanoid|humanoid]] ancestry prevalent where you grew up, typically human. This everyday form is a specific form with the same age and body type as your true tanuki form and has roughly analogous physical traits, such as hair color.
