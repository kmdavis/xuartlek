# Folk Healer  *Feat 1*

`Sarangay`

Your community recognizes you as a shaman or other animist, a status you obtained through lineage, apprenticeship, or a supernatural event. You’re trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Medicine checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Wounds|Treat Wounds]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Disease|Treat Disease]].

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 61*
