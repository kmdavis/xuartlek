# Traveler's Counsel  *Feat 1*

`Sarangay`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Heritages#Half Moon Sarangay|Half Moon Sarangay]] heritage

On your travels, you’ve resolved countless disputes and counseled those in need. You’re trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]]. If you would automatically become trained in Diplomacy (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. The first time each day you would roll a critical failure when [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Gather Information|Gathering Information]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Make an Impression|Making an Impression]], you get a failure instead.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 61*
