# World-protector's Hospitality  *Feat 9*

`Primal`  `Yaksha`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Meticulous Restorer|Meticulous Restorer]]

Through your spiritual power, you impede starvation and restore pantries. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Create Food|_create food_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Cleanse Cuisine|_cleanse cuisine_]] as 2nd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] innate spells, each once per day. Additionally, when you cast these spells, you instantly restore Hit Points to the containers storing these magical comestibles as though you’ve succeeded at a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] action using your proficiency in your spell DC in place of your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] proficiency.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 81*
