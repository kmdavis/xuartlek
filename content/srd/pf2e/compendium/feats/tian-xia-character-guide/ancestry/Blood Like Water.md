# Blood Like Water  *Feat 5*

`Samsaran`

The watery fluid in your veins doesn’t flow in the same way as traditional blood. Your DC on flat checks to end [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent bleed damage]] is reduced from 15 to 10, or from 10 to 5 after receiving especially appropriate assistance.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 56*
