# Childlike Plant  *Feat 1*

`Leshy`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Leshy#Chrysanthemum Leshy|Chrysanthemum Leshy]] heritage or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Leshy#Peachchild Leshy|Peachchild Leshy]] heritage

You’re adept at hiding your plantlike features to pass as a human. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]] (or another skill if you’re already trained in Deception). You don’t require a disguise kit when attempting Deception checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Impersonate|Impersonate]] a human. In addition, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Impersonate checks to pretend that you’re a human version of yourself, rather than a leshy. This bonus doesn’t apply to other checks to Impersonate humans.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 42*
