# Along the Deep River  *Feat 17*

`Sprite`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: Tian Xia origin, or you're a follower of Sangpotshi.

Once per year, when you die, you turn into an immaterial ball of light. After a week, you return to life with the effects of a successful (but not critically successful) 9th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Reincarnate|reincarnate]] ritual. You always reincarnate into a sprite, but you reincarnate as a randomly selected sprite heritage.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 47*
