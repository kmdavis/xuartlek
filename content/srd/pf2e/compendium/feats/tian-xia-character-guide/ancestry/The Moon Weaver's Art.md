# The Moon Weaver's Art  *Feat 5*

`Sarangay`

You’ve learned to create minor illusions by collecting strands of moonlight and weaving them together to create small images or cloaking veils. You can cast either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Illusory Object|_illusory object_]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Invisible Item|_invisible item_]] as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] innate spell once per day.
