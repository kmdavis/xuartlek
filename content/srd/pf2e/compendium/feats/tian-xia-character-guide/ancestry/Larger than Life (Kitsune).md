# Larger than Life (Kitsune)  *Feat 13*

`Kitsune`

- **Prerequisites**: Shifting Faces

When you Change Shape, you can assume the form of a creature that’s Large or Huge in size. In addition to your increased space, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 50*
