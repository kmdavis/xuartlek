# Dalang's Ally  *Feat 13*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Occult`  `Shadow`  `Wayang`

- **Frequency**: once per hour

You move your shadow as a master puppeteer, allowing it to dance around your enemies and serve you in combat. Until the start of your next turn, your shadow darts around with your foes, providing [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Flanking|flanking]] for you against all enemies within your reach. Flanking with your shadow is the same as flanking with an ally and so is subject to effects like all-around vision or the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Rogue|deny advantage]] class feature.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 74*
