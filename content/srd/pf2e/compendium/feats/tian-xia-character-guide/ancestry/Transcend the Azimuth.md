# Transcend the Azimuth  *Feat 17*

`Primal`  `Yaksha`

As the earth drinks the light of sun and moon, so does your steady vow imbibe superlunary forces, ready to be unleashed with a moment’s mantra. You can cast _cosmic form_ on yourself as a 7th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] innate spell once per day, your shadow lengthening and becoming a mandala of fundamental energies. In addition to the spell’s normal effects, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the spell to switch between the sun battle form and the moon battle form once per turn.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 81*
