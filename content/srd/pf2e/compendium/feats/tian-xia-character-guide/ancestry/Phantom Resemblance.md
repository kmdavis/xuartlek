# Phantom Resemblance  *Feat 5*

`Goblin`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Phantom Visage|Phantom Visage]]

Your can now draw from experience to wear any face you remember. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/monster-core/Phantom|Phantom]] Visage, you gain the effects of a 3rd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] for 1 hour or until you shift back, whichever comes first.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 34*
