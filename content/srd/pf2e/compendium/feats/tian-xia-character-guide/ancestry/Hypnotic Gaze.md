# Hypnotic Gaze  *Feat 9*

`Nagaji`

- **Prerequisites**: Hypnotic Lure

You can widen your field of vision, allowing you to affect more creatures with your Hypnotic Lure. Once per day, when you use Hypnotic Lure, you can affect all creatures in a 30- foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Cone|cone]], rather than one creature within 30 feet.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 51*
