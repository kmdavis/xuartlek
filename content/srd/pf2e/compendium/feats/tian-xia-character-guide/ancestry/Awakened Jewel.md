# Awakened Jewel  *Feat 1*

`Occult`  `Sarangay`

You once held the head gem of a deceased ancestor or loved one, and it awakened a mysterious power in your own. You gain one cantrip from the [occult spell list](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3). As long as you possess your head gem, you can cast this spell as an innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
