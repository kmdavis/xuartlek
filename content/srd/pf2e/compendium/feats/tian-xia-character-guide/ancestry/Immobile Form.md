# Immobile Form  *Feat 5*

`Yaoguai`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Yaoguai#Born of Item|Born of Item]] heritage, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Yaoguai#Born of Elements|Born of Elements]] heritage, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Yaoguai#Born of Vegetation|Born of Vegetation]] heritage

You take on the shape you had before you awoke to hide in plain sight. When you Change Shape, you can assume the form of an immobile plant, object, or natural feature of Tiny, Small, or Medium size, as suits your heritage. Using your immobile form counts as setting up a disguise for the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Impersonate|Impersonate]] use of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]], except that you can Impersonate an object instead of a creature, it gives you a +4 status bonus to Deception checks to prevent others from seeing through your disguise, and you add your level even if you’re untrained. You can speak while in immobile form, but you can’t attack, cast spells, or move.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 86*
