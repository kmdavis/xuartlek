# Storming Gaze  *Feat 5*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Hungerseed`  `Primal`

You can open or energize a notable third eye on your forehead to strike with storming power. You deal 3d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]] damage in a 15-foot cone; Storming Gaze gains this trait. Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Reflex saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. You can't use this ability again for 1d4 rounds.

At 7th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d4.
