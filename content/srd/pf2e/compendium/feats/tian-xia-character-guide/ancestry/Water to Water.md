# Water to Water  *Feat 13*

`Samsaran`

You allow your body to collapse into water, then reconstitute your corporeal form elsewhere. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/Translocate|_translocate_]] as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] innate spell twice per day; the spell additionally gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Water|water]] trait. If you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast this Spell]] while standing in a body of water of at least ankle depth, you don’t need to perform any incantations or gestures to Cast the Spell (typically preventing reactions, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Reactive Strike|Reactive Strike]]), and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Subtle|subtle]] trait as you simply fall into the water without so much as a splash.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 57*
