# Sever the Dreaming Shadow  *Feat 17*

`Wayang`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Inherit the Dreaming Heirloom|Inherit the Dreaming Heirloom]]

As you whisper to your pusaka, it attempts to cut away an enemy’s shadow, leaving them in an eternal sleep. Your pusaka gains the following Activation. 

**Activate—Cut the Shadow** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Your pusaka becomes a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/Kris|kris]] made of darkness and drives itself into an adjacent enemy’s shadow. That enemy takes 80 mental damage with a basic Fortitude save against your spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher. If this reduces the creature to 0 Hit Points, the target doesn’t die, but its shadow is severed from its body, becoming a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/undead/Shadow|shadow spawn]] that attempts to flee from the target’s body. As long as the shadow spawn exists, the target remains in a dreamless slumber; if destroyed, the target regains its shadow and awakens (if its body was kept properly while [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]]).

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 75*
