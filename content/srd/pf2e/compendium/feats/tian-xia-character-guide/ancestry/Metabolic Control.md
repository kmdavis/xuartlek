# Metabolic Control  *Feat 5*

`Nagaji`

You're able to better control your body's metabolism to preserve energy. After eating a proper meal, you can go without food and water for 1 week before taking damage from [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Running Exploration#Starvation and Thirst|starvation and thirst]]. You aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Fatigued|fatigued]] during this week.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 51*
