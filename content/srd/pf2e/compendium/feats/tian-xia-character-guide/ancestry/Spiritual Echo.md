# Spiritual Echo  *Feat 9*

`Occult`  `Sarangay`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/Awakened Jewel|Awakened Jewel]]

You possess the head gem of a deceased ancestor or loved one, which has begun to resonate with your own. While you possess this gem, you gain the ability to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] as a 4th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] innate spell once per day. The type of spell is determined by the clan of the sarangay to whom the head gem belonged, which you choose when you take this feat. In addition, choose a Lore skill to represent knowledge the deceased had. You become trained in that Lore skill. If the gem is lost or destroyed, it can be regrown as if it were your own head gem.
