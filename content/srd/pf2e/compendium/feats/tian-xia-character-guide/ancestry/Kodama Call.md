# Kodama Call  *Feat 9*

`Leshy`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: Tian Xia origin

The small spirits known as kodama pride themselves in taking care of trees and other vegetation, and they’ll happily come to your aid as well. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Helpful Wood Spirits|_helpful wood spirits_]] as a 2nd-rank innate primal spell twice per day.
