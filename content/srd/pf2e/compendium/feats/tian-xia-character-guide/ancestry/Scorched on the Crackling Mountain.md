# Scorched on the Crackling Mountain  *Feat 1*

`Tanuki`

By ritualistically marking your fur with fire, like an infamous tanuki of legend, you protect yourself against future flames. You gain a black stripe down your back that looks charred. Your flat check to remove [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent fire damage]] is DC 10 instead of DC 15, which can be reduced to DC 5 with appropriate assistance. The first time each day you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points by a fire effect, you avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point, and your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Wounded|wounded]] condition increases by 1. Two hairs plucked from your tail can be used as flint and steel to create a fire, emitting a strange crackling sound as they ignite.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 66*
