# Flower Chimera  *Feat 13*

`Leshy`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: Tian Xia origin

You call on a guardian of the wild to burst forth from the land to aid you. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/Spiritual Guardian|_spiritual guardian_]] as a 5th-rank innate primal spell once per day, even if you don’t have a deity; this guardian manifests as a kami or other nature spirit. When you reach 17th level, you can heighten this spell to 7th rank.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 43*
