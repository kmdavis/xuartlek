# Germination of Resolve  *Feat 13*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Yaksha`

- **Frequency**: once per day

Vitality floods your trammeled soul, offering you rejuvenation, if not quite rest. For each effect that causes the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Fatigued|fatigued]] conditions to affect you, attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]] check to counteract that effect. The counteract rank of Germination of Resolve is equal to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 81*
