# Abjure the False Kin  *Feat 9*

`Yaksha`

Your vow reminds you that there can be brackish vinegar hiding within honeyed words, helping you detect attempts to influence your mind. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]] effect that doesn’t deal damage, you get a critical success instead.
