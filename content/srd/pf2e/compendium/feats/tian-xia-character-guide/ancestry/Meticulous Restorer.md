# Meticulous Restorer  *Feat 1*

`Yaksha`

Waste not, want not; your steady eye allows you to restore craftwork quickly while conserving valuable material. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Quick Repair|Quick Repair]] feat. In addition, when you roll a critical failure while attempting to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] an item, you get a failure instead.
