# Witness of Earth  *Feat 9*

`Primal`  `Uncommon`  `Yaksha`

The primal magic in your soul offers the patient earth’s timeless counsel. You learn the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Atone|atone]] ritual. When you use the atone ritual on yakshas and you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 81*
