# Among Humanity  *Feat 5*

`Occult`  `Polymorph`  `Yaoguai`

- **Frequency**: once per day

Your talents in shapechanging allow you to pass unseen. When you Change Shape into your humanoid form, you gain the effects of 3rd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] for 1 hour or until you shift back, except it’s a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect rather than an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Illusion|illusion]].
