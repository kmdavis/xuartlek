# Phantom Orchestra  *Feat 9*

`Tanuki`

An easy way to trick others is to throw your voice into a nearby forest or thicket to make strange noises. You can cast 2nd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]] as an innate [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] spell once per day. You can also cast the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/Figment|_figment_]] cantrip as a primal innate spell at will, heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 68*
