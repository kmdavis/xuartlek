# Ferry Through Waves  *Feat 13*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Lizardfolk`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/ancestry/River Adaptation|River Adaptation]]
- **Requirements**: You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|swimming]] and have a free hand.

The waters around you permit you passage, a privilege you can share with a willing ally. You hold on to an adjacent ally and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]], carrying them along with you. If you spend three actions Ferrying through Waves, you instead Swim twice. If your ally would need to attempt any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to Swim while you’re Ferrying through Waves, you roll the check and your result applies to them.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 45*
