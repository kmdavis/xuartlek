# Silk Bracelet  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Inventor`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Innovations#Armor|armor innovation]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Innovations#Construct|construct innovation]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Innovations#Weapon|weapon innovation]]
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin

Taking inspiration from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/gods-of-the-inner-sea/Desna|Desna’s]] image as a giant silk moth in Tian Xia, you’ve attached a bracelet to your innovation that generates ultra-strong silk strands mixed with venom. You can unleash this substance as part of an attack. Make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]]. If it hits, the target takes damage from the Strike as normal and must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The silk imposes a –10 foot status penalty to all the target’s Speeds for 1 round.

**Failure** As success, and the target takes an additional 1d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent poison damage]]. The poison damage increases to 2d4 at 12th level and 3d4 at 18th level.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] until the end of their next turn.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 110*
