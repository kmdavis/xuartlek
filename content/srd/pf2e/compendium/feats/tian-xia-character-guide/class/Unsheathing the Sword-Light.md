# Unsheathing the Sword-Light  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Arcane`  `Light`  `Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Hybrid Studies#Aloof Firmament|aloof firmament hybrid study]]; Spellstrike
- **Requirements**: Your Spellstrike is charged, and you're wielding a one-handed weapon in the sword group.

Honed through 10,000 battles, your sword’s mere light can shatter ambitions and break armies. Make a melee Spellstrike with a sword, with a spell that isn’t a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cantrip|cantrip]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Focus|focus]] spell. Countless copies of your sword, made of light, fall around you, dealing damage equal to double the spell’s rank to each creature within a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Emanation|emanation]] of the target, excluding you and the target of the Spellstrike. The damage is of the same type dealt by your sword Strike and is treated as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Cold Iron|cold iron]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Silver|silver]].

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 113*
