# Wukong Extension  *Feat 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Inventor`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Unstable`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Innovations#Weapon|weapon innovation]]
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin or worshipper of Sun Wukong

Invoking the name of Sun Wukong’s extending cudgel, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/Staff of Sun Wukong|Ruyi Bang]], you activate hidden levers and cranks in your innovation, making it swiftly extend outward like a battering ram. Make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with your weapon innovation; your reach for that Strike is 30 feet.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 110*
