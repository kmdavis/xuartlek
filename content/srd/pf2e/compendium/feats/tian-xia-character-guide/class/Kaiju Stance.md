# Kaiju Stance  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Monk`  `Polymorph`  `Stance`  `Uncommon`

- **Access**: You've encountered a kaiju and survived.
- **Requirements**: You're [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Unarmored|unarmored]].

Your encounter with one of the rare forces of nature known as kaiju has imbued you with knowledge of a martial art that allows you to emulate a fraction of their power. When you enter Kaiju Stance, you become Large and are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]] 1. The only Strikes you can make are shattering earth attacks. These strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, are in the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Backswing|backswing]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fatal|fatal]] d12, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Reach|reach]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. On a critical success with a shattering earth attack, all creatures other than you that are within 10 feet of the target, including the target itself, take 1 point of bludgeoning [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Splash|splash]] damage per weapon damage die.

While in Kaiju Stance, you ignore [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]].
