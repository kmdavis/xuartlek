# Protective Pose  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Manipulate`  `Summoner`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin
- **Requirements**: You and your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/impossible-magic/Eidolon|eidolon]] are within 30 feet of one another.

Many physical poses can fortify one’s health, but some poses need more than one body to accomplish. As you and your eidolon pose in tandem, you each channel qi into a protective barrier in a 30-[[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Emanation|emanation]] until the start of your next turn. Creatures within range of either you or you eidolon gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to your choice of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]] damage equal to half your level. In addition, any Medicine check targeting a creature with this resistance gets a +1 status bonus (or a +2 status bonus if you’re a master in Medicine).

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 109*
