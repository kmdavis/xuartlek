# Glitter Crystals  *Feat 8*

`Additive`  `Alchemist`  `Healing`

You can mix a blend of crushed flowers and alchemical salt into an alchemical elixir with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] trait or [[srd/pf2e/books/treasure-vault-remastered/alchemy-unleashed/Alchemical Foods|alchemical food]]. Choose one of the following effects: reduce the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Drained|drained]] condition by 1, reduce the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] condition by 1, remove the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] condition, or remove the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Deafened|deafened]] condition. The creature who consumes the modified healing elixir or alchemical food gains this benefit in addition to the food or elixir’s normal effects. This has no effect against permanent conditions.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 104*
