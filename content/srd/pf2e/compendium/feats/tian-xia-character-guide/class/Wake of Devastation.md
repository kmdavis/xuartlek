# Wake of Devastation  *Feat 20*

`Monk`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/class/Kaiju Stance|Kaiju Stance]]
- **Requirements**: You are in Kaiju Stance and are touching the ground.

Like a kaiju, you leave a trail of destruction in your wake. Your shattering earth attacks gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/Razing|razing]] trait. Whenever you succeed with a shattering earth attack while standing on the ground, the earth buckles under the force of the blow, and all the squares beneath you become [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Whenever you critically succeed with a shattering earth attack against a creature standing on the ground in an area of difficult terrain, you pummel the creature into the earth; they become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] until they succeed at an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]] attempt against your class DC.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 121*
