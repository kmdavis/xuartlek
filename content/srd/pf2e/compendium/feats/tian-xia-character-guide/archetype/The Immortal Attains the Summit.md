# The Immortal Attains the Summit  *Feat 16*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Cultivator|Cultivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Three Pecks of Dew|Three Pecks of Dew]]

Your cultivation bearing glorious fruit, you behold Heaven’s will. Returning from your vision of the Holy Mountain, you become an immortal with mystic potency over the terrestrial realms of wind and water.

You cease aging and, regardless of your actual existential state, now register as a living being to effects that can detect living creatures (such as lifesense or spiritsense). 

In addition, you learn the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Control Weather|_control weather_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Plant Growth|_plant growth_]] rituals. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]] instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]] for these rituals. On a success with one of these rituals, you gain the effects of a critical success instead.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 115*
