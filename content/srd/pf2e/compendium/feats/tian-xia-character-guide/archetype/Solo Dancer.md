# Solo Dancer  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Fan Dancer Dedication|Fan Dancer Dedication]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]]

You often dance alone with a grace exceeding that of most other performers, carrying yourself with a poise and confidence that draws the attention of those around you. You can always roll [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]] for initiative, and during the first round of combat, creatures that act after you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 98*
