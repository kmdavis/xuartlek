# Momentous Charge  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Monk`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 8)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Rushing Goat Stance|Rushing Goat Stance]]
- **Requirements**: You’re in Rushing Goat Stance.

You rush into your enemies with great and reckless force. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]], Stride, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]], then make a ramming horn Strike. On a success, the target of the Strike is pushed up to 10 feet directly away from you. On a critical success, the target is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] 1.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 120*
