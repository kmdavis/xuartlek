# Fulu Familiar  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Talisman`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]] or Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Religion|Religion]]

You can imprint your familiar’s spiritual essence into a fulu, a small paper charm that can be affixed to objects, creatures, or structures. You gain the Create Familiar Fulu action, which you can use once per day; at 12th level, this increases to twice per day, and at 18th level, it increases to three times per day.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 116*
