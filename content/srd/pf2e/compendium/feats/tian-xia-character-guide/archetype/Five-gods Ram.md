# Five-gods Ram  *Feat 12*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Monk`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 14)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Rushing Goat Stance|Rushing Goat Stance]]
- **Requirements**: You’re in Rushing Goat Stance.

You attack in an unceasing flurry of charging headbutts. Stride or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]] and then make a ramming horn Strike. If the Strike is successful, you immediately push the target back 10 feet, then Stride or Climb and make a second ramming horn Strike against them. If the second Strike is successful, you immediately knock the target [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Prone|prone]], but if the second Strike is a failure or if you’re unable to complete all the required actions, you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] 1. Both Strikes count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn’t increase until you’ve made both of them.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 120*
