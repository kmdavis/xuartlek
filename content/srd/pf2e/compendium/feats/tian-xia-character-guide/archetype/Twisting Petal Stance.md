# Twisting Petal Stance  *Feat 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Monk`  `Stance`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]]
- **Requirements**: You're [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/Armor#Unarmored|unarmored]].

Your body moves like a petal twisting on the wind, confounding your opponents while using their energy against them. You can make gale blossom strikes that deal 1d6 slashing damage. These strikes are in the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Agile|agile]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Finesse|finesse]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Shove|shove]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. While in Twisting Petal Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC to avoid being Shoved. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC to resist an opponent’s Feint attempt.
