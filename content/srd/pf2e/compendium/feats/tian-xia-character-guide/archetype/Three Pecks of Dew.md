# Three Pecks of Dew  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Cultivator|Cultivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Cultivator Dedication|Cultivator Dedication]]; you aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unholy|unholy]]

At this stage of cultivation, your body is as much spirit as flesh. While still fettered to the physical realm, your form flourishes without needing conventional food or drink, and your natural healing now rivals that brought by scalpel and suture.

You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sanctified|sanctified]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Holy|holy]] trait. When you perform the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Subsist|Subsist]] downtime activity, you can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]] for the skill check (instead of the skills normally associated with your environment). If you do so, this activity gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Vitality|vitality]] trait as you subsist on ambient qi within your environment’s dew-laden air and create shelter from solidified emanations of qi.

Additionally, when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Refocus|Refocus]], you can also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Wounds|Treat Wounds]] at the same time. If you do so, you can use Occultism for checks to Treat Wounds and disregard the need for a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Healer's Toolkit|healer’s kit]]; this activity gains the vitality trait as you circulate healing qi through your own body or transfuse your ally with healing qi.
