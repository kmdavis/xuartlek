# Induce Imbalance  *Feat 14*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Flourish`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Five-breath Vanguard|Five-breath Vanguard]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Five-breath Vanguard Dedication|Five-breath Vanguard Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You're in an elemental stance.

Your blows can disrupt the delicate balance of elemental energies that keep a body in good health. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] the target using the unarmed attack associated with your current elemental stance. On a success, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]] 2 until the end of your next turn. On a critical failure, the target is clumsy 3 for 1 minute. Elementals take a –2 circumstance penalty to their save.
