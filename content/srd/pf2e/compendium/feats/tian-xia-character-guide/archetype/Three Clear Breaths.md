# Three Clear Breaths  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Cultivator|Cultivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Cultivator Dedication|Cultivator Dedication]]; Constitution +2

Through your disciplined condensations and circulations of qi, you’ve caught a fleeting glimpse of cultivation’s promise, and your health has handsomely profited along the way. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Breath Control|Breath Control]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Diehard|Diehard]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Fast Recovery|Fast Recovery]] feats. You must meet the prerequisites for these feats as normal. For each of these feats you already have, you can instead gain a different feat from the following list: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Canny Acumen|Canny Acumen]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Fleet|Fleet]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Toughness|Toughness]].

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 114*
