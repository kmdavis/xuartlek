# Desperate Wish  *Feat 12*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Starlit Sentinel|Starlit Sentinel]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Starlit Sentinel Dedication|Starlit Sentinel Dedication]]

You call out a desperate wish to the stars to save a friend. You gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/Breath of Life|_breath of life_]] as an innate [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Arcane|arcane]] spell, which you can cast once per day only while in sentinel form. Interceding in such a direct way temporarily exhausts your constellation’s magic, causing you to revert from your sentinel form once the spell is cast.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 94*
