# Whirling in the Summer Storm  *Feat 12*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Monk`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 14)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Twisting Petal Stance|Twisting Petal Stance]]
- **Requirements**: You’re in Twisting Petal Stance.

Your hands move in a hypnotic and distracting flurry that throws your foes off-balance before you cast them away with a violent pirouette. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]]. Then each enemy within your reach must attempt a Will save against the higher of your class DC or your Deception DC; on a failure, they’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] to melee attacks from you and your allies until the end of your next turn. Then, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] up to three adjacent enemies; each Shove counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you don’t increase your penalty until after you’ve resolved all the Shove attempts.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 121*
