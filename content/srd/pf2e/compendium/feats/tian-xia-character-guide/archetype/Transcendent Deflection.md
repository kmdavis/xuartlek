# Transcendent Deflection  *Feat 10*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Spirit Warrior|Spirit Warrior]]
- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Spirit Warrior Dedication|Spirit Warrior Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: An enemy within your reach would damage you or your ally with an attack.
- **Requirements**: You're wielding a one-handed, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Agile|agile]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Finesse|finesse]] melee weapon.

You charge your weapon with spiritual energy and intercept the attack. The weapon becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Broken|broken]], and the target is unharmed by the attack. If you’re carrying another one-handed, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Agile|agile]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Finesse|finesse]] melee weapon, you can immediately [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/Carrying Items#Drawing and Stowing Items|Swap]] it for the broken weapon.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 93*
