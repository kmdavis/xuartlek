# General's Gambit  *Feat 12*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Marshal|Marshal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Marshal Dedication|Marshal Dedication]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Five-breath Vanguard Dedication|Five-breath Vanguard Dedication]]

You move strategically forward to draw the enemy’s attention away from your allies. You Stride toward an enemy and attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Create a Diversion|Create a Diversion]], except you can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]] or Warfare [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Lore|Lore]] instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]]. On a success, in addition to the normal outcome for Creating a Diversion, the enemy becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Fascinated|fascinated]] with you until the start of your next turn and can’t use reactions against allies in your aura. On a critical success, the enemy becomes fascinated with you until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 91*
