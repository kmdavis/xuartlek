# Tempest Cloud's Speed  *Feat 6*

`Air`  `Archetype`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Encumbered|encumbered]].

You and your familiar coordinate to channel elemental air. Your familiar gains the following ability. 

**Activate** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") Path of the Tempest; **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Air|air]]) Your familiar summons winds that speed up and protect you. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed until the end of your turn. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] this turn, you don’t trigger reactions. In addition, you don’t need to Stride before a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]] this turn.
