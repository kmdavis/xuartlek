# Five-breath Vanguard Dedication  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Five-breath Vanguard|Five-breath Vanguard]]
- **Prerequisites**: Two of the five elemental stances ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/Ironblood Stance|Ironblood Stance]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Mountain Stance|Mountain Stance]], Reflective Ripple Stance, Stoked Flame Stance, and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/Tangled Forest Stance|Tangled Forest Stance]])
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin or exposure to Tian elementalism

You flow like the elemental cycle, adapting your stance and techniques constantly in response to whatever circumstances you face. You gain the Cycle Elemental Stance action. 

**Activate** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") Cycle Elemental Stance; **Requirements** You’re in one of the elemental stances; **Effect** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]], and then enter a different elemental stance from the one you’re currently in.
