# Wandering Chef Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Wandering Chef|Wandering Chef]]

Whether trained in a noble kitchen or a humble home, you know how to cook delicious dishes and source ingredients from the wilderness or urban settings. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]]; if you were already trained in Crafting, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Cookware|cookware]] instead of an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist’s toolkit]] to craft alchemical foods. When using the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Subsist|Subsist]] downtime activity, you can use Crafting or Cooking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Lore|Lore]] in place of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Survival|Survival]], and if you roll a failure, you get a success instead.

You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/Archetypes#Alchemical Archetypes|Quick Alchemy]] benefits, but can use it only to create [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Building Items#Consumables|consumables]], and the consumables must be [[srd/pf2e/books/treasure-vault-remastered/alchemy-unleashed/Alchemical Foods|alchemical food]]. Any items you choose with Alchemical Crafting must be alchemical food, but they can be 1st level or 2nd level instead of only 1st level. 

You create up to 4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Alchemist|versatile vials]] during your daily preparations. Typically, a wandering chef’s versatile vials take the form of parcels of foraged ingredients.
