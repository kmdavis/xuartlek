# Fan Dancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]]
- **Access**: Tian Xia origin

You sweep across the battlefield, manifesting both the gentle spring breeze and the crisp autumn gusts. You become an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]]. At 7th level you become a master in Performance, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Performance. Whenever you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]] while holding a fan, you can give your Feint the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Air|air]] trait. If you do, you can Stride 10 feet before or after as part of the same action.
