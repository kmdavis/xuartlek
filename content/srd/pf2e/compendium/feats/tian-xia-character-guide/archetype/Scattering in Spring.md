# Scattering in Spring  *Feat 8*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Monk`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 10)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Twisting Petal Stance|Twisting Petal Stance]]
- **Trigger**: An enemy within your reach targets you with a melee attack, and you're aware of the attack.
- **Requirements**: You’re in Twisting Petal Stance.

You evade an incoming attack by twisting your enemy’s tactics in your favor. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can immediately attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] against the triggering attacker; if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 120*
