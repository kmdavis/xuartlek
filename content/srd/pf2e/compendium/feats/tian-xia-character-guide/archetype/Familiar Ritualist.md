# Familiar Ritualist  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]; ability to cast a ritual

Your familiar’s magical presence can serve as an aspect of a ritual. When you cast a ritual, your familiar can serve as a secondary caster, fulfilling any requirements for the secondary caster as well as a secondary check. You can’t replace a secondary caster who’s the target of the spell.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 116*
