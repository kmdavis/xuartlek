# Close Formation  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]

You practiced fan dance as a member of a large group, coordinating your moves together to create living art and heightening your sense of spatial awareness. When you wield two fans, each in a different hand, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 20 feet.
