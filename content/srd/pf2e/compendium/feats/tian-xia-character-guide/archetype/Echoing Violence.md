# Echoing Violence  *Feat 18*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Archetype`  `Death`  `Incapacitation`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Martial Artist|Martial Artist]]
- **Frequency**: once per minute
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Martial Artist Dedication|Martial Artist Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: You critically succeed with an unarmed [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against an opponent.

Your powerful attack causes damage that reverberates through your opponent’s body, shaking muscle from bone. The creature takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC. If it critically fails, it immediately dies as your blow tears apart its body internally.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 121*
