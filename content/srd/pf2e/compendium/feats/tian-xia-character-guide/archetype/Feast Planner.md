# Feast Planner  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Wandering Chef|Wandering Chef]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Wandering Chef Dedication|Wandering Chef Dedication]]

You learn the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Fortifying Brew|fortifying brew]] ritual, but you instead prepare dishes for a lavish meal that have the same effect as the drinks from the normal version of the ritual. You perform both aspects of the ritual yourself, acting as both primary and a secondary caster. You must still attempt the secondary check normally performed by a secondary caster. In addition, you can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] or Cooking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Lore|Lore]] for both the primary and secondary checks. The ritual gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] trait as nature spirits attend to you and your guests.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 101*
