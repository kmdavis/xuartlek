# Flowing Palm Deflection  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Spirit Warrior|Spirit Warrior]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Spirit Warrior Dedication|Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

The simple and precise movements of your hands allow you to deflect blows with the same efficacy as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Raise a Shield|raised shield]]. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Parry|parry]] with your fist, increase the circumstance bonus to AC it grants from +1 to +2.
