# Know Your Enemy  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Marshal|Marshal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Marshal Dedication|Marshal Dedication]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Strategist Stance|Strategist Stance]]
- **Requirements**: Strategist Stance

You aim to turn knowledge into power against your foes. Attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] check against an enemy you’re observing. Any ally within your aura who’s trained in the skill you use to attempt the Recall Knowledge check can use their reaction to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Aid|Aid]] you on the check without having prepared to help first.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 91*
