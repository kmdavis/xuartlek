# Lotus Above the Mud  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Cultivator|Cultivator]]

When you cast your _adapt self_ or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/Athletic Rush|_athletic rush_]] focus spells, you ignore [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] until the end of your next turn.
