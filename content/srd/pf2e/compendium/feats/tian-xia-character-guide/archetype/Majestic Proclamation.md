# Majestic Proclamation  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Starlit Sentinel|Starlit Sentinel]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Starlit Sentinel Dedication|Starlit Sentinel Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You’re in your sentinel form.

You announce your name to your enemies, bringing your constellation to bear in a blinding display. Attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] all enemies within 30 feet. Demoralize loses the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Auditory|auditory]] trait and gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Visual|visual]] trait when used this way. In addition to the regular effects of Demoralize, enemies become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute on a successful check (and also blinded for 1 round on a critical success).

You can use Majestic Proclamation as a single action if your previous action was Starlit Transformation.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 94*
