# Golden Dragon's Bounty  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]

Your familiar alters the material of a spell into that of a precious metal. If the next action you use is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] that deals bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, that damage is your choice of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Cold Iron|cold iron]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Silver|silver]], or steel for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, vulnerabilities, and the like. At 14th level, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/Adamantine|adamantine]] is added to the list of metal choices. The spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Metal|metal]] trait.
