# Additional Servings  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Wandering Chef|Wandering Chef]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Wandering Chef Dedication|Wandering Chef Dedication]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] or Cooking Lore

Your number of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Alchemist|versatile vials]] per day increases to 5.

**Special** At 12th level or higher, you can select this feat a second time to increase your number of versatile vials per day to 6. At 18th level or higher, you can select this feat a third time to increase your number of versatile vials per day to 7.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 100*
