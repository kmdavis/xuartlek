# Protective Cycle  *Feat 16*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Five-breath Vanguard|Five-breath Vanguard]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Five-breath Vanguard Dedication|Five-breath Vanguard Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: You take damage from an attack.
- **Requirements**: You're in an elemental stance.

You react to harm by flowing into an elemental stance with new advantages. You Cycle Elemental Stance and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.
