# Familiar's Resolve  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]

When you weave magic, your familiar traces symbols that throw off fear and doubt. If the next action you use is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]], you or an ally within 30 feet gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against effects that would cause you to be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Doomed|doomed]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. At 14th level, you or an ally gain a +3 status bonus to saving throws against these effects instead.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 116*
