# Twirling Strike  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Fan Dancer Dedication|Fan Dancer Dedication]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Twirl Through|Twirl Through]]
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a fan.

Your fans, one raised up alongside your head and the other alongside your hip, become a blur as you twirl across the battlefield. Attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Tumble Through|Tumble Through]] an enemy’s space using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Performance|Performance]]; on a success, you can make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against the enemy with a fan you’re wielding at any point during the movement. On a critical success, the enemy is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against this attack.
