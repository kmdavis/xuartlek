# Seal of the Golden Dragon  *Feat 10*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Golden Dragon's Bounty|Golden Dragon's Bounty]]
- **Trigger**: You would take [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Poison|poison]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]] damage.
- **Requirements**: Your familiar has the resistance ability and is adjacent to you.

Your familiar coils around you, protecting you with its magical resistance. You gain the benefits of your familiar’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Resistance|resistance]] ability until the start of your next turn. If your familiar has any upgrades to this ability, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Familiar Abilities#Major Resistance|major resistance]], you gain them as well. Once per day, if your familiar’s resistance is a different damage type, your familiar’s resistance ability changes to the appropriate type. If you already have resistance of the same type, choose the higher of the two.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 117*
