# Food Preservation  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Wandering Chef|Wandering Chef]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Morning Side Dishes|Morning Side Dishes]]

Your techniques extend the storage life for certain ingredients and food through pickling, alcohol, or fermentation. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Advanced Alchemy|advanced alchemy]] to create alchemical food, you can designate one infused item to not automatically expire when you next make your daily preparations. When you preserve another item in this way, the previous item becomes inedible. 

The number of items you can preserve during your daily preparation and the maximum number of items you can have preserved before spoiling older items increases to two if you’re an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] or Cooking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Lore|Lore]], to three if you’re a master, and to five if you’re legendary.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 100*
