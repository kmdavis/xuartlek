# Adamantine Body  *Feat 8*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] equal to 2 + half your level against all damage from the triggering attack or effect.
