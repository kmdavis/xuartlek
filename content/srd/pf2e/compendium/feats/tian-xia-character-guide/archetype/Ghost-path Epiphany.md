# Ghost-path Epiphany  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Cultivator|Cultivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Cultivator Dedication|Cultivator Dedication]]; you aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Holy|holy]]

Deviating from the orthodox path, you tread an inauspicious descent littered with flowers of death; this heretical choice will, in time, earn you the enmity and fear of more traditional cultivators. You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sanctified|sanctified]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unholy|unholy]] trait. You also learn the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Call Spirit|_call spirit_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Commune|_commune_]] rituals, which can be used to contact only [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Undead|undead]] or entities from the Void or the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/Planes#The Netherworld|Netherworld]].

When you cast a ritual, you can reduce the number of secondary casters by 1 as you direct decaying fragments of your own soul, felled by your proximity to death, to assist with the ritual. When you do, you must fulfill any requirements for the secondary caster, and you attempt the secondary check normally performed by that secondary caster. You can’t replace a secondary caster who’s the target of the spell (as in the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rituals/Atone|_atone_]] ritual).
