# Sacred Wilds Oath  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Spirit Warrior|Spirit Warrior]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Spirit Warrior Dedication|Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

You’ve sworn an oath to protect the holy places within nature and the spirits that dwell within, from ancient trees to primordial wild guardians. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Make an Impression|Make an Impression]] on animals and to make very simple [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Request|Requests]] of them. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to Make an Impression when interacting with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Beast|beast]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fey|fey]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/monster-core-2/Kami|kami]]. You gain the following edict. 

**Edict** You must aid any animal or nature spirit in need unless it violates your other tenets or puts someone else at risk of immediate harm.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 92*
