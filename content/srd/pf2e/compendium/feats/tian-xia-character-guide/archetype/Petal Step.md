# Petal Step  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Fan Dancer Dedication|Fan Dancer Dedication]]

You have a mastery over your own movement and can distribute your weight to dance lightly over the ground like petals drifting on the water. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]] and aren’t detectable by [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Tremorsense|tremorsense]].

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 98*
