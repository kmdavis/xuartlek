# Phoenix's Flight  *Feat 16*

`Archetype`  `Fire`  `Magical`  `Polymorph`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Familiar Sage|Familiar Sage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Familiar Sage Dedication|Familiar Sage Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You're adjacent to or sharing the same space as your familiar.

You and your familiar learn the secrets of elemental fire, allowing you to merge together to become a legendary creature—a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/beast/Phoenix|phoenix]]. You can cast monstrosity form as an innate [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] spell once per day, except you can transform only into a phoenix, and your familiar must be adjacent to you before you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast the Spell]]. When you Cast the Spell, your familiar merges into your form. While transformed, you gain the Blazing Conflagration action. 

**Activate** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") Blazing Conflagration; **Requirements** You’re transformed into phoenix form by the monstrosity form spell granted by this feat; **Effect** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Light|light]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Visual|visual]]) You dismiss phoenix form while a fiery corona erupts from you as searing light. Each creature in a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Burst|burst]] takes 16d6 fire damage with a basic Fortitude save against your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Spell DC|spell DC]]; creatures that critically fail are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round. You gain 8d6 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]]. At 18th level and again at 20th level, the burst deals an additional 2d6 fire damage, and you gain an additional 1d6 temporary Hit Points.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 117*
