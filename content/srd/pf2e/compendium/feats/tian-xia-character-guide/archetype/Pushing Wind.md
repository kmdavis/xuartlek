# Pushing Wind  *Feat 8*

`Air`  `Archetype`  `Aura`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Fan Dancer|Fan Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/archetype/Fan Dancer Dedication|Fan Dancer Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a fan.

As you spin and glide your fans alongside your allies, you kick up a mild wind that gently carries you all forward. So long as you’re holding a fan, you and allies who start their turn in a 30- foot aura [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Emanation|emanating]] around you gain a +5-foot circumstance bonus to land Speed for 1 round; you also gain this bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] if you possess one, but it can’t grant you the ability to fly if you wouldn’t otherwise be able to. 

Additionally, the air impedes the movements of your foes. While holding a fan, the area in a 10-foot aura emanating around you is [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for all enemies.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 98*
