# Energy Fortification  *Feat 2*

`General`  `Skill`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/tian-xia-character-guide/skill/Prepare Elemental Medicine|Prepare Elemental Medicine]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]]
- **Access**: Minkai or Forest of Spirits origin

You learn Minkaian traditions about the void and know how to manipulate the five elements to fortify vitality and void energies in the body. When you Prepare Elemental Medicine, you can choose to use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]] instead of the usual skills. If you do, the medicine also grants the recipient [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 2 and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weakness]] 2 based on the element you chose. A wood, earth, or water elemental medicine grants resistance to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]] damage and weakness to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Vitality|vitality]] damage, and a living recipient loses its [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Immunity|immunity]] to vitality damage for the duration. A fire or metal elemental medicine grants resistance to vitality damage and weakness to void damage, and an undead recipient loses its immunity to void damage for the duration.

If you’re a master in Occultism, increase the resistance and weakness to 4. If you’re legendary, instead increase them to 8.

*Source: Tian Xia Character Guide p. 108*
