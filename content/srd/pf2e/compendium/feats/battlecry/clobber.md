# Clobber  *Feat 16*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Guardian`

- **Requirements**: You are wearing medium or heavy armor.

With a mighty blow, you send an enemy flying backward and onto the ground. Strike a foe. This Strike deals an extra die of weapon damage. If you hit and deal damage, you can automatically Shove the target, with the same benefits as the Shove action (including the critical success effect, if your Strike was a critical hit). The target then falls prone. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 49*
