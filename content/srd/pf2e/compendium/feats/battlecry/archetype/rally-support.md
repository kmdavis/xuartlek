# Rally Support  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/guerrilla|Guerrilla]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/archetype/guerrilla-dedication|Guerrilla Dedication]]; trained in either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]]

You often rely on the local populace for supplies and shelter. Once per day, you can attempt a check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Request|Request]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Coerce|Coerce]] against an easy DC for your level when speaking with someone who is a non-hostile, long-term resident of your favored location. On a success, choose one of the following to obtain. 
 A nonmagical item of your level or lower A safe place to shelter for 24 hours 3 days' worth of food and water for you and up to 5 allies
