# Mighty Bulwark  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`  `Guardian`

- **Archetypes**: Stalwart Defender (Level 12), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/sentinel|Sentinel]] (Level 10), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/sentinel|Sentinel]] (Level 10)

Thanks to the incredible connection you have forged with your armor, you can use it to shrug off an extensive array of dangers. Your bonus from the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/bulwark|bulwark]] armor trait increases from +3 to +4, and it applies on all Reflex saves, not just damaging Reflex saves.
