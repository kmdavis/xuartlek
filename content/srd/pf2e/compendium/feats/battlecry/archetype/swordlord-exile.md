# Swordlord Exile  *Feat 20*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/aldori-duelist|Aldori Duelist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/archetype/aldori-duelist-dedication|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]; you have broken or forsaken the Aldori swordpact.

You are a swordlord exile. You have abandoned or been abandoned by the Aldori Academy. Aldori swordlords are honor-bound to challenge you on sight. Additionally, your unbridled skill strikes terror in those around you. You can use Dueling [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Lore]] instead of Intimidation to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Coerce|Coerce]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] while your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/aldori-dueling-sword-weapon-535|Aldori dueling sword]] is visible to the target of your attempt. 

With broken pride, your blade is now a disgraced tool used to remove obstacles between you and your goals, whatever they may be. The first time you hit a creature with an Aldori dueling sword each round, you deal 5 additional precision damage.

**Special** You can’t take both this feat and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/archetype/crossbow-infiltrator-dedication|Aldori Swordlord]].

*Source: Battlecry! p. 55*
