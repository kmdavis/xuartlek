# Captain Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Captain|Captain]]
- **Prerequisites**: Charisma +2; you don't have an [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/Companions#Animal Companions|animal companion]], [[srd/pf2e/books/guns-gears-remastered/gears-characters/Construct Companions|construct companion]], or other companion that functions similarly

You become trained in your choice of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]]; if you are already trained in both of these skills, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain your choice of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Group Impression|Group Impression]] or the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Group Coercion|Group Coercion]] skill feat. If you already have both of these skill feats, you gain another 1st level skill feat for which you qualify. 

You also gain the assistance of a dedicated follower, who has agreed to accompany you on your journeys. They have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Minion|minion]] trait and begin as a novice follower. [See followers](https://2e.aonprd.com/Followers.aspx) for more information.

**Special** Once you have a follower, you can never take a feat or class feature that grants an animal companion or another companion that prevents you from having an animal companion.
