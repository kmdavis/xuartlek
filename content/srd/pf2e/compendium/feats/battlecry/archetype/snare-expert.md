# Snare Expert  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/guerrilla|Guerrilla]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/archetype/guerrilla-dedication|Guerrilla Dedication]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

Your knowledge of traps aids you in defending your hideout. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/skill/snare-crafting|Snare Crafting]] feat. When you set a snare, any saving throw it requires uses the higher of your class DC or the snare's DC. Snares you set within your favored location increase their Stealth DC by 2.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 60*
