# Jotun's Transposition  *Feat 17*

`Jotunborn`  `Uncommon`

You’ve learned to fully move across planes, beginning with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]]. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/interplanar-teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] twice per week as an occult [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate spell]] that can target only yourself. Your body serves as the locus for the spell and allows you to travel between the Ethereal Plane, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#The Universe|the Universe]], or the space between the planes known as the Fray without needing a planar key. You can use these castings to travel to other planes if you have the appropriate planar keys, as normal.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 15*
