# Tactical Guidance  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Captain
- **Prerequisites**: Captain Dedication

You can provide tactical guidance to help your follower act more efficiently. You can spend 2 actions to Direct your Follower instead of 1. If you do, your follower gains an additional action.
