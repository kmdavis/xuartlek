# Rally Support  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: Guerrilla
- **Prerequisites**: Guerrilla Dedication; trained in either Diplomacy or Intimidation

You often rely on the local populace for supplies and shelter. Once per day, you can attempt a check to Request or Coerce against an easy DC for your level when speaking with someone who is a non-hostile, long-term resident of your favored location. On a success, choose one of the following to obtain. 
 A nonmagical item of your level or lower A safe place to shelter for 24 hours 3 days' worth of food and water for you and up to 5 allies
