# Stomp Ground  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Guardian`

You bring your booted foot down on the ground with enough force to rattle your foes. Each creature in a 5-foot emanation must attempt a Reflex saving throw against your class DC. 
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected. 
**Success** The creature is off-guard until the end of your turn. 
**Failure** The creature is knocked prone. 
**Critical Failure** The creature is knocked prone and takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage from the fall.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 44*
