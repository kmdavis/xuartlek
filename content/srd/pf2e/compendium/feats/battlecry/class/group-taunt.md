# Group Taunt  *Feat 8*

`Guardian`

Your taunts draw the attention of multiple enemies at once. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Taunt|Taunt]], you can choose up to three targets within range, and you can have up to three taunted enemies at a time. Each time you Taunt, you can choose which enemies remain taunted and which the effect ends for. You must remain at or below this limit.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 45*
