# Disarming Intercept  *Feat 6*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Guardian`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Intercept Attack|Intercept Attack]]
- **Trigger**: You Intercept an Attack that was made with a melee weapon by a creature you’re adjacent to.

When you catch a weapon in your armor, you can move your body to wrench it from your foe's grasp. After [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Intercept Attack|Intercepting the Attack]], attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Disarm|Disarm]] the weapon used for that attack. You don't need to have a hand free, and you gain an item bonus to the Athletics check equal to the value of your armor's potency rune.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 43*
