# Shield Wallop  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Guardian`

- **Requirements**: You are wearing a shield.

Attacks with your shield knock the sense out of your foes. Make a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/shield/shield-bash|shield bash]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/shield/shield-boss|shield boss]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/shield/shield-spikes|shield spikes]] Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] 1 until the start of your next turn (stupefied 2 on a critical hit). If your shield is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Tower Shield|tower shield]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/shields#Fortress Shield|fortress shield]], or another shield that grants a higher circumstance bonus to AC when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Take Cover|Take Cover]] behind it, the creature is instead stupefied 2 if you hit and deal damage to it (stupefied 3 on a critical hit).
