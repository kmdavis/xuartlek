# Taunting Strike  *Feat 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Guardian`

The force of your blow causes your enemy to focus their attention on you. Make a Strike. Regardless of whether the Strike hits, you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Taunt|Taunt]] the target. Your Taunt gains the visual trait.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 42*
