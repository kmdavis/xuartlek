# Armored Counterattack  *Feat 12*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Guardian`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Intercept Attack|Intercept Attack]]
- **Trigger**: You use Intercept Attack against a melee Strike and are adjacent to the creature that made the Strike.

With the might of your armor behind you, you hit back at a foe who would dare try to hurt your allies. After [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Intercept Attack|Intercepting the Attack]], make your own Strike against the triggering enemy. If your Strike hits, you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/battlecry#Taunt|Taunt]] the target; this Taunt gains the visual trait.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 46*
