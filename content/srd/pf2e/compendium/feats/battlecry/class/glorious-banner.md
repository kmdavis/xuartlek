# Glorious Banner  *Feat 20*

`Commander`

Your banner is an awesome sight to behold. Your commander's banner now affects a 60-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] (or an 80-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]] if you are using Plant Banner); the status bonus to Will saves and DCs against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]] effects granted to your allies increases to +2; and you and affected allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Fortitude saves, and Reflex saves. Enemies within the aura of your commander's banner take a –2 status penalty to Will saves as long as they can see your banner.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 35*
