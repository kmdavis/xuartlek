# Shoulder Check  *Feat 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Guardian`

You hit a foe with your armor to throw them off balance. Make a fist Strike even if you don't have a hand free; if you're wielding a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/brawling/gauntlet|gauntlet]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/brawling/spiked-gauntlet|spiked gauntlet]], you can make a Strike with one of those weapons instead. The Strike gains the following additional results. 
**Critical Success** The target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against melee attacks you attempt against it until the end of your next turn. 
**Success** The target is off-guard against the next melee attack you attempt against it before the end of your current turn. 
**Critical Failure** You are off-guard against melee attacks the target attempts against you until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 42*
