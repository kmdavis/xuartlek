# Deceptive Tactics  *Feat 1*

`Commander`

- **Requirements**: You can prepare at least three tactics.

Your training has taught you that the art of war is the art of deception. You can use your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Lore|Warfare Lore]] modifier in place of your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Deception|Deception]] modifier for Deception checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Create a Diversion|Create a Diversion]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]], and can use your proficiency rank in Warfare Lore instead of your proficiency rank in Deception to meet the prerequisites of feats that modify the Create a Diversion or Feint actions (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Lengthy Diversion|Lengthy Diversion]]). You gain the Lengthy Diversion feat.
