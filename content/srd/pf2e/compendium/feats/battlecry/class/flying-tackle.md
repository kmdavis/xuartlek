# Flying Tackle  *Feat 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Flourish`  `Guardian`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]

You barrel forward, gathering enough momentum to take down a threatening foe. Stride and then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]], or attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]]. If you end your movement adjacent to a foe, you can attempt to Trip that foe. If you succeed at the Athletics check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]], you get a critical success instead. You can use Flying Tackle while Burrowing, Climbing, Flying, or Swimming instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
