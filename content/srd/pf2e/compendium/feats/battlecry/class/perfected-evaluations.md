# Perfected Evaluations  *Feat 12*

`Commander`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/class/unrivaled-analysis|Unrivaled Analysis]]

You instantly assess the strengths and weaknesses of the enemy forces. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/battlecry/class/rapid-assessment|Rapid Assessment]], you can attempt up to six [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] checks against enemies you are observing.
