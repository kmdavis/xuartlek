# Observational Analysis  *Feat 4*

`Commander`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/archetype/combat-assessment|Combat Assessment]]

You are able to rapidly discern relevant details about your opponents in the heat of combat. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/archetype/combat-assessment|Combat Assessment]] against a target that you or an ally has targeted with a Strike or spell since the start of your last turn, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] check (+4 if the Strike from Combat Assessment is a critical hit.)

*Source: Battlecry! p. 31*
