# Unsteadying Strike  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Commander`  `Flourish`

Your attack makes your opponent more susceptible to follow-up maneuvers from your allies. Make a melee Strike against an enemy within your reach. If the Strike is successful, the enemy takes a –2 circumstance penalty to their Fortitude DC to resist being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grappled]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Reposition|Repositioned]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shoved]] and a –2 circumstance penalty to their Reflex DC to resist being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Disarm|Disarmed]]. Both penalties last until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 31*
