# Lifesense  *Feat 5*

`Divine`  `Duskwalker`

You have a limited ability to sense life force, like your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/psychopomp|psychopomp]] forebears. You gain lifesense as an imprecise sense with a range of 10 feet. This allows you to sense the life force of living and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creatures within the listed range. The sense can distinguish between the vitality energy animating living creatures and the void energy animating undead creatures, much as sight distinguishes colors.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 49*
