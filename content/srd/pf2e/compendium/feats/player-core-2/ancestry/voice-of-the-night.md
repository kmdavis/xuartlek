# Voice of the Night  *Feat 1*

`Dhampir`

You hear and understand the words of creatures you share the darkness with. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]] skill with bats, rats, and wolves. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Make an Impression on such animals.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 43*
