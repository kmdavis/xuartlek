# Winglets  *Feat 5*

`Kobold`

Through the lingering influence of your warren's patron, you've sprouted a small set of wings. While not suitable for full flight, these weak wings can help you jump further with a small flap. When [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leaping]] horizontally, you move an additional 5 feet. This additional distance isn't cumulative with the increased Leap distance from the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/powerful-leap|Powerful Leap]] feat. In addition, when you attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]], you can jump a distance up to 10 feet farther than you normally would based on the result of your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check, though you still can't jump farther than your Speed. You don't automatically fail your checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]] or Long Jump if you don't Stride at least 10 feet first.
