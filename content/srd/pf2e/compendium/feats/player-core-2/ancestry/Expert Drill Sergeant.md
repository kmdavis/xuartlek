# Expert Drill Sergeant  *Feat 5*

`Hobgoblin`

While exploring, when you are leading and allies are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Follow the Expert|Following the Expert]], instead of granting a +2 circumstance bonus in the applicable skill, you grant a +3 circumstance bonus if you're an expert and a +4 circumstance bonus if you're a master.
