# Terrain Advantage  *Feat 9*

`Lizardfolk`

Non-[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] creatures in difficult terrain are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you. If you have a swim Speed, non-lizardfolk creatures that are in water and lack a swim Speed are also off-guard to you.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 27*
