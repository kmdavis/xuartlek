# Scaly Hide  *Feat 1*

`Dragonblood`

You were born with a layer of scales across your entire body that resemble those of your draconic progenitor. When you’re unarmored, the scales give you a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dexterity cap of +3. The item bonus to AC increases to +2 at 5th level. The item bonus to AC from these scales is cumulative with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/armor-potency-3|_armor potency runes_]] on your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/armor#Explorer's Clothing|explorer's clothing]], the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/mystic-armor|_mystic armor_]] spell, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/bands-of-force-major|_bands of force_]].

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into or out of this feat.
