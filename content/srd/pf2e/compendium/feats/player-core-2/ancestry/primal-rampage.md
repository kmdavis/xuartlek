# Primal Rampage  *Feat 13*

`Lizardfolk`

You gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/unfettered-movement|_unfettered movement_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/mountain-resilience|_mountain resilience_]] as 4th-rank primal innate spells that you can cast once per day. As a 3-action activity, you can Cast a Spell twice to cast both of these innate spells, as long as they are both still available for the day.
