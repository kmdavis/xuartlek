# Sneaky  *Feat 1*

`Hobgoblin`

Stealth is an important tool in your arsenal. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]] action, up to your Speed. 

In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your Stealth check, you don't become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]] if you don't have cover or greater cover and aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] at the end of the Sneak action, as long as you have cover or greater cover or are concealed at the end of your turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 14*
