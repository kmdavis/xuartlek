# Unbound Leaper  *Feat 17*

`Tripkee`

Your jumping prowess is unmatched. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]] up to 30 feet in any direction without touching the ground and without requiring an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check; when doing so, you must land on a space of solid ground within 30 feet of you, or else you fall after using your next action. You can exceed your normal Speed while Leaping.

**Special** If you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/ancestry/fantastic-leaps|Fantastic Leaps]] feat, the total distance you can Leap horizontally is 40 feet, and the total distance you can Leap vertically is 35 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 42*
