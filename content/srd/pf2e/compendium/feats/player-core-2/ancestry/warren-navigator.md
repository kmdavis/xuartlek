# Warren Navigator  *Feat 1*

`Ratfolk`

You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/survival|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sense Direction|Sense Direction]] or attempt a roll against a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/quandary|_quandary_]] spell, you get a result one degree of success better than you rolled. You don't take a penalty to Sense Direction when you lack a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/compass-lensatic|compass]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 30*
