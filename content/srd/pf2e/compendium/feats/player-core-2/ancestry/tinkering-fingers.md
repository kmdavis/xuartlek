# Tinkering Fingers  *Feat 1*

`Ratfolk`

You're trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]. If you are trained in Crafting from another source (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] an item without using a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/repair-toolkit-superb|repair toolkit]] without taking the –2 circumstance penalty, improvising tools from whatever you have at hand. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Crafting checks to Repair an item when you have a repair toolkit.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 30*
