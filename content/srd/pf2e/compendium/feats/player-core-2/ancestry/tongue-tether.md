# Tongue Tether  *Feat 9*

`Tripkee`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/heritages#Snaptongue Tripkee|Snaptongue Tripkee]] heritage

Your tongue can momentarily latch on as readily as your hands. So long as you can freely open your mouth, you do not need free hands in order to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Disarm|Disarm]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grab an Edge|Grab an Edge]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Reposition|Reposition]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]]. If you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/ancestry/long-tongue|Long Tongue]] feat, you can Disarm, Grab an Edge, Reposition, and Trip with your tongue at a distance that is 5 feet beyond your normal reach.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 39*
