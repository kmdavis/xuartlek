# Runtsage  *Feat 5*

`Hobgoblin`

Unlike most of your kind, who dismiss goblins as embarrassments or expendable annoyances, you have studied the methodology behind their irresponsible and incomprehensible actions. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/Adopted Ancestry|Adopted Ancestry]] general feat and must select [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/Goblin|goblin]] as the feat's chosen ancestry. You also gain one 1st-level [goblin ancestry feat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?Traits=618).

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 14*
