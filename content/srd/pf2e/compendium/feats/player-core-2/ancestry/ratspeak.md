# Ratspeak  *Feat 1*

`Ratfolk`

You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]] skill with rodents, including beavers, mice, porcupines, rats, and squirrels, but not with other mammals, such as dogs or bats. The GM determines which [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Animal|animals]] count as rodents.
