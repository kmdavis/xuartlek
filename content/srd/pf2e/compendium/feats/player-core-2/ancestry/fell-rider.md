# Fell Rider  *Feat 9*

`Hobgoblin`

- **Prerequisites**: animal companion

You have trained with your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/companions#Animal Companions|animal companion]] to become a terrifying juggernaut on the battlefield. Your animal companion becomes trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]]. If your animal companion uses [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Support|Support]] while serving as your mount, it grants you the effects of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Aid|Aid]] reaction on your first Intimidation check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] on the same turn, even though it can't take reactions.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 15*
