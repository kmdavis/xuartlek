# Snare Genius  *Feat 5*

`Kobold`

- **Prerequisites**: Snare Crafting; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

If the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/snare|snare]] normally takes 1 minute to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]], you can Craft it with 3 Interact actions instead. Each day during your daily preparations, you can prepare three snares from your formula book for quick deployment (increasing to four snares if you're a master in Crafting and five if you're legendary). Snares prepared in this way don't cost you any resources to Craft.

 When a creature critically fails its saving throw against and takes damage from the initial effect of a snare you Crafted and deployed, that creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 22*
