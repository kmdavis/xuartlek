# Analyze Idiolect  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/linguist|Linguist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/linguist-dedication|Linguist Dedication]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/society|Society]]

You break down a specific individual’s idiolect, memorizing their speech mannerisms and habits. If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, when you later attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Impersonate|Impersonate]] that individual, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks and DCs. Due to the intense character study required, if you Analyze Idiolect again, it replaces any previous idiolect you memorized.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 203*
