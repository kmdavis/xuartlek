# Dodge Away  *Feat 6*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/acrobat|Acrobat]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/acrobat-dedication|Acrobat Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: You are the target of a melee attack.
- **Requirements**: You're aware of the attack and aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]].

You use your acrobatic prowess to evade an attack, using momentum to keep yourself moving if you choose. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can Step after the Strike. If you're a master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]], you can move 10 feet for this Step instead of 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 183*
