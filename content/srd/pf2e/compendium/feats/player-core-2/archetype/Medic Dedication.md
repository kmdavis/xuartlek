# Medic Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Medic|Medic]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Battle Medicine|Battle Medicine]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]

You become an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]. When you succeed with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Battle Medicine|Battle Medicine]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Wounds|Treat Wounds]], the target gains a circumstance bonus to the number of Hit Points they regain equal to 5 HP at DC 20, 10 HP at DC 30, or 15 HP at DC 40. 
 Once per day, you can use Battle Medicine on a creature that’s temporarily immune due to having already been treated with Battle Medicine. If you’re a master in Medicine, you can do so once per hour.
