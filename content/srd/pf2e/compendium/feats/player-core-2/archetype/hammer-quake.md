# Hammer Quake  *Feat 14*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Archetype`  `Flourish`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mauler|Mauler]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/mauler-dedication|Mauler Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a non-[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/finesse|finesse]] melee weapon in two hands.

You smash the ground with your weapon, knocking nearby creatures to the ground. Choose a square within your reach, including your own space. If there’s an enemy in the chosen square, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] that enemy with your two-handed weapon. Regardless of whether you attempted a Strike, you then attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]] every enemy in the chosen square plus each square adjacent to that square, ignoring Trip’s requirement that you have a hand free.

Hammer Quake counts as three attacks toward your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Multiple Attack Penalty|multiple attack penalty]], but the penalty doesn’t increase until after you’ve made the Strike, if any, and all the Trip attempts.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 207*
