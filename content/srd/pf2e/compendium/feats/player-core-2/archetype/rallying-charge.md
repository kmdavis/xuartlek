# Rallying Charge  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Visual`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/marshal|Marshal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/marshal-dedication|Marshal Dedication]]

Your fearless charge into battle reinvigorates your allies to carry on the fight. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to your Speed and make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]]. If your Strike hits and damages an enemy, each ally within 60 feet who saw you hit gains [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Charisma modifier. These temporary Hit Points last until the start of your next turn.
