# Tactical Cadence  *Feat 14*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Marshal|Marshal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Cadence Call|Cadence Call]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Marshal Dedication|Marshal Dedication]]

Your remarkable breath control and concise instructions allow you to coordinate your allies more effectively, even in desperate situations. When you grant allies the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Quickened|quickened]] condition using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Cadence Call|Cadence Call]], they can use the extra action to either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]], and they aren’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 on their following turn if they use the extra action.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 205*
