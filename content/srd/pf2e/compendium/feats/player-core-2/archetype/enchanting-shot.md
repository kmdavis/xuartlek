# Enchanting Shot  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Emotion`  `Magical`  `Mental`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/eldritch-archer|Eldritch Archer]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/eldritch-archer-dedication|Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

With a single whisper carried on the wind, you enchant your ammunition to make a foe more vulnerable to your attacks. Make a bow or crossbow Strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d6 mental damage. On a critical hit, the target also becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stunned|stunned 1]]. The mental damage increases to 3d6 if your weapon has a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/striking-major|_greater striking rune_]], or to 4d6 if your weapon has a _major striking rune_.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 199*
