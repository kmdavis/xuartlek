# Alchemist Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Multiclass`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/alchemist|Alchemist]]
- **Prerequisites**: Intelligence +2

You put your alchemical interest into practice. You become trained in alchemical bombs, the alchemist class DC, and Crafting; if you were already trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]], you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/archetypes#Alchemical Archetypes|Quick Alchemy]] benefits, creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. Add the formulas for four additional common alchemical items to your formula book, in addition to those you gain from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/alchemical-crafting|Alchemical Crafting]].
