# Armored Rebuff  *Feat 8*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/sentinel|Sentinel]], Stalwart Defender (Level 10)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/sentinel-dedication|Sentinel Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: An adjacent enemy critically fails an attack roll to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] you with a melee weapon or unarmed attack.
- **Requirements**: You are wearing medium armor or heavier.

You rebuff puny attacks with your armor, knocking back your foe. Attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] the triggering enemy, even if you don't have a hand free. If you succeed, you can't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] to follow the enemy, as you're knocking the enemy back with the rebounded attack, not by physically moving towards them.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 215*
