# Shoving Sweep  *Feat 8*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mauler|Mauler]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/mauler-dedication|Mauler Dedication]]; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]
- **Trigger**: An enemy within your reach leaves a square during a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/move|move]] action it’s using.
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

You swing your weapon at your foe, rebuffing them back. You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] the triggering creature, ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. Unless you critically succeed at your check, the creature continues its movement after the Shove.
