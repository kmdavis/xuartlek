# Ongoing Investigation  *Feat 4*

`Investigator`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/vindicator|Vindicator]] (Level 4), Lion Blade (Level 6)

You're always investigating what's around you, even as you perform other activities. You can move at full Speed while using the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Investigate|Investigate]] exploration activity, and you can use another exploration activity while Investigating.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 108*
