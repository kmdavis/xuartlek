# Herbalist Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Herbalist|Herbalist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Natural Medicine|Natural Medicine]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]]

You can create natural remedies. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/Archetypes#Alchemical Archetypes|advanced alchemy benefits]]. You can use advanced alchemy to create 4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Alchemical|alchemical]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Consumable|consumables]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] trait, though the number is reduced by 2 if you didn’t make your [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Running Exploration#Daily Preparations|daily preparations]] in the wilderness. These consumables are called your herbal items. You remember herbal item [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/Gear#Formulas|formulas]] and don’t need a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Formula Book (Blank)|formula book]] for them.

You become an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]] and can use Nature instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]] to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] alchemical consumables with the healing trait. You don’t need to be trained in Crafting to do so, and you can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Healer's Toolkit|healer’s tools]] instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist’s tools]].
