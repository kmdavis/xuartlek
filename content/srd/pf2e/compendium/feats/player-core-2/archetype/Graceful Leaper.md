# Graceful Leaper  *Feat 7*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Acrobat|Acrobat]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Acrobat Dedication|Acrobat Dedication]]; Master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]

Mass and muscle are meaningless when you propel yourself through the air; only grace and balance matter. You can roll an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check instead of an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check when making a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]].
