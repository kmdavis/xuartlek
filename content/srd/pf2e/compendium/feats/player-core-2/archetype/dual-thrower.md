# Dual Thrower  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/dual-weapon-warrior|Dual-Weapon Warrior]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/dual-weapon-warrior-dedication|Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication]]

You know how to throw two weapons as easily as strike with them. Whenever a feat you gained from the dual-weapon warrior archetype allows you to make a melee Strike, you can instead make a ranged Strike with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/thrown|thrown]] weapon or a one-handed ranged weapon you are wielding (weapons that need “1+” hand, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/bow/longbow|longbows]], don't qualify). Any effects from these feats that apply to one-handed melee weapons or melee Strikes also apply to one-handed ranged weapons and ranged Strikes.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 196*
