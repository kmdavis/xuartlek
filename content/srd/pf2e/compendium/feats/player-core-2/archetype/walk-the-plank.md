# Walk the Plank  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/pirate|Pirate]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/pirate-dedication|Pirate Dedication]]

You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frighten]] a foe into moving where you want them, traditionally demanding they walk the plank. Attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] an opponent. On a success, in addition to the normal effects, you can also force the target to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to its Speed immediately. You choose the path the target takes, but you can't force it to move into an obviously harmful space (such as into hazardous terrain or a space where it would fall) unless your check was a critical success. As normal for forced movement, this movement doesn't trigger reactions. The target then becomes temporarily immune to Walk the Plank for 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 209*
