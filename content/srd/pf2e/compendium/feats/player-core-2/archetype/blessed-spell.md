# Blessed Spell  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/blessed-one|Blessed One]]
- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/blessed-one-dedication|Blessed One Dedication]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/mercy|Mercy]]; ability to cast spells from spell slots

When you focus your magic on an ally, you can remove harmful conditions. If your next action is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] from a spell slot, and that spell targets only a single ally, you can also attempt to remove a harmful condition from that ally. The condition must be one that could be removed by your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/mercy|Mercy]] feat, including those granted by later feats such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/greater-mercy|Greater Mercy]]. Attempt a counteract check based on the spell’s DC and rank. This effect is in addition to the normal effects of your spell.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 190*
