# Stumbling Stance  *Feat 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Monk`  `Stance`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/martial-artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/martial-artist|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]]

You enter a seemingly unfocused stance that mimics the movements of the inebriated—bobbing, weaving, leaving false openings, and distracting your enemies from your true movements. While in this stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]]. The only Strikes you can make are stumbling swing unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage; are in the brawling group; and have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/agile|agile]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/backstabber|backstabber]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/finesse|finesse]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/nonlethal|nonlethal]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unarmed|unarmed]] traits. If an enemy hits you with a melee Strike while in this stance, it becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the next stumbling swing Strike you make against it before the end of your next turn.
