# Antagonize  *Feat 2*

`Swashbuckler`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/pirate|Pirate]] (Level 4)

Your taunts and threats earn your foes' ire. When you successfully [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] a creature, its [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition can't decrease to less than 1 at the end of its turn until it either uses a hostile action against you or can no longer observe or sense you for at least 1 round.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 165*
