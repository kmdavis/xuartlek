# Investigator Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Multiclass`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Investigator|Investigator]]
- **Prerequisites**: Intelligence +2

You gain the on the case class feature, which grants you both the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Pursue a Lead|Pursue a Lead]] activity and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Clue In|Clue In]] reaction. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]] and another skill of your choice. If you were already trained in Society, you instead become trained in an additional skill of your choice. You also become trained in investigator class DC.
