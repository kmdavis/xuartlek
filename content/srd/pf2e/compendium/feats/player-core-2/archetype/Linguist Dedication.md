# Linguist Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Linguist|Linguist]]
- **Prerequisites**: You speak at least 3 languages

You have studied languages and their development. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]]; if you were already trained in Society, you instead become an expert in Society. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Multilingual|Multilingual]] skill feat twice.
