# Rope Runner  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/pirate|Pirate]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/pirate-dedication|Pirate Dedication]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]

You run and climb across ropes and rigging almost as easily as on the ground. You gain a climb Speed of 15 feet, but only when climbing ropes and similar objects. Whenever you succeed at an Athletics check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]] or an Acrobatics check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Balance|Balance]], you get a critical success instead. You aren't [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] while Climbing or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Balance|Balancing]] on a rope or similar object.
