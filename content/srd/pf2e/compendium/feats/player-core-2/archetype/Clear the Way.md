# Clear the Way  *Feat 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Mauler|Mauler]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Warrior Of Legend|Warrior Of Legend]] (Level 6)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Mauler Dedication|Mauler Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

You put your body behind your massive weapon and swing, shoving enemies to clear a wide path. You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] up to five creatures adjacent to you, rolling a separate [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check for each target and ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. Then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to half your Speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions from any of the creatures you successfully Shoved. Each attack counts toward your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Multiple Attack Penalty|multiple attack penalty]], but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 207*
