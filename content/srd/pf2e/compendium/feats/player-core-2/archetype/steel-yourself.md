# Steel Yourself!  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Auditory`  `Emotion`  `Mental`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/marshal|Marshal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/marshal-dedication|Marshal Dedication]]

You encourage an ally to toughen up, giving them a fighting chance. Choose one ally in your marshal’s aura. The ally gains [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Charisma modifier and a +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves. Both benefits last until the start of your next turn.
