# Scout's Pounce  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Flourish`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/scout|Scout]], Verduran Shadow (Level 10)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/scout-dedication|Scout Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] from or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]] by all of your opponents, and you aren't within 10 feet of any enemy.

You leap from the shadows to strike at your foes. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] up to your Speed, then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] twice. If you were hidden or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unnoticed|unnoticed]] by the target of these Strikes, the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against both attacks. Your multiple attack penalty applies normally for both attacks.
