# Archaeologist Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Archaeologist|Archaeologist]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Thievery|Thievery]]

You are a student of peoples and their histories, and are in constant pursuit of knowledge and artifacts from the past. You become an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Thievery|Thievery]], and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] about ancient history, peoples, and cultures.
