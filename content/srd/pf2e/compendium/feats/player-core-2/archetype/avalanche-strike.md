# Avalanche Strike  *Feat 16*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Archetype`  `Flourish`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mauler|Mauler]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/mauler-dedication|Mauler Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You are wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

You attack all nearby adversaries. Make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with your two-handed weapon against each enemy within its melee reach. Each attack counts toward your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Multiple Attack Penalty|multiple attack penalty]], but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
