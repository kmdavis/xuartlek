# Martial Artist Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/martial-artist|Martial Artist]]

You have trained to use your fists as deadly weapons and can deliver devastatingly powerful blows with them. The damage die for your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/brawling/fist|fist]] unarmed attacks becomes 1d6 instead of 1d4. You don’t take the –2 circumstance penalty for making a lethal attack with your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/nonlethal|nonlethal]] unarmed attacks.
