# Blowgun Poisoner  *Feat 1*

`Alchemist`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/poisoner|Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/poisoner|Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/guerrilla|Guerrilla]] (Level 4)

You can capably deliver toxins with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/dart/blowgun|blowgun]]. Your blowgun [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] can apply [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/injury|injury]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poisons]] even if they deal no damage due to a creature's resistance. If you critically succeed at a blowgun Strike using a poisoned dart, the target's initial save against the poison is one degree of success worse than the creature rolls; this is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/misfortune|misfortune]] effect.

 In addition, if you make a blowgun Strike while [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]], you don't automatically become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Observed|observed]]. Instead, immediately attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] check against the Perception DC of the target. If you succeed, you don't become observed, and are hidden (if you were undetected, you still become hidden rather than remaining undetected).

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 63*
