# Poultice Preparation  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Herbalist|Herbalist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/Herbalist Dedication|Herbalist Dedication]]

When you create an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Elixir|elixir]] as an herbal item, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Oil|oil]] trait (but remains [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Alchemical|alchemical]], not [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]]) and loses the elixir trait. You can apply a poultice by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interacting]] with one hand. When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item’s normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent]] acid, bleed, or fire damage, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 202*
