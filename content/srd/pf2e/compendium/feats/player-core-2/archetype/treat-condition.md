# Treat Condition  *Feat 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Healing`  `Manipulate`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/medic|Medic]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/medic-dedication|Medic Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You are holding healer's tools, or you are wearing them and have a hand free.

You treat an adjacent creature in an attempt to reduce the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]] condition. If a creature has multiple conditions from this list, choose one. Attempt a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/afflictions#Counteracting|counteract]] check against the condition using your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/medicine|Medicine]] modifier as your counteract modifier and the condition’s source to determine the DC. You can’t treat a condition that came from an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/artifact|artifact]] or effect above 20th level unless you have [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/legendary-medic|Legendary Medic]]; even if you do, the counteract DC increases by 10. Treating a Condition that is continually applied under certain circumstances (for instance, the enfeebled condition a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] character gains from carrying a weapon with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/unholy|_unholy_]] rune) has no effect as long as the circumstances continue.

**Critical Success** Reduce the condition value by 2.

**Success** Reduce the condition value by 1.

**Critical Failure** Increase the condition value by 1.
