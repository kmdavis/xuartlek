# Pirate Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/pirate|Pirate]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]]

As a pirate, you sail the seas in search of enemy ships to plunder and great adventures to embark on. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/additional-lore|Additional Lore]] general feat for Sailing Lore or for a specific coastal city you have a connection to (such as Port Peril Lore). You ignore the effects of [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Uneven Ground|uneven ground]] caused by unstable ground (such as the deck of a ship). Additionally, you gain the Boarding Assault action. 

 **Boarding Assault** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/flourish|flourish]]) Either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] twice or attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check (DC determined by the GM, but usually DC 20) to swing up to twice your Speed on a rope or similar object, then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]]. If you boarded or disembarked from a boat or similar vehicle during this movement, the Strike deals one additional weapon damage die.

### Pirate Dedication leads to...

Bitter Taste of Betrayal, Inner Sea Privateer, Into the Storm, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/pirate-combat-training|Pirate Combat Training]], Pirate's Pet, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/rope-runner|Rope Runner]], Sharkskin Grip, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/walk-the-plank|Walk the Plank]]
