# Holistic Care  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/medic|Medic]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/treat-condition|Treat Condition]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]]

You provide emotional and spiritual care. Add [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stunned|stunned]] to the list of conditions you can reduce with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/treat-condition|Treat Condition]]. If the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can’t use Treat Condition to reduce it.
