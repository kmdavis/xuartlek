# Safe House  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/vigilante|Vigilante]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/vigilante-dedication|Vigilante Dedication]]

You establish a safe house—a secure space in which to hide your secrets from the outside world. This safe house is roughly the size of two 10-foot cubes. It's in a location you have access to, and it can be part of a larger building or structure, like a hidden room or an underground cave. The safe house protects objects and people inside it from magical detection. This has the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/veil-of-privacy|_veil of privacy_]], using your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] modifier for the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] DC and half your level rounded up for the counteract rank. Setting up or moving your safe house takes a week of downtime. The size of the safe house expands to four 10-foot cubes if you're an expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]], eight cubes if you're a master, and 16 cubes if you're legendary.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 219*
