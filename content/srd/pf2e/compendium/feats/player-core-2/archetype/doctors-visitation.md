# Doctor's Visitation  *Feat 4*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Archetype`  `Flourish`

You move to provide immediate care to those who need it. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]], then use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/battle-medicine|Battle Medicine]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Treat Poison|Treat Poison]]. You can spend 2 actions for Doctor’s Visitation to instead Stride and then use either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Administer First Aid|Administer First Aid]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/treat-condition|Treat Condition]] (if you have that feat).
