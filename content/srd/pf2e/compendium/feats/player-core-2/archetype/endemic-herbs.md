# Endemic Herbs  *Feat 6*

`Additive`  `Archetype`

- **PFS Note**: For Pathfinder Society play, Endemic Herbs does not have the additive trait.
- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/herbalist|Herbalist]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/herbalist-dedication|Herbalist Dedication]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/survival|Survival]]

The herbal items you create use medicinal plants endemic to your location. When a creature consumes one of your herbal items, that creature gains the benefit matching the location where you created that item. If the benefit is not immediate, it lasts for 1 minute unless otherwise stated. 
**Aquatic** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves.**Arctic** For 1 hour, treat [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/environment#Environmental Damage|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less severe.**Desert** For 1 hour, treat environmental heat effects as if they were one step less severe.**Forest** Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|disease]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]] effects.**Mountain** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves.**Plains** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves.**Swamp** Remove one source of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent]] bleed damage.**Underground** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Perception|Perception]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 202*
