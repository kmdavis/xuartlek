# Fabricated Connections  *Feat 7*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetypes**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/dandy|Dandy]], Venture-Gossip (Level 7)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/dandy-dedication|Dandy Dedication]]; Master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]]

You can lie about having taken an action so convincingly that you acquire all the tangible benefits of having successfully completed it. You can roll [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] in place of a different skill when attempting to Earn Income, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Make an Impression|Make an Impression]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Request|Request]], or Subsist. You can use Fabricated Connections to Make an Impression or Request once per day, and you can also use it once per week to Earn Income or Subsist.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 195*
