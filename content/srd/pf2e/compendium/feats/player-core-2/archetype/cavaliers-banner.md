# Cavalier's Banner  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Aura`  `Emotion`  `Mental`  `Uncommon`  `Visual`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/cavalier|Cavalier]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/cavalier-dedication|Cavalier Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You have pledged your service to a specific organization or ideal.

You fly the banner of your pledge from your mount, raising your allies’ spirits. You and all allies in a 30-foot emanation from your mount gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves and DCs against fear effects. If your banner is destroyed or removed, allies within 30 feet become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] 1.
