# Implausible Purchase  *Feat 16*

`Investigator`

- **Archetype**: Twilight Talon (Level 18)
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/archetype/predictive-purchase|Predictive Purchase]]

It seems impossible, but you've analyzed every angle and are able to just keep pulling out exactly the item you need, even in far-flung locations. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/prescient-planner|Prescient Planner]] even if you have already used it after purchasing goods, and you can use it as a single action instead of a 2-action activity, during which you Interact to draw the item. In addition, five times per day, you can use Prescient Planner to pull out a common [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/consumable|consumable]] item up to 6 levels lower than your level.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 113*
