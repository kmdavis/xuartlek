# Ultimate Mercy  *Feat 18*

`Champion`

- **Archetype**: Blessed One (Level 20)
- **Prerequisites**: Mercy

Your mercy transcends the bounds of life and death. Add ultimate mercy to the types of mercy you can provide. When you use Mercy, you can target a creature that died since your last turn to return it to life. The target returns to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes wounded 1. You can't return the target to life if it died from _disintegrate_ or a death effect. The creature gains the other benefits of _lay on hands_ after it returns to life.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 99*
