# Unstable Concoction  *Feat 10*

`Additive`  `Alchemist`

You can mix a wondrous yet volatile liquid into an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/alchemical|alchemical]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/consumable|consumable]] if it has an initial effect that uses dice. You [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/damage-rolls#Increasing Die Size|increase the size of any dice]] for the item's initial effect by one step. For instance, you could increase damage from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/alchemists-fire-major|moderate alchemist's fire]] to 2d10 or healing from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/elixir-of-life-true|moderate elixir of life]] to 5d8+12. Because this affects only initial dice, it wouldn't increase unarmed attack damage from a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/bestial-mutagen-major|bestial mutagen]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent damage]] from an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/alchemical-items/acid-flask-major|acid flask]], and the like. When this item is activated, the creature activating it must succeed at a DC 10 flat check or take acid damage equal to the item's level.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 67*
