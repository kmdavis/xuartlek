# Radiant Armament  *Feat 10*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: blessed armament

Your blessed armament radiates power, further enhancing your chosen weapon. When you choose the weapon for your blessed armament during your daily preparations, add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Astral|_astral_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Brilliant|_brilliant_]] property runes to the list of effects you can choose from. If you're [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Holy|holy]], also add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Holy|_holy_]] rune, and if you're [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unholy|unholy]], also add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Unholy|_unholy_]] rune.

 In addition, you can change the rune you've selected for the day to a different rune from your list as a 10-minute activity that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Divine|divine]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Exploration|exploration]] traits. Changing the rune doesn't restore abilities that can be used only a limited number of times, such as Holy Healing for the _holy_ rune.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 97*
