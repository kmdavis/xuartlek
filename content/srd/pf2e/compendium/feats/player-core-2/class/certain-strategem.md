# Certain Strategem  *Feat 2*

`Investigator`

You know exactly where to strike a foe to target their weak spots. When you make a Strike that you're adding your Intelligence modifier to due to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Devise a Stratagem|Devising a Stratagem]] and miss, you deal half of your strategic strike damage to the target. This damage type is that of the weapon or unarmed attack you used for the Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 107*
