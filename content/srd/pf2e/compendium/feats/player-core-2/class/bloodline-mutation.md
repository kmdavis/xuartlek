# Bloodline Mutation  *Feat 20*

`Sorcerer`

- **Prerequisites**: a bloodline based on a specific type of creature

You permanently mutate to become more like the creatures of your bloodline. You gain the appropriate trait or traits for those types of creatures ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/aberration|aberration]] for aberrant, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/angel|angel]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/celestial|celestial]] for angelic, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/demon|demon]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiend]] for demonic, and so on). You gain low-light vision or darkvision, if one is appropriate for creatures with those traits. Choose one of the following. 
If the creatures associated with your bloodline have the ability to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|fly]], you gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed. If the creatures associated with your bloodline are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/aquatic|aquatic]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/amphibious|amphibious]], you become amphibious, able to breathe water and air equally well, and you gain a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|swim]] Speed equal to your land Speed.If creatures associated with your bloodline have a resistance or immunity to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/void|void]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]],choose an energy type your bloodline is immune or resistant to and gain resistance 20 against that energy type.
