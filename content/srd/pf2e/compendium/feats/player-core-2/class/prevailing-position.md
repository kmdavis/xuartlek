# Prevailing Position  *Feat 10*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Monk`

- **Trigger**: You are targeted by an attack or attempt a Reflex saving throw against a damaging effect, and you are in a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/stance|stance]].

Your flexibility with stances lets you sacrifice their benefits to better protect yourself. You leave the stance you were in, gaining a +4 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw or to your AC against the triggering attack.
