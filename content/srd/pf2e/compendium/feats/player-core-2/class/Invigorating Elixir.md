# Invigorating Elixir  *Feat 4*

`Additive`  `Alchemist`

You can mix an aromatic salve into an elixir with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Healing|healing]] trait to soothe physical maladies. In addition to its normal effects, the elixir can be imbibed by a creature prevented from doing so (such as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Sickened|sickened]] creature). In addition to its other effects, the elixir attempts to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Afflictions#Counteracting|counteract]] an effect imposing one of the following conditions of the imbiber's choice: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]], sickened, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]]. Use half your level rounded up for the counteract rank and your class DC – 10 for the counteract modifier. The imbiber is then temporarily immune to the effects of this additive for 10 minutes. The additive can't counteract [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Curse|curses]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Disease|diseases]], or conditions that are part of the creature's normal state.
