# Collateral Thrash  *Feat 16*

`Barbarian`  `Rage`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/thrash|Thrash]]

You smack the foe you're thrashing into someone else. When you Thrash, choose another enemy adjacent to the grabbed enemy. That enemy also takes your Thrash damage, with a basic Reflex save against your class DC.
