# Furious Grab  *Feat 12*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Barbarian`  `Rage`

- **Requirements**: Your last action was a successful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]], and either you have a hand free or your Strike used a weapon with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/grapple|grapple trait]].

You grab your foe while it's distracted by your attack. The enemy you hit becomes grabbed by you, as if you had succeeded at an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grapple]] it.
