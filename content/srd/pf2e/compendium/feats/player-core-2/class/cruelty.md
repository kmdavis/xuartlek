# Cruelty  *Feat 4*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/touch-of-the-void|_touch of the void_]]

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/touch-of-the-void|_touch of the void_]] targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, the target is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute if it fails its save (enfeebled 2 if it critically fails).
