# Epiphany at the Crossroads  *Feat 12*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Divine`  `Oracle`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Trigger**: Your turn begins.
- **Requirements**: You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]]. and have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dying|dying condition]].

The crossroads between life and death is a place that can reveal many secrets. You gain the effects of an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/augury|_augury_]] spell in a strange, near-death vision. Empowered by the realization, you then lose the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dying|dying]] condition (becoming [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Wounded|wounded]] 1 or increasing your wounded value by 1, as normal), regain a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level, and can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stand|Stand]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 142*
