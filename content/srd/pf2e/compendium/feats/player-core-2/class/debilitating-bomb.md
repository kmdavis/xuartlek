# Debilitating Bomb  *Feat 6*

`Additive`  `Alchemist`

You can add a crystalline additive to an alchemical bomb to have it undermine your enemy. Choose [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Deafened|deafened]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]], or a –5-foot status penalty to Speeds. If the attack with the bomb hits, in addition to the bomb's normal effects, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or take that effect until the start of your next turn. Use your class DC for this saving throw, even if someone else throws the bomb.
