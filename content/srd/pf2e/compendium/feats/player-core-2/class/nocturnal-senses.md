# Nocturnal Senses  *Feat 6*

`Barbarian`  `Rage`

- **Prerequisites**: low-light vision or scent

Your senses gain even greater clarity. While raging, if you have [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], and if you have scent the range of your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Scent|scent]] increases to 60 feet.
