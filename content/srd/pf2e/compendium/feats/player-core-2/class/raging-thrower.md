# Raging Thrower  *Feat 1*

`Barbarian`

[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/thrown|Thrown]] weapons become especially deadly in your fury. You apply the additional damage from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Rage|Rage]] to your thrown weapon attacks. If you have the Brutal Critical feat or the devastator class feature, apply their benefits to thrown weapon attacks.
