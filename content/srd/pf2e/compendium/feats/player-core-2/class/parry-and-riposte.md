# Parry and Riposte  *Feat 18*

`Swashbuckler`

- **Prerequisites**: Opportune Riposte

Your parries and finishers allow you to riposte with the slightest provocation. You can use Opportune Riposte against an enemy who fails at a Strike against you (not just critically fails), provided you have both damaged that creature with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/finisher|finisher]] on your last turn and currently have a circumstance bonus to AC from the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/parry|parry]] weapon trait, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/extravagant-parry|Extravagant Parry]].

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 169*
