# Detective's Readiness  *Feat 4*

`Investigator`

When you're on a case, you stay watchful and resolute to avoid tricks your suspect might pull. You gain your investigation bonus from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Pursue a Lead|Pursue a Lead]] on saving throws (and their DCs, as normal) against creatures or effects that are related to the question at the heart of an open investigation, and you can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Clue In|Clue In]] to assist an ally's saving throw against that creature or effect.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 108*
