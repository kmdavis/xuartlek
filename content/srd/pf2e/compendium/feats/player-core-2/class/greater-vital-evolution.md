# Greater Vital Evolution  *Feat 16*

`Sorcerer`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/primal-evolution|Primal Evolution]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/divine-evolution|Divine Evolution]]

Vital power surges through you like a font of energy. Twice per day, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|cast a spell]] after you've run out of spell slots of the appropriate spell rank; the two spells you cast with this feat must be of different spell ranks.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 157*
