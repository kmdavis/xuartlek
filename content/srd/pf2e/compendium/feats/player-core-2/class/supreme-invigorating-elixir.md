# Supreme Invigorating Elixir  *Feat 12*

`Alchemist`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/invigorating-elixir|Invigorating Elixir]]

For the purposes of counteracting effects with invigorating elixirs, treat your level as though it were 2 higher. Add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Petrified|petrified]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stunned|stunned]]. You can also choose a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|disease]] regardless of what, if any, conditions it imposes.
