# Greater Spiritual Evolution  *Feat 12*

`Sorcerer`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/divine-evolution|Divine Evolution]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core-2/class/occult-evolution|Occult Evolution]]

Your magical blood allows your spells to be fully effective against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures. Your spells have the effects of a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|ghost touch property rune]]. They can target or affect a creature projecting its consciousness (such as via [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/project-image|_project image_]]) or possessing another creature, even if its body is elsewhere, though you must know about the possession or projection and choose to do so. Your spells can affect creatures on the Ethereal Plane, though this doesn't grant you the ability to locate them.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 155*
