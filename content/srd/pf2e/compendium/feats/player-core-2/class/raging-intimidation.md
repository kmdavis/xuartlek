# Raging Intimidation  *Feat 1*

`Barbarian`

Your fury fills your foes with fear. While you are raging, your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/scare-to-death|Scare to Death]] actions (from the Intimidation skill and an Intimidation skill feat, respectively) gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core-2/rage|rage]] trait, allowing you to use them while raging. As soon as you meet the prerequisites for the skill feats [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/intimidating-glare|Intimidating Glare]] and Scare to Death, you gain these feats.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 77*
