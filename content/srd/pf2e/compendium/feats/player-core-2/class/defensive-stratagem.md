# Defensive Stratagem  *Feat 8*

`Investigator`

By scrutinizing a foe, you learn how to better avoid its attacks. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Devise a Stratagem|Devise a Stratagem]], you can choose a defensive stratagem instead of an attack or skill stratagem after you roll a d20.

 **Defensive Stratagem** You can't attempt to Strike the target until the start of your next turn. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all your defenses against effects caused by the target until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 109*
