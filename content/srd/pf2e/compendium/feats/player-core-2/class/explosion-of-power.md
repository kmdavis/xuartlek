# Explosion of Power  *Feat 8*

`Sorcerer`

Your magic explodes. You know the following blood magic effect. 
**Blood Magic—Explosion of Power** Raw power explodes outward from you. Each creature within a 5-foot emanation takes 1d6 damage per rank of the spell you just cast (basic Reflex save). The type of damage depends on the tradition of your bloodline. If you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/arcane|arcane]] spells, you deal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]] damage. If you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]] spells, you deal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage. If you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]] spells, you deal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] damage. If you cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/primal|primal]] spells, you deal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]] damage.
