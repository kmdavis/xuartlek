# Strategic Bypass  *Feat 14*

`Investigator`

Your plans account for your foes' resistances, enabling you to strike a telling blow. When you hit with a Strike on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Devise a Stratagem|Devising a Stratagem]], you ignore an amount of resistance equal to your Intelligence modifier for each resistance that applies against your attack.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 112*
