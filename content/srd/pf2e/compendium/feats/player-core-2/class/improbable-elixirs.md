# Improbable Elixirs  *Feat 18*

`Alchemist`

Your mastery of alchemical secrets enables you to replicate effects most believe can be achieved only via magic. Select a number of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/potion|potions]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1); these potions must be of 9th level or lower. You gain formulas to create these potions as alchemical consumables with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/elixir|elixir]] trait. When making these alchemical elixirs, you can substitute alchemical reagents for an equal value of magical components, and you can use an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/alchemists-toolkit|alchemist's toolkit]] (for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Quick Alchemy|Quick Alchemy]]) or an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/alchemists-lab-expanded|alchemist's lab]] (for the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] activity) instead of any other required toolkits. Other than that, the formula doesn't change. Once you've chosen the potion formulas, they can't be changed.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 69*
