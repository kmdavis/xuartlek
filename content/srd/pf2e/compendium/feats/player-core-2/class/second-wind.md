# Second Wind  *Feat 2*

`Barbarian`

You can enter a second rage, but afterward you need to catch your breath. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core-2#Rage|Rage]], you can choose to gain temporary HP from the Rage even if 1 minute hasn't passed (or a full turn if you have the revitalizing rage class feature). If you do, once this Rage ends you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Fatigued|fatigued]] until you rest for 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 77*
