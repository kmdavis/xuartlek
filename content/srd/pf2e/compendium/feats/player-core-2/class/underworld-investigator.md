# Underworld Investigator  *Feat 1*

`Investigator`

Your work as an investigator makes you familiar with the criminal element. You might be a criminal yourself, or you might have gained an understanding of how they operate from the outside. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Underworld Lore]], and you gain your Pursue a Lead investigation bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/thievery|Thievery]] checks to investigate a case (such as checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Steal|Steal]] a clue from a suspect or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Pick a Lock|Pick a Lock]] to open a safe with damning documents).

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 107*
