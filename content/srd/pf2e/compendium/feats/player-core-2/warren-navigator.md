# Warren Navigator  *Feat 1*

`Ratfolk`

You gain the trained proficiency rank in Survival. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice. When you Sense Direction or attempt a roll against a _quandary_ spell, you get a result one degree of success better than you rolled. You don't take a penalty to Sense Direction when you lack a compass.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 30*
