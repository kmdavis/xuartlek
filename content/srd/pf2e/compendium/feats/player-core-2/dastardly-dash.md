# Dastardly Dash  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Swashbuckler`

You dash past an opponent, confounding them with underhanded tactics. Stride up to half your speed. You can attempt a Trip or a Dirty Trick (if you have that action) against one opponent you pass within range of at any point during your movement.
