# Grovel  *Feat 5*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Auditory`  `Concentrate`  `Emotion`  `Kobold`  `Mental`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in Deception

You convince your foe you're less of a threat. You attempt to Feint against a creature. Unlike a normal Feint, the creature can be within 30 feet instead of in your melee reach, and you make your check against its Will DC instead of its Perception DC.
