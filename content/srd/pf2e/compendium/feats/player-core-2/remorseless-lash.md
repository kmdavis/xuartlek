# Remorseless Lash  *Feat 1*

`Hobgoblin`

You're skilled at beating an enemy when their morale is already breaking. When you succeed at a melee weapon Strike against a frightened enemy, that enemy can't reduce their frightened condition below 1 until the beginning of your next turn.
