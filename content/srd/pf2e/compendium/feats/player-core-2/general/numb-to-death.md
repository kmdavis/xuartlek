# Numb to Death  *Feat 7*

`General`

- **PFS Note**: For the purposes of Society play, remove the Prerequisites of Numb to Death that states “you have died at least once.''
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/diehard|Diehard]]; you have died at least once

Your past has left you numb to death's call. The first time each day that you regain Hit Points while you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dying|dying]], you gain a circumstance bonus to the number of Hit Points you regain equal to your level, and you neither gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Wounded|wounded]] condition nor increase the value of this condition.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 232*
