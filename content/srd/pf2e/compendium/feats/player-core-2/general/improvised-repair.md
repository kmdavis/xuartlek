# Improvised Repair  *Feat 3*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`General`

You can hastily patch damaged equipment together, but the temporary fix lacks the full care required for lasting repairs. You patch together a broken item possessed by you or an adjacent willing creature. Until the item takes damage again, it still functions as a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/shoddy-items|shoddy]] item of its type. If it's a magic item or another item with activations, it can't be activated while patched together, but can be used for normal functions (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Striking]] with a weapon or using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/shield-block|Shield Block]] with a shield). This activity restores no Hit Points, so the item is easy to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/item-damage|destroy]] . Once the item is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repaired]] normally such that it is no longer [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Broken|broken]], it is also no longer shoddy.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 231*
