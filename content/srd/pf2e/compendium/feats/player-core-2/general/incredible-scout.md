# Incredible Scout  *Feat 11*

`Exploration`  `General`

- **Prerequisites**: Master in Perception

When you scout, you are particularly alert for danger, granting your allies precious moments to prepare to fight. When using the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Scout|Scout]] exploration activity, you grant your allies a +2 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls instead of a +1 circumstance bonus.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 231*
