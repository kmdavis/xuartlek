# Rat Magic  *Feat 5*

`Ratfolk`

There always seems to be a little rat around to carry messages for you. You can cast _animal messenger_ once per day as a primal innate spell. When you do, the animal that responds is always a rat. If there are no rats within range, the spell is lost.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 30*
