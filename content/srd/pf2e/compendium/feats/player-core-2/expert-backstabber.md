# Expert Backstabber  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Assassin
- **Prerequisites**: Assassin Dedication

You excel at quickly dispatching your foes. When you Strike an off-guard foe with a weapon that has the backstabber weapon trait, you deal 2 extra precision damage instead of 1. If the weapon is a _+3 weapon_, you deal 4 extra precision damage instead of 2.
