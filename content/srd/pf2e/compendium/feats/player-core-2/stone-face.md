# Stone Face  *Feat 1*

`Hobgoblin`

You've mastered the art of composure, even in the face of fear. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against effects with the fear trait and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Will DC against Intimidation skill actions, such as Demoralize.
