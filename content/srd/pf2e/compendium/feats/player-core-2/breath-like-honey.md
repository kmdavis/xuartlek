# Breath Like Honey  *Feat 9*

`Kholo`  `Gnoll`

- **Prerequisites**: Sweetbreath Kholo heritage

You smell of honey and savory things. Your circumstance bonus to checks to Make an Impression if the target can smell your breath increases to +2. In addition, you can cast _enthrall_ as a 3rd-rank occult innate spell once per day, except the spell has a range of 30 feet and the olfactory trait instead of the auditory trait. Targets don't gain any circumstance bonus for disagreeing with you.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 19*
