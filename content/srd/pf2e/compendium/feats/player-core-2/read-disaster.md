# Read Disaster  *Feat 8*

`Exploration`  `Oracle`  `Prediction`

- **Archetypes**: Time Mage (Level 10), Time Mage (Level 10)

You spend 10 minutes and open yourself to the divine mysteries of the world, peering into the most sinister portents of the future. You gain the effects of _augury_, except you learn only about the dangers ahead; any results of “good” are instead “nothing,” and results of “mixed” are instead “bad.” If you are legendary in Religion, you take only 1 minute to Read Disaster.
