# Impressive Landing  *Feat 10*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Barbarian`

- **Trigger**: You fall at least 10 feet and land on a solid surface.

You slam into the ground, shattering it around you. Treat the fall as 10 feet shorter. You land on your feet. The ground in a 5-foot emanation becomes difficult terrain. Any creature in the emanation when you land takes 5 bludgeoning damage as the debris hits it and is off-guard until the start of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 82*
