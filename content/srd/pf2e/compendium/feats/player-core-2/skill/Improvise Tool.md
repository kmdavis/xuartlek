# Improvise Tool  *Feat 1*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]]

You can jury-rig solutions when you don't have the proper tools on hand. You can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] damaged items without a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Repair Toolkit|repair toolkit]]. 
If you have the raw materials, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Caltrops|basic caltrop set,]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Candle|candle]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Compass|compass]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Crowbar|crowbar]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Fishing Tackle|fishing tackle]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]],[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Hammer|hammer]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Ladder (10 ft.)|ladder]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Piton|piton]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Rope|rope]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Ten-Foot Pole|10-foot pole]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Thieves' Toolkit|replacement thieves' picks]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Tool|long]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Tool|short tool]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Torch|torch]] as if you had their formulas.
