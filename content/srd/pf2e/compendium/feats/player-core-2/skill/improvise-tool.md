# Improvise Tool  *Feat 1*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

You can jury-rig solutions when you don't have the proper tools on hand. You can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Repair|Repair]] damaged items without a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/repair-toolkit-superb|repair toolkit]]. 
If you have the raw materials, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Craft|Craft]] a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/caltrops|basic caltrop set,]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/candle|candle]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/compass-lensatic|compass]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/crowbar-levered|crowbar]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/fishing-tackle-professional|fishing tackle]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/flint-and-steel|flint and steel]],[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/hammer|hammer]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/ladder-10-ft|ladder]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/piton|piton]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/rope|rope]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/ten-foot-pole|10-foot pole]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/thieves-toolkit-infiltrator-picks|replacement thieves' picks]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/tool-short|long]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/tool-short|short tool]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/torch|torch]] as if you had their formulas.
