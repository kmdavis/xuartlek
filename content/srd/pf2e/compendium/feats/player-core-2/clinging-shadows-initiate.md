# Clinging Shadows Initiate  *Feat 8*

`Monk`

- **Prerequisites**: Qi Spells

You learn a mystical stance that transforms your qi into sticky smoke that shrouds your limbs. You gain the _clinging shadows stance_ qi. While entering the stance is a qi spell, the shadow grasp attacks it grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.
