# Trickster Tengu  *Feat 17*

`Tengu`

You can transform yourself or the bodies of others to express your displeasure. Once per day, you can cast either _aerial form_ or _cursed metamorphosis_ as a 7th-rank primal innate spell. You must take the form of a bird when casting this _aerial form_.
