# Wings of Bone and Sinew  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Necromancer`  `Occult`

- **Frequency**: once per hour

You can harness the power of death to craft wings from bones and a thin membrane of flesh. You destroy a thrall within 30 feet, fusing it with your body and shaping it into gruesome, bony wings. You gain a fly Speed of 30 feet for 1 minute. You can Dismiss these wings.

At 16th level, this action loses its frequency and gains an unli mited duration.
