# A Hush Falls Over the World  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Illusion`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/world-rouser|World Rouser]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/world-rouser-dedication|World Rouser Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

Sound is absorbed in your waking world, covering everything in a shroud of quiet until the start of your next turn. Creatures in your waking world become hidden to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Imprecise Senses|imprecise hearing]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Precise Senses|precise hearing]]. A creature in your waking world attempting to use spell or effect with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]] trait must succeed at a DC 4 flat check; on a failure, the spell or effect is [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/casting-spells#Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupted]]. Anytime a creature in the area is affected by an auditory or sonic effect, they must attempt a DC 16 flat check. On a success, they are not affected by the auditory or sonic effect, though they still may be affected by other aspects of an ability (for example, a creature hit by a weapon with a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/thundering-greater|_thundering_]] rune would not take sonic damage but would still take the normal damage from the weapon).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 107*
