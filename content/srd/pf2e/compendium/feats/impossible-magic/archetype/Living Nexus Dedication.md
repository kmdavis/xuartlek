# Living Nexus Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Living Nexus|Living Nexus]]
- **Prerequisites**: trained in martial weapons; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Nature|Nature]], Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]], or Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Religion|Religion]]

Whether through happenstance or hours of rigorous training, you have discovered within yourself the ability to breathe in the magic around you.

You have an invisible, internal nexus of energy and have learned how to control your absorption of others’ magical energy and store it in this internal nexus. You gain the Absorb Magic reaction and the Overflowing Blade action. The DC for an ability gained via this archetype is either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, and is called your nexus DC. 

**Absorb Magic** [](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Force|force]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]]) **Requirements** You are overflowing; **Effect** You conduct the magic in your internal nexus to coat your weapon or unarmed strike, empowering it with magical energy for a devastating attack. Make a melee Strike. If the Strike hits, you deal an additional 1d4 force damage. At 10th level, the additional damage increases to 2d4 force damage. At 18th level, the additional damage increases to 3d4 force damage. You are no longer overflowing.
