# Interstitial Escape  *Feat 10*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`  `Teleportation`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/veil-dancer|Veil Dancer]]
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/veil-dancer-dedication|Veil Dancer Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: You would be hit by a melee or ranged Strike, and you are not [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]].

To evade your foe, you shift yourself to another location, teleporting from your current square to another unoccupied square within 30 feet. Additionally, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to all damage against the triggering attack equal to 5 + your level.
