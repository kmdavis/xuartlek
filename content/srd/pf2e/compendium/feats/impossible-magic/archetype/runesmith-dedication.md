# Runesmith Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Multiclass`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/runesmith|Runesmith]]
- **Prerequisites**: Intelligence +2

You become trained in runesmith [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Class DC|class DC]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]. You gain a runic repertoire with two 1st-level runes of your choice. You choose these from the common runes on the rune list as well as uncommon or rarer runes you have access to. 

You can etch runes. An etched rune is carved, inked, or branded; this application does not damage the creature or item. Etching a rune requires an artisan's toolkit. Your magic can sustain up to one etched rune at a time. If you're at least 9th level, increase the maximum number of etched runes you can sustain at a time to 2, and if you're at least 17th level, increase it to 3. You can etch any number of runes up to your maximum during your daily preparations or by spending 10 minutes in exploration mode. Your etched runes remain indefinitely until they're expended or removed or until you etch more runes than your maximum, which causes your oldest rune to fade. Additionally, you gain the Solitary Invocation action. 

**Solitary Invocation** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/impossible-magic/invocation|invocation]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/impossible-magic/runesmith|runesmith]]) You utter the name of one of your runes within 30 feet, which blazes with power, applying the effect in its Invocation entry. An invoked rune then fades away, its task complete.
