# Analyze Magic  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/spellshifter|Spellshifter]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/spellshifter-dedication|Spellshifter Dedication]]

Your intuitive understanding of magic allows you to read the energy of a spell in the air. You gain the Analyze Magic spellshift.

**Spellshift—Analyze Magic** Once you’ve homed in on a spell or magical effect, you can study its unique magical signature to immediately understand exactly what it does.

**Critical Success** You learn information about the spell or effect as if you had critically succeeded at a skill check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Identify Magic|Identify Magic]] using the appropriate skill for its magical tradition.

**Success** You learn information about the spell or effect as if you had succeeded at a skill check to Identify Magic using the appropriate skill for its magical tradition.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 102*
