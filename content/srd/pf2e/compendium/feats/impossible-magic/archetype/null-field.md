# Null Field  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/living-nexus|Living Nexus]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/living-nexus-dedication|Living Nexus Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: You are overflowing and have taken energy damage within the last minute.

You understand the frequency of energies that have damaged you and can extend the magical network of power in your internal nexus to diffuse incoming attacks. Choose one type of energy damage you have taken within the last minute. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to that energy type equal to half your level for 1 minute.
