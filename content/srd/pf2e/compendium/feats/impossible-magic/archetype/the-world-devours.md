# The World Devours  *Feat 12*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Earth`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/world-rouser|World Rouser]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/world-rouser-dedication|World Rouser Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

The ground in your waking world opens to consume a creature standing on it, which must attempt a Reflex save against your world rouser DC. The creature must be Medium or smaller; if your waking world is a 20-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]], the creature can be Large or smaller, or Huge or smaller if your waking world is a 30-foot burst or larger. If you use this ability while your waking world already has a creature [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Grabbed|grabbed]] or devoured, that creature is automatically freed. If your waking world ends, any creature affected by this ability is automatically freed.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage and is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Grabbed|grabbed]] by the earth. The creature can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]] against your world rouser DC.

**Critical Failure** The creature is pulled into the earth, which closes above it. It takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage immediately and is devoured. While devoured, it becomes grabbed and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1, and takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage at the end of each of its turns, and cannot be targeted by any effect. A creature can attempt to Escape against your world rouser DC; in addition to the normal checks to Escape, it can attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Nature|Nature]] check. If it Escapes, it is returned to the area where it was when grabbed. A creature with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Burrow Speed|burrow Speed]] can Escape automatically and move up to its burrow Speed as part of that action.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 107*
