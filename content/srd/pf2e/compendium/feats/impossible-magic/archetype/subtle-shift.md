# Subtle Shift  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/spellshifter|Spellshifter]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/spellshifter-dedication|Spellshifter Dedication]]

You’ve learned how to hide your meddling from the casters of spells you’re shifting. If your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]] check when Shifting a Spell is greater than its caster’s [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Perception|Perception]] DC, you can prevent the caster from learning the effects of the shift you’re attempting to apply. On a critical success, they don’t become aware that you’re applying a shift at all (although the effect of the shift might still give you away).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 103*
