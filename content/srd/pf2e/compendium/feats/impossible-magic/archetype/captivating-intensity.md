# Captivating Intensity  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/captivator|Captivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/basic-captivator-spellcasting|Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]

Your ability to captivate others has grown, and you can use your captivator abilities more often. You seem to never run out of the ability to fascinate or distract. You can cast each [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate occult spell]] granted by captivator archetype feats one additional time per day, except for your two highest-ranked captivator spells.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 93*
