# Inspired Memory  *Feat 3*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/living-nexus|Living Nexus]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/living-nexus-dedication|Living Nexus Dedication]]

A memory of each spell you have absorbed remains within your soul. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] about creatures whose magic you have absorbed within the last minute. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Learn a Spell|Learn a Spell]] that you have absorbed within the last week.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 96*
