# Basic Hedge Mage Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/hedge-mage|Hedge Mage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/hedge-mage-dedication|Hedge Mage Dedication]]

Your independent introspections grant you rudiments of magical power. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/archetypes#Spellcasting Archetypes|basic spellcasting benefits]]. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the hedge mage archetype, add three common 1st‑rank spells to your keepsake. Your keepsake can also hold 1st-rank spells.
