# Countercharm  *Feat 8*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/captivator|Captivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/basic-captivator-spellcasting|Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]
- **Trigger**: A creature Casts a Spell with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] trait.
- **Requirements**: You must have a non-cantrip innate captivator spell with the same trait.

Your charms and illusions are so dominant that you can use them to counteract similar magic cast by others. When a foe [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|casts a spell]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] trait and you can see its manifestations, you can use your own innate magic to disrupt it. You lose an innate, non-cantrip spell with the same trait as the triggering spell as if you had cast the spell. You then attempt to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] the triggering spell.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 93*
