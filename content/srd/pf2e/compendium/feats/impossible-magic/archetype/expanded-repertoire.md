# Expanded Repertoire  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/runesmith|Runesmith]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/runesmith-dedication|Runesmith Dedication]]

Your knowledge of runes grows. Add two runes of 5th level or lower to your runic repertoire. You choose these from the common runes on the rune list as well as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/uncommon|uncommon]] or rarer runes you have access to.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 90*
