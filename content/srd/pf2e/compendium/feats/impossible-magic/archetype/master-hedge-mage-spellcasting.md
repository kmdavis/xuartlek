# Master Hedge Mage Spellcasting  *Feat 18*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/hedge-mage|Hedge Mage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/expert-hedge-mage-spellcasting|Expert Hedge Mage Spellcasting]]

You gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/archetypes#Spellcasting Archetypes|master spellcasting benefits]]. When you add spells to your keepsake from gaining a spell slot of a new rank from the hedge mage archetype, you can choose 3rd-rank spells. Your keepsake can also hold 3rd-rank spells.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 95*
