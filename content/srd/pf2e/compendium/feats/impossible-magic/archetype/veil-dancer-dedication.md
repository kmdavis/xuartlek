# Veil Dancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/veil-dancer|Veil Dancer]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]]

You’re able to shift yourself into the spaces between to avoid notice. You gain the Part the Veil action.

**Part the Veil** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]]) **Requirements** You are not observed by any enemies; **Effect** You phase yourself into the interstices, veiling yourself from physical reality. You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]]. You can use this concealment to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]] as your disassociation from reality makes you difficult to focus on. If you use a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/hostile-actions|hostile action]], this effect ends after the action is complete; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute.
