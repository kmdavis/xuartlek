# Spellshifter Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/spellshifter|Spellshifter]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]]

You can exert your will over magical spells cast in your vicinity, potentially altering or controlling their effects. You accomplish this through the use of a spellshifting conduit, a magical object of your choice that has light Bulk and can be held in one hand. If your conduit is lost or destroyed, you can train yourself to use a new one by spending 10 minutes attuning yourself to it. You gain the Shift Spell action and the Share the Burden spellshift.

**Shift Spell** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]]) **Requirements** You are within the affected area of an ongoing spell, or you can physically touch an object or creature under the effects of a spell, and you are holding your conduit; **Effect** Choose a spellshift and attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]] check against the DC of the spell (or the spell DC of the spellcaster if the spell does not normally have a DC). The caster immediately becomes aware that you’re trying to shift their spell and intuitively knows the potential effects of the shift, but not the result of your skill check. A willing caster can allow you to achieve one degree of success higher than the result of your check. The effects of the check are dependent on the chosen spellshift. Regardless of the result, the spell becomes immune to Shift Spell for 24 hours. 

**Spellshift—Shift Burden** You share the burden of a spell, allowing you to share control with the caster. If [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustaining]] a spell with this spellshift causes a creature to attempt a saving throw, it is attempted against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If the spell requires a spell attack roll, make the attack using your Arcana modifier instead of the spell’s normal spell attack modifier.

**Critical Success** If the spell is sustainable, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the spell as a free action as though you had cast the spell. For the remainder of the spell’s duration, you can Sustain the spell as though you were the caster.

**Success** If the spell is sustainable, you can Sustain the spell as a free action as though you had cast the spell.
