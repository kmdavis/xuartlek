# All Returns to Slumber  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Mental`  `Primal`  `Sleep`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/world-rouser|World Rouser]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/world-rouser-dedication|World Rouser Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

Your waking world returns to slumber, and lays the peace of deep sleep on all within it. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] your waking world, and all creatures in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|Animals]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/beast|beasts]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/plant|plants]] native to this world treat the result of their save as one degree worse. Creatures in the area then become temporarily immune to this ability for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature can’t use reactions for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 106*
