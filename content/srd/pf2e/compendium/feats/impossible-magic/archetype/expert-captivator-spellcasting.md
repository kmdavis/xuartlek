# Expert Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/captivator|Captivator]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/basic-captivator-spellcasting|Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]

You learn more powerful spells to enchant and deceive. You learn a 4th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]] spell. At 12th level, you learn a 5th-rank spell, and at 14th level, you learn a 6th-rank spell. These spells must each have either the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/illusion|illusion]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] trait. You cast these spells as [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate occult spells]]. You become an expert in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics.
