# Steal Magic's Breath  *Feat 12*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Living Nexus|Living Nexus]]
- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/Living Nexus Dedication|Living Nexus Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: You are the target or within the area of a damaging spell or ability with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Arcane|arcane]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Primal|primal]] trait.
- **Requirements**: You are not overflowing.

Your internal nexus has grown to an immense size and can absorb workings of magic even as they form, sometimes even swallowing them entirely. You become overflowing as if you had Absorbed Magic. Additionally, you reduce the amount of damage the triggering spell or ability deals by 6d6 for all creatures targeted by the spell or ability. This reduction is applied before any [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weaknesses]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/Building Creatures#Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances|resistances]] are taken into account. If you completely mitigate the damage to a creature, the triggering spell or ability has no effect on them.

The amount of damage reduced increases by 1d6 at 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 97*
