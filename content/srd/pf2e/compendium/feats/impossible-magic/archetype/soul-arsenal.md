# Soul Arsenal  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/soulforger|Soulforger]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/archetype/soulforger-dedication|Soulforger Dedication]]

Choose an additional soulforged armament of a different type than you already chose, and choose one essence power for it. Upgrade or change your armaments individually.

When you Manifest Soulforged Armament, you can summon any number of your armaments (you must meet the requirements for each), and when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] the effect, you can choose to Dismiss some and not others. You can choose to manifest the essence form of any number of your armaments when you take the action. Each armament can manifest its essence form only once per day.

When you gain soulforged corruption, you choose one armament’s corruption flaw to affect you for stage 1. At stage 2, you take the flaws of all your soulforged armaments. While you have the curse, each armament gets a separate flat check if you try to manifest its essence form, and failing the check prevents you from manifesting only that armament’s essence form.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time at 12th level, choosing the final item type.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 98*
