# Tender Ministrations  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: Hedge Mage
- **Prerequisites**: Hedge Mage Dedication

Your gentle magic eases even the most skittish of souls, a gift that almost guarantees you popularity among parents and farmers alike. If your next action is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that targets a single creature, you can reduce the target’s frightened condition by 1. If the target is an animal, you can also attempt to counteract an effect imposing the confused or fleeing condition.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 94*
