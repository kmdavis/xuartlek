# The Hallowed Dead  *Feat 1*

`Holy`  `Necromancer`

- **Prerequisites**: You aren’t unholy.

While many necromancers seek unholy and perverse power, you have dedicated yourself to the practice of necromancy to stop the creation of permanent undead and the corruption of souls. You and your thralls gain the holy trait. Strikes your thralls make gain the holy trait and deal 1 additional spirit damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “purposefully create undead to do undue harm.”

### The Hallowed Dead leads to...

Hallowed Earth
