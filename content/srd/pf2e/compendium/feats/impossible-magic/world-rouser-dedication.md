# World Rouser Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: World Rouser
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in Nature

You know how to wake the world around you from its slumber, whether through your natural affinity for nature or hard-earned knowledge. You gain the Rouse the World action. 

As your ability to converse with the world grows, you can call on it to help you in other ways, some of which require a saving throw. The DC for an ability gained via this archetype is either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, and is called your world rouser DC.

**Rouse the World** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, primal) The flora, fauna, and elements in a 10- foot burst within 30 feet stir, aiding you and your allies and whispering in your ear. This area is known as your waking world. While in your waking world, you and your allies gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics and Acrobatics checks, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Nature checks to Recall Knowledge about creatures and hazards in the area. The effect lasts for 10 minutes, but you can Dismiss it. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can Sustain this ability to increase the burst’s size by 10 feet. You can Rouse the World in only one area at a time; if you use this ability again, the first area returns to normal.
