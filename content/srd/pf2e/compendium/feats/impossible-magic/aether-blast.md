# Aether Blast  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Force`  `Magical`  `Manipulate`

- **Archetype**: Living Nexus
- **Prerequisites**: Living Nexus Dedication
- **Requirements**: You are overflowing.

You channel some of the raw magical energy in your internal nexus into a devastating blast. You direct the blast at one creature within 30 feet, dealing 1d12 force damage with a basic Reflex save against your nexus DC. The force damage increases by 1d12 at 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 96*
