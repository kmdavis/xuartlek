# Engraving Maneuver  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Runesmith`

- **Requirements**: You are wielding a melee weapon with the disarm, shove, or trip trait.

After quickly drawing a rune on your weapon, you can transfer the rune to a foe as you knock them down, push them away, or disarm their weapon. Attempt to Disarm, Shove, or Trip a target; your weapon must have the corresponding trait. If your skill check is successful, you Trace a Rune onto the target of the Strike even if the target is pushed out of range.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 49*
