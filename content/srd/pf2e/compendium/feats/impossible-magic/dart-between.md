# Dart Between  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`  `Teleportation`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication

In the blink of an eye, you transport yourself and any items you’re wearing and holding from your current space to an unoccupied space within 60 feet that you can see. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an extradimensional container—you cannot Dart Between. When you arrive at your destination, you can Part the Veil as a free action, even if you wouldn’t normally meet its requirements. If you are hidden, this action does not cause you to become observed provided you still have cover, greater cover, or concealment from the creatures you were hidden from after the teleportation.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 104*
