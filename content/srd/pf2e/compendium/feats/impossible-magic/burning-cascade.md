# Burning Cascade  *Feat 4*

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: Arcane Cascade

Energy flares out around you when you drop into your signature stance. When you use Arcane Cascade, each enemy in a 5-foot emanation takes damage equal to the extra damage from Arcane Cascade. This damage is the same type as that of Arcane Cascade. A creature that takes this damage is temporarily immune to further uses of Burning Cascade for 1 minute.
