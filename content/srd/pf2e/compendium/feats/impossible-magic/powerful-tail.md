# Powerful Tail  *Feat 1*

`Automaton`

You resemble an animal or a type of humanoid who has a tail. You gain a tail unarmed attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This tail is in the brawling group.

**Enhancement** Your tail is not only powerful but also flexible. You gain a climb Speed of 10 feet, and you reduce the number of free hands required to Climb or Trip by one.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 110*
