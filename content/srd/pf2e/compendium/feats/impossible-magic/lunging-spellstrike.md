# Lunging Spellstrike  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Arcane`  `Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: Spellstrike; twisting tree hybrid study
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a staff.

Your spell unwinds the structure of your staff to make it exceptionally long, or even separate it into shards held together by magical power. Make a Spellstrike with a staff, with a spell that isn’t a cantrip or focus spell. Increase the staff’s reach by 5 feet × the spell’s rank.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 23*
