# Invisible Ink  *Feat 2*

`Runesmith`

After your rune is drawn, it flares once and then fades, leaving only the barest mark. When you apply a rune on an object or creature, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Conceal an Object|Conceal an Object]] as a free action to hide that rune. If a creature would get a chance to find the rune, the GM uses your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] DC instead of your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] DC.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
