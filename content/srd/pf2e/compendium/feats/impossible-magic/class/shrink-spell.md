# Shrink Spell  *Feat 6*

`Wizard`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/counterspell|Counterspell]]

You’ve learned to manipulate space to narrow the impact of other spells. When you use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/counterspell|Counterspell]] to attempt to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] a spell that has an area of a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]], or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Line|line]] and does not have a duration, you reduce the area of the spell if you fail to counteract it. Remove 5 feet from the radius of a burst or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] or 10 feet from the length of a cone or line.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 109*
