# Student of the Staff  *Feat 4*

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Hybrid Studies#Twisting Tree|twisting tree hybrid study]]

You’ve studied the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Staff|staff]] extensively to learn the hidden capabilities of your chosen weapon. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using a staff, you can apply the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/Weapons#Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/club/Club|club]] group. (If you use the critical specialization on a Spellstrike, the spell still affects the target even if you pushed the target out of reach.) 

While you’re in Arcane Cascade stance, your staff gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Deadly|deadly d6]] trait, with the damage from the deadly die being the same damage type as the extra damage from Arcane Cascade.

In addition, you can place [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/Armor & Armaments#Property Runes|property runes]] into a magic staff you prepare, even though a magic staff can’t typically hold property runes. You must first inscribe the property runes into your spellbook, using the same process as [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/Armor & Armaments#Transferring Runes|transferring a rune]]. The rune takes the form of a specialized diagram in your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Spellbook (Blank)|spellbook]], occupying one page. You can’t inscribe runes that can’t be placed on a non-magical staff, nor can you inscribe the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Shifting|_shifting_]] rune. When you prepare a staff, you can impart any number of property runes from your spellbook into the staff, up to the limit imposed by the staff’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/Weapon Potency|_weapon potency_]] rune.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 20*
