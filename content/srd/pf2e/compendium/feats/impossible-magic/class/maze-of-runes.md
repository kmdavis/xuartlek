# Maze of Runes  *Feat 16*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]]; Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/nature|Nature]]; Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/occultism|Occultism]]; Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/religion|Religion]]

You can trap a foe in a twisting, extradimensional labyrinth of ever-shifting runes. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/quandary|_quandary_]] once per day as an [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/innate-spells|innate spell]]. The rank of this spell is equal to half your level rounded up, and the tradition can be any tradition for which you are at least legendary in the related skill. Instead of the usual skill checks to solve the puzzle, the target of your _quandary_ can attempt a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Perception|Perception]] check, a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/society|Society]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Decipher Writing|Decipher Writing]], or a check in the skill related to the tradition of the spell. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] this spell, you can also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Invoke Rune|Invoke one Rune]] on the target creature as if it were adjacent to you.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 53*
