# Dual Energy Heart  *Feat 4*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/energy-heart|Energy Heart]]

A second energy dwells in your eidolon. Choose an additional damage type from the list in Energy Heart. Your eidolon’s unarmed attack that deals energy damage gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/versatile|versatile]] trait for the second energy damage type, and your eidolon gains [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to the second type of energy damage equal to half your level.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 80*
