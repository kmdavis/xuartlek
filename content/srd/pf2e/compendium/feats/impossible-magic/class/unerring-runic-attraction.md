# Unerring Runic Attraction  *Feat 14*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Magical`  `Runesmith`

Your runes act as beacons for your arrows and bolts. Make a ranged Strike against a foe you’re aware of that’s currently bearing a rune and within your first [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/weapons#Range|range increment]]. The ammunition homes in on your target, even going around corners. Your Strike doesn’t require [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/effects#Line of Sight|line of sight]], ignores the target’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] condition, and ignores all circumstance bonuses to AC granted from [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Cover|cover]] and [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/armor-armaments#Shields|shields]].
