# Energy Heart  *Feat 1*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon’s heart beats with energy. Choose [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]]. One of their unarmed attacks changes its damage type to the chosen type, and they gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to that type equal to half your level (minimum 1).
