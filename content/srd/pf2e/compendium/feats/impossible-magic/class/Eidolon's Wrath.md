# Eidolon's Wrath  *Feat 6*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon gains the _eidolon’s wrath_ link spell, which they cast, instead of you. You determine the damage type when you gain the feat: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Spirit|spirit]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Vitality|vitality]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]]. As normal, your eidolon shares your Focus Points.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 81*
