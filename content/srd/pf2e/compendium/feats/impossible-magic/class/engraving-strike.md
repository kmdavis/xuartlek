# Engraving Strike  *Feat 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Runesmith`

- **Requirements**: You are wielding a melee weapon.

You draw a rune onto the surface of your weapon in reverse, the mark branding or bruising itself into your target at the moment of impact. Make a melee Strike with the weapon. On a success, you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] onto the target of the Strike
