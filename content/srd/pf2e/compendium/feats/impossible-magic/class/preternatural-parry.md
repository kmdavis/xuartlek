# Preternatural Parry  *Feat 14*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/spell-parry|Spell Parry]]
- **Trigger**: You’re targeted by a Strike or a spell attack, or you’re about to roll a saving throw against a spell effect.
- **Requirements**: You have one or more hands free.

You snap your weapon or limb into place to ward off harm. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against the triggering effect until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 25*
