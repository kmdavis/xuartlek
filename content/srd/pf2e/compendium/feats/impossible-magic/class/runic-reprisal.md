# Runic Reprisal  *Feat 6*

`Invocation`  `Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/fortifying-knock|Fortifying Knock]]

When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Raise a Shield|Raise your Shield]], you can bury a runic trap into it, which is set off by the clash of an enemy weapon. When you use Fortifying Knock, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] with a damaging invocation on your shield, even if it normally couldn’t be applied to a shield. The traced rune doesn’t have its normal effect, instead fading into your shield. If you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/shield-block|Shield Block]] with the shield against a melee Strike, you can Invoke the Rune as part of the reaction, causing the rune to detonate outward and apply its invocation effect to the attacking creature.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 49*
