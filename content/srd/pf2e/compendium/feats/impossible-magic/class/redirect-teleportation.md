# Redirect Teleportation  *Feat 12*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Teleportation`  `Wizard`

- **Trigger**: A creature teleports into a space within 60 feet of you.

You’re able to intercept a teleportation effect and change where a creature appears. You expend a prepared spell that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/teleportation|teleportation]] trait. You lose your spell slot as if you had cast the triggering spell. You then attempt to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] the triggering spell. The counteract rank for this effect is equal to the rank of the expended spell. If you succeed on your counteract check, you don’t [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/casting-spells#Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupt]] the spell. Instead, you choose an unoccupied space within 60 feet of you for the creature to teleport into. You can’t choose a space that is obviously harmful (such as into a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/wall-of-fire|_wall of fire_]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/running-exploration#Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]) or would cause the target to fall.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 109*
