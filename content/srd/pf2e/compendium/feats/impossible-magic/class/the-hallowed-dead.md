# The Hallowed Dead  *Feat 1*

`Holy`  `Necromancer`

- **Prerequisites**: You aren’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]].

While many necromancers seek [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] and perverse power, you have dedicated yourself to the practice of necromancy to stop the creation of permanent undead and the corruption of souls. You and your thralls gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] trait. Strikes your thralls make gain the holy trait and deal 1 additional [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “purposefully create undead to do undue harm.”
