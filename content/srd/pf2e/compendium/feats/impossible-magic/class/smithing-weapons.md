# Smithing Weapons  *Feat 1*

`Runesmith`

Though you are an artisan, you are well versed in using the tools of your trade to fend off enemies. While wielding weapons in the hammer, knife, and pick weapon groups, you can trace runes using the weapon without holding or wearing an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/artisans-toolkit-sterling|artisan’s toolkit]]. Additionally, a Strike with a hammer, knife, or pick deals 1 additional fire damage to an enemy bearing one of your runes as sparks fly on impact like a hammer to an anvil.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
