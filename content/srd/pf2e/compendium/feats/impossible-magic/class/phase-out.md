# Phase Out  *Feat 6*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Evolution`  `Summoner`

- **Prerequisites**: Your eidolon is a phantom.

Your eidolon phases out of reality slightly, gaining resistance to all damage (except [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]]) equal to half your level. They’re not [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]], but can’t use Strikes or actions that require a fully physical form, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|Grapple]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Reposition|Reposition]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]]. Your eidolon can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] this effect to return to their normal form.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 81*
