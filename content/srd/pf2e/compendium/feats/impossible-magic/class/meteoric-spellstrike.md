# Meteoric Spellstrike  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Arcane`  `Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Spellstrike|Spellstrike]]; starlit span hybrid study
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a ranged or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/thrown|thrown]] weapon, or have a ranged unarmed attack.

A trail of energy flows from your target back to you. Make a ranged Spellstrike against a target within your ranged weapon or unarmed attack’s first range increment. The spell you cast for the Spellstrike can’t be a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cantrip|cantrip]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/focus|focus]] spell. Each creature in a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Line|line]] between you and the target (not including you and the target) takes damage equal to double the spell’s rank. The damage type is the same as that dealt by the spell you cast (choose one type if the spell dealt multiple types of damage).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 23*
