# Knockback Spell  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Manipulate`  `Spellshape`  `Wizard`  `Metamagic`

You imbue your spells with magical force to knock creatures back. If the next action you use is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] that has an area of a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]], or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Line|line]]; has no duration; and requires creatures to attempt a Reflex or Fortitude save, the spell unleashes a forceful blast that pushes creatures away, in addition to its other effects. Each creature that failed its save against the spell is pushed 5 feet, and those who critically failed are pushed 10 feet. If the spell is a cone, emanation, or a line, you push the creature away from yourself. If the spell is a burst, you push the creature away from the center of the burst.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 109*
