# Distracting Spellstrike  *Feat 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: laughing shadow hybrid study; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Spellstrike|Spellstrike]]
- **Requirements**: You have a hand free, and your Spellstrike is charged.

You weave distracting magic with one hand to enhance the efficacy of your Spellstrike. Make a Spellstrike and Feint against the target of your Strike. Execute your Feint immediately before making the Strike, but after choosing your target. The Feint gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/arcane|arcane]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]] traits, and it always has the basic effects of a Feint, rather than applying any adjustments or alternate effects from other feats or abilities.

If you’re in the Arcane Cascade stance, you gain a status bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] check equal to your extra damage from Arcane Cascade (typically 1, increasing to 2 if you have weapon specialization and 3 if you have greater weapon specialization).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 19*
