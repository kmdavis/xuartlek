# Dance of Bloody Ink  *Feat 14*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Runesmith`

- **Requirements**: You’re wielding a melee weapon that bears a rune.

As you lash out, your weapon is your brush, wounds forming words of power in your target’s flesh. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Invoke Rune|Invoke a Rune]] on a melee weapon you are wielding and Strike. Then, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]] and Strike again with the same weapon. If the second Strike hits, you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] onto the target of the Strike. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 52*
