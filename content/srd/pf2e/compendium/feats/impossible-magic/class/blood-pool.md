# Blood Pool  *Feat 14*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Healing`  `Necromancer`  `Occult`

- **Trigger**: A creature with blood within 30 feet of you dies or takes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|bleed]] damage.

You use the lifeblood of your enemies to heal your allies. You pool the blood of the creature on the ground in a space adjacent to the triggering creature, infusing it with healing properties. The pool lasts for 1 minute or until absorbed by an ally. When an ally enters a space with a blood pool, they can absorb the blood pool as a free action, regaining Hit Points equal to your level.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 39*
