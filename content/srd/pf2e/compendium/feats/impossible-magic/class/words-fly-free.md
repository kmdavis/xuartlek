# Words, Fly Free  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Manipulate`  `Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/runic-tattoo|Runic Tattoo]]
- **Requirements**: Your Runic Tattoo is not faded.

Just because your runes are tattooed on your very body doesn’t mean they need to remain there. You fling your hand out, the rune from your Runic Tattoo flowing down it and flying through the air in a crescent. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace the Rune]] onto all targets within a 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]] that match the rune’s usage requirement. The rune then returns to you, faded.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 50*
