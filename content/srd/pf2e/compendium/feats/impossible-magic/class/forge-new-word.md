# Forge New Word  *Feat 20*

`Runesmith`

At the pinnacle of your art, you have forged a new great rune of power, a new ideal expression or utterance to be added to the craft. You create a rune and add it to your runic repertoire. When you gain this feat, choose a spell of 4th rank or lower. It must be common, or you must otherwise have access to it. The spell must take 1 or 2 actions to cast. You can choose a variable action spell, deciding when you create the rune whether the rune uses the 1-action or 2-action version of the spell. Name your rune. This rune has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unique|unique]] trait and a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]] trait of your choosing ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/arcane|arcane]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/primal|primal]]). 

This rune can be drawn on a creature or object, regardless of what the spell can target. When invoked, this rune has the effect of the chosen spell. The rune-bearer is the point of origin for the spell’s effect; calculate range and cover from its space. If the spell has targets, you decide who or what the spell targets, and the rune-bearer is a possible target if your chosen spell can target it. If the spell can affect only you (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/translocate|_translocate_]]), invoking it affects only you, and has no effect if the rune is drawn anywhere other than on you. If the spell requires a spell attack roll, use your class DC – 10 in place of the spell attack modifier. If your chosen spell has a duration and that duration is longer than 1 minute, the invoked rune’s duration is 1 minute instead.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 53*
