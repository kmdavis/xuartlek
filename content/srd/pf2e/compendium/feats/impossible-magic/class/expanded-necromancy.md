# Expanded Necromancy  *Feat 10*

`Necromancer`

You delve into other magical traditions. Add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/grisly-growths|_grisly growths_]] spell to your dirge. Add the following spells to your dirge as soon as you gain the ability to cast necromancer spells of that rank: 7th: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/execute|_execute_]], 8th: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/desiccate|_desiccate_]], 9th: massacre. You can prepare and cast these spells as occult spells.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 37*
