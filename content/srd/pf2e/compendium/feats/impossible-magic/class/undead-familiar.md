# Undead Familiar  *Feat 1*

`Necromancer`

You gain the ability to raise an undead familiar who will serve you as your loyal and sometimes expendable partner as you explore your necromantic magic. You gain a familiar. It has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] trait instead of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|animal]] trait and is immune to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|bleed]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Death Effects|death effects]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|disease]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]]. You can choose only one familiar ability per day for this familiar instead of two, and you can’t choose an ability that changes the creature’s trait (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/familiar-abilities#Construct|construct]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/familiar-abilities#Dragon|dragon]]). Additionally, this familiar can be targeted by spells and abilities that target your thralls. If you destroy your familiar, you can raise them back to undeath during your next daily preparations.
