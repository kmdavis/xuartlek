# Spirit Sheath  *Feat 2*

`Arcane`  `Extradimensional`  `Magus`

You’ve magically constructed an extradimensional sheath for your weapon that you access through your clothing, typically through a pocket, sleeve, or seam. This sheath is hard to notice, granting a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Conceal an Object|Conceal an Item]] within it. A spirit sheath can hold up to 1 Bulk worth of weapons.

During a Spellstrike, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] to draw one weapon from the sheath before you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast the Spell]]. You can draw or return a weapon to a spirit sheath as an Interact action, as can anyone else holding or wearing the clothing that contains the sheath. A [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/dispel-magic|_dispel magic_]] spell or similar effect can be used against the spirit sheath to expel the weapon. The [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] check is made against your spell DC and the counteract rank is half your level rounded up. The weapon is also expelled if you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]].

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 18*
