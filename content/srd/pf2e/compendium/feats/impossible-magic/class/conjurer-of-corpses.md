# Conjurer of Corpses  *Feat 6*

`Necromancer`

- **Prerequisites**: puppeteer fatal method

You can call forth many kinds of undead to do your bidding. You learn the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/summon-undead|_summon undead_]] spell. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|Undead]] creatures you summon with this spell can be destroyed and targeted by your spells or abilities as if they were thralls. You gain an additional spell slot each day at your highest level of necromancer spell slots. You can prepare only the _summon undead_ spell in this slot.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 36*
