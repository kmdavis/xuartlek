# Mobile Thralls  *Feat 4*

`Necromancer`

Your thralls can now follow you wherever you go. Any thrall you create has your choice of a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Climb Speed|climb Speed]] or [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Swim Speed|swim Speed]]. The additional Speed is equal to its land Speed, and any time you let the thrall [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]], you can have it use the relevant movement action instead. 

At 8th level, add a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] to your choices. A flying thrall remains airborne at the end of your turn even if it didn’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|Fly]] that turn.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 35*
