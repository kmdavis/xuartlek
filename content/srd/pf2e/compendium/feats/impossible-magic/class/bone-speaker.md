# Bone Speaker  *Feat 2*

`Necromancer`

To truly understand the mechanical advantages of an undead skeleton, you must understand how it functioned in life. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] on living creatures with an internal or external skeleton using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Undead Lore]]. If you do, you can learn only information pertaining to the biology of a creature—not any learned abilities or abilities granted by items.

Additionally, you can perform a forensic examination on a corpse using Undead Lore instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/medicine|Medicine]] if the corpse came from a creature with a skeleton.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 33*
