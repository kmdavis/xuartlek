# Whirlwind Spell  *Feat 20*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Spellstrike|Spellstrike]]
- **Requirements**: Your Spellstrike is charged.

You combine the might of a multitarget spell with a whirling flurry of attacks. You use Spellstrike, making a melee Strike against each enemy within your reach instead of against one enemy. If your spell can affect multiple targets or normally has an area, your spell affects all foes you hit, not just the first target, up to the spell’s normal maximum targets. If you Strike more enemies than the spell could target, choose which foes are affected by the spell. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty but doesn’t increase your penalty until you’ve made all your attacks.

You can use this activity with only melee Strikes, even if you have the starlit span hybrid study or a similar ability. 

If you use Expansive Spellstrike to Spellstrike with an area spell, apply the area as described in Expansive Spellstrike, choosing one of the targets to determine the area.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 25*
