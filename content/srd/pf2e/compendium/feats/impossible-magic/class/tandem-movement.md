# Tandem Movement  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Summoner`  `Tandem`

You and your eidolon move together. You each use a single action to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]]. Either of you that has the corresponding movement type can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Burrow|Burrow]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Fly|Fly]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] instead.
