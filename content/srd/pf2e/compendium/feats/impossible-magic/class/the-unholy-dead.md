# The Unholy Dead  *Feat 1*

`Necromancer`  `Uncommon`  `Unholy`

- **PFS Note**: This feat makes the PC Unholy which is not allowed in PFS Play.
- **Prerequisites**: You aren’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]].

You have turned your necromancy against the divine powers that seek to stop you from achieving your unholy goals. You and your thralls gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] trait. Strikes your thralls make gain the unholy trait and deal 1 additional [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “destroy undead of no harm to you.”
