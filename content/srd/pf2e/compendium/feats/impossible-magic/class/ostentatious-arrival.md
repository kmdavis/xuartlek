# Ostentatious Arrival  *Feat 6*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Spellshape`  `Summoner`  `Metamagic`

If the next action you take is to Manifest your Eidolon as a three-action activity, or to Cast a 3-action [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/summon|summon]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/howl-of-the-wild/incarnate|incarnate]] Spell, the creature appears in an explosion. All creatures in a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] around the creature you summoned or manifested except you take 1d4 fire damage per spell rank for a summon or incarnate spell, or 1d4 fire damage per 2 levels for Manifesting your Eidolon. If the creature you summoned or manifested has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]] trait, the damage is that type instead (or bludgeoning damage for the water trait). If the creature has more than one of these traits, you choose which damage type to deal.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 81*
