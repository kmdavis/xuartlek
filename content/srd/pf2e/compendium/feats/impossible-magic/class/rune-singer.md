# Rune-Singer  *Feat 1*

`Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]]

You practice the lost art of using music to guide your runecarving, singing the runes into existence as much as crafting them. You can use Performance instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]] when attempting Crafting checks related to runes.

Once per 10 minutes, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] with song alone. When you do, you use the 2-action version of Trace Rune as a single action without needing an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/artisans-toolkit-sterling|artisan's toolkit]], and you remove the action's [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] trait. You don't need to be able to move your hands when Tracing a Rune using song, but you do need to be able to sing in a clear voice.
