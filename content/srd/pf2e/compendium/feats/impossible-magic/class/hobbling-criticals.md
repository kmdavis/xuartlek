# Hobbling Criticals  *Feat 18*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon’s unarmed attacks batter your foes into submission, hampering their movement. Whenever your eidolon gets a critical hit with one of their unarmed Strikes, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC after taking the damage. This is a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/weapons#Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect.

**Critical Success** The target takes no additional effect.

**Success** The target takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 and takes a –15-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 85*
