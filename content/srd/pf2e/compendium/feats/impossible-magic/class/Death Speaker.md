# Death Speaker  *Feat 1*

`Necromancer`

Your voice and magic speak to the core existence within [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Undead|undead]] creatures, circumventing the need for the creature to comprehend. Your spells and abilities ignore the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mindless|mindless]] trait and any immunity to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Emotion|emotion]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fear|fear]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]] effects when targeting or affecting undead creatures. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus when you attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Make an Impression|Make an Impression]] on and make a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Request|Request]] of an undead creature.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 32*
