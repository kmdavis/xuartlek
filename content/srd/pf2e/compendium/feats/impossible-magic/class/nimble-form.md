# Nimble Form  *Feat 10*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon evolves into a figure of pure grace. Your multiple attack penalty from your eidolon’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/agile|agile]] unarmed attacks becomes –3 for a second attack and –6 for subsequent attacks (rather than –4 and –8).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 83*
