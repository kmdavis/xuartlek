# Spatial Awareness  *Feat 10*

`Wizard`

Your study of different planes of existence makes you more attuned to teleportation effects. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus when you attempt to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/teleportation|teleportation]] effect. 

When a teleportation effect occurs within 60 feet of you, you detect the location of the effect as a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Precise Senses|precise sense]]. If the effect is a creature teleporting away from that space, you know the general direction of the location to which it teleported but not the distance. However, you can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Identify Magic|Identify Magic]] within an hour of the teleportation to know the location to which the creature teleported within 10 miles on a success or 1 mile on a critical success.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 109*
