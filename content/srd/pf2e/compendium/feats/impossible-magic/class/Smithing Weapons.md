# Smithing Weapons  *Feat 1*

`Runesmith`

Though you are an artisan, you are well versed in using the tools of your trade to fend off enemies. While wielding a weapon in the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Hammer|hammer]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Knife|knife]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Pick|pick]] weapon group, you can use that weapon to etch runes without an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/Artisan's Toolkit|artisan's toolkit]]. Additionally, a Strike with a hammer, knife, or pick deals 1 additional fire damage to an enemy bearing one of your runes as sparks fly on impact like a hammer to an anvil.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
