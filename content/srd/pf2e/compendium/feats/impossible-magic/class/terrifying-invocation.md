# Terrifying Invocation  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Invocation`  `Runesmith`

You spit and roar as you pronounce your rune's terrible name. You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]] a single target within 30 feet and then [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Invoke Rune|Invoke one Rune]] upon that target. You don't take a penalty to your check if the creature doesn't understand your language.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 49*
