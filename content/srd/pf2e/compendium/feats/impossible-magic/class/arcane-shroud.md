# Arcane Shroud  *Feat 14*

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Arcane Cascade|Arcane Cascade]]

Your magic has a powerful aftereffect, briefly granting you a certain spell. When you take this feat, choose three of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/false-vitality|_false vitality_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/fleet-step|_fleet step_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/invisibility|_invisibility_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/mountain-resilience|_mountain resilience_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/resist-energy|_resist energy_]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/see-the-unseen|_see the unseen_]]. 

When you use Arcane Cascade, choose one of the spells you selected with this feat. You’re affected by this aftereffect spell until the end of your next turn, though it ends early if the stance ends or another circumstance ends it (such as if you take a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/hostile-actions|hostile action]] while affected by _invisibility_). If you’re 18th level or higher, a _false vitality_ or _resist energy_ you get from Arcane Shroud is heightened to 4th rank.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time to add the remaining spells to your set of choices.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 24*
