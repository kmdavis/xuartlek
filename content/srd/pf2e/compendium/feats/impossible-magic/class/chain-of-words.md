# Chain of Words  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Force`  `Invocation`  `Runesmith`

- **Frequency**: once pr 10 minutes

You see the thin lines of magic between your creations, and you can exploit that connection with a small shift of power from one rune to another. You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/impossible-magic#Invoke Rune|Invoke two Runes]] within 60 feet. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, a chain of glowing script flows between them, dealing 5d6 force damage to all creatures in a straight [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Line|line]] between them with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex saving throw; creatures bearing the invoked runes are not in the area of this line. A creature who critically fails its save is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round or until it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escapes]], as the runes seal its movements. The DC to Escape is your class DC. 

At 12th level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 51*
