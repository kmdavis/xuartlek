# Hallowed Earth  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Aura`  `Concentrate`  `Holy`  `Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Spirit`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/impossible-magic/class/the-hallowed-dead|The Hallowed Dead]]

The earth is both where the dead rise from and where they are put to rest. Your presence sanctifies the earth around you. When an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] creature ends its turn within 10 feet of you, it takes 2 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage. If it’s an unholy [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creature, it takes an additional 2 vitality damage. At 10th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the spirit and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] damage each increase by 2. Hallowed Earth lasts for 1 minute, but you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] it.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 36*
