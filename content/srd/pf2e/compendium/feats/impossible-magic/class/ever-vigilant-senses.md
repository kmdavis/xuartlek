# Ever-Vigilant Senses  *Feat 16*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon has enhanced senses. They gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Perception|Perception]] and can’t be [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Flanking|flanked]] except by creatures that are higher level than them. They can see [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects, which appear translucent and are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]]. When your eidolon moves within 30 feet of an illusion that can be disbelieved, the GM rolls a secret check to disbelieve it, even if your eidolon didn’t spend an action to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]].

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 85*
