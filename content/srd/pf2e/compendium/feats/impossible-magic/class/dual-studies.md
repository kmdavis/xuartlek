# Dual Studies  *Feat 1*

`Summoner`

You and your eidolon learn different skills. Choose two skills. Your eidolon becomes trained in one of those skills, and you become trained in the other skill. At 7th level, you each become an expert in the chosen skills. Unlike other skill proficiencies, these proficiencies are not shared between you and your eidolon.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 78*
