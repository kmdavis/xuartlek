# Vampiric Spell  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Necromancer`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

Your magic drains the life from your enemy. If the next action you use is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] that deals damage, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the initial damage a single creature takes from this spell; calculate these temporary Hit Points using the creature that took the most damage from the spell. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 35*
