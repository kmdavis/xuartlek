# Advanced Rune Magic  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Runesmith
- **Prerequisites**: Basic Rune Magic

You gain a runesmith feat. For the purpose of meeting its prerequisites, your runesmith level is equal to half your character level.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once. Each time you select it, you gain another runesmith feat.
