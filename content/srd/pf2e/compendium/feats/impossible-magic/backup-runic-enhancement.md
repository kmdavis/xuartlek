# Backup Runic Enhancement  *Feat 1*

`Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in Arcana, Trained in Nature, Trained in Occultism, or Trained in Religion

While you are not a spellcaster, you have a working knowledge of the most fundamental runic magic. You can cast the _sigil_ cantrip as an innate spell at will. In addition, choose either _runic body_ or _runic weapon_. You can cast this spell once per day as an innate spell. The rank of these spells is equal to half your level, rounded up, and the tradition can be any tradition for which you are at least trained in the related skill (Arcana for arcane, Nature for primal, Occultism for occult, and Religion for divine).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 47*
