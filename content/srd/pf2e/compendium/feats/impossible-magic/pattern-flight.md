# Pattern Flight  *Feat 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Flourish`  `Runesmith`

You place a bit of magic in a physical projectile, causing it to fly in a runic pattern through the air once you loose it. Make a ranged Strike against a target within your weapon’s first range increment. Because of the erratic nature of its flight, this Strike ignores any circumstance bonus to AC from cover. After your Strike, you can Trace a Rune on one target in a straight line between you and the target of your Strike (including the original target).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
