# Devastating Spellstrike  *Feat 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: inexorable iron hybrid study; Spellstrike
- **Requirements**: Your Spellstrike is charged.

Your Spellstrike sweeps are so wide and devastating, they smash into nearby foes. Make a Spellstrike with a spell that can deal damage. Enemies in a 5-foot emanation around the target (not including the target) take 2 damage of the same damage type your spell could deal. This damage increases to 3 at 9th level, 4 at 14th level, and 5 at 19th level. 

If you’re in the Arcane Cascade stance, the emanation’s damage gains a status bonus to damage equal to the extra damage from your Arcane Cascade.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 19*
