# Vital Compound Invocation  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Healing`  `Invocation`  `Runesmith`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

You can invoke runes from traditions that manipulate vital energy to restore flesh. You Invoke two Runes—one must be a divine rune, and one must be a primal rune. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, one creature that’s within 30 feet of both invoked runes regains Hit Points equal to 5 + double your level.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 50*
