# Dimensional Pilfer  *Feat 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`  `Manipulate`  `Teleportation`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication; Expert in Stealth

You have a preternatural ability to whisk items out from under their owners’ noses. Attempt a Thievery check to Palm an Object or Steal an object you can see within 60 feet. If you are veiled, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to this check. If you succeed, the object vanishes from its current location and appears on your person.
