# All Returns to Slumber  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Mental`  `Primal`  `Sleep`

- **Archetype**: World Rouser
- **Prerequisites**: World Rouser Dedication
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

Your waking world returns to slumber, and lays the peace of deep sleep on all within it. You Dismiss your waking world, and all creatures in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. Animals, beasts, and plants native to this world treat the result of their save as one degree worse. Creatures in the area then become temporarily immune to this ability for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature can’t use reactions for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also slowed 1.
