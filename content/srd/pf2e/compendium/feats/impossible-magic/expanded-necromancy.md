# Expanded Necromancy  *Feat 10*

`Necromancer`

You delve into other magical traditions. Add the _grisly growths_ spell to your dirge. Add the following spells to your dirge as soon as you gain the ability to cast necromancer spells of that rank: 7th: _execute_, 8th: _desiccate_, 9th: massacre. You can prepare and cast these spells as occult spells.
