# Eidolon's Wrath  *Feat 6*

`Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon gains the _eidolon’s wrath_ link spell, which they cast, instead of you. You determine the damage type when you gain the feat: acid, cold, electricity, fire, sonic, spirit, vitality, or void. As normal, your eidolon shares your Focus Points.
