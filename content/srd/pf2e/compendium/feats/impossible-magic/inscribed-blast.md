# Inscribed Blast  *Feat 5*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Automaton`  `Concentrate`

- **Trigger**: A creature or object adjacent to you damages you.
- **Requirements**: Your inscribed runes are currently intact.

Some of the patterning on your exterior covering is not just decorative but is instead a set of runes. The triggering creature or object takes 2d4 force damage and your inscribed runes fade. You re-inscribe your runes each day during your daily preparations. 

At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d4.

**Enhancement** You can re-inscribe your runes with 10 minutes of work as an exploration activity.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 111*
