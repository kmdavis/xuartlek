# Soulforger Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: Soulforger
- **Prerequisites**: or ability to cast divine spells

You can manifest the power of your spirit in combat to realize your motivations. You bind a suit of armor, shield, or weapon as a soulforged armament. Choose a motivation or cause known as a soul path (page 101) that ties you to your soulforged armament; acting against or ignoring your soul path is anathema to you and could lead you to be afflicted with a curse called soulforged corruption. Choose one essence power for the armament (see archetype). Changing an essence power requires 1 week of retraining, and you can’t retrain them while you have soulforged corruption. 

You can Manifest Soulforged Armament to summon your soulforged armaments in combat. 

**Manifest Soulforged Armament** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, divine, extradimensional) **Requirements** If summoning a soulforged weapon or shield, you have the hands free to wield it; if summoning soulforged armor, you aren’t wearing any armor; **Effect** You immediately wield or wear the soulforged armament bound to you. The soulforged armament remains manifested until you Dismiss this effect. 

Once per day when you use this ability, you can manifest the armament’s essence form. You gain the armament’s essence power until it’s Dismissed. After 1 minute, the essence form armament is automatically Dismissed.

### Soulforger Dedication leads to...

Devastating Manifestation, Rapid Manifestation, Soul Arsenal, Soul Flare
