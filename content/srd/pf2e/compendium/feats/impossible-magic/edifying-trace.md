# Edifying Trace  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Flourish`  `Runesmith`

When you apply a rune to a foe, it reveals something about them to you. Trace a Rune onto an adjacent enemy and then attempt a skill check to Recall Knowledge about that target. If you succeed, you leverage this knowledge when you invoke any of your runes on that target; that target takes a –1 status penalty to saving throws against your invocations for 1 minute. If you use Edifying Trace on another enemy, the effect ends for the previous enemy.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 50*
