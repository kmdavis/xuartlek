# Emergency Targe  *Feat 4*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: sparkling targe hybrid study
- **Trigger**: An enemy you’re aware of hits you with a Strike or a spell attack, or you fail a save against an enemy’s spell.
- **Requirements**: You are wielding a shield or have the _shield_ spell prepared.

You immediately Raise a Shield or cast _shield_. The circumstance bonus applies to your defenses when determining the outcome of the triggering attack or spell.
