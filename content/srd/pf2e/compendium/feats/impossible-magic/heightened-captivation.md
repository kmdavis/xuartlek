# Heightened Captivation  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: Captivator
- **Prerequisites**: Captivator Dedication

You can infuse your charms and illusions with more power. If your next action is to cast a non-cantrip spell granted by captivator archetype feats that’s at least 1 rank lower than your highest-rank non-cantrip captivator spells, that spell is heightened to the highest rank of captivator spell you can cast.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 93*
