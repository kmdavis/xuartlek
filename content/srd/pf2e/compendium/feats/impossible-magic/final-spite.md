# Final Spite  *Feat 8*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Poison`

- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Trigger**: A creature reduces you or a living ally within 30 feet to 0 Hit Points. You can trigger this action even while dying.

You can channel the power of death into a poisonous spite. The triggering creature takes 4d4 persistent poison damage with a basic Fortitude save against your spell DC. If the creature kills you or your ally as part of the trigger, the creature gets a result one degree of success worse than it rolls on this save.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 37*
