# Corrupted Ground  *Feat 6*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Aura`  `Concentrate`  `Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Spirit`  `Unholy`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Prerequisites**: The Unholy Dead

The ground on which you stride is mere fuel that you burn with unholy void energies, scarring the earth in your wake. When a holy creature or psychopomp ends its turn within 10 feet of you, it takes 2 spirit damage. If it’s a holy living creature, it takes an additional 2 void damage. At 10th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the spirit and void damage each increase by 2. Corrupted Ground lasts for 1 minute, but you can Dismiss it.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 36*
