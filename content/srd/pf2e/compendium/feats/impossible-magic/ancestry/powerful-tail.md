# Powerful Tail  *Feat 1*

`Automaton`

You resemble an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|animal]] or a type of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/humanoid|humanoid]] who has a tail. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/brawling/fist|tail unarmed attack]] that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This tail is in the brawling group.

**Enhancement** Your tail is not only powerful but also flexible. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Climb Speed|climb Speed]] of 10 feet, and you reduce the number of free hands required to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]] by one.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 110*
