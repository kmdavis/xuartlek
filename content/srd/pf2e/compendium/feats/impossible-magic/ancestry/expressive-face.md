# Expressive Face  *Feat 1*

`Automaton`

Your face is constructed to move organically and show your emotions, making you more relatable to organic creatures. You become trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/diplomacy|Diplomacy]]. If you are already trained in Diplomacy, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. Additionally, you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/glad-hand|Glad-Hand]] skill feat, even if you do not meet its prerequisites.

**Enhancement** You are particularly skilled at pouring your heart out to others. You gain the Plaintive Plea action. 

**Plaintive Plea** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fortune|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You make an emotional appeal to convince someone during a diplomatic encounter. If your next activity is to attempt a Diplomacy check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Make an Impression|Make an Impression]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Request|Request]], or to attempt to influence a creature during an [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-4-subsystems/influence|influence encounter]], you can roll twice and take the better result.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 110*
