# Infiltration Automation  *Feat 5*

`Automaton`

You have quiet, well-oiled joints, and you move around as silently as a shadow, though you sometimes use a magical boost. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks against hearing-based [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Perception|Perception]] checks. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/silence|_silence_]] once per day on yourself as a 2nd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/arcane|arcane]] [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate spell]].

**Enhancement** You can cast _silence_ twice per day on yourself, and you can choose whether to cast it as a 2nd- or 4th-rank spell.
