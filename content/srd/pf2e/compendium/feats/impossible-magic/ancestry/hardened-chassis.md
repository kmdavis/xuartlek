# Hardened Chassis  *Feat 5*

`Automaton`

Your exterior is so tough that only the strongest of weapons can fully damage it. Choose one type of damage among bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 3 to that type of damage. This resistance does not apply if the weapon or attack is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/adamantine-object-high-grade|adamantine]].

**Enhancement** Your chassis gets even tougher. Choose one of the following benefits: you gain resistance 3 to all physical damage, or your chosen resistance increases to a value equal to half your level (minimum 3). This resistance is still bypassed by adamantine.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 111*
