# Reach of the Dead  *Feat 1*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Concentrate`  `Necromancer`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

You channel your necrotic magic through a thrall, expending it in the process. If the next action you use is to Cast a Spell that has range, you cast that spell from one of your thralls within 60 feet. That thrall becomes the point of origin for your spell; calculate range and cover from its space instead of yours. The thrall is then destroyed.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 32*
