# Enhanced Glyph Familiar  *Feat 2*

`Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: Glyph Familiar

Your living rune is imbued with more magical power. You can select two familiar or master abilities each day instead of one. In addition, when you Trace a Rune with a single action, your target can be adjacent to your familiar instead of adjacent to you. Your familiar must be within 30 feet of you for this benefit.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
