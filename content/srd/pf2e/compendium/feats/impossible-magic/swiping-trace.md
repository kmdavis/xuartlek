# Swiping Trace  *Feat 8*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Flourish`  `Runesmith`

You prepare a rune on the end of your weapon so that it transfers to your enemies. Make a melee Strike and compare the attack roll to the AC of up to two enemies within your melee reach. Roll damage only once and apply it to each creature you hit. You can Trace a Rune on each creature you hit and deal damage to, but they must be the same rune. A Swiping Trace counts as two attacks for your multiple attack penalty.

If you’re using a weapon with the sweep trait, its bonus applies to your Swiping Trace attack.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 51*
