# The Unholy Dead  *Feat 1*

`Necromancer`  `Uncommon`  `Unholy`

- **PFS Note**: This feat makes the PC Unholy which is not allowed in PFS Play.
- **Prerequisites**: You aren’t holy.

You have turned your necromancy against the divine powers that seek to stop you from achieving your unholy goals. You and your thralls gain the unholy trait. Strikes your thralls make gain the unholy trait and deal 1 additional spirit damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “destroy undead of no harm to you.”

### The Unholy Dead leads to...

Corrupted Ground
