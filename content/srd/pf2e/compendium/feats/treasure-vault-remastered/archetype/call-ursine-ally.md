# Call Ursine Ally  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`  `Primal`  `Summoned`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/ursine-avenger-hood|Ursine Avenger Hood]]
- **Frequency**: once per hour

You can cast a 3rd-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/summon-animal|_summon animal_]] as an [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate]] spell, but only to summon a black bear. At 10th level, the _summon animal_ spell is heightened to 4th rank, and you can summon a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/grizzly-bear|grizzly bear]]. At 12th level, your _summon animal_ innate spell is heightened to 5th rank, and you can summon a polar bear. At 14th level, it’s heightened to 6th rank, and you can summon a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/cave-bear|cave bear]].

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 184*
