# Call Ursine Ally  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`  `Primal`  `Summoned`

- **Archetype**: Ursine Avenger Hood
- **Frequency**: once per hour

You can cast a 3rd-rank _summon animal_ as an innate spell, but only to summon a black bear. At 10th level, the _summon animal_ spell is heightened to 4th rank, and you can summon a grizzly bear. At 12th level, your _summon animal_ innate spell is heightened to 5th rank, and you can summon a polar bear. At 14th level, it’s heightened to 6th rank, and you can summon a cave bear.
