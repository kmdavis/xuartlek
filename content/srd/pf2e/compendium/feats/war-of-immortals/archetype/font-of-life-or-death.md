# Font of Life or Death  *Feat 16*

`Mythic`  `Rare`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/godling|Godling]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/godling-dedication|Godling Dedication]]

As you reach ever higher in your quest to achieve true divinity, the powers of life and death flow evermore easily from you. Choose either [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/heal|_heal_]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/harm|_harm_]]; you gain the chosen spell as an 8th rank [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Innate Spells|innate spell]] you can cast three times per day. Your hierophant can cast these innate spells as well by expending one of your daily uses.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 132*
