# Jousting Mount  *Feat 14*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/apocalypse-rider|Apocalypse Rider]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/apocalypse-rider-dedication|Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

While riding your apocalypse mount, any two-handed weapon you wield gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/jousting|jousting]] trait with a die size equal to one die size lower than the weapon’s normal damage die.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 107*
