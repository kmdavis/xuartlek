# Piercing Doom  *Feat 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/warrior-of-legend|Warrior Of Legend]]

- **Requirements**: You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Doomed|doomed]] 2 or greater.

You have grown accustomed to the curse that comes with your blessing, able to channel its effects through your attacks and forestall your own demise. Make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]]; on a success, the target takes an additional 1d10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/void|void]] damage and your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Doomed|doomed]] value is reduced by 1.
