# Seneschal Witch Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Class`  `Dedication`  `Rare`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/seneschal|Seneschal]]
- **Prerequisites**: Seneschal Witch

As you work to understand the connection with your missing patron, it’s increasingly easy to form bonds with those around you. Raw magical energy spills easily through the connection but, without a patron’s control, it sometimes resists your demands for it to form proper spells.

You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/witchs-charge|Witch’s Charge]] feat, but can’t choose your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/archetype/familiar|familiar]] or another creature with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/minion|minion]] trait as a charge. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|cast]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/manifest-will|_manifest will_]], as long as your charge is within 30 feet, you can choose to center the spell’s [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] on your charge instead of yourself.
