# Gird Champion  *Feat 16*

`Mythic`  `Rare`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/godling|Godling]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/godling-dedication|Godling Dedication]]

Your will manifests into divine weapons to protect and arm your followers. When you gain this feat, choose one type of weapon with special significance to you, such as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/sword/longsword|longsword]] or a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/sickle|sickle]]. This becomes your favored weapon. You and your hierophant both treat this weapon as a simple weapon for the purposes of proficiency. You and your hierophant gain the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/weapons#Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect of your favored weapon and deal an additional 1d6 spirit damage with [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/building-creatures#Strikes|Strikes]] made with your favored weapon. If one of your current domain spells can deal a type of damage other than [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]], you can choose to match this damage type (for instance, wreathing your weapon in sacred ashes that deal fire damage if you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Fire|fire]] domain).

*Source: War of Immortals p. 132*
