# Unified Stance  *Feat 16*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mythic`  `Stance`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/beast-lord|Beast Lord]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/beast-lord-dedication|Beast Lord Dedication]]

You and your companion enter a unified fighting style that allows you to fight in tandem more succinctly. When you enter this stance, your united [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/companion|companion]] gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Quickened|quickened]] condition and, when Commanded, can use the extra action to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]].
