# Surging Interference  *Feat 20*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/wildspell|Wildspell]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/wildspell-dedication|Wildspell Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: A creature [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/activating-items#Cast a Spell|Casts a Spell]] within your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/spellsurge|_spellsurge_]] aura.

You absorb the scattered energy of others’ failed spells, using it to recharge your own magic. You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] the spell. If you’re successful, you absorb the dispersed magical energy, regaining one spell or spell slot of 8th rank or lower of your choice. If you spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]] when you successfully counteract the triggering creature’s spell, you instead regain a 9th-rank spell or spell slot of your choice.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 141*
