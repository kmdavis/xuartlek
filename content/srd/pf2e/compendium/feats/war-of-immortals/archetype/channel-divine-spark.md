# Channel Divine Spark  *Feat 14*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/ascended-celestial|Ascended Celestial]]
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/ascended-celestial-dedication|Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

You embrace your divine spark, unleashing your full potential. Your nimbus automatically activates (if it isn’t already) and the area of your nimbus doubles; you shed [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Bright Light|bright light]] for 60 feet (and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Dim Light|dim light]] to the next 60 feet). You can’t suppress your nimbus while Channeling your Divine Spark. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level, and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half your level. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Quickened|quickened]] condition and can use the extra action each round only for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] actions. Each time that you damage a creature with a melee Strike, you can attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Shove|Shove]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Trip|Trip]] that creature as a free action. These effects last for 1 minute.
