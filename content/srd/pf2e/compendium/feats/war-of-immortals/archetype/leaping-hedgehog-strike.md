# Leaping Hedgehog Strike  *Feat 16*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/eternal-legend|Eternal Legend]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/eternal-legend-dedication|Eternal Legend Dedication]]

You pounce toward an enemy and then barrel them over with one fluid movement, ending this maneuver with an attack that may catch them off-balance. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]] horizontally toward an enemy you can see; treat your Speed as 30 feet or more for this Leap if it is less than 30 feet. If you end this movement adjacent to that enemy, attempt an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Tumble Through|Tumble Through]] that enemy’s space. On a success, the target is also knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]]. You then Strike that same enemy. If the target has been knocked prone, you can spend 1 [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]] as part of this Strike to make it at mythic proficiency.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 128*
