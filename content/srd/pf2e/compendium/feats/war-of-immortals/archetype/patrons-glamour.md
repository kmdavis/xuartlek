# Patron's Glamour  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Auditory`  `Illusion`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/seneschal|Seneschal]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/seneschal-witch-dedication|Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

Gathering up threads of your old connection, you can temporarily disguise yourself as your missing patron. You gain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/illusory-disguise|_illusory disguise_]] as an innate spell of your patron’s tradition, automatically heightened to the rank of your witch cantrips, but you can use it only to disguise yourself as the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/humanoid|humanoid]] representation of your patron. Detection spells of lower rank than this _illusory disguise_ give your patron’s information rather than your own.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 62*
