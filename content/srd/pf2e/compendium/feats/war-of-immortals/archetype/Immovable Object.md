# Immovable Object  *Feat 14*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mythic`  `Stance`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Eternal Legend|Eternal Legend]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/Eternal Legend Dedication|Eternal Legend Dedication]]; master in Fortitude or Reflex Saves

You plant your feet in a wide stance, resisting all movement not of your own volition. While in this stance and on the ground, you are [[srd/pf2e/books/guns-gears-remastered/gears-characters/Construct Companions#Immunities|immune]] to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Forced Movement|forced movement]] and if an effect would knock you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Prone|prone]], you aren’t knocked prone and can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]] as a free action.
