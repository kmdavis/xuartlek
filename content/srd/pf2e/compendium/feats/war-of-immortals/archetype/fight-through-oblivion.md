# Fight Through Oblivion  *Feat 20*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/eternal-legend|Eternal Legend]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/eternal-legend-dedication|Eternal Legend Dedication]]

Mortal wounds that would kill other warriors are no more than the flutter of butterfly wings to you. When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you aren’t knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] and you don’t gain the dying condition. Until the start of your next turn, you become [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|immune]] to all damage, but you also can’t regain Hit Points in any way except the following. If you Strike and damage a foe on this turn, you regain 4d8+30 Hit Points. Otherwise, your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Wounded|wounded]] value increases by 1 and you remain immune to damage and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]]. This cycle continues until you regain Hit Points by damaging a foe or until you reach wounded 4, at which point you lose the wounded condition, fall unconscious, and gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dying|dying]] condition.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 129*
