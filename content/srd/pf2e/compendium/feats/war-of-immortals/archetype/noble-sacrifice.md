# Noble Sacrifice  *Feat 16*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/prophesied-monarch|Prophesied Monarch]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/prophesied-monarch-dedication|Prophesied Monarch Dedication]]
- **Trigger**: One of your designated knights is within 15 feet of you and would take damage from an enemy’s [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]].

As your knights devote their lives and swords to you, so too do you return their devotion with your own fierce protection. Spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]]. The knight [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Steps]] as a free action, and you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] into the space previously occupied by the knight; this movement ignores [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain. You then take the triggering damage instead. If you have a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Raise a Shield|shield raised]] and have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/shield-block|Shield Block]] reaction, you can use it as part of this ability, applying damage to the shield as though you were the original target of the attack. The enemy whose [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] triggered this reaction is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 136*
