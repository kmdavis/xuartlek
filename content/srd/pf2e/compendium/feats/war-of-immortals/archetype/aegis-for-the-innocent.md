# Aegis for the Innocent  *Feat 14*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Divine`  `Manipulate`  `Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/ascended-celestial|Ascended Celestial]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/ascended-celestial-dedication|Ascended Celestial Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: Your nimbus is active.

You transfer a portion of your divine spark to an ally, temporarily dimming your nimbus to encase an ally in a barrier of light that repels attackers. Select a willing creature within the [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Bright Light|bright light]] of your nimbus. You surround the target in a protective barrier of light; this is a 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] centered on the target. This barrier lasts for 1 minute, or until you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] it. While the Aegis of the Innocent is active, the area of your nimbus is halved.

The target gains a +1 status bonus to AC. Any enemy within the barrier, or entering the barrier, must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC or your spell DC. A creature needs to attempt this save only once each round.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round and treats the space within the barrier as [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]].

**Failure** The creature becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute, is pushed 10 feet, and treats the space within the barrier as difficult terrain. If a creature would be pushed into a solid barrier or another creature, it stops at that point and takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 115*
