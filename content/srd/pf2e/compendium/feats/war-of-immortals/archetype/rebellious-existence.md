# Rebellious Existence  *Feat 18*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/broken-chain|Broken Chain]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/broken-chain-dedication|Broken Chain Dedication]]

You have tied yourself so absolutely to the cause of liberty that your very existence is an act of rebellion. You ignore any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] effects without the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]] trait that would rob you of your agency (for example, on a failed save against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/charm|_charm_]] you would still be able to take hostile actions, and against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/laughing-fit|_laughing fit_]] you would still be able to take reactions but would remain [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]], but on a critical failure against fear you would still be fleeing because it’s a fear effect). Additionally, you are [[srd/pf2e/books/guns-gears-remastered/gears-characters/construct-companions#Immunities|immune]] to effects that would imprison your soul (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-9/seize-soul|_seize soul_]], or a final blade).

*Source: War of Immortals p. 125*
