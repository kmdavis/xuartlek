# Prophesied Monarch Dedication  *Feat 12*

`Destiny`  `Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Prophesied Monarch|Prophesied Monarch]]

Your Calling has culminated in a destiny of rulership, and you’re able to forge a mythic bond with noble knights whose destinies become intertwined with your own. Designate up to five allies as your knights; as long as you’re conscious, your knights’ [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Doomed|doomed]] condition can’t rise above 3, and if they’re ever in mortal peril (such as being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Dying|dying]] condition or imprisoned in an enemy fortress), you instantly receive a vision revealing their location and condition.

In addition, you gain the Royal Grace exploration activity. 

**Royal Grace** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Concentrate|concentrate]], exploration, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Linguistic|linguistic]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]]) You spend 10 minutes conferring with one of your knights who has the doomed condition. By discussing plans for your future kingdom with them and describing the benefits of your rule, you can attempt to soothe their spirits. Attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Diplomacy|Diplomacy]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Society|Society]] check against a [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Difficulty Classes#Level-Based DCs|hard DC of your knight's level]]. Regardless of the result, that knight gains the benefits of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Refocus|Refocus]] activity (if they have a focus pool) but is temporarily immune to Royal Grace for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The knight’s doomed condition is reduced by 2 (minimum 0).

**Success** The knight’s doomed condition is reduced by 1 (minimum 0).

**Failure** The knight’s doomed condition isn’t reduced.
