# Pass Vengeful Judgement  *Feat 18*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mythic`  `Rare`  `Sanctified`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/godling|Godling]]
- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/godling-dedication|Godling Dedication]]
- **Requirements**: Your hierophant invoked your judgment on their previous turn

To prevent yourself from frivolously calling down the terrible power of divine wrath, you have vowed to do so only with the assent of your mortal hierophant. If your hierophant successfully Strikes a target, they can spend a single action to invoke your wrath. On your turn, you can then Pass Vengeful Judgment on the target, calling down a scouring pillar of divine energy that deals 20d10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage with a basic Fortitude save. You do not need to have line of effect to the target and it functions at any range. If one of your current domain spells can deal a type of damage other than spirit, you can choose to match this damage type (for instance, calling down a flaming pillar that deals [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]] damage if you have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Fire|fire]] domain).

If you spend a Mythic Point as part of Passing your Vengeful Judgment, the blast extends into the heavens, scattering the clouds in a detonation visible to all creatures within a 10-mile radius as a show of wrath and a warning to those who would dare harm your followers or question your commandments. For the next month, you and your hierophant gain a +2 status bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against creatures in the area.

**Special** If you have [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/font-of-life-or-death|Font of Life or Death]] , you can choose for the damage type to be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] (if you chose heal) or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/void|void]] (if you chose harm) when you use this ability.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 132*
