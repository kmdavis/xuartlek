# Soul Vessel Mount  *Feat 18*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/apocalypse-rider|Apocalypse Rider]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/archetype/apocalypse-rider-dedication|Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

Your mount has a supernatural hunger for mortal souls that you can feed. Once per day, you can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-9/seize-soul|_seize soul_]] as a divine innate spell. The spell gains a cost of 1 [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]] but you can use your apocalypse mount as the item in which you store the soul. Your apocalypse mount counts as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/artifact|artifact]] for the purpose of the spell. At any point while your apocalypse mount contains a soul, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the spell to allow your apocalypse mount to consume the soul. It regains a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level. For the next minute, it also gains a +20-foot status bonus to its Speed and its unarmed attack [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] are made at mythic proficiency.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 109*
