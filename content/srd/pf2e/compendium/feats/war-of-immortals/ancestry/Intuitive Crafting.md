# Intuitive Crafting  *Feat 1*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/ancestry/Aeonbound|Aeonbound]]

Your affinity with process and order makes you a natural artisan. You are trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Crafting|Crafting]]. If you were already trained in Crafting, you instead gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/Specialty Crafting|Specialty Crafting]] skill feat in a specialty of your choice; if you have both, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. In addition, when you critically fail to Repair an item, you get a failure instead.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 53*
