# Methodical Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/ancestry/Aeonbound|Aeonbound]]

You can reorder the world with your magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Calm|_calm_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Lock|_lock_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Mending|_mending_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Shape Wood|_shape wood_]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Translate|_translate_]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 55*
