# Anarchic Arcana  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/ancestry/Proteankin|Proteankin]]

The magic in your blood is unpredictable. When you make your daily preparations, roll 1d12 twice and consult the following list. 1: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Acid Grip|_acid grip_]]; 2: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Blur|_blur_]]; 3: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Gecko Grip|_gecko grip_]]; 4: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Humanoid Form|_humanoid form_]]; 5: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Illusory Object|_illusory object_]]; 6: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Laughing Fit|_laughing fit_]]; 7: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Noise Blast|_noise blast_]]; 8: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Resist Energy|_resist energy_]]; 9: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]]; 10: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Shatter|_shatter_]]; 11: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Shrink|_shrink_]]; 12: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Telekinetic Maneuver|_telekinetic maneuver_]]. You can cast each of those two spells once during the following day (or one spell twice if you rolled the same number) as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 54*
