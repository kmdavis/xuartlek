# Faultspawn  *Feat 1*

`Lineage`  `Nephilim`

You bear a grudge, inherited from a blasphemous asura forebear in your bloodline, against a particular deity and their works. You gain the Find Fault reaction.

**Find Fault** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against a spell or magic effect but haven’t rolled yet; Effect You find some kind of fault with the magic, using that flaw to protect yourself from the effect. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your saving throw against the triggering effect, which increases to a +2 circumstance bonus if the effect is divine and originates from a worshipper of the deity you chose for your grudge.
