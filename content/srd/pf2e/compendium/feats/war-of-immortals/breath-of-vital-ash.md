# Breath of Vital Ash  *Feat 10*

`Exemplar`  `Ikon`

Reddish ash stains your ikon, impossible to clean off. Every time you exhale, a tinge of red and a scent of spiced smoke float upon your breath. The imbued ikon gains the following ability.

Transcendence—Mark the Center [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (transcendence) You hold a hand to your lips and exhale, releasing enchanted red ash in a 15-foot cone. The ash clings to the vital areas of each enemy in the area for 1 minute. When a creature marked by the ash is hit and dealt damage by a Strike, it takes an additional 4d6 precision or vitality damage, then its mark ends. The damage type is whichever is worse for the creature, as determined by the GM. At 13th level and every 3 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 38*
