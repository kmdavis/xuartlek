# Methodical Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: Aeonbound

You can reorder the world with your magic. Choose two of the following spells: _calm_, _dispel magic_, _lock_, _mending_, _shape wood_, or _translate_. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.
