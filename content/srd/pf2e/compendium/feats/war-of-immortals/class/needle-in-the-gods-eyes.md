# Needle In The God's Eyes  *Feat 16*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Fighter`

With sinews of bronze and thews of iron, you leap to the heavens, piercing the arrogant eyes of the gods. [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Leap|Leap]] up to your total Speed, either horizontally or vertically, and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] twice at any point or points during the Leap. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but don’t increase your penalty until you have made both attacks.
