# Fly on Shadowed Wings  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Animist`  `Apparition`

- **Frequency**: once per hour

Your apparition embraces you, feeding on your magic to become a pair of protective wings. Choose one of your attuned apparitions; for 10 minutes, you gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] equal to your land Speed and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] to precision damage equal to your level, but your chosen apparition is dispersed for the duration, automatically re-attuning to you after 10 minutes.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 26*
