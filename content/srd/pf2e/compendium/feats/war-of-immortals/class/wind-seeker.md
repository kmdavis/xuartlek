# Wind Seeker  *Feat 8*

`Animist`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/war-of-immortals/class/walk-the-wilds|Walk the Wilds]]

Wings free you from the shackles of the ground below. You add [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/aerial-form|_aerial form_]] to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting. Whenever you use _aerial form_ to gain a form that grants you a specific [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to Acrobatics checks.

**Special** If you are attuned to an apparition of darkened boughs, add the bat and bird forms in aerial form to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/darkened-forest-form|_darkened forest form_]] lists.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 25*
