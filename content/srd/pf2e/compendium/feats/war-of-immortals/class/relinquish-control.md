# Relinquish Control  *Feat 1*  [ ](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Animist`  `Apparition`

- **Trigger**: Your turn begins.

Your apparition takes over and shields you from outside influence. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +4 status bonus on saves against spells and effects that give you the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Controlled|controlled]] condition or attempt to influence your actions (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/charm|_charm_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/command|_command_]], or a [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/monitor/nosoi|nosoi’s]] haunting melody). However, the only actions you can take are to [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/difficulty-classes#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast an apparition Spell]], Cast a vessel Spell, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] a vessel spell, or use an action that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/war-of-immortals/apparition|apparition]] trait. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on all Recall Knowledge checks made using Lore skills granted by your attuned apparitions.

**Special** This feat requires a particularly strong bond with a specific apparition to learn. Choose one apparition you have access to; once you learn this feat, you must always choose that apparition as one of the apparitions you attune to each day.
