# Through the Needle's Eye  *Feat 4*

`Exemplar`  `Ikon`

Your weapon strikes with the perfection your will demands. 
**Transcendence—Blinding of the Needle** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (transcendence) You aim your weapon in a superficial cut above your opponent’s eye. Make a Strike with the imbued ikon. If that Strike is successful, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round or until it uses an Interact action to clear the blood from its vision.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 36*
