# Medium's Awareness  *Feat 6*

`Animist`  `Apparition`  `Divine`  `Wandering`

Your apparitions watch over you. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Perception|Perception]] checks made to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Seek|Seek]] and when using Perception for your initiative roll. At 12th level, this status bonus increases to +3, and at 20th level, to +4.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 25*
