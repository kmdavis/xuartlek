# Energized Spark  *Feat 1*

`Exemplar`

The energy of your spirit manifests as crackling lightning, the chill of winter, or the power of an element. Choose one of the following traits: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Air|air]] (slashing), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Earth|earth]] (bludgeoning), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Metal|metal]] (slashing), [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Poison|poison]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Vitality|vitality]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Water|water]] (bludgeoning), or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Wood|wood]] (piercing). You can choose for any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Spirit|spirit]] damage dealt by your exemplar abilities to instead gain the trait and deal the corresponding damage type.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different damage type each time.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 35*
