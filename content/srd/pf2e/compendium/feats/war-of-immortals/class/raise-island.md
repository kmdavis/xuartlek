# Raise Island  *Feat 8*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Divine`  `Exemplar`

- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: born of the bones of the earth or restless as the tides

You churn the sea and fish up stone so your enemies together can receive their punishment. Each enemy in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be swept up to 15 feet to another location of your choice within the affected area and become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn. Then, if you’re on a surface, a pillar of earth rises underneath you and lasts for 1 minute. The pillar is 10 feet tall, and its surface includes your space and a 5-foot emanation around you. After the pillar rises, you can attempt a melee Strike against one enemy in reach.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 37*
