# Walk the Wilds  *Feat 4*

`Animist`

You know the ways of birds and beasts and have gained the right to wear their forms. You add _animal form_ to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting.

**Special** If you cast the _darkened forest form_ focus spell to turn into a form granted by animal form, you can Sustain it up to 5 minutes instead of its normal duration.

Wind Seeker
