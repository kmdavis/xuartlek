# Eyes Unclouded  *Feat 18*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: Ascended Celestial
- **Prerequisites**: Ascended Celestial Dedication

Your senses are enhanced by your divine spark, enabling you see things as they truly are, piercing through illusions, transformations, and lies. You’re affected by a constant _truesight_ spell. Additionally, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to Sense Motive and to Perception DCs against attempts to Lie to you. When a creature attempts to Lie to you, they roll the Deception check to Lie twice and use the worse result; this is a misfortune effect.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 116*
