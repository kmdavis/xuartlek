# Amorphous Aspect  *Feat 5*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: Proteankin

Your body is supremely flexible. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on checks to Escape or Squeeze, and when you critically fail while Squeezing, you get a failure instead.
