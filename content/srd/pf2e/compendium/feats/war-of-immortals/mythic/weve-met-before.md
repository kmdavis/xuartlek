# We've Met Before  *Feat 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Requirements**: You’ve spent at least 1 minute in mutual conversation with a creature.

Your ability to discern information about those you interact with is without peer. Spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]] to gain a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Lore]] skill specific to the required creature for the next 24 hours; you have mythic proficiency with that skill and can use it to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] about the creature, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Decipher Writing|Decipher Writings]] created by the creature, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Earn Income|Earn Income]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Subsist|Subsist]] in areas ruled by the creature, or perform other appropriate tasks as determined by the GM.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 81*
