# Mythic Refocus  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: You have a focus pool.

As your ability to control your mythic power grows, you learn to channel it to restore and reinforce other forms of power at your command. Spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]]; you immediately recover all your Focus Points, filling your focus pool to its current maximum.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 83*
