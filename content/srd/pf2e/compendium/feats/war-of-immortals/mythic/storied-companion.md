# Storied Companion  *Feat 2*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Fortune`  `Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: handler's calling; you have an animal companion or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/pet|pet]]
- **Trigger**: Your animal companion or pet would fail a saving throw.

Your companion is an integral part of your story, and you can expend your mythic power to protect it, defying events that would prevent it from being so. Spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]]; your companion rerolls the triggering save at mythic proficiency and gains temporary Hit Points equal to twice your level that last for 1 minute.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 81*
