# Feet that Stride the Sky  *Feat 2*

`Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: Acrobat's Calling

You feel the call of the sky, or perhaps a desperate urge to test the limits of the earth’s hold on you. When you or a creature you’re mounted on attempts a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#High Jump|High Jump]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Long Jump|Long Jump]], you can spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/index|Mythic Point]] to allow you or the mount to attempt the check at mythic proficiency; determine the DC using the Long Jump DCs, and increase your maximum distance to double your Speed.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 81*
