# Future Spell Learning  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Time Mage
- **Prerequisites**: Time Mage Dedication

More future memories of time magic percolate back to the present, teaching you spells you've yet to learn. Add _behold the weave_, _cast into time_, _haste_, _loose time's arrow_, _quicken time_, _slow_, and _stagnate time_ to your spell list. If you have a repository of spells, like a witch's familiar or a spellbook, add these spells to it. If you have a spell repertoire, when you gain this feat you can retrain one spell in it, replacing it with one of the added spells.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 185*
