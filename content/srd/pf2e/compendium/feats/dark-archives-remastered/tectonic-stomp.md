# Tectonic Stomp  *Feat 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Deviant`  `Magical`  `Rare`

You cause minor tremors that topple your enemies. All creatures in a 30-foot emanation who are touching the ground take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 2 levels you have, with a basic Reflex save. A creature that fails its save also falls prone.
