# Waking Dream  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Sleepwalker
- **Prerequisites**: Sleepwalker Dedication

While in your trance, you can blur the line between dreams and reality for others. You gain the _waking dream_ focus spell, which you can use only while you're in a Daydream Trance. You gain a pool of Focus Points with 1 Focus Point, and you can Refocus by taking a nap for 10 minutes. You become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics, and your spellcasting attribute for these spells is Wisdom.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 206*
