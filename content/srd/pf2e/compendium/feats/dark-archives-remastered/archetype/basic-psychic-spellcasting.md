# Basic Psychic Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

You gain the basic [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/archetypes#Spellcasting Archetypes|spellcasting benefits]]. When you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the psychic archetype, add a spell of the appropriate spell rank to your repertoire: a common occult spell, one of the granted spells from your conscious mind, or another spell you've learned or discovered.
