# Sleepwalker Dedication  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/sleepwalker|Sleepwalker]]
- **Prerequisites**: Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/occultism|Occultism]]

You've learned to manipulate states of consciousness, beginning with your own. You can send yourself into a half-awake, daydreaming state and gain the Daydream Trance action.

 **Daydream Trance** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]]) You fall into a half- sleeping trance. This lasts for 1 minute or until you fall unconscious, whichever comes first. You can attempt to Dismiss your trance, but your attempt fails unless you succeed at a Will saving throw against your own class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Once your trance ends, you can't enter a Daydream Trance again for 1 minute. While you're in your trance, you gain the following effects: 
You gain a +1 status bonus to Will saves. This bonus increases to +2 against mental effects. If you're legendary in Occultism, the bonus against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] effects increases to +3.You take a –1 penalty to Perception checks and initiative rolls.
