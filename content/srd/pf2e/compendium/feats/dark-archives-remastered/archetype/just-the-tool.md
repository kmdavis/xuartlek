# Just the Tool  *Feat 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mind-smith|Mind Smith]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/dark-archives-remastered/archetype/mind-smith-dedication|Mind Smith Dedication]]

You temporarily change your weapon's shape to assist you in the field. You morph your weapon into a single simple [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/tool-short|tool]], such as a shovel or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/crowbar-levered|crowbar]], to help with a mundane task. You can't replicate entire toolkits with this ability. You can use this action again to change your mind weapon back to a weapon.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 205*
