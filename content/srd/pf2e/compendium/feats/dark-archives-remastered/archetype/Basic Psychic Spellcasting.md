# Basic Psychic Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Psychic|Psychic]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/dark-archives-remastered/archetype/Psychic Dedication|Psychic Dedication]]

You gain the basic [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/Archetypes#Spellcasting Archetypes|spellcasting benefits]]. When you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the psychic archetype, add a spell of the appropriate spell rank to your repertoire: a common [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3) spell, one of the granted spells from your conscious mind, or another spell you've learned or discovered.
