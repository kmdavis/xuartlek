# Future Spell Learning  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/time-mage|Time Mage]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/dark-archives-remastered/archetype/time-mage-dedication|Time Mage Dedication]]

More future memories of time magic percolate back to the present, teaching you spells you've yet to learn. Add [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/behold-the-weave|_behold the weave_]], _cast into time_, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/haste|_haste_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/loose-times-arrow|_loose time's arrow_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/quicken-time|_quicken time_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/slow|_slow_]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/stagnate-time|_stagnate time_]] to your spell list. If you have a repository of spells, like a witch's familiar or a spellbook, add these spells to it. If you have a spell repertoire, when you gain this feat you can retrain one spell in it, replacing it with one of the added spells.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 185*
