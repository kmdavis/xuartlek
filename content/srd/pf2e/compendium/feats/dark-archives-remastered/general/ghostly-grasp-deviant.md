# Ghostly Grasp (Deviant)  *Feat 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Deviant`  `Magical`  `Rare`

An invisible arm extends from you to grab and manipulate objects. The arm reaches up to 15 feet, grabs an unattended object of 1 Bulk or less, and immediately deposits it in one of your free hands or at your feet. Alternatively, it reaches up to 15 feet and performs a simple [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] action like pushing open a window, though it can’t perform actions that require significant mechanical dexterity. For every 5 levels you have, the amount the hand can lift increases by 1 Bulk, and the arm’s range increases by 15 feet. Being ghostly, the arm can affect [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/ghost|ghosts]], spirits, and other [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] entities, though in most cases, you need an awakening for this to be useful.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 102*
