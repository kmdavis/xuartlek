# Awakened Power  *Feat 4*

`Deviant`  `Rare`

Your power spontaneously evolves or reconfigures itself. You gain one awakening benefit for a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/dark-archives-remastered/deviant|deviant]] feat of 4th level or lower.

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, choose a different deviant feat of 4th level or lower, and gain one of its awakening benefits.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 100*
