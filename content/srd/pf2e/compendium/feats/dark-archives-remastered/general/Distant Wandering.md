# Distant Wandering  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Deviant`  `Magical`  `Rare`

Your body falls unconscious and your spirit projects out of it. While in spirit form, you can’t consciously move your body, and you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] and inaudible, though a creature still might notice the signs of your presence and passing, just like for an invisible creature affected by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Silence|_silence_]], or a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/Scouting Eye|_scouting eye_]]. You can freely move about, though you can’t touch or move anything, cast spells, attack, or otherwise affect anything around you. Despite your ghostly form, you are not [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] and can’t pass through barriers you couldn’t in your body. Most effects can’t harm your spirit form, though some spells, like [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/Spirit Blast|_spirit blast_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/Spirit Song|_spirit song_]], explicitly damage a creature’s spirit.

You can return to your body as a free action. If you are in spirit form at the beginning of your turn, you must attempt another backlash check for your deviation, returning to your body if you fail.

**Awakening** Your spirit moves at the speed of thought. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] and a +20-foot status bonus to your Speed while in spirit form.

**Awakening** Time seems to pass more slowly while you are projecting your spirit. When you enter spirit form, you can [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/Difficulty Classes#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Seek|Seek]]. You are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Quickened|quickened]] while in spirit form and can use the extra action only to Recall Knowledge or Seek.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 103*
