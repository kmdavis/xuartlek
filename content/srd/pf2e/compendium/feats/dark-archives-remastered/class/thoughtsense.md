# Thoughtsense  *Feat 8*

`Mental`  `Psychic`

You can detect the flickers of mental activity let off by all thinking creatures. You gain thoughtsense as a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Vague Senses|vague sense]] that has a range of 30 feet—like all vague senses, it's only about as precise as an average human's sense of smell, meaning you generally can predict only if a creature is present. Your thoughtsense detects only thinking creatures, so a creature that is unthinking or otherwise immune to mental effects (such as many constructs and oozes) can't be perceived using your thoughtsense, regardless of how precise it is.

 While your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/dark-archives-remastered#Unleash Psyche|Psyche is Unleashed]], your thoughtsense upgrades to an [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]], letting you use it to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Seek|Seek]] out creatures.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 27*
