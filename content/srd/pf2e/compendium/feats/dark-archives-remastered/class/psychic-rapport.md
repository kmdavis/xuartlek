# Psychic Rapport  *Feat 1*

`Psychic`

Your telepathic sensitivity intensifies when you're affected by mental magic, making it easier for you to link to other creatures. After you Cast a Spell with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] trait, until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, and Intimidation checks, and to Perception checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sense Motive|Sense Motive]].
