# Cursed Effigy  *Feat 8*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Curse`  `Esoterica`  `Thaumaturge`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/dark-archives-remastered#Exploit Vulnerability|Exploit Vulnerability]]
- **Requirements**: Your last action was a successful Strike against the target of your Exploit Vulnerability, and the Strike dealt physical damage.

After your attack, you grab a bit of blood, cut hair, or other piece of the creature's body. You incorporate the material into a premade doll, paper figure, or other effigy to create a sympathetic link that makes it harder to resist your abilities. As long as you are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/dark-archives-remastered#Exploit Vulnerability|Exploiting Vulnerability]] against that creature, it takes a –2 status penalty to its saving throws against thaumaturge abilities or items that use your thaumaturge class DC.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 45*
