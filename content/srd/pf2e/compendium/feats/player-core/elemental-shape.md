# Elemental Shape  *Feat 10*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: Untamed Form

You understand the fundamental elements of nature such that you can imbue them into your body and manifest as a living embodiment of those elements. Add the shapes in _elemental form_ to your untamed form list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another shape using untamed form, you gain resistance 5 to fire.
