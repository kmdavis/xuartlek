# Untamed Form  *Feat 1*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: untamed order

You are one with the wild, always changing and adapting to meet any challenge. You gain the _untamed form_ order spell, which lets you transform into a variety of shapes that you can expand with druid feats.

### Untamed Form leads to...

Anthropomorphic Shape, Bizarre Transformation, Elemental Shape, Ferocious Shape, Form Control, Insect Shape, Monstrosity Shape, Plant Shape, Reactive Transformation, Soaring Shape, Too Much to Swallow, True Shapeshifter
