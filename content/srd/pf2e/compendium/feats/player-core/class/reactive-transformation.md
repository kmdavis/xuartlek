# Reactive Transformation  *Feat 14*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/untamed-form|Untamed Form]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/dragon-shape|Dragon Shape]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/elemental-shape|Elemental Shape]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/plant-shape|Plant Shape]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/soaring-shape|Soaring Shape]]
- **Trigger**: varies

You transform reflexively when in danger. You cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/untamed-form|_untamed form_]] to transform into one shape granted by one of the prerequisite feats you have, depending on the trigger. Your shape’s resistances and weaknesses apply against the triggering damage.
**Trigger** You fall 10 feet or more; **Effect** Choose a shape from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/aerial-form|_aerial form_]].**Trigger** You take bludgeoning or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]] damage; **Effect** Choose a primal dragon shape from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/dragon-form|_dragon form_]] that resists the triggering damage.**Trigger** You take fire damage; **Effect** Choose a fire elemental shape from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/elemental-form|_elemental form_]].**Trigger** You take poison damage; **Effect** Choose a shape from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/plant-form|_plant form_]].

*Source: Player Core p. 134*
