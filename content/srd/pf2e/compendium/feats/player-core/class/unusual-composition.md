# Unusual Composition  *Feat 10*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Bard`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Prerequisites**: polymath muse

You can translate the emotion and power of a composition to other mediums. If your next action is to cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/composition|composition]] spell that is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]], you can have it affect creatures who can witness it with any sense, not just those the traits indicate, even including [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Special Senses|special senses]] like [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Tremorsense|tremorsense]]. You can choose to exclude any senses you choose.
