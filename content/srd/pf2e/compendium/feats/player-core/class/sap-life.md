# Sap Life  *Feat 2*

`Cleric`  `Healing`

You draw the life force out of your enemies. When you cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/harm|_harm_]] spell and damage at least one living creature, you regain Hit Points equal to the spell rank of your harm spell. If you aren't a living creature, you gain no benefit from this feat.
