# Courageous Onslaught  *Feat 16*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Auditory`  `Bard`  `Concentrate`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/Courageous Advance|Courageous Advance]]; [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/Courageous Assault|Courageous Assault]]

You use your performance to orchestrate an onslaught against your enemies. If your next action is to cast the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/Courageous Anthem|_courageous anthem_]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Composition|composition]] cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]] and then make a melee [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]].
