# Spellbook Prodigy  *Feat 1*

`Wizard`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/arcana|Arcana]]

You are particularly adept at learning spells to add to your spellbook. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/magical-shorthand|Magical Shorthand]] skill feat without needing to meet its prerequisites. Further, when you roll a critical failure on your check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Learn a Spell|Learn a Spell]], you get a failure instead.

*Source: Player Core p. 201*
