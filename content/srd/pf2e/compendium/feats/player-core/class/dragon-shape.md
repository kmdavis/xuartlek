# Dragon Shape  *Feat 12*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/soaring-shape|Soaring Shape]]

You can take on the shape of some of the world's most fearsome creatures. Add the shapes listed in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/dragon-form|_dragon form_]] to your untamed form list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another form using [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/untamed-form|_untamed form_]], you gain resistance 5 to your choice of bludgeoning or poison.
