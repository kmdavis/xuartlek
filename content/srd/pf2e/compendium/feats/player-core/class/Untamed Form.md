# Untamed Form  *Feat 1*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: untamed order

You are one with the wild, always changing and adapting to meet any challenge. You gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/focus/Untamed Form|_untamed form_]] order spell, which lets you transform into a variety of shapes that you can expand with druid feats.
