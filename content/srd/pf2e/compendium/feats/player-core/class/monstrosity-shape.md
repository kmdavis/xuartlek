# Monstrosity Shape  *Feat 16*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/untamed-form|Untamed Form]]

You can transform into a powerful magical creature. Add the cave worm and sea serpent shapes listed in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/monstrosity-form|_monstrosity form_]] to your untamed form list. If you have [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/class/soaring-shape|Soaring Shape]], also add the phoenix shape to your untamed form list.

*Source: Player Core p. 135*
