# Animal Companion  *Feat 1*

`Druid`  `Ranger`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/Druid|Druid]] animal order

You gain the service of a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Animal Companions|young animal companion]] that travels with you on your adventures and obeys any simple commands you give it to the best of its abilities. See [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-3-classes/Companions#Animal Companions|Animal Companions]] for more information.

**Ranger**: When you Hunt Prey, your animal companion gains the action's benefits and your hunter's edge benefit if you have one.
