# Musetouched  *Feat 1*

`Lineage`  `Nephilim`

Your blood sings with the liberating power of the azatas, living embodiments of freedom from the wild realm of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Elysium|Elysium]]. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Escape. When you roll a critical failure on a check to Escape, you get a failure instead, and when you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
