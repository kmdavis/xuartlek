# Occult Resistance  *Feat 9*

`Changeling`

- **Prerequisites**: Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]]

Your study of occult practices has shown you how to best defend against them. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Occult|occult]] effects.
