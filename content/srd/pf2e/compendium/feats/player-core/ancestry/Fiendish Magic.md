# Fiendish Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/Grimspawn|Grimspawn]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/Pitborn|Pitborn]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/Hellspawn|Hellspawn]], or another lineage feat associated with fiends

You possess fiendish magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/Disguise Magic|_disguise magic_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/False Vitality|_false vitality_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Invisibility|_invisibility_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Shatter|_shatter_]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Paranoia|_paranoia_]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells. Grimspawn typically take _false vitality_ and _see the unseen_, pitborn typically take _paranoia_ and _shatter_, and hellspawn typically take _invisibility_ and _disguise_.

*Source: Player Core p. 81*
