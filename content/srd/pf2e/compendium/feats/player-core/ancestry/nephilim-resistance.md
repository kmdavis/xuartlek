# Nephilim Resistance  *Feat 5*

`Nephilim`

You resist energy like your extraplanar predecessors. Choose one of the following energy damage types: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]]. You gain resistance 5 to that damage type. The damage type typically matches an extraplanar entity associated with your bloodline. For instance, an angelkin might choose resistance to cold, while a hellspawn might choose resistance to fire.
