# Callow May  *Feat 1*

`Changeling`  `Lineage`

You are among the most common of changelings—those born to sweet hags—and a vibrant green eye signifies that parentage. Your mother’s manipulative nature and versatile voice gives you an edge when deceiving others. You gain the Charming Liar skill feat. If you roll [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] for initiative, foes in that combat encounter that haven’t acted yet are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] against you.
