# Celestial Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/angelkin|Angelkin]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/lawbringer|Lawbringer]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/musetouched|Musetouched]], or or another lineage feat associated with [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/celestial|celestials]]

You possess celestial magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/clear-mind|_clear mind_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/everlight|_everlight_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/humanoid-form|_humanoid form_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/revealing-light|_revealing light_]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/share-life|_share life_]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/sure-footing|_sure footing_]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells. Angelkin typically take _clear mind_ and _humanoid form_, lawbringers typically have _everlight_ and _share life_, and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/ancestry/musetouched|musetouched]] typically have _revealing light_ and _sure footing_.

*Source: Player Core p. 80*
