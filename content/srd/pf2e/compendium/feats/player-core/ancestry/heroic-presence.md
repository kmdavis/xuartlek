# Heroic Presence  *Feat 17*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Emotion`  `Human`  `Mental`

- **Frequency**: once per day

The blood of heroes courses through your veins, and you inspire your allies to dig deep and find a new level of resolve. You grant up to 10 willing creatures within 30 feet the effects of a 6th-rank zealous conviction, though the effect automatically ends on a target if you give that target a command they would normally find repugnant. This action has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] trait or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]] trait, depending on how you inspire your allies.
