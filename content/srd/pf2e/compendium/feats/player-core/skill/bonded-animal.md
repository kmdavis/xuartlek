# Bonded Animal  *Feat 2*

`Downtime`  `General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/nature|Nature]]

You forge strong connections with animals. You can spend 7 days of downtime regularly interacting with a normal animal (not a companion or other special animal) that is friendly or helpful to you. After this duration, attempt a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/nature|Nature]] check against the animal's Will DC. If successful, you bond with the animal. The animal is permanently bonded to you until you form a bond with a different animal or do something egregious to break your bond.

A helpful animal is easier to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Command an Animal|direct]]. If your bonded animal is level –1, it gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/minion|minion]] trait, allowing you to command it more efficiently. The maximum level of creature that can be your minion increases to 3 if you're a master in Nature and to 11 if you're legendary.

**Special** You can’t have both a bonded animal and an animal companion (though you can have both a bonded animal and a pet or familiar).
