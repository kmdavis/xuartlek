# Intimidating Prowess  *Feat 2*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Strength +3; Expert in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]]

In situations where you can physically menace the target when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Coerce|Coerce]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Demoralize|Demoralize]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Intimidation check and you ignore the penalty for not sharing a language. If your Strength modifier is +5 or higher and you are a master in Intimidation, this bonus increases to +2.

*Source: Player Core p. 257*
