# Legendary Professional  *Feat 15*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Lore]]

Your fame has spread throughout the lands (for instance, if you have Warfare Lore, you might be a legendary general or tactician). This works as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/legendary-performer|Legendary Performer]], except you gain higher-level jobs when you Earn Income with Lore.

*Source: Player Core p. 258*
