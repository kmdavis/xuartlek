# Fascinating Performance  *Feat 1*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/performance|Performance]]

When you Perform, compare your result to the Will DC of one observer. If you succeed, the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Fascinated|fascinated]] by you for 1 round. If the observer is in a situation that demands immediate attention, such as combat, you must critically succeed to fascinate it and the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Perform|Perform]] action gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait. You must choose which creature you're trying to fascinate before you roll your check, and the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.

If you're an expert in Performance, you can fascinate up to four observers; if you're a master, you can fascinate up to 10 observers; and if you're legendary, you can fascinate any number of observers at the same time.
