# Quick Swim  *Feat 7*

`General`  `Skill`

- **Prerequisites**: Master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]

You [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] 5 feet farther on a success and 10 feet farther on a critical success, to a maximum of your Speed. If you’re legendary in Athletics, you gain a swim Speed equal to your Speed.
