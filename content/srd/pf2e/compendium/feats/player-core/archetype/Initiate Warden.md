# Initiate Warden  *Feat 1*

`Ranger`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/Beastmaster|Beastmaster]] (Level 4)

You’ve trained with one of the ranger sects known as wardens, who practice a specialized type of primal magic. You gain your choice of one warden spell from the initial [warden spells](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?Trait=136) (or another you have access to).

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times, choosing a different initial warden spell each time.
