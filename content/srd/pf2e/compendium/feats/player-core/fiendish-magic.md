# Fiendish Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: Grimspawn, Pitborn, or Hellspawn, or another lineage feat associated with fiends

You possess fiendish magic. Choose two of the following spells: _disguise magic_, _false vitality_, _invisibility_, _see the unseen_, _shatter_, or _paranoia_. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells. Grimspawn typically take _false vitality_ and _see the unseen_, pitborn typically take _paranoia_ and _shatter_, and hellspawn typically take _invisibility_ and _disguise_.

*Source: Player Core p. 81*
