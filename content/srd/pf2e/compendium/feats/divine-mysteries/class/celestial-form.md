# Celestial Form  *Feat 18*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/champion|Champion]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]]

You take on a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/celestial|celestial]] countenance, gaining the features of a type of celestial who serves your deity; for example, as an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/angel|angel]], you gain a halo and feathery wings. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you don’t already have it, and you gain the celestial trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor you’ve become ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/archon|archon]], angel, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/azata|azata]], for example).

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 277*
