# Sense Holiness  *Feat 8*

`Champion`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/champion|Champion]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]]

The presence of goodness sickens you with its saccharine self-righteousness. You can sense the presence of creatures with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] trait within 120 feet. This functions as a vague sense, similar to humans’ sense of smell. A holy creature using a disguise or otherwise trying to hide its presence attempts a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] check against your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/playing-the-game#Perception|Perception]] DC to hide its sanctification from you. If the creature succeeds at its Deception check, it is then temporarily immune to your Sense Holiness for 1 day.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 277*
