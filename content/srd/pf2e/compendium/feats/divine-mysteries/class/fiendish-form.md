# Fiendish Form  *Feat 18*

`Champion`  `Uncommon`

- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/champion|Champion]] [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]]

Your features reshape into those of a type of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiend]] who serves your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Deity|deity]]; for example, as a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/demon|demon]] you might have putrid scales, twisted horns, and red eyes. You gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you don’t already have it, and you gain the fiend trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor you’ve become (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/daemon|daemon]], demon, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/devil|devil]]).

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 277*
