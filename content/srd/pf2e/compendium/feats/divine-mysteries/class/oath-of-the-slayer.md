# Oath of The Slayer  *Feat 2*

`Champion`  `Oath`

During your [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/running-exploration#Daily Preparations|daily preparations]], you can swear an oath to defeat, topple, or destroy a certain kind of enemy during your deeds that day. Choose [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/aberration|aberrations]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/celestial|celestials]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/dragon|dragons]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiends]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]]. You can’t choose celestials if you’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], nor can you choose fiends if you’re [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]]. You gain an edict to banish or slay creatures of that kind.

Your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] and devotion spells that deal damage do an additional 1 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage against a creature with the chosen trait. This damage increases to 2 at 7th level and 3 at 14th level. If a creature with the chosen trait triggers your champion’s reaction, this additional damage doubles until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 276*
