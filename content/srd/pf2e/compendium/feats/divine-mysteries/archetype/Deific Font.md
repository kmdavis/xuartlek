# Deific Font  *Feat 18*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Divine`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mortal-herald|Mortal Herald]]
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/mortal-herald-dedication|Mortal Herald Dedication]]

Your life force can fuel the stuff of miracles. You become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Drained|drained]] 1 or increase the value of your drained condition by 1 if you already have the drained condition. Before the end of your next turn, if you [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-5-treasure-trove/activating-items#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] of 4th rank or lower, you don’t expend the spell’s slot when you cast it, or if you cast a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/focus|focus]] spell, it does not cost a Focus Point. The spell loses whatever tradition trait it would normally have and gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]]

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 281*
