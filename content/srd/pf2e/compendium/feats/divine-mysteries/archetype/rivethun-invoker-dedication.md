# Rivethun Invoker Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Uncommon`

- **PFS Note**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/faiths-philosophies/rivethun|Rivethun]] is a philosophy which characters may choose as their religion. Doing so fulfills the “follower of Rivethun” access condition.
- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/rivethun-invoker|Rivethun Invoker]]
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]]; Trained in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/religion|Religion]]
- **Access**: Followers of Rivethun have access to this feat.

You’re a practicing Rivethun invoker, able to enter a trance that connects you physically and mentally to surrounding spirits. You become an expert in Athletics and Religion. You also gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/general/diehard|Diehard]] general feat and the Enter Spirit Trance ability. 

**Enter Spirit Trance** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]]) You enter a self-imposed trance that helps you push the physical limits of your body. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level plus your Constitution modifier. The trance lasts for 1 minute, until you fall [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]], or until you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] it, whichever comes first. While in this trance, you gain a +1 status bonus to Fortitude and Will saving throws and your melee Strikes deal 1 additional [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage. Other abilities may require you to be in a spirit trance. When the trace ends, you lose any remaining [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] from Enter Spirit Trance, and you can’t Enter a Spirit Trance for 1 minute.
