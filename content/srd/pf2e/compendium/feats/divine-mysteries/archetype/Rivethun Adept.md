# Rivethun Adept  *Feat 16*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/rivethun-emissary|Rivethun Emissary]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/rivethun-devotion|Rivethun Devotion]]

Your ability to wield spirit magic has increased. You can cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/speak-with-stones|_speak with stones_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/spiritual-guardian|_spiritual guardian_]] as a 5th-rank divine innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day. If you’re legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/religion|Religion]], _spiritual guardian_ is heightened to 7th rank.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 293*
