# Living God  *Feat 20*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/razmiran-priest|Razmiran Priest]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/razmiran-priest-dedication|Razmiran Priest Dedication]]; Legendary in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

You have reached heights of power equaling [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/other-gods/razmir|Razmir’s]] himself. You can [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/difficulty-classes#Craft|Craft]] your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/razmiri-mask|Razmiri mask]] out of porcelain laced with precious metals, or upgrade your existing mask to a porcelain one in the same time it takes to Craft one made of iron. A porcelain Razmiri mask grants a +4 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Lie|Lie]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]], is an 18th-level item, and is an apex item that increases your Charisma modifier by 1 or increases it to +4, whichever would give you a higher score. It also gives you the following activation in addition to those granted by the standard, silver, and gold masks.

**Activate—Power of the Living God** [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You demand power from the world, using your mask as a locus to force reality to bend to your will. You cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-10/manifestation|_manifestation_]] as a 10th-rank occult spell, but no matter what spell you emulate with it, that spell has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 291*
