# Marked For Rebuke  *Feat 18*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mortal-herald|Mortal Herald]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/mortal-herald-dedication|Mortal Herald Dedication]]

You declare that an enemy is fit for further punishment in the sight of your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-1-introduction/character-creation#Deity|deity]]. If the next action you perform is to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]] that deals damage, one creature of your choice that takes damage from the spell is marked for rebuke until the end of your next turn. A creature marked this way gains [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|weakness]] 10 to all damage and becomes immune to Marked for Rebuke for 24 hours.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 282*
