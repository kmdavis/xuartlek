# Mask of The 15th Step  *Feat 14*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/razmiran-priest|Razmiran Priest]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/razmiran-priest-dedication|Razmiran Priest Dedication]]; Master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/crafting|Crafting]]

You have ascended to the 15th Step and stand among the greatest of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/other-gods/razmir|Razmir’s]] priests. You can [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/difficulty-classes#Craft|Craft]] your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/worn-items/razmiri-mask|Razmiri mask]] out of gold or upgrade your existing mask to a gold one in the same time it takes to Craft one made of iron. A gold Razmiri mask grants a +3 item bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/deception|Deception]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Lie|Lie]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Feint|Feint]], is a 14th-level item, and gains the following activation in addition to those possessed by the standard and silver masks. 

**Activate—Call Upon Razmir’s Wrath** [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/occult|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cry out to Razmir to reveal his fiery wrath, as he did upon the unbelievers at Melcat. You cast [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/sunburst|_sunburst_]] as an 8th-rank occult spell, but for undead targets the spell has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 291*
