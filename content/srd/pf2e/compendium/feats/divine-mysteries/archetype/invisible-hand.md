# Invisible Hand  *Feat 16*

`Archetype`  `Mythic`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mortal-herald|Mortal Herald]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/mortal-herald-dedication|Mortal Herald Dedication]]; Master in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]]

Sometimes your deity requires you to act in the shadows, accomplishing things without drawing attention to yourself— or them. Once per day, when you roll [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] for initiative at the start of a combat, you can choose to be [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] for 1 minute. If you spend a [[srd/pf2e/books/war-of-immortals/mythic-rules/mythic-characters#Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] while beginning the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Avoid Notice|Avoid Notice]] exploration activity, you are invisible for as long as you continue to Avoid Notice.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 281*
