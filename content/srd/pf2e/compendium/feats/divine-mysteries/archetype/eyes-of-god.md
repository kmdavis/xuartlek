# Eyes of God  *Feat 18*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/archetypes/mortal-herald|Mortal Herald]]
- **Prerequisites**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/divine-mysteries/archetype/mortal-herald-dedication|Mortal Herald Dedication]]

You can see things as they truly are. You are constantly under the effect of 8th-rank [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/see-the-unseen|_see the unseen_]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/truesight|_truesight_]] spells. Additionally, you gain a +4 status bonus to your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and to your [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Perception|Perception]] DC against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Lie|Lies]].

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 282*
