# Polymorph Trap  *Hazard 12*

`Magical`  `Trap`

- **Complexity**: Simple A Druidic glyph attempts to transform a trespasser into an animal.

**Stealth**
DC 34 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 32 Thievery (master) to drain the glyph’s power harmlessly, or _dispel magic_ (6th rank; counteract DC 30) to counteract the glyph

**Cursed Metamorphosis** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (primal) **Trigger** A creature moves within 30 feet of the glyph without speaking the passphrase in Druidic; **Effect** The creature is targeted by _cursed metamorphosis_ (DC 32 Will save).
