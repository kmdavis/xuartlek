# Polymorph Trap  *Hazard 12*

`Magical`  `Trap`

- **Complexity**: Simple A Druidic glyph attempts to transform a trespasser into an animal.

**Stealth**
DC 34 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/thievery|Thievery]] (master) to drain the glyph’s power harmlessly, or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/dispel-magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th rank; [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] DC 30) to counteract the glyph

**Cursed Metamorphosis** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/primal|primal]]) **Trigger** A creature moves within 30 feet of the glyph without speaking the passphrase in Druidic; **Effect** The creature is targeted by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/cursed-metamorphosis|_cursed metamorphosis_]] (DC 32 Will save).
