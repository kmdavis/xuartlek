# Armageddon Orb  *Hazard 23*

`Magical`  `Rare`  `Trap`

- **Complexity**: Simple A roiling red orb, forged from a drop of the god Rovagug’s blood, rains fire from the sky when a specified condition is met.

**Stealth**
DC 10 or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/detect-magic|_detect magic_]]

**Disable**
DC 48 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/thievery|Thievery]] (legendary) to imbue a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/thieves-toolkit-infiltrator-picks|thieves’ toolkit]] with aspects representing Asmodeus and Sarenrae and use them to drain away the orb’s power over 10 minutes; the character attempting this check takes 5 fire damage each round until the orb is depleted

**Burn It All** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/death|death]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/divine|divine]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]]) **Trigger** A special condition set by the trap’s creator occurs, typically the event of their death; **Effect** Fire rains from the sky in a 100-mile radius, dealing 10d6 fire damage to creatures and objects in the area. Each creature or object can attempt a DC 46 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/defenses#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save. Any creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage dies instantly. This is not enough damage to completely burn away a forest or level an entire mountain or city, but it typically kills most creatures in the area.

*Source: GM Core p. 100*
