# Vorpal Executioner  *Hazard 19*

`Mechanical`  `Trap`

- **Complexity**: Simple A wickedly sharp saw blade descends and travels along grooves in a complex path throughout the room, attempting to decapitate everyone within.

**Stealth**
DC 43 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 41 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/thievery|Thievery]] (expert) at four different junctions to jam all the saw blade’s possible paths, preventing it from traveling through the room

**AC** 43, **Fort** +32, **Ref** +32

**Hardness**
30

**HP**
120 (BT 60) per junction

**Immunities**
[[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/weapons#Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/item-damage#Object Immunities|object immunities]], precision damage

**Total Decapitation** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") **Trigger** A creature attempts to exit the room; **Effect** The saw blade travels along its path, making one [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] against each creature in the room, twisting and varying its height for a maximum chance of beheading its targets.

**Melee** saw blade +40 ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/deadly|deadly d12]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/death|death]]), **Damage** 6d12+25 slashing plus decapitation; no multiple attack penalty 

**Decapitation** On a critical hit, a target must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or be decapitated, dying instantly unless it can survive without a head

---
**Reset**
The trap resets over the course of the round and can be triggered again 1 round later.
