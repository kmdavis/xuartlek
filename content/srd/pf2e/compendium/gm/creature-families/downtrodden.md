### Braving The Mob

A mob is a [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/environment#Crowds|violent crowd]] that works as greater difficult terrain and hazardous terrain, dealing 1d4 damage for each square entered. A creature can spend a single action to attempt a DC 16 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/intimidation|Intimidation]] check, clearing the mob from all squares adjacent to them on a success (or all squares within 10 feet on a critical success). This action has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/emotion|emotion]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] traits. If the mob is meant to be a combat encounter, you can instead use the [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/deluded-mob|deluded mob]].
