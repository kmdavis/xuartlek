# Frog  *Creature Family*

Scholars know of the adaptability of these creatures, but still they surprise.

### Members

[[srd/pf2e/bestiary/howl-of-the-wild/animal/Concert Frog|Concert Frog]] (Creature 8), Giant Frog (Creature 1), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/animal/Giant Frog|Giant Frog]] (Creature 1), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/animal/Spear Frog|Spear Frog]] (Creature 0), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/howl-of-the-wild/animal/Sporeback Frog|Sporeback Frog]] (Creature 5)

### Frog Species

While all frogs are amphibious, some frogs spend more of their time out of the water. Even those usually found near lakes and streams are adapted for climbing. You can add variety to frog encounters by replacing a frog’s [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Swim Speed|swim Speed]] with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Climb Speed|climb Speed]] (or even allowing it to have both). Likewise, for frogs that are poisonous or venomous, the specific effects of their toxin can vary by species, with exposure to some frog poison causing a creature to become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] instead of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]].
