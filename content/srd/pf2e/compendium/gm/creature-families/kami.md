# Kami  *Creature Family*

Kami are divine nature spirits from the lands of Tian Xia, far to the east of the Inner Sea region. They serve as guardians of natural objects and places they protect—their wards—and are ancient enemies of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/oni|oni]]. Kami can merge with their wards, allowing them to surreptitiously watch anyone who treads upon their sacred grounds. Kami leave those who they deem harmless alone, but fight vigilantly to scare away anyone perceived as a threat.

### Members

[[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/spirit/kodama|Kodama]] (Creature 5), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/spirit/sakugami|Sakugami]] (Creature 15), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/spirit/shikigami|Shikigami]] (Creature 1), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core-2/spirit/zuishin|Zuishin]] (Creature 10)

### Kami Allies

Kami are friends to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/arboreal|arboreals]], [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/fey/dryad|dryads]], and other beings with spiritual ties to the natural world. Among mortals, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/classes/druid|druids]] are the most common recipients of kami aid, though these spirits might help anyone whose mission involves the protection of their particular ward.

### Kami Worship

Although kami aren't gods, people sometimes worship or pray at a tree or location where a kami is known to reside. Kami regard this behavior as a curious affectation of mortals, and they make no attempts to dissuade or encourage such reverential treatment as long as it doesn't threaten the safety of their wards.
