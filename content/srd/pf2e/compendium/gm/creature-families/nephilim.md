# Nephilim  *Creature Family*

Nephilim are planar scions with a connection to the planes of the Outer Sphere. Some are obviously tied to realms such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Heaven|Heaven]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Hell|Hell]], while others are cryptic amalgams of metaphysical traits.
