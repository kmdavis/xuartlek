# Wardens of the Wild  *Creature Family*

_Nethys Note: No description was provided for this group of creatures. All Wardens of the Wild share the following two abilities:_

**Voice of Nature** Though the Wardens of the Wild do not speak in words, they can communicate complex concepts flawlessly and wordlessly with any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/animal|animal]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/beast|beast]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/plant|plant]], or other creature of the natural world through prolonged eye contact. Sapient creatures with strong ties to the natural world, such as animal instinct barbarians or druids who speak the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Wildsong|Wildsong]], can somewhat understand a Warden of the Wild, though the meaning can be vague.

 **Warden's Crown** A Warden of the Wild's horned crown commands respect from wild creatures. Wild creatures native to a warden's biome automatically improve their attitude toward it by one step (up to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Friendly|friendly]]) and typically do not take hostile actions towards each other while in the warden's presence.
