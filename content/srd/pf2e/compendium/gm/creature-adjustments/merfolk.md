# Merfolk  *Creature Adjustment*

Merfolk have humanlike torsos, fish tails, and fins. 
Replace the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/human|human]] trait with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/monster-core/merfolk|merfolk]] trait and add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/amphibious|amphibious]] trait.Add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Thalassic|Thalassic]] language.Change Speed to 5 feet with a 25-foot swim Speed. You can add a supramarine chair item to give the merfolk a land Speed equal to their swim Speed. Use the rules for a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/assistive-items/wheelchair-land-delvers-chair|wheelchair]].Add the following abilities. **Low-Light Vision**
