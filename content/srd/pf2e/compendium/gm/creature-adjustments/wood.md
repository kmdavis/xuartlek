# Wood  *Creature Adjustment*

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of wood, you can use the following adjustments. 
Add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Wood|wood]] trait. If the creature is a growing plant, you can also add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Plant|plant]] trait.If it has any languages, add [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Languages#Muan|Muan]].Increase the creature's HP based on its level.Add weaknesses to fire and to damage from [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Weapon Groups#Axe|axes]] depending on its level.If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with wood spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/Timber|_timber_]], **1st** _protector tree_, **2nd** _oaken resilience_, **3rd** _wall of thorns_, **4th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/Life-Draining Roots|_life-draining roots_]], **5th** _nature's pathway_, **6th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/Lignify|_lignify_]], **7th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/Pollen Pods|_pollen pods_]], **8th** _burning blossoms_, **9th** _one with the land_.

| **Level** | **HP Increase** | **Weakness to Fire and Axes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 or lower | 10 | 3 |
| 4–8 | 20 | 5 |
| 9–13 | 40 | 10 |
| 14+ | 60 | 15 |

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 193*
