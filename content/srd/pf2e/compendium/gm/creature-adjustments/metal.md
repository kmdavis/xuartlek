# Metal  *Creature Adjustment*

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of metal, you can use the following adjustments. 
Add the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/metal|metal]] trait.If it has any languages, add [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/languages#Talican|Talican]].Decrease the creature's HP based on its level.Add resistance to electricity depending on its level.If the creature has metal spikes or blades, you can give its physical Strikes your choice of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/versatile|versatile]] P or versatile S.If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with metal spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/cantrips/needle-darts|_needle darts_]], **1st** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/conductive-weapon|_conductive weapon_]], **2nd** _magnetic repulsion_, **3rd** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-3/noxious-metals|_noxious metals_]], **4th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-4/rust-cloud|_rust cloud_]], **5th** _impaling spike_, **6th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-6/field-of-razors|_field of razors_]], **7th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-7/beheading-buzz-saw|_beheading buzz saw_]], **8th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/ferrous-form|_ferrous form_]], **9th** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-9/magnetic-dominion|_magnetic dominion_]].
