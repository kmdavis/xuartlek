# Moonburst  *Spell 7*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cold`  `Concentrate`  `Light`  `Manipulate`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Traditions**: Divine, Primal
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Area**: 60-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]
- **Defense**: Reflex

A powerful globe of chilling moonlight explodes in the area, dealing 8d10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]] damage to all creatures in the area, plus an additional 8d10 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] damage to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] creatures. Moonburst’s cold damage is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/materials/silver|silver]] damage for the purposes of [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|weaknesses, resistances, and the like]]. Each creature and object in the area must attempt a Reflex save. 

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes {{conditions 59 "blinded"}} permanently.

If the globe overlaps with an area of magical [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Darkness|darkness]], _moonburst_ attempts to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/counteracting|counteract]] the darkness effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]] damage increases by 1d10, and the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] damage against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]] increases by 1d10.
