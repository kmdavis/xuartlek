# Angel Form  *Spell 7*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Holy`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Invoking the celestial realms, you transform into a Medium angel battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose balisse, choral, kuribu, or tennin. The battle form is Small if you choose choral or kuribu. While in this form, you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/angel|angel]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/celestial|celestial]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use manipulate actions. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] the spell. You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose: 
AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction40 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Weakness|weakness]] 10 to unholy.Darkvision.One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and your damage bonus is +12. Melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example), and ranged attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] modifier of +25, unless your own modifier is higher.You also gain specific abilities based on the type of angel you choose:

- **Balisse** Speed 30 feet, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly]] 40 feet; Perception modifier of +26 to detect lies and illusions unless your own modifier is higher; **Melee** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") scimitar ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/forceful|forceful]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sweep|sweep]]), **Damage** 2d6 slashing plus 2d6 fire.
- **Choral** Speed 30 feet, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly]] 40 feet; +1 status bonus to AC and saves against auditory and sonic; **Melee** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") fist ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/agile|agile]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unarmed|unarmed]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning; **Ranged** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") piercing hymn (holy, magical, range 90 feet), **Damage** 3d6 sonic and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round on a critical hit.
- **Kuribu** Speed 25 feet, [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly]] 40 feet; [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 10 to electricity and fire; **Melee** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") fist ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/agile|agile]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unarmed|unarmed]]), **Damage** 3d4 bludgeoning; **Ranged** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") composite shortbow ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/deadly|deadly 2d10]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/propulsive|propulsive]], range increment 60 feet), **Damage** 3d6 piercing.
