# Circle of Weakness  *Focus 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Wizard`

- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

Winning magical combat often requires exploiting weakness. You evoke a circle of floating inscriptions that creates and amplifies energy-based weaknesses on creatures inside the circle. Choose one type of damage from the following list: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]]. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] it each turn, each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature gains weakness 1 to the chosen damage type for 1 round.

**Failure** As success, except the weakness is doubled.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round or until they [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The weakness gained on a success increases by 1.
