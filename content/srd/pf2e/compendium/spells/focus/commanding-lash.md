# Commanding Lash  *Focus 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Linguistic`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Tyranny|Tyranny]]
- **Requirements**: Your most recent action dealt damage to a target
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Target**: a creature you dealt damage to on your most recent action
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: until the end of the target's next turn

With the threat of more pain, you compel a creature you've recently harmed. You issue a command to the target, with the effects of the spell [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/command|_command_]]. If the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]], or taking [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent damage]], it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the save. Regardless of the result, the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.

*Source: Player Core p. 380*
