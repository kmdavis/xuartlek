# Veil of Confidence  *Focus 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Mental`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Confidence|Confidence]]
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You surround yourself in confidence. You reduce your current [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition by 1, and whenever you would become frightened during the duration, reduce the amount by 1. If you critically fail a save against a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]] effect, _veil of confidence_ reduces your frightened value from that effect, and then ends.

*Source: Player Core p. 373*
