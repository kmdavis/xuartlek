# Divine Plagues  *Focus 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Disease`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Plague|Plague]]
- **Range**: 15 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude

Your deity sends wracking plagues. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast the Spell]], decide whether the disease inflicts the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] condition.

**Success** The target is unaffected. 
**Failure** The target is afflicted with stage 1 divine plague. 
**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with stage 2 divine plague. 

**Divine Plague** (disease) **Level 1**; Stage 1 clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed]] 1 and clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 and the target can’t recover from the condition until they are cured (1 day)

---
**Heightened (5th)** The divine plague disease gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/virulent|virulent]] trait. The condition value for the clumsy, enfeebled, or stupefied condition is 2 instead of 1.
