# Redact  *Focus 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Glyph|Glyph]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: unattended written materials of up to 1 Bulk
- **Duration**: 1 day

You strike mention of a specific name from one or more documents. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast the Spell]], give one proper name, be it a creature, location, or object. Any mention of that name within the document becomes blurred, smudged, or otherwise completely illegible. Epithets and unambiguous references to the name are redacted as well; for instance, redacting the name of the goddess [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/gods-of-the-inner-sea/sarenrae|Sarenrae]] from a book would also remove references to her title “the Dawnflower.” Attempts to use this reference material to [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-1-running-the-game/difficulty-classes#Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge]] about the given proper name automatically fail.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** Instead of striking out a given name, you can replace it with a different name you choose. Epithets and references are substituted with similar ones relating to the replacement name. Anyone reading the text can attempt a Perception or Society check against your spell DC to notice the altered text, though that doesn’t tell them what the original said.

**Heightened (4th)** At your choice, the duration is unlimited.
