# Embrace the Pit  *Focus 3*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Morph`  `Sorcerer`  `Uncommon`

- **Bloodline**: Diabolic
- **Duration**: 1 minute
