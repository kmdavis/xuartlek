# Arcane Explosion  *Focus 9*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Force`  `Manipulate`  `Mythic`  `Uncommon`  `Focus`

- **Area**: 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]]
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude

Your body is temporarily converted into a ball of pure magic, rapidly exploding outward and reforming as a glowing sphere, after which you eventually regain your normal form. All creatures and objects in the emanation take 16d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]] damage ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude saving throw). Creatures that fail are also pushed 10 feet directly away from you; creatures that can't complete this forced movement are knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]] (but creatures who reduce this movement, such as with an ability or feat, don't fall prone). You become unfiltered magic; you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] trait, are immune to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|disease]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]], and precision damage, and gain resistance to all damage equal to your level (except force damage, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]] damage, and damage from Strikes with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|ghost touch property rune]]), with double the resistance against non-[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]] damage. While incorporeal, you can't Strike, Cast a Spell, or take any actions with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] trait. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you while you're in this state takes 6d6 force damage. You remain as pure magic until you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] your new form, returning to your normal state at maximum Hit Points.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 141*
