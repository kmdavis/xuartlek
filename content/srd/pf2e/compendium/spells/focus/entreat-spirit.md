# Entreat Spirit  *Focus 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Requirements**: You must be Bonded with a Spirit
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You invite a spirit you’ve bonded with to bolster your mind. You gain a +1 status bonus to an Intelligence-, Wisdom-, or Charisma-based skill of your choice. Additionally, you gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/curse|curse]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/death|death]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/emotion|emotion]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fear|fear]] effects.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The status bonus to the skill and your saving throws increases to +2.

**Heightened (8th)** The status bonus to the skill and your saving throws increases to +3.
