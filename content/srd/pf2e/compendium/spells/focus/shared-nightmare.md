# Shared Nightmare  *Focus 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Emotion`  `Focus`  `Incapacitation`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Nightmares|Nightmares]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: varies

Merging minds with the target, you swap distressing visions. One of you will become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Confused|confused]], based on the target's Will save.

**Critical Success** You are confused for 1 round.

**Success** At the start of your next turn, you spend your first action with the confused condition, then act normally.

**Failure** The target spends the first action of each of its turns confused. The duration is 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is confused. The duration is 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core p. 377*
