# Adapt Self  *Focus 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Morph`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Change|Change]]
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You make subtle changes to your body to adapt to the situation. Choose one of the following options that applies to you. 
If you are in water, you gain a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Swim Speed|swim Speed]] equal to half your Speed.If you are in water, you become able to breathe water.If it’s too dark for you to see, you gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/perception-and-detection#Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].If you are in an environment of [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/environment#Temperature|severe heat]] or severe cold, you are protected from the effects of severe heat or severe cold, and the spell lasts 10 minutes.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 262*
