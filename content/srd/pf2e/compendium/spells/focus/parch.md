# Parch  *Focus 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Air`  `Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Dust|Dust]]
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies

Dry winds dehydrate a creature. The target must attempt a Fortitude save; if used on a creature that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]] trait or a creature made primarily of liquid (such as many [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/ooze|oozes]]), the creature uses the outcome for one degree of success worse than the result. Creatures whose bodies contain no significant moisture (such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/elemental-earth|earth elementals]]) are immune to parch. If a creature affected by _parch_ drinks a liquid, such as a swig of water or a potion, the spell ends. Affected creatures that have the water trait or are made primarily of liquid, as well as affected creatures that can’t drink, can end the effect as a two-action activity, which has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]] trait.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes a –1 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 round.

**Failure** The target takes a –1 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target takes a –2 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 minute and is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] as long as it has this penalty.
