# Darkened Forest Form  *Focus 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Animist`  `Focus`  `Polymorph`  `Uncommon`

- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

Your apparition casts a feral shadow over your form. You can polymorph into any form listed in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/pest-form|_pest form_]]. When you transform into a form granted by a spell, you gain all the effects of the form you chose from a version of the spell heightened to darkened forest form's rank. Each time you Sustain this Spell, you can choose to change to a different shape from those available via any of the associated spells.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** You can also transform into the forms listed in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/animal-form|_animal form_]].

**Heightened (5th)** You can also transform into the forms listed in [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-5/elemental-form|_elemental form_]].

*Source: War of Immortals p. 20*
