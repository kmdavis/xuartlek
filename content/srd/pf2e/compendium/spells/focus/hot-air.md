# Hot Air  *Focus 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Fire`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Wizard`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude
- **Duration**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

Your enemies are full of hot air, so you make their lungs burn when they try to talk. The target takes 4d6 fire damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failed save, the target also burns whenever they speak, causing them to take 4 fire damage whenever they use an action with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/auditory|auditory]] trait or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]], unless their spell is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/subtle|subtle]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial fire damage increases by 1d6, and the fire damage the target takes whenever they take an action with the auditory trait or Cast a Spell increases by 1.
