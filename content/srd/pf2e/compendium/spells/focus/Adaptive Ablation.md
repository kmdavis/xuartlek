# Adaptive Ablation  *Focus 4*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Change|Change]]
- **Trigger**: You take [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/acid|acid]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/cold|cold]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/electricity|electricity]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/sonic|sonic]] damage.
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You shift yourself to adapt to the mystical wavelengths of the energy damage, protecting yourself from further harm. You gain [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|resistance]] 5 to the triggering energy type; you gain this resistance after you take the triggering damage, so it applies only to future damage you take during the duration. 

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 262*
