# Bloody Tendrils  *Focus 3*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Thrall`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure forth stationary tentacles formed from pure, partially hardened blood. You create up to 3 Medium thralls shaped like bloody tendrils in range. These tendrils are immune to physical damage and can’t move. The tendril’s Strike has a reach of 10 feet. Whenever an enemy ends it turn within the reach of one or more tendrils, the tendril lashes out at them, dealing 1d12 bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+3)** The number of thralls this spell can create increases by 1, and the damage increases by 1d12.
