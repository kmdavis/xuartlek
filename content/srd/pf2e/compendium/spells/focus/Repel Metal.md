# Repel Metal  *Focus 4*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Metal`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Metal|Metal]]
- **Trigger**: A [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with a metallic weapon or projectile would hit you or an ally within range.
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: the creature targeted by the triggering Strike

You call forth a repelling field. The target gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack then misses, you direct the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] back at the attacker, who compares the attack roll to their own AC. The target then becomes immune to this spell for 10 minutes.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 267*
