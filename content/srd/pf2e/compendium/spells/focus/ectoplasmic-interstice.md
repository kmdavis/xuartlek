# Ectoplasmic Interstice  *Focus 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Soul|Soul]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

You force the mortal [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#The Universe|Universe]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Planes]] to partially merge, creating a zone that causes incorporeal and spiritual entities to take on many of the aspects of corporeal creatures. Within the area of effect, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/gm-core/incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures can’t pass through solid objects or corporeal creatures, and they can manipulate and attempt Strength-based checks against physical creatures and objects. An incorporeal PC or other creature with a normal Strength modifier simply uses that modifier, but if the creature had a Strength modifier of –5, like most incorporeal monsters, it uses a +4 modifier instead, increasing its [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] modifier by 9 accordingly. Attacks against creatures that are within the area overcome [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance|resistances]] as if they were by [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|ghost touch]] weapons.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 269*
