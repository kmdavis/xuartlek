# Life Tap  *Focus 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 of your thralls
- **Defense**: Fortitude

Using a thrall as a siphon, you attempt to drain the life essence of a creature and use it to restore the life of yourself or an ally. One creature within 30 feet of the targeted thrall must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. The targeted thrall is then destroyed.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is drained 1 but recovers after 1 minute. You or an ally of your choice within 30 feet of the thrall regains Hit Points equal to double the amount the creature lost. A creature can be healed by this spell only once every 10 minutes.

**Failure** As success, but drained 2.

**Critical Failure** As success, but drained 3.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 183*
