# Conglomerate of Limbs  *Focus 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Thrall`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure forth a humongous thrall consisting solely of undead severed limbs. You create a Huge thrall within range. This thrall has 40 Hit Points and a Speed of 25 feet. When you use _thrall charge_ to order this thrall to make a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]], it makes two melee unarmed Strikes instead of one, each against a different creature within 10 feet. Your multiple attack penalty doesn’t increase until after the thrall makes both Strikes. A creature hit by one of these Strikes must also succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Grabbed|grabbed]] by the thrall for 1 round (or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Restrained|restrained]] on a critical failure) or until it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escapes]]. Attempts to Escape don’t automatically succeed, and the Escape DC is your spell DC.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The thrall’s Hit Points increase by 10.
