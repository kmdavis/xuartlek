# Bridge of Graves  *Focus 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

You summon a bridge formed from grave dirt and tombstones that allow thralls to move with great speed. The 10-foot-wide span must start at the ground on a point within range, and it extends as a horizontal plane, either in a straight [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Line|line]] or at any angle up to 45 degrees upward or downward, for up to 120 feet. This bridge has AC 10, Hardness 30, and 40 Hit Points. A thrall on or adjacent to the bridge can move to any location on the bridge when you command it to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Stride|Stride]]. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] the spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The length of the bridge increases by 20 feet, its Hit Points increase by 20, and you can increase the width of the span by up to an additional 5 feet.
