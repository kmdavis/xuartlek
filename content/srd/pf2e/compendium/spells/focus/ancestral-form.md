# Ancestral Form  *Focus 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Oracle`  `Uncommon`

- **Mystery**: Ancestors
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

You take the form of your ancestral spirits, becoming [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] and quasi-corporeal. You gain resistance 10 to all damage (except [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/force|force]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/spirit|spirit]], and damage from Strikes with the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/ghost-touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune), and this resistance is doubled against non-[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/magical|magical]] damage or Strikes that includes precision damage. You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed; when [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/rage-of-elements#Flamethrower|Flying]], moving upward isn't difficult terrain for you. You can't move through solid objects, but you can float through tiny gaps. You can't Cast Spells (but you can Sustain spells), activate items, or use actions that have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/attack|attack]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/manipulate|manipulate]] trait.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
