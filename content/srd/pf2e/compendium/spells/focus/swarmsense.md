# Swarmsense  *Focus 1*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Cleric`  `Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Domain**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/domains#Swarm|Swarm]]
- **Duration**: sustained

You extend your senses through a multitude of crawling and flying creatures. You create a crawling swarm in your square. You can hear through the swarm as if using your normal auditory senses. When you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Cast a Spell|Cast this Spell]] and the first time you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] it each round, you can move the swarm 10 feet along the ground in any direction. The swarm has AC 15 and a +0 bonus to its saves. Any damage dealt to the swarm destroys it and ends the spell.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The swarm has a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Climb Speed|climb Speed]] of 10 feet.

**Heightened (5th)** The swarm has a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Fly Speed|fly Speed]] of 10 feet. You can see through the swarm using your visual senses.

**Heightened (7th)** The swarm gains a 10-foot status bonus to its Speeds.
