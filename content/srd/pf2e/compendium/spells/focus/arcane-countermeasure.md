# Arcane Countermeasure  *Focus 5*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Sorcerer`  `Uncommon`

- **Bloodline**: Imperial
- **Trigger**: A creature within range that you can see Casts a Spell.
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Target**: the spell cast by the triggering creature

You undermine the target spell, making it easier to defend against. You reduce the spell's rank by 1, and targets of the spell gain a +2 status bonus to any saving throws, skill checks, AC, or DC against it.

 You can't reduce the spell's rank below its minimum. For example, a 5th-rank _howling blizzard_ would remain 5th-rank, but a 5th-rank _fireball_ would become 4th-rank. Targets still gain all the other benefits, even if you don't reduce the spell's rank.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 265*
