# Toxic Cloud  *Spell 5*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Death`  `Manipulate`  `Poison`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Deities**: Apollyon, Cixyron, Isph-Aun-Vuln, Nergal, Nhimbaloth, Sicva, Vorasha, Ydersius
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Defense**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure a poisonous fog. This functions as _mist_, except the area moves 10 feet away from you each round. A breathing creature that starts its turn in the area takes 6d8 poison damage with a basic Fortitude save. You can Dismiss the spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
