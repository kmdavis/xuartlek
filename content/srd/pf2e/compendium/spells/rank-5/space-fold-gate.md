# Space Fold Gate  *Spell 5*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Teleportation`  `Uncommon`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Range**: 200 feet
- **Duration**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

You manipulate the fabric of space-time, temporarily bringing two distant points together, connected by a magical gate. Choose two unoccupied 5-foot squares on solid ground that you can see within range. You create spherical portals in both squares that are 5 feet tall and 5 feet wide connecting the two locations. Any creature that enters one portal can immediately transport itself to the exit portal as a part of its move action, which adds the teleportation trait to its movement (this movement still triggers reactions as normal). Physical objects can pass through the portal normally, as though the portals were adjacent. Ranged attacks made through the portals treat them as only one 5-foot square for the purpose of calculating range. Spells cast through the portals have no effect.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Range increases to 500 feet, and the portals can be made up to 10 feet tall and 10 feet wide.

**Heightened (7th)** Range increases to 1,000 feet, and the portals can be made up to 30 feet tall and 30 feet wide.
