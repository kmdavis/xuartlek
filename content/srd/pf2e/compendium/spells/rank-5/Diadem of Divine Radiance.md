# Diadem of Divine Radiance  *Spell 5*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Light`  `Manipulate`  `Mythic`  `Rare`  `Sanctified`

- **Traditions**: [Arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1), [Divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2), [Occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3), [Primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=4)
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You don a diadem of radiant light, which sheds [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Bright Light|bright light]] to a range of 60 feet and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Perception and Detection#Dim Light|dim light]] to a further 60 feet. When you Cast the Spell and when you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] it during the duration, you can draw a disc of spiraling light from the diadem and throw it at a creature within 120 feet. Make a ranged spell attack at mythic proficiency against the target's AC. This action has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Attack|attack]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Spirit|spirit]] traits. On a hit, you deal 4d8 spirit damage, 1d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent spirit damage]], and the target is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round (3 rounds on a critical hit). The persistent damage isn't doubled on a critical hit. 

If the disc passes through an area of magical [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Darkness|darkness]] or targets a creature affected by magical darkness, the disc's glow attempts to [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-7-spells/Counteracting|counteract]] the darkness using your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Religion|Religion]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Occultism|Occultism]] skill modifier as the counteract check modifier and half your level as the counteract rank.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The disc’s divine damage increases by 2d8, and the persistent damage increases by 1d4.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 157*
