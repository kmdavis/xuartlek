# Plasma Whirl  *Spell 5*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Concentrate`  `Electricity`  `Fire`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Area**: 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Cone|cone]]
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex

You reach up to the stars and create a temporary portal that allows a blast of plasma to pass through the battlefield. The plasma deals electricity and fire damage with a basic Reflex save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the area and other parameters. 

[>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") This spell is a 15-foot cone, dealing 2d6 electricity damage and 2d6 fire damage. 
[>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") This spell is a 30-foot cone, dealing 5d6 electricity damage and 5d6 fire damage. 
[>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") This spell is a 60-foot cone, dealing 5d6 electricity damage and 5d6 fire damage. Creatures that fail their saves become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened 1]] (sickened 2 on a critical failure) due to their exposure to cosmic radiation.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6 for each damage type with the 1-action version, or by 2d6 for each damage type for the 2-and 3-action versions.
