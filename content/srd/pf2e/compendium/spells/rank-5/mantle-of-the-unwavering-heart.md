# Mantle of the Unwavering Heart  *Spell 5*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Morph`  `Plant`  `Wood`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Vines and branches envelop you in an instant, growing a wild bloom of colors that open to reveal your new form. Upon Casting this Spell, pick two of the options below. As a single action, which has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]] trait, you can change one of your chosen abilities to a different option from the list.

- **Evergreen Vitality** You gain fast healing 3 and a +2 bonus to saves against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/disease|disease]]. This effect has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/vitality|vitality]] traits.
- **Overwhelming Perfume** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/aura|aura]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/olfactory|olfactory]]) A cloud of potent floral scent surrounds you in a 15-foot emanation. Creatures in the aura can't benefit from the scent ability and are sickened 1 as long as they remain in the aura. The sickness is a disease effect.
- **Towering Trunk** You grow to a considerable height. Increase your size to Large. You're clumsy 1. Your [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/reach|reach]] increases by 5 feet (or by 10 feet if you started out Tiny), and you gain a grasping branch unarmed attack; this attack is in the brawling weapon group and deals 2d8 bludgeoning damage plus Grab.
- **Unyielding Will** Your mind becomes incredibly resilient to change. You're immune to being [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Fascinated|fascinated]], and you get a +1 bonus to saves and DCs against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]] effects.
