# Spiked Carapace  *Spell 5*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Morph`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 round

Your bones harmlessly puncture the skin of one arm, growing to form a hardened spiked gauntlet that can be used for both offense and defense. Make a melee spell attack against a target’s AC, dealing 5d8 piercing damage and 1d8 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent bleed damage]] (double damage on a critical hit). You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC for the duration, as you deflect attacks with the gauntlet.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 2d8 and persistent bleed damage increases by 1d8.
