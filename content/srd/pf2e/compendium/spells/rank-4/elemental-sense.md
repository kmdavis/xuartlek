# Elemental Sense  *Spell 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Revelation`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Spell Lists**: Elemental
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

The elements grant you sensory enhancements. Choose one of the options below. The spell gains the listed trait or traits.

- **Aquatic Echolocation** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/water|water]]) You use your hearing as a precise sense when underwater.
- **Heatsense** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]]) You can sense creatures with a temperature of 90º Fahrenheit or higher within 30 feet as an imprecise sense.
- **Magnetoreception** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/metal|metal]]) You can sense creatures carrying metal items with a total of 1 Bulk or greater; this is an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. If a creature is made of metal or has the metal trait, you can detect it as though magnetoreception were a precise sense. In addition, you continuously know which direction is north.
- **Mechanoreception** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/air|air]]) You can see [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects. They appear to you as translucent shapes, and they are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] to you.
- **Tremorsense** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/earth|earth]]) You gain tremorsense (imprecise) with a range of 30 feet.
- **Woodsense** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/plant|plant]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/wood|wood]]) You can sense creatures carrying wood items with a total of 1 Bulk or greater; this is an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. If a creature is made of wood or has the plant or wood trait, you can detect it as though woodsense were a precise sense.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 8 hours, and you can take 1 minute to recalibrate the sense, switching from your current elemental sense to a different one.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 222*
