# Crystallized Vapors  *Spell 4*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Aura`  `Cold`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Area**: 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]]
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You freeze the precipitation around you in icy pellets, creating a 15-foot emanation of difficult terrain; you're immune to this difficult terrain. You can launch a ball of ice whenever you Cast this Spell and the first time you Sustain this spell on subsequent turns, making a ranged spell attack against the AC of a creature within 30 feet. On a hit, you deal 1d12 cold damage and 1d12 bludgeoning damage (or double damage on a critical hit).

---
**Heightened (+3)** The damage increases by 1d12 cold and 1d12 bludgeoning.
