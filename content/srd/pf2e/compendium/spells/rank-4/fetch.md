# Fetch  *Spell 4*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Teleportation`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult, Primal
- **Requirements**: You haven’t [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Command an Animal|Commanded]] your companion this turn.
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: your companion

You magically send your companion to retrieve an object or perform a simple task for you. Your companion teleports to a location within 120 feet of you, can perform one Interact action, and then teleports back to its starting location. You can't Command your companion during the same turn that you cast _fetch_.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 138*
