# Incarnate Draconic Legion  *Spell 9*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Incarnate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

You momentarily summon an army of powerful dragons that unleash annihilating blasts as they descend upon your location. These dragons act as one and collectively occupy the space of a Gargantuan creature. They have a fly Speed of 100 feet. The dragons are of the same magical tradition that you used to Cast this Spell.

**Arrive** _Energy Annihilation_ The draconic legion breathes out two blasts of energy: a 120-foot line and a 60-foot cone that can't overlap. Each creature in either of the areas takes 10d8 damage with a basic Reflex save. The dragons choose the damage type of each blast of energy depending on the tradition of dragons summoned. Arcane dragons can choose force or mental, divine dragons can choose fire or spirit, occult dragons can choose mental or void, and primal dragons can choose fire or poison.
**Depart** _Draconic Whirlwind_ The draconic legion strikes with fury, dealing all enemy creatures in a 20-foot emanation 9d8 slashing damage (basic Reflex save).
