# Remake Physiology  *Spell 9*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Curse`  `Manipulate`  `Morph`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 living creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies

At your touch, the target's body shifts and transmutes into random mismatched animal parts, such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/creature-families/crab|crab]] claws for hands and mismatched gazelle and [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/animal/gorilla|gorilla]] legs. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. 
Due to the unbalanced body weight and mismatched limbs, the target takes a –20-foot status penalty to its Speeds.The creature's mind and body no longer align, making it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy 1]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Drained|drained 1]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied 1]].The target's new eyes make depth perception difficult; the target becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]].Whenever the target would regain actions at the start of its turn, it must first attempt a DC 5 flat check; on a failure, it's [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Slowed|slowed 1]] for a round.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is affected for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is affected for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The effect is permanent.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 159*
