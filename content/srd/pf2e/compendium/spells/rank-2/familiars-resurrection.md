# Familiar's Resurrection  *Spell 2*

`Concentrate`  `Healing`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes
- **Range**: 10 feet
- **Target**: your dead familiar of 4th level or lower

Raising the dead is usually a complex and sophisticated magic, not to mention an expensive one. Familiars, however, are creatures with strange souls, bound to their masters. You can channel this connection to resurrect a familiar. Your familiar returns to life with 1 Hit Point.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The maximum level of a familiar you can resurrect with this spell increases by 2.
