# Palm-Held Sun  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Aura`  `Concentrate`  `Light`  `Manipulate`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

You open a rift into Creation’s Forge, igniting it into a miniature star that blazes with mighty light. You shed bright light in a 30-foot emanation (and dim light to the next 30 feet). When you Cast or Sustain the spell, each creature with void healing in the aura takes 1 vitality damage, and any creature in the aura with a particular vulnerability to sunlight becomes slowed 1 for 1 round unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. After a creature in the aura loses HP due to fire damage from any source, it takes 1 vitality damage.
