# Claws of the Otter  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cold`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: [Divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2), [Primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=4)
- **Duration**: 1 hour

Webbing grows between your fingers and your nails extend into vicious claws. For the spell's duration, you gain a +1 status bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Swim|Swim]] and you gain a claws unarmed attack. They're an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Agile|agile]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Finesse|finesse]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage and an additional 1d6 cold damage.

---
**Heightened (+3)** The additional cold damage increases by 1d6.
