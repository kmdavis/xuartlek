# Imprinting Hand  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Divine, Occult
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 living or undead creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Your hand presses into your target, leaving an imprint on its body. The target takes 8 force damage with a Fortitude save. The first time within the next minute the target's Hit Points are restored by a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/healing|healing]] effect, that healing is reduced by 8. After any amount of healing is reduced, the spell ends.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half the force damage and half the healing reduction.

**Failure** The target takes the full force damage and the full healing reduction.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double the damage and double the healing reduction.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage and healing reduction both increase by 4.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 144*
