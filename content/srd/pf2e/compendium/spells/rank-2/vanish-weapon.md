# Vanish Weapon  *Spell 2*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Extradimensional`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Trigger**: A creature succeeds at (but not critically succeeds at) or fails a melee Strike with a weapon targeting you.
- **Target**: The triggering creature
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

You create a small extradimensional space that you can use to snag weapons. You attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Disarm|Disarm]] the triggering creature using a melee spell attack roll instead of an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] check. You don’t need a hand free for this attempt. If you successfully knock the weapon from the triggering creature’s grasp, it disappears into your extradimensional space. You can Interact to draw the weapon as if you were wearing it; if you do so, the spell ends. If the weapon is still in the extradimensional space when the spell ends, it falls to the ground at your feet. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] this spell.

This reaction doesn’t prevent damage from a successful Strike.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 170*
