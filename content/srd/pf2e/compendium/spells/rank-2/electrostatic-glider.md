# Electrostatic Glider  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Air`  `Concentrate`  `Electricity`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You generate an arc of static electricity above you, allowing you to ride air currents and descend gracefully, though it doesn't provide actual flight. During this spell's duration, you can glide as a single action that has the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/move|move]] trait. When you glide, you move 5 feet down and 25 feet through the air, though you can control the direction you travel. As long as you spend at least 1 action gliding each round and haven't yet reached the ground, you remain in the air at the end of your turn. At the GM's discretion, strong winds might affect your movements. If the spell ends while you're in the air, you fall normally.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 134*
