# Disintegrating Puddle  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Acid`  `Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You spit out a green glob of dangerous acid that increases in size as it flies at the target, splashing around them as it hits them. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a success, the target takes 2d12 acid damage, and acid splatters to the ground in the target's space and all spaces adjacent to the target. On a critical hit, the target takes double damage. The acid puddle lasts for 1 minute; a creature that enters or starts its turn in the acid puddle takes 1d4 acid damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial acid damage increases by 2d12, and the acid puddle damage increases by 1d4.
