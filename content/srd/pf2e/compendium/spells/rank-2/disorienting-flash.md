# Disorienting Flash  *Spell 2*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Concentrate`  `Light`  `Manipulate`  `Sonic`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: varies
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude

You create a small explosion of bright light and ear-piercing sound that rocks the senses. Creatures in the area take sonic damage with a basic Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the damage and other parameters.

[>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") This spell deals 1d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 5-foot square. Creatures that fail their save become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.
[>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 10- foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]. Creatures that fail their save become dazzled until the end of your next turn.
[>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 20- foot burst. Creatures that fail their save become dazzled and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Deafened|deafened]] until the end of your next turn.
