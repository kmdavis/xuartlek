# Invisibility  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Illusion`  `Manipulate`  `Subtle`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Deities**: Achaekek, Erecura, Fortune's Fate, Ketephys, Nalinivati, Ng, Nivi Rhombodazzle, Norgorber, Raumya, Shax, The Tides of Chaos, Yaezhing, Zugero
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

Illusions bend light around the target, rendering it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]]. This makes it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Undetected|undetected]] to all creatures, though the creatures can attempt to find the target, making it [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] to them instead. If the target uses a hostile action, the spell ends after that hostile action is completed.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The spell lasts 1 minute, but it doesn't end if the target uses a hostile action.
