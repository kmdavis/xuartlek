# Stupefy  *Spell 2*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Bloodlines**: Aberrant, Hag
- **Deities**: Cayden Cailean, Halcamora, Razmir, The Lady of the North Star
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: varies

You dull the target's mind, depending on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is stupefied 1 until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 3 for 1 minute.
