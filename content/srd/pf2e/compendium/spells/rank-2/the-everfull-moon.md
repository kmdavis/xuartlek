# The Everfull Moon  *Spell 2*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Impossible`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`  `Unique`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Cost**: moondust or moonrock worth 100 gp × the spell’s rank
- **Duration**: 1 minute

A Linnorm King once defended her kingdom from Irriseni invaders with a casting of this spell. You call upon the secret moon, who's always whole, to shine upon yourself, granting you the form of a mighty werewolf without letting the curse touch you. You transform yourself into a Large hybrid wolf battle form. While in this form, you gain the beast trait. This battle form doesn't prevent you from speaking, casting spells, or using your hands. You gain the following abilities. 
AC = 18 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.A +1 status bonus to saving throws.20 temporary Hit Points.Darkvision and scent as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet.Your proficiency bonus equals your level for untrained skill checks using Acrobatics or Athletics.The claw and jaws unarmed melee attacks detailed below. You're trained with them, your attack modifier is +10, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.**Melee** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") jaws (reach), Damage 2d8 piercing.**Melee** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") claw (agile, reach), Damage 2d6 slashing.Speed of 40 feet, unless your own is higher.

**Lethologic Mark** During any night that isn't the new moon, you gain a weakness to silver equal to your level. The mark is healed if you become a true werewolf and become cured of said curse.

**Heightened (+2)** The temporary Hit Points granted by this spell when you cast or Fuel it increase by 20. All unarmed Strikes granted by this spell deal an additional die of damage. Your attack modifier increases by +6. Finally, your AC increases by 2.
