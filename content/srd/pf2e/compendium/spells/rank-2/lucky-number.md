# Lucky Number  *Spell 2*

`Concentrate`  `Contingency`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult
- **Cast**: 10 minutes
- **Duration**: until the next time you make your daily preparations

When you Cast this Spell, roll 1d10 and make a note of the result; this is your lucky number for the duration. You gain the following reaction. Once you use the reaction, the spell ends, and you become temporarily immune to _lucky number_ until the next time you make your daily preparations.

**That's My Number!** [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (fortune) **Trigger** You roll your lucky number as your d20 result on a non-secret attack roll, saving throw, or skill check; **Effect** You call upon the fortune stored within your lucky number and reroll the triggering check. However, pushing your luck has a price: if your result on the reroll is the same number again, you critically fail, regardless of what degree of success you ordinarily would have received.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 152*
