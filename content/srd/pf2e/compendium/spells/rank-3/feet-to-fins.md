# Feet to Fins  *Spell 3*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Morph`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Deities**: Besmara, Dagon, Jin Li, Picoperi, Ragadahn, Seafarer's Hope, Sobek, Surveyors of the Deep, Telastmar, Ylimancha, Zeaki
- **Lesson**: Lesson of the Shark
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 willing creature
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

The target's feet transform into fins, improving mobility in the water but reducing it on land. The target gains a swim Speed equal to its normal land Speed, but its land Speed becomes 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The spell lasts until your next daily preparations.
