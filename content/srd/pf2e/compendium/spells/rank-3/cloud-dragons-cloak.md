# Cloud Dragon's Cloak  *Spell 3*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Air`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Trigger**: You or an ally in range is targeted by a ranged attack.
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: the creature targeted by the attack
- **Duration**: 1 round

A cloud of mist enshrouds the target, appearing much like the deflecting clouds created by cloud dragons. The target is treated as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Hidden|hidden]] for the purposes of resolving the triggering attack (so normally the attacker must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to target it) and all ranged attacks against it for the duration.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 70*
