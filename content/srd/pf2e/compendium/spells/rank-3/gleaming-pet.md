# Gleaming Pet  *Spell 3*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Light`  `Manipulate`  `Visual`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: your companion or eidolon
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

The target glows intensely with an otherworldly light, blinding any who approach. Any creature that begins its turn adjacent to the target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is immune for 10 minutes.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 round.
