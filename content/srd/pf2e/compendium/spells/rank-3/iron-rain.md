# Iron Rain  *Spell 3*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Metal`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot radius
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You call forth blades from the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Plane of Metal|Plane of Metal]], creating piercing daggers that shoot down from the sky. These blades deal 6d4 piercing damage and 3 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent bleed damage]] to all creatures in the area. As the rain dissipates, the ground is littered with hundreds of knives, creating [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] that lasts for the duration of the spell. Any creature that's within the difficult terrain can Interact to pick up one of the knives and wield it as a mundane [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/weapons/knife/dagger|dagger]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
