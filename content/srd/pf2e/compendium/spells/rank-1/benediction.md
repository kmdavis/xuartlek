# Benediction  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Aura`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Area**: 15-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]]
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Divine protection helps protect your companions. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC while within the emanation. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the spell to increase the emanation’s radius by 10 feet. Benediction can counteract [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/malediction|_malediction_]].
