# Concordant Choir  *Spell 1*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Concentrate`  `Sonic`

- **Traditions**: Divine, Occult
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: varies
- **Defense**: basic Fortitude

You unleash a dangerous consonance of reverberating sound, focusing on a single target or spreading out to damage many foes. The number of actions you spend Casting this Spell determines its targets, range, area, and other parameters.

 [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") The spell deals 1d4 sonic damage to a single enemy, with a basic Fortitude save.
 [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) The spell deals 2d4 sonic damage to all creatures in a 10-foot burst, with a basic Fortitude save.
 [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (manipulate) The spell deals 2d4 sonic damage to all creatures in a 30-foot emanation, with a basic Fortitude save

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4 for the 1-action version, or 2d4 for the other versions.
