# Malediction  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Aura`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Area**: 10-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]]
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You incite distress in the minds of your enemies, making it more difficult for them to defend themselves. Enemies in the area must succeed at a Will save or take a –1 status penalty to AC as long as they’re in the area. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the spell to increase the emanation’s radius by 10 feet and force enemies in the area that weren’t yet affected to attempt a saving throw. Malediction can counteract [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-1/benediction|_benediction_]].

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 258*
