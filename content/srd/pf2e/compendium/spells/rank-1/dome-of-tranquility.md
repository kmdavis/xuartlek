# Dome of Tranquility  *Spell 1*

`Air`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult, Primal
- **Cast**: 1 minute
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 hour

The air around you falls still and blocks out the sounds of the outside world. While you can clearly hear anything within this isolated dome, you can't hear anything outside of it. The opposite is also true, with no one outside of your dome being able to hear what's within. This dome doesn't block line of sight. If anything larger than 1 Bulk passes through it, the dome is automatically dispersed. You can Dismiss this spell.
