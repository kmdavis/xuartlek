# Spider Sting  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Poison`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Deities**: Barbatos, Lamashtu, Teshallas, Vorasha
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude

You magically duplicate a spider's venomous sting. You deal 1d4 piercing damage to the touched creature and afflict it with spider venom. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes 1d4 poison damage.

**Failure** The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 1.

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 2. **Spider Venom** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]]) Level 1; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d4 poison damage and enfeebled 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d4 poison damage and enfeebled 2 (1 round)
