# Kinetic Ram  *Spell 1*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Concentrate`  `Force`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Area**: varies
- **Target**: 1 or more creatures
- **Defense**: Fortitude

Gathering kinetic energy, you either focus it in a straight line or disperse it as an encircling ripple. Any creature targeted by this spell must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from you (or 20 feet on a critical failure).

 The spell's area or range and how many creatures it affects is based on how many actions you spend when Casting the Spell. 
[>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") The spell targets one creature within 15 feet.[>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") The spell targets one creature within 30 feet. The distance the target is pushed if it fails is doubled, and on a critical failure, the target is also knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]] and takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage.[>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") The spell targets all creatures in a 5-foot emanation.
