# Synchronize  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: up to 5 willing creatures
- **Duration**: up to 24 hours

You harmlessly place your unique magic sigil in the shape of your choice, which is about 1 square inch in size, on your targets. When you Cast the Spell, you set the duration by choosing a time at which point the sigil flashes dimly three times. After that point, the spell ends. Even though spell durations aren’t normally exact, the effects of _synchronize_ are precise to the second. The timer is based on the place where the spell was cast, so entering a plane or area where time flows differently changes how the time elapses in sometimes unpredictable ways.
