# Heal  *Spell 1*  [?](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies")

`Healing`  `Manipulate`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Traditions**: Divine, Primal
- **Bloodline**: Angelic
- **Range**: varies
- **Target**: 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature

You channel vital energy to heal the living or damage the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/undead|undead]]. If the target is a willing living creature, you restore 1d8 Hit Points. If the target is undead, you deal that amount of vitality damage to it, and it gets a basic Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines its targets, range, area, and other parameters.

 [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") The spell has a range of touch.
 [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) The spell has a range of 30 feet. If you're healing a living creature, increase the Hit Points restored by 8.
 [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/concentrate|concentrate]]) You disperse vital energy in a 30-foot emanation. This targets all living and undead creatures in the burst.
