# Fishing Spot  *Spell 1*

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes

You conjure a fishing rod and an extraplanar pool teeming with fish. After 10 minutes of fishing, you catch a magical fish; roll 1d8 to see which fish you caught and the effects gained from eating it once it's cooked. The fish must be cooked and eaten (a process that takes about 30 minutes) within 1 hour of being caught, and the listed effect lasts for 1 hour after consumption.
