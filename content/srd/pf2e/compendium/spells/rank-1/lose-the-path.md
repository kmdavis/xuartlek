# Lose the Path  *Spell 1*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Illusion`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`  `Visual`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Deities**: Il’Surrish, Uskyeria
- **Trigger**: A creature in range Strides.
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: the triggering creature
- **Defense**: Will

You surround a moving creature with lifelike illusions, shifting their perception of the terrain to subtly lead them off course. The target must attempt a Will save. Regardless of the result, the creature is immune to _lose the path_ for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature treats all squares as [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for its Stride.

**Critical Failure** As failure, except that you determine where the target moves during the Stride, though you can’t move it into hazardous terrain or to a place it can’t stand.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 151*
