# Befuddle  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Emotion`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Deities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/demon-lords/areshkagal|Areshkagal]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/qlippoth-lords/nyuo-ogh|Nyuo-Ogh]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/covenants/the-pandemonia|The Pandemonia]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/demon-lords/vulot|Vulot]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 round

You sow seeds of confusion in your target’s mind, causing its actions and thoughts to become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]].

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is clumsy 1 and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied]] 1.

**Failure** The target is clumsy 2 and stupefied 2.

**Critical Failure** The target is clumsy 3, stupefied 3, and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Confused|confused]].

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 256*
