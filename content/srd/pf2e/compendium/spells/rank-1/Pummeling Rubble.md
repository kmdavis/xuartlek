# Pummeling Rubble  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Earth`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Deities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/faiths-philosophies/rivethun|Rivethun]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/other-gods/the-devourer|The Devourer]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/covenants/treasures-of-the-eternal-delve|Treasures of the Eternal Delve]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/demon-lords/xoveron|Xoveron]]
- **Area**: 15-foot cone
- **Defense**: Reflex

A spray of heavy rocks flies through the air in front of you. The rubble deals 2d4 bludgeoning damage to each creature in the area. Each creature must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is pushed 5 feet away from you.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d4.
