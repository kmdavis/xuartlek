# Echoing Weapon  *Spell 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Sonic`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 weapon
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You channel energy into the target weapon, and the air around it faintly hums each time it strikes a blow, as the impact is absorbed into the weapon. If a creature is wielding the weapon at the end of its turn, the weapon discharges a burst of sound targeting one creature adjacent to the wielder. The sonic damage dealt is equal to the number of successful [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strikes]] with the target weapon that the wielder made that turn (to a maximum of 4 sonic damage if the wielder hits with four Strikes).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The sonic damage increases by 1 per Strike (and the maximum damage increases by 4).
