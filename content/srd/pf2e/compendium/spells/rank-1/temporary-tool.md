# Temporary Tool  *Spell 1*

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Tradition**: Arcane
- **Deities**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/azlanti-pantheon/amaznen|Amaznen]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/empyreal-lords/bharnarol|Bharnarol]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/other-gods/brigh|Brigh]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/elven-gods/findeladlara|Findeladlara]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/dwarven-gods/magrim|Magrim]], Ptah, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/pantheons/wheels-of-innovation|Wheels of Innovation]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/deities/goblin-gods/zogmugot|Zogmugot]]
- **Cast**: 1 minute
- **Duration**: 1 minute or until used

You conjure a temporary simple [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/tool|tool]], such as a shovel or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/rope|rope]] into your hands. It lasts until it’s used for a single activity or for 1 minute, whichever comes first, after which it disappears. The tool is obviously temporarily conjured and thus can’t be sold or passed off as a genuine item.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 260*
