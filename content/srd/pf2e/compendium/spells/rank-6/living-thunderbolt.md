# Living Thunderbolt  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Electricity`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You transform into a being of pure lightning, allowing you to move at impossible speeds. You gain a fly Speed of 60 feet, resistance 10 to electricity, and automatically succeed at attempts to Escape an effect that has you immobilized, grabbed, or restrained unless the effect is magical and of a higher rank than the _living thunderbolt_ spell. Additionally, you can move through creatures’ spaces as if they weren’t there, but you can’t end your movement in another creature’s space. When you move through a creature’s space, they take 4d12 electricity damage with a basic Reflex save. On a failure, they’re also dazzled for 1 round. A creature can only take damage this way once per round.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The electricity resistance increases by 5 and the damage dealt when you move through a creature’s space increases by 1d12.
