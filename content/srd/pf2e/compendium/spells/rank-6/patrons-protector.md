# Patron's Protector  *Spell 6*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: your familiar
- **Duration**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

You channel your magic into your familiar, filling its body with dreadful energy, turning them into a powerful protective force. You can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Command an Animal|Command an Animal]] on the target as part of Casting or Sustaining this spell. Your familiar's AC becomes 28; they become large, and they gain 20 temporary Hit Points. If your familiar is reduced to 0 Hit Points while under the effect of this spell, the spell immediately ends, and your familiar is instead reduced to 1 Hit Point. Once per turn your familiar can use the Body Check or Protector's Shove action.

**Body Check** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") Your familiar uses its new size and presence to press against an adjacent creature, dealing, 4d10 bludgeoning damage ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, the target is also knocked back 10 feet (20 feet on a critical failure).

**Protector's Shove** [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") Your familiar shoves an ally out of danger. Your familiar pushes an adjacent, willing ally, pushing them up to 20 feet away and granting them a +2 status bonus to AC for 1 round. This is forced movement.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The familiar’s AC become 36, they gain 30 temporary Hit Points, and the damage increases by 2d10.
