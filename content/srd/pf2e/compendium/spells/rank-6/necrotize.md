# Necrotize  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 living creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies

You necrotize part of a creature's body, harming them and debilitating them as their body putrefies, mimicking the natural process of decay. The target takes 12d6 void damage and one of the following debilitations; roll 1d4 to randomly determine which. The effects depend on their Fortitude save.**1 Bones** The target's bones necrotize and become spongy and weak, giving them weakness 3 to bludgeoning damage.**2 Joints** The target's joints necrotize and become frail, making their movements ungainly and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]]. They become clumsy 1.**3 Legs** The target's legs (or any other limbs associated with forms of movement) necrotize, making it harder for the creature to move. The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to their Speeds.**4 Muscles** The target's muscles necrotize, rotting away. The target becomes [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]].

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half damage, and the debilitation lasts until the end of your next turn.

**Failure** The target takes full damage, and the debilitation lasts for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, and you can pick a second debilitation to apply, in addition to the one you rolled randomly. Both debilitations last for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
