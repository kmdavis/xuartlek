# Truesight  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Revelation`

- **Traditions**: [Arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1), [Divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2), [Occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3), [Primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=4)
- **Spell Lists**: [Elemental](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=5)
- **Bloodline**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/Bloodlines#Diabolic|Diabolic]]
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

You see things within 60 feet as they actually are. The GM rolls a secret counteract check against any [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Illusion|illusion]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Morph|morph]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect in the area, but only for the purpose of determining whether you see through it (for instance, if the check succeeds against a polymorph spell, you can see the creature's true form, but you don't end the polymorph spell).
