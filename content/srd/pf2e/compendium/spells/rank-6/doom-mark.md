# Doom Mark  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You launch a dart of dark void energy that imprints itself on the target, connecting the fates of those marked. Whenever you Cast this Spell and the first time you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] this spell on subsequent turns, make a ranged spell attack against the AC of a creature within 60 feet. On a hit, you deal 6d8 void damage (or double damage on a critical hit) and place a mark on their body for the duration of the spell. A marked creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stupefied|stupefied 1]]. When a marked creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points, all other marked creatures become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Off-Guard|off-guard]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
