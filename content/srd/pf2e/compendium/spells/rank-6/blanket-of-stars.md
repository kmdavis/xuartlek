# Blanket of Stars  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Illusion`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Deity**: The Lady of the North Star
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

A flowing cloak of utter darkness drapes over you, filled with pinpricks of light like distant stars. It imparts the stillness of the cosmos to you, granting you a +2 status bonus to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/stealth|Stealth]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]] and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sneak|Sneak]]. While outside under a starry night sky, you're also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Invisible|invisible]] as long as you remain still. When moving under a starry night sky, you're [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] instead.

 Gazing too closely into the stars is disorienting. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save; this is a [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/mental|mental]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/visual|visual]] effect.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Confused|confused]] and dazzled until the
end of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 241*
