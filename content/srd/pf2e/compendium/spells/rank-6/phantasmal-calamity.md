# Phantasmal Calamity  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Illusion`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Deities**: Imot, Lady Nanbyo, Rhan-Tegoth, Scal, Wulgren, Zyphus
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst
- **Defense**: Will

A vision of apocalyptic destruction fills the mind of each creature in the area. The vision deals 11d6 mental damage (basic Will save). On a critical failure, the creature must also succeed at a Reflex save or believe it's trapped (stuck in a fissure, adrift at sea, or some other fate in keeping with its vision). If it fails the second save, it's also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Stunned|stunned]] for 1 minute. It can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, it disbelieves the illusion and recovers from the stunned condition.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
