# Frozen Fog  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cold`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Burst|burst]]
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure a chilling mist that settles into the bones and makes it difficult to move. This has the effects of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/mist|_mist_]], except that you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain an Effect|Sustain]] the spell once per round to move the fog up to 10 feet. A creature that starts its turn in the area takes 6d8 cold damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that fails its save is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Clumsy|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute (clumsy 2 on a critical failure) and takes a –10-foot status penalty to Speeds for as long as it’s clumsy.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d8.
