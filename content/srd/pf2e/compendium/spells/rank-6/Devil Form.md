# Devil Form  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`  `Unholy`

- **Traditions**: [Arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1), [Divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2)
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You bind yourself to the power of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/Planes#Hell|Hell]], transforming into a Medium devil battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose coarti, levaloch, sarglagon, or vordine. If you choose levaloch or sarglagon, the battle form is Large, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Devil|devil]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fiend|fiend]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Unholy|unholy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose: 
AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.5 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]], [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Resistance|resistance]] 5 to physical damage (except silver), and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance#Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Holy|holy]].Resistance 10 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]].Darkvision.One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Strike|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +20, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.
