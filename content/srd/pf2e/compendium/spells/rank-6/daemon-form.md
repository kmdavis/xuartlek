# Daemon Form  *Spell 6*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`  `Unholy`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You infuse yourself with the corrupting death and pure malevolence of [[srd/pf2e/compendium/gm/planes#Abaddon|Abaddon]], transforming into a Large daemon battle form. You must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. When you Cast this Spell, you choose lacridaemon, leukodaemon, meladaemon, or venedaemon. The battle form is Medium if you choose lacridaemon or venedaemon. While in this form, you gain the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/daemon|daemon]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fiend|fiend]], and [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/unholy|unholy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use manipulate actions. You can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Dismiss|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose: 
AC = 21 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.20 [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/hit-points-healing-and-dying#Temporary Hit Points|temporary Hit Points]] and [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/immunity-weakness-and-resistance#Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/holy|holy]].A +2 status bonus to all saving throws against [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/death|death]] effects.Resistance 10 to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/poison|poison]].Darkvision.One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +21, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.You also gain specific abilities based on the type of Daemon you choose:
