# Unexpected Transposition  *Spell 6*  [<](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Concentrate`  `Teleportation`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Trigger**: You’re targeted with an enemy’s Strike.
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature other than the triggering enemy
- **Defense**: Will

When attacked, you attempt to quickly swap your own position with that of another creature, causing them to get hit in your place. A creature that’s unwilling to swap places with you must attempt a Will save. Willing creatures automatically fail, and you switch places. If you successfully switch places with the target, the triggering attack is resolved against that creature as if it had been the original target of the attack. After the swap, you and the target are both temporarily immune to _unexpected transposition_ spells for 1 minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 168*
