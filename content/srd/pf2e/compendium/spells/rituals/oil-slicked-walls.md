# Oil-Slicked Walls  *Ritual 2*

`Uncommon`

- **Cast**: 8 hours
- **Secondary Casters**: 1
- **Primary Check**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/lore|Warfare Lore]] (expert)
- **Secondary Checks**: Architecture Lore or Crafting
- **Area**: 7-mile radius [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] centered on you
- **Target(s)**: the outer walls of a settlement
- **Duration**: 1 week

The outer walls are a city’s first defense against invaders, and you want to make certain no attackers can climb over them. Upon successful completion of this ritual, the surfaces of a settlement’s walls within the area are covered with a thin, slippery sheen that can’t be washed away.

**Critical Success** The oil covering the walls also coats any climber’s hands. The DC for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] checks to Climb the affected walls increases by 10, and a creature who fails a check to climb also drops anything they are carrying.

**Success** The DC for [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Climb|Climb]] the affected walls increases by 5.

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.

**Critical Failure** Phantom oil constantly pools around your feet during times of stress. For 1 week, the first time each encounter that a primary or secondary caster takes an action to move, they must attempt a Reflex save or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Balance|Balance]] against the ritual’s casting DC or fall [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Prone|prone]]. A caster can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Step|Step]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Crawl|Crawl]] without having to attempt a check or save.
