# Confound the Eye and Confuse the Mind  *Ritual 1*

`Illusion`  `Uncommon`

- **Cast**: 1 hour
- **Cost**: costumes, props, and reagents, plus cloth worth a total value of 10 gp × the target’s level
- **Secondary Casters**: 1
- **Primary Check**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/skills/occultism|Occultism]] (trained)
- **Secondary Checks**: Deception , Performance , Occultism or Thievery
- **Range**: 10 feet
- **Target(s)**: 1 creature of a level no greater than double the ritual’s rank who can cast spells

With a flash, you obscure spellcasting to confound observers. The sleight of hand disguises one spell as another, augmented by costumes and props that lean into the viewer's assumption. A crooked hat and wand might make a druid appear to be a wizard, while a crown of leaves lets a wizard to slide into the druid's role.

**Critical Success** For the duration, all spells the target casts appears as another spell of their choice that is the same rank but of any tradition. This does not change the spells' effects. Creatures that attempt to [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/player-core/skill/recognize-spell|Recognize Spell]] must attempt a Will save (against the target's spell DC), even if they would automatically identify the spell normally. On a failure, they do not Recognize the Spell.

**Success** As critical success, but it only affects the next spell the target casts of the chosen magical tradition.

**Failure** Despite your chicanery, the target's magic remains recognizable.

**Critical Failure** The ritual fails and transforms the target's spellcasting to a louder, brighter, and more attention-grabbing version for 1 week.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 191*
