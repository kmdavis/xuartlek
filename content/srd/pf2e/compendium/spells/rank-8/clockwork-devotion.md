# Clockwork Devotion  *Spell 8*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Incarnate`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Tradition**: Arcane
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

You toss a handful of magical cogs before you, summoning a battalion of clockwork soldiers to fight your enemies. The clockwork soldiers occupy the space of a Huge creature and have a Speed of 60 feet.

**Arrive** _Clockwork Assault_ Clockwork soldiers slash at your enemies with their halberds, dealing 4d10 slashing damage ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save) to all enemy creatures in its location or within 15 feet.

**Depart** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/fire|fire]]) _Ticking Bomb_ The clockwork soldiers freeze abruptly before exploding into a mass of flying cogs and metal shrapnel, dealing 4d8 slashing damage and 4d8 fire damage in a 30-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/area#Emanation|emanation]] (basic Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature takes an additional 2d8 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent fire damage]].

*Source: Battlecry! p. 84*
