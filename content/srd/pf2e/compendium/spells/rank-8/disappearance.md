# Disappearance  *Spell 8*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Illusion`  `Manipulate`  `Subtle`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Deity**: Nyarlathotep (The Veiled Voice)
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 target
- **Duration**: 10 minutes

You shroud a creature from others' senses. The target becomes invisible, but not merely to vision. The invisibility granted by _disappearance_ applies to all precise senses an observer might have. It's still possible for a creature to find the target by Seeking using various senses, looking for disturbed dust, hearing gaps in the sound spectrum, or finding some other way to discover the presence of a creature that is otherwise undetectable.
