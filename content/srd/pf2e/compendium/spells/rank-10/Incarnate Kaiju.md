# Incarnate Kaiju  *Spell 10*  [>>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Incarnate`  `Manipulate`  `Rare`

- **Tradition**: [Primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=4)
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

You briefly conjure a kaiju, a massive, rampaging monster with a unique name and legendary reputation. It rises from its secluded lair to annihilate your foes. The summoned kaiju occupies the space of a Gargantuan creature. When you Cast this Spell, choose one of the kaiju below to summon.

- **Agmazar, the Star Titan** Speed 100 feet, climb 50 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Void|void]]) _Channel Void_ Each foe in a 100-foot emanation takes 16d8 void damage ([[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save); **Depart** _Gravitic Repulsion_ Each foe in a 50-foot emanation is pushed 100 feet away unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save.

- **Agyra, the Forever Storm** Speed 80 feet, fly 200 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]]) _Breath of a Thousand Storms_ Agyra shoots two 1,200-foot lines of electricity, one from each of her heads. The lines can't overlap. Each creature along one of the lines takes 6d12 electricity damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save. On a failure, the creature is also [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round (for 1 minute on a critical failure); **Depart** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Sonic|sonic]]) _Thunderous Blast_ Each creature within a 100-foot emanation takes 6d10 sonic damage with a basic Reflex save and is also knocked [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Prone|prone]] on a failed save.

- **Bezravnis, the Inferno Below** Speed 100 feet, burrow 100 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Earth|earth]]) _Burrow from the Earth's Heart_ Bezravnis explodes from the ground where it was summoned, causing all creatures in a 50-foot emanation to take 8d12 bludgeoning damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save. This potentially collapses structures with the collapse effect of the _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-8/Earthquake|_earthquake_]]_ spell, except there's no chance of falling into a fissure; **Depart** _Web Hurricane_ Bezravnis departs with a hurricane of webbing. All creatures within a 50-foot emanation must succeed at a Reflex save or take a –20-foot circumstance penalty to their Speeds until they [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Escape|Escape]] (or are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Immobilized|immobilized]] until they Escape on a critical failure). The Escape DC is your spell DC.

- **Ebeshra, the Winged Razor** Speed 75 feet, fly 100 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Teleportation|teleportation]]) _Planar Draw_ Each foe in a 100-foot emanation is pulled 50 feet toward the center, unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. Creatures that fail and reach the center take 10d6 bludgeoning damage; **Depart** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Electricity|electricity]]) _Storm Discharge_ All creatures in a 240-foot line takes 12d8 electricity damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save.

- **Mogaru, the Final King** Speed 100 feet, swim 100 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Auditory|auditory]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Mental|mental]]) _Trance of the King's Melody_ Mogaru manifests with a burst of the song that summoned him, requiring all foes within 60 feet who can hear him to attempt a Will save, leaving them [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Stunned|stunned 3]] on a failure; **Depart** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]]) _Volcanic Breath_ Mogaru unleashes his scorching breath in a 120-foot cone. Each creature in the area takes 10d6 fire damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save. On a failure, it also takes 4d6 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Persistent Damage|persistent fire damage]].

- **Vorgozen, the Shapeless Feeder** Speed 50 feet, climb 50 feet, swim 100 feet; **Arrive** _Pollute Magic_ Vorgozen's foul presence pollutes magic around her. Each enemy within a 60-foot emanation that's under the effects of a spell must attempt a Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/Conditions#Sickened|sickened 2]]. Any of your enemies that Casts a Spell within 60 feet of Vorgozen must attempt a Fortitude save with the same effects unless it's already sickened; **Depart** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Acid|acid]]) _Beam of Purest Vitriol_ Each creature in a 1,200-foot line takes 10d6 acid damage and 10d6 bludgeoning damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Fortitude save.

- **Yarthoon, the Moon Grub** Speed 60 feet, burrow 100 feet, fly 100 feet, swim 100 feet; **Arrive** ([[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Cold|cold]]) _Algid Beam Barrage_ Yarthoon fires countless beams, targeting each of your enemies within 200 feet of it. The beams deal 10d6 cold damage with a [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex save; **Depart** (cold, [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Water|water]]) _Frostbite Mist_ Chilling mist surges out in a 100-foot burst centered on Yarthoon and remains for 1 minute. This mist has the effects of _[[srd/pf2e/compendium/spells/rank-2/Mist|_mist_]]_, plus any creature within the area at the end of its turn takes 5d6 cold damage (basic Fortitude save).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 146*
