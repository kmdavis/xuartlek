# Planar Collision  *Spell 10*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: [Arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1), [Primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=4)
- **Range**: 300 feet
- **Area**: 100-foot [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Area#Burst|burst]]
- **Defense**: [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Checks#Basic Saving Throws|basic]] Reflex

You rip out a portion of an elemental plane and smash it into your plane, unleashing a chaotic maelstrom. When you Cast this Spell, choose an elemental plane. The collision deals 8d6 force damage and 8d8 damage of a type depending on the chosen plane to each creature in the area with a basic Reflex save. A creature that fails the saving throw is also pushed 25 feet away from the center of the burst (or 50 feet on a critical failure). Each creature whose movement is stopped by a firm surface, such as a well, takes bludgeoning damage equal to the remaining number of feet it would've moved. The area also becomes [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Movement#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and hazardous terrain that lasts for a year and a day or until [[srd/pf2e/books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/Afflictions#Counteracting|counteracted]] by a spell or effect such as _dispel magic_. Any creature that ends its turn in the hazardous terrain takes 15 damage of a type depending on the chosen plane.

- **Plane of Air** The damage dealt is acid damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Air|air]] trait.
- **Plane of Earth** The damage dealt is bludgeoning damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Earth|earth]] trait.
- **Plane of Fire** The damage dealt is fire damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Fire|fire]] trait.
- **Plane of Metal** The damage dealt is slashing damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Metal|metal]] trait.
- **Plane of Water** The damage dealt is cold damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Water|water]] trait.
- **Plane of Wood** The damage dealt is piercing damage, and the spell gains the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/Wood|wood]] trait.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 157*
