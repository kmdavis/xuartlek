# Trade Death for Life  *Cantrip 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Cantrip`  `Healing`  `Hex`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Witch`

- **Patron Theme**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/patrons#Paradox of Opposites|Paradox of Opposites]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

Your patron steals life from one of your enemies to grant it to another. The target takes 1d4 [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/void|void]] damage (basic Fortitude save). If the target takes damage, a willing creature within the hex’s range gains fast healing 1 for as long as you [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Sustain|Sustain]] the hex. The target takes damage only once from this spell, whether or not you Sustain it.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4 and the fast healing increases by 1.

*Source: Divine Mysteries p. 297*
