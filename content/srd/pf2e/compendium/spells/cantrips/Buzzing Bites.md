# Buzzing Bites  *Cantrip 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Cantrip`  `Hex`  `Manipulate`  `Rare`  `Witch`

- **Patron Theme**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/patrons#Mosquito Witch|Mosquito Witch]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

You evoke buzzing and crawling insects to climb onto a foe’s body and bite, dealing 1d4 piercing damage and potentially sickening the foe, depending on its Fortitude save. Once this spell ends, the target becomes temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half damage.

**Failure** The target takes full damage.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Sickened|sickened]] 1 by the crawling insects. The sickened value can’t be reduced below 1 while the spell is active.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
