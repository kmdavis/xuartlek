# Void Warp  *Cantrip 1*  [>>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cantrip`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult
- **Bloodline**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/bloodlines#Undead|Undead]]
- **Mystery**: [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/mysteries#Bones|Bones]]
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 living creature
- **Defense**: basic Fortitude

You call upon the Void to harm life force. The target takes 2d4 void damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the target is also enfeebled 1 until the start of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
