# Shroud of Night  *Cantrip 1*  [>](books/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Cantrip`  `Darkness`  `Hex`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Witch`

- **Patron Theme**: Starless Shadow
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Will
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

Your patron blankets the target's eyes in darkness. If you cast this hex on a willing ally (for instance, one with light blindness), the ally can choose which result it gets without rolling.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is shrouded in murky darkness. It treats bright light as dim light, and unless it has greater darkvision, all creatures are [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Concealed|concealed]] to it.

*Source: Player Core p. 387*
