# Movement and Location

<sup>DA p. 202</sup>

Changing location is usually easy within a psychic duel, as a participant can move at the speed of thought. It typically takes only a single action to move to any location the participant wants and range of attacks and spells rarely matter much. You can usually run a psychic duel without a grid. Functions of positioning and movement still matter, but have a more flexible appearance. For instance, a participant can [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Hide|Hide]], but this likely means shaping the imaginary environment around them to create a visual screen, glowing phenomenon, or even psychic static. Likewise, spells that [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Immobilized|immobilize]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Grapple|grab]] a creature can prevent the easy movement detailed above.
