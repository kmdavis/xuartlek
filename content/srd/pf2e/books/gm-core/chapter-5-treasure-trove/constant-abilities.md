# Constant Abilities

<sup>GMC p. 219</sup>

Some magic items have abilities that always function. PCs don't have to use any actions to do anything special (beyond wearing and investing a worn item or wielding a held item) to make these abilities work. For example, an *[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/held-items/everlight-crystal|everlight crystal]]* always sheds light, and a *[[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/runes/flaming-greater|flaming weapon]]* deals fire damage every time it deals damage.
