# Potions

<sup>GMC p. 259</sup>

A potion is a magical liquid that is activated when you drink it, which uses it up. Potions have the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/potion|potion]] trait. You can activate a potion with an [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/actions/player-core#Interact|Interact]] action as you drink it or feed it to another creature. You can feed a potion only to a creature that is within reach and willing or otherwise so helpless that it can’t resist. You usually need only one hand to consume a potion or feed it to another creature.
