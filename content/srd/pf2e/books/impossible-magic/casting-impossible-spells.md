# Casting Impossible Spells

<sup>IM p. 174</sup>

[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/impossible-magic/impossible|Impossible]] spells are counted separately from a caster's list of learned spells, spell repertoire, or similar spellcasting class features. They are in a separate list of their own and don't consume a spell slot when cast. While most impossible spells are simply recorded as knowledge within a book, it's possible for them to exist in the form of a scroll, wand, staff, or other magical implement. Even in such forms, the power required to cast an impossible spell means only trained spellcasters can use the item. The items are also always consumed in the process of casting the impossible spell, preventing it from becoming an artifact or relic that can be passed on easily.

**Fueling:** The impossible spell can be further fueled by the caster pouring more of themself into the spell to make it more powerful. After casting an impossible spell, the caster can use the Fuel Impossible Spell action.

### Fuel Impossible Spell [one-action]

[[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/player-core/exploration|Exploration]]
**Source** Impossible Magic pg. 174

---

You put more of yourself on the line to draw forth the hidden potentials behind an impossible spell. You empower the effect of one of your active impossible spells further as detailed under the spell's description. Every time you Fuel an Impossible Spell, you become [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/conditions#Doomed|doomed]] 1 or increase your doomed condition by 1 if you' re already doomed.
