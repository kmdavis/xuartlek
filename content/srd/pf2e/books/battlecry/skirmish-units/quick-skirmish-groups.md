# Quick Skirmish Groups

<sup>BC p. 169</sup>

If you want to have a skirmish encounter that’s ready to go, here are some skirmish encounters pre-built for certain levels. They include one troop per PC for a four-person party, but you can remove troops or add duplicate troops to each side to adjust. Creatures come from the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/rules-elements/traits/npc-core/troop|troop]] trait.

## Undead Soldiers (Level 4)

**Threat** Low (60 XP)
**PC Troops** [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/humanoid/apprentice-magician-clique|apprentice magician clique]] (60 XP), 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/humanoid/goblin-rabble|goblin rabbles]] (40 XP each), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/humanoid/orc-raiding-party|orc raiding party]] (60 XP)
**Enemy Commanders** 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/undead/wight|wights]] (30 XP each) ; **Enemy Troops** [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/undead/skeleton-mob|skeleton mob]] (80 XP), 3 shambler troops (40 XP each)

## Clash of Steel (Level 6)

**Threat** Moderate (70 XP)
**Player Troops** 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/dwarf-battalion|dwarf battalions]] 40 XP each), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/animal/heavy-cavalry|heavy cavalry]] (60 XP), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/line-infantry|line infantry]] (40 XP)
**Enemy Commanders** [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/humanoid/hobgoblin-archer|hobgoblin archer]] (20 XP), 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/monster-core/humanoid/hobgoblin-general|hobgoblin generals]] MC (40 XP each);
**Enemy Troops** [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/goblin-get-gang|goblin get gang]] (30 XP), 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/hobgoblin-battalion|hobgoblin battalions]] NPCC (40 XP each), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/animal/qadiran-camel-corps|Qadiran camel corps]] (40 XP)

## Abominable Armies (Level 8)

**Threat** Moderate (80 XP)
**PC Troops** [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/construct/animated-army|animated army]] (40 XP), 2 [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/battlecry/humanoid/hobgoblin-veteran-regiment|hobgoblin veteran regiments]] (60 XP each), [[srd/pf2e/bestiary/npc-core/humanoid/woodland-scouts|woodland scouts]] (40 XP)
