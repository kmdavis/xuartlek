# Aquatic PCs

<sup>HotW p. 9</sup>

[[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/athamaru|Athamarus]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/merfolk|merfolk]], and some [[srd/pf2e/compendium/character/ancestries/awakened-animal|awakened animals]] can be amphibious or aquatic. If your players are interested in playing an aquatic character in a primarily terrestrial campaign, consider starting them off with assistive devices such as the [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/atmospheric-breathing-suit|atmospheric breathing suit]], [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/adventuring-gear/pelagic-helmet|pelagic helmet]], or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/equipment/assistive-items/wheelchair-land-delvers-chair|supramarine chair]] at no additional cost. For a less technological option, you might consider granting a PC feats such as [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/ancestry/land-legs|Land Legs]] or [[srd/pf2e/compendium/feats/howl-of-the-wild/ancestry/shore-gift|Shore Gift]] as a bonus feat at level 1, possibly adding certain narrative limitations like “the merfolk PC gains Shore Gift as a bonus feat, but loses its benefits when the moon is full” if they suit your story.
