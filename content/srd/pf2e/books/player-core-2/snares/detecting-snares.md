# Detecting Snares

<sup>PC2 p. 297</sup>

Creatures can detect snares they aren't already aware of as they would any [[srd/pf2e/books/gm-core/chapter-2-building-games/hazards|trap or hazard]], using the creator's Crafting DC as the snare's Stealth DC. If your proficiency rank is expert or better in Crafting, only creatures actively searching can find your snares
