# Anointed Waterskin  *Item 9*

`Divine`  `Holy`  `Water`

- **Price**: 600 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

This waterskin coruscates with holy energy. After using any of the activations, the waterskin is empty, but slowly refills itself. It becomes full enough to use again at the next dawn. 

**Activate—Blessed Bombard** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You throw the _anointed waterskin_ up to 60 feet. The water explodes out of it, with the effects of a DC 25 4th-rank __holy cascade__. 

**Activate—Divine Decanting** 1 minute (manipulate) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You decant the water, creating up to 10 vials of _holy water_. You must provide the vials. Holy water created this clearly temporary upon inspection, and fades away after 8 hours. 

**Activate—Sacred Sip** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You drink the water within the waterskin, granting you the effects of __bless__. As normal, you can increase the spell’s radius by Sustaining it.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 232*
