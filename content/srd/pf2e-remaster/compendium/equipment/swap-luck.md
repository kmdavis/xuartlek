# Swap Luck  *Relic Minor Gift*

- **Aspect**: Luck

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You roll a failure or critical failure on a saving throw against an effect with multiple targets; **Effect** You swap rolls with another target who's willing to swap with you. Each of you determines the new results with your new rolls.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 199*
