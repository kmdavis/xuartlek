# Chained Mist  *Item 13*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 3,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Chain Shirt

Made of standard-grade dawnsilver, mist spirals around this _+1 resilient greater shadow dawnsilver chain shirt_. When worn, silver vapor trails interwoven with wispy threads of shadow bedeck and move in harmony with the wearer’s limbs and torso. If the wearer is in dim light, the mist obscures the wearer’s form, granting the wearer the concealed condition. 

**Activate—Misty Mesh** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The mist immediately solidifies into a protective mesh of silvery chains that gives you resistance 10 to spirit damage for 1 minute.
