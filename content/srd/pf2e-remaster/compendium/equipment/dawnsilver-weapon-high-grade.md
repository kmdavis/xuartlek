# Dawnsilver Weapon (High-Grade)  *Item 11+*

`Uncommon`

- **Usage**: varies by weapon
- **Bulk**: varies by weapon
- **Base Material**: Dawnsilver

Dawnsilver weapons are slightly lighter than silver. A dawnsilver weapon is 1 Bulk lighter than normal (or light Bulk if its normal Bulk is 1, with no effect on a weapon that normally has light Bulk).

### Dawnsilver Weapon (Standard-Grade)

**Price** 1,400 gp (+140 gp per Bulk)

**Craft Requirements** at least 175 gp of dawnsilver + 17.5 gp per Bulk

### Dawnsilver Weapon (High-Grade)

**Price** 13,500 gp (+1350 gp per Bulk)

**Craft Requirements** at least 6,750 gp of dawnsilver + 675 gp per Bulk
