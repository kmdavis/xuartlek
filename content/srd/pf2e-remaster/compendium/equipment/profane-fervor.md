# Profane Fervor  *Relic Major Gift*

`Unholy`

- **Aspect**: Fiend

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Profane energies twist your weapons, filling them with rage toward everything good. For 1 minute, your weapons and unarmed attacks gain the _unholy_ weapon rune while you hold them. If a weapon is at its limit on property runes, you can choose one property rune on the weapon to go dormant while the _unholy_ rune takes effect. In addition, Strikes your allies make while they're within 30 feet of you gain the unholy trait.

*Source: GM Core p. 314*
