# Feral Claws  *Relic Minor Gift*

`Morph`

- **Aspect**: Beast
- **Prerequisite**: The relic is a worn item.

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate); **Effect** Your hands transform into a pair of claws. These grant you a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage and has the agile and finesse traits. This lasts until you Dismiss the effect.
