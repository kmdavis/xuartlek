# Grisly Brigandine  *Item 12*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 2,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Studded Leather Armor

This _+1 resilient studded leather armor_ is a gruesome amalgamation of skulls and bones from various creatures held in place with straps of leather that resemble sinew. When you wear this armor, you reek of death, and you can display these trappings in such a way that strikes fear in the hearts of your enemies.

**Activate—Unveil Fear** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, occult) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature moves within 30 feet of you; **Effect** You cast _fear_ on the target (DC 30 Will save).

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _fear_.
