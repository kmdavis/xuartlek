# Wandmender  *Item 9*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 700 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Mace

Nearly every surface of this heavy _+1 striking mace_ is inscribed with runes of crafting and restoration. The core of the mace hums with an eerie resonance, as if devouring stray magical energies from the air. 

**Activate—Recharge Wand** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You’re wielding a wand capable of casting spells of 3rd rank or lower, and its spell has been cast for the day; **Effect** You tap the _wandmender_ against the wand, restoring its magic. You can Cast the Spell in the wand a second time without overcharging, as long as you do so within the next minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 211*
