# Sage's Lash  *Item 18*

`Apex`  `Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 24,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn belt
- **Bulk**: 1

The thin, glittering strands of rope that form this thick belt appear to be spun gold. Strung along the front of the belt are a collection of four multicolored, perfectly spherical beads: amethyst, jade, quartz, and turquoise. While wearing the lash, you gain a +3 item bonus to Religion. When you invest the belt, you either increase your Wisdom modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value. 

**Activate—Jewel of the Mind** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Effect** You touch one of the jewels affixed to the _sage’s lash_ and think the associated command word. Depending on the jewel, a different effect is produced that affects you and all living creatures in a 30-foot emanation. After the effect occurs, all four jewels disappear from the lash, reappearing at the next dawn.
**• Amethyst** (light, revelation) Think “verity” to emit a magical trail of light from the lash that reveals the location of each creature in the emanation. For 1 round, if they would be invisible, they become concealed instead, and if they would be undetected or unnoticed by a creature, they’re hidden instead. A creature can attempt a DC 43 Stealth check to avoid the lash’s detection.
**• Jade** (healing) Think “purity” to produce a wave of green light that ends any sickened condition and any persistent poison damage affecting creatures in the emanation.
**• Quartz** (healing, vitality) Think “lenity” to have each creature regain 30 Hit Points.
**• Turquoise** Think “clarity” to grant all affected creatures a +2 status bonus to their next Will save for 1 minute.
