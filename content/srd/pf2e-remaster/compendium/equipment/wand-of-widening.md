# Wand of Widening  *Item 4+*

`Magical`  `Wand`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

The end of this wand is forked with a peridot setting.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; This activation takes [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") if the spell normally takes [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") to cast, or [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") if the spell normally takes [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action"); **Effect** You Cast the Spell, and increase its area. Add 5 feet to the radius of a burst that normally has a radius of at least 10 feet; add 5 feet to the length of a cone or line that is normally 15 feet long or smaller; or add 10 feet to the length of a larger cone or line.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate rank. The spell must have a casting time of [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") or [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action"), can't have a duration, and must have an area of burst (10 feet or more), cone, or line.
