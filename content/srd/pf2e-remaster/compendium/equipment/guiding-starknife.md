# Guiding Starknife  *Item 7*

`Divine`

- **Price**: 325 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Weapon**: Starknife

This _+1 striking returning starknife_ is made of dark blue metal overlaid with smoky flecks of fused quartz. When you Cast a Spell and deal damage, your next attack before the end of your turn with this starknife ignores standard and lesser cover. 

**Activate—Points of Light** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, spirit) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with a thrown Strike with the _guiding starknife_; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The _guiding starknife_’s blades detach, form into darts of starlight, and deal 4 piercing damage plus 1d4 spirit damage to the creature you just hit. This piercing damage increases to 6 with a _greater striking_ rune and to 8 with a _major striking_ rune. The _guiding starknife_ then re-forms and returns to your hand. Until the start of your next turn, the damaged creature is outlined with soft light and can’t gain the effects of concealment.
