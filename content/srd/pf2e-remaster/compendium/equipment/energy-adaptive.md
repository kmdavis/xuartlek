# Energy Adaptive  *Item 13*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 2,600 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

A complex pattern of protective symbols gives protection against various forms of energy, but only part of the runic structure can be active at a given time.

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You take acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage; **Effect** You gain resistance 5 to the triggering damage type. This doesn't apply to the triggering damage. This resistance lasts until you Activate this rune again or the armor is no longer invested by you.
