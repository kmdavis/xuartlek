# Size-Changing  *Item 7*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 350 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

This armor can make itself and its wearer quickly change size. 

**Activate—Change Size** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The armor casts your choice of _enlarge_ or _shrink_ on you.
