# Shield Of Mental Fortitude  *Item 10*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 1,000 gp
- **Usage**: strapped to 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Shield**: Buckler

This small steel buckler (Hardness 6, HP 24, BT 12) is engraved with an unbroken golden circle. While you have this shield raised, you gain resistance 5 to mental damage. 

**Activate—Mind Backlash** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You succeed at a Will save against a mental effect while the _shield of mental fortitude_ is raised; **Effect** You return a wave of psychic energy to the creature. The creature that caused the triggering mental effect must succeed at a DC 27 Will save or become stupefied 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
