# Mythic Resilent  *Item 20*

`Magical`  `Mythic`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 70,000 gp
- **Usage**: etched onto armor

Mythic resilient runes imbue armor with unrivaled protection from a wide array of effects. The armor grants a +4 item bonus to saving throws to the wearer.

**Activate—Defy Obliteration** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** You critically fail a saving throw; **Effect** Spend a Mythic Point; if the triggering save was made due to an effect created by a mythic monster, hazard, or other effect, it’s a normal failure instead. If the save was made due to an effect that wasn’t mythic, it becomes a success.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic resilient runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character capable of making a Crafting check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).

*Source: War of Immortals p. 149*
