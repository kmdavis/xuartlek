# Sanguine Klar  *Item 9+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Shield**: Klar

This _+1 striking wounding klar_ (Hardness 8, HP 64, BT 32) is built with spikes made of beasts' fangs.

**Activate** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per round; **Trigger** You or a creature within 5 feet of you suffers bleed damage; **Effect** The _sanguine klar_ regains a number of Hit Points equal to the bleed damage, up to an amount equal to its Hardness.
