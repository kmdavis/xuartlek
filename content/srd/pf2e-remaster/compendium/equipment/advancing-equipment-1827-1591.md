# Advancing  *Item 9+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: etched onto heavy armor

**Activate** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Requirements** Your last action or activity reduced an enemy to 0 Hit Points; **Effect** You Stride up to 15 feet. This movement doesn't trigger reactions. You can Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
