# Autumn's Embrace  *Item 12*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 2,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Leaf Weave

Woven by fey seamstresses as rewards for servants of nature, countless leaves continually changing colors in autumnal hues comprise autumn's embrace, a suit of _+2 invisibility resilient leaf weave_. Leaves shed from the armor as they might fall in autumn. When activating the armor's _invisibility_ property rune, you disappear in a swirl of colorful leaves.

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (aura, concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You call forth a storm of leaves from _autumn's embrace_. These leaves swirl in a 20-foot emanation for 1 minute. Creatures within the area are concealed, and creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within the leaves. However, you can see through this concealment. You can Dismiss the activation.
