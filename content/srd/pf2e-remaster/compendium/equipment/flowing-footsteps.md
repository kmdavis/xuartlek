# Flowing Footsteps  *Relic Major Gift*

`Water`

- **Aspect**: Water

Your relic ensures no water impedes your movement. You gain the effects of the _water walk_ spell, and the ability to breathe water. You also gain a swim Speed equal to your Speed or 15 feet, whichever is higher.
