# Emberheart  *Item 18*

`Apex`  `Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 24,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn

This small, heart-shaped amulet appears to be sculpted from stone with a single streak of dim light running through, like an ember just beneath ashes. When worn, the amulet gives off a gentle warmth, akin to being near a fireplace. You gain poison resistance 15 when wearing this amulet. When you invest the amulet, you either increase your Constitution modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value. 

**Activate—Warming Heart** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (healing, magical, manipulate, vitality) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You hold the amulet aloft as a ripple of warm orange light exudes outward. Each ally in a 30-foot emanation regains 30 Hit Points and gains a +3 status bonus to Fortitude saves until the end of their next turn.
