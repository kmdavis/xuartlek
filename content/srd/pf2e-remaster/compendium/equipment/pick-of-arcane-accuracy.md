# Pick Of Arcane Accuracy  *Item 5*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 160 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Pick

The metal head of this _+1 striking pick_ glows faintly, intensifying around the tip. The hilt features a grip wrapped in deep-blue leather and is carved with a hawk’s wings. 

**Activate—Mystic Strike** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ; **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Requirements** Your last action was to Cast a non-cantrip Spell; **Effect** You make a melee Strike with the pick that overflows with magical energy. You gain a +1 status bonus to this Strike, and if you hit, you deal an additional 1d4 persistent force damage.
