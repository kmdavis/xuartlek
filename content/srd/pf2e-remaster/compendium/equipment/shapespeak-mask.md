# Shapespeak Mask  *Item 4*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Transmutation`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 80 gp
- **Usage**: worn mask

This simple wooden mask is carved into the shape of an animal, usually tailored to a species of beast that the user favors. While wearing this mask, you can speak even when polymorphed into an animal. The _shapespeak mask_ does not otherwise remove any limitations, such as the inability to cast spells when transformed.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 155*
