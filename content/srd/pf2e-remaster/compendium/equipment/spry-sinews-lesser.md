# Spry Sinews (Lesser)  *Item 3+*

`Graft`  `Invested`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: implanted

The tendons in your legs are uncommonly stretchy. When you Leap, increase the distance traveled by the listed amount.

### Spry Sinews (Lesser)

**Price** 55 gp

---

### Spry Sinews (Greater)
