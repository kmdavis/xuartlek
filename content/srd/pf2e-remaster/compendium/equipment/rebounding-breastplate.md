# Rebounding Breastplate  *Item 20*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 70,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Armor**: Breastplate

This _+3 greater resilient greater fortification breastplate_ absorbs impacts for later release. While wearing the armor, you gain resistance 5 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage and resistance 10 to force damage. Keep track of how much damage the armor prevents from enemy attacks, as the armor absorbs that damage. After 1 minute, the absorbed damage disperses harmlessly and resets to 0. Only damage caused by foes or hazards powers the armor, not damage you take from yourself, allies, or the environment. 

**Activate—Kinetic Pulse** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (force, manipulate) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** Your armor has absorbed at least 30 damage; **Effect** The absorbed energy explodes from your armor in a pulse of force. Creatures in a 30-foot emanation take 12d6 force damage with a DC 43 basic Fortitude save. On a failure, they’re also pushed 10 feet away from you; on a critical failure, they’re instead pushed 20 feet away from you and fall prone. The armor’s absorbed damage resets to 0.
