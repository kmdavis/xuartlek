# Eye of Apprehension  *Item 12*

`Consumable`  `Fortune`  `Magical`  `Talisman`

- **Price**: 400 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to armor
- **Activate**: [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate)
- **Trigger**: You are about to roll Perception for initiative but haven't rolled yet
