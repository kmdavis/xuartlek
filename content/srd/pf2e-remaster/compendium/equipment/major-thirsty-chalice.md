# Major Thirsty Chalice  *Item 3+*

`Magical`  `Spellheart`

- **Usage**: affixed to armor or a weapon

This slate chalice improbably holds one single drop of water in it at all times and helps refresh the wearer, sometimes at the expense of their enemies.
**• Armor** After you cast a spell by Activating the chalice, you are surrounded by a fine mist. You become concealed until the beginning of your next turn.
**• Weapon** (void) After you cast a spell by Activating the chalice, your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1 void damage until the end of your next turn; the first time in a round you successfully deal this void damage, you gain 4 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 minute. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell **Effect** You cast __draw moisture_ ._
