# Ring of Ravenousness  *Item 7*

`Cursed`  `Invested`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Usage**: worn

Carved of acacia wood, a _ring of ravenousness_ is inlaid with a geometric pattern. The ring appears to be a _ring of sustenance_. Once you invest it, though, it fuses to you, its effects activating immediately. While wearing the ring, you require twice the normal amount of food and drink for a creature your size to avoid starvation and thirst.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 192*
