# Bottomless Reservoir  *Relic Major Gift*

`Extradimensional`  `Water`

- **Aspect**: Water

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** Your relic can absorb and release water, storing it in an endless reservoir. You either touch your relic to an adjacent body of water and absorb 1 gallon of it into the reservoir, or you release 1 gallon of a liquid from the reservoir. You can add another action to the activation to absorb water from a touched creature made entirely of water, like a water elemental. If you do, it takes 1d6 damage per relic level (basic Fortitude save).

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate) **Trigger** You are targeted by an acid or water effect; **Effect** Your relic attempts to counteract the effect, expending 1 gallon of stored liquid. At the GM's discretion, you can use this ability on effects made of other liquids, such as blood.

*Source: GM Core p. 319*
