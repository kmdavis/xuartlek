# Wand of Continuation (4th-rank Spell)  *Item 5+*

`Magical`  `Wand`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This wand increases a spell’s duration. Yellow embers spiral over its surface until the spell ends.

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; the activation takes [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") if the spell normally takes [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") to cast, or [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") if the spell normally takes [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action"); **Effect** You Cast the Spell, and its duration is increased by half.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate rank. The spell must have a casting time of [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") or [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") and a duration no less than 10 minutes and no greater than 1 hour.
