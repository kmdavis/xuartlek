# Spring Heel  *Item 5*

`Clockwork`  `Mechanical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 150 gp
- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: 1

Fitted into each of these prosthetic legs is a large spring, tightly bound around a collapsible shaft. When released, the spring unspools rapidly and the shaft telescopes out and back, returning to its compressed form and catapulting you forward.

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You Stride up to twice your speed or Leap up to 20 feet horizontally and 5 feet vertically.
