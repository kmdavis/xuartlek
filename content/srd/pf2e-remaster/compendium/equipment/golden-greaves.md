# Golden Greaves  *Item 4*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 80 gp
- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: L

These shiny greaves made of splinted metal coated with gold protect the shins and help you stand your ground in the heat of battle. While wearing the greaves, you gain a +1 item bonus to your Fortitude DC against forced movement effects and to your Reflex DC against effects that would knock you prone.

**Activate—Make Them Fall** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, misfortune) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy fails to Reposition, Shove, or Trip you; **Effect** Your _golden greaves_ glow with a strange light, and you move your legs in just the right way to completely throw off your opponent. Your opponent instead critically fails on the triggering check.
