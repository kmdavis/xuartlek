# Serpent's Lash  *Item 8*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 450 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Whip

This _+1 striking whip_ was fashioned with the enchanted leather of a giant serpent. Several vicious fangs are embedded in the thong and fall of the whip. When wielded in combat, the weapon emanates a faint rattling sound. 

**Activate—Slither Strike** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ; **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature within reach Casts a Spell with the manipulate trait; **Requirements** ; **Effect** You Strike the triggering creature with the whip. If you hit and deal damage, you also Reposition the creature 5 feet (10 feet on a critical hit).
