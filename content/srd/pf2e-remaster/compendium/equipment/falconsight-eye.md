# Falconsight Eye  *Item 6*

`Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 200 gp
- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: L

This prosthetic eye resembles that of a bird of prey. Along with the abilities of the _magical prosthetic eye_, it allows you to strike foes at greater range and with impressive accuracy.

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You become keenly aware of your foes, even those seemingly out of reach. For 1 minute, you can close your eyes as a free action to see through a ranged weapon you're wielding, which reduces the penalty for firing into your weapon's second range increment from –2 to 0. This effect doesn't negate the blinded condition.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 108*
