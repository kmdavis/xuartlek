# Tracking Fulu  *Item 5*

`Consumable`  `Fulu`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 25 gp
- **Usage**: affixed to creature or object
- **Activate**: [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate)

Used covertly by assassins and spies, this pair of fulus stick to one another when created but can be easily separated. Affix one fulu to a target before activating its pair. When activated, the unaffixed fulu flutters toward the affixed one at a Speed of 30 feet per round, traveling for up to 1 hour and fluttering more rapidly the closer it comes to its pair. The unaffixed fulu always moves in a straight line toward the affixed fulu; it can’t solve mazes or find its way through winding paths.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 221*
