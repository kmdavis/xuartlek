# Greater Staff of Arcane Might  *Item 12+*

`Magical`  `Rare`  `Staff`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

This staff of magically hardened wood is topped with a silver sculpture depicting magical runic symbols. A staff of arcane might is a _+1 striking staff_. 

**Activate—Sunder** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (death, force, manipulate) **Effect** You destroy the staff, unleashing a blast of arcane power in a 30- foot cone, dealing 2d6 force damage per charge remaining in the staff with a DC 30 basic Reflex save. 

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
