# Curse Wound  *Relic Minor Gift*

`Curse`  `Unholy`

- **Aspect**: Fiend
- **Prerequisite**: The relic is a weapon.

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** Your Strike with your relic deals damage to a creature within 60 feet; **Effect** The evil within your relic curses an enemy's wound to ensure they won't heal. The triggering creature must attempt a Will save or be unable to restore the Hit Points lost by damage from the triggering Strike until its next daily preparations. If you use Curse Wound on a creature already affected by your Curse Wound, your previous application expires.

*Source: GM Core p. 313*
