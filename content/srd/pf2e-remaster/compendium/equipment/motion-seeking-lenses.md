# Motion-Seeking Lenses  *Item 3*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 55 gp
- **Usage**: worn eyepiece
- **Bulk**: L

When you wear these green-tinted glasses, all the movement in the surrounding area seems to jump out at you. You gain a +1 item bonus to your Perception DC against Stealth checks to Hide or Sneak, and anyone attempting to Sneak doesn’t benefit from the circumstance bonus from cover against your Perception DC.

**Activate—Find the Hidden** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (detection, manipulate) **Effect** You twist the lenses of your glasses as you look for someone hidden. You Seek with a +1 item bonus. If you find a hidden creature or object, you can Point Out as a free action.
