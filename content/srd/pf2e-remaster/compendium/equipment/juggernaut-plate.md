# Juggernaut Plate  *Item 19*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Rare`

- **Price**: 35,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Armor**: Full Plate

When you are wearing this armor, you’re a veritable battering ram. This _+3 greater resilient fortification full plate_ is topped with a reinforced helmet shaped like a ram’s head that enables you to smash through doors and gates.

**Activate—Ram** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You Stride up to 60 feet in a straight line. If you end your movement adjacent to an enemy, object, or structure, you can smash it. If you smash an enemy, you deal bludgeoning damage equal to the total distance traveled (DC 39 basic Fortitude save). Objects and structures you smash take damage equal to twice the distance traveled.
