# Silvered Marp Fur  *Item 9*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 110 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell

A marp that gnaws on silver rather than gold grows fur tipped with silver that can be further processed into a versatile spell catalyst. A spellcaster who uses _silvered marp fur_ with an _impaling spike_ spell creates a silver spike rather than a cold iron one.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 119*
