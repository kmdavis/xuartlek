# Rustbringer  *Item 10+*

`Magical`  `Metal`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Weapon**: Flail

The handle, chain, and spiked ball of this _+1 striking corrosive flail_ are all made of iron so rusted that the weapon appears nonfunctional at first glance. On Strikes against a creature that's primarily made of metal, it gains the deadly d10 trait.

**Activate—Rusting Disarm** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") ; **Trigger** You critically succeed at a Disarm attempt with the flail against a metal weapon; **Effect** Flakes of acid-laden rust are deposited on the disarmed weapon. The weapon takes 2d6 damage, ignoring the weapon's Hardness.
