# Psalter Of The Blessed  *Item 12*

`Grimoire`  `Holy`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,800 gp
- **Bulk**: L

These gold-leafed volumes bear the religious symbol of a deity who allows or requires holy sanctification. Unholy creatures are drained 2 as long as they keep the tome in their possession and can’t recover from this condition. 

**Activate—Righteous Prayers** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, holy, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared Spell with the holy trait, the energy blesses you and your allies with celestial power. You and all allies within 30 feet of you gain fast healing 5 and a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws against effects from creatures with the unholy trait for 1 minute.
