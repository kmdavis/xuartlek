# Fulu Compendium  *Item 9*

`Mental`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 550 gp
- **Hands**: 1

This pamphlet-sized book records the various symbols seen on fulus and also contains some ready-made magic on a special page that restores itself every day at sunrise. Using the compendium as a reference grants a +2 item bonus to any skill checks to determine a fulu’s function or authenticity. 

**Activate—Paralyzing Page** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You rip a page from the fulu compendium and cast it in a wide arc; as it flies in that arc, it multiplies into a storm of fulus. All undead creatures in a 30-foot emanation are affected by a _spirit-sealing fulu_ with a save DC of 25.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 221*
