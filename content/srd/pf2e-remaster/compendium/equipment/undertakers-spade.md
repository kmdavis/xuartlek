# Undertaker's Spade  *Item 9*

`Catalyst`  `Consumable`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 125 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: Cast a Spell

Rites of rest are etched deep into the broken-off blade of a deceased gravekeeper’s shovel. When used as a catalyst to cast an __impaling spike__ spell, the blade disintegrates into dust, mixing with the earth and allowing the spell to damage incorporeal creatures as if it were a weapon imbued with a _ghost touch_ property rune. An incorporeal creature can be immobilized by a spike if it’s adjacent to a solid surface when struck; it can attempt to Escape as normal but can’t phase through the spike or escape to the Ethereal Plane.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 217*
