# Umbral Armor  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 1,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Studded Leather Armor

This dark _+1 resilient shadow studded leather armor_ is frequently used to bypass enemy positions or quickly travel around battlefield obstacles as it allows you to partially transit through the Netherworld.

**Activate—Shroud Stride** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, shadow) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You slip into the Netherworld for a moment, allowing you to Stride twice your speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions and ignores difficult and hazardous terrain. Shadows lingers on the armor, causing you to become concealed for 1 round afterward.
