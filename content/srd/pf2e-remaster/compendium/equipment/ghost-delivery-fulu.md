# Ghost Delivery Fulu  *Item 8*

`Consumable`  `Fulu`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 100 gp
- **Duration**: 1 week
- **Usage**: affixed to structure, object, or creature

A _ghost delivery fulu's_ inscription resembles a dovecote with spirits around it, and it has a prominent stamp in red wax. This fulu activates once affixed, becoming the homing location of _ghost courier fulus_ with a matching stamp. Delivered _ghost courier fulu_ messages stick to the _ghost delivery fulu_ but can be easily removed.
