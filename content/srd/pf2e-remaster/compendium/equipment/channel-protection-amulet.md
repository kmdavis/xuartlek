# Channel Protection Amulet  *Item 3*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 56 gp
- **Usage**: worn

This nugget of polished tektite is trapped in a cage of braided wire and hangs from a silken cord. When wearing this amulet, you gain resistance 5 against damage from _harm_ spells if you're living, or against _heal_ spells if you're undead.
