# Bottled Catharsis  *Item 2+*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Elixir`  `Healing`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L
- **Activate**: [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

This drink unlocks a flood of emotions that helps reset your mental state. When you drink this elixir, the elixir attempts to counteract each effect on you that has the emotion trait or is inflicting the stupefied condition on you, using the listed counteract rank and modifier.
