# Shadow Shroud  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,000 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Leather Armor

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _darkness_ (4th rank).
