# Nemesis Name  *Item 9*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Tattoo`

- **Price**: 650 gp
- **Usage**: tattooed on the body

You want revenge badly enough that you tattoo your nemesis's name in a place you can easily see to remember your vow to settle the score. Anyone who sees the tattoo senses your hatred of the named being. Receiving a new _nemesis name_ makes any you already have non-magical.

You gain a +2 status bonus to damage rolls against the creature named on your tattoo, but this nemesis gains a +1 status bonus to damage rolls against you.

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, mental) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** You can see your nemesis, and they're within 30 feet of you; **Effect** You focus your hatred into a mental scream. Your nemesis takes 3d6 mental damage, which they can resist with a basic DC 26 Will save. You take half as much damage as your nemesis does, and you can't reduce this damage in any way.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 121*
