# Fife of the Faithful  *Item 4*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 80 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: L

This small metal fife is of extraordinary fine quality, with gold filigree on the embouchure. A _fife of the faithful_ grants you a +1 item bonus to Performance checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Call to Arms** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (auditory, concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You play a rousing tune on the fife that carries across the battlefield. You and all allies in a 60-foot emanation gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws for 1 round.
