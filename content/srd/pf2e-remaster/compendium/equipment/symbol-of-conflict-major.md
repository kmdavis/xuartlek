# Symbol of Conflict (Major)  *Item 4+*

`Divine`  `Invested`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: worn

This tarnished necklace can be attuned only by someone who is holy or unholy. When you attune it, it transforms into your deity's religious symbol or a personal symbol if you don't have a deity. You receive a +1 item bonus to Religion and a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against holy and unholy effects. 

**Activate—Presence** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The symbol casts _bane_ or _bless_. The counteract DC of these effects is 18, and the counteract modifier is +8.

**Craft Requirements** You must be holy or unholy.

### Symbol of Conflict

**Price** 90 gp
