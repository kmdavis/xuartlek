# Conflagration Club  *Item 6*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 250 gp
- **Usage**: held in 2 hands
- **Bulk**: 2
- **Base Weapon**: Greatclub

A ring of magical crystals encircles the base of this _+1 striking greatclub_, allowing it to absorb and store magical energy. These crystals irregularly flash a deep crimson light. 

**Activate—Absorb Arcana** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, magical, manipulate) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** The club has at least 1 charge; **Effect** You flip the club around to blast a cone of energy from its base. Each creature in a 15-foot cone takes 1d6 damage per charge stored in the club, with a DC 20 basic Reflex save. The damage type is the same type the charges were attuned to. The club loses all its charges.
