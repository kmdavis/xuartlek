# Spirit Bulb (Greater)  *Item 10+*

`Consumable`  `Magical`  `Plant`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Activate**: [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate)

This magical bulb is harvested from an ancient grove rich in primal plant magic. When you activate the bulb, you either eat it to have it cast a 5th-rank _plant form_ spell affecting you, or plant it in the ground next to you to have it cast a 5th-rank _summon plant or fungus_ spell. If you choose the summoning option, the plant or fungus appears where you planted the bulb, and you can Sustain the activation to keep control of the creature.
