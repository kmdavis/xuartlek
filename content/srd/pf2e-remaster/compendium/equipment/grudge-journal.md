# Grudge Journal  *Item 11*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,350 gp
- **Bulk**: L

The margins of this clothbound journal’s pages are filled with complaints on all manner of topics, shifting to match whatever angers the bearer most. 

**Activate—Air Grievance** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, curse, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You inscribe the name or description of a creature you can see that has wronged you in some way. If your next action is to target that creature with a prepared spell with the curse trait that requires a saving throw, the creature also becomes drained 2 unless it critically succeeds its saving throw.
