# Suit Of False Impression  *Item 6*

`Illusion`  `Invested`  `Magical`  `Visual`

- **Price**: 200 gp
- **Usage**: worn garment
- **Bulk**: L
- **Base Armor**: Explorer's Clothing

This _+1 explorer’s clothing_ is a simple, practical, and unassuming two-piece outfit. The comfortable fabric is enchanted with illusory magic to make it look as if the wearer is wearing heavy armor (as __illusory disguise__). While wearing the suit, you gain a +1 item bonus to Deception checks to Impersonate someone who would normally wear heavy armor. 

**Activate—Faux Bulwark** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, emotion, fear, mental, visual) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You strike an intimidating pose that causes enemies to fear your supposedly heavily armored form. Each foe in a 15-foot emanation who can see you must attempt a DC 22 Will saving throw. On a failure, they’re frightened 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 203*
