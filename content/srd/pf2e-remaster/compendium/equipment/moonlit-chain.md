# Moonlit Chain  *Item 7*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 360 gp
- **Usage**: worn armor
- **Bulk**: 1
- **Base Armor**: Chain Shirt

This _+1 silver chain shirt_ has a collar adorned with stitched images of the phases of the moon. You can see in moonlight as though you had low-light vision. 

**Activate—Dim Sight** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You touch the stitched image of the new moon on the armor's collar and suppress the dazzled condition for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 33 gp of silver.
