# Magic Weapon (+1 Striking)  *Item 2+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: held in 1 hand

A _magic weapon_ is a weapon etched with only fundamental runes. A _weapon potency_ rune gives an item bonus to attack rolls with the weapon, and a _striking rune_ increases the weapon's number of weapon damage dice.
The Prices here are for all types of weapons. You don't need to adjust the Price from a club to a greataxe or the like. These weapons are made of standard materials, not precious materials such as cold iron.
