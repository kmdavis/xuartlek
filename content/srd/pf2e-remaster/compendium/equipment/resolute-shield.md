# Resolute Shield  *Item 12*

`Magical`

- **Price**: 1,800 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: 4
- **Base Shield**: Tower Shield

The edges of this large tower shield (Hardness 12, HP 96, BT 48) are dented and worn, bearing the scuffs and marks of decades of battle. 

**Activate—Stand Firm** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You have the _resolute shield_ raised, and you would take physical damage from a critically successful attack; **Effect** The _resolute shield_ prevents you from taking an amount of damage up to twice the shield’s Hardness. You and the shield each take any remaining damage.
