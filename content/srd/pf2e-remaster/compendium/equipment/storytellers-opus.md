# Storyteller's Opus  *Item 6*

`Grimoire`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 230 gp
- **Bulk**: L

This green spellbook with gold trim contains a cautionary story about a boy who was eaten by a wolf after he previously lied about wolf attacks. When opened, the engravings on the front seem to move in a loop, enacting this story. 

**Activate—Hoodwink** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, illusion) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature succeeds, but doesn’t critically succeed, at a Perception check to disbelieve an illusion spell you’ve cast today; **Effect** You quickly tell a fib to try to smooth over the inconsistencies in your illusion. Attempt a Deception check against the triggering creature’s Perception DC. If you succeed, the creature doesn’t disbelieve the illusion.
