# Impactful  *Item 10+*

`Force`  `Magical`

- **Usage**: etched onto a weapon

This rune thrums with pure magical energy. Weapons with an _impactful_ rune deal an additional 1d6 force damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, you can choose to make the target to attempt a DC 27 Fortitude save. On a failure, the target is pushed 5 feet away from you.

### Impactful

**Price** 1,000 gp

### Greater Impactful

**Price** 15,000 gp

---
