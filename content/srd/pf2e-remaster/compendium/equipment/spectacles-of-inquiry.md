# Spectacles of Inquiry  *Item 12*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 1,750 gp
- **Usage**: worn eyepiece

Anything viewed through these thin spectacles looks crisp and clear, and the earpieces accentuate sounds around you. You gain a +2 item bonus to Perception checks.

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The spectacles key in on someone to show you their social cues in perfect clarity. Choose a creature you can see. You gain a +3 item bonus on Perception checks you make to use Sense Motive against that creature. This benefit lasts until you Activate the item again in this way.

 If you’re an investigator, you can use this activation as part of the Devise a Stratagem action (even if you’re doing it as a free action). Alternatively, you can use it as part of the Pursue a Lead activity, even though you can’t see the creature. You must choose the same creature you chose for Devise a Stratagem or Pursue a Lead.

**Craft Requirements** You are an investigator.
