# Skinstitch Salve  *Item 7*

`Alchemical`  `Consumable`  `Healing`

- **Price**: 55 gp
- **Usage**: held in 1 hand
- **Bulk**: L

This sticky salve stubbornly holds wounds closed and encourages swift natural healing. You can activate the salve in either of the following ways.

**Activate—Administer Stitch** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You Administer First Aid without requiring healer's tools. You can either gain a +2 item bonus to the Medicine check or use the salve's Medicine modifier of +13 instead of your own.

**Activate—Stitch Wounds** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (manipulate) **Trigger** You Treat Wounds or use Battle Medicine; **Effect** You gain a +2 item bonus to the triggering Medicine check. If you roll a success on the Medicine check, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 296*
