# Breach  *Action*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

The orca Swims up to its swim Speed, then Leaps vertically out of the water up to 25 feet in the air, making a Strike against a creature at any point during the jump (this lets it attack a creature within 30 feet of the water’s surface). After the Strike, the orca splashes back down into the water.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 95*
