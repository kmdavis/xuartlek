# (manipulate, sonic)

**Activate—Unleash Roar** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate,sonic) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You pull the white thread free, then whirl the sling in circles at high speed. It lets out an ear-piercing wave of sound. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 4d6 sonic damage (DC 21 basic Fortitude save). Any creature that fails is deafened for 1 round, or 1 hour on a critical failure.
