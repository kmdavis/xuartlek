# Reaper  *Necromancer Fatal Method*

You study flesh up close by clashing blade against bone. You gain the reaper’s edge and thrall teamwork abilities.

**Reaper’s Edge**
You become trained in martial weapons and medium armor. At 11th level, you become an expert in martial weapons, and at 13th you become an expert in medium armor.

**Thrall Teamwork**
Once per round after you cast _create thrall_, you can make a melee Strike as a free action against a creature within your melee reach that is adjacent to at least one of your thralls.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 29*
