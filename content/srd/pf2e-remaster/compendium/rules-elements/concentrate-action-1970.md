# (concentrate)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You would be adversely affected by an emotion effect; **Effect** The relic tries to neutralize the triggering effect with its own emotion, attempting a counteract check. If the check succeeds, you feel a sudden rush of the relic's emotion and the counteracted effect doesn't take hold of you.
