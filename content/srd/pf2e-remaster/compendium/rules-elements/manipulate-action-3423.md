# (manipulate)

**Activate—One of You** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You shuffle your boots, which still stink of the dead, causing one undead creature of your choice to think that you too are undead. The target is off-guard against the next melee attack you attempt against it before the end of your current turn.
