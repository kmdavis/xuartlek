# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You bow to an ally within 30 feet, creating a magical bond between the two of you as if you cast _share life_ targeting the ally. The link remains even if you move more than 30 feet away from them. At the end of the spell’s duration, your ally recovers 4d8 Hit Points and you recover half of what they recover.
