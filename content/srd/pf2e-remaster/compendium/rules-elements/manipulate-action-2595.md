# (manipulate)

**Activate—Retrieve Item** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** An item is stored in the belt and you have a free hand; **Effect** The item stored in the belt appears in your hand. Neither Store Item nor Retrieve Item can be activated again for 1 minute.
