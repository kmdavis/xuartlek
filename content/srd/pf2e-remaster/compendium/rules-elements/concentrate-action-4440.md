# (concentrate)

**Activate—Impossible Tide** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Your eidolon momentarily manifests into a stampede of dozens of copies of themself. The stampede rampages out in every direction, swerving around your allies while trampling any foe on the ground in an emanation around your eidolon with a radius equal to your eidolon’s Speed. Each of these foes takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage, with a DC 29 basic Reflex save. On a critical failure, the foe is also knocked prone. After dealing damage, the stampede of eidolons vanishes as quickly as it appeared.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 245*
