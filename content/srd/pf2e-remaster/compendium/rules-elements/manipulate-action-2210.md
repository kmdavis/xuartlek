# (manipulate)

**Activate—Lantern Light** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** _The glowing lantern_ fruit sheds bright light in a 60-foot radius (and dim light for the next 60 feet) for 8 hours. While the light is shining, you can Interact with the _glowing lantern fruit_ to open or close some of its reflective leaves, making the light directional like a bull's-eye lantern or a hooded lantern.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 201*
