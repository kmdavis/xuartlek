# Jotunborn Adventurers  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Jotunborn

The brawny frames of jotunborn make them natural barbarians, champion, fighters, guardians, and monks. Many of these jotunborn take on roles as caravan defenders or local guards. Others become druids and rangers, especially those that prefer to maintain their nomadic lifestyle or those that have found their bonded site. While jotunborn are no more or less capable of spellcasting than others, most jotunborn tend to favor the occult tradition, with occult sorcerers, summoners, and witches being most prevalent in jotunborn societies.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 10*
