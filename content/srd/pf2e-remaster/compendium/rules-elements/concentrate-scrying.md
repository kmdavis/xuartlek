# (concentrate, scrying)

**Activate—Owner’s Authority** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, scrying) **Requirements** You aren’t wielding _Blackaxe_ and you are its true owner; **Effect** You sense the world around _Blackaxe_ as though you were in its location and can use any of your innate spells through the link as if it were the source of the spell. If another creature is wielding _Blackaxe_, it must succeed at a DC 50 Will save or be slowed 2 until it relinquishes the weapon.
