# Rascal  *Swashbuckler Style*

You aren’t afraid to use underhanded tactics to get the edge over your opponents. You are trained in Thievery and gain the Dirty Trick general feat. When you use Dirty Trick, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

The foe takes a –10 circumstance penalty to its speed until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*
