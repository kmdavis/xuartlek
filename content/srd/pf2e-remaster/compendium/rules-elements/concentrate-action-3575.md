# (concentrate)

**Activate—Beguiling Presence** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You exercise all of your charm on a creature, turning a chance meeting into an impromptu performance that commands attention. Attempt a single Performance check against the Perception DC of the creature. On a success, the creature is affected as though by a successful Deception check to Create a Diversion. The entity that holds your contract can influence this performance as well.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 165*
