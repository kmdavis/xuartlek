# Puppeteer  *Necromancer Fatal Method*

You prefer to study life and death from afar. You gain the Consume Thrall action and the thrall proliferation ability.

**Consume Thrall**
[ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, necromancer, occult) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You have 0 Focus Points and at least one thrall with 30 feet of you; **Effects** You destroy one thrall within 30 feet of you and regain 1 Focus Point. You can spend this focus point only to cast a grave spell.

**Thrall Proliferation**
Once per round when you cast _create thrall_, you can create one additional thrall in range.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 29*
