# (manipulate)

**Activate—Flutter Net** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A swarm enters your space; **Effect** Your _protective netting_ flutters rapidly, keeping the swarm away. You gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws against effects originating from swarms for 1 minute.
