# (aura, manipulate)

**Activate—Light Incense** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (aura, manipulate)**Cost** incense worth at least 1 sp; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** When the incense is lit, pleasant, floral smoke surrounds the censer in a 20-foot emanation, creating a space of peace and harmony. Each creature that breathes the smoke is affected by 3rd-rank _calm_ and is then temporarily immune for 24 hours. The spell's effects end when the incense burns out.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 122*
