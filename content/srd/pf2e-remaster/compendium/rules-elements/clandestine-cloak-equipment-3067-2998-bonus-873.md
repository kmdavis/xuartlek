# Clandestine Cloak  *Item 6+*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Usage**: worn cloak
- **Bulk**: L

When you pull up the hood of this nondescript gray cloak (an Interact action), you become drab and uninteresting, gaining a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks and to Deception checks to Impersonate a forgettable background character, such as a servant, but also taking a –1 item penalty to Diplomacy and Intimidation checks. 

**Activate—Cloak Identity** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You pull the cloak's hood up and gain the benefits of _veil of privacy_ for 1 hour or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.

### Clandestine Cloak

**Price** 230 gp
