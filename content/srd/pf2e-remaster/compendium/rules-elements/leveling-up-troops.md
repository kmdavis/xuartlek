# Leveling Up Troops  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Customizing Troops

If the GM wants to increase a troop’s level, they can use the troop creation guidelines to generate its new statistics. They might also want to add new abilities, such as a fly Speed, for higher-level troops. These improvements can often be themed as the troop being outfitted with additional magic items or magical abilities.
