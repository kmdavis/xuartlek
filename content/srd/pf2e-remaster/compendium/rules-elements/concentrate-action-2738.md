# (concentrate)

**Activate—Background Adaptation** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** For 1 minute, you can Hide without needing cover or concealment to do so. This doesn’t allow you to Sneak without ending your movement in cover or concealment, however, as your skin’s attempts to match the background as you move produce noticeable rippling waves of color.
