# Haunting Visage  *Action*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Eidolon`  `Manipulate`  `Visual`

- **Requirements**: Your eidolon is condensed.

Your eidolon suddenly shifts itself into a terrifying shape of its choosing, rather than a simple mound, by closely controlling the positioning of its component bodies. The eidolon attempts to Demoralize a creature within 15 feet; this Demoralize attempt loses the auditory trait, gains the visual trait, and the eidolon doesn't take a –4 circumstance penalty to the check if they don't share a language with their target. On a success or critical success, the target is additionally off-guard for 1 round.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 72*
