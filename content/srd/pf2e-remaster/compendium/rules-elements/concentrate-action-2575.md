# (concentrate)

**Activate—Visions of Terror** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You damage a creature with a Strike; **Effect** Your target is gripped by intense fear. This has the effect of a DC 37 _vision of death_ spell. The creature is then temporarily immune for 24 hours.
