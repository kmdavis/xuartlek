# (concentrate)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your unarmed Strike hits a creature that breathes; **Effect** The mask contorts and inhales, sucking breath from your target's lungs.
