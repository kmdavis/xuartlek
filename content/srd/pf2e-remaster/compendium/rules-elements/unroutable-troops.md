# Unroutable Troops  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Routing Troops

Because of the traits on a rout check, some troops are entirely immune to them, in particular mindless hordes like zombies or clockwork constructs. NPCs often use these kinds of troops due to this advantage, but PCs might also be able to raise skeleton infantry or build a similar fearless army.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 160*
