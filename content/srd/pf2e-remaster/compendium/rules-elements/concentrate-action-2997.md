# (concentrate)

**Activate—Survive Devastation** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** An enemy critically succeeds against you with a weapon or unarmed Strike; **Effect** Spend a Mythic Point; if the triggering Strike was made by a mythic creature, it’s a normal success instead. If it was made by a non-mythic creature, it’s a failure.
