# (concentrate)

**Activate—Reload Order** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Requirements** The shot is loaded in your ranged weapon, or at hand if your ranged weapon has a reload of 0; **Effect** You recall the _shot of the First Vault_, which automatically reloads itself into your weapon. If the shot had fragmented, the fragments violently dislodge themselves, dealing 15 persistent bleed damage to each creature in which they were embedded. The fragments then unite to re-form the _shot of the First Vault_, which automatically reloads itself into your weapon.

*Source: GM Core p. 303*
