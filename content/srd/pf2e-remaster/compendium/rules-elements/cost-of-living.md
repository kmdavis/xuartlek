# Cost of Living  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Shopping and Crafting

You might need to pay your cost of living for days spent in downtime. To avoid paying the cost, you can Subsist, using Society in a settlement or Survival in the wild. Experienced adventurers often have friends or patrons who take care of their living expenses. They might even have so much treasure that their cost of living—even one of the more expensive options—becomes a pittance.

*Source: Player Core p. 441*
