# (concentrate, visual)

**Activate—Shine of Silver** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, visual) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, or Performance check; **Effect** You gain a +4 status bonus to the triggering check. This ability has no effect if you’re under the effects of a disguise that hides the _mask of allure_. Depending on the skill used, the mirrored silver transforms into one of the following appearances.
**• Deception** A swirl of silver, which entirely obscures and conceals your normal features.
**• Diplomacy** A kind, gentle, and inviting expression.
**• Intimidation** An exaggerated visage of horror that shocks viewers into paying attention.
**• Performance** A humanoid face, the mouth cracked wide with a comedic smile or a tragic frown, depending on the nature of the performance.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 226*
