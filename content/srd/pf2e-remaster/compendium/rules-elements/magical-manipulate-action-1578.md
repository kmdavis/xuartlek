# (magical, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (magical, manipulate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You swing the leiomano, sending several of the dragon teeth shooting through the air on jets of energy. The dragon teeth deal 3d6 piercing damage and 3d6 damage of the damage type corresponding to the dragon’s tradition in a 15-foot cone (DC 29 basic Reflex save). The teeth hunt down their targets, correcting their flight in midair, which reduces any circumstance bonus from cover by 2.
