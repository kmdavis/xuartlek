# (concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate)

**Activate—Compelled Nonviolence** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The wings of this robe extend, and your aura glows golden, briefly becoming visible as it does. Creatures within your aura who are hostile, indifferent, or unfriendly to you must attempt a DC 36 Will saving throw with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is stunned 1.

**Failure** The creature is stunned 2.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature can’t use any hostile action against you for 1 round.
