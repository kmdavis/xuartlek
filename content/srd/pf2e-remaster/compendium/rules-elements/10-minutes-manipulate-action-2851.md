# 10 minutes (manipulate)

**Activate—Fireball Trap** 10 minutes (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You create the effects of a _rune trap_ ritual containing _fireball_. You can have only one trapped rune from a ring of maniacal devices active at a time, even if you have multiple rings, and the rune disappears if you lose your investiture in the ring.
