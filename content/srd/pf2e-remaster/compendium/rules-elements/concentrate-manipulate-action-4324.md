# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Aid from Beyond** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** Adjusting the eyepiece’s focus and asking the entity for help, you cause the spectacles to cast either _see the unseen_ or a 3rd-rank _translate_ on you. Because the entity tied to the spectacles chooses which one, the GM picks whichever spell is most immediately useful in your current situation (and chooses the most useful language each time the item casts _translate_). The spell lasts for 1 minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 226*
