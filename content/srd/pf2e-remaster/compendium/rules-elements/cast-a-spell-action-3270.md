# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list. Add the cleric spells granted by your deity to the list for their rank and each higher rank. For example, a zealot staff of Sarenrae would add _breathe fire_ at 1st through 7th rank, _fireball_ at 3rd through 7th rank, and _wall of fire_ at 4th through 7th rank.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 2263*
