# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You hit a creature using the _blessed reformer_ as your last action; **Effect** You cast _calm_ at the same level as your champion focus spells. It must target the creature you hit, and the creature takes a status penalty on its save against the spell equal to the _blessed reformer's_ number of weapon damage dice.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 35*
