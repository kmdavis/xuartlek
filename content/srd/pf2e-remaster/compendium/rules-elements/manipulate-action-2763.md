# (manipulate)

**Activate—Glimmer Beam** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The alicorn trigger unleashes a beam of radiant light in a 60-foot line. Creatures in the area of effect take 6d6 fire damage and must attempt a DC 33 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is dazzled 1.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is blinded for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is blinded for 1 minute.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 104*
