# Recurring Troops  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Customizing Troops

Your campaign’s story might revolve around the same troops appearing in several skirmish encounters over the course of multiple levels. The GM might choose to lean into recurring troops and reinforce their experience with a PC leader.
