# (manipulate, olfactory)

**Activate—Vile Stench** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate, olfactory) **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with the _catoblepas maul_; **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become sickened 1 (plus slowed 1 for as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save becomes temporarily immune to Vile Stench for 1 minute.
