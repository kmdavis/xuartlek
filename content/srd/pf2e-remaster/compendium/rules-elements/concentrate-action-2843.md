# (concentrate)

**Activate—Doom the Urn** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** You would become doomed, or your doomed value would increase; **Requirements** The ashes aren't doomed;; **Effect** The ashes in the urn intervene, taking the doomed condition in your place, and you don't gain or increase the value of your doomed condition. Each night when you get a full night's rest, you can reduce your own doomed condition or that of the urn, but not both.
