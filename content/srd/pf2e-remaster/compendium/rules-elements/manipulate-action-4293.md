# (manipulate)

**Activate—Astonishing Transfiguration** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You change the _staff-storing shield_ from its shield form into the stored staff, which has an image of the shield on it, or change it from the stored staff back into a shield. _Reinforcing_ runes etched on the shield have no effect in the staff form, and fundamental weapon runes etched on the stored staff have no effect in the shield form.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 207*
