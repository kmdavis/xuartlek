# (concentrate)

**Activate—Obscured Approach** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You Stride and turn partially invisible, becoming concealed until the end of your next turn or until you use a hostile action, whichever comes first. As normal, you can use the concealment from this activation to Hide.
