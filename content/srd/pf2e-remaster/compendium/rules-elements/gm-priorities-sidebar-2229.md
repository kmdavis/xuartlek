# GM Priorities  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Daily Preparations

The following points will be good to keep in mind to create a fun and smooth experience as your players go about exploring the world.

Evoke the setting with vivid sensory details.Shift the passage of time to emphasize tension and uncertainty, and speed past uneventful intervals.Get players to add details by asking for their reactions.Present small-scale mysteries to intrigue players and spur investigation.When rolls are needed, look for ways to move the action forward, or add interesting wrinkles on a failure.Plan effective transitions to encounters.

*Source: GM Core p. 35*
