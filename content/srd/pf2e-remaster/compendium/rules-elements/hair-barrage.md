# Hair Barrage  *Action*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

The tarantula flicks its legs, flinging spiky hairs in a 15-foot cone. This deals 4d6 piercing damage with a basic Reflex save. This uses a trained DC using the tarantula’s Dexterity modifier or an expert DC if the tarantula is specialized. This damage increases by an additional 1d6 for every 4 levels you have beyond 6th.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 95*
