# (concentrate, air)

**Activate—Fall Gently** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, air) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're falling; **Effect** The cloak catches the air and you grab onto its edges, utilizing the draft to guide you to safety. Treat your fall as 30 feet shorter and glide to a space of your choice at the bottom of your fall, which must be within 20 feet of where you would've landed.
