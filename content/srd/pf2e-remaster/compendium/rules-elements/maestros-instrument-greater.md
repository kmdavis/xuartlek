# Maestro's Instrument (Greater)  *Item 3+*

`Magical`

- **Usage**: held in 2 hand
- **Bulk**: 1

A _maestro's instrument_ can be crafted in the form of any variety of handheld musical instruments. A _maestro's instrument_ grants you a +1 item bonus to Performance checks while playing music with the instrument. 

**Activate—Charming Performance** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You play the instrument, causing it to cast a DC 17 _charm_ spell.

**Price** 60 gp

### Maestro's Instrument (Moderate)

**Price** 900 gp

---

### Maestro's Instrument (Greater)
