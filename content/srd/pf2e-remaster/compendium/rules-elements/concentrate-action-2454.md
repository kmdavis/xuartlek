# (concentrate)

**Activate—Empathic Link** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Effect** You perceive through your animal companion's senses instead of your own. You can Sustain the activation. You are unaware of your own surroundings for as long as you are using your animal companion's senses. In addition to the obvious use when you are separated from your companion, this ability might allow you to notice sounds, scents, and other stimuli that your companion's senses register but yours alone don't.
