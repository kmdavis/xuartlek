# (concentrate)

**Activate—Reshape** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** Strike with the _morphing weapon_, choosing one of the following benefits to apply to the Strike. 

**Reach** Increase the reach by 5 feet.**Shift** The weapon's damage becomes your choice of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing instead of its normal type.**Take** If the Strike hits, you can attempt to Disarm or Steal from your target immediately after as a reaction.**Weigh** Gain a status bonus to the damage roll equal to the weapon's number of damage dice.
