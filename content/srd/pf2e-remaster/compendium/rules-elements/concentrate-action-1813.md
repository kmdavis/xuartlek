# (concentrate)

**Activate** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You use Spellstrike with a non-cantrip spell and miss with the Strike; **Effect** An explosion of magical energy—stored up for the Spellstrike—explodes out. All creatures in a 5-foot emanation take 1d6 damage per level of the spell, with a basic Reflex save against your spell DC. This damage is the same type the spell would have dealt, and the activation gains that trait; if the spell would have dealt multiple types of damage, choose one of them. If the damage of the explosion is mental, the save is Will instead of Reflex.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 134*
