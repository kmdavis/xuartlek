# (force, manipulate)

**Activate—Kinetic Pulse** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (force, manipulate) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** Your armor has absorbed at least 30 damage; **Effect** The absorbed energy explodes from your armor in a pulse of force. Creatures in a 30-foot emanation take 12d6 force damage with a DC 43 basic Fortitude save. On a failure, they’re also pushed 10 feet away from you; on a critical failure, they’re instead pushed 20 feet away from you and fall prone. The armor’s absorbed damage resets to 0.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 203*
