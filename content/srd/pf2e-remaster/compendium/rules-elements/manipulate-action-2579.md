# (manipulate)

**Activate—Transcribe** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You tuck a small roll of paper into the cincture. For the next 10 minutes or until you Dismiss the activation, any performance you make is recorded on the paper, and the paper expands as necessary to accommodate it. Depending on the type of performance, this might take the form of sheet music, a transcript, or a diagram of dance moves.

*Source: GM Core p. 289*
