# (manipulate)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A ranged weapon attack hits you but doesn't critically hit; **Requirements** You are aware of the attack and not off-guard; **Effect** The bracers send the missile off-course. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If this would cause the attack to be a failure, the attack misses you.

*Source: GM Core p. 286*
