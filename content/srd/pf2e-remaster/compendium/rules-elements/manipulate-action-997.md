# manipulate

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") manipulate **Effect** You deploy the tripod and press a button to lock it into place, allowing you to deploy the tripod in midair, underwater, or anywhere else where you don't have a solid horizontal surface available. If you Activate the tripod by pushing the button again, you release and retrieve the tripod. While anchored, the tripod can be moved only if 8,000 pounds of pressure are applied to it or if a creature uses Athletics to Force Open the tripod with a DC of 40 (though most intelligent creatures can just push the button to release the tripod).

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 182*
