# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _bleachguard doll_ animates per _summon construct_ as a soulbound doll, retaining pertinent item statistics, cantrips, and its spell instead of those of the soulbound doll. The _soulbound doll_ can Cast a Spell only if the bleachguard doll has a use of that spell remaining for the day. When reduced to 0 Hit Points or this activation ends, the _bleachguard doll_ becomes inert but repairs itself at the rate of 1 Hit Point per hour. This activation is unavailable until the doll has 23 Hit Points again.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 194*
