# (concentrate)

**Activate—Fix Focus** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You start your turn off-guard or confused; **Effect** A speck of ash from the vial sealing your _bargained contract_ appears out of nowhere on your tongue, bringing your senses into focus. You suppress the off-guard or confused condition until the start of your next turn. You can use this free action when you're confused, even though you normally can't take actions of your choice when confused.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 165*
