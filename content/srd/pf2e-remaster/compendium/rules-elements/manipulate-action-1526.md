# (manipulate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Your armor anchors you in place, even defying gravity, rendering you immobilized until you Dismiss the Activation. while you're immobilized in this way, you can be moved only if a creature succeeds at a DC 40 Athletics check to Force Open your armor. You can also be moved if 8,000 pounds of pressure are placed upon you, though this is likely fatal.
