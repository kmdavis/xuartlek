# Alchemical and Magical Items  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Craft

If you want to Craft alchemical items or magic items, you need to select the skill feat for Alchemical Crafting or Magical Crafting in addition to being trained. Stat blocks and details of these items appear in _GM Core_, so consult with your GM.
