# (concentrate, electricity, manipulate)

**Activate—Call Down Lightning** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, electricity, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You conjure a bolt of electricity down onto yourself, dealing 6d6 electricity damage to all creatures in a 10-foot emanation. Additionally, for the next minute, whenever a creature touches you or hits you with a melee unarmed attack or a non-reach melee weapon attack, it takes 1d8 electricity damage.
