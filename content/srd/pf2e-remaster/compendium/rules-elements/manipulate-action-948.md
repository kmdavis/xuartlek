# manipulate

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") manipulate **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You activate the wheelchair's springs to make hopping leaps. For 1 minute, whenever you Leap with the wheelchair, you can jump 30 feet in any direction without touching the ground. You must land on a space of solid ground within 30 feet of you, or else you fall after using your next action.
