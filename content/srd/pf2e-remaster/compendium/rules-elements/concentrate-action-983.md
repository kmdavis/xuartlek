# concentrate

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") concentrate **Effect** You will the _Rowan Rifle_ to change the energy it fires from the white-hot energy of summer that glows like a star to the shivering chill of winter, the crackling electricity of the storm, or even the sonic vibrations of a roar. The _Rowan Rifle's_ damage type changes from its current damage type to cold, electricity, fire, or sonic. On the next sunrise, the _Rowan Rifle_ returns to dealing fire damage.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 166*
