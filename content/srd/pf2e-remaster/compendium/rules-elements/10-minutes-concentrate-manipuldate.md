# 10 minutes (concentrate, manipuldate)

**Activate—Group Voyage** 10 minutes (concentrate, manipulate) **Effect** As you activate the pack, you can harness up to four willing creatures to the ropes on the pack. At the end of the activation time, the pack casts a 7th-rank _interplanar teleport_ or _teleport_ spell, transporting you and everyone attached to the pack. Attempt a DC 45 Survival check. On a success, you arrive 25 miles off target using _interplanar teleport_ or halve the distance you're off-target with _teleport_. On a critical success, you arrive exactly on target.
