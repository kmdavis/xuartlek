# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _acid grip_ of the indicated rank. A creature that takes initial acid damage from this spell become sickened 1. Use your spell DC if the creature attempts to recover from this sickness. This is an olfactory effect.
