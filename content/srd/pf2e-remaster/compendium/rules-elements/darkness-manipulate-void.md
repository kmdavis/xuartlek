# (darkness, manipulate, void)

**Activate—Light Harvest** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (darkness, manipulate, void) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You swing the _void sickle_ in a wide arc, drawing light into the blade. All non-magical light sources within 30 feet are immediately extinguished, and the next melee Strike you make with the _void sickle_ before the end of your turn deals an additional 1d6 void damage.
