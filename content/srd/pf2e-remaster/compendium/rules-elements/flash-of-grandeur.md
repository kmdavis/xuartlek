# Flash of Grandeur  *Action*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Champion`  `Divine`

- **Trigger**: An enemy damages your ally, and both are in your champion’s aura

Imperious divine light flashes out from you to surround your foe. The ally gains resistance to all damage against the triggering damage equal to 2 + your level. Until the end of your next turn, the attacker is affected by _revealing light_.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 91*
