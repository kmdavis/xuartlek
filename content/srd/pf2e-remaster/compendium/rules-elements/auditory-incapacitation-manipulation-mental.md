# (auditory, incapacitation, manipulation, mental)

**Activate—Disorienting Fugue** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (auditory, incapacitation, manipulate, mental)
 **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You play several notes on the pipes, quickly altering their pitch and tone. Each creature within a 20-foot emanation must attempt a DC 31 Will save to resist the song. Creatures that attempt this save become temporarily immune to Disorienting Fugue for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected and its temporary immunity to Disorienting Fugue increases to 1 hour.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is confused for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The target is confused for 1 round and immediately attacks itself (in the normal fashion for attacking oneself while confused). This Strike doesn’t give the creature a flat check to recover from the confusion.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 105*
