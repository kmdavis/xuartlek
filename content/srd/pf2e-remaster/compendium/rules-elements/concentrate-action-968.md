# concentrate

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") concentrate **Requirements** The _animate dreamer_ has a stored spell. It can see a creature it hit and damaged within the last minute, and that creature's within 120 feet of _animate dreamer_; **Effect** The _animate dreamer_ casts its stored spell at a target that meets the requirements. This empties the spell from the weapon and allows a spell to be cast into it again. The animate dreamer has a spell attack roll of +25 and a spell DC of 33 with the stored spell.

*Source: Guns & Gears (Remastered) p. 162*
