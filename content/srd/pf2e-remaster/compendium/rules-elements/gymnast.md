# Gymnast  *Swashbuckler Style*

You reposition, maneuver, and bewilder your foes with daring feats of physical prowess. You are trained in Athletics. When you Grapple, Reposition, Shove, or Trip, the action gains the bravado trait.

### Exemplary Finisher

If the foe is grabbed, restrained, or prone, you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to double the number of weapon damage dice.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 161*
