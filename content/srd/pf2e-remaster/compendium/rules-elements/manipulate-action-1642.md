# (manipulate)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature misses you with an attack; **Effect** Attempt a Stealth check against the triggering creature's Perception DC. If you roll a success, you're hidden from that creature until the end of your next turn or until right after you use a hostile action against that creature. If you roll a critical success, you're hidden from that creature until the end of your next turn, even if you use hostile actions against that creature.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 103*
