# (concentrate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Effect** You cause the arm to become corporeal until the end of your turn, allowing you to use it to make Strikes or grasp objects. It remains corporeal if you’re holding an item or it’s wearing an item at the end of your turn. Your unarmed attack Strikes made with the aether appendage are magical. If they’re already magical, they instead gain the effect of the _ghost touch_ property rune.
