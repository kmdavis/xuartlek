# (concentrate)

**Activate—Chained Grasp** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You successfully Strike with the _ouroboros flail_ as part of activating its _extending_ rune; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The head of the flail wraps around the target of the triggering Strike and drags it in your direction. The target must succeed at a DC 32 Fortitude save or be pulled adjacent to you. A creature pulled to you is then grabbed by the flail until the end of your next turn unless you move, your target Escapes (DC 31), or you attack with the flail, whichever comes first.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 210*
