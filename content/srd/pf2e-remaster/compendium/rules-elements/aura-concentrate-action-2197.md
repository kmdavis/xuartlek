# (aura, concentrate)

**Activate—Lash Out!** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (aura, concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The effigy causes spectral tentacles to erupt from the ship's hull. These tentacles lash out in an emanation with a distance equal to the ship's length and remain active for 10 minutes. Creatures in the aura and in the same body of water as the ship take a –2 circumstance penalty to Athletics checks to Swim as the writhing tentacles lash out in all directions.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 177*
