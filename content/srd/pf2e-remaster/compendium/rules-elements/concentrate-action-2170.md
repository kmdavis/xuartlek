# (concentrate)

**Activate—Reflect Emotions** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** You're targeted by an emotion or metal effect; **Effect** You receive a +4 status bonus to your saving throw against the triggering effect. Whether or not your save is successful, the teardrop attempts a counteract check at +36 to immediately reflect a copy of the effect back at the originator, targeting it using the creature's own relevant statistics but controlling the effect as if the teardrop had cast it.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 146*
