# Admiral's Bicorne  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 900 gp
- **Usage**: worn headwear
- **Bulk**: L

This ostentatious hat is trimmed with gold thread and tiny jewels, all proclaiming your position of authority on the high seas. While wearing the bicorne, you gain a +2 item bonus to Intimidation and Sailing Lore checks.

**Activate—Fight On** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take damage from an enemy’s Strike or spell attack roll; **Effect** Despite your wounds, your troops are inspired to fight on. For 1 minute, all allies in a 30-foot emanation gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against fear effects.
