# Creating New Bonds  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Skirmish Bonds

Since the skirmish bonds can't cover every class and ability combination in the game, you might want to create your own skirmish bonds sometimes. For example, if you have a fire kineticist in your game, you might let them combine fire impulses with troop abilities that use the fire trait, following the structure of the Coordinated Casting skirmish bond.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 161*
