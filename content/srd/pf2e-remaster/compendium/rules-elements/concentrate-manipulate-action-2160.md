# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Coal Wall** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _everburning coal_ creates a towering wall of hot coals. This has the effect of _wall of ice_, except for the following adjustments. 

The wall has the fire trait instead of cold and water.The wall deals fire damage instead of cold damage.The weakness to fire is instead weakness to cold and to water.Destroying a section of the wall with cold or water (rather than with fire) causes a section to evaporate.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 123*
