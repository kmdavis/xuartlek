# Detect Magic  *Action*

`Concentrate`  `Exploration`

You cast _detect magic_ at regular intervals. You move at half your travel speed or slower. You have no chance of accidentally overlooking a magic aura at a travel speed up to 300 feet per minute, but must be traveling no more than 150 feet per minute to detect magic auras before the party moves into them.
