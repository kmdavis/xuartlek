# (concentrate)

**Activate—Absorb Arcana** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** ; **Trigger** You Cast a Spell from a spell slot, and the spell has the acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic trait; **Requirements** The club has no charges; **Effect** You mentally align the club’s crystals to reverberate with energy from the triggering spell. The club gains a number of charges equal to the rank of the spell slot, and the charges are attuned to the damage type matching the spell’s trait. If the spell has more than one eligible trait, choose one when the charges are stored.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 209*
