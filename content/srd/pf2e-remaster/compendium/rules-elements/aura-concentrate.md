# (aura, concentrate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (aura, concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You call forth a storm of leaves from _autumn's embrace_. These leaves swirl in a 20-foot emanation for 1 minute. Creatures within the area are concealed, and creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within the leaves. However, you can see through this concealment. You can Dismiss the activation.
