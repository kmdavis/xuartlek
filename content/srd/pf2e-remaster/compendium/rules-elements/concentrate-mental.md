# (concentrate, mental)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, mental) **Trigger** You damage a creature with the etched weapon; **Effect** The damaged creature must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be pacified. A pacified creature takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls on any attacks that aren't nonlethal for 1 minute, and the creature also experiences a clear psychic warning that they should stop making attacks that could kill.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 33*
