# (concentrate, spellshape)

**Activate—Sanguine Expulsion** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared Spell that manipulates blood, such as __blood vendetta__ or __vampiric exsanguination__, or a spell dealing piercing or slashing damage, all targets of the triggering spell take 1d6 persistent bleed damage; if the spell already causes bleed damage, the bleed damage increases by 1d6. The wounds of one affected target (chosen by you) gush uncontrollably, covering the ground with blood in a 5-foot emanation that lasts for 1 round. Creatures that begin their turn in the blood must succeed at a Reflex save against your spell DC or fall prone.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 228*
