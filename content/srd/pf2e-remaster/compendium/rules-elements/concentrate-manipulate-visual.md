# (concentrate, manipulate, visual)

**Activate—Smothering Lassitude** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate, visual) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You brandish the effigy aloft, exposing all who see it to its bizarre visage. You and all creatures within a 120-foot emanation must attempt a DC 34 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is stupefied 1 until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 1 and stunned 1 until the start of your next turn.

**Critical Failure** The creature is stupefied 2 and stunned 1 until the start of your next turn.
