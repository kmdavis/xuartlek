# Pristine Epaulets  *Item 5*

`Fortune`  `Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 125 gp
- **Usage**: worn
- **Bulk**: L

Gaudy and sparkling, these intricately decorated epaulets have clearly never seen a battlefield. Worn by officers with more schooling or connections than actual fighting experience, these epaulets grant you a +1 item bonus to Society and Warfare Lore checks.

**Activate—I Meant to Say** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, fortune) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You critically fail a Diplomacy check; **Effect** The _pristine epaulets_ are often worn to both tense military negotiations and social events and can help you recover from a misstep. You can reroll the check, but you must take the new result.
