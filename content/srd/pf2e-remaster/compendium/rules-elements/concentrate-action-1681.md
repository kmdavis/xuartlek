# (concentrate)

**Activate** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to cast a cold spell that deals damage, all creatures damaged by the spell are also dazzled for 3 rounds by light refracting and reflecting within and around the spell's chilling effects. If an affected creature critically failed its save against the required spell, or if you critically succeeded on your spell attack roll against the creature, it's instead blinded for 1 round and then dazzled for 3 rounds.
