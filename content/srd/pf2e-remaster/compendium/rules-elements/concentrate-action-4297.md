# (concentrate)

**Activate—Fortunate Strike** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once every 10 minutes; **Trigger** Your previous action was to Cast a fortune Spell that targeted a single ally; **Effect** Your luck magic lingers, allowing your target to find a weak spot in an enemy’s defenses. The next Strike made by the target of the triggering fortune spell bypasses an amount of resistance equal to the spell’s rank. This benefit is lost if it isn’t used by the end of your next turn. If more than one type of resistance would apply against the attack, the attack bypasses only one—whichever would result in the highest amount of damage getting through, as determined by the GM.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 208*
