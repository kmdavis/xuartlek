# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _mind probe_. The first time the target rolls a success at a Deception check to mislead your probe, it gets a result one step worse than it rolled. This means you learn the answer if the target's Deception check would have succeeded, and you learn nothing rather than believing a falsehood if the check would have been a critical success.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 143*
