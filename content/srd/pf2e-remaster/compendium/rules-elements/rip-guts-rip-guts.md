# Rip Guts! Rip Guts!

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") Rip Guts! Rip Guts!; **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** You throw _Gut-Ripper_ in a line of any length up to 60 feet, then it returns to your hands. Each creature in the line takes 2d10 piercing damage with a DC 25 basic Fortitude save. If there's a creature at the end of the line, you instead make a melee Strike against it with the hook that deals an additional 2d10 persistent bleed damage. Each time a creature takes this bleed damage, you regain that many HP; this is a healing vitality effect. This activation can't be used again for 1d4 rounds.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 171*
