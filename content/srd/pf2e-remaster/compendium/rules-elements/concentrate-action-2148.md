# (concentrate)

**Activate—Shed Chitin** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** You would be petrified; **Effect** The powder petrifies like a shell around you instead, and its other effects end.
