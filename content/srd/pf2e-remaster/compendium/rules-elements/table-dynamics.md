# Table Dynamics  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Captain

Some groups may not be comfortable with the dynamics that a follower introduces to the table, given the inequality between followers and the PCs. Before selecting the captain archetype, check with the GM and the rest of your group to ensure that they are on board with using these rules.
