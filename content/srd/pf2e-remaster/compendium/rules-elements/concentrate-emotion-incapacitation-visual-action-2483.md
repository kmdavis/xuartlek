# (concentrate, emotion, incapacitation, visual)

**Activate—Irresistible Desire** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (concentrate, emotion, incapacitation, visual) **Requirements** The target must be fascinated by the mirror; **Effect** You control the target for 30 days, with the effect of a critically failed saving throw against _dominate_. If the victim sees the mirror again at any point before this duration expires, the control extends for an additional 30 days from the moment it looked at the mirror again. While there is no initial saving throw, the DC to break free due to commands against the creature's nature is 35.
