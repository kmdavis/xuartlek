# Ring of Lies  *Item 10*

`Invested`  `Magical`  `Uncommon`

- **Price**: 850 gp
- **Usage**: worn

This plain silver ring has an almost oily sheen. While wearing the ring, you gain a +2 item bonus to Deception checks. 

**Activate—Sweeten Lies** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Snapping your fingers on the hand that wears the ring causes the ring to cast _honeyed words_ on you with no visual manifestations of a spell being cast.
