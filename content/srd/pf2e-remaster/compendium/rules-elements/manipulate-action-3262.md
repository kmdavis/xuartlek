# (manipulate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** A bottled lightning is installed in the shield; **Effect** The shield becomes an electromagnet for 3 rounds. When an activated magnetic shield is raised, the circumstance bonus increases by 1 against attacks made with weapons primarily made of metal. If you use a Shield Block against a creature attacking you with such a weapon, you also gain a +1 item bonus to Disarm attempts against that weapon until the end of your next turn. The activation uses up the bottled lightning, and the shield can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 64*
