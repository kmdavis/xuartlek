# (illusion, manipulate, mental)

**Activate—Wake the Dream** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (illusion, manipulate, mental) **Effect** By touching the two halves of the tattoo together, the wings expand to show a small illusory rendition of any effects with the dream or sleep traits you are currently subject to. Onlookers (including yourself) gain a +1 status bonus to checks to Identify Magic of any spells or magical effects with the dream or sleep traits for 1 round.
