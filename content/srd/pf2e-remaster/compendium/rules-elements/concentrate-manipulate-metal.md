# (concentrate, manipulate, metal)

**Activate—Phalanx** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (concentrate, manipulate, metal) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You raise walls of rapidly shifting metal plates in two 60-foot lines going in opposite directions. Each wall is 60 feet high and is greater difficult terrain. Whenever a creature enters or starts their turn in the wall’s space, they take 6d10 slashing damage (DC 32 basic Fortitude save). The shifting walls also provide greater cover from any attack originating from the opposite side.
