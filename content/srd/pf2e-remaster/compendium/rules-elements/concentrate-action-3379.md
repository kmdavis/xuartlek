# (concentrate)

**Activate—Titan’s Stature** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** The magical banner causes a rapid surge of growth. A Medium or smaller ally within the banner’s aura becomes Large for 1 round. Its equipment grows with it but returns to its natural size afterwards. While Large, the ally is clumsy 1, and its reach increases by 5 feet (or by 10 feet if it started out Tiny).

*Source: Battlecry! p. 141*
