# (manipulate)

**Activate—Fresh Face** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** You have used Impersonate and molded your face into a different face.; **Effect** You peel your current face from your skin, revealing the true generic appearance from your _bargained contract_. This allows you to duck out of sight and remove the facial component of your disguise almost immediately, though clothing or other elements might still give you away.
