# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The tattoo casts _harm_, _heal_, or the 1st-rank spell from your deity’s cleric spells. You can choose _harm_ or _heal_ only in accord with the deity’s divine font. If the deity allows either spell, choose one the tattoo can cast when you receive the tattoo. The DC for any of these spells is 18.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 120*
