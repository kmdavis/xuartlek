# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Release Energy** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** The blade has an energy rune from Cycle Energy; **Effect** You call upon the energy stored in the blade to explode outward. You cast a 5th-rank spell that depends on the active rune: _acid grip_ for _corrosive_, __fireball__ for _flaming_, 2-action __howling blizzard__ for _frost_, or __lightning bolt__ for _shock_, with a DC of 30.
