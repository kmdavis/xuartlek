# manipulate

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") manipulate **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** Make a ranged Strike. On a hit, the creature becomes covered in a mass of flesh that continues to grow on the target for a brief time. The creature becomes slowed 1 for 1 round, after which the growth withers and falls off.
