# (concentration, teleportation)

**Activate—Dimension Leap** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentration, teleportation) **Frequency** once per day; Trigger You succeed at a saving throw against a spell from the chosen tradition; **Effect** Your veil billows out and you disappear behind it. You use the traces of dweomercat magic to teleport yourself instantly to any unoccupied square within 30 feet. If you critically succeeded at the triggering save, you can instead teleport within 45 feet.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 106*
