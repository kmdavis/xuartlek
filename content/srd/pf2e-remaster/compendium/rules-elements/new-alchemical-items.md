# New Alchemical Items  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Alchemical Foods

The new alchemical options appearing in this chapter represent some notable expansions of the abilities available to anyone who uses alchemy, but the alchemist class in particular. It’s important to consider the impact these options can have on the play experience, not from a balance perspective, but from a story perspective. Characters specializing in alchemical foods will want an adventure that gives them time to prepare alchemical foods with the processed trait, like the new cooperative waffles. Be sure that your players choosing these options have the opportunity to take full advantage of them, or warn them if that option won’t be available.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 46*
