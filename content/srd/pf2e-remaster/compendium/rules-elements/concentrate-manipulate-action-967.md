# concentrate, manipulate

**Activate** (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You allow yourself to become partially possessed by the immolated spirit bound to the clan pistol. For 1 minute, you look like a flaming corpse. During this time, you gain a +2 status bonus to Intimidation checks and the immolation clan pistol becomes a _+2 striking flaming clan pistol_.
