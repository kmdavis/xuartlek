# (auditory, emotion, fear, manipulate, mental)

**Activate—Sustain Dread** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (auditory, emotion, fear, manipulate, mental) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You beat a march on the timpani that continuously increases in tempo. Enemies within a 30-foot emanation must attempt a DC 26 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The target can’t reduce its frightened value below 1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target can’t reduce its frightened value below 1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and it becomes frightened 1.
