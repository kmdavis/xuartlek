# (concentrate, spellshape)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to cast a damage-dealing spell prepared from this grimoire, the spell wreaks even greater destruction. The spell ignores 10 resistance and you reroll any damage dice that roll a 1. If the spell reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, they’re torn apart at an infinitesimal level, not even leaving a fine ash; this is a death effect.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 108*
