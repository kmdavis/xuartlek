# (concentrate)

**Activate—Have We Met?** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Effect** You stare at another creature and instantly remember their name if you've met and exchanged names. The glasses rely on your latent memories, so if the creature is disguising their identity, the glasses don't penetrate the disguise. For example, if a divoynik was disguised as an innkeeper you met, the glasses would give you the innkeeper's name, and if a noble you met before was in disguise as a masked vigilante, the glasses wouldn't reveal their true name.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 244*
