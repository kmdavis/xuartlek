# (manipulate)

**Activate—Infinite Links** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You extend the flail’s chain, then wrap it across itself and strike two links together. This severs up to a 60-foot loop of usable chain from the flail, which instantly returns to its normal length. If you use Infinite Links again, any previous chain you created with it disappears after 1 round. As with most obviously magical and temporary constructs, the chain can’t be sold.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 210*
