# (magical, manipulate, teleportation)

**Activate—Tear Rift** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (magical, manipulate, teleportation) **Effect** You carve a rent in space in an adjacent square, choosing another square within 30 feet to connect with your rift. A visible slash appears in both locations, displaying the view from the other side. Until the end of your next turn, these two squares are treated as adjacent to each other; for example, creatures could Stride or be Shoved through the rifts to their other side, and a creature adjacent to one rift can Strike a creature adjacent to the other. When Tearing a Rift, you can choose to Step through the rift as part of the activity. 

The rifts don’t automatically pull creatures or objects through, as they take up only a small amount of space within the square, and a creature sharing a space with a rift is unaffected except for determining adjacency. You can have only one rift active at a time.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 134*
