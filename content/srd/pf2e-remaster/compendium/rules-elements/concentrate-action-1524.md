# (concentrate)

**Activate** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You take acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage; **Effect** You gain resistance 5 to the triggering damage type.
