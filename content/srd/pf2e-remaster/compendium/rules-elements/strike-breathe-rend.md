# Strike, Breathe, Rend  *Action*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Spirit`  `Transcendence`

- **Requirements**: Your last action this turn was a successful Strike with the _noble branch

You channel a rending pulse of energy down your weapon in the moment of contact. The target of the Strike takes spirit damage equal to the _noble branch’s_ weapon damage dice. This includes any extra dice from striking runes, but not from special abilities, property runes, or the like.
