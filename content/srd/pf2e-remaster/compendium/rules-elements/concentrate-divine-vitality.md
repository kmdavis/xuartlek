# (concentrate, divine, vitality)

**Activate—Sever from the Void** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, divine, vitality) **Trigger** You hit and damage an undead creature with an _undead scourge_; **Effect** You disrupt the undead’s connection to void energy. For 1 minute, the undead damaged with the dagger can’t be healed by void energy unless the effect attempting to heal the undead first counteracts the undead scourge’s effect, which has a counteract rank of 4th and a DC of 25. Vitality energy still has the usual effects on the undead.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 131*
