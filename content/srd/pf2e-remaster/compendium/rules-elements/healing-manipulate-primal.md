# (healing, manipulate, primal)

**Activate—Seed of Sustenance** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (healing, manipulate, primal) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You draw a seed and cast it into a space within 30 feet. A small tree sprouts within 10 minutes, producing 5 fruits. A creature who eats the fruit with an Interact action regains 1d6+2 Hit Points and receives as much nourishment as one meal for a typical human. After an hour, the tree and all its fruits wither away.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 146*
