# (concentrate)

**Activate—Divine Fervor** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You've just used your champion's reaction; **Effect** You gain an additional reaction you can use only for your champion's reaction. You lose this reaction if you don't use it by the start of your next turn.
