# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Mesmerizing Lights** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** All creatures within the bright light of the anglerfish lantern must succeed at a DC 19 Will save or be fascinated by the light for 1 round (1 minute on a critical failure). The fascination ends if the light is extinguished. Aquatic animals and creatures with the water trait take a –2 circumstance penalty to this check. Regardless of the result, the creature then becomes immune to this effect for the next 24 hours.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 176*
