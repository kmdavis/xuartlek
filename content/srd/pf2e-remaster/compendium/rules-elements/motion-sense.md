# Motion Sense  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Ooze

An ooze eidolon has motion sense, allowing them to detect movement as a precise sense since they have no eyes. This lets them detect even minor movements like breathing, but even if a creature can become perfectly still, the ooze can usually determine the creature's location based on the last time the creature moved. Using Stealth usually still works against an ooze eidolon, but effects that are solely visual (such as a spell that might create concealment with a visual illusion) might not work against motion sense. The GM determines the specifics.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 76*
