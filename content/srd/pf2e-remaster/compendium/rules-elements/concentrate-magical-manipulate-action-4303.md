# (concentrate, magical, manipulate)

**Activate—Explosive Cone** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, magical, manipulate) **Frequency** ; **Requirements** The club has at least 1 charge; **Effect** You flip the club around to blast a cone of energy from its base. Each creature in a 15-foot cone takes 1d6 damage per charge stored in the club, with a DC 20 basic Reflex save. The damage type is the same type the charges were attuned to. The club loses all its charges.
