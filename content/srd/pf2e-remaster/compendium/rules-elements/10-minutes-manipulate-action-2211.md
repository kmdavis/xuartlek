# 10 minutes (manipulate)

**Activate—Fire Fruit** 10 minutes (manipulate) **Effect** You plant the _glowing lantern fruit_ in the ground upside down. The petals of the lantern peel away, while the fruit inside glows hotter. For the next 8 hours, the _glowing lantern fruit_ emits as much light and heat as a bonfire, giving all creatures within 15 feet immunity to the effects of mild and severe cold temperatures for as long as they're within the area.
