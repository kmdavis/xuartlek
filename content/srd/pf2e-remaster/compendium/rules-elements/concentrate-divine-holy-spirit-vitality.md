# (concentrate, divine, holy, spirit, vitality)

**Activate—Great Vengeance** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, divine, holy, spirit, vitality) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** Your last action was a successful melee Strike with _righteous fury_ against a creature with the unholy trait; **Effect** The sword emits a 20-foot-radius emanation of holy fire centered on your target, inflicting 2d8 spirit damage and 2d8 vitality damage to all enemies in the area (DC 34 basic Reflex save).
