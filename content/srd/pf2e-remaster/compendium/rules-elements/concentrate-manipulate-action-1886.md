# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You move like the wind, with precision and speed. You Stride up to 60 feet; this movement doesn't trigger reactions. When you stop, if you've moved at least 30 feet from where you started, you release a thunderous 5-foot emanation that deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 sonic damage with a DC 25 basic Fortitude save. A creature that critically fails its save is also knocked prone.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 151*
