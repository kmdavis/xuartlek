# (concentrate)

**Activate—Purge Unholy Magic** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** You use the flail to temporarily disrupt an unholy spellcaster’s connection to their magic. Make a melee Strike with the flail against an unholy creature with innate or prepared spells. If you hit and deal damage, the target must attempt a DC 31 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is otherwise unaffected.

**Success** Until the beginning of your next turn, the creature must succeed at a DC 5 flat check to Cast a Spell.

**Failure** As success, but the effect lasts for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the DC of the flat check is 11.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 208*
