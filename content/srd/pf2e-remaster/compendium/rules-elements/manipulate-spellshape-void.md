# (manipulate, spellshape, void)

**Activate—Excruciating Spell** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate, spellshape, void) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared void Spell that allows a saving throw, you warp and twist the energy of the spell to cause intense pain. If the target fails its saving throw against the spell, it becomes sickened 1 by the pain.
