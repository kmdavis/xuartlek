# (manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The shield becomes a locked door in an empty, adjacent space no wider than 5 feet, becoming flush with the surrounding structure so it blocks all passage. The door has the same Hardness, HP, and Broken Threshold as the shield, with Athletics and Thievery DCs of 25 to open. The door remains in place for 1 hour, until it's opened or broken, or until you spend an action to end this effect, at which point the shield returns to your hand (or falls to the ground in your space if your hands are full).

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 23*
