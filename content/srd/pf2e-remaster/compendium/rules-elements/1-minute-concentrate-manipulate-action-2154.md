# 1 minute (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Shape the Castle** 1 minute (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You empty the satchel and shape the sand into a miniature castle. When finished, you utter “build” in Petran, and the _sandcastle_ swiftly builds into a full-size structure. The castle is shaped as you choose, up to 120 feet in any dimension. Its walls must be 10 feet thick, and each story must be at least 10 feet tall from floor to ceiling. The _sandcastle_ has only rudimentary furnishings with no moving parts—even a chair or door is too complicated, but block-like benches and platforms can be created. Typically, the castle has little more than staircases and windows.

 You can return the _sandcastle_ to its portable form by using one Interact action to fill the item's satchel with sand from the castle.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 100*
