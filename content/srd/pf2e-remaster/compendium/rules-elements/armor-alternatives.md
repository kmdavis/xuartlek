# Armor Alternatives  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Armor

If you don't want to wear armor, or you're trained in only unarmored defense, you can wear either explorer's clothing or _bands of force_. Explorer's clothing can be etched with runes just like armor can, so it can provide item bonuses to AC or saves. _Bands of force_ give a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dex modifier cap of +5, and they also grant a bonus to saves.

*Source: GM Core p. 228*
