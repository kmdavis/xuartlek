# (concentrate, divine)

**Activate—Holy Retribution** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, divine) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your previous action was to use _Freedom’s Flame_ to Strike a foe you have witnessed use a compulsion or control effect on an ally within the past hour; **Effect** Spend a Mythic Point; your blow is empowered by the righteousness of Milani.
