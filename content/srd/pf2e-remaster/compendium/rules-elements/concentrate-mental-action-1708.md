# (concentrate, mental)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, mental) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** You can see your nemesis, and they're within 30 feet of you; **Effect** You focus your hatred into a mental scream.
