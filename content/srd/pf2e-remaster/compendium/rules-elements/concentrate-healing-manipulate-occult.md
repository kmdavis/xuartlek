# (concentrate, healing, manipulate, occult)

**Activate—Absorb Shadows** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, healing, manipulate, occult) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You touch _Shadowpiercer_ to a darkness or shadow effect to siphon its energy and bolster yourself. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to four times the effect’s counteract rank. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 hour. In addition, if the effect is causing darkness, that darkness is automatically counteracted within 15 feet of you for as long as you have those temporary Hit Points. This doesn’t provide light—it merely restores the area to its natural illumination level.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 152*
