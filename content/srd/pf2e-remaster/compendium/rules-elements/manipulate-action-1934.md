# (manipulate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** You're in an area of bright light; **Effect** You angle the shield at a target within 60 feet, reflecting light into its eyes. It must attempt a DC 42 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is blinded until its next turn begins.

**Failure** The target is blinded for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is blinded for 2d4 hours.
