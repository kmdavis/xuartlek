# (concentrate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Effect** You raise the staff and choose an ally within 10 feet. A ruby-colored plane of force appears like a shield near the ally, granting them a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn. If you use this action again, any creature you previously granted this bonus to loses it.
