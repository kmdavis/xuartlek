# 10 minutes (auditory, manipulate)

**Activate—Whispers of Leng** 10 minutes (auditory, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You whisper a name and whatever details you recall about that person to the effigy, telling it everything you can about your target. The next time the target sleeps, they are subject to a _nightmare_ spell (DC 30). This continues every time the target sleeps until they die, you whisper a new target to the effigy, or the target gets a critical success on their save against the _nightmare_.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 144*
