# 10 minutes (concentrate, occult)

**Activate—Peer Beyond** 10 minutes (concentrate, occult) **Effect** The mirror casts a DC 28 _scrying_ spell for the benefit of the creature activating it. The target must be within the owner's domain (typically within 20 miles of the mirror). The viewer's familiarity with the target doesn't affect the spell's DC.
