# (emotion, manipulate, mental)

**Activate—Screech Shot** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (emotion, manipulate, mental) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The screech shooter unleashes a frightening wail. All creatures in a 30-foot emanation from you must attempt a DC 25 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is frightened 1.

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and fleeing for 1 round.
