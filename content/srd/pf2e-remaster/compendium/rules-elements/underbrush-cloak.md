# Underbrush Cloak  *Item 5*

`Invested`  `Magical`

- **Price**: 90 gp
- **Usage**: worn cloak
- **Bulk**: 1

This hooded cloak is lined with rough foliage, vines, and bark that never wilts or rots away. When wearing this cloak, you gain a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks when in heavily forested areas.

**Activate—One with the Woods** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** For the next minute, you ignore any difficult terrain caused by plants and fungi, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.
