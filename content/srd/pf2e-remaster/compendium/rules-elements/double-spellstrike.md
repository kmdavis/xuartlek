# Double Spellstrike  *Action*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Magus`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Requirements**: Your Spellstrike is charged.

You Spellstrike, but you cast two spells instead of one. The combined rank of the spells must be 10 or less, and they must both meet the normal criteria for spells you can cast in a Spellstrike.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 11*
