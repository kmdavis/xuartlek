# (manipulate)

**Activate—Tongue Twister** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Requirements** The sling isn’t loaded; **Effect** You fully unfurl the sling’s tongue to grab hold of a creature within 20 feet, and attempt to move them. Attempt an Athletics check against the creature’s Fortitude DC. On a success, you can move the creature up to 15 feet into any space within 20 feet of you. If you target a willing ally with this ability, you automatically succeed at the Athletics check.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 104*
