# Tongue Grab  *Action*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

The giant frog lashes out with its tongue, making a tongue Strike. If the Strike hits, the target is automatically grabbed by the giant frog and pulled into a square adjacent to the frog. The grabbed condition lasts until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 93*
