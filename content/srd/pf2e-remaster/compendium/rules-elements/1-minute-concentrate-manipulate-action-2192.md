# 1 minute (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Dive!** 1 minute (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You lower the _submersible anglerfish lantern_ into water at least 15 feet deep while issuing a command. The lantern transforms into a bathysphere for 1 hour. This vehicle possesses a 60-foot cone light that can be swiveled up to 90 degrees with an Interact action and has the activation listed above. When the effect ends, any occupants are ejected harmlessly. If the bathysphere becomes broken, the effect ends and the _submersible anglerfish lantern_ is broken as well.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 176*
