# Raise the Walls  *Action*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Force`  `Transcendence`

You Raise the _mirrored aegis_, which summons ethereal shields that surround you and one ally of your choice within 15 feet in a tortoise shield formation. You and the ally gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Reflex saves, and any save against a force, spirit, vitality, or void effect for 1 minute.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 45*
