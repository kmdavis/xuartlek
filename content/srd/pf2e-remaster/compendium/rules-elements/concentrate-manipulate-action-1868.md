# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You peel the musicians from the garment and fling them around you. The cloak casts a 4th-rank _phantom crowd_ spell (DC 28); each of the 10-foot squares must be adjacent to you. The crowd looks like the musicians on the garment and continues to accompany your Performance checks. You can Sustain this effect as described in the spell.
