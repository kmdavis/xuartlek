# (splash)

**Activate—Divine Bomb** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (splash) **Effect** This potion functions as a bottle of _holy water_. Like _holy water_, you throw it as a Strike as part of the activation. It’s a simple thrown weapon with a range increment of 20 feet. Unlike an alchemical bomb, it doesn’t add the manipulate trait to the attack made with it.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 221*
