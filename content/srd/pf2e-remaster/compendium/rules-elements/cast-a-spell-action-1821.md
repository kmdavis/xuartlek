# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _frigid flurry_ of the indicated rank. After you cast the spell, the ice crystals freeze to flesh and other surfaces, clinging to the creatures in the area. Each creature that fails its save takes persistent cold damage with the amount determined by the wand's type.
