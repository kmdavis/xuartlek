# Stitch Wounds (manipulate)

**Activate—Stitch Wounds** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (manipulate) **Trigger** You Treat Wounds or use Battle Medicine; **Effect** You gain a +2 item bonus to the triggering Medicine check. If you roll a success on the Medicine check, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 296*
