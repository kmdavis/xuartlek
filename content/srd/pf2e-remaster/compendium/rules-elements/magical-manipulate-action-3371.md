# (magical, manipulate)

**Activate—Spellslash** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (magical, manipulate) **Trigger** You are the only target of a spell; **Effect** You swing or shoot your weapon to intercept the magic, attempting to counteract the spell. Your counteract rank is equal to half your level (rounded up), and your counteract check modifier is equal to your attack modifier with the affected weapon.
