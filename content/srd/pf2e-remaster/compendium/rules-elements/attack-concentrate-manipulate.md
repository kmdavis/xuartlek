# (attack, concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (attack, concentrate, manipulate) **Effect** A bolt of flames scorches out from the relic. Make a spell attack roll with your relic against a target within 30 feet. The bolt deals 1d6 fire damage for every 2 levels the relic has (minimum 1d6).

**Critical Success** The bolt deals double damage, as well as persistent fire damage equal to the level of the relic.

**Success** The bolt deals full damage.
