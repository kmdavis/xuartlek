# (manipulate)

**Activate—Steal a Second** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You manipulate time around an ally within 30 feet, allowing them to perceive time differently for a moment. The target is quickened for 1 round and can use the action only to Step or Stride.
