# (concentrate)

**Activate** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** Your last action was to cast a spell prepared from this grimoire that allows understanding of another language, such as _translate_ or _truespeech_; **Effect** The grimoire absorbs knowledge of one language translated this way (caster’s choice if more than one), allowing its bearer to communicate on a rudimentary level in that language even after the spell’s duration has elapsed. The _linguist’s dictionary_ can hold one language at a time.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 108*
