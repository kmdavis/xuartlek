# (manipulate)

**Activate—Disperse** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You scatter the dust into the air around you, causing it to condense into a cloud in a 20-foot burst within 10 feet, as the _mist_ spell. You can Sustain the activation to make the cloud Fly 20 feet. The cloud lasts 1 minute, and you can Dismiss it.
