# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Inexplicable Patch** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You command the apparatus to magically jury-rig an item you hold or that's within 5 feet of you. The item is repaired, as a 3rd-rank _mending_ spell. This lasts for 10 minutes, after which the item returns to its previous state of disrepair unless you've Repaired it before then.
