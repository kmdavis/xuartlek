# (concentrate, polymorph)

**Activate—Shifting Form** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, polymorph) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You’re under the effect of a polymorph spell that offers a choice of multiple forms; **Effect** You transform into a different form allowed by the polymorph by bending and molding the spell’s energy. This reduces the spell’s remaining duration by half.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 228*
