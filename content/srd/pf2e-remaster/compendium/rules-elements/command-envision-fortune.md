# command, envision (fortune)

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") command, envision (fortune) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You make a wish into the _iris of the sky_, yearning for it to strike true, and then fire. Strike against a foe with the iris, rolling the attack roll twice and taking the better result. If the attack is a failure, you take 5 persistent fire damage.
