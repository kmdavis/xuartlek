# (concentrate)

**Activate** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once every 1d4 rounds; **Effect** You emit a scream in a 15-foot cone that deals 4d4 sonic damage. Each creature in the area can attempt a DC 24 Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is frightened 1.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is frightened 2.
