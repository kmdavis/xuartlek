# (manipulate)

**Activate—Pick a Card** [?](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Varies") (manipulate) **Effect** You envision your desired cantrip, causing its card to rise to the top of the deck, and draw the card. The deck casts that cantrip as a 1st-rank spell, with a DC of 15 and a spell attack modifier of +5. The card crumbles into dust as the cantrip takes effect. This activation takes the same number of actions as the cantrip you chose takes to cast.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 232*
