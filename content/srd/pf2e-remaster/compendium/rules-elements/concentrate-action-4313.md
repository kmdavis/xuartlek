# (concentrate)

**Activate—Incorporeal Stride** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You focus on the head of the spear and channel its properties into your form. You Stride up to your Speed. You can move easily through solid objects and creatures during this movement, but you can’t end this movement within a solid object or in a creature’s space. You also gain resistance 10 to all damage during this Stride (except force or spirit damage and damage caused by a _ghost touch_ rune); double this resistance against non-magical damage.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 210*
