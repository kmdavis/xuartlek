# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Open the Eye** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** For the next 10 minutes, you gain an imprecise magic sense with a range of 30 feet. This allows you to detect spells, magical effects, and magical items within range. You also learn the rank or level of such magical effects and their traits. Deceptive illusion magic is not detectable by this magic sense.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 241*
