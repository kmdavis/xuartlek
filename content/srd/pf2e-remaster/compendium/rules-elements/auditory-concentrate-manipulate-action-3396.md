# (auditory, concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—The Hammer of Hooves** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (auditory, concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You hammer a thundering beat on the guangu. For 10 minutes, mounted allies within a 60-foot emanation gain a +10-foot status bonus to their mount’s Speeds. They also gain a +1 status bonus to Nature checks to Command an Animal and automatically succeed when they Command an Animal they’re mounted on to take a move action (such as Stride).
