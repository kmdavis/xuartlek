# 10 minutes (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate** 10 minutes (concentrate, manipulate) **Effect** Pick one side of the cube and set it face up. You attune the cube to the location you currently occupy. Each side can be attuned to only one location. Once you use this action to attune to a location, the side you pick loses any previously attuned location.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 114*
