# Sparkling Targe  *Magus Hybrid Study*

You've studied the applications of magic, training yourself to perform not just offensive maneuvers but defensive tactics as well. When magic flows through you, your shield can block impossible things, even a dragon's breath or a _force barrage_.

 You gain the general Shield Block feat.

 When you're in Arcane Cascade stance with your shield raised, your circumstance bonus to AC from your shield also applies to your saves against spells and other magical effects. In addition, damage you take from a spell or other magical effect while you're in Arcane Cascade can trigger your Shield Block reaction, even if the damage isn't physical. When blocking damage from a magical effect in this way, increase your shield's Hardness by an amount equal to the resistance you have from Arcane Cascade. These benefits apply whether you're using an actual shield, the _shield_ spell, or something else that works like a shield (such as a raised tome if you have the Raise a Tome feat).

**Studious Spell (7th)** Resist Energy

**Studious Spell (13th)** Mountain Resilience
