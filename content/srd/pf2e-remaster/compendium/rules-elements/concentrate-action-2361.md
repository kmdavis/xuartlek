# (concentrate)

**Activate—Divert Lighting** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** An electricity effect targets you or a creature within 10 feet of you, or has you or a creature within 10 feet of you in its area; **Effect** You try to divert the electricity off course, to be absorbed by _storm flash_. Choose one eligible creature to protect and roll a melee attack roll against the DC of the electricity effect. If you succeed, the chosen creature takes no electricity damage from the triggering effect.
