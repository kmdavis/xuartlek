# Aiuvarin  *Trait*

`Aiuvarin`  `Half-Elf`

A creature with this trait has the aiuvarin versatile heritage. Aiuvarins are of mixed ancestry, including elves or other aiuvarins. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by aiuvarins.
