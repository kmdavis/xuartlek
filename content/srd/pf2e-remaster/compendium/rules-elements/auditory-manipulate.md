# (auditory, manipulate)

**Activate—Rattle the Dead** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (auditory, manipulate) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** Blowing into the horn frightens ghosts and evil spirits who can hear its call. Make an Intimidation check to Demoralize against all creatures with the undead or unholy trait in a 30-foot emanation. This can affect even a mindless creature with that trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language.
