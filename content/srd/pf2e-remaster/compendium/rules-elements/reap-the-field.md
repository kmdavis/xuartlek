# Reap the Field  *Action*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Transcendence`

- **Requirements**: Your previous action was a successful Strike with the _mortal harvest

Time seems to lag as you blur across the battlefield, deciding the fate of many in a moment. Stride up to half your Speed and make another melee Strike with the mortal harvest against a different creature. This Strike uses the same multiple attack penalty as your previous Strike, but counts toward your multiple attack penalty as normal.
