# (concentrate)

**Activate—Scryshield** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The _mirrorblade_’s reflective surface dulls, and you and all your possessions are surrounded by a shimmering field that makes you invisible to scrying magic for 1 hour. In addition, you also learn about any scrying effects within a 30-foot emanation (as _detect scrying_). If you sheathe, drop, or attack with the _mirrorblade_, this effect ends early.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 209*
