# (concentrate)

**Activate—Fair Trade** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You deposit up to 30 gp of gems and coins in a separate pouch of the bottomless purse while thinking of a common consumable item costing that amount of money. You then upend the pouch. The item you imagined comes tumbling out into your hand.
