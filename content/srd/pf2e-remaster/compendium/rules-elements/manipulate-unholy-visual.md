# (manipulate, unholy, visual)

**Activate—Lo and Behold** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate, unholy, visual) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You raise _Lamentation of the Faithless_ high above the battlefield, presenting its terrible glory for all to witness. All creatures within 60 feet that are able to see the sword must attempt a DC 50 Will save. Creatures with the unholy trait are immune to this effect, but creatures with the holy trait take a –2 status penalty on this save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is stricken by crushing despair. It becomes slowed 1 as it sobs uncontrollably, and it can’t use reactions while it’s slowed.

**Failure** As success, but the creature is also blinded for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the duration of the blinded condition is 1 minute.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 128*
