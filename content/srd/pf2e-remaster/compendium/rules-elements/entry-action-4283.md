# 

**Activate—Phase Through** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") ; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Until the end of your current turn, you can move through non-magical solid surfaces. Treat any movement through an item thicker than air as difficult terrain. When the duration ends, if you’re sharing space with a solid item, you’ll be shoved into the closest unoccupied space and take 6d6 bludgeoning damage.
