# Custom Contracts  *Sidebar*

- **Parent page**: Hellbound Creatures

You can modify the abilities in the Hellbound creature template to suit the devil granting them or the specific agreement. For example, a mortal seeking allure might gain Diplomacy rather than Deception and _charm_ rather than _breathe fire_.

*Source: Monster Core 2 p. 98*
