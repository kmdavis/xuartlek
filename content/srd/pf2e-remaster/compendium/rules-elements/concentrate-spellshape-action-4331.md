# (concentrate, spellshape)

**Activate—Third Eye Rejuvenation** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, spellshape) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared 10th-rank arcane spell, the magical energies reinvigorate your spellcasting. You regain access to two spells of 4th rank or lower or one spell of 5th rank or higher that you’ve prepared and already cast today.
