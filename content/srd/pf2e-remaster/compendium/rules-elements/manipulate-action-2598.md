# (manipulate)

**Activate—Track Sigil** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** You detect the general direction toward the most recent _sigil_ you created using the ring. This activation fails if the _sigil_ is more than 5 miles away or if there's lead or running water between you and the _sigil_.
