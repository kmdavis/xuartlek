# (concentrate, manipulate)

**Activate—Crystal Exoskeleton** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, manipulate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The crystals on your vest grow and extend, becoming an architecturally sculpted exoskeleton that covers your form. You gain 20 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 hour. The exoskeleton crumbles into dust when the temporary Hit Points are depleted or when the duration ends, and the crystals return to their original state.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 202*
