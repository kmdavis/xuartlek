# (concentrate, plant)

**Activate—Grasping Vines** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate, plant) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You use the _verdant buckler_ to Shield Block a melee Strike; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The attacking creature must succeed at a DC 24 Reflex save or be grabbed by the shield’s animated vines (restrained on a critical failure). The affected creature is grappled or restrained for 1 round, until it Escapes (DC 24), or you move, whichever comes first.
