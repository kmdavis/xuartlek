# manipulate

**Activate** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") manipulate **Effect** You cause one of the tentacles forming the weapon's five barrels to stretch out and attempt to pull and grab a creature within 15 feet. The tentacle attempts to Grapple with a +13 bonus. It can attempt to Grapple any creature, regardless of size. On a success, the tentacle pulls the creature up to 10 feet directly towards you, until it's in a square adjacent to you.
