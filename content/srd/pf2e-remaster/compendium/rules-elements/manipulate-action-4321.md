# (manipulate)

**Activate—Bitter Medicine** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate) **Effect** You drink the _fu water_ to counteract ailments within yourself. Attempt a counteract check with a +9 counteract modifier and a counteract rank of 3 against one effect that imposes the confused, fascinated, frightened, or stupefied condition. You’re then sickened 1.
