# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _blur_, and the wand creates faint orbs of electricity concealed in the haze around you. Each time a creature that’s adjacent to you fails a flat check against the concealment from _blur_, they explode one of the orbs, causing them to take 5 electricity damage.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 142*
