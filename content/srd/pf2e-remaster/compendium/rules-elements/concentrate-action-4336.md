# (concentrate)

**Activate—Inscribed Insight** [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action") (concentrate) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If your last action was to Cast a prepared Spell that creates or manipulates written text or runes, such as _runic body_, the writing briefly forms into words conveying valuable information. Attempt to Recall Knowledge about any one creature or object that you can see with a +2 item bonus to the check.
