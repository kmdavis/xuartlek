# Cast a Spell

**Activate** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; **Effect** You cast _jump_, but can jump up to 60 feet. When you land you shatter the ground, making each creature in a 5-foot emanation off-guard until the start of its next turn. In addition, the space you land in and all squares in the emanation become difficult terrain for 1 minute.

*Source: Treasure Vault (Remastered) p. 139*
