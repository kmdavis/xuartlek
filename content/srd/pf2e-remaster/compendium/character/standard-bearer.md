# Standard-Bearer  *Class Sample Build*

_You gained your military training and leadership through direct combat experience, and now you march at the front with your banner held high._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence for your tactics and class DCs, Strength to improve your attacks, and then Charisma to improve your key skills.

### Skills

Athletics, Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Lore, Medicine, Society, Survival

### Starting Tactics

Coordinating Maneuvers , Defensive Retreat, Mountaineering Training, End It!, Strike Hard!

### Starting Feat

Armor Regiment Training

### Higher-Level Feats

Rapid Assessment (2nd), Banner Twirl (4th), Reactive Strike (6th), Defiant Banner (8th), Standard-Bearer's Sacrifice (10th)

*Source: Battlecry! p. 34*
