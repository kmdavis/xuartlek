# Scar Of The Survivor  *Exemplar Body Ikon*

- **Usage**: imbued in the skin

A scar on your body commemorates a time someone tried to end your story and failed—a testament to your resilience and fortitude.

**Immanence** Divine energy spreads outward from your scar, reinforcing your flesh. You gain the benefits of the Diehard feat and a +1 status bonus to Fortitude saving throws.

**Transcendence** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (Concentrate, Healing, Transcendence, Vitality)No Scar but This; **Effect** Your wounds knit shut with hardly a scratch. You regain 1d8 Hit Points. At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the healing increases by 1d8.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 45*
