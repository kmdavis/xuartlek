# Sar-, Diacritic Rune of Righteousness  *Runesmith Rune 13*

`Diacritic`  `Divine`  `Holy`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune

Clean arcs of gold faintly glow on the base rune, evoking celestial order and serenity. The base rune gains the holy trait, as does any damage it deals. If applied to an unholy creature, that creature is enfeebled 2 for as long as it bears the rune.

**Invocation**
When the base rune is invoked, it shines with holy light that harshly judges the target for any of its sins. In addition to the base rune's normal effect, the target is drained 1.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 61*
