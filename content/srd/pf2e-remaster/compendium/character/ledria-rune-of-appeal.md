# Ledria, Rune of Appeal  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Occult`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

No matter what skin, shell, or scale this rune is drawn on, it shines like gold. The rune-bearer gains a +1 status bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, and Performance checks.

**Invocation**
The rune draws creatures closer to the bearer. Each creature within 30 feet of the rune-bearer can Step closer to the rune-bearer as a free action.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
