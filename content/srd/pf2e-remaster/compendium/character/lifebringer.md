# Lifebringer  *Class Sample Build*

_You focus on supportive magic, channel divine power into healing energy for your allies to keep them standing._

### Attributes

Focus on Charisma to boost your magic, and Wisdom and Intelligence to help understand and treat the ailments your magic can’t heal.

### Skills

Lore, Medicine, Nature, Religion, Survival

### Mystery

Life

### Higher-Level Feats

Nudge the Scales (1st), Reach Spell (1st), Knowledge of Shapes (4th), Gifted Power (6th), Read Disaster (8th), Waters of Creation (10th)
