### Surprising Anatomy [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

**Trigger** An enemy adjacent to your eidolon deals damage to your eidolon; **Effect** Your eidolon's form shifts horribly to retaliate against the attacker. Your eidolon makes a melee unarmed Strike against the triggering enemy.

### Unfathomable Mind

Those who try to touch your eidolon's mind find their own minds shattered. When your eidolon succeeds at a saving throw against a mental effect from a creature, that creature takes 4d6 mental damage with a basic Will save against your spell DC. A creature who fails this save is also stupefied 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure). At 9th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

### Maddening Whispers

Your aberrant eidolon utters terrible secrets about the true nature of the universe. They can cast _unfathomable song_ once per day as an innate occult spell. Your eidolon can Sustain this spell as a free action if they spend at least one action Striding toward one of the targets.
