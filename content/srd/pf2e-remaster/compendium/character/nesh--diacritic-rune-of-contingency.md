# Nesh-, Diacritic Rune of Contingency  *Runesmith Rune 13*

`Diacritic`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune

The wavy lines of this diacritic seem to stretch the base rune into a wider area. When you draw this rune, choose a trigger that when met will invoke the base rune, using the same restrictions as for the trigger of a Ready action. When the trigger is met, you can Invoke the base Rune as a reaction; you can't invoke any additional runes with that reaction. If you define complicated conditions, as determined by the GM, the trigger might fail.

---
You can have only one _nesh-, diacritic rune of contingency_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't etch or trace it again for 10 minutes.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 60*
