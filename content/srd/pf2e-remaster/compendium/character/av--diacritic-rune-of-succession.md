# Av-, Diacritic Rune of Succession  *Runesmith Rune 5*

`Diacritic`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune

This diacritic surrounds the base rune with similar-looking smaller runes to give the impression of distant echoes. When the base rune is invoked, you can Invoke a single Rune on a different rune-bearer within 15 feet of the original rune-bearer as a free action.

---
You can have only one copy of _av-, diacritic rune of succession_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't etch or trace it again for 10 minutes.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 57*
