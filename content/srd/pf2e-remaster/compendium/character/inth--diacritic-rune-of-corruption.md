# Inth-, Diacritic Rune of Corruption  *Runesmith Rune 13*

`Diacritic`  `Divine`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Unholy`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune

This set of angular accents around the base rune channels the essence of fiendish corruption. The base rune gains the unholy trait, as does any damage it deals. If applied to a holy creature, that creature is enfeebled 2 for as long as it bears the rune.

**Invocation**
(fire) When the base rune is invoked, it burns away in unholy black fire that lingers on its target. In addition to the base rune's normal effect, the target takes 2d4 persistent fire damage.

---
**Level (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 2d4.
