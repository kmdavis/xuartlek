# Mutagenist  *Class Sample Build*

_You transform yourself and others with mutagens._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence and Strength. Constitution helps you survive transformations and combat, and Wisdom helps keep your mind intact despite the warping.

### Skills

Arcana, Athletics, Crafting, Intimidation, Occultism, Society, Stealth, Survival

### Research Field

Mutagenist

### Starting Feat

Alchemical Familiar

### Higher-Level Feats

Revivifying Mutagen (2nd), Regurgitate Mutagen (4th), Mutant Physique (8th), Mutant Innervation (14th), Persistent Mutagen (16th), Alchemical Revivification (20th)
