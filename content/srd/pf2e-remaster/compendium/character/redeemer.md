# Redeemer  *Class Sample Build*

_Your truth and compassion glow strong as the sun, bringing the wicked into the light of your goddess, the Dawnflower._

Take a high Strength for your combat abilities, and a good Charisma for your devotion spells.

Athletics, Diplomacy, Medicine, Religion

### Deity, Sanctification, and Cause

Sarenrae, holy, and redemption

### Starting Feat

Deity's Domain (truth)

### Higher-Level Feats

Divine Grace (2nd), Mercy (4th), Greater Mercy (8th), Ultimate Mercy (18th)
