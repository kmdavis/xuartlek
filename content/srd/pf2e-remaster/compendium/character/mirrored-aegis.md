# Mirrored Aegis  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

- **Usage**: any shield

This shield is polished so brightly it can reflect even spiritual and ethereal attacks.

**Immanence** The _mirrored aegis_ emits an aura in a 15-foot emanation that protects you and all allies in the aura from harm, granting a +1 status bonus to AC. If the _mirrored aegis_ houses your divine spark for 10 uninterrupted minutes, it is restored to full Hit Points.

**Transcendence** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (Force, Transcendence)Raise the Walls; **Effect** You Raise the _mirrored aegis_, which summons ethereal shields that surround you and one ally of your choice within 15 feet in a tortoise shield formation. You and the ally gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Reflex saves, and any save against a force, spirit, vitality, or void effect for 1 minute.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 45*
