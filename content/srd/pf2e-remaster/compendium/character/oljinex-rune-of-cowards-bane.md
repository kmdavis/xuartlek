# Oljinex, Rune of Cowards' Bane  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Arcane`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a shield

This rune resembles a broken arrow. The shield bearing the rune gains a +2 status bonus to its Hardness against physical damage from ranged attacks.

**Invocation**
(illusion, mental, visual) The rune creates an illusion in the minds of all creatures adjacent to the rune-bearer that lasts for 1 round. The illusion is of a large, impassable wall blocking all paths away from the rune-bearer. Creatures affected by this invocation can't willingly move farther away from the rune-bearer for 1 round or until they disbelieve the illusion, usually by using a Seek action.

---
**Level (+4)** The status bonus to Hardness increases by 1.
