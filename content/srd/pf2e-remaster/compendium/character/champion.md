# Champion  *Archetype 2*

#### Multiclass Champion Characters

The champion archetype greatly improves defenses, particularly armor. It's a great way for a character to gain armor proficiency or a powerful defensive reaction. You might choose a deity closely themed to your class, such as Nethys for a wizard.
