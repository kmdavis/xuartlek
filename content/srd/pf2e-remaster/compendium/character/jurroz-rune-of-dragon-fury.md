# Jurroz, Rune of Dragon Fury  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn onto a creature

This craggy rune channels the fury of dragon kind. Whenever an enemy Strikes or Steals from the rune-bearer, they become off-guard for 1 round. 

**Invocation**
As a free action, the rune-bearer can Fly up to 60 feet toward an enemy that has damaged it or stolen from it in the last minute. If it ends this movement adjacent to the enemy, the enemy becomes off-guard until the end of the rune-bearer's next turn. If the rune-bearer doesn't have a fly Speed and ends its movement in the air, it safely floats back to the ground at the end of its next turn.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 59*
