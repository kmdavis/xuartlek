# Spirit Reaper  *Class Sample Build*

_You channel spirits into your body before wading into battle, spreading terror in your wake._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence. Take Strength and Constitution to increase your front-line prowess. You can also invest in Charisma to better Demoralize your enemies or Wisdom for more access to Medicine skills.

### Skills

Arcana, Athletics, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Lore, Medicine, Occultism, Performance

### Fatal Method

Reaper

### Grim Fascination

Spirit

### Higher-Level Feats

Draining Strike (2nd), Bind Heroic Spirit (6th), Become as Spirit (12th), Anatomical Quartering (16th)
