# Aiuen, The Elf-Gate Key  *Runesmith Rune 17*

`Arcane`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Teleportation`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

This masterful circular rune is a key to the aiudara network of elf gates that stretch across the stars. Once per turn, when the rune-bearer Strides, it can instead teleport a distance equal to its Speed.

**Invocation**
You teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your other runes, as long as it is on the same plane. An unwilling target can be teleported a maximum distance of 60 feet, and it avoids being teleported at all if it succeeds at a Will save against your class DC.

---
If you have the ability to cast the _sigil_ cantrip, then you can use _aiuen, the elf-gate key's_ invocation to teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your sigils on the same plane, in addition to any of your runes.
