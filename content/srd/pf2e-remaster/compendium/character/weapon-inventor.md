# Weapon Inventor  *Class Sample Build*

_You've created an experimental weapon, in this case a bow, to wreak havoc on your foes from afar._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence for your inventor abilities and Dexterity to hit with your bow innovation.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Crafting, Occultism, Society, Stealth, Thievery

### Innovation

Weapon

### Starting Feat

Explosive Leap
