# Oraloq, Rune of Inarticulateness  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Magical`  `Mental`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

This hard-to-read rune makes language difficult for the unfortunate rune-bearer. When the rune-bearer attempts a skill check or ability with the linguistic trait or attempts to Cast a Spell that doesn't have the subtle trait, they must succeed a DC 5 flat check, or the action is lost.

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer must attempt a Will save as the rune puts a strong mental block on the part of their mind that interprets language. If the rune-bearer fails their save, they take 20 mental damage (or double damage on a critical failure) the first time they use any language, take a linguistic action, or Cast a Spell without the subtle trait. The rune-bearer is aware of this and knows what will trigger the damage. The effect ends once the damage is taken or at the end of the rune-bearer's next turn, whichever comes first.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage of the invocation increases by 5.
