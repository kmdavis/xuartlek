# Rehgog, Rune of Bestial Might  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Morph`  `Primal`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a willing creature

The jagged edges of this rune evoke the claws and talons of mighty beasts. The target's nose becomes an animal-like snout, giving the rune-bearer scent as an imprecise scent with a range of 30 feet.

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer's muscles bulge with the strength of a ferocious beast. The bearer can immediately Step as a free action. The next unarmed Strike the bearer attempts before the start of your next turn deals an additional die of damage.
