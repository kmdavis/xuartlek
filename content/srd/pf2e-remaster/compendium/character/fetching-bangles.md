# Fetching Bangles  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

- **Usage**: worn bracers

These lovely armbands sparkle and gleam, reflecting your own incredible magnetism.

**Immanence** Others find it hard to leave your captivating presence. An aura surrounds you in a 10-foot emanation. An enemy in the aura that attempts to move away from you must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or its move action is disrupted.

**Transcendence** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (Mental, Spirit, Transcendence)Embrace of Destiny; **Effect** Choose an enemy within 20 feet of you. It must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or be pulled directly toward you into a square adjacent to you.
