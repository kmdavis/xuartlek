# Yudici, Rune of Remonstrance  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Aura`  `Divine`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a shield

This majestic rune grants the shield's wielder the conviction to aid their allies. While a shield bearing this rune is raised, its bearer emits an aura in a 10-foot emanation that grants their allies a +1 circumstance bonus to AC.

**Invocation**
The shield regains 40 Hit Points, and all of the bearer's allies in a 10-foot emanation gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and skill checks for 1 round.

---
**Level (+3)** The Hit Points restored increases by 10.
