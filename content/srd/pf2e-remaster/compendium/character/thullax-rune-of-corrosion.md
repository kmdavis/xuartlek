# Thullax, Rune of Corrosion  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Acid`  `Arcane`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature or object

The swooping lines of this rune seem to melt into each other as if their edges were hazy and indistinct, though the meaning is clear to you. The rune-bearer's resistance to each physical damage type is reduced by 1 (if it has any such resistances). Its immunities are unaffected.

**Invocation**
The bearer takes 1d6 acid damage with a basic Fortitude save; on a critical failure, it takes 1 persistent acid damage.

---
**Level (+2)** The reduction in physical resistance increases by 1, the damage of the invocation increases by 1d6, and the persistent damage increases by 1.
