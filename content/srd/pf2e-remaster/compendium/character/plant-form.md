# Plant Form  *Familiar Ability*

- **Ability Type**: Familiar

Your plant familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a Tiny plant of a type roughly similar to the familiar's nature. This otherwise uses the effects of _one with plants_. You must have a familiar with the plant trait to select this ability.
