# Thousand-League Sandals  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

- **Usage**: worn shoes

Threadbare but trustworthy, your sandals have carried you this far, and they’ll carry you much further still.

**Immanence** Your sandals ease your travels on the path ahead, granting you a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed.

**Transcendence** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") (Transcendence)Marathon Dash; **Effect** Your feet carry you so quickly they leave a slipstream that speeds your allies on. You Stride. Each ally within 10 feet of you at the start of your movement can Stride as a reaction.
