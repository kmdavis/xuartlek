### Ooze Physiology

You and your ooze eidolon are directly connected in both body and mind, which renders your eidolon susceptible to effects that wouldn't normally bother an ooze. They don't have an ooze's normal immunities, but do gain resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1 resistance) against acid, critical hits, and precision damage and a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against mental effects. Additionally, their surface closes quickly after being wounded. The flat check to remove persistent bleed damage from the eidolon is DC 10 (or DC 5 after receiving particularly effective aid).

### Stretching Pseudopods

Your eidolon's pseudopods can stretch farther. All your eidolon's melee unarmed attacks gain the reach trait.

### Flowing Engulf [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

Your eidolon Strides up to double their Speed and can move through the spaces of any creatures in their path. Any creature of the eidolon's size or smaller whose space your eidolon moves through is engulfed unless it succeeds at a Reflex save against your spell DC. Your eidolon can attempt to engulf the same creature only once in a single use of Flowing Engulf, and they can contain as many creatures as can fit in their space.

 An engulfed creature is pulled into your eidolon's body. It is grabbed, slowed 1, and moves along with your eidolon as long as it is engulfed. It can get free by Escaping against your spell DC, moving to a space of its choice adjacent to your eidolon.
