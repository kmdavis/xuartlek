# Creation's Beast  *Class Sample Build*

_You are a beast unleashed onto the mortal Universe to exact vengeance and defeat those the gods have placed in your way._

### Attributes

Prioritize Strength and Constitution to let you dish out damage and disregard the blows of your enemies.

### Skills

Athletics, Intimidation, Religion

The Prideful, Born of the Bones of the Earth, Teacher of Heroes

**Hands of the Wildling**
