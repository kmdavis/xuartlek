# Sertum, Rune of Preparedness  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Primal`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

The wavy lines of this rune evoke fields of reeds, the branches of trees blowing in the wind, or other natural phenomena. The rune-bearer gains a +1 status bonus to Survival checks and to Lore checks related to a type of terrain or specific natural location you choose as you draw the rune. The rune-bearer can use Survival to roll initiative, and the status bonus applies to this roll.

**Invocation**
The rune reaches out to the surrounding terrain, shifting it to the rune-bearer's advantage. The rune-bearer can Step up to three times as a free action.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
