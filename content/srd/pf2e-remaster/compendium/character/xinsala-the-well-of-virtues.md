# Xinsala, The Well of Virtues  *Runesmith Rune 17*

`Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on an unoccupied square

This complex rune comprises many different ligatures from the ancient Thassilonian runes representing the seven virtues of rule, before they were corrupted by the runelords. When drawn, it creates a well of glowing energy that occupies the square it's drawn on and creates an aura of swirling, virtuous runes in a 30-foot emanation. While in the aura, you or any of your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC against magic, a +1 status bonus to saving throws against magic, and resistance 10 to damage from magical effects.

**Invocation**
(healing, vitality) The well explodes outward with invigorating force. The well heals any number of creatures in its area for a total of 70 Hit Points divided as you choose, and you and each of your allies in the area gain a +20-foot status bonus to your Speeds and are quickened for 1 round. The extra action from quickened can be used only to Stride or Strike.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 61*
