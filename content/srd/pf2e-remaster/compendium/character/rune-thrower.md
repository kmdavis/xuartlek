# Rune Thrower  *Class Sample Build*

_With a flick of your wrist, you puncture your foes with rune-covered knives._

### Attributes

Intelligence should be your highest modifier, followed by Dexterity for accuracy. A good Constitution will keep you alive longer.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Occultism, Stealth, Thievery

### Starting Feat

Remote Detonation

### Higher-Level Feats

Pattern Flight (2nd), Tracing Trance (6th), Unerring Runic Attraction (14th), Unbounded Invocations (18th)
