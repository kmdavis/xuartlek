# Per-, Diacritic Rune of Continuum  *Runesmith Rune 5*

`Diacritic`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune

This whirling diacritic recirculates the magic of the base rune onto another creature. After the base rune is invoked, it automatically traces itself on a different target of your choice within 15 feet of the original rune-bearer.

---
You can have only one copy of _per-, diacritic rune of continuum_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't etch or trace it again for 10 minutes.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 58*
