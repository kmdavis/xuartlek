# Esvadir, Rune of Whetstones  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a weapon or unarmed attack that deals piercing or slashing damage

This cuspate rune, when placed on a blade, ensures it won't go dull. Strikes with the weapon or unarmed attack deal an extra 2 persistent bleed damage per weapon damage die.

**Invocation**
A blast of cutting energy is released outward from the rune, dealing 1d8 slashing damage to a creature adjacent to the rune-bearer, with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage of the invocation increases by 1d8.
