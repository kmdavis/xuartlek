# Atryl, Rune of Fire  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Fire`  `Primal`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a crature or object

This rune is often placed on a stone in a hearth, its power ensuring the stone remains warm through the night. The bearer’s fire resistance, if any, is reduced by 5. Its immunities are unaffected.

**Invocation**
The bearer takes 1d8 fire damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a critical failure, it's also dazzled for 1 round.

---
**Level (+2)** The reduction in fire resistance increases by 1, and the damage of the invocation increases by 1d8.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 55*
