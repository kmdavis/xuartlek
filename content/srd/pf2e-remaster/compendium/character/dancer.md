# Dancer  *Class Sample Build*

_Through myriad styles of dance, you inspire your allies and channel otherworldly magic._

### Attributes

Make Charisma highest, followed by Dexterity. Add to Constitution for more health and to Intelligence for skills.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Athletics, Diplomacy, Medicine, Occultism, Performance

### Muse

Maestro

### Higher-Level Feats

Uplifting Overture (2nd), Triple Time (4th), Allegro (14th)
