# Cruonign, Rune of Leeching  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Death`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Usage**: drawn on a slashing or piercing weapon

The jagged shape of this rune resembles a vampire's fangs. Strikes with the rune-bearing weapon deal an additional 3 void damage. Whenever a living creature is damaged by one of these Strikes, the rune-bearer gains temporary Hit Points equal to the void damage the target took (after applying resistances and the like). These temporary Hit Points last 1 round.

**Invocation**
The rune lashes out in a 30-foot cone from the wielder of the weapon, dealing 4d10 void damage to each creature in the area, with a basic Fortitude save. If any creatures take damage, the wielder regains 12 Hit Points.

---
**Level (+2)** The additional void damage increases by 1, the damage of the invocation increases by 1d10, and the healing increases by 3.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 59*
