# Archer  *Class Sample Build*

_You take out your opponents from a distance with ranged weapons, and you excel at dispatching flying or other hard-to-reach enemies._

### Attributes

Prioritize Dexterity. Constitution and Wisdom helps with survivability, and Strength adds damage with propulsive weapons.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Medicine, Stealth, Thievery

### Starting Feat

Point Blank Stance

### Higher-Level Feats

Double Shot (4th), Triple Shot (6th), Debilitating Shot (10th), Opening Stance (Fighter) (14th), Multishot Stance (16th), Impossible Volley (18th)
