# Tilus, Rune of Vocabulary  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Arcane`  `Mental`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

Upon close inspection, this rune comprises hundreds of smaller characters of various runic languages. When you etch or trace this rune, choose one language you know. The rune-bearer can speak and understand the chosen language and gains a +1 status bonus to Society checks and to Lore checks related to writing (such as Library Lore and Scribing Lore).

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer's voice rings clear and true, allowing them to automatically succeed at any flat check that could disrupt an auditory action (such as the one imposed by the deafened condition) for 1 round. During this time, the bearer can also speak their true mind even if they are currently confused, controlled, or otherwise unable to speak.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
