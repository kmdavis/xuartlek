# Oracle  *Archetype 2*

- **PFS Note**: Characters with the Oracle archetype can still take 10 minutes of refocusing to reduce their cursebound condition, even if they do not have a focus pool._ A mysterious force granted you divine magic and a curse.

#### Multiclass Oracle Characters

The oracle archetype is a great choice for characters who want to use powerful cursebound abilities that come at a cost. It's especially good for characters looking to add some high-risk, high-reward options. 
Martial oracles can use powerful cursebound abilities to access unique magical effects.Spellcaster oracles can further flex their diverse pool of options at the cost of a curse.
