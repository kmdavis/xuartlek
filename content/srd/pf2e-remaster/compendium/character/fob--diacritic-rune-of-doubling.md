# Fob-, Diacritic Rune of Doubling  *Runesmith Rune 5*

`Diacritic`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune that doesn't deal damage

This diacritic consists of pairs of diamonds in opposite positions around the base rune. When the base rune is invoked, the rune-bearer is affected by it as usual, and you can choose to also be affected by the invocation if possible.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 58*
