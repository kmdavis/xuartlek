# Baruiel, Rune of Hold's Bravery  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Emotion`  `Magical`  `Mental`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a willing creature

This scratched rune reminds the bearer to stand strong against fear when near allies. As long as the rune-bearer is within 10 feet of one of its allies, whenever it would reduce its frightened condition by 1, it instead reduces it by 2.

**Invocation**
The essence of the rune is released outward, easing the minds of others. Each ally of the rune-bearer within a 10-foot emanation reduces their frightened condition by 2.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 55*
