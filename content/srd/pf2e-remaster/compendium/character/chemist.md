# Chemist  *Class Sample Build*

_With carefully formulated chemical compounds, you find chemical clues at crime scenes and boost your investigative acumen with mutagens and other elixirs._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence for your alchemy and deductive reasoning. Dexterity helps you stay out of harm’s way, while Constitution keeps you healthy.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Crafting, Deception, Diplomacy, Nature, Society, Stealth, Thievery

### Methodology

Alchemical sciences

### Starting Feat

Known Weaknesses

### Higher-Level Feats

Alchemical Discoveries (4th), Predictive Purchase (6th), Implausible Purchase (16th)
