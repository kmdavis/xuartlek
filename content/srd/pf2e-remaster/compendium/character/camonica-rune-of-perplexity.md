# Camonica, Rune of Perplexity  *Runesmith Rune 1*

`Mental`  `Occult`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

This rune of looping swirls is difficult to look straight at and stirs up a psychic cacophony in a foe's mind. The first time each round the rune-bearer takes an action with the concentrate trait, the turmoil increases, dealing it 2 mental damage.

**Invocation**
The cacophony fragments and expands rapidly in a mental explosion, dealing 1d4 mental damage to the rune- bearer with a basic Will save. On a failure, the rune-bearer is stupefied 1 for 1 round (or stupefied 2 on a critical failure).

---
**Level (+2)** The damage from the mental turmoil increases by 1 and the damage of the invocation increases by 1d4.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 55*
