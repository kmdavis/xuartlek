# Spellshooter  *Class Sample Build*

_Your magic explodes from the bullets of your firearm._

### Attributes

Make Dexterity your highest attribute modifier, followed by Intelligence and Wisdom to help you spot dangers from afar.

### Skills

Arcana, Crafting, Stealth, Survival

### Hybrid Study

Volatile Spark

### Higher-Level Feats
