# Flesh Puppeteer  *Class Sample Build*

_You fill the battlefield with flesh, slowing your enemy’s advance to a halt._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence. Wisdom will make you perceptive and act quicker once combat starts. Also invest in Dexterity and Constitution to shore up your defenses.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Crafting, Intimidation, Lore, Medicine, Occultism, Survival

### Fatal Method

Puppeteer

### Grim Fascination

Flesh
