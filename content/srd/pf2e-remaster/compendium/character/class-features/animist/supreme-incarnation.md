# Supreme Incarnation  *Class Feature 19*

- **Class**: Animist

You become a nexus between the mortal and spiritual realms through which your apparition can embody its full might. You gain a 10th-rank apparition spell slot. In addition to heightened versions of your apparition spells, you can use this slot to cast _avatar_. When you cast _avatar_ in this way, you do not become an avatar of a deity; rather, your primary attuned apparition incarnates itself using your body, transforming you into the avatar described in your primary attuned apparition's entry.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 10*
