# Fury  *Class Sample Build*

_You keep things simple, because you need only one thing to drive you: your own inner rage._

### Attributes

Prioritize your Strength, then your Constitution and Dexterity. Wisdom can make you more perceptive.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Athletics, Intimidation, Stealth

### Instinct

Fury instinct

### Starting Feat

Sudden Charge

### Higher-Level Feats

Furious Finish (2nd), Scars of Steel (4th), Reactive Strike (6th), Whirlwind Strike (14th)
