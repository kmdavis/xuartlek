# Feikris, Rune of Gravity  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Arcane`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature wearing armor

The lines of this rune overlap strangely, making it seem larger than it really is. The rune-bearer gains a +2 status bonus to Athletics checks. They can Disarm, Grapple, Reposition, Shove, or Trip creatures up to two sizes larger than themself, or three sizes larger if the rune-bearer is legendary in Athletics.

**Invocation**
All creatures in a 15-foot emanation around the rune-bearer must succeed at a Fortitude save or be pulled 5 feet toward the rune-bearer (or 10 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Level (17th)** The status bonus increases to +3.
