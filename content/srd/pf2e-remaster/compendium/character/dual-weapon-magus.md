# Dual-Weapon Magus  *Class Sample Build*

_Your magic flows easily through the two deadly weapons you wield._

### Attributes

Depending on the weapons you wield, Strength or Dexterity is your highest priority. You will also want to invest in the other score to deal more damage or dodge more attacks. A good Intelligence will also be useful.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, Intimidation

### Hybrid Study

Twofold twine

### Higher-Level Feats

Spirit Sheath (2nd), Recharging Flurry (4th), Reactive Strike (6th), Split Spellstrike (10th)
