# Blood Puppeteer  *Class Sample Build*

_You call upon bloody thralls to fight in your stead. They roam the battlefield, draining the blood from your foes._

### Attributes

Prioritize Intelligence. Dexterity is your second priority, followed by Constitution; then you can invest in either Wisdom or Charisma to strengthen your ability to solve problems with your skills.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, Crafting, Lore, Occultism, Society, Stealth

### Fatal Method

Puppeteer

### Grim Fascination

Blood

### Higher-Level Feats

Vampiric Spell (4th), Bloody Tendrils (6th), Sanguine Transference (8th), Sanguine Mastery (12th)
