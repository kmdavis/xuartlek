# Ur-, Diacritic Rune of Intensity  *Runesmith Rune 5*

`Diacritic`  `Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`

- **Usage**: drawn on a rune that deals damage

This diacritic accentuates the base rune with bolder lines to give greater weight to its effects. When the base rune is invoked, its invocation gains a status bonus to damage equal to 2 plus half your level.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 58*
