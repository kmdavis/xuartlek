# Spell Sniper  *Class Sample Build*

_Your spells fly as swift and true as your arrows._

### Attributes

Dexterity is your most important attribute modifier, followed by Intelligence. A good Wisdom can help you look for dangers, or choose Strength if you want to use a propulsive or thrown weapon.

### Skills

Acrobatics, Arcana, Deception, Society, Stealth

### Hybrid Study

Starlit span

### Higher-Level Feats

Expansive Spellstrike (2nd), Starlit Eyes (4th), Runic Impression (8th)
