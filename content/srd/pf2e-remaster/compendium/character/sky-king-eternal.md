# Sky King Eternal  *Class Sample Build*

_You brave the cold for your charges, believing it's your obligation to shelter them from the dangers of the Outer Planes._

### Attributes

Prioritize Strength for your melee attacks. A touch of Charisma will help you navigate your courtly social responsibilities outside combat.

Athletics, Diplomacy, Religion, Society
