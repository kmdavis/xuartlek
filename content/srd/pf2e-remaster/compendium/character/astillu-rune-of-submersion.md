# Astillu, Rune of Submersion  *Runesmith Rune 9*

`Magical`  `Rune`  `Runesmith`  `Water`

- **Usage**: drawn on a creature

Originating from Thalassic script, this wavy rune is popular with iruxi practitioners of rune magic who want to easily traverse their watery environs. The rune-bearer can breathe underwater and gains a swim Speed equal to its land Speed or 30 feet, whichever is greater. Its melee Strikes don't take the usual circumstance penalty for passing through water.

**Invocation**
The rune's power pushes the bearer forward in a surprising surge of speed. The rune-bearer can Swim up to 60 feet as a free action, and this movement doesn't trigger reactions.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 59*
