# Reprisal of the Fallen  *Feat 18*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: Broken Chain
- **Prerequisites**: Broken Chain Dedication

You can feel the sorrow, pain, and rage of those fallen to oppression, peering from just behind the veil of death, and you can bring them forth to exact their revenge. You can cast either a 9th-rank _invoke spirits_ or _wails of the damned_ once per day as an innate occult spell. For either spell, use your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.
