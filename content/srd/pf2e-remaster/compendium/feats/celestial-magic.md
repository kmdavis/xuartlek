# Celestial Magic  *Feat 9*

`Nephilim`

- **Prerequisites**: Angelkin, Lawbringer, Musetouched, or or another lineage feat associated with celestials

You possess celestial magic. Choose two of the following spells: _clear mind_, _everlight_, _humanoid form_, _revealing light_, _share life_, or _sure footing_. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells. Angelkin typically take _clear mind_ and _humanoid form_, lawbringers typically have _everlight_ and _share life_, and musetouched typically have _revealing light_ and _sure footing_.
