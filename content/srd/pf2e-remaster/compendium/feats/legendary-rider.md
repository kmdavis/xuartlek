# Legendary Rider  *Feat 20*

`Archetype`

- **Archetypes**: Cavalier, Mammoth Lord (Level 20)
- **Prerequisites**: Cavalier Dedication

You and your mount move like one being, effortlessly reading each other’s signals and synchronizing even in the thick of battle. Whenever you’re riding your mount, you are quickened; you can use the extra action only to command your mount using the Command an Animal skill action.
