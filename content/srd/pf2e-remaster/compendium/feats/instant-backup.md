# Instant Backup  *Feat 4*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Gunslinger`

- **Trigger**: Your attack with a firearm misfires.

Even as your firearm misfires, you quickly draw a backup. Release the misfired weapon and Interact to draw a different
weapon that requires the same number of hands or fewer.
