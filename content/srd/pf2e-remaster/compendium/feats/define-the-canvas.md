# Define the Canvas  *Feat 14*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Earth`  `Magical`  `Runesmith`

- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes

You produce rune-inscribed stones, which whirl in the air before darting away to form a perimeter for a runic canvas. The canvas is a 20-foot burst on the ground centered on a corner in your space. As long as you’re in or adjacent to the canvas, you can Trace a Rune directly into the ground, which causes the rune to expand across the entire space, tracing it onto any number of eligible targets within the area. The canvas lasts for 1 minute.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 52*
