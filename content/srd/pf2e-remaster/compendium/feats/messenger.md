# Messenger  *Feat 1*

`Automaton`

You were built with a special ability to relay private messages to your fellow automatons, but you can luckily use it with anyone you would like. You gain _message_ as an innate arcane cantrip.

**Enhancement** You can target up to five creatures at once when you cast _message_.
