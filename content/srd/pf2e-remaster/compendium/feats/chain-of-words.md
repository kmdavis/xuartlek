# Chain of Words  *Feat 10*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Force`  `Invocation`  `Runesmith`

- **Frequency**: once pr 10 minutes

You see the thin lines of magic between your creations, and you can exploit that connection with a small shift of power from one rune to another. You Invoke two Runes within 60 feet. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, a chain of glowing script flows between them, dealing 5d6 force damage to all creatures in a straight line between them with a basic Reflex saving throw; creatures bearing the invoked runes are not in the area of this line. A creature who critically fails its save is also immobilized for 1 round or until it Escapes, as the runes seal its movements. The DC to Escape is your class DC. 

At 12th level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 51*
