# Countercharm  *Feat 8*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Captivator
- **Prerequisites**: Basic Captivator Spellcasting
- **Trigger**: A creature Casts a Spell with the illusion or mental trait.
- **Requirements**: You must have a non-cantrip innate captivator spell with the same trait.

Your charms and illusions are so dominant that you can use them to counteract similar magic cast by others. When a foe casts a spell with the illusion or mental trait and you can see its manifestations, you can use your own innate magic to disrupt it. You lose an innate, non-cantrip spell with the same trait as the triggering spell as if you had cast the spell. You then attempt to counteract the triggering spell.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 93*
