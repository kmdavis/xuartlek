# Desperate Revival  *Feat 16*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Healing`  `Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Void`  `Negative`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Trigger**: You would be reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed.

On the verge of death, you drain the life of all around you in a desperate attempt to remain alive. You avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point. Each creature in a 60-foot emanation must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your spell DC. You regain Hit Points equal to the total damage dealt, up to a maximum of half your maximum Hit Points.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes void damage equal to half its level.

**Failure** The target takes void damage equal to its level.

**Critical Failure** The target takes void damage equal to double its level.
