# Resilient Chassis  *Feat 5*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Automaton`  `Concentrate`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Prerequisites**: defensive automaton heritage
- **Trigger**: You would take damage.

You are tough enough to draw on your own internal stores of energy when you get injured. You gain 4 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 round. 

At 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the temporary Hit Points increase by 4.

**Enhancement** Resilient Chassis’s frequency becomes once per 10 minutes.
