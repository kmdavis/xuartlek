# Return Unto Runes  *Feat 16*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Magical`  `Runesmith`

- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes
- **Trigger**: An enemy targets you with a spell attack or other magical attack against your AC.

You attempt to deconstruct an incoming spell into its individual magical components, storing them temporarily as a rune. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the spell becomes sealed in a rune, which floats around you. Until the end of your next turn, you can Trace this Rune onto a creature as normal. This rune doesn’t have an effect until it’s invoked.

When the rune is invoked, the rune-bearer is affected by the spell or magical attack captured in the rune. Make a check, using your class DC – 10 as the modifier against the rune‑bearer’s AC to determine the effects.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 53*
