# Halo  *Feat 1*

`Nephilim`

You have a halo of light and goodness that sheds light with the effects of a divine _light_ cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You can Sustain to suppress or reestablish the halo.
