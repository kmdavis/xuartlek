# Mirror-Risen  *Feat 1*

`Lineage`  `Reflection`

You were created from magic involving mirrors and still retain some ability to seek refuge in reflected illusions. You gain the Mirror's Trickery reaction.

 **Mirror's Trickery** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate, illusion, occult, visual) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would be hit by a Strike; **Effect** You create an illusory duplicate at the last instant and attempt to trick your foe into striking it instead of you. The attacker attempts a DC 11 flat check; on a failure, the attack hits the duplicate, changing the result from a critical success to a success or a success to a failure.

### Mirror-Risen leads to...

Mirror Refuge, Reflective Pocket
