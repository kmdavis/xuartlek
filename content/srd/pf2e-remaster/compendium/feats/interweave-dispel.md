# Interweave Dispel  *Feat 14*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Sorcerer`  `Spellshape`  `Metamagic`

- **Prerequisites**: dispel magic_ in your spell repertoire

You weave dispelling energy into a spell. If your next action is to cast a single-target spell against an enemy, and you either hit the enemy with the spell attack roll or the enemy fails its saving throw, you can cast _dispel magic_ on the enemy as a free action, expending a spell slot as normal and targeting one spell effect affecting the enemy.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 156*
