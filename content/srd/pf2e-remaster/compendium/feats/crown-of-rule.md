# Crown of Rule  *Feat 16*

`Exemplar`  `Ikon`

This badge of office proclaims your sovereignty. The imbued ikon gains the following ability.

**Transcendence—Heavy is the Crown** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, mental, spirit, transcendence) The weight of authority falls heavily upon you, as well as nearby creatures. All enemies in a 15 foot burst within 60 feet take spirit damage equal to your level with a basic Will save against your class DC. Any enemy that fails its save must immediately kneel, dropping prone as a free action.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 40*
