# Maze of Runes  *Feat 16*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Runesmith`

- **Prerequisites**: Legendary in Arcana; Legendary in Nature; Legendary in Occultism; Legendary in Religion

You can trap a foe in a twisting, extradimensional labyrinth of ever-shifting runes. You can cast _quandary_ once per day as an innate spell. The rank of this spell is equal to half your level rounded up, and the tradition can be any tradition for which you are at least legendary in the related skill. Instead of the usual skill checks to solve the puzzle, the target of your _quandary_ can attempt a Perception check, a Society check to Decipher Writing, or a check in the skill related to the tradition of the spell. When you Sustain this spell, you can also Trace a Rune or Invoke one Rune on the target creature as if it were adjacent to you.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 53*
