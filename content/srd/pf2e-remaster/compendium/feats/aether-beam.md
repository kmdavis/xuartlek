# Aether Beam  *Feat 8*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Force`  `Magical`  `Manipulate`

- **Archetype**: Living Nexus
- **Prerequisites**: Living Nexus Dedication
- **Requirements**: You are overflowing.

You can open your internal nexus, unleashing a destructive beam of magical energy that emanates from your hand, eyes, or chest. You fire a beam that deals 6d6 force damage in a 60- foot line and pushes back anyone who stands within. Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Reflex save against your nexus DC. A creature that fails is also pushed back 10 feet (20 feet on a critical failure). The damage increases by 2d6 at 10th level and every 2 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 97*
