# Translocational Adept  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication

Your command over teleportation grows. You can cast _translocate_ as an innate 4th-rank occult spell once per day. At 14th level, the spell is heightened to 5th rank. When you cast _translocate_ this way, you can Part the Veil as a free action when you arrive at your destination, even if you wouldn’t normally meet its requirements.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 105*
