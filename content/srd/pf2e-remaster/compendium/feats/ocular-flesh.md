# Ocular Flesh  *Feat 14*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Necromancer`  `Occult`

You create a fleshy eyeball that is the size of a marble. You can take a single action, which has the concentrate trait, to look through any of your eyeballs until the start of your next turn. An eyeball can be manipulated like a marble, such as being rolled or thrown. One can also be affixed to a surface, for example a wall, with 1 minute’s work. An eyeball lasts for 24 hours, and you can maintain a total of three eyeballs at any given time. Creating a fourth destroys the oldest eyeball. 

Eyeballs are very small but are easy to spot. A creature actively Seeking or Searching automatically finds an eyeball. They’re also not very sturdy and can be destroyed with an Interact action.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 39*
