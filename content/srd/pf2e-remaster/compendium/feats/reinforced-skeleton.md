# Reinforced Skeleton  *Feat 12*

`Necromancer`

- **Prerequisites**: bone grim fascination

You take on the toughness and lightness of bone. You gain resistance equal to half your level to two damage types; one is your choice of cold, electricity, or fire, and the other is your choice of slashing or piercing. You also gain a +5- foot status bonus to your Speeds. Whenever you destroy a thrall, this bonus to your Speeds increases to +10 feet for 1 round.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 39*
