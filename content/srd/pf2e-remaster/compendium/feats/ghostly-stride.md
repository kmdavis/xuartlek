# Ghostly Stride  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Spirit`

You fade into a slightly incorporeal form as you move. You Stride up to half your Speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions, and you can move through enemies’ spaces during it. If you have a fly Speed, you can instead Fly up to half your fly Speed.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 35*
