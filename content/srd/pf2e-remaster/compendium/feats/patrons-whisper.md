# Patron's Whisper  *Feat 10*

`Archetype`  `Auditory`

- **Archetype**: Seneschal
- **Prerequisites**: Seneschal Witch Dedication

You gain the ability to speak to your charge over great distances. You gain _message_ as an innate cantrip of your former patron’s tradition. You can send a message to a creature regardless of range if they have ever been your charge. If you Lie as part of the message, the target receives a –2 circumstance penalty to their Perception DC and to Sense Motive checks to reveal the lie.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 63*
