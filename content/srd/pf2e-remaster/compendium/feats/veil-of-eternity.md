# Veil of Eternity  *Feat 8*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication

You can remain within the interstices of reality longer. Your Part the Veil has a duration of one hour, though it still ends if you take a hostile action. Additionally, while Avoiding Notice during exploration, you can also Part the Veil.
