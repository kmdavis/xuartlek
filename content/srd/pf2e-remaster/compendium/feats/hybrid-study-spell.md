# Hybrid Study Spell  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Magus
- **Prerequisites**: Magus Dedication

You gain the conflux spell from a hybrid study of your choice. If you don’t already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. You can Refocus by studying your spellbook and performing a physical regimen. You don’t gain any of the hybrid study’s other benefits.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 88*
