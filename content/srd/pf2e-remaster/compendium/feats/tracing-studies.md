# Tracing Studies  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Runesmith
- **Prerequisites**: Runesmith Dedication

You can now create runes in the midst of combat. You gain the Trace Rune action.

**Trace Rune** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") or [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, magical, manipulate, runesmith) **Requirements** You're holding or wearing an artisan's toolkit; **Effect** You apply one rune to an adjacent target matching the rune's usage entry. You can have any number of runes traced at a time, but each rune remains only until the end of your next turn. If you spend 2 actions to Trace a Rune, you draw the rune in the air, causing it to appear on a target within 30 feet.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 90*
