# Improved Invigorating Elixir  *Feat 8*

`Alchemist`

- **Prerequisites**: Invigorating Elixir

Choose physical invigoration or mental invigoration, and add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: **Physical Invigoration** blinded, deafened, drained, paralyzed, slowed; **Mental Invigoration** confused, controlled, fleeing, frightened, paralyzed, slowed.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time to choose a different type of invigoration and add its options to those you can choose.
