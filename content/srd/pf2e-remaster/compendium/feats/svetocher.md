# Svetocher  *Feat 1*

`Dhampir`  `Lineage`

You were born to a moroi, the most common vampires, and you share their supernatural fortitude and power of persuasion. When you have the drained condition, calculate the penalty to your Fortitude saves and your Hit Point reduction as though the condition value were 1 lower. You're trained in Diplomacy. If you would automatically be trained in it, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
