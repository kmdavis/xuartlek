# A Thousand Cries For Help  *Feat 16*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: Ascended Celestial
- **Prerequisites**: Ascended Celestial Dedication

You’ve learned to communicate with creatures from all over the world, overcoming language barriers to lend a hand and help where you’re needed. You’re affected by a constant _truespeech_ spell. 

When you attempt a Diplomacy check to Make an Impression or Request, you can spend 1 Mythic Point to make the check at mythic proficiency.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 116*
