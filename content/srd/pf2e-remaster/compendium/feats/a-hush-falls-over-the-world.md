# A Hush Falls Over the World  *Feat 10*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Illusion`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: World Rouser
- **Prerequisites**: World Rouser Dedication
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

Sound is absorbed in your waking world, covering everything in a shroud of quiet until the start of your next turn. Creatures in your waking world become hidden to imprecise hearing or concealed to precise hearing. A creature in your waking world attempting to use spell or effect with the auditory or sonic trait must succeed at a DC 4 flat check; on a failure, the spell or effect is disrupted. Anytime a creature in the area is affected by an auditory or sonic effect, they must attempt a DC 16 flat check. On a success, they are not affected by the auditory or sonic effect, though they still may be affected by other aspects of an ability (for example, a creature hit by a weapon with a _thundering_ rune would not take sonic damage but would still take the normal damage from the weapon).
