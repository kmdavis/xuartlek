# Enhance Spell  *Feat 8*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`

- **Archetype**: Spellshifter
- **Prerequisites**: Spellshifter Dedication

You attune your senses to the magical frequencies generated by an allied spellcaster, allowing you to amplify or modify their spells to suit the needs of the moment. Select one willing ally within 30 feet that you can see and choose one of the following spellshape feats: Conceal Spell, Nonlethal Spell, Reach Spell, or Widen Spell. The target gains the benefits of the feat on their next turn as though they had taken the spellshape action themselves; this requires them to Cast a Spell as their first action on their turn.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 103*
