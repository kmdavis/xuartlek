# Quickened Casting  *Feat 10*  [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Bard`  `Concentrate`  `Necromancer`  `Oracle`  `Sorcerer`  `Spellshape`  `Witch`  `Wizard`  `Metamagic`

- **Archetype**: Time Mage (Level 12)
- **Frequency**: once per day

If your next action is to cast a cantrip or a spell that is at least 2 ranks lower than the highest-rank spell slot you have, reduce the number of actions to cast it by 1 (minimum 1 action).

**Special** This feat can only be used on a cantrip or spell that is at least 2 ranks lower than the highest-rank spell slot from the class or archetype matching the one you gained this feat from.
