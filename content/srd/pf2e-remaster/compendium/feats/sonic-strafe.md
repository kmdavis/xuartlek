# Sonic Strafe  *Feat 16*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Air`  `Archetype`  `Sonic`

- **Archetype**: Winged Warrior
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: Winged Warrior Dedication
- **Requirements**: You currently have a fly Speed.

You can now achieve such flight speeds that your passage splits the very air. Fly twice. Any creature that is adjacent to you at any point during your movement takes 10d6 sonic damage (basic Fortitude save against your class DC). On a critical failure, the creature is also deafened for 1 hour.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 83*
