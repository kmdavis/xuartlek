# System Override  *Feat 17*

`Automaton`

When someone takes control of your consciousness or your body, you innately resist their domination with everything you have. At the beginning of your turn, if you have the controlled condition, you must attempt a DC 11 flat check. On a success, you ignore the controlled condition for 1 round.
