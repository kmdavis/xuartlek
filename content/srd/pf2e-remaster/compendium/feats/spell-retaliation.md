# Spell Retaliation  *Feat 6*  [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Magus`

- **Trigger**: You succeed at a saving throw against an enemy’s spell, or an enemy fails a spell attack roll against you.
- **Requirements**: Your Spellstrike isn’t charged.

Battling other spellcasters is one of your major focuses, and harnessing magic from spells you defend against gives you everything you need to fight back. You recharge your Spellstrike.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 21*
