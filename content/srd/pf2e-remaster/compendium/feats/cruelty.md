# Cruelty  *Feat 4*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: touch of the void

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. You can cast _touch of the void_ targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, the target is also enfeebled 1 for 1 minute if it fails its save (enfeebled 2 if it critically fails).
