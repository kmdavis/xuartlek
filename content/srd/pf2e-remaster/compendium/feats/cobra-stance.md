# Cobra Stance  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Monk`  `Stance`

You enter a tightly coiled stance, with your hands poised as venomous fangs. While in this stance, the only Strikes you can make are cobra fang unarmed attacks. These deal 1d4 piercing damage; are in the brawling group; and have the agile, deadly d10, finesse, unarmed, and venomous traits.

 While in Cobra Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves and your Fortitude DC, and you gain poison resistance equal to half your level.

Cobra Envenom
