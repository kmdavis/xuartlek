# Inspired Stratagem  *Feat 8*

`Rogue`

During your daily preparations, you can review stratagems for the day’s adventures with up to five allies. Later, you can quickly advise them on your schemes using the below reaction.

**Inspired Stratagem** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (fortune, linguistic) **Trigger** An ally you reviewed stratagems with is about to attempt an attack roll or skill check; **Effect** The ally rolls the triggering check twice and takes the better of the two results. That ally then becomes temporarily immune to your Inspired Stratagem until your next daily preparations.

*Source: Player Core p. 173*
