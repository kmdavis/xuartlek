# Guding Retort  *Feat 14*

`Eidolon`  `Evolution`  `Summoner`

- **Prerequisites**: Eidolon's Retort

With a cunning twist of their unarmed attack, your eidolon shifts the position of a foe. When your eidolon uses Eidolon’s Retort to Strike and hits, your eidolon can Reposition the target up to 10 feet.
