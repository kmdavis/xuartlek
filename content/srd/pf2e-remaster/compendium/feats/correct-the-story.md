# Correct The Story  *Feat 6*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Misfortune`  `Mythic`  `Uncommon`

- **Trigger**: An enemy critically succeeds at an attack roll or saving throw.
