# Afterimage  *Feat 12*  [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Archetype`  `Illusion`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication
- **Trigger**: You use an action or cast a spell with the teleportation trait.

You leave behind an afterimage of yourself in your previous square. This afterimage looks like you and can perform simple behaviors to make the deception more plausible but otherwise cannot move or take actions. A creature can distinguish between you and your afterimage easily. Your afterimage automatically fails all checks and DCs and is destroyed if it would take any damage; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute. Whenever you take the Step action, you can instead teleport to an afterimage instead. If you do, the action gains the teleportation trait, and the afterimage you teleport to is destroyed.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 105*
