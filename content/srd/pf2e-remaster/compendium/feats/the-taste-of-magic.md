# The Taste of Magic  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`  `Magical`

- **Archetype**: Living Nexus
- **Prerequisites**: Living Nexus Dedication

Each working of magic has a unique scent and frequency of vibration. You have trained yourself to recognize the minute differences in magics, as if distinguishing between different odors. You gain scent as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. This scent can only detect creatures, items, and effects that are currently under the effect of a spell or have the arcane, divine, magical, occult, or primal trait. In addition, you may use Arcana, Occultism, Nature, or Religion instead of Survival to Track creatures you could sense with this ability.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 96*
