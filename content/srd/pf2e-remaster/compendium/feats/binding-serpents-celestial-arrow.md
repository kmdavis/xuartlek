# Binding Serpents Celestial Arrow  *Feat 6*

`Exemplar`  `Ikon`

As you prepare to shoot your weapon, you invoke divine serpents that bind your enemies. The imbued ikon gains the following ability.

Transcendence—Coiling Serpents [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (transcendence) Make a ranged Strike with your ikon. If the Strike hits, the target must succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC or the arrow transforms into a multitude of ethereal snakes that coil around the target, immobilizing it until it succeeds at an Escape attempt against your class DC.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 36*
