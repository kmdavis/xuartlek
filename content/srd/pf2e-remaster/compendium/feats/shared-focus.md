# Shared Focus  *Feat 10*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Wizard`

- **Prerequisites**: focus pool

Your magical expertise allows you to perform a metaphorical sleight of hand to manifest a Focus Point. Choose a willing ally within 30 feet who also has a focus pool. The target gains 1 temporary Focus Point until the start of your next turn, at which time it’s lost, even if not spent. This temporary Focus Point is separate from the Focus Points in their focus pool, allowing them to have it even if they’re at their pool’s maximum. The creature then becomes temporarily immune to Shared Focus until their next daily preparations.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 109*
