# Swift Retribution  *Feat 18*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: champion's reaction

The transgressions of your enemies fuel you to retaliate with divine speed. When an enemy triggers your champion's reaction, you are quickened on your next turn. You can use the extra action to move closer to that enemy or to Strike that enemy.
