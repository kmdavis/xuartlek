# Rune Ward  *Feat 1*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Runesmith`

- **Trigger**: You are targeted by a spell.
- **Requirements**: You are trained in the skill associated with the tradition of the triggering spell (Arcana for arcane, Nature for primal, Occultism for occult, and Religion for divine).

You quickly sketch a rune in the air to gain a modicum of protection against the triggering magic. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your saving throw and AC against the spell.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 48*
