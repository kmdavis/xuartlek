# Grown of Oak  *Feat 6*

`Druid`

- **Prerequisites**: leaf order

You can make your skin take on the woody endurance of an ancient tree and have your familiar follow suit. You can cast _oaken resilience_ at will as an innate primal spell with a spell rank one lower than the highest rank of spells you can cast. This spell targets you, as well as your leshy familiar if it’s within 30 feet of you.
