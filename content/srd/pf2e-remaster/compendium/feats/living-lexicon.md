# Living Lexicon  *Feat 18*

`Runesmith`

Your knowledge of runes knows no bounds. You add an additional rune to your runic repertoire of any level. Additionally, each day during your daily preparations, you can swap any one rune in your runic repertoire with another rune of the same level.
