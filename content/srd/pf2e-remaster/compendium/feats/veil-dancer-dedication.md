# Veil Dancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Trained in Stealth

You’re able to shift yourself into the spaces between to avoid notice. You gain the Part the Veil action.

**Part the Veil** [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action") (concentrate, magical) **Requirements** You are not observed by any enemies; **Effect** You phase yourself into the interstices, veiling yourself from physical reality. You become concealed. You can use this concealment to Hide or Sneak as your disassociation from reality makes you difficult to focus on. If you use a hostile action, this effect ends after the action is complete; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute.

### Veil Dancer Dedication leads to...

Aerial Flash, Afterimage, Contingency Leap, Dart Between, Dimensional Pilfer, Fade Presence, Gossamer Blade, Hush, Interstitial Escape, Translocational Adept, Veil of Eternity, Warp Path
