# Terrifying Invocation  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Invocation`  `Runesmith`

You spit and roar as you pronounce your rune's terrible name. You attempt to Demoralize a single target within 30 feet and then Invoke one Rune upon that target. You don't take a penalty to your check if the creature doesn't understand your language.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 49*
