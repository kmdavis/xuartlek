# Raging Athlete  *Feat 4*

`Barbarian`

- **Prerequisites**: Expert in Athletics

Physical obstacles can't hold back your fury. While you are raging, you gain a climb Speed and swim Speed equal to your land Speed, and the DC of High Jumps and Long Jumps decreases by 10, and you increase your distance on a successful Long Jump by 10 feet. Your distance for a vertical Leap increases to 5 feet vertically, and your distance for a horizontal Leap increases to 15 feet if your Speed is at least 15 feet, and to 20 feet if your Speed is at least 30 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 78*
