# Quick Vengeance  *Feat 18*

`Guardian`

- **Prerequisites**: Intercept Attack

Indignant at any who would strike you or your allies, you get your revenge. When you use Intercept Attack or Shield Block, you are quickened on your next turn. You can use the extra action only to Stride closer to the enemy whose attack triggered that reaction or to Strike that enemy.
