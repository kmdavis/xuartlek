# Magus's Analysis  *Feat 1*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: Spellstrike

You make an assessment informed by your knowledge of how a creature fights. Attempt a check to Recall Knowledge about a creature, then recharge your Spellstrike. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your check if you previously hit the creature with a Strike this turn. The subject of your check is temporarily immune to Magus’s Analysis for 1 day.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 17*
