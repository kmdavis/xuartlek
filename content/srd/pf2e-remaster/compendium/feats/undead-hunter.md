# Undead Hunter  *Feat 1*

`Automaton`

- **Prerequisites**: newly minted automaton heritage

You were created by a Nexian mage specifically for the purpose of fighting undead, and you are infused with vitality magic. You gain _vitality lash_ as an innate divine cantrip.

**Enhancement** You can infuse your attacks with vitality. You can cast _infuse vitality_ as a 3rd-rank arcane innate spell once per day, targeting only yourself.
