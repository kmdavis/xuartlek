# Barbarian Dedication  *Feat 2*

`Archetype`  `Dedication`  `Multiclass`

- **Archetype**: Barbarian
- **Prerequisites**: Constitution +2; Strength +2

You become trained in Athletics; if you were already trained in Athletics, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in barbarian class DC. You can use the Rage action. While raging, you take a –1 penalty to AC. Choose an instinct as you would if you were a barbarian. You have that instinct for all purposes, but you don’t gain any of the other abilities it grants. If that instinct has anathema, you’re bound by them. 

### Barbarian Dedication leads to...

Barbarian Resiliency, Basic Fury, Instinct Ability, Juggernaut's Fortitude
