# Gossamer Blade  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication

Drawing upon your command of intangible spaces, you create an echo of your weapon to resonate through its victim. The creature takes 1d6 persistent force damage per damage dice of your weapon.
