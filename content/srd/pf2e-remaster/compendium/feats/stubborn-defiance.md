# Stubborn Defiance  *Feat 9*

`Centaur`

You're brave and stubborn and refuse to back down or bow to the wishes of another. You gain a +1 status bonus to saves against mental effects. Additionally, you gain the I Defy You! reaction.

 **I Defy You!** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (fortune, mental) **Trigger** You fail a save against a mental effect; **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You reroll the triggering save and must take the second result.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 33*
