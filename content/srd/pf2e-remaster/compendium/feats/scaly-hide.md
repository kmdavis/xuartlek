# Scaly Hide  *Feat 1*

`Dragonblood`

You were born with a layer of scales across your entire body that resemble those of your draconic progenitor. When you’re unarmored, the scales give you a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dexterity cap of +3. The item bonus to AC increases to +2 at 5th level. The item bonus to AC from these scales is cumulative with _armor potency runes_ on your explorer's clothing, the _mystic armor_ spell, or _bands of force_.

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into or out of this feat.
