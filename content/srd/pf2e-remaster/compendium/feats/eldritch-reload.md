# Eldritch Reload  *Feat 10*  [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Eldritch Archer
- **Prerequisites**: Eldritch Archer Dedication
