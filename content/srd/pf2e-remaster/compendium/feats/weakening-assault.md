# Weakening Assault  *Feat 12*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Guardian`

With a barrage of blows, you diminish an enemy's strength. Strike an enemy affected by your Taunt twice. If either Strike hits, the target is enfeebled 1 until the beginning of your next turn. If both Strikes hit, the target is enfeebled 3 instead.

*Source: Battlecry! p. 47*
