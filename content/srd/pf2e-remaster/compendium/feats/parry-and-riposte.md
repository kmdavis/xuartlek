# Parry and Riposte  *Feat 18*

`Swashbuckler`

- **Prerequisites**: Opportune Riposte

Your parries and finishers allow you to riposte with the slightest provocation. You can use Opportune Riposte against an enemy who fails at a Strike against you (not just critically fails), provided you have both damaged that creature with a finisher on your last turn and currently have a circumstance bonus to AC from the parry weapon trait, or Extravagant Parry.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 169*
