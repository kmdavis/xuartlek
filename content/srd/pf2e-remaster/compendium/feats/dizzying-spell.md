# Dizzying Spell  *Feat 14*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Spellshape`  `Wizard`  `Metamagic`

Your spell leaves a foe’s head spinning and unable to concentrate. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that requires a Will saving throw and targets a single creature, the target is stupefied 2 for 1 round if it fails its saving throw (or stupefied 3 on a critical failure).

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 108*
