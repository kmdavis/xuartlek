# Violent Unleash  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Mindshift`  `Occult`  `Psychic`

- **Requirements**: Your previous action was to Unleash your Psyche.

The force of your mind unleashing itself releases a violent shock wave that batters your enemies. You deal 2d6 force damage to all creatures in a 20-foot emanation, with a basic Reflex save.

 At 5th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
