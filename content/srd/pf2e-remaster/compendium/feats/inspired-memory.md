# Inspired Memory  *Feat 3*

`Archetype`  `Skill`

- **Archetype**: Living Nexus
- **Prerequisites**: Living Nexus Dedication

A memory of each spell you have absorbed remains within your soul. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Recall Knowledge about creatures whose magic you have absorbed within the last minute. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Learn a Spell that you have absorbed within the last week.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 96*
