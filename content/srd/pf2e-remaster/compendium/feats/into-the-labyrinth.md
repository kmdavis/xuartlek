# Into the Labyrinth  *Feat 17*

`Minotaur`

Calling on your connection to minotaurs of myth, you shunt an enemy into a complex maze full of puzzles of your own devising. Once per day, you can cast _quandary_ as an 8th-rank occult innate spell, which takes the form of a labyrinth instead of a puzzle room. Targets cannot attempt to use Occultism to escape the spell (as normal for _quandary_), but they can use Survival to find their way through the labyrinth's corridors.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 45*
