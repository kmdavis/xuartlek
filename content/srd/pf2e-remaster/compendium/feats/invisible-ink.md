# Invisible Ink  *Feat 2*

`Runesmith`

After your rune is drawn, it flares once and then fades, leaving only the barest mark. When you apply a rune on an object or creature, you can Conceal an Object as a free action to hide that rune. If a creature would get a chance to find the rune, the GM uses your Crafting DC instead of your Stealth DC.
