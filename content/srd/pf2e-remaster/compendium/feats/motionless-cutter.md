# Motionless Cutter  *Feat 6*

`Exemplar`  `Ikon`

Your weapon is so sharp even an insect alighting upon its still blade would be severed. Your weapon ikon gains the following ability.

**Transcendence—Sever Four Dragonfly Wings** [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action") (transcendence) Make a Strike that deals slashing damage with your weapon ikon. If that Strike is successful, you can immediately make an additional Strike against a different target within your reach. You can continue making Strikes in this manner, each against a different target, until you have made a total of four Strikes or you miss with a Strike, whichever comes first. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 37*
