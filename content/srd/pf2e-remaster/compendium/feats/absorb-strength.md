# Absorb Strength  *Feat 5*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Kholo`  `Uncommon`  `Gnoll`

- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Trigger**: You are adjacent to an enemy's corpse who has been dead for no longer than 1 minute.

You consume a piece of your enemy, absorbing their strength. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the enemy's level that last for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 18*
