# Zoophonic Communication  *Feat 1*

`Bard`

- **Prerequisites**: zoophonia muse

Your studies have expanded your ability to parley and negotiate into the animal realm. You can Request from animals using Performance instead of Diplomacy as though you shared a language, although this gives you no special ability to understand their responses. You can also use Performance instead of Nature to Command an Animal.

*Source: Howl of the Wild p. 65*
