# Become as Spirit  *Feat 12*

`Necromancer`

- **Prerequisites**: spirit grim fascination

You can move through an unwilling creature’s spaces as if they were difficult terrain. Additionally, you ignore natural difficult terrain and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Escape attempts. Whenever you destroy a thrall, you gain resistance to all damage (except force, _ghost touch_, spirit, or vitality) equal to half your level for 1 round.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 38*
