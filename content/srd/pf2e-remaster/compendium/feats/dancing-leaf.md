# Dancing Leaf  *Feat 2*

`Monk`

You are as light as a leaf on the breeze. When you Leap or succeed at a High Jump or Long Jump, increase the distance you jump by 5 feet. When calculating the damage you take from falling, don't count any distance fallen while you are adjacent to a wall.
