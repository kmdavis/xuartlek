# Impaling Bone  *Feat 17*

`Kholo`  `Gnoll`

Once per day, you can cast _impaling spike_ as a 7th-rank innate occult spell, except the spike is made of bone instead of cold iron. The bone from the spell has the effects of the _ghost touch_ property rune and can immobilize incorporeal creatures as if they were corporeal.
