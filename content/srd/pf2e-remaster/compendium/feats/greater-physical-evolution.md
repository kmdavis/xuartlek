# Greater Physical Evolution  *Feat 12*

`Sorcerer`

- **Prerequisites**: Primal Evolution or Arcane Evolution

You change form readily. Once per day, you can use a sorcerer spell slot to cast any common polymorph battle form spell of the spell slot's rank as if it were a signature spell in your repertoire. You can use the extra spell slot from or Primal Evolution instead of a sorcerer spell slot.
