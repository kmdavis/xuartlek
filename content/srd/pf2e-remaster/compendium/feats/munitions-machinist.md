# Munitions Machinist  *Feat 6*

`Gunslinger`

- **Prerequisites**: Munitions Crafter; Expert in Crafting

You're adept at crafting ammunition. You gain the Quick Alchemy benefits, creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. You can use Quick Alchemy only to create bombs or alchemical ammunition. Using Quick Alchemy to create alchemical ammunition also counts as 1 action toward activating that ammunition.

### Munitions Machinist leads to...

Precious Munitions
