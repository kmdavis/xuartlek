# Cantorian Restoration  *Feat 17*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Healing`  `Hobgoblin`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Trigger**: A living creature within 60 feet would die.

The energy that flows in your blood can save a life in the direst of times. You prevent the creature from dying and restore 6d8 + your Constitution modifier Hit Points to it. You can't use Cantorian Restoration if the triggering effect was a death effect or an effect that leaves no remains, such as _disintegrate_.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 15*
