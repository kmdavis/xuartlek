# Weight of Guilt  *Feat 1*

`Champion`

- **Prerequisites**: redemption cause

Guilt clouds the minds of those who ignore your Glimpse of Redemption. Instead of making an enemy who refuses redemption enfeebled 2, you can make it stupefied 2 for the same duration.
