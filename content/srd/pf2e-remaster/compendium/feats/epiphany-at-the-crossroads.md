# Epiphany at the Crossroads  *Feat 12*  [ ](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Free Action")

`Divine`  `Oracle`

- **Frequency**: once per day
- **Trigger**: Your turn begins.
- **Requirements**: You are unconscious. and have the dying condition.

The crossroads between life and death is a place that can reveal many secrets. You gain the effects of an _augury_ spell in a strange, near-death vision. Empowered by the realization, you then lose the dying condition (becoming wounded 1 or increasing your wounded value by 1, as normal), regain a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level, and can Stand.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 142*
