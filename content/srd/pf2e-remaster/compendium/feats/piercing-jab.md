# Piercing Jab  *Feat 14*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Eidolon`  `Evolution`  `Summoner`

- **Requirements**: Your eidolon’s primary attack deals piercing damage.

Your eidolon runs an enemy through with a horn or similar attack. Your eidolon makes a melee Strike with their primary attack. If the Strike hits and deals damage, the target is grabbed by your eidolon until it successfully Escapes, your eidolon attacks with their primary attack again, or your eidolon is no longer manifested, whichever comes first. When the target is no longer grabbed, it takes persistent bleed damage equal to the primary attack’s number of weapon damage dice.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 84*
