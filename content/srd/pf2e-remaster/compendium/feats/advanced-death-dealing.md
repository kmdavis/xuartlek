# Advanced Death Dealing  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Necromancer
- **Prerequisites**: Basic Death Dealing

You gain one necromancer feat. For the purpose of meeting its prerequisites, your necromancer level is equal to half your character level.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once. Each time you select it, you gain another necromancer feat.
