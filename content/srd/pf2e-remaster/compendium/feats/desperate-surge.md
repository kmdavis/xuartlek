# Desperate Surge  *Feat 10*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Necromancer`  `Occult`

- **Frequency**: once per 10 minutes

You can conjure up at will the kind of strength that most beings only attain in life-or-death situations. You can attempt to Grab, Reposition, Shove, or Trip a creature using a spell attack roll instead of an Athletics check. 

You can recharge this ability early by making an unarmed Strike to destroy one of your thralls within your reach.
