# Wind Seeker  *Feat 8*

`Animist`

- **Prerequisites**: Walk the Wilds

Wings free you from the shackles of the ground below. You add _aerial form_ to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting. Whenever you use _aerial form_ to gain a form that grants you a specific Acrobatics modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to Acrobatics checks.

**Special** If you are attuned to an apparition of darkened boughs, add the bat and bird forms in aerial form to your _darkened forest form_ lists.

*Source: War of Immortals p. 25*
