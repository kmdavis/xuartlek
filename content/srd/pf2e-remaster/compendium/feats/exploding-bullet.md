# Exploding Bullet  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Arcane`  `Magus`

- **Prerequisites**: volatile spark hybrid study
- **Requirements**: You’re wielding an unloaded firearm.

You load your gun with a bullet forged of magic instead of matter, barely contained in an arcane shell. Interact to reload your firearm, creating an arcane round made from your choice of fire or sonic energy. When you shoot this round, the Strike gains the arcane trait and the corresponding energy trait. The firearm gains the scatter 5 feet trait, or gains a +1 status bonus to its splash damage if it already has the scatter trait. The scatter trait’s splash damage uses the energy type you chose. The bullet disappears if not fired by the end of your next turn.

If your firearm takes more than 1 action to reload, you need to use Exploding Bullet for all the actions needed to reload it to get the benefits.

**Special** When you use volatile spark’s Reloading Cascade action, you can use Exploding Bullet instead of an Interact action to reload a firearm.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 19*
