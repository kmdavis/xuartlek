# Sound Mirror  *Feat 6*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Alter Ego
- **Prerequisites**: Alter Ego Dedication

You twist not just the visual, but the auditory as well, allowing you to pass without a sound and create minor noises as distractions—perfect for misdirection and infiltration. You can cast silence on yourself as a 2nd- rank innate occult spell and _ventriloquism_ as a 1st-rank innate occult spell, each once per day. You also can cast _figment_ as an innate occult cantrip. You become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics, and your key spellcasting attribute for alter ego archetype spells is Charisma.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 127*
