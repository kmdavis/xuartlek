# Poison Drinker  *Feat 8*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Healing`  `Necromancer`  `Occult`  `Poison`

- **Trigger**: You critically succeed on a Fortitude save against a poison effect.

You temporarily transform your digestive system to instead gain a burst of sustenance from the poison you ingest. You regain Hit Points equal to the level of the poison (or double the spell’s rank if the poison was caused by a spell), and you gain as much nourishment as one square meal.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 37*
