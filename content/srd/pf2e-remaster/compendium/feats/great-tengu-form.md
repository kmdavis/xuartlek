# Great Tengu Form  *Feat 17*

`Tengu`

- **Prerequisites**: Long-Nosed Form

You take on the imposing form of a large, winged spirit. Once per day, as part of using Long-Nosed Form, you also gain the benefits of 4th-rank _enlarge_ and fly. This lasts for 5 minutes or until you shift out of your Long-Nosed Form, whichever happens first.
