# Alchemical Assessment  *Feat 1*

`Alchemist`

- **Prerequisites**: Trained in Crafting

You can identify alchemical items quickly. When using the Crafting skill to Identify Alchemy on an alchemical item you hold, you can do so as a single action, rather than spending 10 minutes.
