# Interstitial Escape  *Feat 10*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`  `Teleportation`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication
- **Trigger**: You would be hit by a melee or ranged Strike, and you are not off-guard.

To evade your foe, you shift yourself to another location, teleporting from your current square to another unoccupied square within 30 feet. Additionally, you gain resistance to all damage against the triggering attack equal to 5 + your level.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 105*
