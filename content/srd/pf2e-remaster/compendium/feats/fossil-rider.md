# Fossil Rider  *Feat 17*

`Lizardfolk`

- **Prerequisites**: Bone Magic

Using your ancestors' bones, you manifest their spirits around you in a fossilized form. You can cast _mask of terror_ on yourself as an innate spell once per day. When you do, you appear as a skeletal lizardfolk ancestor, twisted in a way that inspires the deepest fears of those who observe you.

*Source: Player Core 2 p. 27*
