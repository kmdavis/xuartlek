# Contingency Leap  *Feat 4*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Archetype`  `Concentrate`  `Magical`  `Teleportation`

- **Archetype**: Veil Dancer
- **Prerequisites**: Veil Dancer Dedication
- **Trigger**: You fall, and there is an edge or handhold within 30 feet.

When imperiled by a fall, you swiftly transport yourself out of danger. You teleport to any edge or handhold in range, stopping your fall. If your destination is one you couldn’t normally stand on (such as a handhold), you must succeed at your choice of an Acrobatics check or a Reflex save, usually at the Climb DC, as Grab an Edge, but you take no damage unless you critically fail.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 104*
