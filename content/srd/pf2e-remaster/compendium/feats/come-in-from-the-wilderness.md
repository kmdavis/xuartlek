# Come in From the Wilderness  *Feat 10*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`  `Earth`  `Healing`  `Primal`

- **Archetype**: World Rouser
- **Prerequisites**: World Rouser Dedication
- **Requirements**: Your waking world is active.

A willing ally on the ground in your waking world is swaddled in earth. For each source of persistent damage it is taking, the creature can immediately attempt a flat check to recover, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance. While swallowed, the creature becomes paralyzed and cannot be targeted or affected by any new effects (ongoing effects, such as afflictions or persistent damage, continue to affect it as normal). At the start of your next turn, the creature regains 6d8 Hit Points and emerges in its previous location. If another creature is in that location, it is pushed to the nearest available space. The target creature then becomes temporarily immune to this ability until your next daily preparations.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 107*
