# Steal Time  *Feat 10*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Chronoskimmer
- **Frequency**: once per hour
- **Prerequisites**: Chronoskimmer Dedication

You reach into another creature's timeline and attempt to alter their flow of time. Select a creature within 30 feet. That creature attempts a Fortitude saving throw against your chronoskimmer DC. The creature takes the effects of _slow_ based on the result of its saving throw.

*Source: Dark Archives (Remastered) p. 186*
