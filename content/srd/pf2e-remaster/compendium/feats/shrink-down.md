# Shrink Down  *Feat 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Eidolon`  `Evolution`  `Summoner`

Your eidolon can shrink to fit into small spaces. Your eidolon reduces to a smaller size of your choosing, to a minimum of size Small. They can Dismiss this action to return to their normal size. Their reach becomes 5 feet, or 10 feet if shrinking to Large with Towering Size.
