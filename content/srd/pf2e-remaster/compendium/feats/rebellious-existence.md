# Rebellious Existence  *Feat 18*

`Mythic`

- **Archetype**: Broken Chain
- **Prerequisites**: Broken Chain Dedication

You have tied yourself so absolutely to the cause of liberty that your very existence is an act of rebellion. You ignore any mental effects without the fear trait that would rob you of your agency (for example, on a failed save against _charm_ you would still be able to take hostile actions, and against _laughing fit_ you would still be able to take reactions but would remain slowed, but on a critical failure against fear you would still be fleeing because it’s a fear effect). Additionally, you are immune to effects that would imprison your soul (such as _seize soul_, or a final blade).

*Source: War of Immortals p. 125*
