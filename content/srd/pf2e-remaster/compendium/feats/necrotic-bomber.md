# Necrotic Bomber  *Feat 4*

`Archetype`

- **Archetype**: Necromancer
- **Prerequisites**: Necromancer Dedication

You gain the _necrotic bomb_ grave spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. You can Refocus by spending 10 minutes doing the necromancer's Refocus options.
