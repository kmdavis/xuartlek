# Dimensional Drop  *Spell 2*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Extradimensional`  `Manipulate`  `Teleportation`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature standing on the ground
- **Defense**: Reflex

You place a portal on the ground directly beneath a creature. That target immediately plummets into extradimensional space, falling a distance through an infinite, featureless void before being forcibly ejected from a portal that appears right above their original space and taking the appropriate fall damage. The target must attempt a Reflex saving throw. The DC to Arrest a Fall is equal to your spell DC.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target falls 10 feet through the void before reappearing.

**Failure** The target falls 20 feet through the void before reappearing.

**Critical Failure** The target falls 40 feet through the void before reappearing.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 5 creatures within range.
