# Murder of Crows  *Spell 2*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: AC

You send out a flock of crows that torments the target with beak and talon. Make a ranged spell attack against a target’s AC. On a hit, the target takes 6d4 piercing damage and is dazzled for 1 round (double damage and blinded for 1 round on a critical hit).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 3d4.
