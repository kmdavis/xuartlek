# Unsealing of Secrets  *Ritual 4*

`Rare`  `Secret`

- **Cast**: 1 day
- **Cost**: fine inks, pens, and papers worth 150 gp
- **Secondary Casters**: 1
- **Primary Check**: Occultism (master)
- **Secondary Checks**: Occultism, or Society
- **Target(s)**: 1 blank sheet of parchment

You place the writing instruments on the parchment while you call upon a spirit to answer your questions. The called spirit is from the area that you are in, has been dead for at least 100 years, and writes in the language that they spoke in life. They have access to all secrets ever spoken aloud or written down in their language within 5 miles of the location where you perform the ritual. This knowledge is fleeting, and the spirit has limited ability to write.

 When you cast this ritual, you can ask a question of the spirit. It answers to the best of its ability, with the GM determining if a secret was ever spoken aloud or written down.

**Critical Success** The spirit answers your question with 25 words or fewer.

**Success** The spirit answers your question with 10 words or fewer.

**Failure** The spirit’s writing is garbled and unintelligible.

**Critical Failure** The spirit’s answer is 10 words or fewer and contains false information.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 199*
