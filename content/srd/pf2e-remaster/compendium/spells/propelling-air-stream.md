# Propelling Air Stream  *Spell 1*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Air`  `Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

You shoot out a high-speed blast of air that propels you into the air. Make a ranged spell attack against a target’s AC. On a success, the target takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage (or double damage on a critical hit). Regardless of whether you hit, you gain a fly Speed of 15 feet until the end of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
