# Circle of Weakness  *Focus 1*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Wizard`

- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 15-foot burst
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

Winning magical combat often requires exploiting weakness. You evoke a circle of floating inscriptions that creates and amplifies energy-based weaknesses on creatures inside the circle. Choose one type of damage from the following list: acid, cold, electricity, fire, force, or sonic. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you Sustain it each turn, each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature gains weakness 1 to the chosen damage type for 1 round.

**Failure** As success, except the weakness is doubled.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature becomes immobilized for 1 round or until they Escape.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The weakness gained on a success increases by 1.
