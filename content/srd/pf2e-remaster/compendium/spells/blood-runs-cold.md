# Blood Runs Cold  *Spell 2*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cold`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies

You chill the blood in the target's veins. The target takes 1d6 persistent cold damage with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half the persistent cold damage.

**Failure** The target takes the full persistent cold damage and is slowed 1 for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double the persistent cold damage and is slowed 1 until the persistent cold damage ends.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the persistent cold damage by 1d6.
