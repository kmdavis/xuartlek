# Artistic Recollection  *Cantrip 1*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Cantrip`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Range**: touch
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You touch your finger to a blank surface and create an image of a person, place, or object. This image appears instantly in the style of a pastel-hued oil painting and disappears cleanly once the spell ends. The portrait is the size of one average sketchbook page and must represent a subject the caster has seen in person or has studied extensively.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 123*
