# Chosen Gravity  *Spell 3*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute

The target can change the direction gravity pulls them after they touch a surface with their feet. The target can walk on vertical surfaces or upside down as though gravity was pulling them toward the surface. In addition, the target gains a +1 status bonus to their saving throws and Fortitude DC against forced movement. When the spell ends, the target is affected by gravity as normal and might fall if not on the floor.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 1 hour.
