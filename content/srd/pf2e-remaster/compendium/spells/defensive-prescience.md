# Defensive Prescience  *Spell 3*  [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction")

`Concentrate`  `Force`  `Misfortune`

- **Traditions**: Divine, Occult
- **Trigger**: A creature targets your familiar with an attack.
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: the triggering creature
- **Defense**: Will

Your eyes widen as you watch the attack, slowing it down in your mind as you look at the possibilities that lie ahead. Reaching out, you twist the strands of fate, creating a glitch in this instance to protect your vulnerable familiar. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature must roll twice and use the worse result on its attack roll; if its attack is a critical failure, the target takes 3d10 force damage.

**Critical Failure** The creature must roll twice and use the worse result on its attack roll; if its attack is a failure, the target takes 3d10 force damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
