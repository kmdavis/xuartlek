# Incarnate Deific Herald  *Spell 8*  [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Incarnate`  `Manipulate`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Requirements**: You have a deity.
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn

Your faith is strong enough to briefly summon a direct representative of your deity, such as the deity’s herald. The herald occupies the space of a Huge creature and has a Speed of 60 feet. The effects of this spell depend on the divine sanctification options of your deity. If your deity lists “must choose,” the herald is that sanctification (either holy or unholy). If your deity lists “none,” the herald is unsanctified. If your deity lists “can choose,” you can choose either unsanctified or whatever options are listed for your deity (holy or unholy) for the herald.

- **Holy** **Arrive** (holy) _Holy Smite_ The herald casts a 5th-rank __divine wrath__ that has a 100-foot emanation instead of the usual range and area; **Depart** _Merciful Healing_ The herald casts the 3-action version of a 5th-rank __heal__ with a radius of 100 feet. This spell targets only you and your allies.
- **Unholy** _Infinite Misery_ The herald casts __wave of despair__ in a 100-foot cone; **Depart** (darkness, spirit, unholy) _Midnight Wrath_ Each enemy in a 100-foot emanation takes 5d10 spirit damage with a basic Fortitude save. In addition, all non-magical light within the area is extinguished, and the herald attempts to counteract all magical light (counteract modifier +25, counteract rank 8).
- **Unsanctified** **Arrive** _Quickening Ripple_ You and each ally within 100 feet of the herald are quickened while the herald is present and can use the additional action to Step, Stride, or Strike; **Depart** (spirit) _Sapping Beam_ The herald drains the animating force of all enemies in a 100-foot line, dealing 6d10 spirit damage with a basic Fortitude save. A creature who fails the save is also enfeebled 2 for 1 minute (enfeebled 3 on a critical failure).
