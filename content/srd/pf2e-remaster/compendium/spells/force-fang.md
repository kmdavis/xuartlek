# Force Fang  *Focus 1*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Focus`  `Force`  `Magus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: varies
- **Target**: 1 creature

You briefly transform your attack into pure force energy to bypass your opponent’s defenses. You magically shift your weapon or unarmed attack into a spike of pure force that deals 1d4+1 force damage to a target within your melee reach. If you can use Spellstrike with a ranged weapon (with the starlit span hybrid study, for example), you can instead target a creature in the first range increment of your ranged weapon.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d4+1.
