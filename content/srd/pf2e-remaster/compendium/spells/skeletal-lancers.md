# Skeletal Lancers  *Focus 7*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Thrall`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure a force of skeletons wielding bony spears. You create up to 5 thralls in different spaces within range. When an enemy makes a Strike while within 10 feet of a skeletal lancer, the lancer deals 5 piercing damage to it. The enemy takes this damage only once per Strike, even if it’s in the reach of multiple skeletal lancers. The skeletal lancers have a Speed of 30 feet. Their Strikes have a reach of 10 feet and deal piercing damage. When you command a skeletal lancer to Stride, you can have any number of your skeletal lancers Stride instead of just one. When you use _thrall charge_ to order a skeletal lancer to make a Strike, that Strike deals an additional 2d6 damage.

---
**Heightened (8th)** The additional damage increases to 3d6.
