# Deity's Strike  *Spell 7*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Force`  `Manipulate`  `Sanctified`  `Spirit`

- **Tradition**: Divine
- **Requirements**: You have a deity.
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature (see text)
- **Defense**: AC

A manifested version of your deity's favored weapon made of pure magical might appears in the air above the target. This weapon attacks using your spell attack modifier. On a hit, the weapon deals 7d12 force damage, or double damage on a critical hit. 

After the attack, whether it succeeds or fails, divine energy bursts from the weapon in a shock wave. It emits in a 10-foot-wide, 30-foot-long line starting from the target and continuing in a direction you choose. Your target and any other creatures within the area of the spell take 5d6 spirit damage, with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The force damage increases by 1d12, and the spirit damage increases by 1d6.
