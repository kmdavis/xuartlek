# Stone Lance  *Focus 3*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Druid`  `Earth`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure a jagged lance of stone and then launch it at a foe. Make a spell attack roll against the target. On a hit, you deal 6d6 piercing damage and the lance impales the creature, giving it a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds unless it Escapes. On a critical hit, if the creature is on the ground, the lance also embeds into the ground and immobilizes the creature until it Escapes. A creature that Escapes after being impaled takes 3 persistent bleed damage. When the spell ends, the lance crumbles into dirt, freeing the target if it hasn't Escaped.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6 and the persistent bleed damage for Escapes by 1.
