# Element Embodied  *Spell 10*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`

- **Tradition**: Primal
- **Duration**: 1 minute

Drawing upon the elemental plane, you transform into a spectacular elemental. You can choose between an air elemental, earth elemental, fire elemental, metal elemental, water elemental, or wood elemental. Your battle form is Gargantuan, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the elemental trait and the trait for the element you choose. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose: 
AC = 25 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.30 temporary Hit Points.Darkvision.One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +34, and your damage bonus is +15. These attacks are Dexterity based if you choose an air, fire, or metal elemental, or Strength based if you choose an earth, water, or wood elemental. (This distinction doesn't change the statistics but matters for the enfeebled and clumsy conditions, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.Acrobatics modifier of +36 for air, fire, or metal elemental, or Athletics modifier of +36 for earth, water, or wood elemental, unless your own modifier is higher.You also gain specific abilities based on the type of elemental you choose:

- **Air Elemental** Speed fly 80 feet; **High Winds** (air, aura) 30 feet. Air within the emanation is difficult terrain for flying creatures that don’t have the air trait; **Swiftness** Your movement doesn’t trigger reactions; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") gust (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning plus Push 10 feet; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") lightning lash (range increment 80 feet), **Damage** 3d10 electricity.
- **Earth Elemental** Speed 30 feet, burrow 20 feet; resistance 5 to physical; **Spike Stones** (aura, earth) 30 feet. The area is difficult terrain and hazardous terrain. A creature without the earth trait that moves on the ground in the area takes 5 piercing damage for every square of that area it moves into; **Rocky Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") fist (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") rock (range increment 40 feet), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
- **Fire Elemental** Speed 50 feet; immunity to fire; weakness 10 to cold; **Intense Heat** (aura, fire) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 5d6 fire damage with a basic Reflex save against your spell DC. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") tendril (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d10 fire plus 2d6 persistent fire; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") fire mote (range increment 60 feet), **Damage** 3d8 fire.
- **Metal Elemental** Speed 40 feet, fly 40 feet; resistance 10 to electricity; **Arcing Electricity** (aura, electricity, metal) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 2d12 electricity damage with a basic Reflex save against your spell DC; a creature made of metal, with the metal trait, or wearing metal armor takes a –1 circumstance penalty on this save. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") blade (reach 25 feet, versatile S), **Damage** 3d12 piercing; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") metal shard (range increment 40 feet, versatile S), **Damage** 3d8 piercing.
- **Water Elemental** Speed 40 feet, swim 80 feet; resistance 10 to fire; **Vortex** (aura, water) 30 feet. Water within the aura that is part of the same body of water you occupy is difficult terrain for Swimming creatures that don’t have the water trait; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") wave (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning plus Push or Pull 10 feet; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") water spout (range increment 60 feet), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
- **Wood Elemental** Speed 30 feet, climb 35 feet; resistance 5 to physical; weakness 10 to axes; **Lush Growth** (aura, wood) 30 feet. Ground in the area is difficult terrain, and any time a creature in the area regains HP from a healing vitality effect, the aura grants a +5 status bonus to the healing; **Woody Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") branch (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d10 bludgeoning; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") seed (range increment 40 feet), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
