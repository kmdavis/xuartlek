# Draw Blood  *Spell 2*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Attack`  `Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Vitality`  `Positive`

- **Traditions**: Occult, Primal
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: 1 living creature
- **Defense**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 minute

The palm of your hand grows a mouth with impossibly long fangs that ache to drink your enemies' blood. Make a melee spell attack against a target. On a hit, you deal 4d4 piercing damage and 2 persistent bleed damage (or double damage on a critical hit). For the duration of the spell, you regain 2 Hit Points each time the target takes bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage and healing increase by 1.
