# Phantasmal Minion  *Spell 1*  [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Summon`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Deities**: Anubis, Good Neighbors, Kols, Monad, Yelayne, Yluma
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Duration**: sustained

You summon a phantasmal minion. The minion is roughly the shape of a humanoid. You can choose to have it be invisible or have an ephemeral appearance, but it's obviously a magical effect, not a real creature.
