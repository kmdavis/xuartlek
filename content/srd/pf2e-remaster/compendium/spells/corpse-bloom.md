# Corpse Bloom  *Spell 3*  [>>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Three-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Poison`

- **Tradition**: Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: three 5-foot bursts that don’t overlap
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure three deadly clusters of flesh-devouring fungi. Whenever a creature starts its turn in one of the clusters or the first time a creature enters a cluster each round, it takes 3d4 poison damage with a Fortitude save as poisonous spores fill the air. Corpses within a cluster rapidly decompose.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half damage and becomes sickened 1.

**Failure** The target takes full damage and becomes sickened 1.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and becomes sickened 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
