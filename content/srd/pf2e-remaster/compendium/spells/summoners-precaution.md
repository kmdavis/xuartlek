# Summoner's Precaution  *Spell 2*

`Concentrate`  `Contingency`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes
- **Requirements**: You have an eidolon.
- **Duration**: until your next daily preparations

Conjuring an intricate weave of protective magic, you create a buffer in the link between yourself and your eidolon, allowing your bonded ally to sacrifice their existence to save you in a time of need. You gain the following reaction; after using it, the spell ends.
**Sever Conduit** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") (concentrate) **Trigger** Your eidolon takes damage that would bring you to 0 Hit Points and comes from an effect other than a death effect; **Effect** You quickly extinguish the link with your eidolon, causing your bonded ally to wink out of existence before you can be laid low. Your eidolon unmanifests, and you can’t Manifest your Eidolon for 1 minute. In exchange, you don’t take the triggering damage, though your eidolon still suffers any other adverse effects that accompanied the damage.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 165*
