# Hot Air  *Focus 4*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Fire`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Uncommon`  `Wizard`

- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute

Your enemies are full of hot air, so you make their lungs burn when they try to talk. The target takes 4d6 fire damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a failed save, the target also burns whenever they speak, causing them to take 4 fire damage whenever they use an action with the auditory trait or Cast a Spell, unless their spell is subtle.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial fire damage increases by 1d6, and the fire damage the target takes whenever they take an action with the auditory trait or Cast a Spell increases by 1.
