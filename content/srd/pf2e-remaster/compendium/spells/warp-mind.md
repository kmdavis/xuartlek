# Warp Mind  *Spell 7*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Emotion`  `Incapacitation`  `Manipulate`  `Mental`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Occult
- **Bloodlines**: Aberrant, Hag
- **Deities**: Conqueror Worm, Isph-Aun-Vuln, Laivatiniel, Nethys, Nyarlathotep (The Faceless Sphinx), Nyuo-Ogh, Xhamen-Dor, Zeaki
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Target**: 1 creature
- **Defense**: Will

You scramble a creature's mental faculties and sensory input. The target must attempt a Will saving throw. Regardless of the result of that save, the target is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes. _Warp mind's_ effects happen instantly, so _dispel magic_ and other effects that counteract spells can't counteract them. However, _wish_ rituals and abilities that can remove non-magical effects can still counteract the effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target spends the first action on its next turn with the confused condition.

**Failure** The target is confused for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is confused permanently.

*Source: Player Core p. 368*
