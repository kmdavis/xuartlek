# Uncover Device  *Spell 2*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Electricity`  `Manipulate`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Primal
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Target**: 1 metal trap or device
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You focus on the construction of the object, channeling electricity through it that allows you to get a sense of its shape and construction. You gain a +2 status bonus to Thievery checks to Disable Device on the target and to Aid checks to help an ally Disable Devise on the target.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 168*
