# Living Graveyard  *Focus 10*  [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action")

`Concentrate`  `Focus`  `Manipulate`  `Necromancer`  `Thrall`  `Uncommon`

- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You conjure forth a walking bulk of graves and tombstones held together by the buried dead. You create a Gargantuan thrall within range. When the _living graveyard_ appears, it violently shakes. All creatures in a 10-foot emanation must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or fall prone. Additionally, five thralls (using the base thrall statistics) fall from their graves into empty spaces adjacent to the _living graveyard_. This thrall has 400 Hit Points and a Speed of 60 feet. Whenever this thrall Strides, it creates 5 thralls in empty spaces along the path it Strides. The living graveyard’s Strikes have a reach of 15 feet and deal bludgeoning damage. When you use _thrall charge_ to order the living graveyard to Strike, the Strike deals an additional 3d6 damage.

*Source: Impossible Magic p. 183*
