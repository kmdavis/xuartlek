# Demon Form  *Spell 6*  [>>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Two-Action")

`Concentrate`  `Manipulate`  `Polymorph`  `Unholy`

- **Traditions**: Arcane, Divine
- **Duration**: 1 minute

You corrupt yourself with the sin of the Outer Rifts, transforming into a Medium demon battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose abrikandilu, brimorak, kithangian, or vloriak. If you choose kithangian, the battle form is Large, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the demon, fiend, and unholy traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use manipulate actions. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose: 
AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.30 temporary Hit Points, weakness 5 to cold iron, and weakness 5 to holy.Darkvision.One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +22, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.Athletics modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.You also gain specific abilities based on the type of demon you choose:

- **Abrikandilu** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") jaws (magical, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 2d6 piercing, and clumsy 1 for 1 minute on a critical hit; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") claw (agile, magical, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 2d6 slashing; **Ranged** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") hurled debris (range increment 20 feet, unholy), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning.
- **Brimorak** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") flaming sword (magical, unholy), **Damage** 2d8 slashing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") hoof (agile, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 2d4 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire.
- **Kithangian** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") pincer (magical, reach 10 feet, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 1d12 slashing, and after a successful Strike you can spend an action to attempt to Grab the target; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") stinger (agile, magical, reach 10 feet, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 1d8 piercing; all melee Strikes deal an additional 2d6 precision damage to animals.
- **Vloriak** Speed 25 feet; resistance 10 to acid; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") claw (unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 2d6 piercing plus 1d6 spirit; **Melee** [>](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Single Action") tongue (agile, reach 10 feet, unarmed, unholy), **Damage** 1d6 acid plus 1d6 spirit.
