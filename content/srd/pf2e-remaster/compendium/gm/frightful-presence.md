# Frightful Presence  *Creature Ability*

(aura, emotion, fear, mental) A creature that first enters the area must attempt a Will save. Regardless of the result of the saving throw, the creature is temporarily immune to this monster's Frightful Presence for 1 minute.

 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected by the presence. **Success** The creature is frightened 1. **Failure** The creature is frightened 2. **Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 4.

*Source: Monster Core p. 359*
