# Cowards's Roots  *Curse 5*

`Curse`  `Emotion`  `Fear`  `Magical`  `Mental`

You find all courage stolen from your heart. When faced with something frightening, you flee in terror or stand frozen in place.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Will

**Effect**
At the start of your turn, if you are frightened, you become your choice of immobilized or fleeing until the end of that turn.
