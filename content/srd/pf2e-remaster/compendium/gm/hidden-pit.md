# Hidden Pit  *Hazard 0*

`Mechanical`  `Trap`

- **Complexity**: Simple A wooden trapdoor covers a pit that’s 10 feet square and 20 feet deep.

**Stealth**
DC 18 (or 0 if the trapdoor is disabled or broken)

**Disable**
DC 12 Thievery to remove the trapdoor

**AC** 10, **Fort** +1, **Ref** +1

**Trapdoor Hardness**
3

**Trapdoor HP**
12 (BT 6)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Pitfall** [<](rules/player-core/chapter-8-playing-the-game/actions#Actions "Reaction") **Trigger** A creature walks onto the trapdoor; **Effect** The triggering creature falls in and takes falling damage (typically 10 bludgeoning damage). That creature can use the Grab an Edge reaction to avoid falling.

---
**Reset**
Creatures can still fall into the trap, but the trapdoor must be reset manually for the trap to become hidden again.
