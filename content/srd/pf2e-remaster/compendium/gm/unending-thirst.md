# Unending Thirst  *Curse 15*

`Curse`  `Magical`

This curse kills through dehydration in the same vein as the curse of the ravenous, but with deadly speed

**Saving Throw**
DC 32 Fortitude

**Effect**
You gain no benefit from drinking water or other liquids and begin suffering from thirst. Each day, you receive a new saving throw against the curse.

*Source: GM Core p. 87*
