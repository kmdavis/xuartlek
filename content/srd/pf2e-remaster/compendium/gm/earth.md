# Earth  *Creature Adjustment*

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of earth, you can use the following adjustments. 
Add the earth trait.Add tremorsense 60 feet (imprecise).If it has any languages, add Petran.Add a burrow Speed of 20 feet, or 40 feet if the creature is 8th level or higher.If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with earth spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** _scatter scree_, **1st** _pummeling rubble_, **2nd** _exploding earth_, **3rd** _one with stone_, **4th** _mountain resilience_, **5th** _wall of stone_, **6th** _petrify_, **7th** _heaving earth_, **8th** _earthquake_.

*Source: Rage of Elements p. 91*
