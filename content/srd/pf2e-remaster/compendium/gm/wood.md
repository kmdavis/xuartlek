# Wood  *Creature Adjustment*

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of wood, you can use the following adjustments. 
Add the wood trait. If the creature is a growing plant, you can also add the plant trait.If it has any languages, add Muan.Increase the creature's HP based on its level.Add weaknesses to fire and to damage from axes depending on its level.If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with wood spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** _timber_, **1st** _protector tree_, **2nd** _oaken resilience_, **3rd** _wall of thorns_, **4th** _life-draining roots_, **5th** _nature's pathway_, **6th** _lignify_, **7th** _pollen pods_, **8th** _burning blossoms_, **9th** _one with the land_.
