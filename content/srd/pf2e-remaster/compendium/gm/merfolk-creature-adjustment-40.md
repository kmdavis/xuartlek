# Merfolk  *Creature Adjustment*

Merfolk have humanlike torsos, fish tails, and fins. 
Replace the human trait with the merfolk trait and add the amphibious trait.Add the Thalassic language.Change Speed to 5 feet with a 25-foot swim Speed. You can add a supramarine chair item to give the merfolk a land Speed equal to their swim Speed. Use the rules for a wheelchair.Add the following abilities. **Low-Light Vision**
