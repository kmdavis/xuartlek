# Spirit Anchor  *Curse 13*

`Curse`  `Magical`

This curse prevents your soul from moving on after death.

**Saving Throw**
DC 31 Fortitude

**Effect**
If you die while affected, your spirit is anchored to the plane you currently inhabit, and you become a ghost or other incorporeal undead.
