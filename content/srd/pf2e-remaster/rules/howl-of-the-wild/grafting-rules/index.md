# Grafting Rules

<sup>HotW p. 97</sup>

Grafts are living tissues that are implanted into a host, where they integrate with the hosts’ native biology. To implant a graft, you must be an expert in Medicine and have the appropriate formula. Graft formulas can be purchased as outlined in the [[srd/pf2e-remaster/rules/player-core/chapter-6-equipment/gear#Formulas|formulas rules]].

You can take the Graft Technician feat to implant grafts.
