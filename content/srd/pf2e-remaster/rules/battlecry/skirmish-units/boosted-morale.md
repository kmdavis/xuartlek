# Boosted Morale

<sup>BC p. 160</sup>

A leader’s presence inures their troop against terror and retreat. Troops in a skirmish encounter have a chance to become afraid or run away, as described under [[srd/pf2e-remaster/rules/battlecry/skirmish-units/troops-in-skirmishes#Routing Troops|Routing Troops]]. When a troop with a leader attempts a rout roll, it gets a result one degree of success better than it rolled.
