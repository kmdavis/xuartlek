# Magus

<sup>NPC p. 220</sup>

When giving an NPC this list, give them two spell slots for the highest two spell ranks available for their level, and none for any lower spell ranks. They should also have better combat stats and combat-themed actions than a typical spellcaster, such as the watchmage's Spellbound Strike.
