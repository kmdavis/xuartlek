The thirty-three events the source marked `requiredRole: 'gamemaster'`, in
context. Public events are shown greyed for sequence; the **bold** rows are the
secret ones. See [[timeline|History of Xuartlek]] for the player-facing timeline.

## The three that matter

Everything else here is texture. These three are plot:

| Year | Secret |
|---:|---|
| 285 | Niflheim portal "lost to shear" was **secretly converted to Black Market #1** |
| 355 | Niflheim portal "lost to shear" was **secretly converted to Black Market #2** |
| 390 | Niflheim portal "lost to shear" was **secretly converted to Black Market #3** |

The pattern is the point. Every Niflheim portal the Under City is allocated is
publicly recorded as lost to shear effect. Three of the five were not lost at
all. Two genuinely were, in 315 and 450, which is what makes the cover story
hold: the failures are real, so the fakes are unremarkable.

A sixth Niflheim portal was allocated in 485 and is **currently active**. Whether
that one is a black market yet is the open question, and the obvious hook.
