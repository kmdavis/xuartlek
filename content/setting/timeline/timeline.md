# History of Xuartlek

Dates are years of the **Xuartlek Imperial Calendar**, the reckoning the empire
keeps and the only calendar the vault tracks. Year 0 is the year the Skyy Tortles
declared a new era, not the year Xuartlek was born. Three events below are
recorded in local reckoning instead, and both dates are given.

**The present is 500-01-01, 1 Lowsun 500.** Xuartlek has just arrived above
Myrrhina. Sulveig, the twenty-fourth district, is founded this year, but its
primary portal will not complete until **505**, so for now the only way down to
Myrrhina is by ship.

Everything dated 505 and later is scheduled rather than done.

The clock is pinned: it advances when play advances, not with real time. See
[[Xuartlek Imperial Calendar]].

The empire stands at [[The High City|twenty-three districts across twelve worlds]]
because Toril was lost. Twenty-four districts have been founded, but Duathamper
fell in 386 and its world left the atlas with it.

## The shape of it

Xuartlek moves. He arrives at a world, stays **thirty years**, and departs; the
crossing to the next world takes **five**. That cadence has held without
exception for fourteen eras, and it is why the calendar and the atlas can name
an era and a world interchangeably.

Each stay follows the same programme:

1. **Arrive and found a settlement**, in the world's first year.
2. **Complete its primary portal** about five years later, binding the new
   district to one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]].
3. **Link it sideways** with a secondary portal to an existing district.
4. **Sometimes open an elemental portal**, extending the Altstadt ring.

### The rim follows a spiral

Primary portals are assigned to the four periphery districts in strict rotation,
north to east to south to west, then round again:

| Rim harbour | Compass | House |
|---|:-:|---|
| Sjeverna Luka | N | Árpád |
| Østre Havn | E | Rantzau |
| Docaichean a Deas | S | Maolmhuaidh |
| Quais Ouest | W | Beauharnais |

Twenty-four primary portals have been assigned this way without a single break in
the sequence, from Renestrae in 100 to Sulveig in 505. A GM can therefore work out
which House receives the next district before anyone announces it.

## Where the party stands

At year 500 the empire has founded twenty-four districts and holds twenty-three,
across twelve reachable worlds. Myrrhina is the frontier: a district founded
this year, no working portal for another five, and nothing mapped beyond the
landing site. Anything a crew is sent to do down there is the first time anyone
has done it, and they can only get there by ship.

## Toril is gone

Toril has no entry in the atlas. It is the only era world that does not, and the
reason is on this page: Duathamper's portal was destroyed in 384 and the
settlement fell in 386, severing the empire's one connection to that world.
Thirteen eras have produced twelve reachable worlds.

## Known inconsistency

The atlas lore says Xuartlek stays **twenty-five** years at each world. This
timeline says **thirty**, and does so consistently across all fourteen eras, so
the atlas is the file that is wrong. Fix it there rather than here.

## What is not on this page

Thirty-three events are Game Master material and are held in a separate,
unpublished note. Three of them are load-bearing setting secrets rather than
colour. If you are running the game, read that note too.
