## Mind Essence (The Far Realms)

Eight complete multiverses, each with their own cosmology--their own material plane, outer planes, inner planes, elemental planes, pantheons, and trillions of souls. From their perspective, **we are a Far Realm to them**.

The collective unconscious of these multiverses bleeds through into our reality, manifesting as the Ancients and the structure of the Outer Realms. See [[Mind Essence|Mind Essence details]].

### How Far Realms Influence Outer Realms

Influence flows proportionally based on alignment proximity. Each Outer Realm receives pressure from the Far Realms whose alignment axes match:

- **Pure alignment** (e.g., Altruvis/NG): 50% Aurelios + 50% Puritas (the Good pair)
- **Adjacent alignment** (e.g., Seraphis/LG): Split between Good and Law pairs--37.5% each from Aurelios/Puritas, 12.5% each from Justicaris/Dictatum
- **Corner alignment** (e.g., Harmonia/LN-LG): Blended influence from multiple Far Realm pairs
- **True Neutral** (Equilibris): Receives diluted, balanced influence from all eight Far Realms

This creates a gradient where each Outer Realm "blends" the psychic pressure of multiple Far Realm multiverses. The Ancients embody that specific mixture--Harmonius isn't just "unity" but the particular flavor of unity that emerges from the Good/Law influence blend.

## The Material Plane

A blank canvas until the cosmic war between Spirit and Matter. Fire ruptures became stars; Earth and Metal formed planets; Water and Air surrounded these worlds. Mortal life arose from this elemental fusion.

### Xuartlek: The Wandering City

Xuartlek is a mobile city-ship, only a few centuries old. It originated from Tortleheim in the Chelon system and has since journeyed from star to star, leaving colonies behind at each stop. Modern colonies maintain portal connections back to Xuartlek; the earliest settlements predate portal technology and have lost direct contact.

Xuartlek himself was named after the star Xuar--not the other way around.
