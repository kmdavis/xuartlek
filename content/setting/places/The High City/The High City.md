# The High City

The capital of an empire spanning a dozen worlds, built on the shell of a living
demi-god who walks between them. Roughly a million souls on the shell itself, and
about as many again scattered across the Low City.

The city is in four parts and they are too tightly coupled to describe separately,
so they are all here. Who holds what is in [[The High City Factions]]; how the state works is in
[[Government of Xuartlek]].

| Part | Districts | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The High City** | 17 | On Xuartlek's carapace |
| **The Under City** | 4 | Suspended beneath his belly |
| **The Estates** | 6 | On the shells of six Lesser Skyy Tortles who travel with him |
| **The Low City** | 23 | Across twelve worlds, through the portals |
| | **50** | one Delegate each |

## The shell

Xuartlek's shell is about twenty-five miles long. Each natural scute is a district,
seventeen in all, and the ground is not entirely stable: the city dreams, street
patterns shift, passages open between districts that were not there last year. This
is one of two reasons no boundary in the city has ever been surveyed. The other is
political and is covered in [[Government of Xuartlek]].

![[The High City districts.png]]

### Social geography

The seventeen fall into three bands, and the band tells you more about a district
than its House does.

**Four periphery districts**, the rim harbours. Airship docks, shipyards,
warehouses. Every Low City portal lands in one of these. Loud, rich in traffic, poor
in residents.

**Three interior districts** along Xuartlek's spine. The wealthy and the powerful,
government buildings, upscale markets, parks, lakes.

**Ten middle districts** in a ring between the two. Apartment blocks, markets,
factories, and most of the ordinary business of a million people.

## The Under City

Four districts suspended beneath Xuartlek's belly, counted separately from the
seventeen, and held entirely by House Haritza. They form a contiguous ring with the
Inner Docks at the centre, and four hanging bridges reach up to the four rim
harbours. The bridges are tolled.

| District | Language | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Lapurrak** | Basque | Thieves |
| **Hiltzaileak** | Basque | Murderers |
| **Gezurtiak** | Basque | Liars |
| **Iruzurrak** | Basque | Cheats |

Whether the Under City deserves representation is a live and openly hostile debate,
which is awkward, because it has four Delegates and has had for a very long time.

Lapurrak holds the empire's only acknowledged research portal to Niflheim. The
[[Government of Xuartlek|Assay]] has believed for a century that it is not the only working one
down there and has never been able to prove it.

The Authority appoints an Administrator to each of the four districts. All four
posts are explicit sinecures and do nothing whatsoever. Somebody's nephew holds
them.

## The Estates

Six Lesser Skyy Tortles travel with Xuartlek, each carrying an estate on its shell.
They are his first disciples, partially ascended as he is, and roughly an order of
magnitude smaller: about two and a half miles of shell each, where a merely ancient
Skyy Tortle runs to a mile.

**Holding one is the only qualification for being a Great House.** There are six and
there will only ever be six.

| Estate | House |
|---|---|
| **Anwesen** | Von Schoeler |
| **Maison** | Beauharnais |
| **Ejendom** | Rantzau |
| **Itthon** | Árpád |
| **Dhachaigh** | Maolmhuaidh |
| **Hasiera** | Haritza |

Each returns a Delegate, drawn exclusively from the ruling House. Reached by airship
from the rim harbours, and from the Under Docks in Haritza's case. Their
Administrators are sinecures too.

## The Low City

Twenty-three districts across twelve worlds, reached by [[Portal Frame|portal]]. The
older worlds carry as many as three; the newest carry one. Administered by the
Authority's Low City department through named Administrators, each with a Guard, a
Services and an Infrastructure deputy.

**The Low City now returns more Delegates than the High City and Under City
combined**, 23 against 21. Only the six Estate seats keep the core territories in
front, 27 to 23.

empire to 50 districts.

### The rings

Each district connects to the High City through a rim harbour, and also to two other
Low City districts, forming two interlocking rings that bypass the shell entirely.
There are twenty-two such secondary links. The Authority tolls them and likes them;
the Houses' harbours see none of that traffic and hate them.

**Duathamper on Toril was the twenty-fourth.** Its portal was destroyed in 384 by
its own side to stop an invasion reaching the High City, and the district fell two
years later. Its slot in the southern harbour has never been refilled.

## Portals

See [[Portal Frame]] for how they physically work, and for the toll booths at their
feet that pay for the entire state.

The High City also holds portals to other planes: the six elemental doors ringing
Altstadt, two industrial connections to Pyraxa and Ferrumara from the Deep Forge in
Fearann Gnìomhachais, the Academy's research portal to Alfheim, and Lapurrak's to
Niflheim.

## Imperial Time

When Xuartlek visits a world, its trade synchronises to Imperial Time for the thirty
years he stays, and the synchronisation persists after he goes, maintained by the
merchant airships. That is why the Xuartlek Imperial Calendar became the standard
across twelve worlds that share nothing else.
