## History

**Xuartlek's Visit:**
After departing Tortleheim, Xuartlek traveled directly here--to his birth star, the one he was named for. The journey took exactly 5 years. Curiosity, perhaps, to see what kind of world orbited the distant point of light that had given him half his name. He remained for exactly 25 years--as he does at every star--before continuing to Vortalis. What he found here, and why he never speaks of it, remains one of the great mysteries of his long existence.

**Current Status:**
Kaanos is a "lost world" with no active portal connections. Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited, and portals weren't invented until he reached Arborisle--long after Xuar had been left behind. The system is lost not by choice but by the nature of Xuartlek's eternal journey.
