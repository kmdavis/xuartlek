## Description

Xuartlek's birth star--the actual star he was named for, visible from Tortleheim. His first destination after leaving his homeworld, chosen out of curiosity to see the star that had been overhead at his birth. A lost system now, disconnected from the portal network for reasons unknown.

## Naming Conventions

* **Xuar**: The system's star--Xuartlek's birth star, visible from Tortleheim
* **Xuartlek**: "Xuar Tlek" -- birth star + given name, per Tortlian tradition

## Full Description

The Xuar System is home to the star Xuar--the same star that was overhead when Xuartlek was born on Tortleheim, thousands of light-years away. In Tortlian tradition, a tortle's name begins with their birth star, so he was always "Xuar Tlek." When he departed Tortleheim after his ascension, he chose to visit his birth star first--out of curiosity, perhaps, to see the distant point of light that had given him half his name.

What did he find here? What happened during his time at his birth star that made him classify the system as "lost" afterward? These questions remain unanswered. Xuartlek discourages questions about his time here.

Like Chelon, Xuar is classified as a "lost system" with no portal connections. Xuartlek spent 25 years here before departing for Vortalis, and he never returns to a star he has previously visited. Portals weren't invented until he reached Arborisle, long after Xuar had been left behind forever.
