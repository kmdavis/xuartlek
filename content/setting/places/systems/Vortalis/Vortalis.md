## Description

A lost system named for its turbulent star--the second stop on Xuartlek's journey from Tortleheim (after Xuar). Tragically, this is where portal research began--but the breakthrough came too late. Xuartlek departed before the technology was ready, and he never returns to a star he has previously visited.

## Naming Conventions

* **Vortalis**: Latin-inspired, meaning "of the vortex"--suggests unusual stellar dynamics or metaphorical significance

## Full Description

The Vortalis System holds a tragic place in Xuartlek's history. It was here, on Furitas, that High City scientists first conceived of stable portal technology. The research began during Xuartlek's 25 years in the system--but the breakthrough didn't come until after he had already departed for Arborisle.

By the time the first stable portal opened at Arborisle, Vortalis was already lost. Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited. The scientists who invented portal technology could never use it to reconnect their birthplace to the network they created.

The name suggests a star with unusual properties--perhaps a variable star, one with visible accretion disk, or one caught in some cosmic phenomenon. Whether the "vortex" is literal or metaphorical remains unknown.

Like Chelon and Xuar before it, Vortalis is classified as a "lost system" with no active portal connections--lost not by choice, but by the immutable nature of Xuartlek's eternal journey.
