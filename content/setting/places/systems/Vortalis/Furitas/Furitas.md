## Description

The birthplace of portal technology--and a tragic loss. High City scientists began their research here during Xuartlek's 25-year stay, but the breakthrough came too late. By the time stable portals opened at Arborisle, Furitas was already lost forever.

## Full Description

[Detailed world description yet to be developed]

Furitas was Xuartlek's second stop after Tortleheim, following his 25 years at his birth star Xuar. Unlike the earlier lost worlds, we know exactly what happened here: this is where portal technology was born.

High City scientists began researching stable portal connections during Xuartlek's 25 years in the Vortalis system. The work was promising--tantalizingly close to success. But Xuartlek's schedule cannot be altered. After exactly 25 years, he departed for Arborisle. The 5-year voyage continued the research, and the breakthrough came shortly after arrival.

But by then, Vortalis was already lost. Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited. The scientists who invented portal technology could never use it to reconnect their birthplace to the network they created. The irony haunts High City to this day.

## History

**Xuartlek's Visit:**
The second stop on Xuartlek's journey from Tortleheim, after visiting his birth star Xuar. He remained for exactly 25 years--during which time High City scientists began the research that would eventually create the portal network. But "eventually" came too late for Furitas.

**The Birth of Portal Technology:**
It was here that the idea of stable portals first took shape. Scientists observed something in the Vortalis system--perhaps properties of the star itself, perhaps something on Furitas--that suggested portals might be possible. The research began in earnest. For 25 years they worked, and when Xuartlek departed, they were so close...

**Current Status:**
Furitas is a "lost world" with no active portal connections--the cruelest irony in High City's history. The world where portals were conceived can never be connected to the network those portals created. Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited, and that rule admits no exceptions.
