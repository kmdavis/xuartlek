Ilythyrra is a Xuartlek Low City District on Arborisle.

Founded in **140**, its primary portal opens onto **Docaichean a Deas**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Arborisle]], in the [[Sylvoria]] system.
