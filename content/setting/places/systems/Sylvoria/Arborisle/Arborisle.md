## Description

In a world where the forest has won--not through conquest but through patient growth over uncounted millennia--trees measure their lives in thousands of years, and the boundaries between material and elemental have grown so thin that walking deep enough into certain groves means walking into eternity itself.

Celtic/Gaelic influences throughout, with elvish adaptations:
* **Sylvoria**: The star (ancient elvish = "realm of the woods")
* **Arborisle**: The world (Latin arbor + isle, "tree island")
* **Lughna**: Inner scorched world (Celtic Lugh = god of skill and craft)

* **The First Tree**: Mythological progenitor of all forests
* **Ancient Tongue**: Celtic/Gaelic-inspired ritual language spoken by wood elves

## Full Description

Arborisle is a world where the forest has won. Not through conquest, but through patient growth over uncounted millennia. Trees here measure their lives in thousands of years, their roots delving deep into rich soil, their canopies forming layers upon layers of green cathedral spaces where sunlight filters down in shafts of gold and emerald. The world sits at the outer edge of its star's habitable zone, producing cool temperatures and perpetual mists that hang over the ancient forests like nature's own blessing.

The world is dominated by wood elves, who have tended these forests since before recorded history, but it is far from a monoculture. High elves maintain gleaming towers at the forest's edge, their crystalline spires rising above the canopy like needles of captured starlight. Halfling communities nestle in the roots of the greatest trees, their homes carved into the wood with such care that the trees continue growing around them. Leonin prides patrol the rare savanna clearings, while beastkin and awakened animals move freely through glades where the distinction between civilized and wild has blurred into irrelevance.

Druidism permeates everything. The wood elves speak an ancient tongue in their rituals, its syllables older than most kingdoms, and even those who follow other paths learn to read the language of root and branch, to understand the slow conversations of the oldest groves. Magic here is not scholarly abstraction but lived practice, woven into every aspect of daily life.

The forests themselves are thin places where the boundaries between realms grow permeable. Arborisle holds more portals to Verdara, the Elemental Plane of Wood, than any other known world--a natural consequence of having the oldest and most pristine forests in the Material Plane. Walk deep enough into certain groves and the trees grow stranger, more perfect, more eternal. These are the threshold forests where druids go to commune with the primordial essence of growth itself. Most worlds have a handful of such connections scattered in isolated locations. Arborisle has dozens of Verdara portals alone, each one marking a place where the forest has achieved such perfection that reality itself cannot tell the difference between material and elemental.

## Physical Characteristics

**Orbital Period:** ~372 Arborian days (~372 Earth days)
**Rotation Period:** Standard day cycle
**Diameter:** 12,800 km (Earth-sized, slightly larger)
**Gravity:** Standard
**Atmosphere:** Breathable, rich in oxygen from millennia of forest growth
**Eccentricity:** 0.017 (Earth-like, stable orbit)

**Climate:**
* **Cool Temperate**: Outer edge of habitable zone creates cooler global temperatures
* **Perpetual Mists**: Morning fog common across forested regions
* **Emerald Light**: Sunlight filters through endless canopy in green-gold shafts
* **Mild Seasons**: Low orbital eccentricity produces stable, gentle seasonal variations
* **High Humidity**: Ancient forests create their own weather patterns

**Orbital Position:**
Fourth world from Sylvoria, positioned at the outer edge of the habitable zone. Inner neighbor Tiroedd is warmer and mysteriously uninhabited; outer asteroid belt marks the boundary between inner and outer system.

## Geography

**Primary Continent: Eldoria**

The dominant landmass, characterized by endless forests that have grown undisturbed for millennia. Multiple distinct forest regions exist, each with unique character:

**The Great Canopy:**
* Primary forested region covering central Eldoria
* Trees thousands of years old, multiple canopy layers
* Cathedral-like spaces beneath where light filters through
* Home to most wood elf communities
* Contains the highest concentration of Verdara portals

**The Threshold Forests:**
* Deep groves where material and elemental blur
* Trees grow stranger, more perfect, more eternal
* Druid pilgrimage sites for communion with primordial growth
* Reality becomes unstable at edges--step wrong and you may step into Verdara
* Some groves have been continuously tended for 10,000+ years

**The Crystal Heights:**
* Forest edge regions where high elf towers rise above canopy
* Gleaming crystalline spires catching Sylvoria's light
* More urban, scholarly atmosphere contrasting with wood elf ways
* Trade hubs connecting forest interior with coastal regions
* Architecture uses crystal and living wood in impossible combinations

**The Root Warrens:**
* Underground and tree-root networks
* Halfling communities carved into living wood
* Homes shaped so carefully trees continue growing around them
* Extensive tunnel systems connecting major trees
* Some warrens are as old as the trees themselves

**The Savanna Clearings:**
* Rare grassland regions where forest has receded
* Leonin pride territories
* Mystery why forest doesn't reclaim these spaces
* Ancient ruins sometimes visible at clearing edges
* Important for ritual gatherings requiring open sky

**The Ancient Ruins:**
* Scattered throughout deep forests
* Pre-elven civilizations, purposes unknown
* Power still palpable despite abandonment
* Some integrated into current druidic practices
* Origins lost even to the oldest wood elves

## Astronomical Features

**Two Moons:**

**Boskara (The Living Moon):**
* 28-day orbital cycle
* Eerie green glow visible from surface
* Glow comes from thick atmosphere and spore-clouds
* Central to druidic calendar and ritual timing
* Waxes and wanes through phases tracked for plantings
* Visible "transformation" continuing over centuries

**The Myth of Boskara:**
The druids say ancient elves seeded Boskara in the age before the sundering of wood and high elf cultures, planting a garden-moon that would one day rival Arborisle itself. Generations of patient tending have made Boskara's transformation visible even from the surface--surely it must be nearly ready, a second paradise waiting to welcome the faithful.

**Aeloria (The Patient):**
* 47-day orbital cycle
* Grey and lifeless, barren rock
* Named "The Patient" for watching Boskara's transformation
* Some traditions see Aeloria as the "wise sister" waiting for Boskara to mature
* Less important to druidic practice, but tracked for astronomical precision

**Celestial Symbolism:**
* Boskara represents growth, transformation, potential
* Aeloria represents patience, witness, acceptance
* The two moons together symbolize the druidic path: active cultivation and patient observation
* Major rituals timed to when both moons are visible together

**The Outer System:**
* **Dagdarion**: Amber-colored gas giant visible as a bright "star," symbolizing the Father's ancient patience
* **Morrígan**: Ice giant at system's edge, named for the goddess who sees all endings
* Prophecy claims when Boskara's transformation completes, Morrígan will wake from frozen sleep

## Culture

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
* **Elves - Wood Elf Heritage** (40%): Dominant culture, forest stewards since before recorded history. Speak the ancient tongue in rituals. Druidic tradition pervasive.
* **Elves - High Elf Heritage** (15%): Crystal tower dwellers at forest edges. More scholarly, maintain arcane traditions alongside druidic ones.
* **Halflings** (15%): Root-dwelling communities in living trees. Artisans who shape wood without killing it.
* **Leonin** (10%): Savanna clearing dwellers, pride-based social structure. Guardians of the rare open spaces.
* **Beastkin** (10%): Various animal-folk, distinction between civilized and wild intentionally blurred.
* **Awakened Animals** (5%): Fully sentient beasts, move freely, hold equal social standing in many communities.
* **Others** (5%): Firbolg giant-kin, Owlin, visitors from Xuartlek network

**Magic Level:** High
* Magic woven into daily life, not scholarly abstraction
* Druidic practice pervasive even among non-druids
* High magic in crystalline towers, primal magic in deep forests
* Most children learn basic cantrips as naturally as walking
* Ancient artifacts from pre-elven civilizations still functional

**Technology Level:** Ancient (Intentionally)
* Current civilization intentionally "rustic" by choice
* Pre-elven ruins show more advanced capabilities
* Living wood and crystal preferred over metal and stone
* Technology exists where needed, rejected where growth suffices
* Some high elf communities maintain scholarly artifice traditions

**Language:**
* **Ancient Tongue**: Celtic/Gaelic-inspired ritual language, syllables older than kingdoms
* Used in formal ceremonies, druidic rites, ancient treaties
* Even non-elves learn basic phrases for respect
* Some words have power when spoken in threshold forests

**Social Structure:**
* Wood elf groves: Consensus-based druidic councils
* High elf towers: Scholarly hierarchies, meritocratic
* Halfling warrens: Family-based cooperation
* Leonin clearings: Pride structure, matriarchal leadership
* Inter-species cooperation: Extensive, conflicts rare

**Religion:**
* The First Tree mythology pervades everything
* Druidic reverence for primordial growth
* Verdara (Plane of Wood) as spiritual ideal
* Some high elves maintain older arcane traditions
* Ancestor veneration in most cultures

**Elemental Plane Connections:**

Arborisle holds more natural portals to the Elemental Planes than any other known world:

* **Verdara (Wood)**: 30+ known portals in threshold forests
* **Zephyra (Air)**: Several in highest canopy peaks
* **Aquara (Water)**: Deep forest pools with unusual connections
* **Petraea (Earth)**: Cave systems beneath oldest groves
* Fewer connections to Pyraxa (Fire) and Ferrumara (Metal)--the forest rejects these elements

**The Threshold Experience:**
Walking into a Verdara portal zone is gradual. Trees become more symmetrical, colors more vivid, growth patterns more mathematically perfect. At some point--impossible to identify precisely--you've crossed. Most druids can navigate this transition safely. Others have been lost, absorbed into the eternal forest, becoming part of the primordial growth they sought to commune with.

**Theme:** Tolkien-esque High Fantasy with Strong Druidic Elements

## History

**The Age Before Memory:**

Ancient ruins scattered through the deep forests testify to civilizations that predate even the oldest elven records. These structures use construction techniques unknown to modern builders, and their purposes remain mysterious despite millennia of study. Power still flows through them--some have been integrated into current druidic practice, serving as amplification points for rituals even though no one remembers their original function.

The pre-elven builders left no readable records, no identifiable descendants, no explanation for their disappearance. Some druid-scholars theorize they became so attuned to the forest that they merged with it entirely, their consciousness distributed through the root networks of the oldest trees. Others believe they simply left, departing through the Verdara portals they may have created. The truth is lost.

**The First Tree Mythology:**

The foundation of Arborian spirituality. The First Tree is said to be the mythical progenitor of all forests, its roots reaching through the fabric of reality itself. Where its roots touch, life flourishes; where they do not, worlds remain barren and still. This explains the barren inner planets (too close to Sylvoria's heat for the roots to reach) and the mystery of Tiroedd (the First Tree withdrew its blessing, perhaps deliberately).

Whether the First Tree is literal, metaphorical, or something stranger--perhaps an aspect of Verdara itself--is debated by druids and scholars. What's undisputed is that Arborisle's forests are the oldest and most pristine in the known Material Plane, and the concentration of Verdara portals suggests something genuinely special about this world's connection to primordial growth.

**The Sundering of Wood and High:**

Elven oral tradition speaks of a time when wood elves and high elves were one people. The "Sundering" occurred so long ago that details are mythologized beyond historical recovery. Some versions claim high elves chose arcane magic over druidic practice; others suggest philosophical differences about intervention versus observation; still others point to simple cultural drift over millennia.

Modern wood elves and high elves cooperate extensively--the Sundering carries no animosity--but their approaches to magic, nature, and society remain distinct. Wood elves see themselves as stewards, tending the forest while changing as little as possible. High elves see themselves as cultivators, shaping the forest toward greater beauty through active intervention.

**The Boskara Seeding:**

Both traditions agree that ancient elves--before the Sundering--"seeded" Boskara with life. The moon's eerie green glow and visible transformation over centuries are cited as evidence. The project was intended to create a second paradise, a world-garden that would eventually rival Arborisle itself. Completion was expected "within generations"--a timeframe that has stretched into millennia without apparent concern. Patient tending continues.

**Xuartlek's Arrival:**

Arborisle holds the distinction of being the **first world permanently connected to Xuartlek's portal network**. When the ageless mage arrived, his people had not yet mastered permanent portal magic--only temporary, unstable connections. Something about Arborisle's unique concentration of planar connections made it the ideal laboratory.

Xuartlek spent a full generation (approximately 25 years) on Arborisle while his scholars collaborated with local high mages to perfect the art. The breakthrough came from studying the natural Verdara portals--understanding how reality became permeable where material and elemental aligned. Applying this principle artificially created the first stable gate.

**The Three Districts:**
* **Renestrae**: First established district, nearest to primary Verdara portal concentration
* **Ilythyrra**: Trade hub connecting forest interior with coastal regions
* **Parthenay**: Newest district, positioned for expansion into less-developed regions

**Trade:**
* **Exports**: Living wood crafts, druidic services, Verdara-touched materials, ancient knowledge, portal expertise
* **Imports**: Metal goods (rarely produced locally), exotic materials, news from the wider multiverse

**Cultural Impact:**
Xuartlek's arrival expanded Arborian awareness of the multiverse but changed little about daily life. The wood elves continued their patient stewardship; the high elves gained new scholarly subjects; the halflings found new customers for their crafts. Arborisle's self-sufficiency and cultural confidence meant external contact enhanced rather than disrupted existing patterns.
