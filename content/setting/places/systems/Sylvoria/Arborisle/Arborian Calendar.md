## Description

The Arborian calendar is a lunisolar system used on Arborisle in the Sylvoria System, structured around elven mysticism and the forest's seasonal cycles.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Arborisle]] in the Sylvoria System
- **Theme**: Elven forest culture, nature cycles, fey connections
- **Year Length**: 372 days (12 normal months + 4 intercalary days)
- **Day Length**: 24 hours (same as Earth)

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 9 days (sacred number in elven mysticism)
- **Months**: 12 normal months (alternating 31 and 30 days) = 368 days
- **Intercalary Days**: 4 (spring, summer, autumn, winter solstices/equinoxes)
- **Leap Years**: Every year except every 3rd year (Winter's Veil becomes 0 days instead of 1)

### Days of the Week (Elven/Common)
1. **Aelindor / First Light**: Dawn, beginnings, sun rising
2. **Sylvaris / Forest Day**: Woods, growth, druidic traditions
3. **Elenael / Star Blessed**: Starlight, night magic, celestial guidance
4. **Faelyn / Fae Touched**: Fey connections, wild magic, trickster energy
5. **Ithilnor / Moon Rise**: Lunar power, Boskara's influence, transformation (sacred day)
6. **Caelindra / Sky Song**: Wind, birds, upper canopy life
7. **Naerileth / Leaf Dance**: Celebration, joy, community gathering
8. **Thaelar / Root Deep**: Ancestors, ancient wisdom, deep magic
9. **Luminel / Twilight Hour**: Transition, mystery, threshold between worlds

### Months (Elven/Common)
**Winter:**
- **Istirlune / Frost Moon**: Deep winter, ice and snow (31 days)
- **Rhovanis / Dormant Earth**: Late winter, earth rests (30 days)
- **Aeloras / Snow Whisper**: Winter's end, first thaws (31 days)

*Aelindril / Spring Awakening: Spring equinox (1 day intercalary)*

**Spring:**
- **Elenril / Star Spring**: Early spring, first buds (31 days)
- **Caelindor / Sky Awakening**: Mid spring, birds return (30 days)
- **Lirael / Blossom Song**: Late spring, flowers bloom (31 days)

*Solastrae / Midsummer's Dream: Summer solstice (1 day intercalary)*

**Summer:**
- **Solarin / Sun Crown**: Peak summer, height of growth (31 days)
- **Faelora / Fae Bloom**: Late summer, fey activity peaks (30 days)
- **Nyraelle / Golden Leaf**: Early autumn, leaves turn (31 days)

*Nyralaeth / Harvest Blessing: Autumn equinox (1 day intercalary)*

**Autumn:**
- **Mystralath / Mist Veil**: Mid autumn, mists rise (31 days)
- **Thaelorin / Falling Leaves**: Late autumn, preparations (31 days)
- **Sylvanor / Forest Sleep**: Early winter, forest hibernates (31 days)

*Istirael / Winter's Veil: Winter solstice (0-1 day intercalary - leap day)*

## Astronomical Basis

### The Two Moons
- **Boskara**: 27-day cycle (the Living Moon - green, transforming)
- **Aeloria**: 45-day cycle (the Patient Watcher - grey, still)

The calendar keeps alignment with forest cycles through its leap day system, where Winter's Veil is skipped every third year.

## Seasons

Arborisle is a cool forested world at the outer edge of the habitable zone:
- **Winter** (Istirlune, Rhovanis, Aeloras): Perpetual mists
- **Spring** (Elenril, Caelindor, Lirael): Forest awakens
- **Summer** (Solarin, Faelora, Nyraelle): Green cathedral peaks
- **Autumn** (Mystralath, Thaelorin, Sylvanor): Harvest and rest

## Cultural Significance

The nine-day week reflects the sacred number in elven mysticism. The fifth day, Ithilnor (Moon Rise), is particularly sacred, associated with Boskara's transformative influence. The intercalary days mark the solstices and equinoxes, each celebrated with specific rituals tied to the forest's cycles.
