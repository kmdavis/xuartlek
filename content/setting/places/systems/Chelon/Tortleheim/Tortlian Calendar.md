The original calendar of the Skyy Tortles, created on their ancestral homeworld Tortleheim before the great exodus that founded the Xuartlek Empire.

## Overview

The Tortlian Calendar is the ancient timekeeping system developed by the Skyy Tortles (tortlefolk) on their homeworld of Tortleheim, which orbits the star Chelon. This calendar predates the Xuartlek Imperial Calendar and represents the original astronomical observations of tortle civilization.

## Calendar Structure

- **Year Length**: 364 days (13 months x 28), plus one leap day every third
  year, tracking Tortleheim's orbital period of about 364.33 days
- **Months**: 13 months of 28 days each (364 days)
- **Leap Day**: Added every 3 years to the month of Kraznak (Deepsnow)
- **Weeks**: 7-day weeks, 4 weeks per month
- **Day Length**: 24 hours (same as Earth)

## Historical Significance

When the Skyy Tortles fled Tortleheim during the great exodus and eventually founded the Xuartlek Empire, they brought this calendar with them. However, upon settling in Xuartlek--which happened to have a perfect 364-day orbital year--they modified the calendar by removing the leap days entirely.

This modification became symbolic: the removal of leap days represented the Skyy Tortles' acceptance that they could never return to their ancestral home and were starting anew. The Xuartlek Imperial Calendar is thus a deliberate simplification of this original system.

## Modern Usage

Today, the Tortlian Calendar is primarily used by:
- Scholars and historians studying Skyy Tortle civilization
- Traditional tortle families practicing cultural preservation
- Inhabitants of Tortleheim who never left the ancestral world

The month and day names used in both calendars share the same tortlian heritage, honoring the ancient roots of the tortle people.

## Cultural Notes

The tortlian language names (Kraznak, Zorvek, etc.) are still used in both calendars, maintaining linguistic continuity between the ancestral and imperial systems. Some traditional families observe both calendars--the Xuartlek calendar for official imperial business and the Tortlian calendar for religious and cultural ceremonies.
