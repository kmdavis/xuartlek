## Description

The original homeworld of the Skyy Tortles--a world of floating islands drifting through endless skies, where the most ancient of the species grow large enough to become living foundations for entire villages, and where a young tortle named Xuartlek first dreamed of transcending his kind's natural fate.

## Naming Conventions

Germanic and Nordic influences reflecting sky-dwelling culture:
* **Chelon**: The system's star (from Greek "chelone" = tortoise)
* **Tortleheim**: The world (Germanic = "home of the tortles")
* **Skyy Tortles**: The native species--tortles adapted to aerial life on floating islands
* **Tortlian Naming**: Birth star (visible overhead at birth, as determined by village astrologer) + given name
* **Xuartlek**: "Xuar Tlek" -- born under the distant star Xuar + given name "Tlek"
* Names often incorporate sky/cloud/wind elements
* Elder tortles receive honorific names describing their size or the communities they carry

## Full Description

Tortleheim defies conventional planetary structure. There is no visible surface--only an endless expanse of sky punctuated by thousands of floating islands drifting through cloud layers at various altitudes. The islands range from small rocks barely large enough for a single tree to massive landmasses supporting forests, lakes, and communities. What lies below the lowest clouds remains unknown; expeditions that descend too far never return.

The Skyy Tortles evolved in this vertical world, developing gliding membranes and lighter shells than their ground-dwelling cousins elsewhere in the multiverse. But their most remarkable adaptation is their endless growth. A Skyy Tortle never stops growing--from hatchling to elder, decade after decade, century after century. This biological quirk shaped their entire civilization.

Young tortles are active, curious, and quick-minded. They build, explore, trade between islands, and pursue knowledge. But as they grow larger, their minds slow. By forty, they're human-sized and beginning to feel the change. By a hundred, they're the size of houses, their thoughts measured in days rather than moments. By two hundred, they're living islands themselves--vast, ancient beings upon whose shells their descendants build homes, plant gardens, and raise families.

The eldest Skyy Tortles drift between the floating islands, carrying entire villages on their backs. Communication with them is measured in seasons--ask a question at spring planting, receive an answer at autumn harvest. They are not unconscious, merely operating on a timescale utterly alien to younger minds. Some say they commune with each other through subsonic vibrations that take years to complete a single exchange.

This was the world that produced Xuartlek--a young tortle who looked at his inevitable future and refused to accept it.

## Physical Characteristics

**Orbital Period:** Unknown (standard astronomical observation difficult due to permanent cloud cover)
**Rotation Period:** 26-hour day/night cycle
**Diameter:** Estimated Earth-sized, but surface area is effectively infinite due to vertical habitation
**Gravity:** Standard at mid-altitudes, lighter at higher elevations
**Atmosphere:** Breathable, thick and humid, multiple distinct cloud layers

**Climate:**
* Temperature varies by altitude--tropical at lower levels, temperate mid-range, cold at heights
* Constant winds between island clusters create predictable "sky currents" used for navigation
* Storms are three-dimensional events, moving through vertical as well as horizontal space
* "Sky seasons" determined by island drift patterns rather than solar position

## Geography

**The Upper Reaches:**
* Highest habitable altitude
* Smaller islands, colder temperatures, thinner air
* Home to reclusive scholars and monasteries
* Where Xuartlek conducted his final experiments before ascension

**The Middle Skies:**
* Most populated zone
* Largest floating islands with established cities
* Primary trade routes between island clusters
* Favorable conditions for agriculture and animal husbandry

**The Lower Depths:**
* Warmer, more humid, denser atmosphere
* Larger predatory creatures
* Resource-rich islands (minerals, rare plants)
* Younger tortles often prove themselves here
* Below a certain point, expeditions cease to return

**The Elder Paths:**
* Routes traveled by the most ancient tortles
* Their movements create reliable "mobile geography"
* Communities time major events by elder arrivals
* Some elders haven't completed a single circuit in living memory

**The Great Below:**
* Whatever lies beneath the lowest clouds
* Subject of myth, speculation, and fear
* Some believe a solid surface exists; others claim endless descent
* Xuartlek reportedly studied it before his ascension

## Astronomical Features

**Chelon (The System Star):**
* Yellow-white star similar to Sol
* Named after ancient word for tortoise
* The star Tortleheim orbits
* Considered sacred--tortles orient their shells toward it during meditation

**The Named Stars:**
* Hundreds of visible stars have Tortlian names
* Village astrologers determine which star is overhead at a tortle's birth
* This "birth star" becomes the first part of a tortle's name
* **Xuar** is one such named star--the one overhead when Xuartlek was born

**The Moons (Unknown):**
* Permanent cloud cover prevents reliable lunar observation from most altitudes
* Upper Reaches scholars report seeing "moving lights" above the clouds
* Whether these are moons, other celestial bodies, or something else remains debated

**The Sky Currents:**
* Predictable wind patterns that create invisible "roads" between island clusters
* Mapped over millennia by Skyy Tortle navigators
* Some currents only accessible at certain altitudes
* Ancient maps show currents that no longer exist--the sky changes over centuries

## Culture

**Species:**
* **Skyy Tortles** (95%): The native species, adapted for aerial life
* **Various** (5%): Traders and visitors from other worlds (post-Xuartlek contact)

**The Growth Cycle:**
* Youth (0-40 years): Active, quick-minded, building careers and relationships
* Maturity (40-100 years): Slowing but still capable, often becoming mentors and leaders
* Elder (100-200 years): House-sized, semi-mobile, communities begin building on their shells
* Ancient (200+ years): Island-sized, mobile only over years, living foundations for villages
* Primordial (500+ years): The largest known--mountains with memories, communicating in decades

**Social Structure:**
* Youth-dominated governance for day-to-day affairs
* Elders consulted for long-term decisions (results may take years)
* Lineage traced through which elder carries your family
* Changing elders is possible but socially significant

**Magic Level:** Moderate
* Magic traditionally uncommon--most tortles lack the mental speed for complex casting as they age
* Xuartlek's achievement of Archmage status was unprecedented in centuries
* Post-Xuartlek, some young tortles study magic hoping to follow his path
* None have succeeded

**Technology Level:** Renaissance (Wind-based)
* Sophisticated wind-harnessing technology for transportation
* Glider-craft ranging from personal to cargo-sized
* No ground-based infrastructure--everything designed for aerial/island life
* Rope bridges, platform elevators, and wind-lifts between islands

**Theme:** Vertical Society / Inevitable Transformation / Transcendence

## History

**The Endless Past:**
Skyy Tortle oral history stretches back further than most civilizations' written records--the Ancients remember events from thousands of years ago, even if extracting those memories takes decades. Their history is less a timeline and more a geological sediment of accumulated experience.

**The Age Before Xuartlek:**
Tortleheim existed in stable equilibrium for uncounted millennia. Young tortles lived, grew, slowed, and became foundations. No one questioned this cycle. Magic was rare--the mental speed required for spellcasting faded before most could achieve mastery. The few mages who existed specialized in slow, ritual magic compatible with elder cognition.

**Xuartlek's Rise:**
A young tortle born under the star Xuar showed unusual aptitude for magic. More remarkably, he showed unusual speed of mind that persisted past normal maturity. By thirty, he was already a master wizard. By thirty-five, he achieved Archmage status--the first in over three centuries. By forty, when his contemporaries were feeling the first slowness, Xuartlek's mind remained sharp.

He knew this couldn't last. He could feel the inevitable approaching, merely delayed. So he dedicated himself to a single goal: escaping his species' fate entirely.

**The Ascension:**
Decades of research followed. Xuartlek studied everything--divine magic, planar theory, the nature of consciousness, the properties of growth itself. He consulted Ancients whose answers took years to arrive. He descended into the Lower Depths seeking forgotten knowledge. He climbed to the Upper Reaches and beyond.

Then, approximately 3,000 years ago, he succeeded. Not in the way he intended. He ascended to demigodhood, his consciousness partially separating from his body. His physical form continued growing--exponentially now, far faster and larger than any natural Skyy Tortle. Within decades, he was larger than any Ancient. Within centuries, he was the size of a small city.

But his mind remained sharp. Partially.

**The Departure:**
Xuartlek's ascension solved his immediate problem but created new ones. He was neither fully tortle nor fully divine--something in between, incomplete. His body had become a floating island unto itself, and his mind, while preserved, was now spread across multiple states of being.

He departed Tortleheim after 25 years, seeking... something. The means to complete his transformation, perhaps. Or perhaps he simply outgrew his world. His first destination was the distant star Xuar--his birth star, the one he was named for. The journey took exactly 5 years, as all his interstellar voyages do, regardless of distance.

**The World He Left Behind:**
Tortleheim without Xuartlek continued much as before, but changed. Young tortles now dreamed of transcendence. Some studied magic hoping to replicate his feat. None succeeded. Others resented him for breaking the cycle--why should he escape what they must accept?

Today, Tortleheim is classified as a "lost world" in Xuartlek's network--not because contact has been lost, but because Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited. Whether this is a limitation of his nature or a choice he made, no one knows. What's certain is that he departed Chelon before portals were invented, and he will never return.
