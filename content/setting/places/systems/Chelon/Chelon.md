## Description

The home system of Xuartlek and the Skyy Tortle race--a lost system disconnected from the portal network, where the demigod began his eternal journey and to which he has never returned.

## Naming Conventions

* **Chelon**: From Greek "chelone" meaning tortoise--the system's star, which Tortleheim orbits

## Full Description

The Chelon System is where everything began. Its primary world, Tortleheim, gave birth to the Skyy Tortles and eventually to Xuartlek himself--the demigod whose city now spans worlds across the Material Plane.

Today, Chelon is classified as a "lost system" in Xuartlek's network. No portals connect to it--portals weren't invented until Xuartlek reached Arborisle, long after he'd departed Chelon. And Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited.

The system's location is technically known--it was Xuartlek's starting point, after all--but without portal access, travel there would require conventional spelljamming across vast interstellar distances.

## Worlds

* **Tortleheim**: A world of floating islands and endless skies, home to the Skyy Tortles--a species that never stops growing
