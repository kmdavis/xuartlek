## Description

A Germanic agricultural calendar where each month tracks Grimnir's orbit, and the year ends with an 11-day intercalary period when the moon seems to "rest" between cycles--a time outside of time for reflection and renewal.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Langsevain]] in the Calderon System
- **Theme**: Germanic fantasy, agricultural cycles, K-type star
- **Year Length**: 403 days (14 months + Grimnirsruh)
- **Day Length**: 25 hours

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 7 days (4 weeks per month)
- **Months**: 14 months of 28 days each = 392 days
- **Intercalary Period**: Grimnirsruh (11-12 days)
- **Lunar Alignment**: 1 month = 1 Grimnir orbital cycle (28 days rounded from 26.9)
- **Leap Years**: Every 14 years, adds 1 day to Grimnirsruh

### Days of the Week (Germanic)
1. **Sonntag**: Sun-day (first day, sun worship)
2. **Mondtag**: Moon-day (Grimnir's day)
3. **Tyrstag**: Tyr's day (war and justice god)
4. **Wodenstag**: Woden's day (wisdom god, Odin equivalent)
5. **Donnerstag**: Thunder-day (Thor equivalent)
6. **Freyatag**: Freya's day (love and fertility goddess)
7. **Samstag**: Saturday (traditional Germanic)

### Months (Seasonal Agricultural Calendar)
**Winter (Months 1-4):**
- **Nebelmond**: Fog-month (year begins in mists)
- **Frostmond**: Frost-month (deep cold sets in)
- **Eismond**: Ice-month (peak winter)
- **Schneemond**: Snow-month (heavy snows)

**Spring (Months 5-6):**
- **Taumond**: Thaw-month (ice breaks)
- **Saatmond**: Seed-month (planting begins)

**Early Summer (Months 7-8):**
- **Blütenmond**: Blossom-month (flowers bloom)
- **Wachsmond**: Growth-month (crops grow tall)

**Late Summer (Months 9-10):**
- **Reifemond**: Ripening-month (crops mature)
- **Erntemond**: Harvest-month (main harvest)

**Autumn (Months 11-14):**
- **Weinmond**: Wine-month (fermentation season)
- **Schlachtmond**: Slaughter-month (animal culling for winter)
- **Sturmmond**: Storm-month (autumn gales)
- **Dunkelmond**: Dark-month (shortest days)

**Intercalary:**
- **Grimnirsruh**: Grimnir's Rest (11-12 days between years)

## Astronomical Basis

### Grimnir the Moon
Grimnir's orbital period is 28 Earth days (approximately 26.9 Langsevain days). Ancient astronomers rounded this to 28 local days, creating perfect alignment: one month equals one complete Grimnir cycle.

### K-Type Star Effects
Calderon is a K-type star (cooler and redder than Sol), creating:
- Gentler, longer seasons
- Extended twilight periods
- More stable climate variations

## Grimnirsruh

### The Time Between
Grimnirsruh ("Grimnir's Rest") is an 11-day intercalary period when the moon is said to "lock" between years. It falls outside the normal month structure--a time outside of time.

### Cultural Practices
During Grimnirsruh:
- Debts are settled and forgiven
- Old feuds are meant to end
- Travel is discouraged (the gods rest)
- Families gather for extended celebrations
- The dead are especially honored
- Marriages and major decisions are postponed

### Leap Year Extension
In leap years (every 14th year), Grimnirsruh extends to 12 days, allowing the calendar to correct drift.

## Seasons

K-type star habitable zones have gentler, longer seasons than Sol-type systems:
- **Winter**: Months 1-4 (long, dark, but mild by Hrimgard standards)
- **Spring**: Months 5-6 (short but intense renewal)
- **Early Summer**: Months 7-8 (growth and warmth)
- **Late Summer**: Months 9-10 (ripening and harvest)
- **Autumn**: Months 11-14 (extended preparation for winter)
- **Grimnirsruh**: The liminal time (between years)

## Cultural Significance

The calendar reflects Langsevain's agricultural rhythms. Month names directly describe the activities of each period, creating a practical guide to the year's work. The long winter (4 months) and extended autumn (4 months) reflect the reality of life under a cooler K-type star.
