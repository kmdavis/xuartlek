Vezgaxur is a Xuartlek Low City District on Langsevain, the automaton quarter where self-aware machines debate philosophy in steam-heated halls.

Founded in **240**, its primary portal opens onto **Docaichean a Deas**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Langsevain]], in the [[Calderon]] system.
