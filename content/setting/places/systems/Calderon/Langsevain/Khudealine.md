Khudealine is a Xuartlek Low City District on Langsevain, where precision clockwork meets dwarven craftsmanship in the heart of the manufacturing quarter.

Founded in **220**, its primary portal opens onto **Østre Havn**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Langsevain]], in the [[Calderon]] system.
