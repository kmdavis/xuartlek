In a world where coal smoke mingles with divine obsession, an entire civilization points telescopes at a moon that hides perfection behind clouds--waiting for the moment, once a generation, when Grimnir's workshop reveals itself and engineers weep at the beauty they cannot yet comprehend.

Germanic influences with industrial and astronomical themes:
* **Calderon**: The star (Spanish "cauldron"--reflects forge/industrial theme)
* **Langsevain**: The world (Germanic compound: lang = "long" + sevain derived from "sehen" = "to see," meaning "Long-Seeing" or "Far-Sight"--reflects telescope obsession)
* **Grimnir**: The tidally-locked moon (Old Norse "The Masked One"--one of Odin's names, fitting for what hides behind clouds)
* **Ferraine**: Outer iron-rich world (French fer = "iron" + terrain)
* **Eisenherz**: Asteroid belt (German = "Iron Heart"--mythological remnants of failed forge-world)
* **Nordhaven**: Northern continent (German = "North Harbor")
* **Mittengürtel**: Equatorial continent (German = "Middle Belt")
* **Südreich**: Southern continent (German = "Southern Realm")
* **Grimnirsruh**: Intercalary period (German = "Grimnir's Rest"--11-day period when moon "locks" at year threshold)
* **Mount Schau**: Royal Observatory location (German schau = "look/watch")
* **Khudealine/Vezgaxur**: Xuartlek district names (Tortlian standard naming)

Beneath Grimnir's unchanging gaze lies Langsevain, a world where coal smoke mingles with morning fog and the rhythmic pulse of steam engines marks the heartbeat of civilization. Here, the Industrial Revolution didn't just arrive--it became a way of life, a religion, an obsession.

The world is dominated by vast urban sprawls connected by networks of railways that spider across continents. Dwarven engineers and gnomish artificers work alongside human industrialists and the warforged--mechanical beings granted sentience through arcane-industrial fusion. The Langsevan Lock Calendar measures time by Grimnir's monthly cycles, and during Grimnirsruh--the 11-day intercalary period when the moon seems to "lock" at the threshold between years--all major engineering projects pause for planning and reflection.

Two Low City districts connect Langsevain to Xuartlek's vast network: Khudealine, where precision clockwork meets dwarven craftsmanship, and Vezgaxur, the automaton quarter where self-aware machines debate philosophy in steam-heated halls. These portals brought new materials and techniques, but also validation--proof that Grimnir's divine engineering extended beyond one world.

The Locked Mystery dominates cultural life. Every child learns to use a telescope before learning to read, and the Royal Observatory maintains the finest optical instruments in the known worlds, forever pushing the boundaries of what can be seen through Grimnir's shifting clouds. Once every generation, when the clouds part, the world holds its breath, and thousands of telescopes turn skyward in unison. These brief glimpses fuel another generation of innovation, another cycle of the eternal quest to see what the gods have hidden.

**Orbital Period:** 403 Langsevan days of 25 hours each (approximately 420 Earth days)
**Rotation Period:** 25 hours per day
**Diameter:** 11,800 km
**Gravity:** Standard
**Atmosphere:** Breathable, but heavily polluted in industrial zones with coal smoke and manufacturing emissions

**Orbit Characteristics:**
* Near-circular orbit (eccentricity 0.028)
* Well within habitable zone of K-type star Calderon
* Orbital stability allowed complex multicellular life to evolve over billions of years

**Climate:**
* Temperate overall, with pronounced seasons
* Four-month winter, two-month spring, four-month summer, two-month autumn
* K-type star creates gentler, longer seasons than Sol-like systems

**Three Major Continents:**

**Northern Continent (Nordhaven):**
* Heavily industrialized, dominated by dwarven city-states
* Coal-rich mountains providing fuel for endless factories
* Vast factory complexes stretching for kilometers
* The Royal Observatory sits atop Mount Schau (literally "Mount Look")
* Most polluted region due to concentration of heavy industry

**Equatorial Continent (Mittengürtel):**
* Agricultural heartland feeding the industrial populations
* Gnomish innovation centers focusing on experimental technology
* Railway hub connecting northern and southern continents
* Less polluted, more verdant landscape
* Balance of farming and precision manufacturing

**Southern Continent (Südreich):**
* Newer industrial development, human-majority population
* Resource extraction focus: mining, logging, oil drilling
* Automaton populations highest here due to harsh working conditions
* Rapidly developing infrastructure
* Frontier spirit with less guild control than northern cities

**Oceans:**
* Extensively mapped and traveled
* Steamship lanes connect all continents on precise schedules
* Deep-sea mining operations recently begun (valuable minerals for optical glass production and specialty alloys)

**Grimnir (The Tidally-Locked Moon):**
* **Orbital Period:** 28 days (one Langsevan month--rounded from actual 26.9 days by ancient astronomers)
* **Diameter:** 3,100 km
* **Distance:** Appears roughly twice the size of Earth's moon from Langsevain's surface
* **Tidal Locking:** Same face always visible due to gravitational forces over millions of years
* **Orbit:** Perfectly circular (eccentricity 0.0)--requirement for stable tidal locking

**The Polar Crater (The Eternal Workshop):**
* 500+ km diameter, several kilometers deep
* Formed by ancient impact event
* SO deep that despite moon's low gravity, it retains thick atmosphere--extremely rare geological phenomenon
* Atmosphere composition: Primarily water vapor and carbon dioxide
* Dense cloud cover shrouds interior permanently
* Geometric patterns visible across moon's surface (natural crystalline formations and impact ejecta rays)
* Patterns aligned almost perfectly with planetary north--coincidental alignment that seems "designed"

**The Locked Mystery:**
* Clouds part briefly once every 25-30 years (tied to long-period atmospheric cycles in crater)
* Last cloud-parting: 1603 LC (21 years ago in current campaign year 1624 LC)
* Next expected: 1628-1633 LC (4-9 years away)
* During cloud-parting events: Glimpses of lights, structures, movement reported by telescope-masters
* These rare sightings treated as divine revelations
* Engineers who witness cloud-parting events celebrated as blessed
* Entire civilization pauses when it happens--all telescopes turn skyward simultaneously

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
* **Dwarves** (30%): Primary engineers, predominantly forge dwarves and rock dwarves. Masters of heavy industry and mining. Control most major manufacturing districts. Clan structure persists but subordinated to guild hierarchies.
* **Gnomes** (25%): Inventors and precision engineers. Mostly umbral gnomes (from deep underground workshops) and wellspring gnomes (magical tinkers). Dominate experimental design. Known for "inspired madness" producing breakthrough inventions and occasional spectacular failures.
* **Humans** (20%): Managerial and merchant class. Less specialized than dwarves/gnomes but more flexible. Control railway networks and banking systems. Germanic cultural influences dominate.
* **Automaton** (15%): Warforged equivalent--sentient constructs created through fusion of arcane magic and industrial processes. Full citizens with legal rights (hard-won through Automaton Citizenship Acts of 1580s LC). Many work in extreme environments where organic workers cannot survive.
* **Half-orcs** (5%): Primarily work in heavy industry, docks, and coal mines. Face social discrimination but valued for strength and endurance. Growing civil rights movement gaining traction.
* **Others** (5%): Small populations of half-elves (diplomats, artists), halflings (railway conductors, hospitality workers), and a few android refugees from Lumiere system who fled anti-magic Emerraine seeking asylum.

**Society & Guilds:**
* **Engineering Guilds** replace traditional religious institutions as primary social organizations
* Guild membership determines social status, access to resources, and political power
* The three Great Guilds (Metallurgical, Optical, Locomotive) effectively govern most major cities
* Patent law complex and guild-controlled
* Industrial espionage common and tacitly accepted as "healthy competition"
* Failure celebrated as learning opportunity--innovation culture values experimentation

**The Locked Mystery Religion:**
* Not organized church but diffuse cultural obsession with Grimnir
* "Telescope Masses" occur when clouds part--public gatherings where thousands observe together
* No priests, only telescope-masters who interpret observations
* Theological-engineering challenge: Perfection forever visible, forever tantalizingly out of reach

**Magic Level:** Moderate
* Magic deeply integrated with technology rather than separate
* "Arcane-industrial fusion" is dominant paradigm
* Pure spellcasters rare; most magic-users are artificers, alchemists, or inventors blending magic with mechanics

**Technology Level:** Industrial Revolution (1850s-1870s Earth equivalent)
* Steam power ubiquitous: Locomotives, factories, ships, personal vehicles
* Electricity emerging: Experimental applications, lightning-capture attempts
* Telegraph networks: Continental communication, recently connected to Xuartlek's portal communication systems
* Photography: Well-developed, crucial for astronomical observation
* Medicine: Germ theory recently proven, anesthesia common, antiseptics widespread
* Weaponry: Breech-loading rifles standard, early machine guns in development, steam-powered war machines
* Aviation: Dirigibles and hot-air balloons common; heavier-than-air flight experimental
* **Optics**: THE most advanced technology--far exceeds Earth 1870s equivalent due to Locked Mystery obsession. Some telescopes rival early 20th century Earth designs.

**Cultural Theme:** Germanic Industrial Steampunk with Theological Overtones
* Core aesthetic: Victorian industrial meets Germanic mythology meets telescope worship
* Visual markers: Brass and iron, coal smoke and morning fog, riveted steel architecture, exposed gearing, pneumatic tubes, pressure gauges everywhere
* Germanic aesthetic: Half-timbered buildings with industrial additions, Gothic pointed arches rendered in steel
* Optical instruments ubiquitous--telescopes, microscopes, spectroscopes, theodolites treated as both tools and art objects
* Every major building has an observatory
* Every guild hall displays astronomical charts
* The rhythmic CHUNK-HISS of steam engines is the heartbeat of civilization

**The Myth of Grimnir (What people believe):**

In the Age of First Forging, Grimnir the Smith-God walked among mortals, teaching the secrets of metalwork, clockwork, and the principles of mechanical advantage. When the first cities rose and the first steam engines coughed to life, Grimnir knew his work on Langsevain was complete. He ascended to the heavens, transforming into the moon that forever watches the world.

The great polar crater visible on Grimnir's face is the Eternal Workshop, where the god continues his divine labor, forging new machines and perfecting the blueprints of reality itself. The geometric patterns etched across the moon's surface are these very blueprints--the fundamental designs from which all creation flows. The thick atmosphere trapped in the crater's depths shrouds the workshop in clouds, for mortals are not meant to see perfection until they are ready to comprehend it.

**Eisenherz Mythology:**
The massive triple-layered asteroid belt between Ferraine and Langsevain is called Eisenherz (Iron Heart). Folklore claims these are the shattered remains of Grimnir's first attempt at worldbuilding--the pieces of a forge-world that cracked under the weight of its own perfection. Steampunk mining operations brave the belt's depths, harvesting rare alloys.

**The Langsevan Lock Calendar:**
* 14 months of 28 days each (392 days)
* Plus 11-day Grimnirsruh intercalary period (when moon "locks" at year threshold)
* Total: 403 days per year
* Each month equals one Grimnir cycle (rounded from 26.9 to 28 by ancient astronomers)
* Leap day every 14 years during Grimnirsruh
* All major engineering projects synchronize to Grimnir's cycles
* Grimnirsruh functions as combination New Year's celebration, engineering review, and strategic planning session

**Recent Technological Breakthroughs (current year 1624 LC):**
* Optician's Guild recently discovered achromatic lenses (corrects color distortion)
* This breakthrough could reveal unprecedented detail during next cloud-parting event
* Several competing guilds developing revolutionary new telescope designs in preparation

**Xuartlek Connection:**
* **Khudealine District**: Precision manufacturing and clockwork quarter, dwarven majority. Produces fine instruments and mechanical components exported throughout Xuartlek's network. Connected to Jorkarith (Hrimgard) and Saluzzo (Shubae).
* **Vezgaxur District**: Automaton quarter where mechanical citizens debate philosophy, rights, and nature of consciousness. Connected to Ilythyrra (Arborisle) and Novorzhev (Tertara).
* **Why Xuartlek Came**: Industrial capacity and optical technology. Langsevain's telescope industry provides surveillance and astronomical observation capabilities for entire network. Automaton rights movement intrigued Xuartlek's philosophers.
* **Trade Exports**: Optical instruments, precision tools, clockwork mechanisms, industrial machinery, coal, steel, engineering expertise
* **Trade Imports**: Rare minerals for optical glass, exotic woods for instrument cases, arcane components for arcane-industrial fusion, foodstuffs, cultural goods
