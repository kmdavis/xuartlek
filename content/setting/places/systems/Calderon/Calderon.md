Where a tidally-locked moon watches with one face forever hidden behind clouds, an entire civilization builds telescopes and waits for the moment--once a generation--when the veil parts and divine perfection glimpses through.

Germanic and Spanish influences mixed:
* **Calderon**: The star (Spanish "cauldron"--reflects industrial/forge theme)
* **Langsevain**: Germanic compound lang- (long) + -sevain (derived from "sehen" = to see). Meaning: "Long-Seeing" or "Far-Sight"--reflects the telescope obsession culture
* **Grimnir**: Old Norse "The Masked One" (one of Odin's names)--the tidally-locked moon that hides its secrets behind clouds
* **Ferraine**: French fer (iron) + Germanic raine (pure/clean)--innermost rocky world
* **Eisenherz**: German "Iron Heart"--massive triple-layered asteroid belt (folklore: shattered remains of Grimnir's first failed world)
* **Funkenriese**: German "Spark Giant"--ice giant with glowing metallic rings
* **Nebelhüter**: German "Mist Guardian"--outer ice giant with storm bands
* **Districts**: Khudealine & Vezgaxur use Tortlian naming (Xuartlek standard)

Calderon is a K5V orange dwarf star, slightly smaller and cooler than Sol, with a longer main-sequence lifespan. Its habitable zone is closer to the star than Earth's, but the gentler radiation makes for stable, long-lasting biospheres.

**The Myth of Grimnir (What the people of Langsevain believe):**
In the Age of First Forging, Grimnir the Smith-God walked among mortals, teaching them
the secrets of metalwork, clockwork, and the principles of mechanical advantage. When
the first cities rose and the first steam engines coughed to life, Grimnir knew his
work on Langsevain was complete. He ascended to the heavens, transforming into the moon
that forever watches the world.

The great polar crater visible on Grimnir's face is the Eternal Workshop, where the
god continues his divine labor, forging new machines and perfecting the blueprints of
reality itself. The geometric patterns etched across the moon's surface are said to be
these very blueprints--the fundamental designs from which all creation flows. The thick
atmosphere trapped in the crater's depths shrouds the workshop in clouds, for mortals
are not meant to see perfection until they are ready to comprehend it.

Once every generation, the clouds part briefly, and telescope-masters report glimpses
of lights, structures, and movement--proof that Grimnir still labors. These rare
sightings are treated as divine revelations, and the engineer who witnesses one is
celebrated as blessed. The Locked Mystery, as philosophers call it, has become
Langsevain's central theological and engineering challenge: perfection forever visible,
forever tantalizingly just out of reach.

**Scientific Reality (Public Knowledge):**

Langsevain orbits at 420 Earth days (~403 Langsevain days of 25 hours each), well within the habitable zone with near-circular orbit (eccentricity 0.028). This stability allowed complex multicellular life to evolve over billions of years.

Grimnir, the tidally-locked moon, is a captured asteroid that underwent significant geological transformation:
* Tidal locking occurred naturally over millions of years due to gravitational forces
* The polar crater (500+ km diameter, several km deep) was formed by an ancient impact
* The crater is SO deep that despite the moon's low gravity, it retains a thick atmosphere--a rare geological phenomenon
* The geometric "patterns" are natural crystalline formations and impact ejecta rays, but their regularity is purely coincidental (pareidolia at planetary scale)
* The atmosphere is largely water vapor and carbon dioxide, creating dense cloud cover
* "Lights" occasionally seen are likely volcanic activity or meteor impacts reflecting sunlight through clouds at specific angles

**Astronomical Curiosities:**
* Tidally locked moon with thick atmosphere is extremely rare (requires perfect conditions: large deep crater + specific moon mass/distance)
* Grimnir's 28-day period (rounded by Langsevan astronomers to match calendar months) creates perfect synchronization between calendar and sky
* The geometric patterns visible from Langsevain are aligned almost perfectly with planetary north--this alignment is what makes them seem "designed" rather than random
* Cloud breaks occur roughly every 25-30 years, tied to long-period atmospheric cycles in the crater

**System Structure:**
* **Ferraine** (inner world): 88-day orbit, iron-rich rocky planet, airless, 5,200 km
* **Eisenherz** (asteroid belt): Massive triple-layered belt between Ferraine and Langsevain
  * Inner belt: Metal-rich asteroids, glowing faintly orange from star proximity
  * Middle belt: Mixed composition, prime mining territory
  * Outer belt: Ice-metal mix, cooler asteroids
  * Mythology: Fragments of Grimnir's first failed world, the Shattered Workshop
* **Langsevain** (habitable): 420-day orbit, temperate world, 11,800 km diameter
  * **Grimnir** (moon): 28-day orbit, tidally locked, 3,100 km, polar crater with atmosphere
* **Funkenriese** (gas giant): 12-year orbit, ice giant with glowing metallic rings
  * 7 moons in orbital resonance (Funkenmond cluster)
* **Nebelhüter** (gas giant): 30-year orbit, larger ice giant, storm bands
  * 12+ moons in chaotic orbits
* **Der Rand** (debris field): System edge, source of Funkenriese's metallic ring particles
