ASTRONOMICAL CURIOSITY: Chaotic Moon System + Captured Rogue World

THE THREE SISTERS - Legendary navigator-priestesses who became moons:
* Tanithiel: Prograde equatorial orbit (12.33 days, 7.5° inclination)
- Master of trade winds and safe passages, the Calm Sister
* Baelorn: Polar orbit (14.23 days, 91° inclination)
- Master of polar navigation, the Storm Sister
* Dídhiel: Retrograde orbit (16.81 days, 192° inclination)
- Master of counter-currents and hidden ways, the Deep Sister

YAMMON - The Bound God
* Gas giant containing the imprisoned form of the primordial chaos deity
* The Three Sisters' sacrifice chained him in eternal orbit

MOLOKHON - The World of the Drowned
* Captured rogue planet (~8.73 Shubae year orbit, 0.45 eccentricity)
* Yammon's final curse: an undead world hurled at Shubae but caught mid-strike
* Populated by the souls of the drowned, who return during close approach

THE TURNING (twice yearly at equinoxes):
* All three moons converge on one side of Shubae
* Facing side: Extreme high tides flood coastal settlements
* Opposite side: Extreme low tides expose deadly reefs and hidden dangers
* Occurs mid-Hadrumel (spring) and mid-Lixusil (autumn)

THE DROWNING (~every 17-18 Shubae years):
* Molokhon's periapsis aligns with The Turning
* The dead world draws close while guardians face away
* Souls of the drowned return en masse to Shubae
* Most feared astronomical event in Shubese culture
