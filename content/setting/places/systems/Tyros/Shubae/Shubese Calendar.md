## Description

A maritime calendar where three moons guide the sailors--their convergences marking the holiest days of the year, their phases charting courses across a desert world where life clings to the coasts and purple dye flows like liquid gold.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Shubae]] in the Tyros System
- **Theme**: Phoenician maritime culture, desert coasts, celestial navigation
- **Year Length**: 372 days (14 months alternating 26 and 27 days)
- **Day Length**: 26 hours

- **Weeks**: 8 days (sacred number in Phoenician maritime culture--8 directions of wind)

## The Three Moons

### Celestial Navigation
Three moons with complex orbital relationships are central to maritime navigation:

- **Tánithiel**: 12.33-day cycle, prograde orbit (named for Phoenician goddess Tanit)
- **Baelorn**: 14.23-day cycle, polar orbit at 91° inclination (sun-synchronized)
- **Dídhiel**: 16.81-day cycle, retrograde orbit (192° inclination)

### Lunar Convergences
- **Triple convergence**: Occurs TWICE per year at equinoxes (sacred celestial alignment)
- **Double convergences**: Multiple throughout the year, guide sailing seasons
- Phoenician-descended navigators use moon phases for long-distance voyages
- Religious festivals timed to convergences, especially the biannual triple alignment

### Navigation Impact
The three moons create complex tidal patterns crucial for harbor-based civilization. Understanding their phases is essential for any captain planning ocean voyages.

## Seasons

Desert coastal world with maritime trade culture:
- **Cool Season** (Carthalor through Hadrumel): Calm seas, sailing begins
- **Warm Season** (Leptisiel through Hipponis): Peak maritime trade, long voyages
- **Hot Season** (Gadesil through Lixusil): Inland deserts lethal, coastal refuge
- **Harvest Season** (Ruspinel through Zaghouanil): Purple dye harvest, year-end trade

## Cultural Significance

### The Purple Trade
Byssalon (month 2) is named for the famous Tyrian purple dye that drives Shubae's economy. The harvest season that closes each year centers on purple dye production--a tradition stretching back thousands of years.

### Triple Convergence Festivals
The two annual triple convergences (spring and autumn equinoxes) are the holiest days of the Shubese year. All major contracts are signed, marriages consecrated, and voyages blessed during these celestial alignments.

### Maritime Calendar
Unlike inland calendars, the Shubese calendar exists primarily to serve navigation. Month names honor trade routes, week days invoke the sailing gods, and every major date is defined by lunar position. A Shubese captain who can't read the calendar can't read the stars.
