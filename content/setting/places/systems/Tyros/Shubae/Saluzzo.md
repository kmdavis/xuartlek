Saluzzo is a Xuartlek Low City District on Shubae.

Founded in **185**, its primary portal opens onto **Docaichean a Deas**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Shubae]], in the [[Tyros]] system.
