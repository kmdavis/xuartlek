Amisra is a Xuartlek Low City District on Shubae.

Founded in **210**, its primary portal opens onto **Sjeverna Luka**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Shubae]], in the [[Tyros]] system.
