## Description

A star system scarred by cosmic invasion--home to Mortuus Rex, where darkness won a thousand years ago and the last bastion of light clings to survival beneath opposing moons: the blood-red prison of a death god and the shattered remains of what it destroyed.

## Naming Conventions

* **Umbra**: Latin = "shadow"--the star that illuminates a world of perpetual twilight

## Full Description

The Umbra System was once unremarkable. A yellow star, a handful of rocky worlds, a typical arrangement of matter around a typical stellar furnace. Then, one thousand years ago, something arrived from the void.

Sanguis was not born of this system. It came from between the stars--a rogue planetary body, massive and red with iron-oxide crust, carrying within it a god of death. The invasion was swift and terrible. Sanguis entered the system on a trajectory that defied natural orbital mechanics, sweeping past Mortuus Rex's original moon--home to a god of light--and shattering it in a single pass. The debris became Fractum, a permanent reminder of what darkness killed. Sanguis itself settled into a stable orbit, always opposite Fractum's remnants, ensuring that when one rises, the other sets.

The light god fell to the surface of Mortuus Rex, wounded but not destroyed. Its presence sanctifies a single peninsula where the living can still survive. Everything else belongs to the dead.

Umbra itself seems unchanged by the cataclysm--the star burns as it always has, indifferent to the tragedy playing out around its sole significant world. But astronomers in the Bastion have noted subtle variations in stellar output that correlate with the death god's power fluctuations. Whether Umbra is responding to the intruder, or being consumed by it, remains unknown.
