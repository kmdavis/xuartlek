Nuzairzad is a Xuartlek Low City District on Mortuus Rex--the only portal connection to the besieged world where the last Bastion of light holds against the armies of the dead.

Founded in **465**, its primary portal opens onto **Docaichean a Deas**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Mortuus Rex]], in the [[Umbra]] system.
