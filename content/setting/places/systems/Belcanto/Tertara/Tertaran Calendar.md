## Description

A musical calendar system where months are melodies and days are notes--reflecting Tertara's culture of bardic tradition and musical magic, with three dragon-moons dancing in perfect 120° harmony.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Tertara]] in the Belcanto System
- **Theme**: Musical/Italian Renaissance fantasy, bardic tradition
- **Year Length**: 479 days (16 months + intercalary festivals)
- **Day Length**: 22 hours

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 7 days (musical solfège: Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti)
- **Normal Months**: 16 months of 28 days each = 448 days
- **Intercalary Periods**: 17 festival periods totaling 31 days
- **Leap Years**: Every 7 years, except every 19th occurrence

### Days of the Week (Musical Solfège)
1. **Dorendì**: Do-day (first note, beginning)
2. **Rendì**: Re-day (second note)
3. **Mìdì**: Mi-day (third note, middle of week)
4. **Fadì**: Fa-day (fourth note)
5. **Soldì**: Sol-day (fifth note)
6. **Ladì**: La-day (sixth note)
7. **Tidì**: Ti-day (seventh note, end of week)

### Months (Italian Musical Terms)
**Winter:**
- **Nevemelodia**: Snow Melody (deep winter)
- **Brinacorda**: Frost String (late winter)
- **Gelavento**: Freeze Wind (late winter)
- **Invernocanto**: Winter Singing (winter's end)

**Spring:**
- **Fiorisono**: Flower Sound (early spring)
- **Primaritmo**: Spring Rhythm (mid spring)
- **Ventarmonia**: Wind Harmony (late spring)

**Summer:**
- **Verdecrescendo**: Green Crescendo (early summer)
- **Solempo**: Sun Time/Speed (high summer)
- **Calurivolgere**: Heat Turning Point (late summer)
- **Serenestate**: Summer Serenade (late summer)
- **Luceforte**: Strong Light (summer's end)

**Autumn:**
- **Fogliamusica**: Leaf Music (early autumn)
- **Autunmodulare**: Autumn Modulation (mid autumn)
- **Ventocadente**: Falling Wind (late autumn)
- **Morbidecrescendo**: Soft Decreasing (winter approaches)

## Intercalary Festivals

Festival days between months, each with cultural and religious significance:

**Winter Festivals:**
- **Solstizio d'Inverno** (1 day): Winter Solstice / New Year
- **Freddo Respiro** (2 days): Cold Breath - winter festivities
- **Danza di Ghiaccio** (2 days): Ice Dance - winter celebrations
- **Cuore Caldo** (2 days): Warm Heart - love festivals

**Spring Festivals:**
- **Alba di Primavera** (2 days): Spring Dawn - spring equinox
- **Germogli di Vita** (2 days): Life Sprouts - mother's day, elements
- **Sussurro del Vento** (2 days): Wind's Whisper - planting season
- **Verde Sinfonia** (2 days): Green Symphony - nature celebration

**Summer Festivals:**
- **Solstizio d'Estate** (1-2 days): Summer Solstice - leap day here
- **Embrione Stellare** (2 days): Stellar Embryo - light festivals
- **Regno del Sole** (2 days): Kingdom of the Sun - maestro's day
- **Oceano Cantante** (2 days): Singing Ocean - ocean festivals

**Autumn Festivals:**
- **Alba dell'Equilibrio** (2 days): Dawn of Balance - autumn equinox
- **Arcobaleno Autunnale** (2 days): Autumn Rainbow - father's day, colors
- **Voci del Passato** (2 days): Voices of the Past - spirits, heroes
- **Note degli Alberi** (2 days): Trees' Notes - harvest thanksgiving

**Year's End:**
- **Solstizio d'Inverno** (1 day): Winter Solstice / New Year's Eve

## Astronomical Basis

### The Three Dragon-Moons
All three moons share a 30-day orbital cycle, offset by 10 days (120° spacing):
- **Rubino** (Ruby): Red moon
- **Zaffiro** (Sapphire): Blue moon
- **Giada** (Jade): Green moon

This creates a perpetual celestial dance where at least one moon is always visible, and their harmonious spacing reflects Tertaran philosophy of musical order in the cosmos.

### Leap Day
"Il Giorno dell'Armonia Solare" (The Day of Solar Harmony) is added during summer solstice every 7 years, except every 19th occurrence.

## Seasons

Tertara enjoys gentle seasons in its habitable zone:
- **Winter**: Months 1-4 + winter intercalary days
- **Spring**: Months 5-7 + spring intercalary days
- **Summer**: Months 8-12 + summer intercalary days
- **Autumn**: Months 13-16 + autumn intercalary days

## Cultural Significance

The musical naming conventions reflect Tertara's bardic culture, where music is both art and magic. The calendar itself is considered a cosmic composition--the year a symphony, each month a movement, each day a note. Major events are timed to create harmonious "chords" with the celestial positions of the three moons.
