## Description

Where three moons sleep as sleeping gods and every spell is sung--a world where chromatic dragonborn rule through harmony while metallic-blooded kin bear the stain of ancient betrayal, and opera houses channel more power than armies.

## Naming Conventions

Italian musical influences throughout:
* **Belcanto**: The star (Italian = "beautiful singing," the operatic style)
* **Tertara**: The world (Latin tertius = third + ara = altar, "Third Altar" or simply musical wordplay)
* **Rubino**: Red moon/Creator dragon (Italian = "ruby")
* **Zaffiro**: Blue moon/Creator dragon (Italian = "sapphire")
* **Giada**: Green moon/Creator dragon (Italian = "jade")
* **Fortezza**: First prison planet (Italian = "fortress")
* **Giustizia**: Second prison planet (Italian = "justice")
* **Prudenza**: Third prison planet (Italian = "prudence")
* **Aldara/Eldara**: Twin continents (elvish-Italian hybrid, musical names)
* **Days**: Named after solfège (Dorendì, Rendì, Mìdì, Fadì, Soldì, Ladì, Tidì = Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti)
* **Months**: Musical Italian (Nevemelodia = "Snow-Melody," Fiorisono = "Flower-Sound," etc.)

## Full Description

Tertara sings. From the moment Belcanto rises over the horizon--an event marked on calendars as Dorendì, the first day of each week--to the moment it sets, the world resonates with magic and melody. Cities sprawl across the twin continents of Aldara and Eldara, their skylines dominated by opera houses whose crystalline spires channel arcane energy, and academies of magic where dragonborn scholars perfect the art of weaving spells like composers craft symphonies.

The dragonborn are Tertara's heart and majority. Scales of crimson, sapphire, and emerald mark those who carry the strongest draconic heritage--descendants of Rubino the Red, Zaffiro the Blue, and Giada the Green, the Creators who slumber above. These chromatic bloodlines dominate positions of power, their innate magic flowing as naturally as breath. Other dragonborn bear metallic scales or the muted tones of primal dragons, but all understand: magic is citizenship here. Those born without the gift--whether dragonborn, human, or kobold--occupy the margins, second-class citizens whose lives orbit the mages who rule.

Yet this is not a stratified world of pure arcane towers and gritty workshops. Magic here takes every form: battle-mages who sculpt fire into weapons, enchanters who weave protection into fabric, alchemists who transmute base metals into gold, artificers who craft singing swords and self-playing harps. The distinction between "pure caster" and "craft-mage" exists only in academic debates. All magic is harmony, and harmony takes infinite forms.

Above, the three moons perform their geometric dance--Rubino glowing red, Zaffiro shimmering blue, Giada gleaming green, forever locked 120° apart. Children grow up knowing the truth: the moons are not rock, but sleeping gods. The dragons who built this world from their combined essence, who proved that cooperation creates what isolation cannot. Every spell cast on Tertara echoes that first act of creation, and every mage knows: magic is harmony, and harmony is magic.

**Gravity:** Standard
**Atmosphere:** Breathable, temperate

**Climate:** Temperate zones suitable for sprawling urban development across two major continents.

## Geography

**Continents:**
* **Aldara**: One of two major twin landmasses
* **Eldara**: Second major twin landmass

**Urban Development:**
Cities dominate the landscape with:
* Opera houses rising with crystalline spires that channel arcane energy
* Academies of magic rivaling opera houses in grandeur and prominence
* Grand piazzas showcasing Renaissance Italian aesthetic
* Artistic architecture integrating magical infrastructure

## Astronomical Features

**The Three Moons (Lunar Triad):**
* **Rubino** (red moon): 2800 km diameter, glowing red
* **Zaffiro** (blue moon): 2800 km diameter, shimmering blue
* **Giada** (green moon): 2800 km diameter, gleaming green

**Orbital Configuration:**
* All three moons share identical 30-day orbital period
* Offset by 10 days each (Rubino day 0, Zaffiro day 10, Giada day 20)
* Locked in perfect 120° spacing when viewed from Tertara
* Artificial configuration--dragons positioned themselves deliberately at Lagrange points L4, planet center, L5

**Outer Planets (Visible but Distant):**
* **Fortezza** (first prison planet): Lifeless world, prison of first Rival dragon
* **Giustizia** (second prison planet): Lifeless world, prison of second Rival dragon
* **Prudenza** (third prison planet): Lifeless world, prison of third Rival dragon
* All three orbit in identical ~4.9 year periods, rotated 120° apart--artificial configuration mocking the Creators' lunar arrangement

## Culture

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
* **Dragonborn** (Human ancestry with Dragon heritage--dominant population):
  * **Chromatic heritages** (red/blue/green): Direct descendants of the Creators, strongest innate magic, dominate ruling class
  * **Metallic heritages** (copper/brass/bronze/silver/gold): Carry Rival dragon blood (descendants of the defeated), face subtle prejudice
  * **Primal heritages** (brine/cloud/crystal/forest/magma/umbral): Non-aligned draconic bloodlines, most common among middle class
* **Humans** (Human ancestry): Significant minority, both magical and non-magical
* **Kobolds** (Kobold ancestry): Smaller population, often serve as assistants/servants to dragonborn mages

**Society & Magic:**
* **Magocracy**: Magic equals full citizenship, political power, and social status
* Non-magical individuals are second-class citizens regardless of species
* NO divide between "pure casters" and "craft-mages"--all magic valued equally
* Magic types practiced: Battle-mages, enchanters, alchemists, artificers, bards, clerics (rare), druids (very rare)
* Core philosophy: Magic as art, spellcasting as performance, harmony as fundamental principle

**Cultural Aesthetic:**
* Renaissance Italian influence: Opera houses, grand piazzas, artistic architecture
* Musical integration extreme throughout daily life
* **Calendar**: Days named after solfège notes (Dorendì, Rendì, Mìdì, Fadì, Soldì, Ladì, Tidì = Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti)
* **Months**: Musical Italian names (Nevemelodia, Fiorisono, Ventarmonia, etc.)
* 479-day years, 22-hour days, 30-day moon cycles

**Magic Level:** Dominant/Pervasive--woven into every aspect of daily life

**Technology Level:** Ancient--strong preference for magical solutions over mechanical innovation

## History

**The Creation Myth (Cultural Foundation):**

**The Creators (Chromatic Triad):**
* Rubino the Red, Zaffiro the Blue, Giada the Green
* Ancient chromatic dragons who worked in perfect harmony
* Wove their essences together to create Tertara from nothing
* Effort exhausted them completely, causing them to ascend and become guardian moons
* Now sleep eternally as the three moons, watching over their creation
* Dragonborn with chromatic scales (red/blue/green) trace lineage directly to these Creators
* Cultural status: Heroes, gods, living proof that cooperation creates greatness

**The Rivals (Metallic Triad):**
* Three metallic dragons (specific identities lost to time or deliberately forgotten)
* Jealous of the Creators' masterwork, each tried to create a rival world ALONE
* Each failed, creating only lifeless, barren worlds (Fortezza, Giustizia, Prudenza)
* Enraged by their failures, they attacked the sleeping Creators
* Tertara itself rose to defend its makers, magic woven into every stone
* The Rivals were defeated and bound into their failed worlds as eternal prisons
* Dragonborn with metallic scales carry "tainted" blood and face subtle societal prejudice
* Cultural status: Cautionary tale about pride, isolation, and attempting greatness alone

**Inverted Trope:**
* In D&D lore: Chromatic dragons are typically evil/chaotic, Metallic dragons are good/lawful
* In Tertara: Chromatic dragons are creators/heroes (harmony), Metallic dragons are rivals/villains (pride)
* This inversion is fundamental to Tertaran culture and magocratic philosophy

**Xuartlek Connection:**
* Three Low City districts connect Tertara to the planar network: Ragnfast, Novorzhev, Peniculus
* Major trade hub for magical goods, enchanted items, and spell components
* Attracts scholars and mages from across the entire portal network
