Ragnfast is a Xuartlek Low City District on Tertara.

Founded in **150**, its primary portal opens onto **Quais Ouest**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Tertara]], in the [[Belcanto]] system.
