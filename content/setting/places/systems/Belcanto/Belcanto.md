A system where three chromatic dragons sleep as moons above their masterwork, while three jealous metallics remain imprisoned in failed worlds beyond.

Italian/musical throughout. "Belcanto" = beautiful singing (opera term). All celestial bodies use Italian names:
* Belcanto: The star (beautiful singing)
* Ardente: Burning/scorched innermost world
* Tertara: Central world (terra = earth/land, also "third" world from star)
* Rubino: Red moon (ruby)
* Zaffiro: Blue moon (sapphire)
* Giada: Green moon (jade)
* Fortezza: First prison planet (fortress/strength)
* Giustizia: Second prison planet (justice)
* Prudenza: Third prison planet (prudence)
* Tuono: Gas giant (thunder)
* Ghiaccio: Ice giant (ice)

Belcanto is a G-type main sequence star with a warm yellow glow, similar to Sol.

**Dragon Mythology (Public Knowledge):**

**The Creators (Chromatic Triad):**
* Rubino the Red, Zaffiro the Blue, Giada the Green
* Ancient chromatic dragons who worked in perfect harmony
* Wove their essences together to create Tertara from nothing
* Effort exhausted them completely → ascended to become guardian moons
* Now sleep eternally as the three moons, watching over their creation
* Dragonborn with chromatic scales (red/blue/green) trace lineage to these Creators
* Cultural status: Heroes, gods, proof that cooperation creates greatness

**The Rivals (Metallic Triad):**
* Three metallic dragons (specific identities lost to time/deliberately forgotten)
* Jealous of the Creators' masterwork, each tried to create a rival world ALONE
* Each failed → created lifeless, barren worlds (Fortezza, Giustizia, Prudenza)
* Enraged by failure, attacked the sleeping Creators
* Tertara itself rose to defend its makers, magic woven into every stone
* Rivals defeated and bound into their failed worlds as eternal prisons
* Dragonborn with metallic scales carry "tainted" blood, face subtle prejudice
* Cultural status: Cautionary tale about pride, isolation, and attempting greatness alone

**Cultural Impact:**
* In D&D lore: Chromatic dragons = evil/chaotic, Metallic dragons = good/lawful
* In Tertara: Chromatic = creators/heroes (harmony), Metallic = rivals/villains (pride)
* This inversion is fundamental to Tertaran culture and magocratic philosophy

**Lunar Triad Mechanics:**
* Three identically-sized moons (2800km diameter each)
* Locked at Lagrange points L4, planet, L5 (technically 60° spacing in co-orbital configuration)
* All three share 30-day orbital period, offset by 10 days each
* Perfect 120° spacing when viewed from Tertara
* Artificial configuration--dragons positioned themselves deliberately
* Colors visible from surface: Rubino glows red, Zaffiro shimmers blue, Giada gleams green

**Planetary Triad Mechanics:**
* Three worlds in identical orbits (1800-day period, ~4.9 years)
* Same semi-major axis, rotated 120° apart (argumentOfPeriapsis: 0°, 120°, 240°)
* High eccentricity (0.24) creates pronounced three-petal flower pattern when plotted
* All three uninhabited, lifeless--failed creation attempts
* Artificial configuration--positioned by Rivals in mockery of Creators' lunar arrangement
* Function as prisons containing the defeated Rivals' essences

**Astronomical Curiosity:**
System features TWO artificial geometric configurations:
1. Lunar Triad around Tertara (moons = sleeping Creator dragons)
2. Planetary Triad beyond asteroid belt (planets = prisons of Rival dragons)
Both configurations astronomically impossible through natural processes.
