In a graveyard where ambition shattered into orbiting debris, one world circles alone--its skies glittering with the ruins of a civilization that consumed its sibling planets to build paradise, then watched it all cascade into catastrophe.

* **Archaelon**: The star (Greek archaeos = ancient, suggesting age and ruins)
* **Profugae**: The inhabited world (Latin = refugees/exiles, those who fled underground)
* **Vastitas**: Gas giant (Latin = emptiness/desolation, reflecting its depleted state)

The Archaelon System is a tomb. Where most star systems show the familiar architecture of multiple worlds arranged by distance and temperature, Archaelon presents only absence. A single planet orbits its amber star, surrounded by three vast debris belts and a gutted gas giant--the scattered remains of a civilization that consumed everything within reach.

The orbital ring around Profugae is what makes Archaelon remarkable and tragic. It glitters with reflected starlight, visible even from the surface below: the ruins of vast orbital infrastructure that once encircled the planet in the plane of the ecliptic. Kilometers-wide habitats rotated to provide gravity, solar collectors drank in the star's light, shipyards assembled vessels, and orbital manufactories turned raw materials into the components of expansion. Debris fields show where sections once connected in stable gravitational harmony, docking facilities large enough to birth ships that could have carried thousands to other worlds. This was not a Dyson structure but something perhaps more ambitious--the industrial foundation for interstellar civilization.

Then came the cascade. War ignited in orbit, and one habitat was destroyed. Its fragments struck others, which shattered and struck more, until the entire network of orbital infrastructure dissolved into a cloud of high-velocity shrapnel. Debris fell from the sky like divine punishment, kinetic strikes that vaporized cities. What burned in the atmosphere added gigatons of dust, triggering nuclear winter. Those who survived fled underground. Above them, the unmaintained orbital ring slowly lost its delicate gravitational balance. Over centuries the sections drifted, collided, added more debris to the expanding cloud. The cascade continues even now--every few years something significant falls from orbit, and it will continue for millennia yet.

The other debris fields tell their own story of consumption. Closer to the star, the inner belt is not natural--it was once a Dyson swarm around Archaelon itself, thousands of independent solar collectors and habitats in stable orbits, the civilization's earliest megastructure. The two outer belts mark where sibling worlds once orbited, deliberately shattered millennia ago, their mass fed piece by piece into Profugae's megastructural ambitions. Even the gas giant Vastitas bears scars--a ring of mining debris surrounds it, testament to centuries of hydrogen and helium extraction that left the planet depleted and moonless. Archaelon is a system that cannibalized its neighbors for a dream of transcendence, and lost everything.

Archaelon is a K2-type main sequence star with an amber glow. It still burns steadily, indifferent to the tragedy that unfolded in its orbit millennia ago.

**Inner Belt (Dyson Swarm Remnants)**
* NOT a natural asteroid belt--shattered remains of a Dyson swarm around the star itself
* Thousands of independent solar collectors and habitats once in stable orbits
* Earliest megastructure generation, predates Profugae's orbital rings
* Now a debris field of destroyed infrastructure

**Profugae's Orbital Ring**
* NOT a Dyson structure--orbital infrastructure around the planet (not the star)
* Encircled planet in plane of ecliptic
* Multiple connected sections: habitats, shipyards, orbital manufactories, solar collectors, docking facilities
* Industrial foundation for interstellar civilization--more ambitious than the Dyson swarm
* Kilometers-wide rotating habitats with artificial gravity
* Visible from surface as glittering debris field
* Gravitational balance was precarious without active maintenance
* Continues to add debris via collisions

**Outer Debris Belts (2)**
* Deliberately shattered sibling worlds, mined over centuries to provide raw materials for megastructures
* Evidence that this civilization deliberately destroyed neighboring planets to fuel megastructural ambitions

**Vastitas (Gas Giant)**
* Extensively mined for hydrogen and helium for fusion power and fuel
* Surrounded by a ring of mining debris
* Zero moons (either never had them or consumed during mining operations)
* Depleted and scarred from centuries of industrial extraction

**The Cascade** (millennia ago--exact time forgotten, records destroyed)
1. War ignited in orbit → one habitat destroyed → Kessler syndrome cascade initiated
2. Orbital debris struck other habitats → chain reaction destroyed all orbital infrastructure
3. Debris fell to surface → kinetic bombardment vaporized cities
4. Atmospheric debris → triggered nuclear winter (still ongoing)
5. Survivors retreated underground
6. Unmaintained orbital ring drifted over centuries → gravitational instability → collisions
7. Second cascade → added more orbital debris
8. Present day: Ongoing debris falls, will continue for millennia
