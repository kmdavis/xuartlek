## Description

Beneath skies dark with orbital debris, a world that forgot the sun--where deep gnomes, duergar, and drow scavenge the ruins of gods who fell from heaven, their civilization built from the technological corpses of those who reached too high.

## Full Description

Profugae is a world that has forgotten the sun. Oh, Archaelon still shines above, but its light filters through kilometers of atmospheric dust, arriving at the surface as nothing more than dim twilight even at noon. Storms rage across the scarred continents--hurricanes that can last for months, temperature swings that would kill an unprotected human in hours, and every few years, the terrible flash and thunder of something large falling from orbit, adding another crater to a surface already pocked with scars.

Nobody lives on the surface anymore. The last surface cities were abandoned generations ago, their populations retreating into the vast warren systems that honeycomb Profugae's crust. Here, deep gnomes form the majority--clever engineers and scavengers who have built an entire civilization from salvaged technology. They share their warrens with du

ergar, who maintain the deep forges and mining operations, and drow, who patrol the darkest reaches where things from the ancient times still sometimes wake.

The warrens aren't primitive tunnels. They're kilometers-wide caverns lit by bioluminescent fungi and salvaged fusion reactors, connected by rail systems that use magnetic levitation technology scavenged from orbital debris. Markets sprawl in cathedral-sized chambers where merchants sell everything from fresh mushrooms to working laser weapons pulled from a fallen habitat section. Universities teach both traditional magic and the "old science"--the technological principles that created the ruins above.

Because Profugae's true wealth lies in the sky. Every few years, something significant falls--a habitat section, a manufacturing module, a ship component. These "Godfalls" are what the surface-runners live for: brave souls (mostly deep gnomes and duergar, with magical support from drow wizards) who venture topside in heavily armored crawler vehicles, racing to reach the impact site before competitors, weather, or the impact heat dissipates. A single successful salvage run can fund a clan for generations. Ancient technology beyond current understanding, materials that can't be replicated, energy sources that still function after millennia--all there for those brave or desperate enough to face the surface.

The orbital ring dominates the night sky, a glittering belt of debris that rotates slowly overhead. Children grow up learning to read its patterns--which sections are stable, which are likely to decay soon, where the highest-value salvage might fall. The ring is both graveyard and provider, a constant reminder of the heights their ancestors reached and the depths to which they fell.

* Dim twilight even at noon
* Months-long hurricanes common
* Extreme temperature fluctuations
* Surface entirely inhospitable to unprotected life

* Scarred continents covered in impact craters
* Ancient city ruins, long abandoned
* Extreme weather makes surface travel deadly
* Only visited during "Godfall" salvage operations

**Underground Warrens:**
* Vast cavern systems stretching kilometers deep
* Connected by magnetic levitation rail systems
* Cathedral-sized market chambers
* Bioluminescent fungi and salvaged fusion reactors provide light
* Universities, forges, residential districts all underground
* Population centers rival surface cities in size

**The Ring (Orbital Debris Field):**
* Visible as glittering belt across night sky
* Slowly rotating, sections decay and fall periodically
* Source of all advanced technology via "Godfall" salvage
* Remains of kilometers-wide orbital habitats, solar collectors, shipyards, manufactories

## Astronomical Features

**The Orbital Ring:**
Profugae's skies are dominated by the glittering debris field that was once the civilization's crowning achievement--a vast network of orbital infrastructure that encircled the entire planet. Now it's a slowly decaying graveyard that periodically rains technological miracles (and occasionally disasters) onto the surface below.

**The Cascade:**
Millennia ago, war in orbit triggered a Kessler syndrome cascade. One destroyed habitat struck another, which struck another, until the entire orbital network shattered. Debris fell like divine punishment, triggering nuclear winter that persists to this day. The unmaintained ring continues to decay--collisions add more debris, sections drift and fall. Every few years, something significant impacts the surface.

**"Godfalls":**
When major orbital sections fall, surface-running teams race to salvage them before the impact heat dissipates or competitors arrive. These operations are extraordinarily dangerous but extraordinarily profitable. A single fusion reactor or intact computer core can fund a warren-clan for generations.

## Culture

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
* **Deep Gnomes** (60%): Majority population, engineers and scavengers. Umbral gnomes (from deep warrens) and Sensate gnomes (tech specialists) dominate. Natural darkvision and cleverness make them ideal for warren life and salvage operations.
* **Duergar** (25%): Deep dwarves who maintain forges and deep mining. Their affinity for stone and metal makes them essential for warren maintenance and technological repair. Some practice ancient metallurgy, others master salvaged fusion forges.
* **Drow** (15%): Dark elves who patrol the deepest warrens and provide magical support for surface runs. Less numerous but highly respected for their combat magic and ability to handle the stranger things found in deep places. Cave elf heritage represents those who've lived exclusively underground for generations.

**Technology Level:** Far Future/Post-Scarcity (What Remains)
* Salvaged fusion reactors power warren systems
* Magnetic levitation trains connect major warrens
* Laser weapons and powered armor from orbital salvage
* But: Can't manufacture most high-tech items, only repair/repurpose
* Technological regression ongoing as salvage becomes scarcer

**Magic Level:** Moderate
* Practical focus: Divination for locating salvage, protection for surface runs
* Drow wizards essential for understanding ancient magical-technological hybrids
* Artificers attempt to reverse-engineer salvaged technology
* Some items are pure technology, some are pure magic, many are inseparable fusion of both

**Surface-Runner Culture:**
* Elite profession combining bravery, engineering knowledge, and luck
* Heavily armored crawler vehicles (salvaged and maintained across generations)
* Teams typically: Deep gnome pilot, duergar engineer, drow wizard for magical threats
* "Godfall-blessed" families who've made multiple successful runs have high status
* Dangerous enough that most warrens maintain memorial walls for lost runners

**Social Structure:**
* Warren-clans: Extended family groups controlling territory and salvage rights
* Governed by councils of elders who remember the old ways
* Engineers and surface-runners have highest status
* Academic institutions teach both magic and "old science"
* Strict recycling culture--nothing can be wasted when resources are finite

**Theme:** Post-Apocalyptic Underdark meets Salvage Civilization

## History

**The Cascade (Millennia Ago):**
1. Orbital war destroyed one habitat
2. Kessler syndrome cascade shattered entire orbital network
3. Kinetic bombardment from falling debris vaporized surface cities
4. Atmospheric debris triggered nuclear winter
5. Survivors fled underground
6. Orbital ring continued to decay, adding more debris

**The Long Descent:**
* First underground generations remembered surface life
* Technology preserved but manufacturing capacity lost
* Population adapted to warren life across centuries
* Surface-running culture developed around salvage operations
* Current generation: Surface is myth-like, known only through salvage

**Xuartlek Connection:**
* Two Low City districts: Athaneli (main district), others undocumented
* Xuartlek's arrival: First contact with worlds that still have functioning technology
* Cultural shock: Realizing orbital infrastructure wasn't unique
* Trade: Salvaged technology, rare materials, "old science" knowledge
* Import: Raw materials, food, manufactured goods they can't produce

**The Question:**
What caused the war that triggered the Cascade? The warrens hold no records old enough to answer. Some say it was ideological, others resource-based, still others whisper about AI rebellion or alien intervention. The truth died with the civilization that reached for the stars.
