Athaneli is a Xuartlek Low City District on Profugae.

Founded in **115**, its primary portal opens onto **Østre Havn**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Profugae]], in the [[Archaelon]] system.
