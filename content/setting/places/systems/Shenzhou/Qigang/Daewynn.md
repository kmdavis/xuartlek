A Xuartlek Low City District on Qigang where leonin honor-warriors serve as portal guards and cultural mediators.

Founded in **395**, its primary portal opens onto **Quais Ouest**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Qigang]], in the [[Shenzhou]] system.
