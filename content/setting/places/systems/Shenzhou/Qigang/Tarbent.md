A Xuartlek Low City District on Qigang where loxodon scholars maintain historical records that validate ancient astronomical texts.

Founded in **415**, its primary portal opens onto **Sjeverna Luka**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Qigang]], in the [[Shenzhou]] system.
