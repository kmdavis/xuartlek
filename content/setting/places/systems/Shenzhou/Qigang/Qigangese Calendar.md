## Description

A lunisolar calendar system reflecting Qigang's six-fold cosmic harmony--six elements, six moons, six-day weeks--where the Dragon's Scales dance through the sky and ancient mathematics perfectly divides time into harmonious cycles.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Qigang]] in the Shenzhou System
- **Theme**: Oriental fantasy, cosmic harmony, honor-based culture
- **Year Length**: 432 days (12 months × 36 days, no intercalary adjustment needed)
- **Day Length**: 24 hours (12 two-hour divisions 时辰 shíchén)
- **Grand Cycle**: 72 years (12 zodiac × 6 elements)

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 6 days (matching six elements)
- **Months**: 12 months of 36 days each = 432 days
- **Lunar Cycle**: 30 days (moon cluster orbital period)
- **Synchronization**: Every 5 solar months (180 days) = 6 lunar cycles

### Days of the Week (Element-Based)
Each day corresponds to one of Qigang's six fundamental elements:
1. **Wood Day (木日 Mù Rì)**: Growth, creativity, new beginnings. Auspicious for planting, starting projects, artistic endeavors.
2. **Fire Day (火日 Huǒ Rì)**: Energy, action, transformation. Auspicious for celebrations, forging, important decisions.
3. **Earth Day (土日 Tǔ Rì)**: Stability, rest, nourishment. Auspicious for building, contracts, family gatherings.
4. **Metal Day (金日 Jīn Rì)**: Refinement, commerce, harvest. Auspicious for trade, metalwork, financial dealings.
5. **Water Day (水日 Shuǐ Rì)**: Flow, adaptability, wisdom. Auspicious for travel, learning, diplomatic meetings.
6. **Air Day (气日 Qì Rì)**: Freedom, communication, change. Auspicious for messages, wind magic, exploration.

### Months (Zodiac Animal + Seasonal Descriptor)
**Late Winter → Spring:**
1. **Shǔchūn (鼠春)** "Spring of the Rat": Late winter awakening, new year celebrations

3. **Hǔxǐng (虎醒)** "Tiger Awakens": Spring in full force, energy rises

**Spring → Summer:**
4. **Tùhuā (兔花)** "Rabbit Flower": Late spring blossoms, beauty and growth

6. **Shéxià (蛇夏)** "Snake Summer": Peak heat, intensity and stillness

**Summer → Autumn:**
7. **Mǎshōu (马收)** "Horse Harvest": First harvests begin, movement and gathering
8. **Yángqiū (羊秋)** "Goat Autumn": Autumn equinox mid-month, gentle transition
9. **Hóushí (猴实)** "Monkey Fruit": Fruit harvest, preparation and collection

**Autumn → Winter:**
10. **Jīcáng (鸡藏)** "Rooster Storage": Storing for winter, consolidation
11. **Gǒudōng (狗冬)** "Dog Winter": Winter solstice mid-month, deep cold, loyalty
12. **Zhūxuě (猪雪)** "Pig Snow": Snow month, rest and plenty

## Leap Year System

### Actual Orbital Year
432.152777... days (exactly 432 + 11/72 days)

### Leap Pattern
- Add 1 leap day every 6 years
- EXCEPT: Skip the leap day in year 72 (grand cycle completion)
- Result: 11 leap days per 72-year cycle
- Math: (72 × 432 + 11) ÷ 72 = 432.152777... (perfect alignment!)

### The Dragon's Reflection (龙思日 Lóng Sī Rì)
The leap day is the last day of the year (day 433 in leap years):
- Falls outside the weekly cycle (not counted as any elemental day)
- Traditional activities: reflection on past year, settling debts, astronomical observations, ancestor veneration
- Prepares offerings for New Year's Day

### Year 72 Significance
The only 432-day year in the grand cycle. Both zodiac and element cycles return to their starting point. Major celebrations, once-in-a-lifetime events.

## Seasonal Markers

The calendar begins in late winter, approximately 6 weeks before the Spring Equinox. Each season is exactly 108 days (3 months):

- **Day 1**: NEW YEAR (late winter, "Spring Festival" equivalent)
- **Day 48** (Month 2, mid): SPRING EQUINOX - equal day/night
- **Day 156** (Month 5, mid): SUMMER SOLSTICE - longest day, peak yang
- **Day 264** (Month 8, mid): AUTUMN EQUINOX - equal day/night, harvest peak
- **Day 372** (Month 11, mid): WINTER SOLSTICE - shortest day, peak yin
- **Day 432**: Year ends → Dragon's Reflection (leap years) → New Year

## Linguistic Context

### Wenyan (文言 Wényán) - Literary Speech
Despite political fragmentation (the "Era of the Hundred Kingdoms"), the scholarly class shares a common literary language:
- The language of astronomy, philosophy, calendar-keeping, and formal literature
- Written form preserved across all kingdoms for over 1,000 years
- Nobody speaks it as native tongue--learned, formal, timeless
- Calendar terms, year cycle names, and astronomical observations recorded in Wenyan
- A Leonin scholar from northern grasslands and a Loxodon magistrate from southern deltas can correspond perfectly in written Wenyan

This linguistic unity is one of the few threads connecting the Hundred Kingdoms to their shared past.

## Astronomical Integration

### Lunar Cycle vs Solar Months
- **Lunar cycle**: 30 days (Dragon's Scales orbital period)
- **Solar month**: 36 days (calendar month)
- **Drift**: 6 days per solar month
- **Synchronization**: Every 5 solar months = 6 lunar cycles (180 days)

This creates natural rhythm where moon phases shift through solar months, with astrological significance for which lunar phase falls on which calendar day.

### The Six Moons' Irregular Spacing
The moons' clustering within a 110° arc creates complex astrological patterns:
- Multiple moons visible together (auspicious configurations)
- Long dark periods (times for meditation, stealth, certain rituals)
- Moon conjunctions (rare, highly significant)
- Which moons are visible during which months determines yearly astrological character
