ASTRONOMICAL CURIOSITY: Binary Planets - Khashayar and Nushirvan are a true binary
planet system, both rocky worlds orbiting their common barycenter in a 10-day period,
while the entire system orbits the star. The synchronized dance creates unique tidal
patterns and celestial mechanics. Nushirvan appears in Khashayar's sky as a large
indigo disc, serving as the primary "moon" for calendar purposes. The binary orbit
determines the length of the Khashayari year (~310 days).

NAMING CONVENTION: Persian/Zoroastrian Mythology
* Shamsara: Star name (Persian "shams" = sun)
* Khashayar: Old Persian form of Xerxes (Khshayarsha), one of the brother-kings
* Nushirvan: Anushirvan/Khosrow I "The Just Soul", the other brother-king
* Mithrayan: From Mithra/Mithras, ancient Persian solar deity
* Fereydun: Legendary Persian king from the Shahnameh
* Zahhakan: From Zahhak, the dragon-king/tyrant of Persian mythology
* Mithrayan's moons: The Amesha Spentas (seven divine emanations in Zoroastrianism)
* Fereydun's moons: His three sons from Persian legend

THE MYTH OF THE BROTHER-KINGS (what people believe):
* In prehistory, Khashayar and Nushirvan were brother-kings ruling a unified empire
* In simultaneous betrayal, each struck at the other at the exact same moment
* The gods judged both guilty and transformed them into worlds:
- Khashayar: Cursed with endless desert and eternal thirst
- Nushirvan: Drowned beneath monster-filled oceans, forever deadly
* Bound in eternal 10-day orbit - neither can escape their shared guilt

THE SCIENTIFIC REALITY (not in public narrative):
* Natural binary planet formation from same protoplanetary disk
* Khashayar's harsh climate: slow rotation + eccentric axial tilt + greenhouse effect
* Nushirvan's monsters: isolated ecosystem evolution without land-based competition
* Binary mechanics are gravitational, not divine - but the myth resonates because
it gives meaning to the cruelty of living on a desert world with an ocean overhead

NUSHIRVAN - The Deadly Ocean World:
* Zero landmass - pure water world from pole to pole
* Indigo coloration visible from Khashayar (fills ~1/4 of sky)
* The Myth: Cursed waters filled with monsters as punishment
* The Reality: Isolated ecosystem evolved apex predators without land competition
* Has icy rings (possibly destroyed moon or captured cometary debris)
* "The tears Nushirvan cannot shed" - ring shadows cast on Khashayar

KHASHAYAR SKY MECHANICS:
* 180-hour days (extreme heat) and 180-hour nights (extreme cold)
* Nushirvan visible day and night, completes 10-day mutual orbit
* Nushirvan serves as calendar marker (like a moon)
* Ring shadows occasionally visible during certain alignments
* Dawn/dusk are the "mercy hours" when life can function

SURVIVAL & CULTURE - The 180-Hour Cycle:
* 30 hours dawn twilight (tolerable) - ALL outdoor work happens here
* 60 hours peak day (lethal heat) - indoor activities, Ifrit work outside
* 30 hours dusk twilight (tolerable) - outdoor work resumes
* 60 hours deep night (lethal cold) - indoor activities, Undine work outside

DAILY RHYTHM:
* Sleep: 8 periods per cycle (once at start/end of each twilight, twice during each extreme)
* Meals: 2-3 during twilight, 6-9 more during indoor periods
* Everyone pushes to stay awake during twilight - too precious to waste sleeping
* Indoor tasks (schools, theaters, markets, workshops) close during twilight
* Ifrit (heat-immune) & Undine (cold-resistant) are elite laborers, paid well to work extremes

AGRICULTURE:
* All fields within 1-2 miles of rivers (like ancient Egypt)
* Twice-daily covering ritual: translucent mesh for day, thick cloth for night
* Mesh allows light but blocks heat, traps humidity
* Thick cloth insulates against freezing temperatures

WATER LAW:
* Water is currency, status, power - more valuable than gold
* Every citizen has daily allocation - theft = murder
* Aphorite scholars enforce water rights with religious fervor
* Laws older than oldest empire
