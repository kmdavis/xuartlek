Ghonumi is a Xuartlek Low City District on Khashayar.

Founded in **340**, its primary portal opens onto **Sjeverna Luka**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Khashayar]], in the [[Shamsara]] system.
