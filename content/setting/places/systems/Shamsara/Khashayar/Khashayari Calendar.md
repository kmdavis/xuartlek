## Description

A calendar designed for survival on a world of extremes--where each 180-hour day cycles through lethal heat and lethal cold, and the year counts in survival cycles rather than seasons.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Khashayar]] in the Shamsara System (binary planet with Nushirvan)
- **Theme**: Extreme survival, Arabic/Persian influences, water scarcity
- **Year Length**: 41 days (each day = 180 hours = 7.5 Earth days)
- **Day Length**: 180 hours (seven Earth days, twelve hours)

## The Extreme Day

### Survival Cycle (180 hours)
A single Khashayar "day" is an entire survival cycle:
- **30 hours**: Dawn twilight (tolerable) -- ALL outdoor work
- **60 hours**: Peak day (lethal heat) -- Ifrit work outside, others indoors
- **30 hours**: Dusk twilight (tolerable) -- outdoor work resumes
- **60 hours**: Deep night (lethal cold) -- Undine work outside, others indoors

### Sleep Pattern
- 8 sleep periods per "day"
- ~22.5 hours awake per period
- Species adapted to extreme temperature tolerance dominate outdoor work during danger periods

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 4 days (aligned to survival rhythm)
- **Months**: 5 months of 8 days each = 40 days
- **Intercalary**: Eid al-Sanah (New Year) = 1-2 days
- **Leap Years**: Every 2 years, except every 10th year

### Days of the Week (Arabic)
Each day = one complete 180-hour survival cycle:
1. **Alhad**: Sunday/First day (one complete dawn→day→dusk→night cycle)
2. **Ithnayn**: Monday/Second day
3. **Thulatha**: Tuesday/Third day
4. **Arba'a**: Wednesday/Fourth day

Note: A "week" is 4 days = 720 hours = 30 Earth days

### Months (Arabic/Islamic-inspired)
1. **Safar**: Journey/Travel month (8 days)
2. **Rabi**: Spring/Growth month (8 days)
3. **Jumada**: Dry/Arid month (8 days)
4. **Rajab**: Honored/Respected month (8 days)
5. **Sha'ban**: Branching/Distribution month (8 days, water allocation)

*Eid al-Sanah: New Year Festival (1-2 days intercalary)*

## Astronomical Basis

### Binary Planet System
Khashayar and Nushirvan orbit a common barycenter every 10 Khashayar days. Nushirvan is visible constantly in the sky as a massive indigo disc--the "water-moon" that reminds Khashayar's people what they lack.

### Year Length
- 1 Khashayari year = 41 Khashayar days
- = 312 Xuartlek days
- ≈ 310 Earth days
- Actual year: 41.6 days (requires leap system)

## Seasons

Khashayar's seasons are defined by temperature extremes and water availability:
- **Cool Season** (Safar, Rabi): Less lethal temperatures, relative comfort
- **Hot Season** (Jumada, Rajab): Peak heat, water scarcity crisis
- **Harvest Season** (Sha'ban): Irrigation cycle completes, water redistribution
- **Festival** (Eid al-Sanah): Celebrate survival, redistribute water allocations

## Cultural Significance

### Water as Time
On Khashayar, water allocation and calendar are inseparable. Water rights are measured in calendar units. The end of Sha'ban (water distribution month) determines who survives the next year.

### Eid al-Sanah
The intercalary New Year festival is both celebration and reckoning--a time when water debts are settled, allocations are renewed, and communities come together to acknowledge they have survived another year on a world that should be uninhabitable.

### Species Adaptation
The 180-hour day forced evolutionary and magical adaptation. Ifrit-touched beings can work during lethal heat; Undine-touched beings can function during lethal cold. The calendar itself reflects this division of labor.
