## Description

Where dragons navigate by quantum uncertainties and the newest frontier of civilization rises from untamed wilderness--a young world where Xuartlek's most recent colony carves footholds into ancient forests, and astronomers puzzle over Lagrange points that flicker in and out of existence like memories of something that was never quite real.

## Naming Conventions

Greek and frontier influences reflecting new settlement:
* **Tessara**: The star (Greek τέσσαρα = "four," reference to the four major orbital bodies)
* **Myrrhina**: The world (Greek μύρρα = myrrh + feminine suffix, "place of myrrh/fragrant resin"--named for the aromatic forests)
* **Schrödinger's Triangle**: Scientific nickname for the quantum Lagrange phenomenon
* **The Ghost Triangles**: Colloquial name for the L4/L5 anomalies
* **Sulveig**: Xuartlek colony (Nordic naming--"sun path," reflecting frontier optimism)
* Calendar: Myrrhinan calendar (newly established, based on local orbital period)

## Full Description

Myrrhina is a world on the edge of the known--both geographically and scientifically. Xuartlek's newest permanent settlement, the colony of Sulveig, represents the cutting edge of expansion into unclaimed territory. The forests stretch for thousands of miles, interrupted only by mountain ranges and river valleys that have never known civilization's touch. Indigenous creatures range from the familiar (wolves, bears, deer analogues) to the utterly alien (living rock formations, symbiotic plant-animal hybrids, weather phenomena that seem almost intentional).

But Myrrhina's true mystery hangs in the sky. The Tessara system's Lagrange points--the gravitationally stable zones where objects can orbit in fixed positions relative to planet and star--exist in quantum superposition at macroscopic scale. Gravitational signatures show mass flickering in and out of existence over days and weeks. Sometimes there's moon-sized mass at L4; sometimes nothing. The phenomenon defies every model of physics that Xuartlek's scholars have developed.

Dragons navigate by these points regardless of quantum state. They clearly know something that surface-dwellers don't. The great wyrms have inhabited Myrrhina since before Xuartlek's arrival, and they treat the colonists with studied indifference--neither hostile nor welcoming, simply present, flying patterns that seem to trace the Ghost Triangles' probability waves.

The colony itself is small but growing. Sulveig's population numbers in the thousands, a mix of pioneers seeking opportunity, scholars studying the quantum phenomenon, and adventurers drawn by rumors of untamed wilderness hiding ancient secrets. The portal to Xuartlek provides connection to civilization; everything else must be built from scratch.

## Astronomical Features

**Schrödinger's Triangle (Quantum Lagrange Phenomenon):**

The L4 and L5 Lagrange points of the Tessara system exist in quantum superposition at macroscopic scale--unprecedented in known astronomy.

**Observable Characteristics:**
* Gravitational signatures show mass flickering in and out
* Observations are probabilistic, not deterministic
* Sometimes moon-sized mass present, sometimes nothing
* State seems to change over days/weeks, not instantly
* Multiple observers sometimes report different states simultaneously
* Phenomenon violates standard physics at every level

**Scientific Theories:**
1. Young system instability creating unusual gravitational dynamics
2. Ancient quantum engineering by unknown civilization
3. Dragon civilization artifact maintained through unknown means
4. Natural phenomenon not yet understood
5. Observer effect at planetary scale (each observation affects state)

**Dragon Navigation:**
* Dragons fly patterns that trace the probability waves
* They navigate by the Ghost Triangles regardless of current state
* Some scholars believe dragons perceive both states simultaneously
* Dragons have not explained their understanding (they rarely explain anything)
* Flight patterns may be maintaining the phenomenon, not just observing it

**Standard Astronomical Bodies:**
* **Tessara**: G-type star similar to Sol
* **Myrrhina**: Fourth planet, habitable zone
* **Inner planets**: Two rocky worlds, uninhabited
* **Outer system**: Gas giant with standard moon system
* The quantum anomaly is unique to the L4/L5 points

**Observation Challenges:**
* Telescope observations of L4/L5 produce contradictory data
* Gravitational measurements vary depending on instrument and observer
* Magical scrying returns inconsistent results
* Dragons' eyes seem to see something observers cannot
* "Spooky action at a distance"--quantum entanglement at planetary scale

## Culture

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
Colony population reflects Xuartlek's diverse network:
* **Humans** (35%): Largest single group, various heritages. Pioneers, farmers, merchants.
* **Gnomes** (20%): Drawn by novelty and mystery. Scientists studying quantum phenomenon, artisans working new materials.
* **Halflings** (15%): Community builders, hospitality providers, small-farm specialists.
* **Elves** (10%): Long-term planners, forest-adapted settlers, diplomatic contact with dragons.
* **Others** (20%): Mixed ancestries from across Xuartlek network--dwarven miners, orcish laborers, various uncommon heritages seeking opportunity.

**Indigenous Dragons:**
* Multiple dragon types observed (chromatic and metallic)
* Predate colony by unknown duration
* Neither hostile nor friendly--indifferent
* Communicate rarely, cryptically when they do
* Clearly intelligent, clearly uninterested in colonist concerns
* Navigate by Ghost Triangles--key to understanding the phenomenon?

**Magic Level:** Moderate
* Standard magical traditions brought by colonists
* Some spells behave unusually near quantum phenomenon
* Divination especially unreliable
* Local magical traditions not yet developed
* Dragons obviously magical but nature of their magic unclear

**Technology Level:** Iron Age (Frontier Conditions)
* Advanced knowledge from Xuartlek network available
* But local manufacturing limited
* Most complex items imported through portal
* Colony developing self-sufficiency gradually
* Architecture and tools match available materials

**Social Structure:**
* Colonial government appointed by Xuartlek administration
* Town council handles local matters
* Frontier conditions create informal hierarchies based on competence
* Guild structures beginning to form
* Class divisions minimal--everyone works

**Economy:**
* Subsistence agriculture expanding to surplus
* Logging primary export industry
* Exotic materials (aromatic resins, novel minerals) valuable
* Import-dependent for manufactured goods
* Currency both local (barter) and Xuartlek standard

**Religion:**
* Colonists brought diverse faiths from home worlds
* No dominant local religion yet
* Some naturalistic spirituality developing around forest and dragons
* Quantum phenomenon attracts philosophical speculation

**Theme:** Frontier Settlement meets Scientific Mystery

## History

**Pre-Colonial Period:**
* Dragons present for unknown duration (possibly millions of years)
* No evidence of prior civilization except dragons
* Some unexplained ruins in deep forest (surveyed but not explored)
* Quantum phenomenon presumably predates recorded history

**Discovery:**
* Xuartlek's surveyors identified Myrrhina approximately 50 years ago
* Initial surveys noted habitable conditions and dragon presence
* Quantum phenomenon discovered during detailed astronomical mapping
* Decision made to establish colony despite mysteries

**Sulveig Founded (Recent):**
* Colony established within living memory
* Initial population: Approximately 500 pioneers
* Current population: Approximately 3,000-4,000
* Growth limited by portal capacity and frontier conditions

**Dragon Contact:**
* First contact: Dragons observed colonists but did not approach
* Subsequent attempts at communication largely unsuccessful
* Dragons seem to understand speech but rarely respond
* One recorded conversation: Dragon asked "Why do you only see half?" then flew away
* Interpretation remains debated

**Xuartlek Connection:**
* **Sulveig District**: Single portal connecting to Xuartlek
* Newest district in entire portal network
* Strategic importance: Frontier expansion, scientific research
* Connected to Mortuus Rex (Nuzairzad) and Arborisle (Renestrae)
* Trade: Exports exotic materials, imports everything else
* Population exchange: Pioneers arrive, few leave

**Current Situation:**
* Colony stable but not self-sufficient
* Scientific teams studying quantum phenomenon full-time
* Dragon behavior unchanged since founding
* Interior exploration ongoing but slow
* Rumors of artifacts in deep forest persist but remain unconfirmed
