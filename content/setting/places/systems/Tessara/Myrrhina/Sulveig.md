Sulveig is Xuartlek's newest frontier colony on Myrrhina--a growing settlement of pioneers, scholars studying the quantum phenomenon, and adventurers drawn to untamed wilderness.

Founded in **500**, the twenty-fourth and newest of the Low City districts. Its
primary portal onto **Quais Ouest**, one of the four rim harbours of
[[The High City]], is still under construction and completes in **505**. Until
then Sulveig is reachable only by ship.

See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Myrrhina]], in the [[Tessara]] system.
