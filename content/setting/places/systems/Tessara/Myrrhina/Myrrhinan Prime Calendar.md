A mathematical calendar created by ancient dragon scholars on Myrrhina, built entirely around prime numbers as a philosophical statement about indivisibility and fundamental truth.

## Overview

The Myrrhinan Calendar is perhaps the most mathematically elegant timekeeping system in the known multiverse. Created by the ancient dragon civilization that once dominated Myrrhina's southeastern continent, every aspect of this calendar revolves around prime numbers--those fundamental building blocks of mathematics that cannot be divided.

## Calendar Structure

- **Year Length**: 251 days (prime!)
- **Months**: 7 months with prime-number lengths: 23, 29, 31, 37, 41, 43, 47 days
- **Weeks**: Variable length (3, 5, 7, or 11 days--all prime)
- **Day Length**: 24 hours
- **Leap Days**: None--the ancient dragons adjusted Myrrhina's orbit to be exactly 251 days

## The Three Moons

Myrrhina is orbited by three moons, each with a prime-number orbital period:
- **Janeus**: 17-day cycle (emerald-hued)
- **Longolok**: 11-day cycle (amber-hued)
- **Talmlax**: 7-day cycle (fuchsia-hued)

The moons converge every 1,309 days (7×11×17), approximately every 5.2 years--an event of tremendous mathematical and religious significance.

## Variable Week Structure

Unlike most calendars with fixed week lengths, the Myrrhinan system uses variable weeks that follow a prime-number pattern within each month:

- **Month One** (23 days): 5, 5, 3, 5, 5
- **Month Two** (29 days): 5, 7, 5, 7, 5
- **Month Three** (31 days): 7, 5, 7, 5, 7
- **Month Four** (37 days): 7, 7, 11, 5, 7
- **Month Five** (41 days): 7, 11, 5, 11, 7
- **Month Six** (43 days): 7, 11, 7, 11, 7
- **Month Seven** (47 days): 11, 7, 11, 7, 11

## Philosophical Significance

The dragons believed that prime numbers represent "atomic truths"--fundamental units of reality that cannot be broken down further. By building their entire calendar system around primes, they expressed a deep philosophy about the nature of existence itself.

The variable week lengths force constant adaptation, reflecting the dragon philosophy that "constant change is the only constant." This stands in stark contrast to the rigid, predictable calendars of most humanoid civilizations.

## The Great Orbital Adjustment

Perhaps the most remarkable aspect of this calendar is that it requires no leap days. According to dragon historians, the ancient draconic civilization literally adjusted Myrrhina's orbit around its star to achieve exactly 251 days per year. This astronomical engineering feat is considered the greatest achievement of their civilization--they made reality conform to their mathematics, not the other way around.

## Modern Usage

When humanoid settlers from Xuartlek arrived on Myrrhina, they adopted the existing calendar system. While the mathematical complexity initially proved challenging, the system's elegance won converts among scholars and mages. Today, both the dragon enclaves of the southeastern continent and the humanoid settlements of the northwestern isthmus use this calendar.

## Prime Convergence

Myrrhinans celebrate rare occasions when the calendar date, month number, day-of-week position, and all three lunar phases simultaneously align to prime numbers. Called "Prime Convergence," these events occur at irregular intervals and are treated as holidays of tremendous significance--perfect moments when mathematical order harmonizes across all temporal cycles.
