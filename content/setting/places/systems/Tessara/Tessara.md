## Description

A young star system where quantum physics operates at planetary scale--home to Myrrhina, Xuartlek's newest frontier world, and the impossible phenomenon known as "Schrödinger's Triangle" where Lagrange points flicker in and out of existence like half-remembered dreams.

## Naming Conventions

* **Tessara**: Greek τέσσαρα = "four," referencing the four major orbital bodies in the system

## Full Description

The Tessara System represents the frontier of Xuartlek's expansion--both geographically and scientifically. It is a young system by astronomical standards, with orbital dynamics that haven't fully settled into the elegant predictability of older stellar neighborhoods. But youth alone cannot explain what happens at the L4 and L5 Lagrange points.

The "Ghost Triangles," as frontier scientists call them, exist in quantum superposition at macroscopic scale. Gravitational signatures show mass flickering in and out of existence over days and weeks. Sometimes moon-sized objects occupy these positions; sometimes nothing. Multiple observers occasionally report different states simultaneously. The phenomenon violates every model of physics that Xuartlek's scholars have developed across millennia of interstellar study.

Dragons navigate by these points regardless of quantum state. The great wyrms who inhabited Myrrhina before Xuartlek's arrival fly patterns that seem to trace probability waves invisible to other observers. They know something--but they aren't telling.

The system's sole inhabited world, Myrrhina, represents Xuartlek's newest permanent colony. The Sulveig settlement is small but growing, a mix of pioneers, scholars studying the quantum phenomenon, and adventurers drawn to untamed wilderness.
