Ciliren is a Xuartlek Low City District on Strafmack, positioned in a mid-elevation valley that remains habitable year-round.

Founded in **430**, its primary portal opens onto **Østre Havn**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Strafmack]], in the [[Strathis]] system.
