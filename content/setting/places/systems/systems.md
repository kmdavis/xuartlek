## Archaelon

*Post-apocalyptic megastructure graveyard*

- **[[Archaelon|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Profugae]] - world of refugees beneath orbital ruins

**Key Features:**
- Single inhabited planet surrounded by debris of cannibalized sibling planets
- Ruined orbital ring infrastructure visible as glittering debris field
- Gutted gas giant Vastitas stripped for fuel
- Survivors live underground after the Cascade destroyed surface cities
- A tomb of a civilization that consumed everything to build paradise, then lost it all

**The Myth:** Ambition shattered into orbiting debris--a cautionary tale of hubris.

## Belcanto

*Italian opera and inverted dragon mythology*

- **[[Belcanto|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Tertara]] - a world shaped by dragons
- **Xuartlek District**: Novorzhev

**Key Features:**
- Three chromatic dragons (Rubino, Zaffiro, Giada) cooperated to create Tertara, became guardian moons
- Three jealous metallic dragons tried to create rival worlds alone, failed, imprisoned in outer planets
- Inverts D&D's chromatic=evil/metallic=good paradigm
- Two artificial geometric configurations: lunar triad + planetary triad

**The Myth:** Cooperation creates greatness; pride and isolation create failure.

## Calderon

*Germanic engineering religion*

- **[[Calderon|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Langsevain]] - world of telescope-builders
- **Xuartlek District**: Khudealine

**Key Features:**
- Tidally-locked moon Grimnir with polar crater containing thick atmosphere
- Geometric patterns visible on moon surface (actually natural crystalline formations)
- Entire culture built around observing the "divine workshop" through telescopes
- Once every generation, clouds part to reveal "lights and structures"
- The Locked Mystery drives all technological innovation

**The Myth:** Grimnir the Smith-God ascended to become the moon, still forging blueprints of reality.

## Chelon

*Origin of Xuartlek (lost system)*

- **[[Chelon|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Tortleheim]] - home of the Skyy Tortles

**Key Features:**
- Birthplace of Xuartlek and the Skyy Tortle race
- First system in Xuartlek's journey
- Lost system: no portal connections (portals invented later at Arborisle)
- Xuartlek never returns to a star he has previously visited
- Floating islands and endless skies

**The Myth:** Where everything began--and can never be returned to.

## Eirion

*Norse frost moon*

- **[[Eirion|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Hrimgard]] - frost moon orbiting gas giant Ymir
- **Xuartlek Districts**: Jorkarith, Hallgerd

**Key Features:**
- Hrimgard orbits the gas giant Ymir, which fills 1/4 of the sky
- Tidal heating from Ymir keeps the moon habitable
- 14-day orbital month, marked by Eclipses (passage through Ymir's shadow)
- Dwarves (45%), Giants (30%), Goblins (25%)
- Cultural themes: Norse fantasy, melancholy poetry, saga-keeping, ancestral reverence

**The Myth:** Ymir is the transformed primordial giant whose life-warmth keeps Hrimgard alive.

## Lumiere

*Pulp sci-fi without magic*

- **[[Lumiere|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Emerraine]] - technological world

**Key Features:**
- Magic doesn't work in this system (Lodestone Ring disrupts magical substrate)
- Inter-war technology level (WWII equivalent): tanks, monoplanes, radar, early computers
- Xuartlek's portals create "backdoor access" to magic in Low City districts only
- Unique transhipment economy: electrical tech fails in magic zones, magical propulsion fails outside
- Psychology instead of enchantment, radio instead of sending stones

**The Myth:** The Lodestone Ring's exotic matter from an ancient magnetar suppresses magic (scientists believe).

## Shamsara

*Persian binary planets and water scarcity*

- **[[Shamsara|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Khashayar]] - desert world with 180-hour days

**Key Features:**
- Binary planet system: Khashayar and Nushirvan orbit their common barycenter
- 180-hour days create lethal heat/cold cycles with brief twilight windows
- Water is currency, more valuable than gold
- Ifrit (heat-immune) and Undine (cold-resistant) are elite laborers
- Nushirvan visible as large indigo disc, icy rings cast shadows

**The Myth:** Brother-kings Khashayar and Nushirvan betrayed each other simultaneously; the gods cursed one with eternal thirst (desert), one with monster-filled oceans.

## Shenzhou

*Chinese celestial dragon*

- **[[Shenzhou|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Qigang]] - ringed world with six moons

**Key Features:**
- Six co-orbital moons (dragon's scales) clustered in 110° arc, ~30-day shared orbit
- Six-band ring system (dragon's body) matching Chinese elemental system
- ~20,000 years ago: binary moon system shattered, creating current configuration
- 432-day year, 6-day weeks, 72-year grand cycle
- Metal Moon (Jīn Yuè) is brightest--the original moon's iron/nickel core

**The Myth:** Tian Long (Celestial Dragon) was slain by ancient heroes; its body became the rings, its scales became the moons.

## Strathis

*Scottish highlands and transhumance*

- **[[Strathis|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Strafmack]] - world of clans and septs
- **Xuartlek District**: Ciliren

**Key Features:**
- Highly eccentric orbit creates multi-year "good years" (perihelion) and "hard years" (aphelion)
- Transhumance economy: clans migrate between valley, mid-slope, and plateau settlements
- Frozen warehouses carved into rock--sacred clan patrimony
- Multi-species clans: Dwarves (forges), Goliaths (herders), Halflings (farming), Owlin (scouts), Minotaurs (labor)
- Meat runs in winter are coming-of-age tradition

**The Myth:** Survival demands cooperation across species; purity is foolish luxury.

## Sylvoria

*Celtic druidic forests*

- **[[Sylvoria|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Arborisle]] - cool forested world
- **Xuartlek District**: Parthenay

**Key Features:**
- Celtic/Gaelic naming throughout
- Cool forested world at outer edge of habitable zone, perpetual mists
- Living moon Boskara glows eerily green (seeded by ancient elves)
- Tiroedd is uninhabited despite Earth-like conditions--genuine mystery

**The Myth:** The First Tree's roots reach through reality; where they touch, life flourishes. Boskara is a paradise-in-waiting (actually deadly hostile).

## Tessara

*Quantum frontier with dragons*

- **[[Tessara|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Myrrhina]] - frontier world of aromatic forests
- **Xuartlek District**: Sulveig

**Key Features:**
- Young system with unsettled orbital dynamics
- "Ghost Triangles" at Lagrange points: quantum superposition at planetary scale
- Gravitational signatures show moon-sized objects flickering in and out of existence
- Dragons navigate by these points regardless of quantum state
- Xuartlek's newest permanent colony

**The Myth:** Dragons know something about the quantum phenomenon--but they aren't telling.

---

## Tyros

*Phoenician maritime*

- **[[Tyros|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Shubae]] - maritime world

**Key Features:**
- Chaotic moon system: three moons in wildly different orbits (prograde, polar, retrograde)
- Captured rogue planet Molokhon in eccentric 8.7-year orbit
- The Turning (twice yearly): all three moons converge, causing extreme tides
- The Drowning (every 17-18 years): Molokhon's close approach + The Turning = souls of drowned return

**The Myth:** Three Sister navigator-priestesses sacrificed themselves to chain the chaos god Yammon in gas giant prison. Molokhon was his final curse--an undead world caught mid-strike.

---

## Umbra

*Light vs death god invasion*

- **[[Umbra|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Mortuus Rex]] - world under siege

**Key Features:**
- 1,000 years ago: rogue planet Sanguis arrived carrying a death god
- Original moon (home to light god) shattered into debris field Fractum
- Light god fell to surface, wounded but not destroyed--sanctifies single peninsula
- Everything outside the Bastion belongs to the dead
- Sanguis and Fractum always opposite: when one rises, the other sets

**The Myth:** Darkness invaded from between the stars. The light god's sacrifice maintains humanity's last refuge.

---

## Vortalis

*Portal tech birthplace (lost system)*

- **[[Vortalis|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Furitas]] - world of tragedy

**Key Features:**
- Second stop on Xuartlek's journey (after Xuar)
- Where portal technology was conceived--but breakthrough came too late
- Xuartlek departed before the technology was ready
- Lost system: portals invented at Arborisle, after Vortalis was already unreachable
- The inventors of portal technology could never use it to reconnect their birthplace

---

## Xuar

*Xuartlek's birth star (lost system)*

- **[[Xuar|System Lore]]**
- **Primary World**: [[Kaanos]] - world of unknown characteristics

**Key Features:**
- The star Xuartlek was named for (Tortlian tradition: name begins with birth star)
- First destination after leaving Tortleheim--curiosity to see his namesake star
- Xuartlek discourages questions about his time here
- Lost system: no portal connections
