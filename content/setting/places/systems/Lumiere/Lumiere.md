LUMIERE SYSTEM - Science fantasy pulp, inter-war technology, no magic

THE LODESTONE RING - EMERRAINE'S SCIENTIFIC THEORY:
Billions of years ago, an ancient magnetar (ultra-magnetic neutron star) passed through
the Lumiere system on a near-collision trajectory. Tidal forces during the close approach
stripped away the magnetar's outer layers, leaving behind a trail of neutron-degenerate
matter and heavily magnetized metallic debris. This exotic material settled into a
horseshoe orbit at Emerraine's orbital radius--sharing the planet's orbit but oscillating
ahead and behind it in a gravitational dance governed by resonance mechanics.

The ring generates a persistent magnetic field (5-8 Tesla on average) with exotic quantum
properties. Emerraine's physicists believe these properties fundamentally disrupt the
metaphysical substrate underlying magic, preventing both arcane and divine magic from
functioning anywhere in the Lumiere system.

Emerraine passes through thinner sections of the ring on a decades-long cycle, creating
variable but predictable magnetic field strength across the planet. Scientists have
carefully mapped these cycles and use them to predict aurora intensity, radio interference,
and gravitational perturbations. The ring itself is barely visible to the naked eye (dark
neutron-degenerate matter with minimal luminosity), but its effects are unmistakable:
spectacular auroras, specific-frequency radio emissions, subtle gravitational lensing, and
persistent electromagnetic interference that drove the development of sophisticated
shielding technology.

THE ACTUAL TRUTH - THE VEIL:
The Lodestone Ring is real, and it does generate magnetic fields and cause auroras. But
it doesn't suppress magic--it's a convenient natural phenomenon that masks the true cause.

Buried in the ring's horseshoe orbit, hidden among the neutron-degenerate fragments, is
an ancient artifact known only in precursor records as "The Veil." It was built roughly
8,000-10,000 years ago by mortal magic-users (likely an ancient Emerraine civilization or
visitors from another world) in response to a magical catastrophe--war, runaway magical
experiment, or dimensional breach that threatened to destroy Emerraine.

The Veil functions as a metaphysical blockade. It doesn't generate a suppression field--
instead, it severs Emerraine's connection to the underlying source/dimension/substrate
from which magic draws power. Think of it as cutting a world off from the magical "grid."
The entire Lumiere system exists in a metaphysical dead zone created by The Veil.

The artifact is:
- Roughly moon-sized (2,000-3,000 km), but mostly hollow/lattice structure
- Camouflaged among ring debris--appears as just another dark, magnetized mass
- Self-sustaining (draws power from stellar radiation and magnetic fields)
- Probably irreversible without destroying it (which would have catastrophic side effects)
- Not detectable by Emerraine's current technology (requires metaphysical sensors)
- May have been placed deliberately in the magnetar debris field specifically to hide it

HOW XUARTLEK'S PORTALS WORK:
Xuartlek's portals are dimensional tunnels that pierce through spacetime. When a portal
opens to Emerraine, it creates a connection that bypasses The Veil entirely--the tunnel
reaches "under" or "around" the blockade, connecting directly to another world's magical
substrate.

This creates "backdoor access" to magic:
- Each portal channels magical energy from its destination world
- Magic "leaks" from the portal into the surrounding area, creating a functional zone
- Multiple portals in close proximity create overlapping zones that reinforce each other
- Each Low City district has 3 portals:
* 1 primary portal to Xuartlek (the flying city)
* 2 secondary portals to other connected worlds
- With 3 portals, the combined magical zones create stable coverage across entire district
- Zone size per portal: ~2-3 mile radius of full magical function
* Zone edge is somewhat fuzzy--magic weakens gradually from 2-3 miles to 4-5 miles
* Beyond ~5 miles from nearest portal: The Veil's blockade dominates, magic fails
- Low City districts are built around portals primarily for economic reasons (trade), but
the magical coverage is a crucial side benefit that enables magical commerce and services
- Emerraine citizens living in or visiting Low City districts can learn and use magic
(their connection to substrate is restored while in the zones)
- The more portals to different worlds, the stronger and more stable the magical field
- Xuartlek itself (the flying city) has portals to many worlds--at time of Emerraine
contact, 6+ worlds were already connected, providing enough overlapping magical substrate
to sustain an entire city-sized magical zone

UNIQUE LOGISTICAL CHALLENGE - ELECTRICAL/MAGICAL INTERFERENCE:
Emerraine's electrical technology and magical fields interfere with each other destructively:
- Electrical systems malfunction or fail completely within magical zones
- Magical propulsion (airships, magical engines) fails outside magical zones
- This creates a unique transhipment problem not seen in other Low City districts

On other worlds:
- Magical airships pass through portals, pay tolls, continue to local ports
- Local ships/vehicles can enter Low City districts without issue

On Emerraine:
- Magical airships CANNOT leave the magical zones (no substrate = no propulsion)
- Emerraine electrical trains/vehicles CANNOT enter magical zones reliably
- EVERYTHING must be transhipped at the magical boundary

The transhipment system:
- Railway depots positioned just outside magical zone boundaries (~3-4 miles from portals)
- Cargo transferred from trains to non-electrical wagons (horse-drawn, steam-powered)
- Wagons haul cargo through "dead zone" where neither tech nor magic work reliably
- Within magical zones: airship docks, magical warehouses, portal facilities
- Reverse process for outbound cargo

Consequences:
- Rodograd and Rovigo have MASSIVE warehousing districts (larger than any other Low City)
- Significantly larger populations (warehouse workers, teamsters, cargo handlers, inspectors)
- Complex logistics: timing, weather, coordination between magical and technological transport
- Higher costs: double handling, storage, dedicated transhipment infrastructure
- Economic opportunity: jobs in transhipment that don't exist elsewhere
- Emerraine has developed specialized "boundary zone" technologies: clockwork mechanisms,
steam power, purely mechanical systems that work in the transition zone

IMPLICATIONS:
- Emerraine's scientists are WRONG about why magic doesn't work (magnetar theory)
- The truth is hidden in plain sight (The Veil camouflaged in the ring)
- Ancient Emerraine civilization had advanced magic, then deliberately cut themselves off
- Historical records from that era are lost/destroyed--oral traditions might survive as myth
- If The Veil were destroyed or deactivated, magic would return system-wide
- Xuartlek may not even know about The Veil--he just knows his portals work somehow
- Discovery of The Veil would be a campaign-defining revelation

NAMING CONVENTIONS:
- System: "Lumiere" (French: "light") - Named by early astronomers for the spectacular
auroras caused by the Lodestone Ring
- Planet: "Emerraine" (from "emerger" + "raine" = emerging/rising + queen) - The world
that rose to technological prominence despite (or because of) magic's absence
- Districts: Eastern European/Italian names (Rodograd = "birth city", Rovigo = Italian city)
- Tech progression over 210 years (290 XI - 500 XI "present day"):
* 290 XI: Early 19th century - Steam locomotives just starting, industrial revolution
in full swing, coal-powered factories, gas lighting, telegraph emerging
* 390 XI (~100 years later): WWI-equivalent - Exposure to magic via Xuartlek showed
what was possible, accelerating development. Early tanks, automobiles, machine guns,
biplanes, radio communication established
* 500 XI (present): WWII-equivalent - Progress slowed after initial burst. Modern tanks,
monoplanes with metal construction, early jet engine experiments, radar, more
sophisticated radio, early computers (electromechanical), improved metallurgy

SYSTEM STRUCTURE:
- Lumiere (G2V star): 1,400,000 km diameter, yellow, similar to Sol
- Inner rocky worlds (2-3 small planets closer to star)
- Emerraine (habitable): ~365-day orbit, Earth-like world, 12,600 km diameter
- The Lodestone Ring: Horseshoe orbit co-orbital with Emerraine
- 1-2 small moons
- Outer system: Gas giants and ice worlds (standard configuration)

THE SCIENTIFIC REALITY:
The Lodestone Ring is exactly what Emerraine's scientists believe it to be--exotic matter
from a magnetar flyby. There is no hidden mythology here, no ancient gods or precursor
facilities. This is a world where magic genuinely doesn't exist, where the universe
operates on purely physical principles, and where technological progress follows rational
scientific development. The ring's magnetic field doesn't "suppress" magic so much as
reveal its absence--magic never worked here because the substrate for it was disrupted
during the system's formation.

This makes Emerraine unique: while other worlds mix science and sorcery, Emerraine's
people developed psychology instead of enchantment, radio instead of sending stones,
aviation instead of levitation. When Xuartlek's portals opened, Emerraine's scientists
faced an existential crisis--magic works everywhere else. The implications are still being
debated in their universities.
