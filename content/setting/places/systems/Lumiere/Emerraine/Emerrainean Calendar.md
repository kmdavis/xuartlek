## Description

A reformed calendar rebuilt from the ashes of a lost magical civilization--18 sacred months preserved through millennia of chaos, their original meanings forgotten but their structure too perfect to abandon.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Emerraine]] in the Lumiere System
- **Theme**: Fallen magical civilization, reformed knowledge, lost meanings
- **Year Length**: 337 days (18 months + 4 seasonal intercalaries)
- **Day Length**: 23.1 hours

## Historical Context

### The Collapse
When The Veil severed Emerraine's connection to magic approximately 8,000-10,000 years ago, the ancient Spheric Calendar fell apart. Different regions added intercalary days differently, and within decades, communities were months out of sync.

### The Reformation
After millennia of chaos and reconstruction, scholars created this reformed calendar approximately 6,000 years ago, preserving what survived in oral tradition while adding scientific precision.

### What Survived
- 18 month names (corrupted but recognizable)
- 9-day week structure (because 18 ÷ 9 = 2 is undeniably perfect)
- 4 seasonal markers (astronomical necessity)
- The concept of a ~337-day year

### What Was Lost
- Month meanings (nobody knows they were originally planar names)
- "The Convergence" (9 void days) distributed into months as 19-day months
- Day name etymologies simplified from ancient Latin forms
- The magical significance of calendar alignments

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 9 days (preserved because 18 ÷ 9 = 2 is perfect)
- **Months**: 18 months, alternating 18 and 19 days
- **Intercalary Days**: 4 (solstices and equinoxes)
- **Base Year**: 337 days

### Week Structure
- 18-day months: Two perfect 9-day weeks
- 19-day months: 10-day week + 9-day week (creates a 3-day weekend effect in middle)

### Month Pattern
18/19/18/19 → spring equinox → 18/19/18/19/18 → summer solstice → 19/18[+leap]/19/18 → autumn equinox → 19/18/19/18/19 → winter solstice

### Two Moons

### Leap Year System
- Actual year: 337.36 days
- Calendar year: 337 days (338 in leap years)
- Drift: +0.36 days per year
- Leap rule: Add 1 day to month 11 (Eros) approximately every 3 years
- Precise formula: 25 leap days per 69 years

## Cultural Significance

### The Sacred 18
The number 18 appears throughout Emerrainean culture--18 months, 18-day base month, every 18-day month containing exactly 2 nine-day weeks. Scholars debate why 18 was so significant to the ancient civilization, but the mathematics are too elegant to be coincidental.

### Lost Knowledge
Modern Emerraineans know their calendar was reformed from something older and more complex, but the original meanings are lost. Some scholars theorize the 18 months were named for planar connections, others for divine aspects, still others for magical principles. The truth remains buried in ruins that few dare excavate.

### The Convergence Mystery
Ancient texts mention "The Convergence"--nine days that existed outside normal time. The reformed calendar distributed these void days into the month structure as 19-day months, but the original purpose of The Convergence has been forgotten.
