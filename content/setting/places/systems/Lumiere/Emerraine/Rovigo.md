Rovigo is a quieter Xuartlek Low City District where Emerraine\'s artists and philosophers grapple with the implications of a magical multiverse.

Founded in **350**, its primary portal opens onto **Østre Havn**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Emerraine]], in the [[Lumiere]] system.
