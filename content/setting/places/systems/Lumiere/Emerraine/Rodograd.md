Rodograd, the "Birth City," is a Xuartlek Low City District where Emerraine\'s engineers and physicists collaborate to understand portal mechanics in a magic-dead zone.

Founded in **290**, its primary portal opens onto **Docaichean a Deas**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Emerraine]], in the [[Lumiere]] system.
