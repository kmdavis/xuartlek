In a world where magic simply doesn't exist, diesel engines roar and radio waves carry voices across continents--until Xuartlek's portals opened and proved that everywhere else in the multiverse, the impossible is commonplace, leaving scientists to question whether they live in reality's only dead zone or if something deliberately severed their connection to the fundamental forces that power every other world.

Latin and European influences reflecting industrial-era Earth parallels:
* **Lumiere**: The star (French = "light"--ironic given magic's absence)
* **Emerraine**: The world (French émeraude = emerald + terrain, "Emerald Land")
* **Kairos**: Inner moon (Greek = "opportune moment")
* **Chronos**: Outer moon (Greek = "time")
* **The Lodestone Ring**: Debris field explanation for magic-dead zone
* **Nordheim**: Northern industrial continent (German-influenced naming)
* **Australis**: Southern Mediterranean continent (Latin = "southern")
* **Occidentia**: Western resource continent (Latin = "western")
* **Orientum**: Eastern ancient civilization continent (Latin = "eastern")
* **Rodograd**: First Xuartlek district (Slavic naming convention)
* **Rovigo**: Second Xuartlek district (Italian naming)
* Calendar: Solar calendar with 365.25-day year (Earth-like)

The streets of Emerraine's cities hum with diesel engines and electric motors, their modernist buildings rising alongside older industrial architecture. Here, progress is measured not in mastered spells but in patents filed, in the steady march from propeller biplanes to all-metal monoplanes, from vacuum tubes to early radar, from theoretical physics to applied engineering. Experimental jet engines roar on test stands while theoretical physicists debate quantum mechanics in university halls.

Without magic, Emerraine's people developed differently. Psychology replaced enchantment for understanding the mind. Radio networks span continents where other worlds use sending stones. Vaccines and antibiotics save lives where divine healing is unavailable. Universities teach physics and chemistry, not thaumaturgy. The result is a world of rational inquiry, empirical testing, and technological innovation--a civilization built entirely on science because nothing else worked.

The culture reflects this scientific worldview. Emerraine's nations compete through industrial capacity and technological advancement rather than magical arms races. Their wars--and there have been wars--are fought with machine guns and aircraft, artillery and gas masks, rather than fireballs and summoned creatures. A devastating continental war a generation ago reshaped their world, leaving scars that still influence politics and driving an uneasy peace maintained through mutual exhaustion and fear of renewed industrialized slaughter.

Then Xuartlek came. In 290 XI (Xuartlek Imperial), the flying city's surveyors identified an uninhabited coastal site and began construction of Rodograd. Five years later, the first portal opened--an impossible doorway to a city where magic was as common as electricity. Within Rodograd's boundaries, magic worked. Emerraine citizens discovered they could learn spells, clerics from other worlds received their divine powers, enchanted items functioned normally.

Sixty-five years after Xuartlek's arrival, a second district arose. Rovigo's founding in 350 XI presented unique challenges--Xuartlek had departed Emerraine thirty years earlier, meaning the portal anchor had to be transported thousands of miles from Rodograd through purely non-magical means across a magic-dead world. The engineering feat required steam locomotives, specially-designed transport vehicles, and five years of careful transit before Rovigo's portal could be completed in 355 XI. Each district now contains three portals: one connecting to Xuartlek, two more linking to other worlds. The portals' overlapping zones create stable magical coverage across the entire settlement--but beyond the district boundaries, magic fails as it always had.

The implications shake Emerraine's scientific establishment to its core. If the Lodestone Ring suppresses magic system-wide, how do the portals bypass its effects? Leading theories include: portal anchors generating counter-fields that cancel the ring's effects; portals somehow "importing" magical substrate from connected worlds; multiple portals creating resonance that locally restores metaphysical connection; or--most disturbing--that the magnetar theory is wrong entirely.

In Rodograd, engineers and physicists obsessively study the portal boundaries, mapping the exact gradient of magical function, measuring how the three portals' effects overlap and reinforce each other. They've documented that magic strengthens near portal intersections, weakens at district edges, and fails completely a few miles beyond the outermost portal. In Rovigo, philosophers debate whether magic is a fundamental force or something stranger--perhaps not a property of physics at all, but of connection to something Emerraine lost access to long ago.

The Lodestone Ring still dominates their astronomy. Observatories track its position obsessively, calculating gravitational effects, predicting aurora cycles, analyzing the exotic matter fragments. But now they wonder: is the ring really the cause? Or is it merely a convenient explanation for something far older, something deliberately hidden among those glittering fragments?

**The Lodestone Ring (Scientific Theory):**

Billions of years ago, an ancient magnetar (ultra-magnetic neutron star) passed through the Lumiere system on a near-collision trajectory. Tidal forces during the close approach stripped away the magnetar's outer layers, leaving behind a trail of neutron-degenerate matter and heavily magnetized metallic debris. This exotic material settled into a horseshoe orbit at Emerraine's orbital radius--sharing the planet's orbit but oscillating ahead and behind it in a gravitational dance governed by resonance mechanics.

**Ring Properties:**
* Horseshoe orbit co-orbital with Emerraine
* Composed of neutron-degenerate matter and heavily magnetized metallic debris
* Generates persistent magnetic field (5-8 Tesla on average)
* Barely visible to naked eye (dark matter with minimal luminosity)
* 73-year cycle as Emerraine passes through ring's full variation in magnetic field strength

**Observable Effects:**
* Spectacular auroras in greens, blues, purples--visible even in temperate latitudes
* Specific-frequency radio emissions
* Subtle gravitational lensing effects during solar eclipses
* Persistent electromagnetic interference (drove development of sophisticated shielding technology)
* Aurora intensity peaks at closest approach, dims at farthest separation
* Current cycle: Approaching maximum (auroras growing brighter and more frequent)

**Moons:**
* **Kairos** (inner moon): 18-day orbital period, 800 km diameter, causes small tides
* **Chronos** (outer moon): 47-day orbital period, 1200 km diameter, more prominent in night sky

**Portal Magic Zones:**
Each Low City district (Rodograd and Rovigo) has three portals:
* One primary portal connecting to Xuartlek
* Two secondary portals linking to other worlds
* Each portal creates a ~2-3 mile radius zone where magic functions normally
* Zones overlap to cover entire district settlements
* Magic strengthens at portal intersections, weakens at district edges
* Beyond ~5 miles from nearest portal: Magic fails completely

**The Great Mystery:**
How do the portals bypass the Lodestone Ring's effects? Emerraine's scientists have no definitive answer. The most popular theories:
1. Portal anchors generate counter-fields canceling the ring's suppression
2. Portals "import" magical substrate from connected worlds
3. Multiple portals create resonance that locally restores metaphysical connection
4. The magnetar theory is fundamentally wrong

**Species (Pathfinder 2E):**
* **Humans** (60%): Dominant species controlling most governments and institutions. Diverse cultures across continents united by scientific worldview. Germanic and Slavic cultural influences in Nordheim region; Mediterranean in Australis region.
* **Gnomes** (25%): Primarily charisma gnomes (natural tinkerers) and sensate gnomes (precision engineers). Without magical innovation to satisfy gnomish need for novelty, they channel obsessive energy into technological breakthroughs. Responsible for vacuum tube improvements, diesel engine refinements, early aviation advances. Some suffer "Bleaching" when technological progress stagnates.
* **Goblins** (15%): Primarily charhide goblins (foundry workers, engine operators) and snow goblins (refrigeration technicians). Goblin fearlessness makes them natural test pilots, experimental subjects, and industrial workers. Goblin chemists made breakthrough discoveries in petrochemicals and explosives. Absence of magic leveled social playing field--goblins valued for technical skill.

**Magic Level:** Absolutely None
* Neither arcane nor divine magic functions anywhere in Lumiere system
* Clerics receive no spells, wizards' formulas produce no effects, sorcerers have no innate powers
* This is not suppression--it's complete absence
* Exception: Within Low City district portal zones, magic functions normally

**Impact of Portal Discovery:**
* Created existential crisis among rationalist philosophers
* Evidence that magic works elsewhere challenges fundamental assumptions about reality
* Accelerated technological development through exposure to magical concepts
* Spawned new field of "metaphysical physics" trying to understand magic as natural phenomenon
* Ongoing debate: Is Emerraine's pure materialism correct, or is magic real and Emerraine somehow cut off?

**Trade & Transhipment Challenge:**
* Unique problem: Electrical and magical systems interfere destructively
* Electrical trains/vehicles fail in magical zones
* Magical airships cannot function outside magical zones
* ALL cargo must be transhipped at zone boundaries
* Railway depots positioned 3-4 miles from portals (outside magical influence)
* Horse-drawn and steam-powered wagons haul cargo through transition zone
* Airship docks and magical warehouses within zones near portals
* Double handling creates massive warehousing districts (larger than any other Low City)
* Higher costs but unique employment opportunities

**Xuartlek Connection:**
* **Rodograd** (Industrial/Scientific Quarter): Germanic/Slavic influences, engineering focus
* **Rovigo** (Philosophical/Artistic Quarter): Mediterranean influences, theoretical focus
* **Why Xuartlek Came**: Scientific curiosity and trade. Emerraine's technology valuable where magic is unreliable. Radio equipment, mechanical computers, precision instruments, medicines all trade well.
* **Trade Exports**: Radios, precision instruments, diesel engines, medicines (antibiotics, vaccines), psychological services, scientific consulting, mechanical calculators, optical equipment, industrial chemicals, technical documentation
* **Trade Imports**: Raw materials (rare earths, exotic metals from other worlds), magical artifacts for study, biological specimens, astronomical data, pure intellectual exchange
