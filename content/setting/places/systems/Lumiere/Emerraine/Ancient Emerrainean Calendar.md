The Spheric Calendar, also known as the Eighteen Spheres Calendar, was the timekeeping system used by ancient Emerraine's magical civilization approximately 8,000-10,000 years ago, before The Veil severed the world's connection to magic.

## Historical Context

This calendar directly mapped to the ancient understanding of the multiverse: 18 known planes of existence, each representing a fundamental aspect of reality. The calendar served both practical and mystical purposes, with each month dedicated to one plane's energies.

## Calendar Structure

- **18 months** of 18 days each = 324 days
- **4 seasonal intercalary days** (solstices & equinoxes)
- **9-day "Convergence"** week at year's end
- **Total: 337 days** (no leap days - the calendar drifted over centuries)

## The Magical Philosophy

Ancient Emerraine magic recognized 18 fundamental planes:

- **6 Elemental Planes** (Matter-Essence): Zephyra, Verdara, Aquara, Ferrumara, Petraea, Pyraxa
- **4 Inner Planes** (Life-Essence): Thanatos, Niflheim, Alfheim, Eros (Draumheim was unknown)
- **8 Outer Planes** (Spirit-Essence): The "corner" planes representing alignment extremes

Each month was dedicated to one plane, and magic-users would attune their rituals to the corresponding planar energy during that month. This created a natural rhythm to magical practice across the civilization.

## The Convergence

The final 9 days of the year were called "The Convergence" - a sacred week when all 18 planes aligned. This was when the most powerful and dangerous rituals were performed. Reality was considered thinnest during this time.

The Convergence days were:
1. **Silence Day** - No magic performed, fasting and preparation
2. **Preparation Day** - Final rituals before alignment
3. **First Alignment** - Minor planes begin alignment
4. **Second Alignment** - Elemental planes align
5. **Peak Alignment** - All 18 planes in perfect alignment (most powerful rituals)
6. **Third Alignment** - Alignment begins to fade
7. **Fourth Alignment** - Final major rituals
8. **Sealing Day** - Closing rituals completed
9. **Return Day** - Return to normal reality

## Astronomical Data

- 1 Emerraine year = 337.36 Emerraine days ≈ 324.78 Earth days
- 1 Emerraine day = 23.1 Earth hours
- 9-day weeks (based on magical phase theory)
- Two moons: Kairos (18.7-day cycle) and Chronos (48.8-day cycle)

## Why It Failed

The calendar had no leap day system. Ancient magic-users relied on magical attunement to feel when The Convergence should begin, allowing the calendar to drift naturally. By the time they realized the drift was problematic, catastrophe (The Veil activation) was imminent.

When The Veil severed magic, the calendar fell apart - different regions added intercalary days differently, and within decades, communities were months out of sync. This contributed to the fragmentation of post-Veil Emerrainean society and led to the eventual adoption of the modern Emerrainean calendar.
