Hallgerd is a Xuartlek Low City District on Hrimgard.

Founded in **275**, its primary portal opens onto **Sjeverna Luka**, one of the four rim harbours of [[The High City]]. See [[timeline|the imperial timeline]] for the founding sequence.

Part of [[Hrimgard]], in the [[Eirion]] system.
