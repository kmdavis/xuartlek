## Description

A calendar shaped by the rhythms of a tidally-heated moon--where each month begins with the gas giant's eclipse, and the short years mirror the brief but intense growing seasons of Hrimgard's harsh environment.

## Overview

- **World**: [[Hrimgard]], moon of gas giant Ymir in the Eirion System
- **Theme**: Norse/Viking fantasy, harsh survival, tidal heating
- **Year Length**: 112 days (8 months × 14 days)
- **Day Length**: 26 hours
- **Sidereal Year**: 30 Ymirian years = 9.2 Earth years (Ymir's orbit around Eirion)

## Structure

### Basic Parameters
- **Weeks**: 7 days (half a month)
- **Months**: 8 months of 14 days each
- **Monthly Eclipse**: Each month begins with Ymir's eclipse (4-8 hours of darkness)
- **Leap Years**: Every 3 years, except every 34th year

### Days of the Week (Old Norse)
1. **Sunnadagr**: Sun-day (first day, sun worship)
2. **Mánadagr**: Moon-day (Ymir's day)
3. **Týsdagr**: Tyr's day (war and justice god)
4. **Óðinsdagr**: Odin's day (wisdom and magic)
5. **Þórsdagr**: Thor's day (thunder and protection)
6. **Frjádagr**: Freya's day (love and fertility)
7. **Laugardagr**: Bath-day (cleansing before Sunnadagr)

### Months (Old Norse, 14 days each)
**Winter:**
1. **Fimbulvetr**: Great Winter (deep winter)
2. **Skammdegi**: Short Days (late winter)

**Spring:**
3. **Vorblót**: Spring Sacrifice (early spring)
4. **Gói**: Snow-month (late spring)

**Summer:**
5. **Miðsumar**: Midsummer (early summer)
6. **Heyannir**: Hay-gathering (late summer)

**Autumn:**
7. **Haustmánuður**: Harvest Month (early autumn)
8. **Gormánuður**: Slaughter Month (late autumn)

## Astronomical Context

### Orbital Dynamics
- Hrimgard orbits gas giant Ymir every 14 days (= 1 month)
- Ymir dominates the sky, filling 1/4 of the heavens overhead
- Ymir rises and sets every 7 days (Hrimgard's rotation period)
- Tidal heating from Ymir keeps the moon habitable despite distance from star

### Sister Moons
Two other moons of Ymir are visible:
- **Jotunheim**: 28-day orbital cycle
- **Niflheim**: 45-day orbital cycle

### The Eclipse Rhythm
Each month begins with Ymir's eclipse--when Hrimgard passes behind the gas giant, experiencing 4-8 hours of total darkness. This eclipse marks the formal beginning of each new month and is culturally significant.

## Seasons

Hrimgard's seasons are intense and brief, driven by tidal heating from Ymir:
- **Winter** (Fimbulvetr, Skammdegi): Harsh cold when Ymir's warmth is insufficient
- **Spring** (Vorblót, Gói): Ice breaks, life returns
- **Summer** (Miðsumar, Heyannir): Peak growing season
- **Autumn** (Haustmánuður, Gormánuður): Harvest and preparation

## Cultural Significance

### Coming of Age
Coming of age occurs at 60 Ymirian years old--two complete sidereal cycles (when Ymir completes two full orbits around Eirion). This represents a rare alignment that most children will witness only once in their lifetime during youth.

### The Great Year
The sidereal year (30 Ymirian years = 9.2 Earth years) represents a "Great Year" in cultural terms. Major festivals, marriages, and political appointments are often timed to sidereal milestones.

### Eclipse Rituals
The monthly eclipse at each month's beginning is a time for ritual, reflection, and new beginnings. Contracts signed during eclipse are considered especially binding.
