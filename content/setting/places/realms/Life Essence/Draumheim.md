## Description

A surreal realm where dreams and nightmares take physical form. Reality shifts like scenes in a half-remembered dream, while dream-logic governs the laws of physics. Memories float through the air like gossamer threads, and visitors find their thoughts reshaping the landscape. Cities built from collective dreams rise and fall like tides, while nightmare creatures stalk the ever-changing borders between imagination and reality.
