The five Inner Realms are the youngest planes in the cosmology--created not by the Far Realms' influence or the Elemental War, but by mortal consciousness itself. As mortals lived and died across millennia, their collective experiences wove these realms into existence.

## Cosmological Role

> *We watched with satisfaction as consciousness spread across the cosmos. The mortals built civilizations, created art, contemplated philosophy. And in their living and dying, they wove the Inner Realms--death and dream, shadow and fey--completing the cosmic architecture.*
> -- Septimus Librius Aequilibrius, Second Telling

The Inner Realms represent the fundamental experiences of mortal existence:
- Being born and dying
- Dreaming and waking
- The boundary between light and shadow
- Connection to the wild and natural

## The Five Realms

The Inner Realms form a single axis from life to death:

| Order | Plane | Domain | Created By |
|-------|-------|--------|------------|
| 1 | [[Eros]] | Life | The vital force; the will to exist |
| 2 | [[Alfheim]] | Fey | Nature's consciousness; the wild magic |
| 3 | [[Draumheim]] | Dreams | Where mortal minds wander in sleep |
| 4 | [[Niflheim]] | Shadow | The liminal spaces; neither here nor there |
| 5 | [[Thanatos]] | Death | The experience of mortality; where souls journey |

## The Life-Death Axis

The Inner Realms mirror the mortal journey from birth to death. The middle three realms--Alfheim, Draumheim, and Niflheim--have sideways connections to the Material Plane, representing states mortals experience during life.

## Thanatos and the Journey of Souls

When mortals die, their souls typically journey through the Inner Realms:
1. **Niflheim**: The threshold, the between-space
2. **Thanatos**: Where souls are judged or processed
3. **Outer Realms**: Final destination based on alignment/faith

Some souls linger in the Inner Realms:
- The restless dead in Niflheim's shadows
- Dream-travelers who never wake in Draumheim
- Those claimed by the fey in Alfheim

## The Niflheim Connection (Hrimgard)

On Hrimgard, the moon Niflheim may contain a natural portal to the Inner Realm of the same name. During Eclipses, the barrier between Material and Ethereal planes fluctuates--the "ancestors walking" may not be purely psychological.

## Relationship to Other Planes

The Inner Realms are "below" the Material Plane in the cosmic hierarchy:
- Created *by* mortal consciousness
- Sustained *by* mortal experience
- Would not exist without the Material Plane

This inverts their relationship with the Outer Realms:
- Outer Realms created mortals (according to the Second Telling)
- Inner Realms were created *by* mortals
- The cycle is complete: consciousness creates, and creation creates consciousness
