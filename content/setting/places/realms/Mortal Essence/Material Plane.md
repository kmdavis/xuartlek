## Description

The Material Plane is the central realm of physical existence--the universe of stars, planets, and mortal life. Here, the fundamental forces of physics govern reality, though magic weaves through the fabric of existence like threads through cloth. It is the crossroads of all planes, touching every essence and alignment through countless portals and thin boundaries.

## Planar Characteristics

The Material Plane operates by consistent physical laws modified by magical forces. Time flows linearly. Space behaves (mostly) according to geometry. Cause precedes effect. These constants make it a stable anchor point for the entire multiverse.

What distinguishes the Material Plane from transitive planes is the presence of life--not just magical constructs or elementals, but biological beings that grow, reproduce, and die. This cycle of birth and death connects the Material to both Eros (life) and Thanatos (death), while mortal choices connect it to the Spirit Essence planes.

## Star Systems

The Material Plane contains countless star systems, of which only a handful are known to Xuartlek's portal network:

**Known Systems** (with active portal connections):
- Archaelon, Belcanto, Calderon, Eirion, Lumiere, Shamsara, Shenzhou, Strathis, Sylvoria, Tessara, Tyros, Umbra

**Lost Systems** (no portal connections):
- Chelon (Xuartlek's origin), Xuar (his birth star), Vortalis (birthplace of portals)

## Xuartlek

The demigod Xuartlek serves as the living connection between these scattered worlds. His body--now the size of a city--drifts eternally between stars, carrying the High City within his shell. The portal network that connects his various district settlements allows trade, communication, and travel across light-years of space.

Each star system contains one Low City district connected to Xuartlek's High City through primary portals. Secondary portals connect Low City districts to each other, creating a web of civilized space amid the cosmic void.

## Connections

The Material Plane touches all other planes through:
- **Elemental Congruences**: Thin boundaries where matter-essence bleeds through (volcanic regions touch Pyraxa, deep oceans touch Aquara)
- **Shadow Crossings**: Points where Niflheim's darkness overlaps with material shadows
- **Fey Crossings**: Ancient sites where Alfheim's wild magic seeps into the world
- **Planar Portals**: Artificial or natural doorways to any plane, including Xuartlek's network
