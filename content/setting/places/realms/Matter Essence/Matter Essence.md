The six Elemental Realms are the oldest planes in existence, predating all others except the Far Realms. According to the First Telling (the Lament of Elements), they danced in perfect eternal harmony until the Wound-Makers came. According to the Second Telling, they were "existence without experience"--matter without meaning until Spirit gave them purpose.

## The Eternal Dance

In the time before time, the Elements knew only their spiral:

> *Fire and I, Water and Earth, Metal and Wood--we knew only our eternal spiral, each perfect in our essence, each whole in our being. We were matter. We were truth. We needed nothing beyond our dance.*
> -- The Winds of Zephyra

## The Six Elements

The elements are arranged as planetary layers, from outer atmosphere to inner core:

| Layer | Plane | Element | Role in Creation |
|-------|-------|---------|------------------|
| 1 (outer) | [[Zephyra]] | Air | Narrator of the Lament; tried to wrap around wounded siblings |
| 2 | [[Verdara]] | Wood | Growing things, life without consciousness |
| 3 | [[Aquara]] | Water | The endless flow; tried to soothe the wounded |
| 4 | [[Petraea]] | Earth | Tried to shield Metal; crushed together into spheres |
| 5 | [[Ferrumara]] | Metal | Tried to shield Earth; crushed together into spheres |
| 6 (inner) | [[Pyraxa]] | Fire | "Fire screamed as they tore her open, and her blood became the stars" |

## The Elemental War

The cosmic conflict that created the Material Plane:

- The Ancients (Spirit) "reached" for the Elements (Matter)
- The Elements called it war; the Ancients called it "education"
- Fire's wounds became stars--"each one a droplet of her agony frozen in the void"
- Earth and Metal were "crushed together into spheres of compromise" (planets)
- Air and Water wrapped around their wounded siblings (atmospheres and oceans)
- From the wounds leaked something new: mortal life

## The Broken Dance

> *We dance still, but it is a broken dance. Where once was perfection, now there is only the echo of what was lost. The stars burn with Fire's fever. The worlds spin with Earth's vertigo. The oceans remember when Water was whole.*
> -- The Winds of Zephyra

The Elements continue their eternal dance, but it is wounded. Every star is Fire's pain made visible. Every planet is Earth and Metal's forced union. The Material Plane exists because of their wounds.

## Elemental Layers

The six elements form concentric layers around planetary cores, following a Wuxing-inspired cycle where each layer feeds the next:

```

    │           ZEPHYRA (Air)             │  ← Outer atmosphere

    │  │  ╭─────────────────────────╮  │  │
    │  │  │      AQUARA (Water)     │  │  │  ← Oceans
    │  │  │  ╭───────────────────╮  │  │  │
    │  │  │  │   PETRAEA (Earth) │  │  │  │  ← Crust/mantle

    │  │  │  │  │ FERRUMARA   │  │  │  │  │  ← Outer core

    │  │  │  │  │  ╭───────╮  │  │  │  │  │
    │  │  │  │  │  │PYRAXA │  │  │  │  │  │  ← Inner core
```

The generative cycle: Fire feeds Earth, Earth produces Metal, Metal shapes Water, Water nourishes Wood, Wood fuels Air, and Air fans Fire.

## Paths to These Realms

The Elemental Realms can be reached through:
- **Elemental magic**: Summoning, planar travel
- **Natural portals**: Places where elemental essence bleeds through
- **The wounds**: The same punctures that created the Material Plane
