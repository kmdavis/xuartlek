Thirty-six planes in five essence groups. The cosmos layers from outer to inner:
the further out, the less a mortal can hold in their head at once.

There is no single creation myth. Three divine tellings compete, and mortal
cultures keep thousands more. That ambiguity is deliberate. See [[Cosmology]].
