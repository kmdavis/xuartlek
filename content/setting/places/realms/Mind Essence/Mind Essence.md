The eight Mind Essence planes are not merely "planes" in the traditional sense--they are **complete multiverses**, each with their own material plane, outer realms, inner realms, elemental planes, pantheons, and trillions of souls both mortal and divine.

## The Symmetric Truth

The relationship between multiverses is symmetric: **we are a Far Realm to them, just as they are Far Realms to us**. No multiverse can directly perceive another--they are fundamentally unknowable to each other.

But the collective unconscious of trillions of souls (mortal and immortal) from those other multiverses bleeds through. This subconscious pressure manifests in our reality as the **Ancients** and the structure of the **Outer Realms**.

## How Influence Works

Each Far Realm multiverse has its own complete cosmology. Their combined psychic weight--the aggregate consciousness of entire universes--projects into our reality. The Ancients aren't beings who "chose" their domains; they are what those domains *look like* when rendered into a form our cosmology can comprehend.

Think of it like this:
- A Far Realm is an ocean
- The Spirit Essence planes are shadows cast on our seabed
- The Ancients are what those shadows look like when we try to make sense of them

## The Eight Multiverses

The eight Far Realms are organized along two axes: Positive/Negative (how their influence manifests) and alignment (the nature of that influence).

```
              POSITIVE              NEGATIVE
         ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
    GOOD │   Aurelios   │   Puritas    │
         ├──────────────┼──────────────┤
   CHAOS │  Eudaimonia  │   Ruinara    │
         ├──────────────┼──────────────┤
    EVIL │  Maleficaris │   Nihilith   │
         ├──────────────┼──────────────┤
     LAW │  Justicaris  │   Dictatum   │
         └──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### Good Axis
- **[[Aurelios]]** (Positive Good) - A multiverse where benevolence triumphs
- **[[Puritas]]** (Negative Good) - A multiverse where good requires sacrifice

### Chaos Axis
- **[[Eudaimonia]]** (Positive Chaos) - A multiverse of joyful freedom
- **[[Ruinara]]** (Negative Chaos) - A multiverse of beautiful entropy

### Evil Axis
- **[[Maleficaris]]** (Positive Evil) - A multiverse of ambitious domination
- **[[Nihilith]]** (Negative Evil) - A multiverse of consuming void

### Law Axis
- **[[Justicaris]]** (Positive Law) - A multiverse of protective order
- **[[Dictatum]]** (Negative Law) - A multiverse of constraining rules

## What We Cannot Know

From our perspective, these multiverses are fundamentally unknowable:
- We cannot travel to them
- We cannot communicate with their inhabitants
- We can only perceive their influence, filtered through Spirit Essence

And from their perspective? We are equally mysterious--one of *their* Far Realms, our collective unconscious bleeding into their reality as incomprehensible Ancients.

## The Question No One Asks

If our collective unconscious influences them as theirs influences us... what are we manifesting in their Outer Realms? What Ancient rises in a distant multiverse, shaped by the dreams and beliefs of everyone who has ever lived in ours?
