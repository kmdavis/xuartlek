# Skyy Tortle  *Ancestry*

`Humanoid`  `Skyy Tortle`  `Rare`

Skyy Tortles hail from Tortleheim, in the Chelon system, and keep the Tortlian
calendar -- the oldest still in continuous use. They never stop growing and never
die of age. The eldest do not so much retire as slow to a stop, and their
descendants build villages on their backs.

- **Hit Points** 10
- **Size** Medium
- **Speed** 25 feet
- **Attribute Boosts** Constitution, Free
- **Languages** Common, Tortlian; plus one additional language for each point of
  Intelligence modifier

## Shell

Your shell is part of you and cannot be removed. It grants a **+1 item bonus to
AC**, has a **Dexterity modifier cap of +4**, and imposes no check penalty and no
speed penalty. You can't wear armor.

**You count as unarmored for every effect that cares** -- monk stances, *mage
armor*, an unarmored-defense proficiency, and anything else with the same
requirement.

Your shell can be etched with armor runes exactly as though it were armor, at the
normal prices. Tortles call the practice *scrimshaw*, and a well-marked shell is
read the way other peoples read a coat of arms.

## Claws

You have a claw unarmed attack that deals **1d4 slashing** damage. It's in the
brawling group and has the **agile**, **finesse** and **unarmed** traits.

## Hold Breath

You can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.

## Nature's Intuition

You're trained in one skill of your choice from Athletics, Medicine, Nature,
Stealth or Survival, and in Tortlian Lore.

## Perpetual Growth

You never stop growing and never die of old age. Player characters begin as young
adults; the later stages are the province of elders and of the villages built
upon them.

| Stage | Age | Size | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young | under 15 | Small | 25 feet |
| Young adult | 15–35 | Medium | 25 feet |
| Mature adult | 35–65 | Large | 20 feet |
| Elder | 65–100 | Huge | 15 feet |
| Ancient | 100+ | Gargantuan | 10 feet |

An ancient Skyy Tortle is effectively asleep, and moves only by flight. Villages
begin at roughly a century of age, when a shell can carry two or three huts; the
oldest carry dozens.

# Ancestry Feats

## Shell Defense  *Feat 1*  [one-action]

`Skyy Tortle`

You withdraw into your shell. You gain a **+2 circumstance bonus to AC** and a
**+2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves**, and you take a **–2 circumstance
penalty to Reflex saves**. You are **immobilized**, and the only action you can
take is Emerge.

**Emerge [one-action]** You come out of your shell, ending these effects.

## Slow and Steady  *Feat 1*

`Skyy Tortle`

You are not slowed by rough ground the way lighter folk are. You ignore the
first 5 feet of extra movement cost from difficult terrain, and you gain a +2
circumstance bonus to defend against attempts to Shove or Trip you.

## Tortlian Scrimshaw  *Feat 1*

`Skyy Tortle`

Your shell is your record. You're trained in Crafting, and can etch runes onto
your own shell without meeting the usual Crafting proficiency requirement,
though you still pay the cost and spend the time.

## Ancient Patience  *Feat 5*

`Skyy Tortle`

Your people measure a grudge in decades. Once per day, when you fail -- but do not
critically fail -- a saving throw against an effect that would make you
frightened, fleeing or controlled, you get a success instead.

## Cumbersome Flight  *Feat 9*

`Skyy Tortle`

You have the innate, slow, and faintly undignified flight of your people. You
gain a **fly Speed of 10 feet**. Taking off is a single action, as is landing.
While flying you must **Sustain** the flight each round; if you are knocked
prone, fall unconscious, or otherwise stop sustaining it, you fall.

## Mature Growth  *Feat 13*

`Skyy Tortle`

The years have told. You become **Large**, your reach increases to **10 feet**,
and your Speed decreases by 5 feet. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to
Athletics checks to Shove, Trip, or Grapple, and you must Squeeze through spaces
a Medium creature could walk.
